What are UTM tags?

UTM tags are text parameters appended to a URL. An analytics platform that supports them can read the source, medium, and campaign values and group visits into reports. The tags do not record visits by themselves, prove where a visitor came from, or replace analytics configuration and privacy consent requirements. They are labels; measurement depends on the systems and policies you use.

Choose names your team can report on

Agree on a simple naming convention before creating many campaign URLs. Keep source and medium values consistent instead of mixing variants such as Email, email, and e-mail for the same channel. Use the campaign value to identify a launch or time period. Use utm_content only when it answers a useful comparison question, such as which of two links in the same message gets more clicks. Consistency matters more than a particular capitalization style.

Before sharing

  • Open the generated URL and confirm the destination and existing parameters.
  • Do not put names, email addresses, or other personal information in UTM values.
  • Remember that tracking values may appear in browser history and analytics reports.
  • Test the link in an appropriate environment before sending a large campaign.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to fill in every field?

Fill in source, medium, and campaign for useful basic classification. Term and content are optional and should be used only when they add value to your reporting.

Can I tag a URL that already has query parameters?

Yes. Other parameters are preserved. If an entered UTM key already exists, its value is replaced rather than duplicated.

Do UTM tags improve search rankings?

No. UTM parameters are for campaign attribution, not ranking signals. Avoid adding them to ordinary internal links unless you have a specific measurement reason.
