Turn WhatsApp conversations into qualified sales opportunities in seconds.

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: Chispa — WhatsApp conversation management system with smart opportunity qualification

A Workbench stack running on top of the official WhatsApp interface (WhatsApp Business Platform), turning every incoming conversation into an automatically classified and qualified sales opportunity, instead of manually managing conversations inside a web app.

1) Target audience

• Local shops and services in Latin America (restaurants, dental clinics, salons, stores, contractors) that use WhatsApp as their only customer communication channel.

• Small independent businesses (1–5 employees) with no sales team and no budget for traditional CRM tools.

• Customer service staff and field sales agents who handle dozens of conversations daily and need quick classification instead of manual reading.

• Store owners who lose opportunities due to slow replies and failure to follow up on open messages.

• Other markets with heavy WhatsApp usage such as India, Southeast Asia, Africa, and the Middle East after proving the model in Latin America.

2) Points of differentiation (why choose our product)

• Official architecture with no interface injection: we connect through the WhatsApp Business Cloud API and webhooks instead of reading the DOM or simulating clicks — no breakage when the WhatsApp interface updates, and no violation of platform terms.

• Smart qualification inside the conversation itself: intent classification (buy/inquiry/complaint/after-sales), field extraction (product, budget, city, appointment), and a 0–100 readiness score with a clear reason for every rating.

• Free to start with no credit card: 100 qualified conversations per month plus all core response tools, with the paid subscription only needed to scale.

• A pipeline built on conversations rather than an empty form: every conversation is automatically recorded as an opportunity with its source (ad, organic, referral) and expected value.

• Ready-made responses in the brand voice: a response generator in Arabic, Spanish, and English that learns from your activity templates, products, and prices instead of generic copy.

• Compliance built in by design: approved message templates, opt-in/opt-out logs, a block on random mass messaging, and one-click customer data deletion.

• Local privacy: hosting in the LATAM region, encryption, and no sending of sensitive data to the AI provider without explicit consent.

3) First version scope (max 8 tasks)

1. Signup and WhatsApp linking: business account + linking a number via Cloud API (QR/verification code) through an approved provider, with a single workspace.

2. Unified inbox: WhatsApp conversations shown across phone, email, and web with filtering by status, tag, and source, plus full-text search.

3. Contact records: a profile per customer with custom fields (city, product, budget) and a complete activity history.

4. Simplified pipeline (Kanban): new ← qualified ← follow-up ← quote ← won/lost, with quick move via button.

5. AI qualification engine: intent classification + field extraction + readiness score + rating reason + a "no conversation left unqualified" rule.

6. Quick reply templates and AI response generator: pre-written copy for common scenarios with business identity customization.

7. Compliant mass campaigns: opt-in lists, approved templates, honor for opt-out, and an exportable compliance log.

8. Analytics dashboard and export: time to first reply, conversation volume, quote/deal conversion rate, and a monthly CSV report.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: 3 users, 100 qualified conversations per month, unified inbox + Kanban + quick replies with a daily cap + CSV export. No credit card.

• Pro — $12 per user per month: unlimited conversations, mass campaigns, product catalog, follow-up automation, advanced reports.

• Business — $39 per month per team: roles and permissions, multi-step reply sequences, webhooks and API interface, audit logging.

• Usage-based add-ons: extra analytics packages beyond the cap, and backup/large exports.

• Later enterprise path: dedicated hosting or white-label for agencies.

• Revenue logic: value comes from reducing late replies and preventing wasted opportunities; the free tier is enough for an individual and removes friction, and paid plans activate as the team grows.
