FareOrbit — Complete Project Plan

One API that gives you reliable flight prices and smart predictions, with a free start and no complexity.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target audience

• Developers of travel apps and websites, and startups building search or price-comparison engines.

• Small and medium travel agencies that need real-time price data without building complex integrations.

• Travel analytics and market-research platforms, and product teams that rely on price predictions.

• Low-code tool builders, students, and hackathon participants.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Data from licensed, multiple sources (GDS, NDC, and low-cost airlines) instead of relying on page scraping, which increases reliability and reduces legal risk.

• A unified JSON schema that works across all sources, with clear documentation and stable API versions.

• Fast responses through smart caching and result aggregation, with fair and transparent usage limits.

• Price predictions and instant alerts via Webhooks and email, not just live price display.

• A free Sandbox environment and initial SDKs for Python and JavaScript, with a free start and no credit card.

• Privacy compliance (GDPR), transparency about data sources, and a clear error policy.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Developer registration and management of API keys, quotas, and permissions.

2. Flight search (one-way/return/multi-city) with airline, time, and price filters.

3. Unifying results from multiple sources into one JSON schema with conflict handling.

4. Returning deep booking links (Deep Links) through partners without processing payment.

5. A simplified historical price log and alerts via Webhook and email.

6. A developer dashboard showing usage, errors, and response time.

7. A Sandbox environment with test data, clear limits, and interactive documentation.

8. Full documentation with initial SDKs for Python and JavaScript and ready-made examples.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free to start: 1,000 requests per month + permanent Sandbox, no credit card.

• Starter: $29 per month for about 50,000 requests, with basic alerts.

• Growth: $99 per month for about 250,000 requests, with price predictions and priority support.

• Enterprise: custom pricing based on volume and a service-level agreement.

• Additional revenue: commission when a booking is completed through deep links, and sales of advanced analytics and prediction packages.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Publish SDKs and open-source examples on GitHub to attract developers through search.

• Optimize documentation for search engines and target phrases such as "flight price API" and "flight data API".

• Launch on Product Hunt, Hacker News, Reddit, and specialized developer communities.

• Write practical tutorials on Dev.to, Medium, and LinkedIn and include runnable examples.

• Create a Discord server and a free support channel to build a community around the API.

• Participate in hackathons and universities and offer free credits to promising teams.

• List the API in developer directories and free discovery platforms, with video content on YouTube.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: data-source terms of use and redistribution rights; solution: clear licensing agreements, regular audits, and avoiding unauthorized scraping.

• Privacy: user data and alerts; solution: data minimization, encryption, and GDPR compliance.

• Technical: source volatility, schema changes, and rate limits; solution: a source abstraction layer, automated monitoring, and backup data.

• Data quality: outdated or unavailable prices; solution: mandatory timestamps, double validation, and conflict alerts.

• Supplier dependence: a major source outage; solution: multiple sources, request distribution, and a recovery plan.

• Liability: price or booking errors; solution: a clear disclaimer, published service limits, and transferring the booking to the partner.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: FareOrbit

Marketing tagline: One API that gives you reliable flight prices and smart predictions, with a free start and no complexity.
