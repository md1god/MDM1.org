Namaa — Full Project Plan

Know your child's needs before they cry.

Full execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

New product plan for tracking a baby's sleep, feeding, and diapers

1) Target audience

• Parents and families of children aged 0–3, especially in Arab cities.

• App-dependent users (Tech-savvy parents) who want data, not guesswork.

• Care providers: nannies, small daycares, pediatricians who keep a daily child log.

2) Differentiators

• Smart prediction (light ML) for the baby's next sleep and next meal based on weekly patterns, not just a log.

• Offline-first log with later sync — essential for travel and weak-signal areas.

• Night mode: one button logs sleep/diaper in under two seconds.

• Doctor-visit-ready reports (PDF) instead of scattered screenshots.

• Multi-caregiver sharing mode (father, grandmother, daycare) with permissions and activity time per member.

• Native full RTL Arabic design, dark/light, and one-handed sizes.

3) First-version scope (max 8 tasks)

1. User registration and one account per child (multi-child).

2. Sleep tracking (start/stop/duration) with history.

3. Feeding tracking (breast/bottle/formula/solid) with amount and time.

4. Diaper and urination tracking + health notes.

5. Tummy time and activities tracking.

6. Weekly dashboard: total sleep hours, meal count, simple trends.

7. Smart alerts based on the last recorded sleep/feeding time.

8. PDF report export + optional cloud backup.

(Intentionally out of the first version: ML prediction, English translation, desktop app.)

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free to start: one child, all core features, no ads.

• Nominal monthly/yearly subscription (Free-to-start): unlimited children, advanced reports, prediction, caregiver sharing.

• Family plan: father/mother + up to 4 devices.

• B2B: annual daycare subscription (report per child).

• Additional revenue: cloud backup / advanced feeding features in-app.

5) Global rollout plan with free channels

1. App store ASO: Arabic/English title + local screenshots + keywords like "baby sleep tracking".

2. Short content (Reels/TikTok/Shorts): tips "how to know your baby is hungry" showcasing app screens.

3. Parent social groups (Facebook/Reddit/Telegram): share user stories, not direct ads.

4. SEO: short blog posts on sleep stages by age + sleep/feeding comparison tables.

5. Small partnerships: nutritionists and doctors try it free in exchange for a mention.

6. Weekly email: your child's sleep digest = free retention channel.

7. Waitlist + invite campaign among families for the paid-features version.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Privacy and medical: child data is highly sensitive — encryption in transit and at rest, permanent deletion on request, and avoiding any medical claim ("does not replace a doctor's consultation").

• Compliance: GDPR, COPPA, and local data protection laws; avoid collecting location data without consent.

• Security: review notification permissions, account breaches expose children's information — enable 2FA.

• Technical: local/cloud sync conflicts, data loss without internet → idempotent sync and recovery tests.

• Competition: established free products → differentiate through prediction, RTL experience, and caregiver sharing.

• App stores: rejection by Apple/Google under a hesitant health policy → document the policy in advance.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Namaa

Tagline: Know your child's needs before they cry.
