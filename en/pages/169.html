CalGrid — Complete Project Plan

A small step every day, and every step builds your future.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the launch plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Beginners in habit formation: students and people new to the field of self-development.

• Busy people: those who do not have time to use complex apps.

• Global users: those who need a simple interface that supports multiple languages without complexity.

• Those interested in slow improvement: who prefer small daily progress over big goals.

2) Points of Distinction (Why Choose Our Product)

• Absolute simplicity: a single grid to display progress, without tables, complex lists, or exaggerated statistics.

• Direct visual motivation: growth appears as a clear grid, which enhances the feeling of achievement day after day.

• Flexibility in application: allows correcting a missed day without harshly breaking the chain, which encourages continuation.

• Globally open: designed web-first, fast to load, and works on all devices.

• Focus on the problem: it solves only the habit-formation problem, without distracting side features.

3) Scope of the First Version (Maximum 8 Tasks)

• [ ] Sign-up: create an account using email or common accounts.

• [ ] Add a habit: ability to add one or more habits with a custom color chosen for each.

• [ ] Progress grid: display an SVG grid to show days of achievement during the year.

• [ ] Log achievement: log or undo achievement with one click for each day.

• [ ] Streak tracking: automatically calculate the longest achievement streak.

• [ ] Dark mode: support dark mode for a comfortable experience.

• [ ] Data export: ability to export user data in .json format.

• [ ] Basic privacy: secure data storage with simple terms of use.

4) Pricing and Profit Model

• Free to start: all features of the first version are available for free, without ads.

• Freemium model: an optional subscription that supports future features (such as automatic backup and advanced customization) at a symbolic price.

• Gradual growth goal: focus on organic growth first, before relying on revenue.

5) Global Launch Plan Through Free Channels

• Search optimization: targeting long-tail phrases such as "How do I start a simple daily habit" in English and several other languages.

• Communities: valuable participation in self-improvement communities on Reddit, while avoiding direct advertising.

• Short video: creating TikTok and YouTube Shorts clips that explain the idea in less than 30 seconds.

• Reference apps: checking free launch lists such as Product Hunt at launch.

• Educational content: writing short articles about habit formation, and linking them to the product naturally.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal: the need to comply with data protection laws such as GDPR, while providing a clear privacy policy and simple terms of use.

• Technical: ensuring fast grid performance as the number of users grows, in addition to protecting account data and avoiding errors in calculating streaks.

• Operational: the risk of over-reliance on a single channel, which can be avoided by diversifying free channels from the start.

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Slogan

Proposed name: CalGrid

Marketing slogan: "Every day a small step, and every step builds your future."
