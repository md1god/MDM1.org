Nuqta — Complete Project Plan

Turn any video into ready-to-publish points in seconds.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, monetization model, growth plan, and risks. The project is under development and has not been launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Small content creators: They own emerging channels and do not have time to watch the entire video to extract ideas.

• Content marketers: They need to repurpose video content for posts or ads quickly.

• Entrepreneurs: They want to turn lectures or video presentations into ready-to-use action points with minimal effort.

• Beginners in Arabic content: They prefer a tool that focuses more on Arabic and its dialects.

2) Points of Differentiation (Why Choose Our Product)

• Arabic-first focus: Better accuracy in text extraction and understanding of Arabic dialects.

• Practical points instead of a general summary: Produces actionable points, not just a description of the video.

• Ready for every platform: Provides a custom format for each platform with the ability to edit in one click.

• Lightweight and fast: Simple interface, works on mobile and desktop with the fewest steps possible.

• Greater privacy: Processes requests instantly without permanently storing users' links.

3) Scope of the First Version (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. [ ] Link input: Support public links with validity checks.

2. [ ] Text extraction: Convert speech to clear, copyable text.

3. [ ] Summary in points: Generate 3 to 5 practical points from the video.

4. [ ] Rephrasing: Convert points into a format suitable for publishing.

5. [ ] Title suggestions: Generate catchy titles for the post.

6. [ ] Format per platform: Choose a ready-made format for the desired platform.

7. [ ] Quick copy mode: Copy the result in one click without extra steps.

8. [ ] Instant free processing: Enable the first version fully free without the need to create an account.

4) Pricing and Monetization Model

• Free to start: A limited number of daily processing requests, available to everyone without a bank card.

• Advanced plan: A low-cost monthly subscription that allows more processing and higher speed.

• Freemium model: Only non-essential features are restricted, while basic point extraction remains free forever.

5) Global Growth Plan with Free Channels

• Free tool directories: Submit to directories that only accept free submissions.

• Educational content: Write articles explaining "how to turn a video into points for publishing" for search engines.

• Content creator communities: Share practical tips in free communities without aggressive promotion.

• Arabic content creator lists: Promote within free Arabic communities due to the initial focus.

• Organic recommendations: Rely on active users sharing ready-made results with others.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal:

• Copyright: Must be limited to summaries and points, and must not reproduce the full text or protected content.

• Platform policies: Must only access public data and must not exceed it.

• Technical:

• Access changes: Must design an architecture that can switch between text extraction sources if changes occur.

• Extraction accuracy: Difficulty with dialects or noise, so a lightweight model must be used to handle them.

• Costs: A strict daily limit must be set for the free plan to preserve resources.

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Slogan

Proposed name: Nuqta

Marketing slogan: Turn any video into ready-to-publish points in seconds.
