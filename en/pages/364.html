Nabat — Complete Project Plan

Keep your farm, track your profit from anywhere.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the distribution plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Small and medium farmers: especially those who own small plots and want to easily track their expenses.

• Organic farmers and mixed farming: those who need to record daily activities accurately.

• Agricultural cooperatives: to unify records among their members.

• Agricultural extension agents: to support farmers in tracking their production plans.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Works offline (Offline-first): can be used in rural areas with weak connectivity, with automatic sync when the connection returns.

• Simple and fast interface: designed for all users, including those not experienced with technology.

• Free tier to start: lets the farmer begin immediately at no cost.

• Focus on costs: helps track expenses and revenues to clearly know the profitability of each crop.

• Mobile-first: for ease of use while working in the field.

• Full data control: lets the user easily export their data without restrictions.

3) First Release Scope (max 8 tasks)

1. User interface design: create a simplified, responsive design suited to field work.

2. Database setup: build a system to store records locally with sync capability.

3. Fields and seasons registration: add the ability to organize the farm by seasons and crops.

4. Input and cost tracking: record seeds, fertilizers, water, and labor with their costs.

5. Daily activity log: record tasks quickly and easily.

6. Offline support: develop local storage with later sync.

7. Simple reports: generate expense and production reports in an exportable format.

8. User permissions management: to allow sharing records within the farm or cooperative.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Completely free to start: all core features available at no cost.

• Optional group subscription: low-cost monthly subscription for agricultural cooperatives for increased storage and automatic backup.

• Optional add-on services: such as advanced report exports or connecting sensors, added only as needed.

• Careful revenue model: focused on achieving sustainable income without charging small farmers.

5) Global Distribution Plan via Free Channels

• Search engines: create educational agricultural content targeting rural areas in different languages.

• Free workshops: partner with agricultural extension centers to present the solution and train farmers on it.

• Rural communities: participate in agricultural forums and farmer groups online.

• Referral program: encourage farmers to invite their peers in exchange for free perks.

• Short video content: publish simple educational videos on free platforms to increase reach.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal risks:

• Data protection: comply with global and local privacy laws, and ensure farm data is not shared without the farmer's explicit consent.

• Data ownership: emphasize that farm data belongs solely to the user, with full rights to export or delete it.

• Technical risks:

• Weak connectivity: handled through offline support and gradual sync.

• Low-spec devices: very lightweight design to run smoothly on older phones.

• Data loss: provide periodic export options, plus an optional and secure backup system.

7) Suggested Name and Tagline

Suggested name: Nabat

Tagline: Keep your farm, track your profit from anywhere.
