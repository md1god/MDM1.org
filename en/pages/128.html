Zad — Complete Project Plan

Zad — your restaurant fully managed for free, and your profits grow with no monthly subscription.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, profit model, growth plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

Product plan: Restaurant management system

1) Target audience

• Small and medium restaurants (1–20 branches) in the Middle East, North Africa, and Southeast Asia.

• Cafes, fast-food chains, and corporate buffets.

• Accountants and POS providers looking for a flexible, low-cost system.

• Local developers who want to customize the system and distribute it to their clients.

2) Points of distinction (why choose our product)

• Completely free to start: installation, selling, and printing with no monthly subscription.

• Open-source architecture written from scratch, installed on the buyer's server or in the cloud.

• Unidirectional Arabic/English interface with full support for thermal printing and digital menus (QR).

• Smart inventory: automatic conversion of ingredients into finished items upon sale, with depletion and spoilage alerts.

• Reports ready for tax auditing and e-invoicing for each country.

• Works offline and syncs data when the network returns.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Fast POS: tables, modifiers, split bill, void, and multiple payment methods.

2. Menu and pages management: items, options, images, and seasonal menus.

3. Kitchen (KDS) with an order queue and preparation times per station.

4. Inventory and ingredients: recipes (BOM), cost, shortage alerts, partial/full stocktake.

5. Daily sales and profitability reports + cash drawer and shift report.

6. User and permission management (cashier, chef, manager, accountant).

7. Responsive web app + QR self-ordering menu generation.

8. Cloud backup and data export (CSV/PDF) with no data lock-in.

4) Pricing and profit model

• Free forever: POS, menus, kitchen, basic reports, one branch.

• Paid (monthly/yearly SaaS): multiple branches, managed cloud, automated backup, delivery and wallet integrations, advanced reports.

• Services: customization, training, hardware installation, and priority support.

• Local distributor partners who get a share of customer subscriptions.

5) Global growth plan with free channels

• Open-source repository with full documentation and a multilingual README.

• Short videos on YouTube/TikTok: "Build your restaurant in 10 minutes."

• Community groups on Telegram, Discord, and Reddit for restaurants and cafes.

• Local SEO articles: comparisons, cost calculators, operating tips for restaurants.

• Presence at free expos, developer competitions, and a user referral program.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: billing and tax regulations vary between countries; personal data protection compliance (GDPR/local regulations) for customer data.

• Legal: ownership rights for fonts, icons, and images — only open licenses must be used.

• Technical: conflicts between thermal printing queues and different POS systems; they must be supported gradually.

• Technical: data loss on failure — requires tested backups and an offline mode.

• Operational: difficulty switching from existing systems; an import tool for legacy data is needed.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Zad

Marketing tagline: Zad — your restaurant fully managed for free, and your profits grow with no monthly subscription.
