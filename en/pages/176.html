Rasid — Complete Project Plan

Guide your shadow by the stars even when all maps go dark.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

New Product Plan

1) Target Audience

• Hikers and campers in areas without internet coverage (mountains, deserts, forests).

• Trackers and night-camping practitioners who need to determine direction at night.

• Beginner amateur observers (Astronomy) and science students.

• Users in remote areas (safari trips, high seas, disaster zones) where cloud maps fail.

• Age group 18–45, mainly using Android.

2) Points of distinction (why our product)

• Works fully offline: all computations on the device (Compass, Barometer, GPS, Camera, local Almanac).

• Star navigation as a core function: direct conversion to true north by aligning the selected planet/star via the camera (AR).

• Computed readings, not raw: magnetic heading + declination correction + pressure altitude + weather estimate for the next 12 hours.

• Lowest battery consumption by disabling heavy sensors and a power-saving "night" mode.

• Free-to-start: all core features free forever, no paywall in front of the star feature.

• Privacy first: no mandatory account, no uploading of the user's location.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Digital compass with declination and smoothing correction.

2. Barometer altitude indicator + pressure curve for the last 6 hours.

3. AR "observatory" module: identifying the horizon, north, and the altitude of a planet/star.

4. Local weather estimate (Humidity + Pressure trend) without internet.

5. High-contrast night mode + battery saving.

6. Mini star guide: Summer Triangle, Gemini, Polaris (texts + local graphics).

7. Smart alerts: sunset/sunrise, start of darkness, temperature drop.

8. Exporting settings and saving last locations on the device only.

4) Pricing and profit model

• Completely free for core features (compass, altitude, observatory, weather, stars).

• Optional Pro plan (~$2.99/month or $19.99/year): external maps, advanced alerts, extra star tool packs.

• Additional revenue: optional in-app donations + training products (optional Premium feature packs).

• No ads that distort readings or drain the battery.

5) Global rollout plan with free channels

• Arabic/English SEO: articles "How to find your direction by the stars without internet".

• YouTube/Shorts: 30-second videos showing the compass working in the dark.

• Reddit and discussions: r/hiking, r/astrophotography, r/survival + Arabic forums (Reddit, X/Twitter threads).

• Google Play ASO: keywords (offline compass, star navigation, survival tools).

• Free partnerships: university camping groups, astronomy clubs, outdoor bloggers.

• Product Hunt + Hacker News + Open Source pages (if the source is published under a free license).

• Local Telegram/WhatsApp groups: weekly newsletter with short navigational guides.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: location data is sensitive → Location permissions must be clearly declared; avoid any claim of saving lives (Disclaimer: not a substitute for a dedicated compass/GPS).

• Legal: planet/star names are not proprietary, but trademarks of analog devices must be avoided.

• Technical: Android device variability (Sensor fusion, Kalman filter) → testing across a wide range of models.

• Technical: magnetic declination requires manual calibration → step-by-step guided interface.

• Technical: AR accuracy in low darkness → fallback to a voice/text direction mode.

• Safety: not storing track logs by default to avoid privacy issues.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Rasid

Tagline: Guide your shadow by the stars even when all maps go dark.
