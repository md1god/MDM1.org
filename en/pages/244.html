Himma — Complete Project Plan

Renew your presence and publish confidently across all your platforms from one place.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, growth plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: solving the content scheduling problem across social media with AI agents

1) Target Audience

• Creators: owners of personal accounts who need to publish their content regularly without spending a long time.

• Small businesses: stores and projects that do not have a full marketing team.

• Small marketing agencies: need to manage several client accounts from one place.

• Emerging organizations: looking for free tools to start growing quickly.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Free to start: no credit card needed to sign up and for basic use.

• Driven by AI agents: it plans and suggests content based on the user's goals, rather than being just a scheduling tool.

• Easy to use: a simplified interface based on a clear calendar, suitable for beginners.

• Supports both Arabic and English: to enable broader reach globally and across the Arab world.

• Flexible and smart: it chooses the best posting times for each platform based on simple data.

• Reduces cost: it relies on available free models, and gives the user the option to use paid models only when needed.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

• [ ] 1. Accounts system: email login with a simple authentication option.

• [ ] 2. Account linking: linking core social media accounts through a secure system.

• [ ] 3. Scheduling calendar: creating a drag-and-drop calendar to schedule posts.

• [ ] 4. Content generation agent: suggesting suitable texts and images for each platform based on the user's idea.

• [ ] 5. Auto-publishing: scheduling and publishing posts at the specified time.

• [ ] 6. Template library: providing ready-made templates for a quick start.

• [ ] 7. Publish status: showing the status of each post (scheduled, published, failed).

• [ ] 8. Free usage limit: clearly defining the number of accounts and posts for free.

4) Pricing and revenue model

· Plan · Description · Price ·

· Free · A limited number of accounts and posts per month, with basic features. · $0 ·

· Basic · More accounts and posts, with greater access to the content agent. · $9/month ·

· Professional · Almost unlimited, priority support, and extra features for agencies. · $29/month ·

Revenue model: a freemium model, where individual users are usually fine with the free plan, while agencies and small services pay for more capacity.

5) Global growth plan using free channels

• Search engine optimization: writing educational articles such as "best posting times for each platform" in both Arabic and English.

• Creator communities: actively participating in communities such as Reddit and Indie Hackers with useful content, without excessive direct promotion.

• Short content: creating educational videos on Shorts and TikTok explaining solutions to the regular posting problem.

• Product launch platforms: launching the product on free platforms aimed at innovators.

• Word of mouth: encouraging users to share their experience with peers in their fields.

• Arab communities: reaching Arab entrepreneur communities with simplified Arabic content.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

· Risk type · Potential risk · How to mitigate it ·

· Legal · Social media terms of use, and data protection laws such as GDPR. · Complying with each platform's policies, obtaining explicit permissions, and applying clear privacy practices. ·

· Legal · Intellectual property rights of the content the user generates or uses. · Clarifying that content use is for the user only, and guiding them to follow copyright. ·

· Technical · Changes to social media APIs, affecting publishing. · Building a flexible abstraction layer, following platform updates, and keeping up with changes quickly. ·

· Technical · Unexpected consumption of AI model tokens. · Setting clear usage limits in the free plan, and enforcing a maximum request cap per user. ·

· Technical · Security risks such as leakage of account access tokens. · Encrypting all tokens, using best secure storage practices, and reviewing security periodically. ·

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Himma

Tagline: Renew your presence and publish confidently across all your platforms from one place.
