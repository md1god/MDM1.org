Waslah — A Complete Project Plan

Turn your relationships into your first warm introduction to real funding.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, growth plan, and risks. The project is under development and has not yet been launched.

1) Target Audience

• New founders: Entrepreneurs in the pre-seed or seed stage who do not have a budget for funding intermediaries.

• Startups outside major hubs: Especially in emerging markets where public investor lists are less useful.

• Teams with limited resources: Those who prefer to leverage existing relationships rather than cold outreach.

2) Key Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• Focus on real connections: Instead of massive lists, only those who can actually provide a warm introduction are highlighted.

• Completely free to start: Any founder can get an initial action plan without paying or creating a complex account.

• Actions ready to execute immediately: Only the first three actions are shown so users can start right away, without long reports.

• Privacy-first: Data collection is minimized, with priority given to manual input to protect contacts.

3) Scope of the First Version (Maximum 8 Tasks)

• [ ] Onboarding model: Input company summary, stage, sector, and amount needed.

• [ ] Network input: Add contacts manually or via a limited, secure list.

• [ ] Fund matching: Classify funds based only on stage and sector.

• [ ] Identify recommendation points: Determine who among them is most likely to provide a warm introduction.

• [ ] Prioritization: Give each recommendation a clear priority level with a reason.

• [ ] "Do first" list: Create a short, ready-to-apply action list.

• [ ] Show reasons: Display the reason for each match to build user trust.

• [ ] Unified dashboard: Display all results on a single page with full focus.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

| Service | Price | Notes |

| --- | --- | --- |

| Warm introduction plan | Free | Available to everyone and includes the full scope of the first version. |

| Outreach execution service | Paid per campaign | Only activated when the user explicitly requests help with following up on introductions, not for viewing the list. |

Revenue model: Fees for execution, not for displaying match results.

5) Global Free Growth Plan

• Educational content: Publish short guides on the art of getting a warm introduction, with a focus on underserved markets.

• Open communities: Contribute real value to entrepreneur groups without any direct advertising.

• Collaboration with non-profit accelerators: Share a free checklist with their cohorts as a helpful tool.

• Search engine optimization: Organically target local funding terms in multiple languages.

• Downloadable templates: Offer a free warm introduction message template to attract organic traffic.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

| Type | Risk | Mitigation |

| --- | --- | --- |

| Legal | Violation of contact data privacy under GDPR and others. | Rely on manual input, minimize storage, and provide a clear and specific privacy policy. |

| Legal | Confusion between warm introductions and unsolicited bulk messages. | Ban any bulk messaging feature and strictly adhere to the concept of introductions based on an existing connection. |

| Technical | Storing unnecessary data. | Do not save full contact lists; rely on temporary processing where possible. |

| Technical | Misleading users with inaccurate match scores. | Show clear reasons instead of absolute scores, focusing on realism over quantity. |

7) Suggested Name and Tagline

Suggested name: وصلة

Tagline: Turn your relationships into your first warm introduction to real funding.
