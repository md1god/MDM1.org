Wajhah — Complete Project Plan

Every scan opens an editable destination, with print-ready art and privacy-respecting analytics.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Primary audience: small and medium businesses that place QR codes on printed materials: restaurants and cafes, retail and packaging, clinics, real estate, and events.

• Secondary audience: marketing agencies, printers, packaging designers, growth teams, and developers who need an API.

• Market: a global product from day one with Arabic and English interfaces, then Spanish, French, and German.

• Problem: the printed code is static and cannot be changed, art can undermine scannability, analytics are often privacy-heavy, and available tools are scattered across generation, links, and measurement.

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

We do not sell a pretty image on top of a QR code; we sell a code that remains valid for printing, editing, and measurement. The identity and design are new, and all code and assets are written from scratch.

• Print-reliable art: a scan reliability score before export, contrast and quiet zone checks and error correction, and CMYK/SVG/PDF export with bleed.

• Permanent destination editing: a custom domain, a branded short link, an expiry date, and a fallback redirect without reprinting.

• Privacy-respecting analytics: no cookies, aggregated data, ready for GDPR/CCPA/PDPL, with an EU hosting option.

• Campaign and agency operations: folders, tags, bulk creation, API, webhooks, and separate client workspaces.

• Permanent free tier: start without a card, clear limits, and data export on departure.

• Arabic first: prompts, templates, and RTL direction are part of the core design, not a later translation.

3) First Version Scope (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. Platform foundation and first experience: Next.js + TypeScript, Postgres, authentication, workspaces, roles, onboarding, landing page, and free plan limits.

2. Printable QR art generator: Arabic/English prompt, styles, brand logo and colors, 3 variants, scan reliability score, and PNG/SVG/PDF CMYK export.

3. Dynamic links and domains: short link, destination editing without changing the code, custom domain with automatic SSL, expiry date, and fallback redirect.

4. Lightweight analytics: scans, unique visitors, country, device, time, referral source, bot exclusion, and 30/90-day retention.

5. Campaigns and organization: folders, tags, search, bulk creation and updates via CSV, and code label export.

6. API and Webhooks: keys, usage limits, documentation, link creation and editing, analytics reading, and a webhook on first scan.

7. Billing and limits: Stripe and Stripe Tax, plans, usage meter, upgrade and downgrade, invoices, and limit enforcement.

8. Trust and reliability: link reputation checks, blocklists, abuse reporting, rate limiting, audit log, backups, and uptime monitoring.
