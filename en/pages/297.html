Zawtar Gallery — Full Project Plan

Your photos, your gallery, your privacy — all in one place.

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the rollout plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: an open-source web photo gallery with a modern experience and privacy first

1) Target audience

• Amateur photographers and digital camera owners: they want a private or public gallery for their photos with full control over photo privacy.

• Families and individuals: they store photo memories in one place that can be shared with specific people.

• Professional photographers and small teams: they need client proofing and photographer brand protection.

• Users looking for a self-hosted alternative: they want control over their data without high subscription fees.

• Global users: they need an easy interface in multiple languages, with a mobile-first design.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Modern and fast interface: responsive, mobile-first design, with a photo grid optimized for loading speed.

• Smart search and classification: automatic AI tagging (optional, runs locally) and search by time, place, and people.

• Smart sharing: temporary links, view/download permissions, and client image review.

• Privacy and data ownership: full self-hosted operation with a managed cloud option.

• Extensible system: documented plugins and themes interface, with a lightweight marketplace for free and paid plugins.

• Lightweight operating model: runs on cheap servers or Docker containers, with a cloud version that is easy to start with.

3) First version scope (maximum 8 tasks)

1. User system and login, with roles (owner, editor, guest).

2. Bulk upload and support for common formats + thumbnails.

3. Fast grid gallery with lazy loading and pagination.

4. Albums and organizational structures, and manual tags.

5. Public/private sharing and shared links with an expiration date.

6. Search engine optimization (SEO) and a translatable interface (i18n).

7. Self-hosted version via container + easy settings.

8. Basic plugin API + developer documentation.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free to start: fully free self-hosting (open source) + free cloud tier with limits (number of images/month, storage).

• Paid cloud plans (freemium):

• Personal: more storage, custom domain, branding removal.

• Pro: client gallery, watermark, reports, feature priority.

• Team/Agency: multi-user, advanced permissions, priority support.

• Plugin marketplace: a small commission on paid plugins and themes from contributing developers.

• Support and services: support and training packages for small businesses.

5) Global rollout plan using free channels

• GitHub: open-source launch, clear documentation, and a good API to attract developers.

• SEO and content: blog, comparison (alternatives) pages, and tutorials in Arabic and English.

• Communities: forums and groups on Reddit, Discord, and Telegram for photographers, plus posts in self-hosting communities.

• Video: tutorials on YouTube (how to run it with Docker, setting up a professional gallery).

• Partnerships: local photography communities, and ready-made gallery templates for amateurs and professionals.

• Tool directories: listing the product in open-source tools and photographer tools.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal:

• Privacy rights of people in photos (GDPR and local laws) and responsibility for data protection.

• Copyright over photos and the user's responsibility when publishing publicly.

• Third-party plugin approval may add vulnerabilities or license compatibility dependencies.

• Technical:

• Storage performance and displaying extremely large images (requires format conversion, storage sharding, CDN).

• Security vulnerabilities in uploads and shared files (protection against unauthorized access).

• Dependence on external components (continuous updates and maintenance).

• Feature divergence between the self-hosted and cloud versions (dual maintenance risk).

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Zawtar Gallery

Marketing tagline: Your photos, your gallery, your privacy — all in one place.
