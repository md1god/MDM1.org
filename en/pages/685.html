Sakina — Complete Project Plan

Breathe smarter in one minute, and regain your calm wherever you are.

A complete plan for a new digital project covering concept, audience, product, revenue model, rollout, and risks. The project is under construction and not yet launched.

An original product plan for breathing training

1) Target audience

• Office employees aged 25–45 sitting 6+ hours daily with shallow breathing and mental fatigue; we target 40% in major cities.

• University students before exams needing 60–180-second sessions to calm situational stress.

• Adults with mild anxiety or trouble sleeping who avoid long meditation apps; about 30% of health-app users.

• Yoga instructors and naturopathic therapists as a small B2B channel for credibility and group plans.

• Initial markets: English, then Arabic, Spanish, and Portuguese, with full right-to-left support.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Adaptive rhythm: suggests pattern and duration from a quick 3-question input (energy, stress, sleep), not templates.

• 60–300-second micro-sessions for work breaks; smart notifications no more than 3 times daily.

• A weekly "breathing fitness" score (0–100) measures commitment and consistency, with light gamification.

• Works without an account and offline as a PWA under 5 MB; data stored locally by default.

• Privacy first: on-device processing, no audio recording, one-tap data export/deletion.

• Generous free tier: core patterns and daily streaks are free; payment is only for deeper features.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Welcome screen and 3-question assessment to personalize session one under 20 seconds.

2. Six guided patterns (box, 4-7-8, coherent 5.5, diaphragmatic, extended exhale, morning energizer) with visuals and vibration.

3. Recommendation engine sets pattern and duration from assessment and time of day.

4. Daily streak counter and weekly fitness score, with simple visual celebration on completion.

5. Offline PWA with local notifications and one customizable reminder.

6. 4-week beginner program (5 sessions weekly) with a completion badge each week.

7. Basic statistics: breathing minutes, sessions, best streak, and average self-reported calm.

8. Shareable image progress card with only the username and no sensitive data.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free forever: six patterns, beginner program, streaks, and basic statistics, no ads.

• Premium: $3.99 monthly or $24.99 yearly (48% off), with advanced patterns, rhythm customization, and Apple Health/Google Fit sync.

• Family/small team: $49 yearly for 5 users, under $1 per person monthly.

• Instructor/studio license: $199 yearly, with a simple dashboard, training materials, and 30% referral margin.

• Flexible regional pricing (e.g., $1.99 monthly in emerging markets) to raise conversion rather than one global price.

• No data selling; revenue only from subscriptions, building trust and reducing legal risk.

5) Global rollout plan with free channels

• SEO with practical 800–1,200-word articles in 5 languages on questions like "how do I breathe when stressed" and "breathing exercise before sleep."

• 15–30-second videos on TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels, 3 weekly, each showing one exercise.

• Launch on Product Hunt and Reddit in productivity and health communities, with a permanent free version as a draw.

• Ambassadors: 50 yoga instructors in year one get a referral link and 30% of first payment.

• App Store Optimization (ASO) for terms like breathing exercises and stress relief, plus local languages.

• Weekly email list from the landing page with a 7-day group breathing challenge for organic reach.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: avoid medical claims; state clearly that the product is not a medical device and does not replace consultation; review Apple Health and Google Play health policies.

• Privacy: implement GDPR and CCPA via explicit consent, data minimization, and immediate account/data deletion.

• Technical: camera-based pulse accuracy varies; present it as an optional estimate, not for health decisions.

• Device performance: test on older devices, limit long sessions, and offer audio/vibration alternatives when graphics fail.

• Market: strong competition; address it with short sessions, adaptivity, and low regional pricing.

• Quality: A/B test notifications to avoid annoyance; monitor weekly retention, targeting 35% after 4 weeks.

7) Name and tagline

Proposed name: Sakina

Tagline: Breathe smarter in one minute, and regain your calm wherever you are.
