Midad — Complete Project Plan

Your digital memory that stays on your device, capturing what you see and answering you about it with evidence.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, launch plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a personal memory assistant that runs locally

1) Target audience

• Freelancers and consultants who move between dozens of windows and sources daily and lose track of what they saw.

• Researchers and graduate students who gather material from websites, files, and conversations and later remember only that they "saw something."

• Support and sales teams that need to recall what appeared on screen during a call or a ticket.

• Privacy-conscious users who refuse to upload their screen data to an external cloud.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Privacy first: processing and storage on-device by default, and no screenshot is uploaded except by explicit request.

• Organized memory, not just screenshots: turning visual events into queryable knowledge units (sessions, topics, tasks).

• Retrieval by meaning, not by keyword: a natural query like "What did we agree on in Tuesday's meeting?" returns the answer with the visual evidence.

• Works fully offline, and is available on Windows, macOS, and Linux.

• Open source and extensible via community plugins, with a free-to-start model.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Controlled periodic capture of screen activity with automatic masking of sensitive fields (passwords, financial fields).

2. Converting screenshots to text (OCR) and extracting titles, links, and identifiers.

3. Automatically grouping events into organized time-based "sessions."

4. A searchable local index (SQLite + vector index via sqlite-vec or LanceDB).

5. A local chat assistant that answers by citing specific sessions and moments (local RAG via Ollama).

6. A Timeline view to display memories and filter them by source and date.

7. One-click clear/delete/freeze recording, and a configurable retention policy.

8. Exporting memories to Markdown/JSON to ensure no lock-in.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free-to-start model: full personal use for free with practically unlimited local storage.

• A personal Pro plan (low monthly subscription): optional encrypted cross-device sync, smart scheduling, higher-quality models, and long-term archiving.

• A teams plan (per seat): sharing of curated knowledge bases with consent, permissions, and an audit log.

• Additional revenue: commercial licenses for companies that want to run it on-premises with enterprise support and a service-level agreement.

5) Global launch plan via free channels

• Publishing the open-source repository on GitHub with a clear README and one-command installation, plus ready-made binaries.

• Posting on Hacker News and Reddit (r/selfhosted, r/privacy, r/productivity) with a focus on privacy.

• Short educational content on YouTube and Medium: "Your digital memory without leaving your device."

• Free extensions and metrics in VS Code and Raycast/Alfred to generate organic usage.

• Collaboration with local-tool communities (Ollama, LocalLLaMA) and presence on Product Hunt.

• An ambassador program for open-source contributors that grants badges and perks.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legally: privacy and surveillance concerns, and laws such as GDPR/CCPA. Solution: on-device processing by default, explicit disclosure, consent before recording, and an "ignore apps" mode.

• Legally: copyright of saved screen content. Solution: storing stripped-down text with source links instead of copying full content where possible.

• Legally: third-party data in calls. Solution: optional face/name masking and a short retention policy.

• Technically: resource and battery consumption. Solution: adaptive capture and batch analysis during idle time.

• Technically: accuracy of OCR and local models. Solution: multilingual models and continuous improvement with user correction.

• Technically: security of local storage. Solution: encrypting the knowledge base with an OS key (Keychain/DPAPI).

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Midad

Tagline: Your digital memory that stays on your device, capturing what you see and answering you about it with evidence.
