Sahla Commerce — Complete Project Plan

Launch your store and deliver your orders in minutes, free and without complexity.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, growth plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

An original product plan: an integrated commerce and delivery platform

1) Target audience

• Small and medium stores that sell via WhatsApp and Instagram and lack an organized ordering system.

• Grocery stores, pharmacies, and local restaurants that need fast delivery within a specific geographic area.

• Independent delivery coordinators (captains) seeking extra income with flexibility.

• Stores moving from a single store to multiple branches and needing a unified dashboard.

• Emerging markets that lack effective local payment and map solutions.

2) Points of distinction (why choose our product)

• Truly free start: run a full store without a credit card or contract.

• Works first without stable internet: a progressive web app (PWA) with later sync.

• Flexible local payment: cash on delivery + e-wallets + local gateways.

• Native multi-store: seller/driver/supervisor each has a dedicated interface from day one.

• Open and easy to customize: no restrictions on the store's brand.

• Technological lightness: runs on mid-range devices and old phones without heavy apps.

3) First version scope (MVP — 8 tasks max)

1. Simple store registration (name, location, image, working hours).

2. Product catalog management (add/edit, images, prices, stock).

3. Build a cart and order via a shareable store link.

4. Define the delivery zone on the map and calculate fees.

5. Accept the order and assign it to a driver with live status tracking.

6. Cash on delivery + one local electronic payment.

7. Driver dashboard (accept/reject/set delivery point).

8. Merchant tracking dashboard (orders, revenue, statuses).

4) Pricing and profit model

• Free: one store, up to 50 orders per month, basic payment link.

• Growth: low monthly subscription — unlimited orders, multiple branches, reports.

• Business: small commission per order + custom domain and branding.

• Additional revenue: sharing delivery fees, professional captains subscription, and payment gateway fees.

• Principle: no commission on free stores to build trust and spread.

5) Global growth plan with free channels

• Local growth through groups: join merchant and driver groups on WhatsApp and Telegram.

• Content marketing: short videos explaining how to start in 5 minutes (TikTok/YouTube).

• Ambassador program: every merchant who brings a merchant gets a free month.

• Captain partnerships: referral incentives for captains in new cities.

• Partial Open Source + ready-made templates: attract developers via a public components repository.

• Local SEO: multilingual city pages and technical SEO.

• Gradual geographic expansion: city by city instead of a simultaneous global launch.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Protecting customers' and drivers' personal data and complying with local privacy laws.

• Delivery licensing requirements and freelance work regulation in some countries.

• Electronic payment laws and money holding (structural payment risks).

• Usage taxes and e-invoicing for each market.

Technical:

• Intermittent connectivity in emerging markets (mitigated by PWA and sync).

• Map accuracy and unstructured addresses.

• Scalability when orders increase during peak times.

• Account security and prevention of price manipulation and fake orders.

• Multiple payment providers adoption and integration complexity.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Sahla Commerce

Marketing tagline: Launch your store and deliver your orders in minutes, free and without complexity.
