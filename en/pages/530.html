Visora — Complete Project Plan

Turn your AI visibility into tangible growth opportunities.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, business model, growth plan, and risks. The project is under development and not yet launched.

1) Target Audience

• Small and medium-sized businesses: Looking to track their visibility in AI search engines.

• Digital marketing agencies: Need clear reports for their clients on their visibility in AI-powered results.

• Content creators and emerging brands: Want to know if their content is mentioned in AI responses.

• SEO specialists: Seeking to measure the impact of AI on visibility without relying solely on traditional clicks.

2) Competitive Advantages (Why Choose Our Product)

• Focus on action, not just visibility: Focuses on actionable content edits to increase the likelihood of being cited, rather than just statistics.

• Simplified reports: Clear and fast display that allows for decision-making within minutes, without complex tables.

• Multi-model support: Track across multiple AI models with unified results in one dashboard.

• Free to start: A sufficient number of queries for beginners with no credit card required.

3) Scope of the First Version (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. Project setup: Build a structure from scratch with simple configurations.

2. Add two brands: Ability to enter a brand name and related keywords.

3. Initial scan: Check brand visibility in results for specific queries across selected models.

4. Simple dashboard: Display visibility status, number of mentions, and the AI model in which it appeared.

5. Actionable suggestions: Show 2-3 specific suggestions to improve content to increase the likelihood of being cited.

6. Shareable reports: Generate a short, exportable PDF report.

7. Free limit: Apply a clear daily free limit with a usage counter.

8. Basic validation: Add simple input validation and prevent empty queries.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

· Plan · Price · Features ·

· Free · $0 / month · Sufficient daily limit to start, basic dashboard, limited export. ·

· Professional · $19 / month · Increased daily limit, more tracking, unlimited export, broader suggestions. ·

· Agencies · $59 / month · Multiple workspaces, team reports, priority processing. ·

Revenue model: Monthly/annual subscriptions with a discount for annual payments, focusing on conversion from the free plan without requiring a credit card at signup.

5) Global Growth Plan with Free Channels

· Channel · Goal · Implementation ·

· Educational content · Attract business owners · Write short articles about the impact of AI on visibility, with practical examples. ·

· Content SEO · Long-term organic visibility · Focus on high-intent keywords related to tracking visibility in AI results. ·

· Business communities · Build trust · Provide useful contributions to entrepreneur communities without direct advertising. ·

· Free tool lists · Increase visibility · Submit to well-known free tool lists with an accurate and specific description. ·

· Experience sharing · Natural recommendations · Publish short case studies of real brands showing tangible results after optimization. ·

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

· Type · Risk · Mitigation ·

· Legal · Differing AI model usage policies between providers. · Use only authorized methods, comply with each model's terms, and update the usage policy regularly. ·

· Legal · Content rights and trademarks. · Do not store full responses, limit to visibility indicators (mention of name/link if present). ·

· Technical · Frequent changes to model interfaces. · Design separate modules for each model to allow for quick updates when changes occur. ·

· Technical · Rate limits and blocking. · Implement smart delays, daily limits, and intelligent retries to avoid overload. ·

· Technical · Accuracy of results · Display a confidence level for visibility and notify the user when the result is uncertain. ·

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Slogan

Proposed name: Visora

Marketing slogan: Turn your AI visibility into tangible growth opportunities.
