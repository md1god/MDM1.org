Riqash — Full Project Plan

Write by hand and annotate any file — from any device, no install.

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the rollout plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: Riqash

1) Target audience

• Students and researchers who write by hand and annotate PDF files (papers, books, notes).

• Teachers and trainers who teach on slides and files and share annotations.

• Lawyers, doctors, and engineers who need quick commenting on documents.

• Artists and drafters who want a lightweight multi-layer drawing environment.

• Tablet and stylus users looking for a solution that works on all operating systems without installation.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Works directly in the browser (PWA) on Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, and Linux: no installation and no OS lock-in.

• Local-first: all notes are saved on the device and exported to PDF without an account.

• Optional end-to-end encrypted (E2EE) sync with a limited free account.

• Vector digital ink with low latency + palm rejection.

• Search within handwriting via on-device OCR without uploading data.

• Clear, lightweight license: the basic version is completely free for personal use.

3) Scope of the first version (8 tasks maximum)

1. Ink canvas with layers, undo/redo, and page templates (lined, grid, blank).

2. Import PDF files and annotate them with annotation layers independent of the original.

3. Stylus support: pen pressure, velocity sensitivity, and palm rejection.

4. Automatic local saving (IndexedDB) and export of the project and pages to PDF/PNG.

5. PWA experience: install to the home screen and work offline.

6. Handwriting search via local OCR.

7. Optional encrypted sync + read-only share link.

8. Landing page + first appearance on Google and Product Hunt + in-app feedback portal.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free-to-start: all basic editing features, export, local storage, up to 3 active PDF files and 200MB of sync space.

• Pro - $4/month or $36/year: unlimited sync across devices, advanced OCR, template library, version history, priority support.

• Education/teams - $2/student/month: classroom bundles with shared space.

• Additional revenue source: licenses for device-managed enterprises and education plans, with no ads in the workspace.

5) Global rollout plan using free channels

• Multi-language SEO blog (Arabic, English, Spanish): guides on handwriting on PDF and stylus comparisons.

• YouTube/TikTok: short videos demonstrating pen drawing and PDF annotation.

• Product Hunt, Hacker News, and Reddit (r/NoteTaking, r/iPadPro, r/opensource).

• Discord/Telegram community + weekly drawing contests and a best-note prize.

• University ambassadors: student clubs and university community pages.

• Indexing in free SaaS directories, educational coupon sites, and Google Play/App Store Progressive listing.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: sync data privacy (GDPR) - applying E2EE and minimizing stored data, with clear policies; rights of imported PDF files - processing them locally without uploading; licensed font and drawing rights; avoiding patents on pen input/handwriting recognition via early screening.

• Technical: ink responsiveness across diverse devices (GPU acceleration/Windows Ink/Apple Pencil); browser support differences; sync conflicts - resolved with a CRDT model; OCR accuracy on Arabic handwriting and varying handwriting styles; tablet battery management.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Riqash

Tagline: Write by hand and annotate any file - from any device, no installation.
