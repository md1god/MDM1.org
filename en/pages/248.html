Nabaa — Complete Project Plan

Simple financial management for institutions that serve communities.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Small non-bank financial institutions: such as savings and credit associations, microfinance institutions, and financial cooperatives.

• Community finance operators: those who need to manage loans and savings without having large technical infrastructure.

• Emerging financial service providers: those seeking to start a simple financial service at the lowest possible cost.

2) Points of Distinction (Why choose our product)

• Ease of use: designed to work directly without the need for a specialized technical team, with a simplified interface suitable for mobile use.

• Ready to start for free: any institution can use it for free to get started, with no licensing fees or complications.

• Focus on the essentials: it focuses only on the essential tasks (customers, loans, savings, and accounts), which greatly reduces complexity.

• Gradual scalability: it starts simple and can be expanded as the institution grows without rebuilding the entire system.

3) Scope of the First Version (maximum of 8 tasks)

1. Customer management: registering customers, their groups, and their basic data.

2. Loan products: creating different loan types with easily defined terms.

3. Savings products: managing savings accounts with balance tracking.

4. Transaction processing: recording repayment, deposit, and withdrawal.

5. Simplified general ledger: automatic recording of basic financial transactions.

6. Basic reports: daily reports on amounts, loans, and savings.

7. User permissions: managing employee roles and permissions for each role.

8. Institution settings: setting the institution name, currencies, and its basic details.

4) Pricing and Profit Model

• Free to start: available for free to small institutions that use the basic features.

• Freemium model: a small fee is charged for additional services such as automatic backup, premium technical support, and a custom domain.

• Implementation partnerships: specialized partners can provide activation and training services for their own fees, without obligating the institution to purchase a license.

5) Global Rollout Plan with Free Channels

• Open documentation: providing a guide in Arabic and English that can be downloaded for free.

• Open community: creating a community forum for sharing questions and solutions among users.

• Free education: publishing short videos and lessons on how to use it.

• Free partnerships: collaborating with financial empowerment organizations to enable their institutions to use it.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal risks: regulatory compliance requirements vary from one country to another. These will be handled through customizable settings templates, while leaving final compliance responsibility to the institution or its legal advisor.

• Technical risks: these include data security, system stability, and transaction protection. They will be addressed through encryption, backups, and separation of duties to reduce operational complexity.

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Tagline

Proposed name: Nabaa

Marketing tagline: Simple financial management for institutions that serve communities.
