Washik — Complete Project Plan

From search to application in minutes, with a decision you understand and control.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, growth plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched.

1) Target audience

• Job seekers with mid-level experience who apply to 10–50 jobs per month.

• Recent graduates and career changers seeking clear guidance and time savings.

• Applicants for international or remote jobs facing complex, multilingual ATS forms.

• Later: universities, employment centers, and career coaches through a B2B2C model.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Transparent matching: a compatibility score with clear reasons and skill gaps drawn from the résumé and job description—not a black box.

• Honest customization: tailor the résumé and cover letter per job without inventing experience or credentials.

• User-approved autofill: a browser extension that fills and explains fields; it never submits automatically.

• Speed with human review: cut application time from about 30 minutes to under two minutes, with a final confirmation.

• Privacy first: local processing where possible, encryption, export and deletion, and no data sales.

• Practical coverage: gradually support Greenhouse, Lever, and Workday, with continuous compatibility updates.

• Free start: real value without a credit card, lowering the trial and adoption barrier.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Import PDF/DOCX résumés and extract an editable professional profile.

2. Collect jobs from public sources and available ATS APIs, deduplicate, and refresh results.

3. Matching engine gives a compatibility score, reasons, gaps, and improvement suggestions.

4. Generate a tailored résumé and cover letter per job with strict anti-fabrication constraints.

5. Browser extension fills Greenhouse, Lever, and Workday forms with review before submission.

6. Tracking dashboard for applications, statuses, follow-up reminders, and notes.

7. Privacy controls: data export, account deletion, access log, and consents.

8. Free beta on web and extension, measuring activation and autofill quality.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free forever: job search, basic match score, 3 tailored documents monthly, limited autofill.

• Pro: about $14.99 monthly or $99 yearly; unlimited customization, expanded autofill, faster updates, advanced tracking.

• Credits: $5 for 10 tailored applications for non-subscribers.

• B2B: licensing for employment centers and universities, with admin dashboard and anonymized reports.

• Revenue: subscriptions + institutional licenses + optional add-ons. No user-data sales; regional pricing reflects purchasing power.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Practical SEO: pages for each role and city, ATS guides, and a free résumé-job compatibility checker.

• Communities: Reddit, Discord, LinkedIn, and Slack, sharing genuine advice, not direct ads.

• Launch: Product Hunt, Hacker News, and Indie Hackers, with a free demo.

• Short content: YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and LinkedIn on application mistakes and résumé optimization.

• Universities and coaches: free partnerships, joint offers, and training workshops.

• Referrals: one free Pro month per party when a friend activates.

• Localization: interface and content in English, Arabic, and Spanish first, then high-demand languages.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Terms and scraping: rely on official APIs, keep autofill user-requested in-browser, and respect rate limits.

• Privacy: comply with GDPR and CCPA, minimize and encrypt data, and provide export, deletion, and explicit consent.

• Bias: ban protected attributes in scoring; periodically review results and explanations.

• Misrepresentation: ban false experience, require user review, and keep a change log.

• Account security: never store ATS passwords; use OAuth and least privilege.

• Form fragility: ATS interfaces change; use flexible selectors, monitoring, and manual autofill fallback.

• Accuracy: confidence thresholds, continuous testing, and human review before submission.

• Cost and scale: processing queues, caching, and clear spending limits for models and collection.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Washik

Tagline: From search to application in minutes, with a decision you understand and control.
