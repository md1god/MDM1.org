أثر — Complete Project Plan

Know your brand's impact in AI answers before your competitors.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, growth plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

An original product plan to monitor visibility in AI answers

1) Target Audience

• Small and medium-sized businesses: lack the budget for enterprise tools and need a simple, effective solution.

• Independent marketers: looking for quick results without complex reports.

• Local websites: want to know how visible they are in their local markets through AI answers.

2) Competitive Advantages (Why choose our product)

• Free to start: anyone can start without a credit card and with a clear usage limit.

• Action-oriented: instead of overwhelming users with numbers, it provides practical suggestions for each missed opportunity.

• Multi-language support: allows tracking visibility in non-English-speaking markets.

• Extremely simple: designed for beginners, not just marketing experts.

3) Scope of the First Version (Maximum 8 tasks)

1. Account creation: simple sign-up via email.

2. Add brand: enter the brand name and keywords to track.

3. Initial scan: check visibility across a selected set of models (ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews/AI Mode, Perplexity).

4. Quick dashboard: display number of mentions, citations, and missed opportunities at a glance.

5. Practical suggestions: show up to 3 actionable suggestions for each missed opportunity.

6. Daily alerts: email notification when there is a noticeable change in visibility.

7. CSV export: ability to export results for use outside the dashboard.

8. Free credit display: clearly show remaining free scans at all times.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

· Plan · Price · Features ·

· Free · 0$ / month · Limited monthly scans, basic alerts, and practical suggestions. ·

· Growth · 9$ / month · More scans, additional language tracking, and faster alerts. ·

· Growth+ · 29$ / month · Expanded scans, scan priority, and email support. ·

Revenue model: monthly subscriptions with a strong focus on converting free users.

5) Global Growth Plan with Free Channels

• Free tool directories: submit to lists of tools open to the general public.

• Educational content: write very short articles explaining how to improve visibility in AI answers.

• Niche communities: actively participate in marketer communities by answering questions with real value.

• SEO for targeted pages: create short pages targeting common questions about visibility in AI answers.

• Organic exchange: non-paid collaboration with small educational content creators to raise awareness.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

· Type · Risk · Mitigation ·

· Legal · Policy changes in some models · Focus only on publicly observable visibility, and do not store private or protected content. ·

· Technical · Rapid changes in access methods · Build a flexible, adaptable structure with regular reviews of methods used. ·

· Technical · Exceeding quotas or overloading · Enforce a strict quota system for the free version and schedule scans intelligently. ·

7) Suggested Name and Marketing Slogan

Suggested name: أثر

Marketing slogan: Know your brand's impact in AI answers before your competitors.
