Para — Full Project Plan

Simple, fast, free-for-everyone parametric drawing.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target audience

• Engineers and civil engineers: for plans, sections, and simple structural works.

• Students and teachers: in engineering and design to support learning and practical application.

• Model makers and innovators: from hobbyists to innovators who need accurate models.

• Small product designers: they need quick designs before turning to expensive software.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Ease of use: a simple, intuitive interface that requires no deep design-software experience.

• Highly flexible: supports parametric (Parametric) design so the design changes automatically when dimensions are modified.

• Completely free to start: runs directly in the browser with no install needed, and allows free project saving.

• Works on any device: lightweight and fast, running on computers, tablets, and even mid-range devices.

• Open to educational licensing: strong support for schools and universities.

• Focus on the core problem: delivers fast, simple design without the complexity of massive professional CAD programs.

3) Scope of the first version (max 8 tasks)

1. 2D drawing: create lines, circles, arcs, and basic dimensions.

2. 3D drawing: create simple shapes (cube, cylinder, basic solids) and manipulate them.

3. Parametric system: link dimensions and equations to change the design automatically.

4. Simplified interface: a clear, easy toolbar and a concise properties panel.

5. Instant preview: fast 3D view with orbit and fit-to-view.

6. Save and retrieve: save the project locally or in a free account.

7. Basic export: export SVG, DXF, and STL files for later use.

8. Mini tutorial: an interactive lesson for first-time use.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Completely free to start: all first-version features are free with no harsh limits.

• Extra cloud storage: additional project storage quota for a nominal subscription.

• Professional templates: a paid template library for advanced users.

• Institutional educational or industrial licensing: for entities that need user management or special support.

• Main revenue model: optional subscriptions with a focus on keeping the product free and attracting as many users as possible.

5) Global rollout plan using free channels

• Content marketing: short educational tutorials in Arabic and English on free learning platforms.

• Tech communities: participating in forums for engineers, students, and model makers.

• Academic distribution: offering it to universities and schools for free and promoting its use.

• Free social media: short videos (quick walkthrough of a use case) on public platforms.

• Search engine optimization: targeting terms "free parametric drawing", "simple 3D design".

• Free referrals: encouraging users to share their projects, which increases organic spread.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: avoid copying any code or designs, and make sure to use open-source licenses only for libraries.

• Intellectual property: focus on the idea of "solving the problem in new ways" rather than copying another product.

• Technical: 3D graphics performance in the browser may need continuous improvement.

• Parametric complexity: achieve a balance between power and simplicity to avoid product complexity.

• Scalability: build a clean foundation that allows evolution without a full rewrite later.

• User acceptance: some users may prefer their current programs, so the focus must be on easy migration.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Para

Tagline: Simple, fast, free-for-everyone parametric drawing.
