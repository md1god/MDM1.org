Rawnaq — Complete Project Plan

Your routine is built on ingredients, not trends.

Complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, launch plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• End consumer: women and men aged 18–34 in the Gulf and Middle East, interested in Korean skincare and supplements, and those with sensitive skin or a limited budget.

• Small content creators: influencers with 1K to 100K followers in beauty and wellness, who want trustworthy content and affiliate earnings.

• Small and medium-sized brands: K-Beauty and supplement brands that want to enter TikTok Shop and retain customers after a campaign, not just achieve immediate sales.

2) Points of Differentiation (Why Choose Our Product)

• From ingredient to routine, not from trend to cart: recommendations based on ingredients and interactions, not just trending products.

• Ingredient conflict detector: warns before mixing substances that may cause irritation or cancel out each other's effectiveness.

• Comprehensive, adjustable routine: morning/evening, according to skin type, climate, budget, pregnancy, and allergies.

• Arabic-first and RTL: an experience designed for the Gulf and Middle East, with English support for global expansion.

• Free for the consumer, no inventory: a lightweight model based on affiliate and subscriptions, not on purchasing goods.

• Ready for content creators: shareable routine cards and short-video scripts with clear commercial disclosure.

3) MVP Scope (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. Bilingual diagnostic questionnaire (Arabic/English) covering skin type, goal, allergies, climate, and budget.

2. Initial ingredient database (150–200 ingredients) with clear conflict rules.

3. Recommendation engine that builds a morning and evening routine and explains why each step was chosen.

4. Curated product catalog with an ingredient card and purchase/affiliate links.

5. Shareable and saveable routine page, exportable as an image or PDF.

6. Short-video script generator for the user or influencer with a disclosure reminder.

7. Free brand portal to add products and request verification.

8. Basic analytics: clicks, conversions, and routine completion rate.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free for the consumer: diagnosis, routine, catalog, and sharing at no charge.

• Free for brands: product listings and a basic page, to accelerate catalog growth.

• Affiliate commission: 5–15% on sales converted through links.

• Optional brand subscription: $49–199 per month for advanced analytics, verification, and featured placement.

• Routine+ later: $2.99 per month or $19.99 per year for progress tracking and alerts, without disrupting the free version.

5) Global Launch Plan with Free Channels

• Organic TikTok: three fixed templates: “Analyze an ingredient,” “Don't mix these two,” and “Two-week routine.”

• Micro-influencer program: free samples + affiliate code + ready-made routine cards.

• SEO: Arabic/English ingredient encyclopedia and guides such as “Oily skin routine in the Gulf.”

• Free communities: Reddit, Facebook Groups, and WhatsApp/Telegram, with educational rather than promotional content.

• Global launch: Product Hunt, Pinterest, YouTube Shorts, and LinkedIn for brands.

• Referral loop: “Check your friend's routine” for free in exchange for in-app exposure.

• Academic partnerships: pharmacy and beauty students to review content and enhance credibility.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal: medical claims in beauty and supplements, affiliate disclosure, data privacy (GDPR/PDPL), rights to use ingredient data, and TikTok Shop terms.

• Technical: recommendation accuracy and ingredient database quality, AI model hallucination, dependence on TikTok APIs, data security, and multilingual expansion.

• Mitigation: human review of rules, licensed data sources, automatic disclosure, data encryption, and a fallback plan for direct links outside TikTok.

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Tagline

Proposed name: Rawnaq

Marketing tagline: Your routine is built on ingredients, not trends.
