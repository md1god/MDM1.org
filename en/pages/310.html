Fitr — Full Project Plan

Fitness that starts from your fitra.

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the rollout plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: Fitr

1) Target audience

• Home fitness practitioners (18–40 years old) with no gym membership.

• Beginners who need simple workout organization (no complicated explanations).

• Users in the Arab world looking for an authentic Arabic interface (RTL) in fitness apps.

• Professional athletes (weightlifting/calisthenics) who need accurate logging of PRs and training volume.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Offline-first: the app works fully without internet; sync is optional later.

• Privacy first: local storage on the device, plus a JSON/CSV backup file export the user owns.

• Natively Arabic from the ground up: polished RTL design with precise sports terminology, no back-translation.

• No mandatory account: registration is required only for those who want cloud sync.

• Compact and flexible: custom exercises, routine templates, smart rest timer, and clear progress charts.

3) Scope of the first version (8 tasks at most)

1. Local database (SQLite/Hive) + groundwork for optional future sync.

2. Arabic/English exercise library with descriptions and available alternatives.

3. Create and start a workout: log sets × reps × weight.

4. Quick routine templates (ready-made + user templates).

5. Smart rest timer with notifications.

6. Progress charts: weekly training volume, best weights (PRs).

7. Body measurements (weight, circumferences) with charts.

8. Two-language support (AR/EN) + dark mode + backup export/import.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: exercise library, workout logging, templates, measurements, export.

• Pro subscription (starting from ~$4/month or ~$30/year): advanced analytics, custom plans by goal, multi-device cloud sync, automatic exercise substitution, and PDF reports.

• Additional income: featured trainer plans (revenue share) and a small commission.

5) Global rollout plan with free channels

• Free blog/content site: "bodyweight home exercises" articles + ASO with Arabic and English keywords.

• Short videos (TikTok/Reels/Shorts): before/after + one exercise walkthrough every day.

• Communities (Reddit, Discord, WhatsApp/Telegram groups): 30-day challenges to build a habit.

• Partnerships with fitness coaches for reviews and free trials.

• Launch on Product Hunt + a weekly "Workout of the Week" newsletter.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: add an explicit medical disclaimer; avoid therapeutic claims / guaranteed weight loss.

• Health data privacy: comply with store policies and health privacy (GDPR/HIPAA where applicable), encrypt cloud backups.

• Technical: sync conflicts across devices, battery drain from notifications, maintaining and translating the exercise database.

• Commercial: competition with major apps; reduce reliance on a single platform.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Fitr (فِطرة)

Marketing tagline: Fitness that starts from your fitra.
