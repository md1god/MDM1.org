Hawan — Complete Project Plan

Test your flight in a safe virtual world, then launch it into reality with confidence.

Complete execution plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, expansion plan, and risks. The project is under development and not yet launched.

1) Target Audience

• Drone and autonomous systems development teams that need a safe testing environment before field trials.

• Universities, research centers, and robotics students, especially those without labs or powerful computers.

• Inspection, surveillance, agriculture, and delivery companies that want to test operational scenarios without risking their assets.

• Software engineers who want to integrate flight tests into CI/CD pipelines.

• Regulatory bodies and trainers who need repeatable, documented scenarios.

2) Competitive Advantages (Why Choose Our Product)

• Runs entirely in the browser with no installation or local GPU setup, with cloud execution when needed.

• Highly repeatable scenarios with version fingerprints for physics, environment, and sensors.

• Ready-made bridge for ROS 2 and Python SDK, with automated tests in CI/CD.

• Ready library of environments and sensors, plus synthetic training data generation.

• Real-time collaboration via scenario links, comments, and versions instead of sharing large files.

• Exportable readiness and risk reports to support safety and compliance teams.

• Free tier to lower the barrier to entry for students and startups.

3) Scope of First Version (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. Visual drag-and-drop scenario editor with ready-made templates.

2. Basic flight physics simulation including wind, turbulence, and engine failures.

3. Sensor library: RGB and depth camera, LiDAR, IMU, GPS, and barometer.

4. Three ready environments: city, farm, and indoor warehouse.

5. Python SDK and ROS 2 bridge to connect existing control code.

6. Automated test execution with pass/fail reports, including screenshots and trajectories.

7. Flight recording, replay, and analysis of trajectory, energy, and collisions.

8. Scenario sharing via link with view or edit permissions.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free: Public scenarios, limited monthly simulation minutes, and up to two users.

• Pro: $29 per user/month, additional runtime hours, reports, and data export.

• Team: $99 per month per small team, with advanced collaboration, CI integration, and version history.

• Enterprise: Custom pricing for private deployment, SSO, data guarantees, and compliance.

• Additional revenue: Cloud computing minutes, environment and sensor marketplace with commission, and training and certification services.

• Education: Permanent discount for universities and group lab licenses.

5) Global Expansion Plan with Free Channels

• Publish SDK and examples as open source on GitHub, with a quick start guide and ready scenarios.

• Educational content on YouTube and a technical blog to improve search visibility.

• Engage in ROS, Dronecode, Reddit, and Discord communities, and solve user issues publicly.

• Partner with universities through free licenses, graduation projects, and student competitions.

• Launch on Product Hunt and Hacker News, and run a public challenge with symbolic prizes.

• Virtual presence at robotics conferences and publish technical papers on the simulation-to-reality gap.

• Global ambassador program: free access in exchange for regional workshops and training.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

Legal:

• Classification of simulation as dual-use technology may impose export restrictions in some markets.

• Privacy of images and geospatial data, especially when simulating real locations.

• Ownership and licensing of 3D assets, and liability for use in real operational decisions.

• Varying aviation regulations across countries, requiring a clear disclaimer.

Technical:

• The simulation-to-reality gap may create false confidence.

• Cloud computing costs and browser latency.

• Realism of sensors and real-time performance when running large fleets.

• Security of shared scenarios and protection of customer data.

• Maintenance of integration compatibility with changing ROS versions and libraries.

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Slogan

Proposed name: Hawan

Marketing slogan: Test your flight in a safe virtual world, then launch it into reality with confidence.
