Sada AI — Complete Project Plan

A platform that measures your brand mentions in AI answers and turns every mention into a measurable growth opportunity.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout plan, and risks. The project is under development and has not yet been launched.

1) Target Audience

• Marketing teams at startups and mid-sized companies that rely on content to attract customers but lack the budget for enterprise tools.

• SEO and content agencies that need sellable reports for their clients across multiple brands.

• E-commerce and SaaS brands that want to know where their products are mentioned in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews.

• Multilingual content teams, with priority on Arabic and English, as most existing tools are weak at covering Arabic queries and dialects.

• New GEO/AEO specialists who want a lightweight tool they can start using for free without a sales call.

2) Points of Differentiation (Why Choose This Product)

• Linking mentions to results, not just counting them: We track Citation → visit → sign-up/purchase via UTM links and referral analytics to show business impact instead of just a dashboard of numbers.

• Arabic-first: Support for queries in both Modern Standard Arabic and dialects, along with Arabic entity analysis, which is a clear gap among competitors.

• Methodology transparency: A confidence score for each mention, a change log, and clarity on when verification wasn't possible instead of claiming absolute accuracy.

• Actionable recommendations: Not just identifying gaps, but generating a content brief that specifies which pages and sources need to be improved.

• Fair competitive comparison: Share of Voice against competitors, with tracking of who gained and who lost mentions.

• A truly free start: A permanent free plan with no credit card required and a full self-serve experience.

• Privacy by design: No unnecessary storage of personal data, an option for European hosting, and the ability to export or delete data.

3) Scope of the First Version (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. Brand onboarding: Enter the name, website, competitors, markets, and languages.

2. Smart prompt library: Generate queries for purchases, comparisons, and problem-solving, and group them by intent.

3. Multi-model runner: Run periodic checks through available APIs with rate limits and retries.

4. Mention and citation extraction: Detect entities, normalize names, and identify the cited source.

5. Visibility and Share of Voice dashboard: Time-based trends and competitor comparisons by model and language.

6. Instant alerts: Email or Slack alerts for new mentions, lost mentions, or a competitor surpassing you.

7. Gap analysis and content recommendations: Compare model answers to identify differences and generate an actionable brief.

8. Reports and billing: PDF/CSV exports, free plan limits, and Stripe subscriptions.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free: 1 brand, 10 prompts, weekly checks, 1 user, 7-day history.

• Starter — $29/month: 3 brands, 100 prompts, daily checks, 3 users.

• Growth — $79/month: 10 brands, 500 prompts, advanced alerts, API access, 10 users.

• Agency — $199/month: 30 brands, white-label reports, multiple permissions, priority support.

• Enterprise: Custom pricing, SSO, regional hosting, SLA.

• Additional revenue: Extra prompt-run packs, paid setup/audit services, and a 20% discount for annual billing. No seat-based fees on smaller plans to ease adoption.

5) Global Rollout Plan Through Free Channels

• Free visibility check tool: An "AI Visibility Check" for a single brand that generates a short, shareable report carrying the product name.

• Programmatic SEO: Pages for each sector, market, and language such as "AI visibility for fintech in MENA", built from aggregated data, not copied.

• Open prompt library: A public repository on GitHub to attract researchers and practitioners and generate backlinks.

• Educational content: Guides, comparisons, and case studies on the blog and YouTube, with Arabic and English newsletters.

• Communities: Reddit, Indie Hackers, Product Hunt, LinkedIn, and X, through discussions and shared methodologies rather than direct ads.

• Agency partnerships: A recurring commission referral program and joint offers with SEO experts.

• In-product viral loop: Shareable reports and a free month for each successful referral.
