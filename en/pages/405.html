Maydan — Complete Project Plan

Your personal arena — one workout starts now.

A complete execution plan for a new digital product: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the launch plan, and the risks. The product is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: smart personal workout generator

1) Target audience

• Adults (18–45) who don't have a gym membership or struggle to attend regularly.

• Complete beginners who don't know where to start or which exercises are right for their level.

• Time-constrained people (parents, remote workers) who need an effective workout in 10–30 minutes.

• Home or travel fitness practitioners with limited equipment (dumbbells, no equipment at all).

• People returning to activity after a break or a minor injury who want a gradually progressive program.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• True flexibility in inputs: builds the session based on the equipment actually available and the minutes available, not a fixed template.

• A plan, not a single workout: generates a complete week with load and rest progression, then redistributes when a day is skipped.

• Arabic voice mode: voice guidance and rest timers without needing to touch the screen during the workout.

• Learns from your response: lowers or raises the load based on your difficulty rating after each session.

• Offline mode: saves the current session locally so the workout doesn't stop because of weak connectivity.

• Free at its core: everything the user needs is free by default, and payment is only for advanced features.

3) First version scope (8 items maximum)

1. Short survey: goal, level, approximate age, available days and minutes, equipment, any medical restriction.

2. Generate a full weekly plan via LLM with built-in safety rules (load limits, banned risky exercises).

3. Session page: steps for each exercise, suggested weights, sets and reps, illustrative images.

4. Voice mode and timer: start/stop, rest counter, switching exercises by voice.

5. Completion logging and day streak with local on-device storage.

6. Swap any exercise with an equivalent alternative based on equipment without breaking the plan structure.

7. Weekly progress dashboard: total minutes, completed exercises, self-rated difficulty curve.

8. Share/export the plan as an image or a text file.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free forever: one renewable weekly plan, basic voice mode, progress logging.

• Pro — $9/month or $59/year: unlimited plans and instant edits, advanced stats and reports, extra voice library and workout patterns, calendar/reminders integration.

• Entry alternatives: a lifetime pass at a fixed price, and optional cosmetic add-on packs.

• Core revenue comes from subscriptions; no ads inside the training session, out of respect for focus.

5) Global launch plan using free channels

• SEO: FAQ articles in Arabic and English ("15-minute no-equipment exercises"...) with exercise pages ready to rank.

• TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts: publishing a complete short daily workout with voiceover, and before/after for user plans.

• Reddit and specialized forums: sharing free tools in fitness and beginner communities with a direct link to the app.

• Product Hunt launch and free AI tools directories, showcasing the Arabic voice feature.

• Partnerships with small content creators: cross-promotion in exchange for custom plans for their audience, with no money involved.

• Discord/Telegram community for daily follow-up and streak incentives, with transparent weekly updates.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal/medical: no medical advice is provided; explicit disclaimer; users are required to report injuries; avoid any claim of treatment or diagnosis.

• Privacy: compliance with data protection laws (GDPR and equivalents), data minimization, a full local-storage option, and clear consent before processing.

• Intellectual property: image, music, and voice rights from licensed sources; name/trademark similarity checks against existing registries before launch.

• Technical: validating AI output with fixed rules before display; language model API call costs; single-provider dependency may break — keep a backup provider and usage caps.

• Experience quality: unsuitable results erode trust — a rating after every session as a mandatory feedback loop.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Maydan

Marketing tagline: Your personal arena — one workout starts now.
