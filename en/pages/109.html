Darb — Complete Project Plan

Your tasks on your path, on all your devices, with no limits.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

Product Plan: A Native Multi-Source Task Manager

1) Target Audience

• Linux users and the GNOME desktop specifically, who want a native and fast task app.

• Individuals and small teams who keep their data on Nextcloud or CalDAV and refuse cloud lock-in.

• Freelancers and students looking for a free alternative to start with, with an option to pay later for teams.

• Developers and administrators who value transparency, offline sync, and privacy.

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• Fully open source with a native GNOME experience compliant with HIG, not superficial clones.

• Always free to start for the individual user, with no limit on personal tasks.

• True multi-source: Todoist, Nextcloud, CalDAV, and a single local file, with two-way sync.

• Offline-first, then merging changes and resolving conflicts automatically.

• A unified Command Palette and fast natural task entry with keywords.

• A lightweight Plugins interface that lets the community extend without bloating the core.

• Privacy by default: no forced analytics, and data stays with the user or on their server.

• Platform-neutral design (Wayland/GTK) so it runs smoothly on low-end devices.

3) Scope of the First Version (8 tasks maximum)

1. Create/edit/delete tasks, projects, and tags with subtask support.

2. CalDAV and Nextcloud integration: authentication, manual and automatic sync.

3. Todoist integration via its API (OAuth).

4. Local SQLite storage with a change queue and offline work.

5. A sync engine to detect conflicts and resolve them with a clear policy.

6. Due dates, GNOME system reminders, and recurring tasks.

7. Keyboard shortcuts and fast natural-language input.

8. Multi-language support and a light/dark mode interface.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free for life for the individual user: all sources, no task limits.

• A symbolic monthly/yearly Pro subscription plan: multi-device sync, version history, themes, and advanced reminders.

• Team plan: project sharing, permissions, and an activity dashboard.

• Community funding: sponsorships and donations, and institutional support (Support contract) for companies.

• No selling of user data, and no built-in ads.

5) Global Rollout Plan with Free Channels

• Publishing on Flathub and distribution package managers (Debian, Fedora, Arch).

• An open repository on hosting platforms with an attractive README and screenshots.

• Free technical content: explainers on YouTube, and blogs on DEV and Hacker News.

• Presence in GNOME communities and Reddit (r/gnome, r/selfhosted) and Nextcloud forums.

• A Product Hunt and AlternativeTo campaign, and transparent comparisons with alternatives.

• An Early Contributors program to attract translators and testers.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal: respecting Todoist API terms and third-party licenses; building integrations from scratch and not copying protected code or assets.

• Legal: a completely new trademark and avoiding name and logo similarity with any existing product.

• Technical: the complexity of resolving conflicts between multiple sources; mitigation through clear policies and automated tests.

• Technical: fluctuations in third-party APIs; mitigation through an Adapter Layer.

• Technical: maintenance load for an open project; mitigation through a plugin system and contributor documentation.

7) Proposed Name and Tagline

Proposed name: Darb (دَرْب)

Tagline: Your tasks on your path, on all your devices, with no limits.
