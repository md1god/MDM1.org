Sellara — Complete Project Plan

Your smart resale management assistant: better listings, safe negotiation, and synced inventory from the first item.

Digital project plan: idea, audience, product, revenue, launch, and risks. Not launched yet.

1) Audience

• Individual/professional sellers on Vinted, Shopify, eBay, Depop, and Etsy, especially with 50–1000 items.

• Small stores selling clothing, accessories, shoes, used goods.

• Small teams (2–10) needing inventory sync and delegated tasks without losing price control.

• New sellers starting free, upgrading with sales.

• Core problem: time lost on listings, replies, repricing, and cross-platform inventory, plus overselling or sales below the profit floor.

2) Differentiators

• Profit-based decisions: fees, shipping, and discounts before pricing, not just market price.

• Safe-limit negotiation: seller-set minimum, custom tone, "suggest then approve" before sending.

• Security: no passwords; OAuth where available, or a limited-permission browser extension.

• Multi-platform from day one: unified inventory, oversell protection.

• Free start: no sales commission, hidden fees, or profit cut.

• Explainable AI: every suggestion explains why and is reversible.

• Local support, multiple markets: Arabic, English, European interfaces, aware of fee differences.

3) MVP (max 8 tasks)

1. Setup and connection: create account, connect platforms, choose manual, pre-approval, or limited auto mode.

2. Listing from photos: generate title, description, attributes, category, and price, with quick editing.

3. Pricing floor: calculate net profit after fees and shipping; never go below the minimum.

4. Negotiation inbox: reply to buyers, suggest discounts, stop at the minimum.

5. Relisting and markdowns: relist old items; cut prices by time and percentage rules.

6. Favorites campaigns: personalized messages to favoriters, respecting platform limits, no repetition.

7. Inventory sync, oversell prevention: update quantities across platforms, hold on sale, instant alerts.

8. Shipping labels, profit dashboard: retrieve shipping/tracking data; daily sales, net profit, top products.

4) Pricing

• Free: one platform, 25 active listings, AI listings, manual message approval, fee calculator, basic analytics. Free forever.

• Pro — $19/mo or $190/yr: 3 platforms, 500 listings, auto-negotiation to the minimum, scheduled relisting/markdowns, favorites campaigns, shipping labels, full dashboard.

• Business — $49/mo: 10 stores or platforms, team seats, API and Webhooks, custom protection, priority support.

• Add-ons: extra AI credits, store slots, exportable reports.

• Revenue: subscriptions, annual plans, usage add-ons, affiliate commissions from shipping/packaging tools. No sales percentage.

5) Go-to-market, free channels

• Free tools: per-platform fee calculator, listing quality score, "lowest profitable price" calculator.

• Multilingual SEO: platform/category guides, comparison pages, ready templates in Arabic, English, Spanish, French, German.

• Short video: practical tips on TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Reels, with before/after demos.

• Communities: real use cases on Reddit, Facebook groups, and Discord, no spam or mass messages.

• User growth: referral link giving one month of Pro to both, public ideas board, migration help.

• Integration guides: list the product in app stores and e-commerce tool directories.

• Small partnerships: free Pro accounts for resale creators/coaches for honest reviews.

6) Legal, technical risks

Legal:

• Platform terms: automation may be restricted. Fix: official API, rate limits, human approval, no scraping.

• Privacy: buyer/account data under GDPR and CCPA. Fix: minimal collection, encrypted tokens, processing agreements, deletion on request.

• Consumer protection: AI descriptions may mislead. Fix: review before publishing, disclose AI use, seller liable for accuracy.

• Taxes: laws differ by country. Fix: clear financial reports, no tax advice.

Technical:

• API volatility: sudden changes or bans. Fix: abstraction layer, monitoring, fallback assistance.

• Overselling: update conflicts between platforms. Fix: temporary locks, Webhooks, periodic sync.

• AI hallucination: inaccurate text/negotiation. Fix: strict templates, price limits, evaluation tests, pre-send review.

• Token security: leaked permissions. Fix: least-privilege OAuth, encryption, two-factor authentication.

• AI cost: rising usage. Fix: smaller models for simple tasks, caching, free-plan limits.

7) Name and tagline

Proposed name: Sellara

Tagline: Your smart resale management assistant: better listings, safe negotiation, synced inventory from item one.
