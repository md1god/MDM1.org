Discover the winning product before the Latin American market becomes saturated.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, monetization model, growth plan, and risks. The project is under development and has not yet been launched.

1) Target Audience

• E-commerce and dropshipping sellers in Latin America, especially beginners and mid-level sellers in Mexico, Brazil, Colombia, Chile, Argentina, and Peru.

• Owners of Shopify, TiendaNube, and WooCommerce stores looking to test products quickly.

• Media buyers on TikTok, Meta, and Google who need creative ideas and locally relevant marketing angles.

• Small agencies and consultants managing multiple stores who need shareable reports.

• E-commerce education content creators.

Problem: The available data is scattered, competitor tools are global, expensive, and in English. They often showcase products that are not suitable for Latin American markets, as they fail to take into account local shipping costs, cash on delivery, return rates, taxes, and local consumer behavior.

2) Points of Differentiation (Why choose our product)

• Latin America-first: Product and ad signals from Latin American markets in Spanish and Portuguese, not just copied from U.S. market data.

• A single, easy-to-understand opportunity score: Combines demand velocity, competition density, net margin after local costs, and ad angle saturation.

• Realistic profit calculator: Includes shipping, cash on delivery, return rates, platform fees, and local taxes.

• Creative analysis, not copying: Extracts the hook, angle, formula, and ad fatigue indicator, while respecting asset ownership and not republishing ads as-is.

• Data transparency: Each signal includes its source, update date, and confidence score instead of vague numbers.

• Lightweight experience: A mobile-first interface that works as a PWA on slow connections, with a new visual identity inspired by a lighthouse: deep navy, lime green, and clear geometric fonts.

• A truly free start: Useful value without a credit card, building trust before converting to paid plans.

3) Scope of the First Version (Maximum of 8 tasks)

1. Quick registration and customization step: Country, niche, platform, and monthly budget.

2. Product summary with filters by country, niche, price, and opportunity score.

3. Opportunity Score engine v1: Demand, competition, margin, and time trend.

4. Searchable ad creative library by hook, formula, and angle, with assets uploaded under license or from allowed public sources.

5. Margin and full cost calculator in the local currency.

6. Watchlist with a daily summary via email or WhatsApp.

7. Saved searches and alerts when a new product or creative appears.

8. Source management dashboard: Data ingestion, deduplication, quality control, and source health.

4) Pricing and Monetization Model

• Free forever: 30 products per week, one watchlist, basic calculator, and weekly newsletter.

• Growth — $19/month: Unlimited summaries, instant alerts, full creative library, and CSV export.

• Agency — $49/month: 5 seats, white-label reports, API access, and priority support.

• 20% annual discount and adaptive pricing based on each country's purchasing power.

• Local payments: Mercado Pago, Pix, SPEI, PSE, and international cards.

• Additional revenue streams: Transparent referral commissions for tools and suppliers, clearly labeled sponsored placements, and paid market reports. No personal data is sold.

5) Free Channel Growth Plan

• Programmatic SEO in Spanish and Portuguese: Pages such as "profitable products in [niche] in [country]".

• Original short-form content on TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts: Analyzing successful creatives without republishing them.

• Weekly email newsletter "Latin America Radar" featuring top signals for free.

• Communities: WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, Reddit, and specialized Facebook groups.

• Launch on Product Hunt and free listings on Shopify and TiendaNube app stores.

• Partnerships with micro-influencers through a revenue-sharing model or free access.

• Referral program: One free month of Pro in exchange for three active referrals.

• Open quarterly benchmark reports to attract backlinks and media coverage.

• Free workshops with business accelerators and universities, then expansion to other emerging markets through a reusable localization and translation engine.
