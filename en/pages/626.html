Kashif — Full Project Plan

Discover what the market wants before someone else builds it.

A complete execution plan for a new digital product: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target audience

• Solo founders and those seeking a first verifiable idea.

• Small product teams that want to discover customer needs before building features.

• Innovation consultants and business accelerators that need rapid market evidence.

• Content creators in entrepreneurship looking for data-driven ideas.

• Arabic-speaking teams that lack tools for analyzing local conversations.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Signal, not just search: it turns scattered conversations into a “Need Card” containing the quote, recurrence volume, pain intensity, and current competitors.

• Multi-source coverage: Reddit, niche forums, store reviews, Hacker News, and public Discord/Telegram channels, not a single source.

• Supports Arabic and English in analysis and clustering, a rare feature in this category.

• Clarity instead of noise: automatic clustering of similar needs, deduplication, and removal of disguised ads.

• Actionable decision: every need is linked to a build-effort estimate and a pricing opportunity, not just an idea list.

• Free start without a card and a first ready report in minutes.

3) Scope of the first version (max 8 tasks)

1. Enter a keyword or problem description, with automatic synonyms.

2. Collect posts from 4 public sources through their official APIs first.

3. Cluster similar needs and remove duplication and ads.

4. Calculate a pain score from recurrence, phrasing intensity, and temporal recurrence of complaints.

5. Generate a Need Card: summary, 3 quotes, evidence links, existing solutions.

6. Watchlist to track keywords weekly.

7. Weekly email digest + CSV/PDF export.

8. Public demo page with a ready report without registration.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: two searches per day, 50 results per week, one board, limited export.

• Pro — $19/month: practically unlimited research, multiple boards, weekly summaries, full export.

• Team — $49/month: 5 seats, board sharing, comments, permissions.

• Deep Report pay-per-use at $9–29 depending on size.

• Later: API and annual subscription with a 20% discount, while the free tier remains permanent to drive growth.

5) Global launch plan with free channels

• Long-tail SEO: public pages titled “Do people need an app for…?” built on anonymized data.

• Free weekly index: “Top 10 unmet needs this week” shared publicly and attracting links.

• Launch on Product Hunt, Hacker News (Show HN), and founder communities such as Indie Hackers.

• Build in Public on X and LinkedIn with real results, not promises.

• Short comparison content: “How to validate an idea in 15 minutes” via video and blog.

• Referral loop: one free Pro month for each successful referral, and invite accelerator and incubator teams.

• Arabic-first version to capitalize on the market gap, then English and Spanish.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Comply with source API terms, robots.txt, and rate limits, and use official APIs before any alternative.

• Respect content rights: short quotes with a source link, no full republishing.

• Privacy: anonymize usernames, do not store personal data, comply with GDPR, and delete data on request.

• Avoid absolute marketing claims (“guaranteed idea”) to reduce misleading liability.

Technical:

• LLM costs and API limits: caching, batch processing, and a smaller model for simple tasks.

• Clustering quality: noise, duplication, and difficulty detecting sarcasm; continuous human evaluation is required.

• Summary hallucination: every card is tied to clickable textual evidence.

• Fake or organized data (Astroturfing): detect repeated patterns and newly created accounts.

• Data freshness and language coverage: schedule re-collection and gradual expansion.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Kashif (كاشف)

Tagline: Discover what the market wants before someone else builds it.
