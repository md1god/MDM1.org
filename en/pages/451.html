WASL — Complete Project Plan

Input one long-form piece, get ready-to-publish Arabic posts for all your platforms — free to start.

Complete execution plan for a new digital product: idea, audience, product, revenue model, growth plan, risks. The project is under construction and not yet launched.

1) Target Audience

• Arab content creators (YouTube, podcast, newsletter) with 10K+ subscribers — lack a writing team.

• Digital marketing agencies and social media managers handling multiple brands and suffering from repetitive work.

• B2B marketing teams in Saudi Arabia, UAE, Egypt, and the Gulf — need weekly content from internal meetings/webinars.

• Arab SaaS product makers (fintech, e-commerce, edtech) publishing bilingual content.

Primary persona: Founder/marketing lead who personally produces content, currently paying for "8 platforms from one clip."

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• Arabic-first, not Arabized: Dialect detection (MSA/Egyptian/Levantine/Gulf), Eastern/Arabic numerals, diacritics, RTL, platform-matched hashtags — instead of translated English templates.

• Platform-constrained templates: Character limits, structure (hook → point → CTA), emoji policy, hashtag ordering — output tested against actual LinkedIn/X/Instagram limits, not just "long text."

• Source traceability: Every generated sentence linked to the source clip/timestamp — users instantly spot AI hallucinations.

• Authenticity guard: Duplicate detection against source and previously published assets + "AI similarity score" to avoid platform "AI slop" risk.

• Savable prompt chains (Brand Voice): Writer tone samples saved per brand and reused, with tone-match scoring.

• Fast delivery: Draft, review, copy/download full publish pack (Markdown/CSV/PNG cards) — no login to other tools.

• Full freedom: Forever free plan, no credit card, privacy-first processing, instant file deletion.

3) v1 Scope (Max 8 Tasks)

1. Import: Link (YouTube/podcast/blog) or upload audio/video/text + automatic transcription (ASR) with speaker diarization.

2. Segmentation: Chapters and time segments + suggested titles + extraction of "quotable quotes."

3. Brand Voice Profile: Upload 3–5 sample texts → save as Brand Voice + editable Prompt Chains.

4. Asset Generation (7 types): LinkedIn post, X thread, Instagram caption, TikTok/Reels script, newsletter, blog draft, quote cards.

5. Platform Constraints: Character limits, hashtags, CTA, ordering, policy compliance, preview before export.

6. Formatting: Quote cards + audiogram clips with PNG/MP4 export, Arabic fonts and correct RTL.

7. Review Workspace: Edit, version copy, approve/reject, one-click copy, download pack (Markdown/CSV).

8. Free Account + Metrics: Conversion credits, usage log, invite/referral friends, asset history export.

Out of scope for v1: Auto-publishing to platforms, voice cloning, generated images, performance analytics.

4) Pricing & Revenue Model

| Plan | Price | Includes |

|------|-------|----------|

| Free | $0 forever | 3 conversions/month, up to 60 min audio, 2 brand voices, small watermark cards, text export |

| Creator | $19/mo | Unlimited conversions (10 hr audio cap), unlimited brand voices, watermark-free cards, Audiogram |

| Pro/Agency | $49/mo | 5 workspaces/clients, advanced prompt chains, CSV export, priority processing, API |

| Enterprise | Custom | On-prem/self-hosted, SLA, training |

• Revenue model: Subscription + extra credit purchases + (later) paid template marketplace + API fees.

• Why freemium works: Real cost = audio minutes + tokens; 3 conversions/month suffice for discovery, and minimum viable subscription (1 conversion ~$1.2) makes upgrade self-serve at scale.

5) Global Zero-Cost Growth Plan

• Product-led: Public share link per asset pack + "invite friend = extra conversion" reward + free counter no card required.

• SEO: Arabic "how to turn podcast into LinkedIn posts" articles, pages per free template (10 LinkedIn templates) as long-tail keyword landing pages.

• Short video: One daily tutorial on TikTok/YouTube Shorts "one content → 7 outputs" (free in-product tool = publishable content).

• Communities: WhatsApp/Telegram/Dubai/Egypt content worker groups, Discord, posting inside Arab marketing groups.
