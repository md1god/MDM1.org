Mawjud — Complete Project Plan

Mawjud answers your calls 24/7, qualifies leads, and books appointments in your calendar automatically.

Complete execution plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and not yet launched.

Product Plan: Smart Voice Assistant for Receiving Small Business Calls

1) Target Audience

Primary audience (Arab market domestic):

• Dental clinics, aesthetic medical clinics, and small specialized centers

• Home appliance maintenance workshops, HVAC, and plumbing (on-site services)

• Hair salons, beauty, and training centers

• Real estate agencies, CV services, law firms, and consultancies

• Restaurants and table-booking venues, driving schools, small hotels and hostels

• Spare parts warehouses and car maintenance centers

Secondary audience: Gulf markets (KSA/UAE), Jordan, and Egypt via digital retail channels.

Buyer persona: Business owner (no technical team), decides in one day, tries it themselves from their phone. Time to first test call: under 10 minutes.

Starting size: SMBs with 1–20 employees, no full-time receptionist, losing customers due to unanswered calls.

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

| Axis | Our Approach |

|------|--------------|

| Language | Arabic-first: understands dialects (Egyptian, Levantine, Gulf) with automatic Arabic/English switching within a single call. |

| Booking | Deterministic booking engine (State Machine), not generative: no invented times or conflicts; calendar readability guaranteed, and calendar integration is the single source of truth. |

| Audio Stack | Audio stack built from scratch on WebRTC/SIP with in-house STT/TTS supporting <1.2s latency, recording and quality editing. Cost shown transparently per minute. |

| Integrations | Google Calendar / Microsoft 365 / Generic CalDAV / ICS, CSV files, webhooks for Zapier/Slack/HubSpot — no forced heavy CRM installation. |

| Operations | Number porting or QR on shop counter; no PBX migration, no developer, no Asterisk server to manage. |

| Compliance | Consent recording at start, audit log, retention period setting, data export/delete, optionally hosted within region. |

| Transparency | "Talk to a trial agent" badge toggleable on/off, and Calls QA quality dashboard with outcome scoring. |

| Zero Commission | No commission on accepted transactions, no price lock-in — usage-based pricing instead of commission. |

3) V1 Scope (8 Tasks Maximum)

1. Setup Wizard: business name, services, prices, working hours, dialect, emergency numbers, unanswered-question handling.

2. Number Provisioning & In/Out: Local/toll-free/international number, call forwarding, with WhatsApp/SMS fallback if SIP fails.

3. Smart Inquiry Response: From prepared knowledge base (services, prices, hours, directions) with handoff to human on uncertainty.

4. Lead Qualification: Questions per definable criteria (service type, location, time, budget) + automatic tags and propensity scoring.

5. Actual Booking: Real-time availability view, create/edit/cancel, instant confirmation, rescheduling, pre-appointment reminders.

6. Sync: Google/Microsoft/CalDAV + CSV export + webhooks/Slack, unified log for every Lead/Call/Booking.

7. Human Handoff + Text Message: Live transfer to a staff member, or transfer to voicemail transcribed to text via WhatsApp/SMS.

8. Recordings & Analytics: Audio recording + full transcript, usage dashboard (calls, bookings, first response time, conversion rate), and report downloads.

Not in V1 (Deferred): outbound campaigns, in-call billing and payments, multi-branch VeoRM, iOS/Android apps, HIPAA/PHI, sentiment analysis.

4) Pricing & Revenue Model

Free-to-start model: Free trial with no credit card.

| Plan | Price | Includes |

|------|-------|----------|

| Free (Start) | 0 SAR/USD | 100 min/month, 1 number, 1 language, booking + FAQ + transfer, basic dashboard, 7-day recording |

| Pro | $39/month | 600 min, multiple numbers, 2 dialects, CRM-lite, CSVs/Webhooks, advanced reports, 90-day recording |

| Business | $99/month | 1500 min, multiple branches/lines, custom branding, follow-up flows, team permissions |

| Pay-as-you-go | $0.12/min | No commitment, after bundle exhaustion |

| Setup Services | $149 one-time | Knowledge config, voice tone, team training, preview |

| Add-ons | Separate pricing | Advanced API/Webhooks, extra numbers, third language, extended storage |

Revenue Engine: Value = recovering missed calls (average SMB loses 1–3 customers/day). No commission → higher trust. Margin: true cost/min ≈ $0.04–0.08 vs selling price $0.12 + $39 subscript
