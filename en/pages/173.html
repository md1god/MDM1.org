SamaaKit — Complete Project Plan

An integrated astronomy platform that runs from your browser to compute, observe, and analyze with confidence and precision.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the distribution plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

An original product plan: a cloud-based astronomical computation platform

1) Target audience

• Physics and astronomy students at universities who need accurate computations without setting up a complex environment.

• Graduate researchers and small observatories who process observation data and observed data files.

• School science teachers and digital educational content creators.

• Mobile and web app developers who need astronomical computations (times, coordinates, object trajectories) through a simple API.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Works instantly from the browser: no installation and no package management, with an installable library version for those who want it.

• Cloud-first design: process huge observed files on the server instead of downloading them locally.

• Native Arabic/English interface with simplified explanations of astronomical concepts, not just bare functions.

• Unit and coordinate consistency from the start within a single data model that prevents common conversion errors.

• Free for basic use with no restrictions on scientific data, monetizing through advanced tiers.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Converter for astronomical physical units and measurements (length, time, energy, mass).

2. Celestial and terrestrial coordinate transformations.

3. Calculation of astronomical times (sunrise/sunset, twilight, moon phase).

4. Reader/writer for observational data arrays in structured tabular format.

5. Import and display a simple observation file with initial correction.

6. Distance and velocity calculator (preliminary redshift, luminosity distance).

7. REST API for core operations.

8. Export and share results via a permanent link + simple operation log.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free (Free-to-start): individual use, generous computing limits, open community.

• Researcher (Pro): low monthly subscription for processing larger files, batch jobs, and saving projects.

• Team/Observatory (Team): seats + permissions and institutional permissions + support.

• API: a free tier then usage-based pricing.

• Additional income: training and education workshops, and customization for educational institutions.

5) Global distribution plan through free channels

• Open-source repository on hosting platforms + early releases to attract contributors.

• Publish articles and practical comparisons on platforms such as Reddit, Hacker News, and astronomy communities.

• Short tutorials on YouTube and video platforms, and interactive Jupyter examples.

• Collaborate with universities and astronomy clubs and offer free workshops.

• Indexable educational content to improve search engine visibility.

• Participate in hackathons and open scientific conferences.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: respecting the licenses of scientific data and their sources; not copying any code or interfaces from existing products; clarifying our project's license; user data privacy and regulatory compliance.

• Technical: numerical precision and scientific validation of results; scaling when processing huge files; availability of reliable computational libraries; avoiding dependence on a single provider; API security and usage limits.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: SamaaKit

Marketing tagline: An integrated astronomy platform that runs from your browser to compute, observe, and analyze with confidence and precision.
