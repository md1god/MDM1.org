Qiyas — Complete Project Plan

Convert your bank statement into a clean table in seconds, without manual rewriting.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under development and not yet launched.

1) Target Audience

• Accountants: Need to quickly convert bank data to prepare monthly reports.

• Auditors: Look for clean, error-free files to speed up reviews.

• Small business owners: Non-accounting specialists who want to avoid manual rewriting.

• Freelancers: Such as web designers or service providers who receive multiple transfers and need to document them.

2) Points of Differentiation (Why Choose Our Product)

• Smart Arabic language processing: Automatic support for local bank names, date formats, and Arabic currency patterns.

• Template-based conversion: Ability to choose a ready-made template for common accounting software while maintaining column accuracy.

• On-device processing when possible: Direct conversion on the user's device when possible, with minimal data sent to preserve privacy.

• Automatic cleaning: Remove fees, duplicate deductions, and merge unclear lines without manual effort.

• Very simple interface: Designed for beginners with no complex terminology.

3) Scope of the First Version (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. File upload: Support drag and drop for a single PDF file only.

2. Format detection: Automatically recognize the statement structure without manual setup.

3. Data extraction: Extract date, description, debit, credit, balance.

4. Data cleaning: Correct common errors and unify symbols.

5. Quick preview: Display the first 10 lines to verify accuracy before export.

6. Export: Support CSV format with an option for Excel (.XLSX).

7. Save template: Save column settings for use with similar data.

8. Privacy mode: Display a clear notice that the file remains under the user's control.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

· Plan · Price · Features ·

· Free · $0 / month · 5 free conversions per day, CSV export, all basic cleaning features. ·

· Basic · $9 / month · Unlimited conversions, XLSX export, save templates, priority processing. ·

· Professional · $19 / month · All Basic features, custom templates, higher capacity for batch processing in the future, email support. ·

5) Global Distribution Plan Through Free Channels

• Tool directories: Submit to free tool directories related to accounting and manual data entry.

• Communities: Participate in accountant and small business owner communities on public forums to solve problems, not advertise directly.

• Educational blogs: Write short articles on "How to convert a bank statement to an Excel table" with a natural link to the product.

• Short videos: Publish quick explanatory clips with no music or paid ads on short video platforms.

• Multilingual content: Focus first on English, Arabic, and Spanish due to real global demand.

• Free SEO listings: Target keywords like "convert PDF bank statement to Excel" using simple, fast pages.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

· Type · Risk · Proposed Mitigation ·

· Legal · Handling sensitive financial data and privacy requirements in some countries. · Do not store original statement content, use local processing, and write a clear, easy-to-read privacy policy. ·

· Technical · Different PDF structures between banks, difficulty extracting complex tables, errors in currency recognition. · Start by supporting a limited number of the most common patterns, add an easy "manual correction" mode, and test on real samples without sensitive data. ·

· Operational · User misinterpretation or loss of accuracy in the final amount. · Add mandatory preview before export, and display totals for debit and credit to compare with overall totals if present. ·

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Slogan

Proposed name: Qiyas

Marketing slogan: Convert your statement to a clean table in seconds, without manual rewriting.
