Mizan — Complete Project Plan

Your data stays where it is, your decisions become clearer.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, expansion plan, and risks. The project is under development and has not been launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Individual investors: People who track their assets personally and want to see their portfolio performance without complexity.

• Privacy-conscious users: Users who do not want to link their accounts to central servers or share their data with third parties.

• Owners of diversified portfolios: Those who own assets in multiple places and want a unified, fast view of their performance.

2) Competitive Advantages (Why Choose Our Product)

• Privacy first: The app runs entirely on the user's device, with no forced account creation.

• Extreme simplicity: Focuses only on core numbers, instead of cluttering the screen with complex reports.

• Full control: All data is stored locally, with the ability to export or delete it at any time.

• Speed and lightness: A lightweight design that doesn't rely on constant connectivity to run efficiently.

3) Scope of the First Version (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. Build the basic structure: Set up the app interface to run on the user's device.

2. Asset management: Ability to add and delete assets manually with ease.

3. Value tracking: Automatically calculate the total portfolio value.

4. Price updates: Fetch prices from public and open sources when needed.

5. Encrypted local storage: Save data in a local file with basic protection.

6. Data export: Ability to export data in JSON or CSV format.

7. Daily performance view: Show the change in value for a single day only.

8. Offline mode: Ensure all main features work fully without a constant internet connection.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free to start: All features of the first version are completely free, with no restrictions.

• Optional model: In the future, optional paid report templates may be added, without making the app a mandatory subscription.

• Ad-free: No ads will ever be displayed to ensure a distraction-free experience.

5) Global Expansion Plan Through Free Channels

• Privacy awareness: Write short, open articles about the importance of tracking portfolios while keeping data local.

• Free app lists: List the app in open directories that focus on free and tracker-free tools.

• Community engagement: Participate in communities interested in privacy and share value without direct promotion.

• User recommendations: Rely on the simple user experience as the strongest growth tool, with no paid advertising.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal: It must be explicitly clarified that the app is a personal tracking tool only, not a tax or legal advisory tool.

• Technical: Reliance on public price sources may stop at any time, so manual price entry must always be supported as a permanent option.

• Additional technical: Any flaw in the local encryption mechanism could pose a risk, so it must be simplified and thoroughly tested before release.

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Slogan

Proposed name: Mizan

Marketing slogan: Your data stays where it is, your decisions become clearer.
