PanelLingua — Full Project Plan

Translate your pages professionally without leaving your browser.

Full implementation plan for a new digital product: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the launch plan and the risks. The product is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

- Readers of manga, manhwa and comics who do not speak the language of the original work.

- Small independent Scanlation teams.

- Independent publishers and small publishing houses that want fast translation to cut human translation costs.

- Creators who share translated pages on social networks.

2) Differentiators (why our product)

- Fully optional local (Offline / On-device) processing to protect reader privacy, with no mandatory cloud upload.

- Smart layout handling that preserves right-to-left (RTL) speech flow, avoids covering key artwork, and redraws bubbles instead of pasting text over them.

- A human review editor with a side-by-side comparison view (original text vs translation) to raise quality before export.

- Support for multiple formats (Images, PDF, EPUB, CBZ/CBR) from the same file without reorganization.

- A free-to-start model from day one, plus an open API for integration with reading tools.

- A plugin architecture for translation engines and models, so the product is never tied to a single provider.

3) First Release Scope (8 tasks max)

1. Import images plus PDF, CBZ/CBR and EPUB files.

2. Automatic detection of speech bubbles and text regions (OCR).

3. Translation via a free default engine, with the ability to switch engines.

4. Bubble redraw or cleanup, then write the translated text in a suitable font.

5. Support for both LTR and RTL, including vertical typesetting.

6. A manual review editor to correct texts and their positions.

7. Export to the original format or to a chosen format.

8. A desktop app or browser extension with offline operation as a priority.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

- Fully free for local use up to a generous monthly limit (pages/files) - a free start, always.

- Monthly Pro plan: faster translation, higher quality models, batch processing, and no limits.

- Teams/Publisher plan: multiple seats, shared translation memory (Glossary), and version history.

- Extra revenue from a usage-based paid API for developers and platforms.

- No annoying ads inside the reader, to keep the reading experience clean.

5) Global Rollout Plan Using Free Channels

- Release an open source version on GitHub to attract contributors and build technical trust.

- Manga and comics communities on Reddit and Discord, plus translation forums.

- Short tutorial videos on YouTube and TikTok showing before/after translation.

- Work with Scanlation teams to ship free trial builds.

- Multilingual SEO articles targeting "automatic manga translation" and similar queries.

- Take part in Product Hunt and Hacker News, and ship an early beta.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

- Legal: copyright on protected works; the answer is a clear disclaimer, allowing only licensed, personal or public domain works, and hosting no protected content on our servers.

- Legal: user data privacy; the answer is on-device processing by default plus a clear data policy.

- Technical: OCR accuracy on handwriting and overlapping bubbles; the answer is specialized models plus human review.

- Technical: the cost of cloud translation models; the answer is supporting local models and free tier limits.

- Technical: format diversity and layout drift; the answer is a format abstraction layer plus automated tests.

- Technical: translation quality in low-resource languages; the answer is swappable engines and community contributions.

7) Proposed Name and Tagline

Proposed name: PanelLingua

Tagline: Translate your pages professionally without leaving your browser.
