Daftr — Complete Project Plan

Your tasks from the keyboard only — and at terminal speed.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the launch plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: Daftr

1) Target audience

• Software developers and operations engineers who spend most of the day inside the terminal.

• Linux / macOS / WSL users accustomed to fast text tools (vim, fzf, ripgrep).

• Students, writers, and freelancers who want quick capture of tasks without leaving the keyboard.

• Small teams (2–5 people) that need shared lists without complicated systems.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Raw speed: boots in under 100 milliseconds, and works offline.

• Full keyboard control: no mouse, a shortcut for every action, with optional Vim mode.

• End-to-end encrypted (E2EE) sync between devices, while the local copy remains the source of truth.

• A full Arabic interface with RTL support (a rare feature in this category).

• Instant search and smart filters by tags, projects, priorities, and dates.

• Extensions: a CLI interface and customizable community themes.

3) Scope of the first release (8 tasks maximum)

1. Read/write a local task database in a single text file using an open, product-specific format.

2. TUI interface: view, add, edit, delete, and toggle completion status.

3. Keyboard shortcuts + instant search.

4. Sorting and filtering: project, tag, priority, due date.

5. Text import and export (including the famous todo.txt format) for a smooth transition.

6. Sync between two devices via a free account (a lightweight cloud server).

7. RTL support and interface translation for Arabic and English.

8. Publish static binaries for Linux / macOS / Windows via Homebrew and AUR.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free forever: sync for up to two devices and all core features.

• Pro: $4/month — unlimited devices, history snapshots, and shared lists with one person.

• Team: $8/user/month — shared workspaces and permissions.

• Open-source core sponsorship plus donations and sponsorships.

5) Global launch plan using free channels

• Public launch: Show HN, Reddit communities (r/commandline and r/todoapps), and Lobsters.

• Short GIF demos on X, Mastodon, Bluesky, and YouTube Shorts.

• SEO articles in Arabic and English about task management from the terminal, published on Medium and dev.to.

• Participation in Arab developer communities on Discord and Telegram, with Arabic content.

• Explicit comparison pages ("why we're building a faster alternative") without directly criticizing brand names.

• Translating the content for users in Germany, Japan, and Brazil through contributors.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: write all the source from scratch, and avoid copying shortcuts, design, or texts from any existing product.

• Legal: check library licenses (MIT/BSD) before integrating, and document them in a licenses file.

• Technical: data loss during sync → local encrypted backup and automated sync tests.

• Technical: terminal differences → a test matrix across tmux, Alacritty, WezTerm, and iTerm2.

• Business: slow adoption → measure the free-trial conversion rate early (Week 2).

7)

Proposed name: Daftr

Marketing tagline: Your tasks from the keyboard only — and at terminal speed.
