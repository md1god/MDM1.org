About the project

What is MDM1?

MDM1 is a digital bridge between ancient Egyptian wisdom and the blockchain world — a decentralized community whose symbolism carries the struggle between light and darkness, and which aims to offer a Web3 experience that respects Sharia guidelines.

What does the name MDM1 mean?

The name is inspired by ancient Egyptian civilization and its deep symbolism, combining a heritage identity with digital ambition in the world of Web3.

Sharia and compliance

Is MDM1 compatible with Islamic law?

The design follows the general principles of Islamic finance: no interest (riba), no excessive uncertainty (gharar) and no gambling.

The platform and the game

What is the "Pharaoh's Gate"?

An interactive knowledge game inside the platform. You choose one of the gods of ancient Egypt (Ra, Anubis, Isis, Horus), each with a different ability, and answer endless AI-generated questions on a global leaderboard.

How do I reach the platform?

Through the official link mdm1.org/hub.html — the platform includes the game, the gallery and all the tools for interacting with the project.

Community and support

How do I contact the team?

All the official channels are gathered on the community page — do not deal with any account or link that is not listed there.

How do I report a fake account?

Report it directly through the official Telegram channel. The team will never ask you for your private keys.
