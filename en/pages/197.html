Atheer — Complete Project Plan

A lightweight home screen that respects your privacy.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the distribution plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Users of mid-range and low-spec devices: those looking for a lightweight home screen that runs smoothly without heavy memory or battery consumption.

• Privacy-conscious people: individuals who do not want their data collected or their activities tracked.

• Users who prefer simplicity: those who want to customize a simple home screen without too many complications.

• Emerging global audience: especially in areas with limited connectivity, where app efficiency is of great importance.

2) Points of Distinction (Why We Choose Our Product)

• Lightweight and fast: a design built from scratch focused on lower resource consumption to ensure smooth performance across various devices.

• Privacy-respecting: contains no tracking or usage reporting, and relies on local operation without the need for external data.

• Works offline: all core functions are available even without internet.

• Original and modern design: a simplified and visually cohesive interface with usability in mind.

• Moderately customizable: provides only essential customization options, without confusing the user.

• Globally available: designed to support different languages from the first version.

3) Scope of the First Version (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. Project setup: building the app's basic structure using appropriate technologies, with a clean development environment.

2. Main interface design: creating an original design for the home screen with a flexible layout.

3. App drawer: developing a simple and organized list to display apps with quick search capability.

4. Basic customization system: adding the ability to change icons and basic layouts.

5. Control gestures: implementing a set of simple gestures to facilitate navigation.

6. Permissions and privacy management: ensuring that no unnecessary permissions are requested.

7. Performance optimization: testing memory and battery consumption and making initial optimizations.

8. Testing on multiple devices: verifying that the app works on a variety of devices and operating systems.

4) Pricing and Profit Model

• Free to start: all basic features are available for free without ads.

• Voluntary support model: the ability to make one-time donations to support the project's development.

• Optional add-ons: selling unique themes or icon packs for a symbolic amount, without affecting the free user experience.

• Sustainable focus: prioritizing non-advertising revenue to preserve respect for users' privacy.

5) Global Distribution Plan via Free Channels

• Open stores: publishing the app on open-source stores and free distribution to ensure broad reach.

• Community marketing: sharing content in relevant community forums in multiple languages.

• Educational content: producing short clips and educational resources explaining the app's benefits and usage steps.

• Community translation: engaging volunteers to translate the app into as many languages as possible.

• Word-of-mouth recommendations: encouraging users to share their experience naturally without paid campaigns.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal risks:

• Ensuring that no pre-designed elements that may be subject to intellectual property rights are used.

• Complying with the policies of the stores where it will be published from the early stages.

• Technical risks:

• Continuous changes in operating system APIs may affect the app's stability.

• Differences in device performance between manufacturers, requiring continuous testing.

• Maintaining compatibility with different versions of the system without increasing the app's size.

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Slogan

Proposed name: Atheer

Marketing slogan: A lightweight home screen that respects your privacy.
