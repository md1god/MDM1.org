Qafila — Complete Project Plan

Your journey, your memories, and your data — all in your hands and on your server.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the distribution plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

1) Target audience

• Independent travelers (Backpackers) and long-trip enthusiasts who want to archive their trips and maps without giving up their data.

• Families planning shared trips who need a private space to share plans and budget.

• Privacy-conscious users and developers capable of self-hosting (Self-hosting), as well as non-technical users through a ready-made cloud version.

• Travel content creators who want to easily generate pages/maps of their trips.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Data ownership first: one-click self-hosting (Docker Compose) with a managed cloud path for those who don't want to set it up.

• A genuinely free start: the core version is open source and completely free with no limits on the core features.

• Planning + documentation in one place: schedule the trip in real time, then automatically turn it into a documented record with an interactive map after it ends.

• Offline mode: record points and photos locally and sync when the network returns.

• Multilingual support and RTL as a priority, not an afterthought.

• Open import/export: a standard data format that is portable with no vendor lock-in (No Vendor Lock-in).

3) Scope of the first version (max 8 tasks)

1. User accounts and authentication, with a public/private profile.

2. Create a trip and set its dates and estimated budget.

3. A Timeline of points and activities with an interactive map (MapLibre + OpenStreetMap).

4. Upload photos and attachments and link them to points.

5. Track expenses against the budget with a simple summary.

6. Collaboration: invite trip companions with permission roles.

7. Publish/export the trip as a public page or data file (JSON/GPX).

8. Deployment bundle: Docker Compose + simple backup and restore.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free forever (open source): self-hosting with all core features, with no limit on the number of trips.

• Managed cloud — free plan: two active trips + limited photo storage.

• Pro plan (monthly subscription): larger storage, unlimited trips, group sync, custom domain.

• Family/Team plan: multiple seats and advanced roles.

• Additional sources: sponsorship/donations, paid support for self-hosting, and paid integrations with booking services.

• A fixed principle: core features always remain free and open to avoid community resentment.

5) Global distribution plan with free channels

• Publish the repository on GitHub with an explicit license and clear files (README, screenshots, live Demo).

• Launch on free product platforms (Product Hunt, Hacker News, Reddit: r/selfhosted, r/travel).

• A deployment guide on Awesome-Selfhosted and open tools lists.

• Long-term SEO content: "How to self-host your own trip tracker", and neutral comparisons.

• Partnerships with travel communities and bloggers in exchange for a free Pro version.

• A trial version (Live Demo) with no registration to lower the barrier to trying it.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Privacy and data protection (GDPR and similar): must clarify what is stored, with explicit consent and the right to full deletion.

• Rights to uploaded photos and content: clear terms of use and a policy for reporting infringement.

• Map data: respect the terms of open map providers and attribution.

Technical:

• The burden of self-hosting maintenance: solutions with secure-by-default deployment + automatic updates.

• Server security: encryption, periodic updates, dependency scanning (SBOM).

• Offline sync conflicts: a clear conflict resolution strategy.

• Scaling and storage cost in the free cloud version.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Qafila

Marketing tagline: Your journey, your memories, and your data — all in your hands and on your server.
