Lumha — Complete Project Plan

One glance reveals what your skin needs, and a clear plan that starts today.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• End user (free): skincare shoppers aged 18–45, especially those with sensitive or acne-prone skin, in MENA, Europe, and Latin America, with priority for an Arabic RTL interface.

• Paying customer: small and medium beauty ecommerce stores, DTC brands, digital pharmacies, and aesthetic dermatology clinics.

• Secondary segment: content creators and beauty consultants who want a free tool to track their followers' routines.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Privacy first: image analysis is performed on-device by default, and the image is not uploaded except with explicit consent — a decisive advantage under data laws.

• Catalog-independent engine: connects to any store via CSV or Shopify/WooCommerce feed without lock-in or rebuilding.

• Explainable assessment: every score comes with a confidence level and a clear reason, not a "black box" that sells products.

• Truly free to start: a permanent free tier for the user and the store, paying only as you grow.

• Built on dermatologically reviewed rules and recommendations based on ingredients, not trade names.

• Multilingual and RTL-first from the first release, unlike tools translated later.

3) First Release Scope (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Selfie capture with lighting/angle quality guidance and a consent screen.

2. Face landmark detection and skin region segmentation (face landmarks + segmentation).

3. Detection of 6 concerns: dryness, oiliness, redness, pigmentation, pores/acne, fine lines — with a confidence score.

4. Results dashboard explaining each concern and its severity in simple language.

5. Morning/evening routine builder (3–5 steps) that considers budget and skin type.

6. Product matching from the store catalog via CSV/Shopify feed.

7. Shareable/exportable routine card with product links.

8. Merchant dashboard: catalog upload, embeddable widget, and basic statistics.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• User: completely free, with no forced ads.

• Store — Free: 250 scans/month and 100 products, no setup fees.

• Growth: $39/month — 2,500 scans, brand customization, reports.

• Scale: $129/month — 15,000 scans + API + priority support.

• Enterprise: custom pricing for chains and clinics.

• Supplementary revenue: 3–5% referral commission on attributed sales, optional and not forced.

5) Global Go-to-Market Plan with Free Channels

• Programmatic SEO with pages for each skin concern × language (Arabic/English/Spanish), with hreflang.

• Shopify App Store and WooCommerce as free distribution channels for merchants.

• Short videos with content creators on commission instead of upfront cash payment.

• Reddit, Facebook, and TikTok communities through educational, non-advertising content.

• In-product referral model: the shareable routine card brings in a new user.

• Product Hunt + free GitHub SDK to attract developers and merchants.

• Collaboration with dermatologists to publish trusted content that boosts trust and visibility.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: facial data may be classified as biometric (such as BIPA, GDPR Article 9, and Saudi/UAE PDPL) — solution: on-device processing, explicit consent, immediate deletion, and blocking minors.

• Medical claims: avoid diagnosis, and present the product as an awareness/beauty tool with a clear disclaimer.

• Technical: variation in lighting and cameras across devices — solution: capture guidance + calibration + confidence range.

• Skin tone bias: risk of low accuracy for darker skin — solution: diverse data and fairness testing before launch.

• Security and cost: protect images and data, clear usage limits, and a fallback plan when on-device processing is not possible.

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Tagline

Proposed name: Lumha (لمحة)

Marketing tagline: One glance reveals what your skin needs, and a clear plan that starts today.
