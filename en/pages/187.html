GafferOS — Full Project Plan

Manage your sport, build your legacy, straight from your browser with no complexity.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the distribution plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

Product plan: an original sports management simulation game that runs in the browser

1) Target audience

• Sports fans who want a deep management game without a powerful device, a download, or an account registration.

• Players on mid- and low-range phones in fast-growing markets (the Middle East, Latin America, South and Southeast Asia).

• Content creators and modders who want to freely modify leagues, teams, and data.

• The single player who wants short sessions without a competitive commitment or constant internet.

2) Differentiators (why our product is chosen)

• Runs entirely in the browser (client-side) with local storage in IndexedDB and PWA support for offline operation.

• A deterministic and fast simulation engine; a full season can be completed in seconds with reproducible results.

• Multiple sports/leagues via data editable from simple JSON files, with a community "mod market".

• Arabic as a first language and a multilingual interface from day one (a rare differentiator in this genre).

• No account required to play, no mandatory subscription, and no gacha or pay-to-win systems.

• Lightweight design and clean symbolic graphics that run smoothly on weak phones.

3) Scope of the first version (max 8 tasks)

1. A deterministic match simulation engine based on player ratings and tactics.

2. Team management: lineup, transfers, contracts, training and development.

3. Season and league: fixture schedule, standings, knockout rounds.

4. Save games locally (IndexedDB) + export/import a save file.

5. PWA registration and offline support + a full Arabic localization plus a second additional language.

6. An open data editor that allows loading custom leagues and teams in JSON format.

7. A dashboard, statistics, and reports after each match/season.

8. Short onboarding + an optional achievements system.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free to start: all the core gameplay (simulation, seasons, saving, modding) is free and without limits.

• One-time supporter pack: cosmetics, frames, and visual themes only (no effect on balance).

• Optional cloud subscription: save sync across devices and advanced stats, without locking any core feature.

• Strict rule: no loot boxes, no selling match results, and no pay-to-win.

5) Global distribution plan via free channels

• Publish the core as open source on GitHub to attract modders and contributors.

• Presence in specialized forum, Discord, and Reddit communities with an explicit invitation to mod.

• Collaboration with small content creators on YouTube/TikTok/Twitch via free trial copies.

• Search engine optimization (SEO) with shareable league and results pages, and publishing on platforms like itch.io and Product Hunt.

• A community Arabization and localization program that opens new markets without a large advertising cost.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: avoid real league names, trademarks, and players' likenesses; use original fictional data and rely on community mods, while making clear that the product is not officially licensed.

• Legal: comply with privacy policies and GDPR for any analytics, and do not collect personal data without consent.

• Technical: limits of local storage capacity and corruption of save files (solution: backups + manual export).

• Technical: ensure deterministic simulation consistency across devices and browsers, and tune AI balance to avoid unfair results.

• Technical: UI performance on weak devices (solution: chunked rendering and deferred computations).

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: GafferOS

Marketing tagline: Manage your sport, build your legacy, straight from your browser with no complexity.
