Suhail — Complete Project Plan

Your personal sleep guide from your phone, without extra devices.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, launch plan, risks. Under construction; not launched.

Product plan: personal sleep coach working from phone, no extra devices

1) Target audience

• Adults 20–45 with irregular sleep due to office work or study, hundreds of millions worldwide; target 1% seeking a simple solution without a smartwatch.

• Shift workers (about 15–20% of workforce in industrial economies) whose sleep conflicts with circadian rhythm.

• New parents waking at night who need to reduce sleep debt, not generic advice.

• University students 18–24 who sleep late and wake early; high phone use and suited to free spread.

• Frequent travelers with jet lag needing a 3–5 day adjustment plan.

• Initial market: Arab region, Europe, North America; full Arabic support and RTL interface from day one.

2) Differentiators

• Phone-only: microphone, motion processor, screen use; no smartwatch or ring needed, so entry barrier is zero.

• One daily decision: "Tonight's sleep window: 23:10–06:40" instead of complex dashboards.

• Sleep debt and circadian algorithm: chronotype questionnaire, then 14-day self-calibration; weekly adjusted recommendations.

• Privacy first: on-device audio analysis, no raw recordings uploaded; one-tap permanent deletion.

• Authentic Arabic experience: texts, voices, RTL, optional local common sleep/wake times.

• Practical weekly coach: three actionable tips (e.g. bedtime 20 minutes earlier, caffeine after 14:00, morning light exposure).

3) First-version scope (max 8 tasks)

1. Optional login, chronotype questionnaire, sleep history for initial sleep profile.

2. Phone sleep tracking (ambient sound + movement + screen-off detection) processed on device.

3. Quick manual sleep diary (3 questions) if audio tracking is off.

4. Sleep debt score and daily adjustable sleep-window recommendation.

5. Smart alarm within a 30-minute window, waking in lightest estimated sleep stage.

6. Wind-down routine: short breathing exercises + original audio + dim mode.

7. Weekly report: three recommendations, improvement trend, previous-week comparison.

8. Optional cloud sync, export (CSV/PDF), account deletion.

4) Pricing and revenue

• Permanent free: basic tracking, sleep debt score, one weekly report, one audio clip; no ads.

• Plus: $4.99/month or $39.99/year (about 33% off): unlimited recommendations, all sounds, advanced export, full history.

• Family: $59.99/year for up to 5 accounts; simple family targeting.

• B2B for clinics and employee wellness: $2–5/employee/month, anonymized dashboard.

• Seasonal content (e.g. "Jet Lag Adjustment Week"): $2.99 one-time.

• Goals: 3% free-to-Plus conversion, $4 average monthly revenue per paying user, low cloud costs via on-device processing.

5) Global launch via free channels

• ASO in five languages: Arabic, English, French, Spanish, Turkish; keywords such as "sleep coach" and "sleep tracking".

• Short videos on TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts: "Sleep science in 30 seconds", 4 weekly.

• Free web chronotype calculator collecting email and directing users to the app.

• Launch on Product Hunt, Reddit (r/sleep, r/insomnia), and student communities; posts explain, not sell.

• 20 partnerships with health, sleep, and behavioral therapists for lifetime free access, not cash.

• Referral loop: friend invite unlocks a premium week for both; in-app tracking.

6) Legal and technical risks

• Privacy: audio may be sensitive data; on-device processing, explicit consent, data minimization, DPIA before Europe launch.

• Medical claims: not a medical device; does not diagnose insomnia. Use "sleep wellness" language, disclaimer, specialist advice for severe symptoms.

• Recording laws: some states require two-party consent; no raw audio uploaded or retained; clear warning before activation.

• OS limits: iOS and Android restrict background work; use permitted APIs; target under 8% battery use overnight.

• Accuracy vs wearables: show confidence margin, require 14-day calibration, avoid overpromising.

• Content/distribution: license voices/music, follow store policies, backup plan if "smart alarm" wording is rejected.

7) Name and tagline

Proposed name: Suhail

Tagline: Your personal sleep guide from your phone, without extra devices.
