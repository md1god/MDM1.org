Raffi — Full Project Plan

Your whole library in your pocket, all your reading before your eyes.

A full implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, monetization model, rollout plan, and risks. The project is in development and has not yet launched.

A new product plan for tracking a personal library

1) Target Audience

• Readers who own physical or digital books and want to organize them and track their reading.

• Reading trackers enthusiasts looking for a lightweight, fast alternative to plain spreadsheets.

• Families and small libraries that want to catalog their books in a shared way.

• Those who prefer self-hosted operation or a lightweight cloud version that protects their privacy.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Arabic-first interface with full multi-language support and RTL direction.

• Quick registration via barcode or camera without lengthy manual entry.

• Smart insights dashboard: what you read this month, pages remaining, goal for the year.

• Smart local recommendations based on your history, without sending your data to third parties.

• Open export/import (CSV/JSON) with no lock-in on your data.

• Free cloud version to start, with the option to run locally.

3) Scope of the First Version (max 8 tasks)

1. Account creation and secure login.

2. Add a book (title, author, cover, ISBN) manually or via barcode.

3. Reading statuses: to read / currently reading / read / postponed.

4. Track pages and chapters and the completion percentage for each book.

5. "Want to read" list, 5-star rating, and notes.

6. Basic statistics dashboard (books of the year, pages, daily streak).

7. Search and categories (tags) and filtering.

8. Export and import data in CSV/JSON format.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free to start: up to 200 books, basic statistics, full export.

• Plus — $4/month or $36/year: unlimited books, advanced stats, goals and streaks, cloud backups, priority support.

• Self-hosted: free and open source; revenue comes from the managed cloud version and paid add-ons later.

• Future additional income: book-store partnerships with transparent commissions (optional).

5) Global Rollout Plan Using Free Channels

• Short-form content on TikTok/Instagram Reels/YouTube Shorts: reading challenges and library before/after.

• Participation in Goodreads and Reddit communities (r/books, r/selfhosted) with real value, not ads.

• Multilingual SEO blog: "how to catalog your library," free reading-tracking templates.

• Free Notion/Spreadsheets templates pointing to the product as an easier option.

• Referral program: one free month of Plus for each friend.

• Presence at book fairs and local reading clubs.

• Weekly newsletter with reading ideas and community statistics.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: book cover rights and metadata (use an open data source or user input); camera usage rights and image privacy; data protection regulations (GDPR) for user data.

• Technical: barcode reading accuracy and duplicate matching; data loss when self-hosted (solution: encrypted backup); RTL compatibility and Arabic font shapes; cloud storage cost as it grows; ease of forking and exploitation if open source (update management).

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Tagline

Proposed name: Raffi

Marketing tagline: Your whole library in your pocket, all your reading before your eyes.
