Zahir — Full Project Plan

Zahir — show up locally first: on maps and in AI answers.

Full execution plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, launch plan, risks. Not yet launched.

Product plan: "Zahir" - the local visibility platform for SMBs

1) Target audience

• Local small and medium business owners: plumbing, AC & refrigeration, dental clinics, general clinics, maintenance, restaurants, beauty salons.

• Local marketing agencies and freelancers managing 5-50 local business profiles (GBP).

• Multi-location owners in one city or several cities.

• Launch priority regions: the Gulf and Arab Levant first (Arabic content), then English-speaking markets (US/UK/CA/AU) with an English identity.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

1. Operational depth: not just a report - it proposes actionable weekly steps (ready posts + review replies in Arabic/English), published only with user approval.

2. Daily Rank Heatmaps instead of monthly reports, with an interactive map per ZIP/neighborhood.

3. Tracking mentions in ChatGPT/Google AI Overviews/Perplexity for queries like "best plumber in a given neighborhood", plus content suggestions to fill gaps.

4. Arabic-first interface: most global tools are English and clunky for the Arab market - we build a native Arabic experience with full RTL support.

5. True freemium: free value that doesn't end after 14 days, with gated access that naturally pushes users to upgrade.

6. Copy & white-label scalability: agencies sell reports under their own brand - a built-in distribution channel.

3) V1 scope (max 8 tasks)

1. Connect Google Business Profile via OAuth (profile data, reviews, categories).

2. Automatic scoring (Local SEO Audit), 0-100, shareable PDF/page report: missing ratios, photos, hours, categories, NAP.

3. Weekly GBP post generator (3-5 drafts) based on activity and season, with preview before publishing.

4. Review reply generator (positive/negative) with an adjustable tone.

5. Maps ranking tracking per ZIP/neighborhood with heatmap and weekly comparison, via geo-proxy servers or a trusted available API.

6. Checking brand visibility in AI engines (ChatGPT / Google AI) via standard logged prompts.

7. Single dashboard with weekly email report + multi-location panels.

8. Sign-up and payments (Stripe + Mada/local transfer if possible); freemium: 1 free profile, upgrade unlocks detailed maps and auto-publishing.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free (Starter): 1 GBP profile, full audit report, 2 posts/month, 1 weekly ranking - acquisition.

• Pro - $29/month or $249/year: up to 5 profiles, daily rank maps, unlimited posts/replies, AI monitoring, PDF reports under client brand.

• Agency - $99/month: up to 30 profiles, white-label, API & integrations (Zapier/webhook), multi-user team, custom branded reports.

• Extra revenue: paid deep reports (one-off audit $49), extra profile plans, partnership commissions (photo/video design agency).

5) Global distribution plan with free channels

1. Arabic/English SEO long-tail comparisons: "How do I improve my visibility on Google Maps", "best GBP tools" - goal: page 1 in 6-9 months.

2. Free public rank maps for niches/cities with a shareable dashboard - local virality, organic links.

3. Free open GBP post template guide (PDF) for email - waitlist and B2B growth.

4. Reddit (r/smallbusiness, r/SEO) + Facebook Groups for local business owners - sharing value, not ads.

5. YouTube/TikTok Shorts in Arabic/English: "review your Google profile in minutes" with real examples.

6. Partnerships with accountants, trade consultants, chambers of commerce - mutual referrals.

7. Product Hunt + Indie Hackers + directories (There's An AI For That, .toolify) for the English launch.

8. LinkedIn B2B targeting agency founders with before/after case studies.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: comply with Google Business Profile APIs terms (approved read/write, usage limits, "draft" marking before publishing). No risky scraping. Clear privacy policy for business/review data, GDPR/CCPA.

• Technical: Google quota limits, API data update delays, and difficulty tracking Maps rankings accurately (vary by device/language/session) - windows and sampling documented transparently. Proxy costs may rise with scale.

• AI: hallucinated posts/replies -> mandatory human review + system warning instructions.

• Competition: large tools with monopolistic pricing -> we differentiate with Arabic/RTL and Freemium pricing.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Zahir

Tagline: Show up locally first - on maps and in AI answers.
