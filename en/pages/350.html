Mirqab — Full Project Plan

Discover your keywords, track your rankings, and audit your site — free and fully open, and ready for you and AI agents.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a free and open-source alternative to SEO tools

1) Target audience

• Owners of small sites and independent blogs who cannot pay $100+ per month.

• Freelancers & Boutique Agencies managing dozens of sites on a limited budget.

• Developers and DevOps teams who prefer self-hosting and full control over data.

• AI Agents via MCP that need SEO tools callable programmatically.

• Tech communities in emerging markets (the Middle East, Africa, South Asia) where global pricing is prohibitive.

2) Points of distinction (why choose our product)

• Genuinely free to start: an open-source core + a generous free cloud plan, no bank card required.

• Transparency and verifiability: the code and methodology are available for audit instead of a black box.

• Lightweight and frugal: runs on cheap hosting or a local machine, with no reliance on expensive infrastructure.

• Native integration with AI Agents via MCP as a first-class interface, not a later add-on.

• Privacy first: data stays with the user under self-hosting.

• No data lock-in: free import/export in open formats.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Keyword Research: keyword ideas + estimated volume + difficulty from free sources.

2. Rank Tracking: daily tracking of the ranking of a specific set of keywords on search engines.

3. Site Audit: a basic technical check (broken links, missing titles, meta, sitemap, robots.txt, page speed).

4. Backlinks: a simple backlinks inventory (count, referring domains, estimated authority) via available sources.

5. Competitor Snapshot: an initial comparison of two sites (shared keywords, content gaps).

6. Reports & Export: simple PDF/CSV reports shareable with the client.

7. MCP Server: wiring the four tools above as tools consumable by AI Agents.

8. Multi-project & Auth: managing multiple sites + a basic user and roles system.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free (Open Source / Self-host): everything with no limits when run on the user's own infrastructure.

• Cloud Free: two projects, limited keyword tracking, updated weekly.

• Hobby — $9/month: more projects, daily updates, expanded reports.

• Pro — $29/month: high limits, API, managed cloud MCP, team collaboration.

• Agency — $79/month: practically unlimited projects, white-label, priority support.

• Additional revenue: enterprise support (Support/SLA), managed hosting, add-on Marketplaces, community sponsorships.

5) Global distribution plan via free channels

• GitHub: a public repository + a strong README + good-first-issues + an open license.

• Product Hunt: launch described as \"free and open source\".

• Hacker News & Reddit: (r/SEO, r/juststart, r/webdev, r/SideProject) with honest stories, not ads.

• MCP and AI Agents communities: listing in MCP directories and awesome-lists repositories.

• Educational content: free tutorials like \"the free X alternative\" + transparent SEO-friendly comparisons.

• Discord/Telegram: a community for contributors and users.

• Academic and university collaboration: workshops and student contributions.

• An open ambassadors and contributors program to attract the first 1000 users.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: the terms of use of search engines and third-party sites; avoiding intensive direct scraping and relying on licensed sources/APIs or user data.

• Privacy: compliance with GDPR/CCPA when hosting customer data.

• Intellectual property: ensuring no copying of the code or data lists of any closed product.

• Accuracy: free sources are estimates; they must be clearly labeled and false promises avoided.

• Technical: rate limits, IP blocking, crawling cost, and the maintenance complexity of data collection.

• Sustainability: turning the free project into a sustainable one without strangling free users.

• Competitive race: big players may offer free alternatives — the distinction remains in openness and MCP.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Mirqab (Mirqab — the watchtower)

Marketing tagline: Discover your keywords, track your rankings, and audit your site — free and fully open, and ready for you and AI agents.
