Joul — Complete Project Plan

Measure your software's energy, don't guess it.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not yet launched.

1) Target Audience

• DevOps, SRE, and platform engineering teams managing Kubernetes or cloud servers.

• Application developers who want to know the energy footprint of each service, container, or process.

• Sustainability officers and ESG teams that need auditable reports on digital infrastructure consumption.

• Small and medium-sized SaaS companies that lack specialized energy measurement hardware.

• Universities, research centers, and managed service providers.

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• Software-first estimation: works without additional hardware and uses hardware counters when available to improve accuracy.

• Granular coverage: measures at the process, container, Pod, and service level, not only at the server level.

• Carbon-aware: converts energy into estimated emissions based on the cloud region and runtime.

• Lightweight and easy to deploy: a single executable, low resource consumption, and installation within minutes.

• Open and integrable: exports in Prometheus and OpenTelemetry formats, with direct Grafana integration.

• Actionable recommendations: identifies the largest consumers and suggests concrete improvements instead of stopping at the numbers.

• Privacy by default: processing is local, and sensitive data is not sent to the cloud without explicit consent.

• Free start: the core version is open source and free for self-hosting, lowering the barrier to trial.

3) Scope of the First Version (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. Build a lightweight Linux agent as a single executable.

2. Collect CPU, memory, I/O, and GPU metrics from procfs, eBPF, and RAPL when available.

3. Apply energy estimation models calibrated by processor family and workload type.

4. Export metrics via Prometheus and OpenTelemetry.

5. Provide a local web dashboard showing consumption by process and container.

6. Add Kubernetes integration via DaemonSet and attribute consumption to namespace and Pod.

7. Build a carbon calculator based on the carbon intensity of the operating region.

8. Create a weekly report and automated recommendations for the largest consumers.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free forever: the open-source core (Apache-2.0) for self-use without limits.

• Free cloud plan: up to 5 nodes, with 7-day data retention.

• Pro plan: about $6 per node per month, or $0.01 per core/hour, with longer retention, alerts, and reports.

• Enterprise plan: custom annual pricing including SSO, auditing, SLA, on-premises deployment, and support.

• Additional revenue sources: cloud hosting, paid support, enterprise reporting integrations, and training.

5) Global Go-to-Market Plan with Free Channels

• Publish the open repository with clear documentation and quick-start examples.

• SEO content about GreenOps, measuring Kubernetes energy, and reducing carbon footprint.

• Share hands-on experiences on Hacker News, Reddit, LinkedIn, and CNCF communities.

• Live demos at conferences such as KubeCon, FOSDEM, and sustainable technology conferences.

• Free listings in the Grafana, Prometheus, and Kubernetes marketplaces.

• Launch on Product Hunt with a demo version and transparent comparison data.

• University partnerships and student competitions, plus Arabic and English content to serve emerging markets.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Estimation accuracy: figures may be understood as official measurement; solution: clarify methodology and margin, and publish the calibration methodology.

• Privacy and compliance: collecting operational data may touch GDPR; solution: data minimization, encryption, and local retention by default.

• Regulatory claims: the product must not be described as CSRD- or EED-certified without audit; solution: precise wording and audit partnerships.

• Licensing and intellectual property: solution: legal review of the license and trademark before launch.

• Hardware and OS compatibility: RAPL may be absent in some clouds and kernels differ; solution: fallback models and matrix tests.

• Agent security and performance: eBPF and elevated privileges may increase the attack surface; solution: least privilege, package signing, and clear consumption limits.

7) Proposed Name and Tagline

Proposed name: Joul

Tagline: Measure your software's energy, don't guess it.
