AeroFolio — Complete Project Plan

All your trips in one record: memory, cost, and impact.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target audience

• Frequent travelers and long-haul travelers who want an organized personal record.

• Remote workers and digital nomads who need to track costs and distances for tax or budgeting purposes.

• Families and small teams that share travel plans and want a unified view without complexity.

• Travelers seeking delay and cancellation compensation, or wanting to understand their environmental impact and carbon footprint.

• Bloggers and content creators who need exportable statistics and maps.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Complete trip record: combines trip, cost, carbon impact, and travel-stress indicator in one place.

• Smart import: from PDF, ICS, CSV, email, and calendar files, with human review before saving.

• Privacy first: data encryption, full export, permanent deletion, and core functionality offline.

• Travel finance: currency conversion, cost per kilometer, and reports ready for budgeting or an accountant.

• Health awareness: estimates stress and time zones, plus practical tips to reduce travel effects.

• Controlled sharing: family or team space with granular permissions and optional public links.

• Free start: clear value without a credit card, upgrade only when needed.

3) First version scope (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Create an account and personal workspace with secure authentication.

2. Add a trip manually: airports, dates, airline, and flight number.

3. Initial import from PDF, ICS, and CSV with a review and correction screen.

4. Statistics dashboard: number of trips, distance, flight hours, airports, and airlines.

5. Calculate trip cost, convert currencies, and produce an exportable financial report.

6. Estimate carbon footprint using disclosed factors, with a clear note that it is an estimate.

7. Share a family or team space, and export CSV/PDF with an optional public link.

8. Responsive web app that works as a PWA with caching and core offline functionality.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Permanent free: up to 30 trips per year, basic statistics, limited import, and CSV export.

• Pro: $4 per month or $36 per year; unlimited trips, advanced import, full financial and carbon reports, and offline operation.

• Family/Team: $6 per month for five users; shared space, permissions, and aggregate reports.

• Additional future revenue: extended accounting report exports, and optional integrations with travel tools, without selling user data or ads.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Launch on Product Hunt, Hacker News, and Reddit in travel and remote-work communities.

• SEO pages based on airports, airlines, and comparisons: “How to track your trips,” “Calculating travel cost,” “Flight delay compensation.”

• Short content on YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram explaining trip organization, costs, and environmental impact.

• Free partnerships with travel bloggers, tour organizers, and student communities.

• Simple referral program: one free Pro month for each party when inviting an active user.

• Translate the interface into multiple languages, and introduce the product in local forums and traveler groups.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Privacy: trip data is sensitive; GDPR compliance, full transparency, encryption, and a clear deletion policy are required.

• Import: file formats or email and calendar API restrictions may change; updatable parsers and explicit user consent are required.

• Aviation data: some airport and airline data may be license-restricted; open sources and terms of use must be verified.

• Carbon offsets: estimates are not an official certification; methodology and disclaimer must be clear.

• Financial and legal claims: compensation and tax-report features need a notice that they are indicative and not legal or accounting advice.

• Technical: offline sync conflicts, import rate limits, account protection, and performance testing as records grow.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: AeroFolio

Tagline: All your trips in one record: memory, cost, and impact.
