SANAD — Complete Project Plan

Your encrypted email, newsletter, and messages in one place — serving you, not advertisers.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, growth plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product Plan

1) Target Audience

• Content creators and owners of small and medium-sized newsletters.

• Small business owners and local shops (stores, cafes, clinics) that need a dedicated email domain.

• Developers and indie hackers who need transactional sending via API.

• Users interested in privacy and rejecting ad tracking.

2) Points of Distinction (why choose our product)

• Encrypted by default for messages and attachments, with full control of user keys.

• A single account covering: daily email + newsletter + transactional sending via API.

• Complete Arabic and English interface, with clear Arabic documentation.

• Free to start with no bank card, and transparent pricing with published limits.

• Full export freedom: download your data and move your messages at any time.

• Focus on sending reputation (deliverability) instead of heavy visual templates.

3) Scope of the First Version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Account and sign-in system with two-factor confirmation.

2. Receiving and sending email via IMAP/SMTP with TLS support.

3. Adding a custom domain with an automatic DNS setup wizard.

4. Newsletter editor with templates + subscription lists + double opt-in confirmation.

5. Analytics dashboard: open, click, and unsubscribe rates and sending log.

6. API interface + keys for transactional sending with an operations log.

7. Plans, usage limits, and a payment gateway (freemium).

8. Security panel: encryption settings, login log, alerts, encrypted archiving.

4) Pricing and Profit Model

• Free: one domain, 1,000 subscribers, 5,000 messages/month, 2GB storage.

• Individuals: $9/month — additional domains, higher limits, full export.

• Teams: $29/month — collaboration tools, roles and permissions, priority support.

• Growth: usage fees per 1,000 additional messages (pay-as-you-go flow).

• Additional revenue sources: assisted domain setup, migration services, security add-ons (SSO).

5) Global Launch Plan with Free Channels

• SEO blog in two languages (Arabic/English) with comparisons and domain setup guides.

• Presence in free directories and libraries and tool indexing sites.

• Short video content: guides to set up a newsletter in 5 minutes.

• Participation in developer communities and technical discussion platforms.

• Referral program: free credit for every friend who signs up.

• A badge in newsletter footers: "Powered by [Name]" as a viral channel.

• Partnerships with small newsletter creators to review the product and publish their experience.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal: compliance with data protection laws (GDPR/CCPA) and subscription consent, anti-spam laws (CAN-SPAM and equivalents), responsibility for user content, and registration requirements as a service provider.

• Technical: sending reputation and IP address tracking, encryption key management and loss, account hacking attacks, storage capacity limits and hosting costs, and limiting spam without exposing user data.

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Tagline

Proposed name: SANAD

Marketing tagline: Your encrypted email, newsletter, and messages in one place — serving you, not advertisers.
