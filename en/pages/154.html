Sada — Full Project Plan

All your support channels in one fast voice, free to start, and open to everyone.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a unified multi-channel support platform

1) Target audience

• Startups and small teams (2–50 employees) that want a support hub without the cost of enterprise tools.

• E-commerce stores and digital service providers that receive inquiries from several channels.

• Remote support teams that need a shared inbox.

• Developers and managed service providers who sell support as a service (White-label).

2) Points of differentiation (why choose our product)

• A generous free tier that starts immediately without a credit card, with self-hosting as an option.

• Setup in under five minutes: connect one channel and start working right away without complex configuration.

• Modern design and light performance on weak devices and slow networks.

• Built-in assistive AI (summarization, reply suggestions, automatic classification) from the free tier.

• Absolute pricing transparency and clear limits, with no surprise feature gating.

• A focus on privacy: encryption, real data deletion, and compliance with local laws.

3) Scope of the first version (8 tasks maximum)

1. A unified inbox that combines live chat and email in one interface.

2. Conversation management: assignment, tags, statuses, and saved canned replies.

3. A public knowledge base for customizable Help Center pages.

4. An embeddable chat widget with color and logo customization.

5. A basic analytics dashboard: conversation volume, response time, customer satisfaction.

6. An AI assistant for suggested replies and conversation summarization.

7. Team channels: roles, permissions, and instant notifications.

8. An open API and Webhooks for basic integrations.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: two users, 100 conversations per month, one channel, and all basic AI features.

• Growth: 19 dollars per user per month — unlimited channels, advanced reports, white-label.

• Enterprise: custom pricing — SSO, private hosting, service level agreement (SLA), security audit.

• Additional sources: managed hosting subscriptions, an add-ons marketplace, and premium paid support.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• An open repository on GitHub with an open license to build trust and contributions.

• Technical content and SEO for Help Center pages and "the free alternative to..." in Arabic and English.

• Publishing on community platforms: Reddit, Hacker News, Dev.to, Product Hunt.

• Developer channels: integration templates for WordPress, Shopify, Slack, and Telegram.

• An ambassador program and university students who try the product and give feedback.

• Live streams and free educational workshops to show setup step by step.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Data protection (GDPR/CCPA and local laws): the need for a clear privacy policy and consent to processing.

• Responsibility for customer content on the hosted platform.

• Respecting open-source library licenses and avoiding any protected code.

• AI laws and training models on customer data.

Technical:

• Scalability when the number of concurrent conversations rises.

• Reliability of integration with changing external channels (BSP, email providers).

• Costs of AI model calls and variation in their quality.

• Data security and preventing leaks and intrusion.

• Long-term maintenance cost and resisting feature creep.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Sada

Marketing tagline: All your support channels in one fast voice, free to start, and open to everyone.
