Wamda — Complete Project Plan

Run an instant payment node on the Lightning Network with one click, and keep full control over your money.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: an original alternative for an instant payment node on the Lightning Network

1) Target audience

• Developers and payment teams that want to accept instant, low-fee Bitcoin payments.

• Small online stores and service freelancers (freelancer/WooCommerce store/seller).

• Content platforms and apps that need tipping and micropayments.

• Node operators on small servers (VPS / Raspberry Pi) looking for a lightweight, automated solution.

• Beginners who want a self-custodial node without deep technical expertise.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• One-click operation: automatic installation and syncing with no complex manual setup.

• Smart liquidity management: suggesting and opening channels and balancing liquidity automatically.

• Automatic, recurring encrypted backups that protect channel state from loss.

• Self-custodial (non-custodial): keys stay with the user; we do not control the funds.

• Unified interface: web dashboard + mobile app + REST/gRPC APIs with a simple SDK.

• Resource-light and monitored in real time with customizable alerts.

• Free to start with no transaction fees, with a clear upgrade path.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Node lifecycle: start/stop/automatic sync with a status report.

2. Create and encrypt a wallet + generate a recovery phrase and initial backup.

3. Open/close channels and settle instant payments.

4. Liquidity assistant: detecting shortfalls, recommending channels, and balanced push/pull.

5. REST/gRPC APIs + SDK in four popular languages.

6. Web dashboard to display balance, channels, and transactions.

7. Alert system (email/Webhook) for node events and errors.

8. Merchant payment flow: invoice + QR code + payment confirmation.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free to start: one node, basic dashboard, limited alerts, community support.

• Professional plan (monthly subscription): cloud backup, advanced alerts, multiple nodes, SLA, and faster support.

• Managed liquidity service (optional): nominal fees on automated balancing operations.

• Enterprise license / paid support for large teams.

• No fees on the transactions themselves to remove the adoption barrier.

5) Global go-to-market plan using free channels

• Open-source repository under a free license + comprehensive documentation and ready-made examples.

• Developer platforms: Hacker News, Product Hunt, Dev.to, awesome-lists.

• Educational content in Arabic and English (blog + short videos on YouTube).

• Ready-made plugins for WooCommerce and Shopify stores to attract merchants directly.

• Discord/Telegram community + external contributions and rewards for early contributors.

• Search engine optimization (SEO) around the keywords "instant payment node" and "accept Bitcoin in your store".

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Varying regulation of virtual assets and KYC/AML between countries; we present ourselves as a tool, not a financial intermediary.

• Data protection and privacy (GDPR) when storing alerts and cloud backups.

• A clear disclaimer regarding lawful use and not providing financial advice.

Technical:

• Losing backups or channel state means losing funds → encryption and multiple copies.

• Key management, breach risks, and wallet theft.

• Liquidity fluctuation and some payment failures → retries and automated balancing.

• Slow syncing, frequent protocol updates, and potential forks.

• Security of the API and web interfaces against vulnerabilities and attacks.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Wamda

Tagline: Run an instant payment node on the Lightning Network with one click, and keep full control over your money.
