Nabr — Full Project Plan

Translate and dub your videos right from the browser — full privacy, no install.

Full execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, launch plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a browser-based video dubbing and translation studio

1) Target audience

• Content creators on YouTube and TIKTOK and podcasters who need fast translation and dubbing.

• E-learning course creators and training companies distributing their content in multiple languages.

• Small localization agencies and freelance subtitlers managing several projects.

• Researchers and journalists who need accurate transcription with privacy protection for their material.

2) Differentiators

• Runs directly in the browser via WebGPU with no software to install — unlike traditional desktop apps.

• Core processing runs locally on the user's device: video never leaves the device (privacy + no mandatory cloud upload).

• A single visual workspace: transcription -> timing editor with video preview -> translation -> dubbing -> export.

• Brand terminology glossary automatically applied to every translation.

• Export in SRT / VTT / TXT / MP4 with hard-sub.

• Freemium model: free for local transcription, paid when cloud GPU and voice dubbing are needed.

3) First-version scope (8 tasks)

1. Video/audio upload and local text transcription via Whisper in the browser.

2. Timing editor with synchronized video preview (scroll/split/merge sentences).

3. Automatic translation with support for a custom terminology glossary.

4. Simplified speaker diarization and speaker labeling.

5. SRT / VTT / TXT export and an MP4 version with embedded subtitles.

6. Basic audio enhancement (noise cleanup and loudness normalization).

7. Workspaces, accounts, and share links for viewing/review.

8. Free usage limits, a usage dashboard, and a pricing page.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: local transcription in the browser (up to 60 minutes per month), full editor, SRT/VTT export.

• Pro (monthly subscription): cloud GPU for long videos, hard-sub, text dubbing with standard voices, unlimited glossaries, 500 minutes/month.

• Business: team seats, shared workspaces, priority processing, API with usage-based credit.

• Extra revenue source: pay-as-you-go credit for dubbing and cloud processing minutes for non-subscribers.

5) Launch plan using free channels

• SEO content: articles and clips like "how to translate/dub a video into language X in 5 minutes" targeting long-tail search intent.

• YouTube + TikTok: before/after dubbing on well-known videos as proof of quality.

• Product Hunt and Hacker News and Reddit (r/NewTubers, r/eLearning, r/podcasting) with a free offer for early users.

• Free templates: an SRT pack for educational clips plus a "translate a full e-learning course" guide.

• Partnerships with content creators in exchange for a free mention (affiliate with free credit, not cash).

• Discord/user community to gather MVP version feedback.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: consent from the voice owner before dubbing/voice cloning; ownership rights of the uploaded video; deepfake regulations (such as the EU AI Act) and explicit consent for synthetic voices.

• Privacy: even with local processing, the cloud data retention policy and automatic deletion must be documented.

• Technical: WebGPU limits on older devices -> offer an alternative cloud path; Whisper accuracy with music/accents; dubbing quality below paid studios -> set marketing expectations as "fast and editable," not "flawless and professional."

• Competition: costly user switching -> retention through shared templates and glossaries that raise the cost of moving away.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Nabr

Tagline: Translate and dub your videos right from the browser — full privacy, no install.
