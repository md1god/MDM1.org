BaseLens — Full Project Plan

Turn your spreadsheets into reports you understand instantly — without writing a single line of code.

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the rollout plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a custom analytics layer for a single platform

1) Target audience

• Product teams and operations (Ops) teams that build their internal systems on Airtable and suffer from weak native reporting.

• Digital agencies and consultants who manage multiple databases (bases) for their clients and want a unified dashboard.

• Small and medium companies (10–200 employees) that use Airtable as a CRM, project tracker, or inventory database.

• Users in North America and Western Europe first (the market most willing to pay in this pattern).

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Deep native integration with a single platform instead of generic analytics tools that don't understand the structure of tables, records, and linked fields.

• Reports ready in minutes: user activity, record growth, fields with the most gaps, status distribution, group performance.

• Secure sharing via links or embed of dashboards with view-only permissions without exposing sensitive data.

• Clear free-to-start model: starts free with one dashboard, pays when additional dashboards or team features are needed.

• Lightweight cloud structure and quick setup (OAuth connection within two minutes with no code).

3) Scope of the first version (8 tasks maximum)

1. Sign in via OAuth with the Airtable platform and select a database to connect.

2. Periodic sync of records and fields with storage in a cloud database (PostgreSQL + lightweight scheduling).

3. One dashboard covering: timeline activity, status distribution, most active fields, create/close rates.

4. Three customizable ready-made report templates (operations, sales, projects reports).

5. Embed the dashboard on an external site via iframe or a styleable widget.

6. Invite team members with roles (owner / editor / viewer).

7. Brief weekly email alerts on the top changes in the data.

8. Pricing page and payment gateway (Stripe) with a 14-day free trial of paid features.

4) Pricing and revenue model

· Plan · Price · What it includes ·

· Free · $0/month · One dashboard, sync every 24 hours, 2 members ·

· Professional · $29/month · 10 dashboards, sync every hour, alerts, custom embed ·

· Companies · $79/month · Unlimited dashboards, advanced roles, SSO, priority support ·

• Additional revenue from an annual plan with two months discount.

• Low operating costs because a single customer consumes few resources (SQLite/Postgres configured for reading).

5) Global rollout plan with free channels

• SEO content: comparisons and analyses of "the best Airtable reporting alternatives" and fixes for specific problems.

• Free copyable templates: ready-made analysis dashboards published in Airtable, Reddit, and Facebook communities.

• Listing in app directories and public libraries for integrations, with a dedicated page per language.

• LinkedIn and X accounts: short before/after clips showing reports being built in 60 seconds.

• Partnerships with Airtable consultants and agencies through a referral program with mutual discounts.

• Discord and Slack groups centered around operations (Ops) automation with weekly sharing of free templates.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: reliance on an external API; the platform's terms and pricing policies must be followed to avoid suspension.

• Legal: customer data may include personal information; a data processing agreement (DPA) and GDPR/CCPA compliance are required.

• Technical: rate limits may slow down syncing for large databases → requires smart scheduling and caching.

• Technical: API structure changes without notice can break the integration → a monitoring system and backups for connection interfaces.

• Commercial: the platform may ship better native reporting over time → the solution: specialize in vertical templates and a template community that is hard to copy.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: BaseLens

Marketing tagline: Turn your spreadsheets into reports you understand instantly — without writing a single line of code.
