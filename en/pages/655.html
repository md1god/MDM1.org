MomentPulse — Complete Project Plan

Ask one question at the right moment, and turn every answer into a decision that grows your sales.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, monetization model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Small and medium e-commerce stores on Shopify, WooCommerce, and BigCommerce, looking to improve conversion rate without a technical team.

• DTC brands selling directly to consumers that need to understand why customers buy or hesitate at the right moment.

• CRO and marketing teams wanting actionable signals tied to order value and segments, not generic surveys.

• Digital marketing agencies managing several stores that need a lightweight, repeatable tool across clients.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Real context, not generic surveys: every question is triggered by the moment (thank-you page, exit intent, post-delivery), order value, customer segment, and purchase behavior.

• One question only: reduce friction and raise response rate, with simple branching logic that shows a follow-up question when needed.

• No-code readiness: setup via a single script or plugin, with pre-built question templates for every moment.

• Money-directed results: link answers to conversion rate, cart value, and repeat purchases, not just counting replies.

• Privacy first: collect minimum data, anonymize by default, and obtain explicit consent before any tracking.

• Lightweight performance: non-blocking page load, and conditional execution that doesn't affect store speed.

• Real free start: clear value before payment, lowering the trial barrier.

3) First Release Scope (max 8 tasks)

1. Single-question editor with answer types: multiple choice, satisfaction scale, short text, and purchase reason.

2. Contextual execution engine supporting 3 moments: thank-you page, exit intent, and post-delivery.

3. Targeting rules by order value, number of previous orders, and product/category.

4. Lightweight embed script + first plugin for one platform (Shopify) with live preview.

5. Results dashboard showing impressions, response rate, and reason distribution with time filtering.

6. Basic exportable CSV reports linking answers to order value.

7. Privacy and consent management with customer data deletion on request and a clear retention policy.

8. Free account and usage limits with one team invitation and view-only permission.

4) Pricing and Profit Model

• Free: one active survey, up to 500 monthly impressions, two moments, and basic reports.

• Growth — $19/month: unlimited surveys, all moments, advanced targeting, and full export.

• Scale — $79/month: multiple stores/brands, team permissions, result comparison, and priority support.

• Agency — $199/month: agency workspace, limited number of clients, customizable reports with agency branding.

• Additional revenue sources: paid add-ons (AI text analysis, smart follow-up questions), and a one-time managed setup service.

5) Global Go-To-Market Plan via Free Channels

• Long-tail SEO: comparison and solution pages like "exit-intent survey for Shopify" and "post-purchase questions".

• App stores: free launch on Shopify App Store then WooCommerce, optimizing the listing with keywords.

• Practical content: small case studies, ready-made question templates, and short guides on linking answers to conversion.

• E-commerce communities: sharing results and experiences in DTC, Indie Hackers, and relevant Reddit groups and forums.

• Free partnerships: integrations with email and customer service tools, and an in-product referral program.

• Product-Led Growth: "Built with" link on free surveys, and free collaboration invitations for teams.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal: GDPR and CCPA compliance via explicit consent, clear legal basis, data processing agreement, and retention/deletion policy.

• Legal: avoid collecting unnecessary personal data, and don't use answers for advertising without separate permission.

• Technical: embed script impact on page speed; solution is asynchronous loading and strict size limits.

• Technical: script blocking (ad blockers) and partial data loss; solution is an alternative server-side path and transparent estimated stats.

• Technical: free account abuse; solution is per-store limits and lightweight domain verification.

• Technical: data isolation between stores, backups, and runtime error monitoring.
