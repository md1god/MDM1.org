Nabd — Complete Project Plan

All your news in one place, with intelligence running on your server and uncompromising privacy.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a self-hosted news reader

1) Target audience

• Privacy-conscious individuals: those who want to follow the news without ad tracking or algorithms.

• Developers and technical teams: those who need one-click deployment via Docker on their own server.

• Small organizations and newsrooms: teams following many sources who want automated digests.

• Bloggers and content creators: those following competitor sources and niche trends.

• Users in censorship areas/weak networks: those who need lightweight sync and local copies.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• AI summarization locally, with the ability to disable it entirely, without sending data to a third party by default.

• One-click deployment: Docker Compose + a unified image, with default SQLite storage (no complex setup).

• A modern, fast interface that works as a PWA on mobile, with native RTL/LTR support.

• Optional multi-device sync via the user's own server, not a mandatory central account.

• A Plugins system for secure REST interfaces to enable customization without modifying the core.

• Full export/import in OPML/JSON format to avoid data lock-in.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Import and follow RSS/Atom/JSON feeds via URL or OPML.

2. Organize sources into categories and tags with instant search.

3. A clean reading interface (list/cards) with dark mode and RTL.

4. Mark as "unread" and sync reading state locally.

5. An optional summarization engine (local or an API key provided by the user).

6. Save articles to read later and search within stored text.

7. One-click deployment via Docker with scheduled automatic backups.

8. Basic multi-user authentication and simple permissions.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free-to-start: the self-hosted version with all core features, with no limit on the number of sources.

• A cloud "Pro" plan: a low monthly subscription (≈ 4–6 USD) including managed hosting, multi-device sync, and cost-backed summarization.

• A "Team" plan: per-seat pricing with shared categories and a statistics dashboard.

• Additional sources: voluntary financial support, commercial licenses for companies wanting enterprise features, and third-party paid plugins.

• The principle: the core is fully open source; profit comes from convenience (hosting), not from gating features.

5) Global distribution plan via free channels

• Open-source communities: posting on technical project-sharing platforms and self-hosting forums.

• A public repository of ready-made source packs (OPML Packs) by field and language to attract new users.

• Educational content: "Start in 5 minutes" guides and technical comparisons on the product blog and short clips.

• Crowd translation via an open translation platform to support multiple languages and global reach.

• A contributor program: badges and free features for contributors to the code and plugins.

• Presence in font-end/self-hosting communities with live demos (demo instance).

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Copyright: we display only links and excerpts, without republishing the full content; summaries are attributed to the source while respecting robots/terms of use.

• Privacy and data: we do not collect user data in the self-hosted version, with a clear policy for the cloud version and encryption at rest.

• Reliance on external interfaces: avoid depending on a single summarization API through abstraction and multi-provider support.

• Security: handle untrusted feed inputs (SSRF, HTML/JS injection) with strict sanitization and isolation of plugin execution.

• Sustainability: prevent excessive requests via caching and respecting crawling control protocols.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Nabd

Tagline: All your news in one place, with intelligence running on your server and uncompromising privacy.
