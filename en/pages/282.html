Curve — Full Project Plan

Make your data clear at a single glance.

Full execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the rollout plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target audience

• Health-focused people: those who want to track weight, sleep, physical activity, or daily habits.

• Personal finance enthusiasts: those who want to monitor expenses, savings, and budget.

• Beginners: people looking for a simple tool without complexity.

• Privacy-conscious users: people who prefer storing their data locally without being forced to sign up.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Privacy first: users can operate fully without an account, with local data storage.

• Simple design: a lightweight, seamless interface suitable for all age groups.

• Data flexibility: the ability to create custom categories that fit each user's needs.

• Clear charts: offering simplified, easy-to-read charts instead of complex ones.

• Multi-platform: designed to work on phone and web, for easy access at any time.

• Free to start: focused on a free model with no mandatory subscription.

3) Scope of the first version (8 tasks maximum)

1. UI design: creating an original design that fits different screens.

2. Data entry system: building a flexible system for adding daily or custom data.

3. Chart creation: developing simple chart types (line, bar).

4. Local storage: implementing a secure storage system on the device only.

5. Dark mode: supporting dark mode for comfortable use.

6. Data export: the ability to export data in CSV format.

7. Reminders: adding simple reminders for the user.

8. Basic testing: ensuring the app is stable before release.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free version: includes all first-version features fully and permanently.

• Paid version (optional): cloud backups, advanced templates, and priority support.

• Revenue model: freemium model (free to start) with a one-time payment option or a low-cost subscription, without relying on ads in the core version.

5) Global rollout plan with free channels

• Search engine optimization: writing educational articles about personal data tracking.

• Short visual content: sharing tutorial clips on short-video platforms to explain the tool's benefits.

• Free communities: participating in forums and boards specialized in health and finance.

• Open platforms: publishing the app in free stores with a clear description.

• Word of mouth: encouraging users to share their experience.

• Free documentation: creating a simple usage guide on the web to increase reach.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal risks: complying with privacy protection laws in different countries, and focusing on not collecting sensitive data.

• Technical risks: data loss due to app deletion, which we will address through an export and import feature.

• Operational risks: device variability, and we will focus on supporting a wide range of them.

• Competitive risks: with similar tools existing, we will rely on differentiators such as privacy and simple design.

7) Suggested name and marketing tagline

Suggested name: Curve

Marketing tagline: Make your data clear at a single glance.
