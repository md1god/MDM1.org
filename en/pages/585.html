Fahim — Complete Project Plan

Snap your question, and understand the solution step by step instead of copying it.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, growth plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: an AI home tutor from image to understanding

1) Target audience

• Middle school, high school, and university students (13–22 years old) who struggle with homework.

• In the first phase: Arabic-speaking students, then global expansion.

• Secondary audience: parents looking for affordable educational follow-up, and teachers looking for an explanation tool.

2) Points of differentiation (why choose our product)

• Understanding before the answer: it offers gradual hints (Socratic hints) instead of showing the solution directly, reducing reliance on copying.

• Strong support for Arabic and handwritten mathematics via custom OCR + LaTeX format conversion, a weak point for most competitors.

• Review cards generated automatically from mistakes, not only from content, to turn the mistake into learning.

• Student privacy first: no ads inside the learning experience, and images are automatically deleted after processing.

• Effectively free to start with a generous usage limit, without an early paywall.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Capture the question and process images (OCR) for Arabic, English, and mathematics.

2. Step-by-step solution with a "why" explanation for each step in mathematics and science.

3. A conversational tutor that offers gradual hints and asks verification questions.

4. Automatic generation of review cards (Flashcards) with simple spaced repetition.

5. A notebook organized by subject and lesson.

6. A simple user account and cross-device sync (PWA).

7. Export explanations and notes to PDF/image for sharing.

8. A progress dashboard showing weaknesses and the subjects with the most mistakes.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free to start: 20 scans per month + unlimited hints + basic cards and notes.

• Plus: $4.99 per month or $39 per year (practically unlimited scanning with a fair limit, advanced explanations, export, exam mode).

• School/group plans: per-seat annual pricing with a teacher dashboard.

• Revenue: subscriptions + B2B for schools + additional credit packs. No ads and no selling data.

5) Global growth plan with free channels

• Long-tail SEO: "How do I solve [problem type] step by step" pages for every subject and language.

• YouTube Shorts / TikTok / Reels: 30-second videos "photograph the problem and watch the explanation."

• PWA + sharing the solution link to generate natural viral spread among students.

• Discord and Reddit communities and student groups with real help content, not ads.

• Teacher ambassadors and a student referral program with free sessions.

• Gradual Arabization and localization (English, Spanish, Hindi) with the same design system.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: academic integrity and misuse in exams; minors' data (GDPR-K / COPPA); textbook content rights; liability for answer accuracy.

• Mitigation: clear terms of use, an "exam mode" that prevents direct solving, parental consent, image deletion, educational disclaimer.

• Technical: OCR accuracy for handwriting, parsing mathematical formulas, model hallucination, inference cost and response time, misuse.

• Mitigation: multi-model verification, automated review of mathematical steps, caching, usage limits, and quality monitoring.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Fahim

Marketing tagline: Snap your question, and understand the solution step by step instead of copying it.
