Sellara — Complete Project Plan

An open-source commerce platform that launches your store in minutes and grows with you without limits.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, launch plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Small and medium merchants who want an independent store without restrictions from closed hosting platforms.

• Agencies and developers who build stores for multiple clients and need full control over code and design.

• Growing (headless) projects that want to separate the storefront from the order system and rely on an API.

• Local stores in emerging markets seeking a self-hosted solution with prices starting free.

2) Points of Distinction (Why Choose Our Product)

• Free from the start: an open-source core that can be self-hosted with no fees and no product limit.

• Truly API-first: every function is available through an API, allowing headless storefronts and mobile apps with ease.

• One-click setup: run locally or on any cloud via Docker containers without DevOps experience.

• High performance: a lightweight, cached store page that loads faster than traditional solutions.

• Assisting AI: product description generation, catalog translation, and automatic classification.

• Multiple stores and languages from a single dashboard with multi-currency pricing.

• An open plugin marketplace with a security review of plugins before publishing.

3) Scope of the First Version (8 tasks maximum)

1. Product and inventory management (variants, categories, media, SKU).

2. A ready and fast storefront with customizable templates.

3. Cart, order, and order status tracking.

4. Integration with multiple payment gateways, shipping zones, and tax rules.

5. Customer accounts, order history, and addresses.

6. A basic dashboard with sales and inventory reports.

7. A public API with interactive documentation and Webhooks.

8. Self-hosting via Docker and a limited free cloud manager.

4) Pricing and Profit Model

• Free tier: the full open-source core + a managed cloud with usage limits (products/orders/domain) sufficient to get started.

• Growth plan: a monthly subscription for multiple stores, managed backups, and priority support.

• Agencies plan: multiple seats, a staging environment, and delivery via custom domains.

• An optional commission on premium plugins and integrated payment gateways, while keeping direct payment commission-free.

• Ready-made services: data migration and template customization as high-margin revenue.

5) Global Launch Plan with Free Channels

• GitHub as the main hub: a clear license, organized releases, an attractive README, and bilingual documentation.

• Practical SEO content: guides on "building a self-hosted store" and comparisons based on technical facts.

• Communities: Reddit, Hacker News, developer forums, and Discord, with helpful non-promotional replies.

• Plugin and template marketplace: offering free templates to attract and build a referral system.

• Product Hunt, Dev.to, and YouTube: practical technical demos and open releases.

• Universities, conferences, and hackathon programs to adopt early students and developers.

• Hosting partnerships that showcase the product as a ready-made app on their stores.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal: choosing an appropriate license (such as AGPL) and clarifying brand limits before early adoption; compliance with GDPR for customer data, and with PCI-DSS by not storing card data directly.

• Commercial legal: avoiding any quotation, code, or visual identity from existing products, and relying on documented original build.

• Technical — security: a large attack surface in e-commerce, requiring periodic security review and a rapid update system.

• Technical — scaling: high performance under load pressure requires load testing and a design based on caching and queues.

• Technical — external dependency: the product being affected by payment and shipping gateways, requiring an abstraction layer to avoid lock-in.

• Technical — adoption: fierce competition from established platforms requiring clear distinction in development experience and performance.

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Tagline

Proposed name: Sellara

Marketing tagline: An open-source commerce platform that launches your store in minutes and grows with you without limits.
