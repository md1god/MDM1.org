Amnak — Full Project Plan

Scan the menu and know what suits you before you order.

A complete plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, launch plan, and risks. The project is being built and not yet launched.

Product plan for menu scanning and allergen checks

1) Target audience

• People with food allergies, especially the nine common allergens: milk, eggs, fish, shellfish, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat, soy, and sesame.

• Parents choosing food for children in restaurants.

• Travelers facing menus in unfamiliar languages and cuisines.

• People avoiding specific ingredients for health or diet reasons, such as gluten.

• Secondary: restaurants and cafés that want clearer allergen information for customers.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Explain results, not just flag danger: shows why a dish is risky, the suspected ingredient, and analysis confidence.

• Ready ordering script: a short sentence in the local language and English to read to the waiter or chef.

• Direct vs. cross-contact risk: separates a confirmed ingredient from possible preparation contamination.

• Privacy first: on-device image processing where possible, automatic deletion, and no mandatory account.

• Local cuisine support: understands common dishes in Arab, Asian, and Mediterranean cuisines, not only Western food.

• Multiple profiles: one per family member, with a simple, fast UI.

• No ads: to preserve trust in a sensitive health product.

3) First-version scope (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Create an allergy profile: select the nine allergens, add custom ingredients, and set severity.

2. Scan a menu: upload or take a photo, paste menu text, or import a PDF.

3. Extract dishes and ingredients: convert the image to text, then split the menu into dishes and possible ingredients.

4. Risk analysis: classify each dish as “likely safe,” “needs verification,” or “high risk,” with a confidence score.

5. Explain the reason and alternatives: show the suspected ingredient, why it is risky, and suggest a possible modification.

6. Generate an ordering script: short copyable text in the local language and English, with a clear cross-contact question.

7. Store results locally: keep recent scans on the device, not on permanent servers.

8. Report a wrong result: a quick feedback button to improve accuracy.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: 10 scans monthly, one allergy profile, basic analysis, and an English ordering script.

• Paid: $2.99 monthly or $24 yearly; unlimited scans, 5 profiles, extra languages, PDF export, and offline mode.

• Family: $39 yearly, with unlimited family-member profiles.

• Business: restaurant subscription from $99 monthly for organized allergen information, or a lightweight API.

• No ads; discounts for nonprofits and allergy support groups.

5) Global launch plan using free channels

• Search optimization: multilingual pages on “allergens in common cuisines” and “how to order safe food in a restaurant.”

• Short content: TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts showing a real menu scan and a hidden risk.

• Niche communities: useful, non-promotional participation in allergy and gluten groups on Reddit and Facebook.

• Unpaid partnerships: allergy associations, nutrition clinics, universities, embassies, and tourist cities.

• Product Hunt and tool platforms: a free launch to attract early users.

• App store optimization: multilingual keywords and simple illustrative images.

• Local ambassadors: volunteers in major tourist cities who test and share the product.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: not a medical device; no diagnosis or absolute guarantee. A clear warning must state that final verification is the user’s and restaurant’s responsibility.

• Wrong-result liability: errors may cause health harm; use probabilistic language, confidence scores, and encourage direct questions.

• Privacy: images may show people or locations; require automatic deletion, encryption, and GDPR and CCPA compliance.

• Regulatory differences: allergen definitions and cross-contact rules vary by country; the analysis must be described as advisory.

• Technical: poor image quality and lighting, language differences, OCR limits, and possible language-model hallucination.

• Cost and time: cloud analysis may be costly and slow; use hybrid on-device processing and lightweight models.

• Menu changes: ingredients may change without menu updates; link each result to its scan date and alert the user.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Amnak

Tagline: Scan the menu and know what suits you before you order.
