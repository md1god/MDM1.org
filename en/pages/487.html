Wameed — Complete Project Plan

An interactive plasma lab that takes you from the equation to the flash in your browser.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, outreach plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Undergraduate physics and engineering students, and advanced high-school students.

• Teachers and professors of plasma, astrophysics, and fusion.

• Researchers and engineers who need a quick experiment before heavy simulation.

• Science content creators and science museums seeking a reliable interactive demonstration.

2) Differentiators (Why choose our product)

• Runs instantly in the browser with no installation, plus a Python path for those who want full control.

• Combines visual presentation and reproducible code in one space.

• A library of ready, validated scenarios instead of starting from scratch.

• Arabic/English educational interface with exercises and automated grading.

• A genuine free tier for light simulation, with payment for compute and classes.

• Transparency: the simulation core is open source, and the hosted platform is the paid product.

3) Scope of the First Release (maximum 8 tasks)

1. A basic browser simulation engine via WebAssembly/Pyodide for a simplified plasma model.

2. Six ready scenarios: aurora, magnetosphere, electrical discharge, fusion, solar wind, and laboratory plasma.

3. Visual parameter panel: density, temperature, magnetic field, simulation time.

4. Live charts and CSV/PNG export with a shareable run log.

5. Educational mode: automated check questions and instant feedback.

6. Share a scenario via public link, copy it, and edit it.

7. A documented Python/Notebook interface to run the same scenarios programmatically.

8. A free account + limited cloud quota for heavier simulation.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free forever: light simulation, public scenarios, a small monthly cloud quota, no ads.

• Pro for students/individuals: $8 per month, including a larger quota, private saving, and advanced export.

• Classroom/university license: $300–1,200 per year depending on the number of students, with a teacher dashboard and assignments.

• Additional compute on demand: GPU-hour credits when needed.

• Workshops, training, and certificates: supplementary income from the education sector.

• Principle: everything a learner needs to get started remains free.

5) Global Outreach Plan with Free Channels

• An open repository for the core + packages on PyPI to attract developers.

• Short lessons on YouTube, TikTok, and Shorts in two languages.

• Arabic and English SEO articles: “How does plasma work?” with runnable scenarios.

• Translated documentation and a quick-start guide for teachers.

• University ambassadors offering free workshops in exchange for institutional licenses.

• Participation in Reddit, Discourse, Hacker News, and physics forums.

• Integration with GitHub Classroom, science competitions, and student conferences.

• Publishing credentials and methodology to support trust and academic citation.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Dual use: prohibit any scenario aimed at weapons or military fusion, with a clear content policy.

• Compliance and privacy: GDPR and student data protection, and no collection of children’s data without consent.

• Data and library licenses: audit every source and model before publication.

• Scientific accuracy: validation against documented reference cases and documentation of model limitations.

• Performance: browser limits addressed with optional cloud computing and clear limits.

• Security: isolate execution of uploaded code and prevent resource exhaustion.

• Cost sustainability: cap the free quota and measure the cost of each run.

7) Proposed Name and Tagline

Proposed name: Wameed

Tagline: An interactive plasma lab that takes you from the equation to the flash in your browser.
