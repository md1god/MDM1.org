طَلْعَة — Complete Project Plan

From a line on paper to a scene that convinces your client in seconds.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the go-to-market plan, and the risks. The project is currently in build and has not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Small and medium architecture and interior design firms (2–15 employees) that need visual references for clients before starting detailed design.

• Freelance designers and real estate development companies that present multiple design alternatives for a project contract.

• Drafting engineers, students, and designers in real estate photography and marketing who have plans or rough models and need a publishable image.

• Core launch segment: interior design studios in multilingual markets looking to cut review time from days to minutes.

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• Multi-style output in one go: the product generates several styles (Modern, Nordic, Classic, Tropical) from the same drawing, not just a single image.

• Faithful to the original plan: we preserve openings, dimensions, and walls exactly as in the plan, with a visual indicator showing the match percentage, instead of turning the plan into a random scene.

• Collaborative workspace, not just a conversion button: project rooms, comments on the image, and comparable saved Versions.

• Client-ready export: a PowerPoint/PDF file containing the original plan + outputs + a materials and colors fact sheet.

• Arabic-first interface + full right-to-left support, with instant results on mobile.

• Control over physical properties (lighting type, time of day, flooring type, wall color) instead of relying solely on free-form text prompts.

3) First Version Scope (Max 8 Tasks)

1. Upload an image (hand sketch, plan, 3D model screenshot) in a common format with automatic compression.

2. Extract "understanding layers" from the image: walls, openings, furniture, ceiling.

3. Generate one photorealistic output within seconds with 4 style presets.

4. A match counter (Skew Score) comparing the output with the original plan side by side.

5. Quick editing tools: lighting time, color palette, hide/show furniture.

6. Project gallery and output saving with version history.

7. PDF/PNG export with a watermark on the free plan.

8. Subscription billing panel (Stripe) with email sign-up and a free trial account.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free to start: 10 images per month at low resolution with a watermark.

• Pro — 19$/month: 150 images, no watermark, high-resolution export, version history.

• Office — 79$/month: 3 seats, shared folders, logo branding on exported files, priority processing.

• Top-up credits for overage, and an annual plan with ~20% discount.

• B2B additional revenue: an API for large firms and real estate marketing platforms, usage-based.

5) Global Go-To-Market Plan Using Free Channels

1. Daily before/after content (plan ↔ output) on Instagram, TikTok, and Pinterest in multiple languages.

2. Short tutorials on YouTube: "How to present an idea to your client in one minute."

3. Community participation (Reddit, Facebook and LinkedIn groups for interior designers, CAD forums).

4. Free downloadable templates (Notion, Moodboard files) in exchange for an email to build the mailing list.

5. Referral program: free credits for inviting a colleague, and limited deals for academies and students.

6. Collaborations with design content creators in exchange for a free plan (Barter), and sharing user projects with proper credit.

7. Exclusive short webinars with local design studios, and multilingual site translation (SEO).

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

Legal:

• Ownership of outputs and rights: it must be clearly defined who owns the final image (user vs. company).

• Uploading sensitive plans or unpublished projects could be leaked — need an NDA, encryption, and automatic file deletion.

• Risks from the lack of transparency in photorealism: label images as "preliminary concept, not built" to prevent claims that they show a completed building.

• Licensing agreements with AI model providers, and GDPR compliance when serving Europe.

• Trademarks and names: registration check before launch.

Technical:

• Consistency of quality on poor-quality plans and scattered hand sketches.

• Compute costs can eat the margin under heavy usage — credits and processing queues are necessary.

• Response speed during peak hours, and uptime directly affects trust.

• Scalability without degrading privacy, and backups against loss of user projects.
