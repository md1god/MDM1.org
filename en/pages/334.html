Nazm CAD — Full Project Plan

Design your 3D parts with code in minutes, not days.

Full execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the rollout plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

Product plan: an AI-powered, cloud-based programmatic CAD editor

1) Target audience

• 3D printing hobbyists and makers (Makers).

• Mechanical and architecture engineering students and those learning programming.

• Startups and micro-enterprises that need repeatable designs and version control without the cost of traditional software.

• Teachers and STEM curriculum specialists who want simplified educational tools.

2) Differentiators

• Instant 3D preview inside the browser while typing, with no long processing wait.

• An AI assistant that converts a text description into editable design code.

• Built-in distributed version control for designs with real-time cloud sharing.

• One-click export to STL / STEP / DXF with ready-made print and manufacturing settings.

• Arabic and English interface, plus simplified beginner-oriented educational documentation.

3) Scope of the first version (8 tasks maximum)

1. In-browser Python code editor with autocomplete and a custom command library.

2. Interactive 3D viewer running on WebGL.

3. Open-source geometric kernel (such as OpenCascade) behind the service for solid operations.

4. Cloud saving and automatic versioning with share links.

5. Export to STL / STEP / 3MF.

6. AI assistant to generate and explain design code.

7. Ready-made template library (chains, gears, brackets, boxes) that is customizable.

8. Arabic/English documentation and interactive lessons for beginners.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: non-public projects, STL export, limited AI assistant, basic templates.

• Pro — $12 per month: unlimited private projects, STEP export, unlimited assistant, full revision history.

• Team — $25 per user per month: shared workspaces, permissions, Git integration.

• Educational/Academic: an 80% discount or completely free for institutions, with later plans for connected manufacturing services (Quote-to-Part) as an additional revenue source.

5) Global rollout plan using free channels

• Launch on Product Hunt and Hacker News with an interactive live demo.

• Short videos on YouTube, X, and TikTok: "From idea to a finished part in 5 minutes."

• Arabic/English Discord community for support and weekly design challenges.

• Open educational content on GitHub (templates and lessons) to build trust and SEO.

• Partnerships with universities and robotics clubs to offer the educational version for free.

• Mailing lists in 3D printing forums and maker communities.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: licensing for the open geometric kernel and ensuring no infringement of modeling algorithm patents; protecting the trademark and the domain.

• Technical: performance of heavy engineering operations inside the browser/cloud, WebGL display limits for large models, dependence on cloud and AI providers and their rising costs, and the difficulty of competing with established desktop tools.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Nazm CAD

Marketing tagline: Design your 3D parts with code in minutes, not days.
