Ampora — Complete Project Plan

Every watt under your control.

Full plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue, rollout, risks. Not yet launched.

An original product plan in energy and Internet of Things

1) Target audience

• Individuals and smart homes: track electricity and appliance use, cut the bill.

• Property and small-facility managers: clinics, cafés, offices, warehouses — per-zone measurement and alerts.

• Solar and storage operators: see production, consumption and efficiency.

• Device manufacturers and Integrators: connect devices fast without a full backend.

• Labs, universities and government initiatives: open-source platform for efficiency experiments.

2) Differentiators (why choose us)

• Effortless connectivity: templates for common devices (MQTT, HTTP, Modbus, BACnet), auto-discovery, smart setup.

• Energy-first platform: live dashboards for consumption, production and cost — not a general dashboard with energy added later.

• Instant rules and alerting: threshold breach, device offline, or solar drop — via email, webhook, notifications.

• Simple AI: consumption forecasting, behavioral fault detection, actionable savings.

• Drag-and-drop app builder: build your own app (dashboard, report) without code, full customization.

• Open source at heart + managed cloud: free self-hosting or a free cloud account.

• Data privacy and ownership: user data stays with them; open export in standard formats (CSV, MQTT, API).

3) First-version scope (8 tasks maximum)

1. Core Gateway: data from MQTT / HTTP / Modbus with standard device identity.

2. Events and rules engine: conditional rules + alerts via email and webhook.

3. Live energy dashboard: consumption, production, estimated cost, live chart.

4. Dashboard Builder: drag-and-drop components with ready-made templates.

5. Device gateway + provisioning: keys, certificates, remote device shutdown.

6. Documented REST + WebSocket API: for developers and real-time alerts.

7. User accounts and teams: signup, roles, one workspace with a free plan.

8. App Publisher: export the interface as a standalone web app or embed.

4) Pricing and Revenue model

· Plan · Price · Includes ·

· Free (to start) · 0$ / month · 2–5 devices, 2 dashboards, 7 days history, basic alerts, self-hosting ·

· Pro · 29$ / month · 50 devices, 1 year history, predictive AI, advanced alerts, 5 members ·

· Team · 99$ / month · 300 devices, teams and permissions, scheduled reports, priority support ·

· Enterprise / On-premise · priced by scale · self-hosted, private link, SLA, training and support ·

Extra revenue: a marketplace of templates and modules (templates/integrations) at low prices, plus partner commissions from install/maintenance.

5) Global rollout plan with free channels

• GitHub: open-source core repo, clear docs and a public roadmap — the heart of the strategy.

• Technical writing (Blog/SEO): "How to measure your home's consumption in under an hour", comparisons, cases.

• YouTube: short tutorials: connecting a device, building a dashboard, a rules alert.

• Communities and forums: participation in energy, Home Automation, Reddit, Hacker News, Hackaday.

• Product Hunt, Hacker Noon and directories: free trial with a live demo.

• Discord / user community: direct support, feature ideas, an ambassadors program.

• School/university partnerships: projects and labs use the platform free with attribution.

• Shareable tools: a "How much do you save?" calculator and a ready dashboard template — spreads itself (product-led growth).

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Data privacy: GDPR/CCPA compliance for consumption data, encryption and limited retention.

• Device control safety: no claim of driving critical equipment without certifications (CE/UL/FCC as a system).

• Open licensing: a license protecting the project from closed acquisition (e.g. AGPL with a managed commercial exception).

• Intellectual property: no copying brands or interfaces; from-scratch code must be clean.

Technical:

• Internet of Things security: weak keys, obsolete devices, gateway attacks — needs secure OTA updates and audits.

• Protocol compatibility: manufacturer fragmentation requires a maintained abstraction layer.

• Scalability: dense telemetry needs efficient storage and indexing from day one.

• Third-party dependence: external API changes can break integrations.

• Commercial sustainability: a strong free plan may raise costs without enough paid conversion.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Ampora

Tagline: Every watt under your control.
