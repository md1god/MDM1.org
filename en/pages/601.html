Raseed — Complete Project Plan

Turn AI agent usage into clear, fair revenue from the first event.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• SaaS companies adding AI agents to their products and needing to price their usage.

• Independent agent development teams and startups selling solutions to clients.

• AI agencies managing agents for multiple clients and needing explainable billing.

• Enterprise platforms needing cost and margin tracking per agent, department, and client.

• Individual developers who want to start free and move to a paid plan as they grow.

2) Differentiators (Why choose our product)

• Flexible pricing from day one: usage, credit, and outcome in one engine, without building an in-house billing system.

• Real-time, transparent meter: every usage event has a unique ID and audit log, with duplicate and late-event prevention.

• Margin protection: cost-versus-revenue dashboard per agent and client, with spending limits and automatic suspension.

• Free start without a card: a generous test environment, then pay as you grow.

• Original design from scratch: lightweight SDK, Arabic and English interface, and multi-tenant architecture.

• Fast integration: Stripe and local billing tools, with the ability to bring the customer's own payment provider.

• Explainability: an invoice the end customer understands, not just vague consumption numbers.

3) Scope of the First Version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Create an account and project, and issue API keys for test and live environments.

2. Python and Node.js SDKs to send usage events with an idempotency key.

3. Visual pricing engine for usage, credit, and outcome plans, with limits and overages.

4. Credit wallet: prepurchase, automatic deduction, and low-balance alerts.

5. Real-time usage meter and cost and margin dashboard per client and agent.

6. Invoices and subscriptions via Stripe, with basic tax support and PDF export.

7. Consumption anomaly alerts, spending limits, and automatic suspension when the limit is exceeded.

8. Self-service customer portal showing balance, invoices, usage history, and upgrade.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

· Plan · Price · Limits · Profit ·

· Free · $0 · 1,000 events/month, one agent, test environment · Free to attract developers ·

· Starter · $29/month · 100,000 events, 3 agents · Subscription + 0.5% of billed usage value ·

· Growth · $99/month · 1 million events, unlimited agents · Subscription + 0.4% of billed usage value ·

· Scale · Custom · Based on volume · Subscription + 0.25% or a monthly minimum ·

• Free start without a card, and conversion to paid when limits are exceeded.

• Dual revenue: fixed subscription + a small success fee from actual billing.

• Additional services: custom pricing, enterprise integrations, and paid margin reviews.

5) Global Go-to-Market Plan with Free Channels

• GitHub: publish an open-source SDK and integration examples, and attract developers through issues and stars.

• SEO and technical content: guides on agent pricing, cost calculation, and usage-based billing.

• Product Hunt, Hacker News, and Indie Hackers: initial launch with a clear message: "AI agent billing in minutes."

• Communities: Reddit, Discord, and Slack for developers and SaaS companies, with educational rather than promotional posts.

• Integration marketplaces: free listing in Stripe, Vercel, LangChain, and other directories.

• Agency partnerships: a revenue-share referral program with no upfront cost.

• Bilingual content: English and Arabic documentation, and short videos on YouTube.

• Ambassador program: developers who deliver workshops in exchange for free credit and public recognition.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

Legal:

• Taxes and VAT on international invoices, and regulation of payments and prepaid balances.

• Data protection and privacy under GDPR and CCPA, and data processing agreements.

• Liability for billing errors, terms of use, and pricing transparency.

• Compliance with local AI regulations, especially when classifying usage or fraud.

Technical:

• Duplicate or delayed events, addressed with idempotency and reconciliation.

• Provider price changes mid-cycle, addressed with versioned, timestamped pricing.

• Service outages or meter latency, addressed with an event queue, retry, and clear limits.
