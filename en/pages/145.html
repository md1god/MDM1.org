Lubab — Complete Project Plan

Turn any lecture or file into ready-made flashcards and quizzes in minutes.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the distribution plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

An original educational product plan: turning lectures and files into study materials

1) Target Audience

• University students (especially in medicine, engineering, and law faculties) who pile up long recordings and lectures.

• High school students and standardized test takers (SAT, IELTS, general secondary exams).

• Self-learners and the widespread "e-learning" courses.

• Teachers and academic assistants to create supporting materials for their students.

2) Differentiators (Why choose our product)

• Authentic Arabic support: recognizing Arabic speech and dialects and converting it to text, which is weak in competitors.

• Source-grounded: every card/question is linked to the minute in the recording or the page in the file, to reduce hallucination and build trust.

• Review science: built-in "spaced repetition" scheduling, not just content generation.

• Genuinely free entry: the basic tier with no credit card and no hidden cap.

• Integration with student tools: export to Anki and Google Docs, a browser extension, and LMS compatibility.

• Privacy: a local processing/encryption option, and deleting sources after generation.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum of 8 tasks)

1. Uploading materials: PDF, slides, images, audio recording, video, or an educational video link.

2. Multilingual speech-to-text (initial focus on Arabic and English).

3. Generating organized notes with a hierarchical outline.

4. Generating editable flashcards.

5. Generating short quizzes and practice questions of varying difficulty.

6. Attaching the source reference for every output (page/timestamp).

7. A spaced-repetition review loop with a progress dashboard.

8. Export to Anki/PDF and sharing a group via a link.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free forever: a limited monthly number of operations + Arabic support + basic export.

• Pro plan (monthly/yearly): practically unlimited uploads, longer lists, processing priorities, advanced editing.

• Credits for the casual user instead of a subscription.

• Student discount with university email verification, and team/university (B2B) pricing.

• An additional revenue source: customization for universities and analytics dashboards for teachers.

5) Global distribution plan through free channels

• SEO: pages for every university and popular course ("summaries of course X") and review-guide blogs.

• Short content: TikTok/Reels/Shorts clips showing the conversion in seconds.

• Student communities: Reddit, Discord, university Facebook groups, Telegram.

• University ambassadors and internal referral rewards.

• Product Hunt and launching on free product platforms.

• Open integrations: a browser extension and a free API for student developers.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: copyright of uploaded materials and platform policies when content is pulled; compliance with GDPR and FERPA, especially for lecture recordings; liability for the accuracy of outputs.

• Technical: accuracy of recognizing Arabic dialects, processing cost and response speed, hallucination in generation, dependence on external model providers, and scalability at peak times.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Lubab

Tagline: Turn any lecture or file into ready-made flashcards and quizzes in minutes.
