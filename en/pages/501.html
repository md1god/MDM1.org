Midad — Complete Project Plan

Edit your images professionally in the browser, with full privacy and no installation.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, growth plan, and risks. The project is under development and has not yet been launched.

1) Target Audience

• Freelance designers and content creators who need fast, professional editing without installing heavy software.

• Small teams and marketing agencies working on shared projects who need easy reviews without repeatedly exchanging files.

• Students and teachers in design education, especially those with mid-range devices or limited internet access.

• Organizations that handle sensitive images and prefer processing files locally inside the browser.

• Arab content creators and RTL product users, an underserved group in global editing tools.

2) Key Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• Local-first with privacy by default: Processing happens inside the browser. Images are only uploaded to a server if sharing is explicitly chosen.

• Instant and no installation: A PWA that works in the browser and supports offline use after the first load.

• True non-destructive editing: Layers, groups, masks, adjustment layers, vector shapes, and a local version history.

• Practical PSD compatibility: Import and export common elements with clear documentation of support limits rather than claiming full compatibility.

• Easier reviews: Optional encrypted share links with comments and version snapshots, instead of sending large files by email.

• Native Arabic and RTL support: Arabic text shaping, fonts, and a multilingual interface as a core feature, not an afterthought.

• Free with no watermark: The local core is fully free, with no mandatory account and no ads.

• Original identity and design: A completely new interface and code written from scratch, not a copy of any existing product.

3) Scope of First Version (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. Build PWA structure and local project storage using IndexedDB/OPFS with autosave and restore after closure, no mandatory account.

2. Layer rendering engine: Groups, blend modes, opacity, transforms, and layer masks.

3. Selection and masking tools: Brush, lasso, magic wand, and edge refinement.

4. Non-destructive adjustment layers: Exposure, curves, levels, HSL, and black & white.

5. PSD import/export for common elements, and export to PNG/JPEG/WebP and SVG for shapes.

6. Basic brush engine + Arabic/RTL text + simple vector shapes.

7. Undo/redo history, keyboard shortcuts, responsive interface for mobile and tablet, and targeted performance on mid-range devices.

8. Optional encrypted review links with comments and version snapshots, with a clear free limit.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free plan: All local editing tools, unlimited projects, full-resolution export with no watermark, and 3 review links per month.

• Pro plan: ~$6.99/month or $59/year; includes 50GB cloud sync, unlimited review links, 90-day version history, batch processing, and broader PSD support.

• Team plan: ~$12 per user/month; shared workspaces, roles and permissions, comments, and centralized management.

• Education and non-profit organizations: Free or discounted Pro plan.

• Templates and recipes marketplace: 15% commission on sales, with free templates available.

• Additional cloud storage: Optional pay-as-you-go pricing.

• No ads, no data selling, no mandatory image uploads.

5) Global Growth Plan with Free Channels

• Practical SEO: Free tool pages like PSD viewer, format converter, image compressor, and tutorials in multiple languages.

• Short-form content: YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram tutorials showing before/after results and weekly challenges.

• Communities: Product Hunt, Hacker News, Reddit, Discord, Telegram, and design forums with respect for each community's rules.

• Template gallery and monthly challenges: User creations generate shareable content and attract new designers.

• Educational partnerships: Free workshops in schools, bootcamps, and universities, with free educational licenses.

• Localization and community: Crowdsourced UI translation, RTL support, and local ambassadors for Arabic, Asian, and Latin American markets.

• Referral loop: Extra cloud space for successful referrals, with no cash rewards.

• Free add-on ecosystem: Simple SDK to attract developers and build small tools around the product.
