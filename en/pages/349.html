Fussal — Complete Project Plan

Split your song into free audio stems in seconds.

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the launch plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target audience

• Content creators: YouTube, TikTok, Reels, and Shorts video makers.

• Musicians: hobbyists and beginners who want to practice or edit songs.

• Singing and dance coaches: they need a custom karaoke version without the original melody.

• Remix creators: looking to split audio tracks quickly and for free.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Free to start: allows processing a sufficient number of files for free, with the option of extra credits only when needed.

• Fast and simple: a very lightweight interface, no mandatory sign-up, and usable on both phone and computer.

• Practical results: clear splitting of tracks (Vocals, Drums, Bass, etc.) using open-source models without complexity.

• Focus on export quality: standard export is prioritized for free, while high-quality export is available only for an optional credit.

• Lighter: a simplified design aimed at reducing waiting time compared to many crowded tools.

3) Scope of the first version (8 tasks at most)

1. Design: build a simple interface based on drag-and-drop of files to display results.

2. File processing: support for MP3 and WAV formats, with a clear maximum file size.

3. AI separation: integrate one of the open-source separation models (such as Demucs) to split audio tracks.

4. Results display: show each track individually, playable and downloadable.

5. Batch export: the ability to download all tracks in a single ZIP file with appropriate compression.

6. Credit system: free daily or monthly credits, and extra credits when needed without a mandatory subscription.

7. Performance improvement: reasonable memory and processing speed settings on the servers.

8. Comprehensive testing: testing on short, practical files to ensure the stability of the first version.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Completely free: most normal uses will be free without any annoying ads.

• Pay-as-you-go: buying credits only for high-quality export, without forcing the user into a monthly subscription.

• Low cost: relying on open-source models reduces development and operating costs.

• Clean profit: revenue from credit purchases covers server costs, with the possibility of gradual expansion in the future.

5) Global launch plan using free channels

• Search optimization: targeting simple terms like "AI vocal separation", "free karaoke", and "audio stem separation" in English and many other languages.

• Music communities: sharing value on Reddit, Discord, and forums dedicated to music makers, while respecting each community's rules.

• Short content: producing before-and-after separation videos on TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts to show the result directly.

• Free tools: listing the tool in directories of free AI tools so it reaches users with no advertising budget.

• Organic growth: relying on a good user experience, which will lead to natural sharing and increased global reach.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: a user may upload content protected by copyright. Therefore, clear terms of use, a legal warning, and a ban on malicious commercial use must be put in place. Compliance with intellectual property laws in the target countries is also required.

• Technical: high GPU resource consumption and its impact on costs in case of sudden spikes. In addition, result quality varies depending on the file type and the separation model.

• Security: file processing must be temporary, with automatic deletion after processing, and the servers must be properly protected.

• Open-source model: choosing an open-source model must be done with a check of its license to make sure it is compatible with the intended use.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Fussal

Tagline: Split your song into free audio stems in seconds.
