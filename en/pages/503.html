Mirfaq — Complete Project Plan

Search, compare, and apply with greater confidence.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, outreach plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• New job seekers: graduates and those looking for their first job.

• Non-traditional job seekers: people working part-time or seeking a quick change.

• Immigrants and international students: those facing difficulties understanding application requirements in different markets.

• Experienced people in specific fields: those wanting to change career fields without spending hours on manual searching.

2) Points of Distinction (Why Choose Our Product)

• Focus on real fit: relies on analyzing the candidate's skills against job requirements more accurately than keywords alone.

• Full transparency: shows the candidate a list of submitted applications with the status of each, without hiding any action.

• Greater manual control: allows the candidate to review every application before sending it, with editable automatic suggestions.

• Support for non-traditional jobs: focuses on remote work opportunities, temporary work, and projects, not only permanent jobs.

• Privacy first: does not store more sensitive data than necessary and gives the candidate full control over deleting their data.

3) Scope of the First Version (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. Smart profile: create a candidate profile with skills, experience, and job goals.

2. Job search: collect jobs from open public sources with classification by fit.

3. Fit analysis: display the fit percentage with an explanation of its reasons and areas for improvement.

4. Pre-submission review: an interface to review and edit application data before submission.

5. Application tracking list: track the status of every job applied for in one place.

6. Improvement suggestions: simple tips to improve the CV based on target jobs.

7. Free notifications: daily email alerts about new matching jobs.

8. Simple dashboard: a quick view of the number of matching jobs and completed applications.

4) Pricing and Profit Model

• Free model to start: allows searching, reviewing, and tracking completely free.

• Optional monthly subscription: a small paid plan that provides faster alerts, advanced filtering, and unlimited job tracking.

• Focus on value: profit comes only from those who want additional features, not from selling user data.

• Competitive price: much lower than specialized services, with the ability to try before subscribing.

5) Global Outreach Plan with Free Channels

• Professional communities: participate in job seeker communities on public platforms while offering useful advice.

• Educational content: publish short articles about improving CVs and job searching in different markets.

• Guidance from graduates: collaborate with alumni groups and universities to distribute the tool for free.

• Public job listings: connect with open public job sources to increase organic visibility.

• Search engine optimization: create pages targeted at different markets and languages for organic visibility.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal:

• Compliance with data protection laws in different countries.

• Avoid automating application submissions in a way that violates platforms' terms of use.

• Ensure not to collect data that requires special permission.

• Technical:

• Difficulty handling different application forms manually for each job.

• The need to maintain analysis accuracy without relying on complex patterns.

• The need to ensure data privacy with a simple and reliable structure.

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Tagline

Proposed name: Mirfaq

Marketing tagline: Search, compare, and apply with greater confidence.
