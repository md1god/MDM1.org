Nabra — Complete Project Plan

From idea to motion in a single pulse.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Independent content creators, YouTubers, and freelance designers.

• Small studios and marketing agencies that need motion graphics quickly without expensive subscriptions.

• Teachers and students in media and design, especially in markets with limited devices.

• Product teams producing explainer and internal clarification videos.

• The developer community that wants to build plugins and custom tools.

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• Instant local performance: a Rendering engine written from scratch in Rust and running on the GPU, with near-real-time 1080p preview on mid-range devices.

• Integrated visuals and motion: a traditional Timeline editor alongside a flexible Nodes editor in the same product, without needing two tools.

• Full freedom in the free version: export without a watermark, and full operation offline.

• Open, portable project format: understandable files + a plugin SDK isolated with WebAssembly, preventing file lock-in.

• Lightweight and compatible: runs on Linux, Windows, and macOS, and consumes less memory than heavy alternatives.

• Optional, local AI: motion suggestions and transition generation without forcing users to upload their files.

• Optional paid collaboration: share a project via a review link, while core work stays local.

3) Scope of the First Version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. A multi-layer Timeline editor with Keyframes and editable easing curves.

2. A Nodes editor for effects, transformations, and visual compositing.

3. An instant GPU preview engine at 1080p.

4. Text and vector shape (SVG) animation tools with Masking.

5. Import SVG, PNG, video, and audio; export MP4, WebM, GIF, and PNG sequence with an alpha channel.

6. A library of reusable templates and presets.

7. A native project format with autosave and local version history.

8. An initial WASM plugin SDK, and a command-line tool for headless export (Headless Render).

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free forever: all editing and local export tools without a watermark.

• Pro — $9 per month or $90 per year: faster cloud export, an expanded template library, cloud version history, and priority support.

• Studio — $29 per user per month: shared workspaces, review and approval, permissions, API, and an internal publishing option.

• Template and plugin marketplace: 20% commission on every sale, with the creator keeping 80%.

• Educational and nonprofit discounts to build an early user base.

5) Global Go-to-Market Plan with Free Channels

• Publish the open core on GitHub with a clear license and ready-made build files.

• Launch on Product Hunt, Hacker News, and specialized Reddit communities.

• A series of short tutorials on YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram showing how to complete a full effect in one minute.

• A Discord community with free weekly templates and monthly challenges.

• An ambassador program for students and teachers, and free workshops at universities.

• Documentation translated into English, Arabic, and Spanish, and search engine optimization.

• Integrations with video and designer platforms, and technical articles on Dev.to and Medium.

• Release free templates under the community's name to increase participation and reach.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal: verify that the name and trademark do not conflict, audit library licenses and avoid contaminating GPL licenses, clarify licensing for templates, fonts, and media, comply with GDPR in cloud services, and review coding tool licenses and H.264/HEVC patents.

• Technical: varying GPU and driver support, cloud export cost and time, browser performance in collaborative review, plugin security through WASM isolation, data loss risks addressed with autosave and backup, and platform fragmentation and maintenance.

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Tagline

Proposed name: Nabra (نبضة)

Marketing tagline: From idea to motion in a single pulse.
