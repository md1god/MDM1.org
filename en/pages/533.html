Pulse — Complete Project Plan

Ask at the right moment to improve what really matters.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target audience

• Small and medium-sized e-commerce stores: seeking to understand why shoppers abandon carts without conducting long interviews.

• SaaS platforms: wanting to gather quick feedback on the user experience after a specific feature is used.

• Creators and startup founders: needing fast data to make an immediate decision without a large budget.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Appears at the right time: shown based on user behavior, not randomly or annoyingly.

• Fully isolated from design: uses element isolation technology to work on any website without conflicting with its colors or fonts.

• Privacy first: collects as little data as possible and relies on anonymous responses by default.

• Very fast to install: installed with one line of code, with no need for advanced programming expertise.

• Completely free to start: can be used in practice without entering a credit card.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

· Number · Task · Purpose ·

· 1 · Build a mini widget (Widget) · Display only one question with maximum simplicity. ·

· 2 · Fast question editor · Create a question with two options or a text field within minutes. ·

· 3 · Display rules · Define when the widget appears based on a specific page or time. ·

· 4 · Asynchronous code loading · Ensure the original website is not slowed down. ·

· 5 · Style isolation (Shadow DOM) · Prevent any conflict with the website design. ·

· 6 · Simplified dashboard · Show responses aggregated and ready to read. ·

· 7 · Duplicate protection · Prevent the same question from appearing to the same visitor more than once. ·

· 8 · CSV export system · Export responses for easy analysis outside the dashboard. ·

4) Pricing and revenue model

· Plan · Price · Features ·

· Free · $0 / forever · Up to a reasonable response limit, basic display rules, and limited export. ·

· Professional · $9 / month · Unlimited responses, advanced display rules, and full export. ·

· Team · $19 / month · Multiple permissions, response filtering, and priority support. ·

Revenue model: monthly subscriptions, with the free plan remaining strong enough for organic growth.

5) Global distribution plan with free channels

• Free tool directories: add the product to relevant open tool lists for founders.

• Search engine optimization: focus on pages targeting a specific problem such as "cart abandonment reasons" in the most widely used languages.

• Developer and business communities: share practical value without direct promotion, pointing to the link when needed.

• Free referral program: grant one month of the Professional plan for a successful referral, with no upfront cost.

• Short content: create practical examples of decisions based on quick responses and publish them on free channels.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

· Type · Risk · Mitigation ·

· Legal · Privacy requirements in some countries · Reduce data collection to the response only, anonymize by default, and have a clear privacy policy. ·

· Legal · Consent-related regulations · Make use simple without personal tracking, and explain the nature of collection to the user. ·

· Technical · Conflict with website code · Use Shadow DOM and full style isolation. ·

· Technical · Impact on loading speed · Very small code size and asynchronous loading. ·

· Technical · Spam attempts · Limit display per visitor and prevent repeated submissions. ·

7) Suggested name and marketing tagline

Suggested name: Pulse

Marketing tagline: Ask at the right moment to improve what really matters.
