About the project

About MDM1: our vision, mission, core values and journey — a message for every person, built together with the community.

A bridge between ancient human wisdom and a message addressed to every person today.

Our vision

One message that brings every person together — whatever their religion, belief, country or skin colour — around what unites us as human beings more than what divides us.

Our mission

To remind every person of the humanity they share with others — the innocence of childhood we all began with, and the single journey we will all travel to the end.

Core values

  • Honesty — everything we say and publish is clear to everyone; nothing is said in private that contradicts what is announced in public.
  • Transparency — every decision that concerns the community is put openly before everyone; no decision is taken in secret.
  • Fairness — fair principles govern every aspect of the message, with no favouritism and no hidden privileges for any party.
  • Trustworthiness — no deception and no promises we cannot keep; word and deed are one.
  • Community — a message for the community, built with the community's participation; its decisions rest on collective consultation.
  • Openness — open to every person around the world, with no borders, no intermediaries and no discrimination.

Our journey

  • June 2026 — The beginning: the idea began from a deep desire to craft a message that joins ancient human wisdom with today's need for a brotherhood that goes beyond borders.
  • June 2026 — Development: building the foundation of the message by drafting the principles and designing the visual identity.
  • June 2026 — Launch: launching the official website, building the first community and publishing the charter.
  • 2027 — The future: expanding the message so that it reaches every language and culture around the world.
