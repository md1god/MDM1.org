مِشترى — Full Project Plan

Your personal agent searches, compares, and buys on your behalf; no order goes through without your approval.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market, and risks. The project is under construction, not launched.

1) Target Audience

• Arab consumers in the Gulf, Middle East, and North Africa, aged 25–45, shopping across stores and losing time comparing, paying, and tracking.

• Busy people, small business owners, families, residents, and expatriates buying cross-border.

• Best-price seekers and those who struggle to complete purchases for language, technical, or accessibility reasons.

• Global English-as-second-language users, prioritizing the Arab market, then international expansion.

2) Differentiators (Why choose our product)

• Arabic-first: RTL interface and understanding of names, sizes, addresses, and local payment formats, not just translation.

• Explicit approval before every purchase, with adjustable budget caps and daily/monthly limits.

• Final-price transparency: shipping, customs, taxes, warranty, and returns before payment.

• Works across multiple stores, not within one marketplace, and does not rank a store higher for a larger commission.

• A new "one task" design: natural-language request, clear comparison card, single approval button, and an audit log for every step.

• Privacy first: minimal data, no selling user data, no training on conversations without permission.

• A genuinely free, no-credit-card start, lowering the trial barrier and increasing adoption.

3) Scope of the First Version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Preferences profile: budget, sizes, favorite brands, addresses, payment methods, and allergy or halal restrictions.

2. Turn a natural-language purchase request into an editable search and comparison plan.

3. Search multiple stores via APIs and product feeds; add a Browser Extension where no official integration exists.

4. Compare final price, shipping, expected customs, warranty, returns, and trusted reviews in one view.

5. Build a suggested cart with alternatives and request explicit approval before any financial commitment.

6. Purchase via a licensed payment gateway or virtual card, with 2FA/OTP and no full card-data storage.

7. Track orders and notify of price changes, delays, or stockouts.

8. Returns/refund assistant: generate requests, follow up with the store, and alert the user when human intervention is needed.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free: 10 purchase tasks/month, up to 3 stores compared, order tracking, basic support. No credit card.

• Pro: about $7.99/month or $59/year; more tasks, price-drop monitoring, multi-store purchasing, and an advanced returns assistant.

• Family plan later: multiple profiles and budget caps per member.

• Revenue: subscriptions and transparent store referral commissions, with no product-price increase or ranking effect.

• No user fees on the free version; no orders held behind payment.

5) Global Go-to-Market Plan with Free Channels

• Bilingual SEO for long-tail comparison pages and regularly updated buying guides.

• Short videos on TikTok, Reels, and Shorts showing a full purchase task in under a minute.

• Helpful posts on Reddit, Quora, and tech/expat forums, without disruption/spam.

• Launch on Product Hunt and GitHub with an open-source browser extension to attract developers.

• Local WhatsApp/Telegram groups, plus a referral system giving extra free tasks.

• Partnerships with small creators for referral commission, not upfront payment.

• Local landing pages per market with real success stories and transparency notices.

• An educational email program for new users, reducing churn.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal: agency and authorization, consumer protection, distance selling and returns, payment regulation, privacy under GDPR, CCPA, and PDPL, commission disclosure, store terms and anti-scraping rules, purchase-error liability, and anti-fraud/anti-money laundering.

• Technical: store-site changes and payment failures, 2FA, payment security and PCI DSS, Prompt Injection from web pages, specification hallucination, stale prices/inventory, model cost and latency, data leaks, and reliance on external APIs.

• Mitigation: licensed payment providers, mandatory human approval, audit logs, spending limits, continuous testing, quality monitoring, liability insurance, and clear legal transparency.

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Tagline

Proposed name: مِشترى

Marketing tagline: Your personal agent searches, compares, and buys on your behalf, and no order goes through without your approval.
