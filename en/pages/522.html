Jazm — Full Project Plan

From every meeting to a clear decision and an executed task.

Implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue, go-to-market, risks. Not yet launched.

1) Target Audience

• Core: B2B sales teams at SMBs (5–50 seats) holding 10–40 meetings weekly.

• Daily users: sales reps, customer success managers; decision-makers: sales managers/RevOps leads.

• First sectors: SaaS, professional services, agencies, consulting.

• Geography: bilingual MENA teams, then global Arabic-speaking or multi-market teams, then LATAM/SEA.

• Problem: decisions get lost between recordings and notes, reps enter CRM data manually, and summaries vary in quality, especially with mixed Arabic and English in one meeting.

2) Differentiators

• Truly bilingual: Arabic, English, and common dialect transcription/understanding, including mixed sentences.

• Decision/commitment log, not just a summary: every decision, promise, and task ties to person, due date, and deal.

• Optional bot: upload a recording or connect a calendar without a bot joining the meeting.

• CRM with human approval: suggest HubSpot/Pipedrive updates, review before writing, with a change log.

• Sector templates: SaaS Discovery, agency renewal, customer follow-up, pricing objections.

• Decision-first design: decision/task cards, not a long wall of text.

• Privacy first: configurable retention, data isolation, no recordings used for model training.

• Free start without credit card and clear pricing for small teams.

3) First-version scope (max 8 tasks)

1. Import via audio/video upload or a Google Meet / Teams / Zoom link, with an optional bot.

2. Arabic/English transcription with speaker separation and timestamps.

3. Structured summary: decisions, objections, risks, open questions.

4. Extract tasks with owner, due date, status; review before save.

5. Detect and tag deal signals: budget, competitor, next step.

6. Sync HubSpot/Pipedrive with customizable fields or retry weights.

7. "Commitment Log" dashboard per account/deal with simple alerts.

8. Secure sharing via expiring link or PDF export, with basic search.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free forever: 5 meetings monthly, 45 minutes each, 7-day retention, 1 seat, trial CRM sync.

• Pro: $14/user/month annually, or $18 monthly; 60 processing hours monthly and 90-day retention.

• Team: $29/user/month annually, with fair use, SSO, admin permissions, and team reports.

• Enterprise: custom pricing with Data Residency, DPA, security review.

• Additional revenue: setup/integration fees ($500–2000), API/extra usage, 20% recurring partner commission.

• Target margin: 75%+ via on-demand processing, per-task model routing, ASR cost control.

5) Global go-to-market: free channels

• Bilingual SEO: comparison pages, meeting-minutes templates, and guides on writing a CRM update after a meeting.

• Small free tools: meeting cost calculator, sales objections library, agenda generator.

• Product-led growth: free share links with light branding, and invite a colleague after the first meeting.

• Communities: RevOps Co-op, r/sales, LinkedIn, Indie Hackers, Product Hunt, Show HN.

• Partnerships: CRM consultants, business accelerators, and sales coaches offering the tool to clients.

• Content: build in public on LinkedIn, meeting teardown videos, and a weekly newsletter.

• Affiliate marketing: 20% recurring for the first year.

• Expansion: local launch in MENA, then dedicated LATAM/SEA pages. No paid ads before proving retention.

6) Legal and technical risks

• Recording consent: laws vary by country; the bot must announce recording, allow disabling where required, and offer admin policies.

• Privacy/compliance: GDPR, CCPA, DPA, subprocessor list, immediate deletion, configurable retention, Data Residency.

• Data use: no model training on customer recordings, review provider terms, clarify note ownership.

• ASR accuracy: dialects and code-switching may reduce quality; fix with fast human correction, transcript confidence, and timestamps.

• Hallucination: tie every decision/task to an audio excerpt, show confidence, and never generate nonexistent numbers or promises.

• Prompt injection: meeting content may contain malicious instructions; treat text as data only, with no execution rights.

• CRM integration: API limits and schema changes; use queues, retries, clear error logs.

• Security/cost: encryption, tenant isolation, SSO, audit logs, penetration testing, fallback models, cost control.
