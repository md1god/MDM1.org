Mizan — Complete Project Plan

One interface for all models, with full control over cost and quality without changing your code.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target audience

• Product teams and startups building AI applications that want to avoid lock-in to a single provider.

• Individual developers and small teams who hold their own keys (BYOK) and need precise cost control.

• Companies with privacy and compliance requirements that prefer self-hosting within their infrastructure.

• Multi-tenant SaaS platforms that need routing and budget policies per customer or project.

• AI Ops teams responsible for monitoring, tracing, and reducing inference bills.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Full compatibility with the OpenAI API as a drop-in replacement: chat/completions, streaming, tools, embeddings, without changing client code.

• Smart multi-policy routing: by cost, latency, quality, or priority, with fallback and automatic retry.

• Strict budgets at the key/project/user level, with daily and monthly limits and immediate cutoff when exceeded.

• Encrypted BYOK with an option for managed keys, and no hidden markup on provider prices.

• Dual caching: exact match, and optional semantic caching that can be disabled per project.

• Self-hosting with one command via Docker Compose, and an open-source core under the Apache 2.0 license.

• RTL-first dashboard in Arabic and English, with per-request cost tracking and custom tags.

• No technical lock-in: log export, copyable routing, and compatibility with Vercel AI SDK and LangChain.

3) MVP scope (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Build an OpenAI API-compatible Gateway with project key authentication and streaming.

2. Integrate 5 providers: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Groq, and Together, with unified error handling.

3. Routing engine: priority, weights, cost-based selection, fallback, timeouts, and retry.

4. Budget engine: daily/monthly caps per key and project, and return 429 when exceeded.

5. Cost and usage tracking per request, with an initial summary dashboard.

6. Exact-match cache + optional semantic cache with TTL.

7. Encrypted BYOK storage + self-deployment via Docker Compose and env configuration.

8. Simplified admin dashboard (keys, providers, budgets) + documentation and Python/JavaScript examples.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: permanent free self-hosting, or cloud up to 50,000 requests per month, two projects, 3 keys, community support.

• Pro — $29/month: one million requests, unlimited projects, semantic caching, 5 seats.

• Team — $99/month: SSO, audit logs, 15 seats, advanced budget alerts.

• Enterprise: self-hosting license, SLA, dedicated support, and contract-based pricing.

• Revenue: subscriptions + usage overage ($0.20 per 1,000 requests) + enterprise support. No markup on provider tokens when using BYOK.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• GitHub: open core, strong README, two-minute Quickstart, and a clear comparison with alternatives.

• Documentation SEO: pages targeting "OpenAI-compatible gateway", "LLM router", and "multi-provider fallback".

• Community launch: Show HN, Product Hunt, Reddit (r/LocalLLaMA and r/LLMDevs).

• Technical content: articles on Dev.to and Medium, a YouTube video building a complete app, and a cost-savings case study.

• Free integrations: Vercel AI SDK, LangChain, LlamaIndex, n8n, Dify, Open WebUI.

• Attraction tools: cost-savings calculator, ready-made Next.js/FastAPI templates, and a Discord and GitHub Discussions community.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Provider terms of use regarding resale and managed keys.

• GDPR/CCPA compliance and retention of logs that may contain personal data.

• Clarity of the open-core license and contributor rights, and trademark protection.

Technical:

• Differences in provider behavior (tools, streaming, token accounting) and changing APIs.

• Increased latency due to the routing layer, and semantic caching errors.

• Securing keys, tenant isolation, and preventing abuse and fraud.

• Handling rate limits, streaming requests, and fallback when a provider is down.

Proposed name: Mizan

Marketing tagline: One interface for all models, with full control over cost and quality without changing your code.
