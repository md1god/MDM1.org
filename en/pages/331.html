Zefira — Full Project Plan

Turn your website visitors into qualified sales meetings instantly and without a hefty cost.

Full execution plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout, and risks. Not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

Targets small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs) in the business-to-business (B2B) sector, especially:

• Software as a Service (SaaS) companies with a website but no complete sales team.

• Marketing agencies and consultancies that want to turn visitors into customers without hiring a full-time salesperson.

• Startups that need to generate sales opportunities around the clock on a limited budget.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Quality over quantity: instead of maximizing leads, it filters only visitors with genuine intent, saving sales team time.

• Privacy first: designed from day one for data protection rules such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and global privacy laws.

• Easy setup: needs no technical expertise and installs in minutes via a simple code snippet.

• Multilingual: interacts with visitors in many languages to enable global expansion.

• Full human control: the sales team reviews conversations and accepts or rejects appointments before booking, reducing errors and building trust.

• Cost-effective: built for SMBs that cannot afford traditional sales agents.

3) First Release Scope (8 tasks maximum)

1. Landing page: a simple page presenting the product with free account sign-up.

2. Chat tool: a live chat window embeddable in any website.

3. Smart qualification logic: classifies visitors using questions customized per company.

4. CRM integrations: basic connections with the most popular Customer Relationship Management (CRM) systems.

5. Appointment booking: links the team calendar to book meetings automatically after qualification.

6. Dashboard: shows visitor count, conversation count, and qualified opportunities.

7. Instant alerts: notifies the sales team when a new lead is qualified.

8. Custom settings: lets companies edit questions, replies, and availability times easily.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

A Free-to-Start model with optional upgrades as needed:

• Free plan: for startups, includes a limited number of qualified opportunities per month at no cost.

• Growth plan: fixed monthly fee with more qualified opportunities, ideal for growing companies.

• Flexible usage-based plan: extra payment per qualified opportunity beyond the plan limit, avoiding large bills without usage.

This model lets companies try the product risk-free before paying, and scales revenue with the value delivered.

5) Global Rollout Plan Using Free Channels

• Search Engine Optimization (SEO): educational content answering companies' questions, attracting organic traffic worldwide.

• LinkedIn: practical experiences and useful tips focused on SMBs, to build trust and attract the target audience.

• Specialized communities: active participation in Reddit and Discord groups for entrepreneurs and startups, offering real value with no paid ads.

• Organic referrals: a referral program rewarding users who invite other companies, accelerating organic spread.

• Strategic partnerships: working with agencies and designers serving small businesses, offering the product as a complementary solution.

• Short video content: short videos explaining the problem and solution in multiple languages, on free platforms to increase global reach.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal risks:

• Commercial communication laws: compliance with laws such as the TCPA in the United States and similar laws elsewhere when using contact data.

• Data protection and privacy: compliance with the GDPR and other local laws, plus explicit visitor consent before collecting data.

• Transparency: making clear that the interaction happens through an AI system, preserving trust with users.

Technical risks:

• Answer accuracy: possible errors in system replies, reduced through human oversight and continuous review.

• AI hosting costs: artificial intelligence model costs rise with usage, so they must be monitored and tuned wisely.

• CRM integrations: changes to CRM APIs may affect integration stability and need periodic maintenance.

• Data security: protecting customer and visitor data via strong encryption, strict access controls, and regular security reviews.

7) Proposed Name and Tagline

Proposed name: Zefira

Tagline: Turn your website visitors into qualified sales meetings instantly and without a hefty cost.
