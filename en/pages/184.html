Noventa — Complete Project Plan

Train your team, plan smart, and make glory in ninety minutes.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the distribution plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: An open-source football management game

1) Target audience

• Sports management game players looking for a deep, free experience that does not require a powerful device.

• Open communities and developers who want to mod the game and add their own leagues and teams.

• Players in emerging markets who find it hard to pay the high prices of paid games.

• Creators and content makers who want to build shareable scenarios and challenges.

2) Points of distinction (why choose our product)

• Free from the start: full access to the core of the game with no payment.

• Deep moddability: team, league, and player data can be imported and exported in an open format.

• Light performance: runs on modest devices and browsers without needing high specs.

• Open-source transparency: the code is available for auditing and community development, which builds trust and accelerates innovation.

• Native Arabic support and an RTL interface alongside other global languages.

• No "pay-to-win" principle: superiority inside the game is never bought.

3) Scope of the first version (8 tasks)

1. A simplified statistical match engine that simulates results and events.

2. An editable database for teams and players with fictional or community content.

3. A full league system with standings, promotions, and relegation.

4. Team management interface: lineup, tactics, training.

5. A basic transfer market with contracts and budgets.

6. A save/load game system with time progression (seasons).

7. A multilingual interface with RTL support and Arabic selected by default.

8. An onboarding package and a playable tutorial match.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Fully free to start (Free-to-Start): all core gameplay features are available at no cost.

• Optional cosmetic packs (kits, logos, themes) at a symbolic price.

• Optional sponsorship / donations through support platforms.

• A "supporter" edition that grants a badge, pride, and early access to experimental features without any gameplay advantage.

• Commercial licenses for studios and schools to use the engine for education.

5) Global distribution plan through free channels

• An open-source repository with clear documentation and an invitation to contribute.

• Free game distribution platforms and publishing a web version playable instantly.

• Reddit, Discord, and Hacker News communities specialized in games and open source.

• Collaboration with content creators on YouTube, Twitch, and TikTok to showcase gameplay challenges.

• Hackathons and community participation to build league packs in new languages and regions.

• Sharing via Product Hunt and game development forums for each release.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: avoid using the names, logos, or images of real players without a license; rely on fictional or licensed content.

• Legal: comply with open-source licenses and privacy (GDPR) when collecting any data.

• Technical: balance the accuracy of the match engine with performance on weak devices.

• Technical: compatibility of mod packs with version updates (a clear versioning system).

• Technical: preventing cheating if multiplayer is added later.

• Community: ensuring contributor continuity through clear governance and a declared development roadmap.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Noventa

Tagline: Train your team, plan smart, and make glory in ninety minutes.
