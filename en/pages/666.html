Madar — Complete Project Plan

Fewer questions, deeper understanding, and faster decisions.

Complete plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, launch, and risks. It is under construction and not yet launched.

Problem: Enterprise survey tools are costly, complex, force too many questions before a useful result, and poorly serve Arabic content.

Idea: An original platform for AI-built adaptive surveys that gathers the fewest answers and turns them into clear decisions, with full data ownership.

1) Target audience

• Product/UX teams needing fast, low-cost research.

• SMBs priced out of enterprise platforms.

• Research/marketing agencies running multi-client surveys.

• HR, education, and nonprofit teams.

• Arab market first, then global via multilingual support and RTL interfaces.

2) Differentiators (why us)

• Natural-language building: describe a survey in Arabic or English to generate an editable draft in an original design.

• Adaptive questions: smart paths show only relevant questions, raising completion and cutting annoyance.

• Arabic and RTL first: an original experience, not translation, with ready templates for Arab markets.

• Privacy by default: data ownership, explicit consent, self-hosting, and no customer data used for model training.

• Actionable analytics: automatic open-text summaries, sentiment classification, segments, and recommendations.

• Publishing freedom: link, website or app embed, QR, email, and offline mode.

• Fair pricing: free start and pay-as-you-go, not per-user charges.

3) First-version scope (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Original drag-and-drop builder, generated from a text description, with Arabic/English and RTL support.

2. Adaptive logic: branching, conditions, and follow-ups that reduce question count.

3. Multi-channel publishing: link, embed, QR, email, and offline kiosk mode.

4. Response collection with explicit consent, bot protection, and rate limits.

5. Analytics: instant summaries, segments, comparisons, CSV/Excel export, and API.

6. AI engine for open-text analysis, summaries, and recommendations.

7. Team workspace: roles, comments, invitations, and Slack, Sheets, and Webhook integrations.

8. Free plan, billing, and initial Docker self-hosting package.

4) Pricing and revenue model

· Plan · Price · Limits ·

· Free · $0 · 3 active surveys, 100 responses/month, basic analytics, platform branding ·

· Starter · $19/month · 1,000 responses/month, branding removal, full export ·

· Growth · $49/month · 5,000 responses/month, AI analytics, advanced branching, integrations ·

· Business · $149/month · 25,000 responses/month, SSO, custom domain, advanced roles ·

· Self-hosting · $149/month or $1,490/year · internal deployment, updates, initial support ·

Revenue: subscriptions, extra AI credits, self-hosting licenses, and setup/training services, with education and nonprofit discounts.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Bilingual SEO: practical guides and an Arabic/English template library targeting search intent.

• Product-led growth: permanent free plan, shareable reports, and a referral program with credits.

• Integration marketplaces: free listings in Slack, Notion, HubSpot, and WordPress to reach new users.

• Communities: Product Hunt, Reddit, Indie Hackers, LinkedIn, and X, with short case studies.

• Academic/nonprofit partnerships: free research plan in exchange for published experiments and templates.

• Arab markets: ready RTL templates, free webinars, and partnerships with bootcamps and local agencies.

• Product transparency: public roadmap, changelog, and open API to attract developers.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• GDPR and CCPA compliance: consent, data processing agreement, retention period, and deletion on request.

• E-marketing laws: prior consent and a clear unsubscribe option.

• AI use: no model-provider training on customer data, and an option to disable smart analytics.

• Protect the trademark and name before launch, and review terms of use and privacy policy.

Technical:

• Embed security: prevent XSS via CSP, tenant isolation, and request rate limiting.

• Anti-bot and fake-response prevention, and email deliverability.

• Scalability and analytics reliability at peak.

• WCAG accessibility and RTL testing across browsers and devices.

• Reliance on AI providers: fallback plan and optional local models.

• Self-hosting support complexity: clear documentation and a simplified deployment package.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Madar

Tagline: Fewer questions, deeper understanding, and faster decisions.
