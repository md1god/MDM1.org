DoseNest — Full Project Plan

From first dose to last pill — track your treatment with peace of mind.

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the launch plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a companion app for GLP-1 medication users (injections and pills)

1) Target audience

• Patients with type 2 diabetes and obesity treated with GLP-1 (weekly injection or daily pills).

• Beginners in treatment during the first 3–6 months (they need reminders, dose tracking, and side-effect logging).

• People who combine injections and pills in a single treatment pattern (many in the current wave).

• Parents / adults monitoring a relative's medication.

• Non-English speakers in the Middle East and Southeast Asia markets (local language support).

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Mixed-dose awareness: supports a weekly injection + daily pills in the same profile, with separate and overlapping reminders.

• Injection site rotation: a simple body map (abdomen/thigh/arm) where each dose is logged and repetition of the site is warned against.

• Estimated drug level curve: a timeline chart that estimates drug accumulation and decline based on a standard half-life (not a substitute for a doctor, but an awareness tool).

• Side effects tied to the dose: logging nausea/constipation/fatigue with their daily timing and their link to the next dose appointment.

• Protein and water notes: because protein deficiency and dehydration are real dangers with this treatment — we remind and we track.

• Shareable summary for the doctor: a simple PDF: adherence, side effects, rotation notes.

3) Scope of the first version (8 tasks maximum)

1. Create medication profile: medication type, form (injection/pill), dose, start date.

2. Smart scheduling: weekly reminders for injections and daily reminders for pills.

3. Adherence logging: "took it / missed it / took it late".

4. Injection site rotation map (abdomen/thigh/arm) with a warning against repetition.

5. Side effect log (severity 1–5 + timing).

6. Estimated drug level curve (simple estimate with a medical disclaimer).

7. Daily tracking: protein (grams) + water (ml).

8. Export a PDF summary to share with the doctor.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free to start: one medication profile, reminders, basic log — no credit card required.

• Pro — $4.9/month or $39/year: advanced curves, multiple pills + injections, PDF export, monthly reports, additional language support.

• Family — $69/year: up to 3 family member profiles (one caregiver for a parent or partner).

• B2B2C later: licensed device for clinics and digital pharmacies (not in the first release).

5) Global launch plan with free channels

1. Short-form content on TikTok/Reels: "how to rotate your injection site" and "how much protein you need with GLP-1" in the target market languages.

2. Support groups on Reddit and Facebook for diabetes and obesity — sharing useful tools without annoying ads.

3. Partnerships with trusted health content creators (micro-influencers) in exchange for free Pro usage.

4. SEO: FAQ pages about GLP-1 pills in Arabic, English, and Indonesian.

5. Weekly email: adherence summary + one tip (free, drives upgrade).

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: describing the drug curve as "estimated" and avoiding any therapeutic claim; compliance with HIPAA/GDPR/PDPL and app store health policies.

• Privacy: sensitive health data — encryption in transit and at rest, permanent deletion on request.

• Medical: an explicit disclaimer that the app is not a substitute for a doctor, and prevention of self-modification of doses.

• Technical: accuracy of half-life values for different drugs; over-reliance on notifications can disable them; maintaining a low-risk database for App Store policies.

• Competitive: ease of copying — protection through local languages and the quality of the rotation and curve experience.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: DoseNest

Marketing tagline: From first dose to last pill — track your treatment with peace of mind.
