Mizan — Complete Project Plan

All your wealth in one scale, staying on your device alone.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the launch plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

Product plan: Personal wealth management app (original version)

1) Target audience

• Individuals seeking financial independence who track their net worth instead of merely recording expenses.

• Employees and freelancers whose income is in multiple currencies and whose assets are spread across several banks and platforms.

• Small investors in stocks and cryptocurrencies who want a single unified picture of their assets.

• Privacy-conscious people who refuse to link their bank accounts or share their data.

• The global English-speaking market first, with Arabic support from day one.

2) Differentiation points (why choose our product)

• Privacy first: all data is stored locally and encrypted on the device, with no obligation to create an account.

• Focus on net worth, not just budgeting: we combine assets, liabilities, and portfolios into a single indicator and timeline.

• AI-powered (optional): explains the numbers and suggests general financial steps without providing "investment advice."

• Native multi-currency support with exchange-rate and price updates, which most free solutions lack.

• Works offline, and free export/import of your data (CSV/JSON) with no lock-in.

• Fast entry experience (Quick-add) that reduces the burden of manual entry that puts users off.

• Free to start with no limits on a single account, which builds higher trust than locked models.

3) Scope of the first version (max 8 tasks)

1. Manual entry of assets and liabilities with automatic net worth calculation.

2. Multi-currency support and daily exchange-rate fetching (storing the last value when the network is down).

3. Tracking investment portfolios (stocks, cryptocurrencies) by entering quantity and price.

4. Recording income and expenses with support for recurring transactions.

5. A dashboard with charts for monthly and yearly net worth trends.

6. Encrypted local storage + manually exported backup.

7. Multi-platform release via Flutter (Android/iOS first, then Web and Desktop).

8. Financial goals (Goals): a net worth target with a simple estimate of the time to reach it.

4) Pricing and profit model

• Free plan (Free forever): unlimited tracking of one account, one currency, basic charts, full export.

• Pro plan (Pro): sync across devices, unlimited accounts and portfolios, multi-currency, advanced analytics, alerts.

• One-time lifetime payment (Lifetime): an option for those who dislike subscriptions, at a fixed price.

• Family plan: multiple seats at a reduced price.

• Ethical policy: no selling of data, and no ads that target sensitive financial data.

5) Global launch plan via free channels

• Launch on Product Hunt and Hacker News (Show HN) with a "privacy first" story.

• Reddit communities: r/personalfinance, r/financialindependence, r/privacy with useful, non-promotional content.

• Publish the repository open source to attract contributors and trust.

• Google Play / App Store / F-Droid stores + search engine optimization (ASO/SEO) with long-tail keywords.

• TikTok/YouTube Shorts with "how to calculate your net worth in a minute" clips.

• FIRE blogs and forums and Telegram/Discord groups, with translation into the most-requested languages.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: avoid phrasing any output as licensed "financial advice"; a clear disclaimer, and compliance with GDPR/CCPA and immediate data deletion.

• Legal: connecting banks via Open Banking/PSD2 requires licenses and accredited intermediaries — we postpone it beyond the first version.

• Legal: exchange-rate and stock price data licenses must be documented accurately.

• Technical: loss of user data — solved via automatic backup and easy export.

• Technical: encryption security and its keys on the device; independent audit is required.

• Technical: the cost of updating prices at a large scale — we rely on caching and reducing calls.

• Technical: scope creep — we adhere to the first-version limits and resist adding features early.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Mizan (ميزان)

Marketing tagline: All your wealth in one scale, staying on your device alone.
