Naqsh — Complete Project Plan

An electronic frame that listens to bird chirps and turns them into nature paintings on an e-ink screen.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a frame that listens to birds and draws them

1) Target audience

• Bird keepers and birdwatching enthusiasts who want to document what visits their garden without carrying equipment.

• Nature lovers in cities seeking a quiet daily connection to the environment.

• Fans of "calm technology" and smart decor who prefer a slow, low-power screen.

• Schools and educators for teaching children bird sounds and scientific observation.

• Nature conservation organizations as a tool for monitoring and contributing to citizen science.

2) Differentiators (why our product)

• Original artistic identity: a watercolor "nature notebook" style, derived from our own machine learning models, not a copy of any existing artwork.

• Fully local processing: sound recognition runs on-device without uploading any recording to the cloud (higher privacy and better legal compliance).

• A living journal: a timeline log with species, confidence, and weather, not just a fleeting image.

• Scalability: regional species packs and additional installable art packs.

• Low power: an e-ink screen that runs on battery for months and stays "always in place".

• Free start: open reference hardware + free firmware, with revenue from packs and services.

3) Scope of the first version (8 tasks)

1. Continuous audio capture via microphone with local voice activity detection (VAD).

2. On-device bird recognition model, initially ~50 local species.

3. Original artwork generation engine in Naturalist style.

4. Display pipeline on e-ink screen (grayscale + dithering + partial refresh).

5. Local database for every detection (species, time, confidence, optional weather).

6. Companion app (BLE/Wi-Fi) to view the journal and set the region and family.

7. A free starter art pack + purchase of one additional pack.

8. OTA updates and full offline operation + reference case and hardware design.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free to start: open firmware and hardware design, plus a free unlimited basic species pack.

• Advanced art and species packs: one-time purchase (~$5–15 per pack).

• Optional cloud subscription (~$3–5/month): journal backup + multi-frame sync + periodic packs.

• Institutional licensing: schools and conservation organizations, and aggregated monitoring reports.

• Hardware sold at a small margin as a ready-made option, with "bring your own device" support.

5) Global rollout plan using free channels

• Publish the open-source firmware on GitHub to attract developers and modders.

• Community launches on Show HN, Product Hunt, and Reddit (r/birding, r/eink, r/DIY).

• Creator content on YouTube and TikTok: "Which bird visited my frame today?"

• Partnerships with bird clubs, local bird societies, and university biology departments.

• Free species packs as bait to attract users, and translated versions for non-English communities.

• Monitoring data (with user consent) for citizen science as a source of positive publicity.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: recording and privacy laws (GDPR and consent laws) — Solution: local processing, no storage of human voice, and immediate deletion of recordings.

• Data licensing: verify the licenses of model training recordings and use only free sources.

• Hardware compliance: FCC/CE certification and electrical safety.

• Technical: recognition accuracy in city noise, and false negatives.

• Hardware: power consumption for always-on listening, e-ink refresh limitations and ghosting, microphone quality.

• Modeling: model size on an embedded device, quality of generated art, and its evolution offline.

• Business: supply chain and hardware cost, and brand protection.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Naqsh

Tagline: An electronic frame that listens to bird chirps and turns them into nature paintings on an e-ink screen.
