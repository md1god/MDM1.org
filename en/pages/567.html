Nefes — Complete Project Plan

Quiet growth for your channel, without exhausting you.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, monetization model, expansion plan, and risks. The project is under construction and not yet launched.

1) Target Audience

• Small and medium-sized business owners: They don't have a full marketing team and need a constant presence on platforms.

• Individual creators: They want to expand to multiple platforms without spending hours daily on production.

• Local professionals: Such as restaurants, shops, and trainers who lack experience in designing marketing content.

2) Points of Differentiation (Why Choose Our Product)

• Focus on local voice: Instead of generic templates, the product learns each client's brand style and regional dialect.

• Limited smart automation: It suggests content and displays it for approval before publishing, giving control without overwhelming the user.

• Gradual brand building: Focus on growing one channel first efficiently, then expand automatically based on actual results.

• Cost priority: Uses efficient models with consumption only when needed to reduce costs for free users.

3) Scope of First Version (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. Define identity: Enter brand description, audience, and tone of voice.

2. Daily suggestion: Generate one ready-to-publish post idea based on goal and identity.

3. Content creation: Generate a simple short image or video with post text and suggested hashtags.

4. Approval flow: Simple interface to view suggestion, approve, edit, or reject.

5. Smart scheduling: Choose the best time to publish based on channel patterns.

6. Safe publishing: Connect one channel and publish only with explicit approval.

7. Basic metrics: Display views and engagement for the last 7 days.

8. Smart alerts: Notify user when tone or posting frequency needs adjustment.

4) Pricing and Profit Model

| Plan | Price | Features |

| :-- | :-- | :-- |

| Free | $0 | One channel, daily suggestion, publish with approval, basic statistics. |

| Basic | $9 / month | Two channels, automatic scheduling, optimized smart suggestions, weekly reports. |

| Professional | $29 / month | Up to 5 channels, priority processing, advanced insights, limited support. |

Primary revenue source: Recurring subscriptions, while keeping most production features available in the free plan to ease the experience.

5) Global Expansion Plan with Free Channels

• Free tool lists: Add the product to open marketing tool directories with a short description and simple screenshots.

• Educational content: Create short lessons (text or video) explaining how to grow one channel without a team, and publish them on platforms read by business owners.

• Direct outreach: Participate in small business communities helpfully, answering questions instead of direct advertising.

• Referral program: Simple referral system that rewards users who invite friends with access to additional features for a limited time.

• Search optimization: Optimize the landing page for long-tail keywords related to "grow a channel without time".

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

| Type | Risk | Preventive Action |

| :-- | :-- | :-- |

| Legal | Rights of generated models or content | Use models with clear licensing, and clarify that the user is responsible for using content in accordance with platform laws. |

| Legal | Platform connection policies and user data | Obtain specific permissions, store minimum data, and follow strict privacy policies. |

| Technical | Sudden changes in API interfaces | Build a flexible integration layer with the ability to quickly disable a specific feature when needed. |

| Technical | Usage costs during rapid growth | Monitor daily consumption, intelligently limit free usage, and prioritize efficient models first. |

| Technical | Misuse of tone or content | Mandatory approval system in first version, simple filtering rules, and ability to suspend account in case of severe violation. |

7) Suggested Name and Marketing Slogan

Suggested name: Nefes

Marketing slogan: Quiet growth for your channel, without exhausting you.
