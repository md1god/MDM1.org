MyClinic — Full Project Plan

Your digital clinic works from day one, with no fees.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the rollout plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

New product plan in the field of medical records and remote care

1) Target audience

• Small and medium clinics (general practice, dentistry, dermatology, psychotherapy) in emerging markets.

• Primary care centers and pharmacies linked to clinics.

• Independent physicians and mobile medical teams that need remote consultations.

• Small companies that offer health packages to employees and want a system without high licensing costs.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Genuinely modular system: each module (appointments, follow-up, prescriptions, billing) is enabled on demand without loading the entire system.

• Mobile-first operations: an easy interface on the phone for doctor and patient, with support for weak connectivity (work offline then sync).

• Privacy by design: full data encryption, a clear audit log, and local or cloud hosting chosen by the customer.

• A genuinely free start: a free version for individuals and small clinics with no credit card, with gradual upgrades as they grow.

• Fast customization: ready-made templates for different specialties and documented APIs for integration with labs and pharmacies.

3) Scope of the first release (8 tasks maximum)

1. Basic electronic patient record with a brief medical history.

2. Appointment scheduling and direct booking over the web.

3. Simple video consultations with session notes.

4. Printable and verifiable electronic prescriptions.

5. Secure login with roles (doctor, staff, patient).

6. Encryption of data in transit and at rest, plus an audit log.

7. A free cloud demo that can be launched with a single click.

8. Written documentation for the integration interfaces (API) with one integration example.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free forever: one doctor, limited patients, core modules — for quick entry and community building.

• Professional: a monthly subscription per clinic (unlimited users + additional modules).

• Enterprise: pricing by scale, with private hosting, dedicated support, and a service level agreement.

• Additional revenue: a marketplace of modules and templates, paid integrations with labs and insurance companies, and installation and training services.

5) Global rollout plan using free channels

1. Public repository: publish the code with clear documentation and a ready-to-run first release, and attract the earliest contributions.

2. SEO and content: a blog and explainer videos in Arabic, English, and Spanish that answer common clinic questions.

3. Community: an open forum and a chat channel, plus an ambassador program among physicians in different countries.

4. Partnerships: cooperation with medical schools and developer communities to hold free events and workshops.

5. Add-ons directory: a page showcasing the free modules to attract users and then convert them to the paid plans.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: health data protection regulations differ between countries (such as HIPAA, GDPR, and local controls); they may impose restrictions on storing data outside the country, and sensitive medical data must be redacted from audit logs and analytics databases.

• Legal: electronic prescriptions and remote diagnosis are not allowed in some countries for some specialties — the feature must be evaluated market by market.

• Technical: time-sensitive synchronization with lab and insurance systems may fail or be delayed; retries and clear error logs are required.

• Technical: a malicious attack or a data breach destroys trust; periodic penetration tests, precise permissions, and incident responses are required.

• Operational: over-reliance on community contributions may slow down critical fixes; a support and vulnerability response policy is required.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: MyClinic

Tagline: Your digital clinic works from day one, with no fees.
