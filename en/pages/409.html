Brickora — Full Project Plan

Build your ideas piece by piece, and share them with the world in seconds.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the launch plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: an app for building virtual models from construction blocks

1) Target Audience

• Beginners: children (8+ with their parents) and beginners who want to build simple models quickly without complex instructions.

• Designers and hobbyists: people who make models to display or 3D-print them.

• Teachers: math, engineering, and arts teachers who want free in-class educational tools.

• Sharing communities: creators who make tutorials and video clips for building projects.

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• Simple drag-and-drop interface: first model done in under two minutes, with a "simplified" mode for kids.

• Works in the browser with no install: WebGL/WASM technology, with cloud project sync.

• Open piece library: neutral non-commercial pieces + upload reference images as a background layer for copy-by-eye.

• Multiple exports: transparent images, STL/3MF files for 3D printing, and step-by-step build instructions (PDF).

• Real-time collaboration: share a link and edit together, suitable for classrooms and communities.

• Offline mode with optional local saving, and a lightweight load suitable for low-end devices.

3) Scope of the First Version (8 Tasks Maximum)

1. 3D drag-and-drop editor: add/rotate/delete pieces, with smart grid snapping.

2. Basic piece library (100–200 pieces) with color-neutral shapes and sizes.

3. Colors, layers, and element freezing, with undo/redo.

4. Local saving + transparent PNG image export.

5. Simple STL export for 3D printing.

6. Instant landing page and email-only login.

7. PWA version that works offline and can be installed on phones.

8. Ready-made templates (car, house, rocket) the user can start from immediately.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free to start: the editor, the basic library, saving, and image export — no credit card required.

• Plus plan ($5–7/month): unlimited cloud sync, STL/3MF export, advanced piece library, optional watermark removal.

• Education plan ($29/year per teacher): classes of up to 30 students and a dashboard.

• Additional revenue: paid templates and piece packs from designers (70/30 model in favor of the creator).

5) Global Launch Plan Using Free Channels

• Short video: YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram Reels — building challenges and timed challenges.

• Teacher partnerships: Telegram and Discord groups for teachers, and a free educational forum.

• Extension stores: publish the extension/app in free browsers and app stores.

• Open Dev: an open repository for the SDK command-line interface and APIs, and multilingual SEO articles.

• Monthly community contests with symbolic prizes (a free paid-plan month).

• ProductHunt and free design-tool portals at launch.

• Collaboration with content creators: small accounts that create building content in exchange for a free product mention.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

Legal:

• Completely avoid mentioning any commercial brand name of any block company, and use "compatible building pieces" as a general description.

• Ensure the piece design does not infringe on patents/protected designs; consult a lawyer before launching in markets such as the US/EU.

• Reference image rights: allow uploading only user images and do not distribute them.

Technical:

• Performance on low-end devices: simplify graphics, check FPS, and test on older phones.

• Data loss when sync fails: a local file system with a conflict-resolution solution (CRDT/snapshots).

• 3D exports not printable: automatic wall-thickness and hole checks.

• 3D editor development speed: stick to a small scope and ready-made graphics libraries (Three.js/WebGPU).

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Tagline

Proposed name: Brickora

Marketing tagline: Build your ideas piece by piece, and share them with the world in seconds.
