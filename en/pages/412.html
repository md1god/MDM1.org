Madar — Complete Project Plan

«Your mission starts here — a free autopilot that evolves with you.»

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the rollout plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

New product plan: an autopilot platform for drones

1) Target audience

• Small businesses in agriculture, aerial surveying, and local delivery.

• Universities, robotics clubs, and engineering students.

• Small drone manufacturers (OEMs) who need ready-made control software.

• Municipal authorities (monitoring, disaster response, mapping surveys).

2) Differentiators

• The core is free and open, with revenue from cloud services rather than from software.

• Web mission planner with drag and drop, no need to write code.

• Full in-browser simulator to test scenarios before real flight.

• Arabic and English interface and simplified documentation (rare in this field).

• Works offline (Offline-first) and relies on any common controller board.

3) Scope of the first release (8 tasks maximum)

1. A control core that maintains stability and executes flight commands (Multirotor and Plane).

2. Web mission planner: drawing the path, points of interest, and altitude limits.

3. Browser simulator for testing and training without a real aircraft.

4. Mobile app for takeoff/landing and live monitoring.

5. Essential safety: return-to-home on signal loss and motor/battery fault diagnostics.

6. A payload library for cameras and sprayers through standard ports.

7. Small fleet management dashboard: mission maps, flight logs, and analytics.

8. Arabic/English documentation with ready-made project examples for universities.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free for individuals and limited manufacturers: core + simulator + one project.

• Pro — 29$/month: fleet of up to 10 aircraft, analytics, priority support.

• Business — 199$/month: unlimited fleet, analytical reports, API integration, maintenance contracts.

• OEM licensing: an embedded license for drone manufacturers + a margin from selling certified hardware.

• Additional revenue: a marketplace for add-ons and paid flight plans from developers.

5) Global rollout plan using free channels

• Training videos and flight tests on YouTube and TikTok.

• Open repository + Discord for the developer and student community.

• SEO articles in Arabic and English about «the first free autopilot that supports Arabic».

• Participation in university competitions and Drones meetups to gain the first 100 users.

• Posts on X/LinkedIn and sharing open projects on Reddit and Hackaday.

• Telegram and Facebook channels in the agriculture and delivery markets.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: civil aviation regulations (FAA/EASA/the local authority) may require a license for commercial operation; and technology export may restrict some countries.

• Technical: safety responsibility on failures; requires intensive field testing and rapid security updates.

• Security: possibility of signal interception; requires encryption and device allowlists.

• Commercial: a saturated open-autilot market with competition; we focus on the Arabic user experience and the free price.

• Operational: dependence on a replaceable hardware supply chain (sensors/processors).

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Madar (مدارات)

Tagline: «Your mission starts here — a free autopilot that evolves with you.»
