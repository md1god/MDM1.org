Najma — Full Project Plan

Turning moments of happiness into real reviews that grow with every visit.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, expansion plan, and risks. The project is under development and has not been launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Small and medium-sized restaurants: especially those with no dedicated marketing team.

• Cafes and fast-food outlets: with high daily foot traffic of new and returning customers.

• New restaurants: looking to build their reputation quickly with zero budget.

2) Key Differentiators (Why choose our product)

• Focus only on happy customers: a very short satisfaction survey to gauge happiness before asking for a review.

• Smart local language: AI-powered thank-you messages tailored to each country's language automatically.

• Extremely simple: no technical experience needed, works directly via QR code only.

• Privacy-first: does not force customers to enter personal data, and explicitly asks for consent before sending any message.

3) Scope of First Version (Max 8 tasks)

• [ ] Restaurant dashboard: simple interface to create an account and manage the tool.

• [ ] QR generator: create a unique QR code for each restaurant with one click.

• [ ] Satisfaction survey: only two questions with quick buttons.

• [ ] Smart review routing: redirect only satisfied customers to the review page, and gently thank dissatisfied customers.

• [ ] AI thank-you messages: generate a custom thank-you message based on the customer's response.

• [ ] Send via link or SMS: support both channels with the option to disable either.

• [ ] Language and country settings: automatically detect language based on restaurant settings.

• [ ] Free plan to start: full access to core features with no subscription.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

· Plan · Price · Description ·

· Free · $0 / month · Unlimited QR codes, satisfaction survey, and smart review routing. ·

· Growth · $9 / month · Basic analytics, more SMS messages, and advanced AI thank-you templates. ·

· Multi-branch · $19 / month · Support for multiple branches, consolidated reports, and priority support. ·

Revenue model: only monthly subscriptions, no commission on customers.

5) Global Expansion Plan with Free Channels

• Local SEO: create city-specific pages targeting small restaurant owners.

• Short educational content: 1-minute videos explaining how to increase reviews with minimal effort.

• Business owner groups: actively participate in restaurant communities worldwide by providing value before promotion.

• Open business directories: add the tool to free restaurant marketing directories.

• Free ambassador program: incentivize satisfied restaurant owners to share the tool in exchange for free benefits.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

· Type · Risk · Proposed Solution ·

· Legal · SMS laws and consent requirements in some countries · Request explicit consent before sending, and comply with local laws in each country. ·

· Legal · Data protection and privacy laws · Minimize data collection, do not store phone numbers longer than necessary, and have a clear privacy policy. ·

· Technical · High SMS provider costs at the start · Rely on links first, enable SMS only with daily limits, and use the best available rates. ·

· Technical · Abuse or unsolicited messages · Limit sending to only one request per visit, and add a mechanism to report abuse. ·

7) Suggested Name and Slogan

Suggested name: Najma

Slogan: Turning moments of happiness into real reviews that grow with every visit.
