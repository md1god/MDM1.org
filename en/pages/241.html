Seerah — Complete Project Plan

Your CV and cover letter are auto-tailored to each specific job, ATS-compatible from the first try.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the go-to-market plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

An original product plan: a smart tool to help job seekers automatically tailor their applications

The idea in brief: The job seeker pastes the job description and their CV, and immediately gets an ATS-optimized CV, a tailored cover letter, a keyword gap analysis, and a dashboard to track their applications. It is the job seeker's counterpart to the systems that employers buy.

1) Target audience

• New job seekers and recent graduates who lack experience writing ATS-compatible CVs.

• Professionals applying to dozens of jobs weekly who need fast tailoring.

• Job seekers in multilingual markets (Arabic/English) and remote workers seeking international opportunities.

• Career coaches and graduates at career development centers (secondary users).

2) Points of differentiation (why choose our product)

• Per-job tailoring: the CV and cover letter are rewritten for each posting instead of one static file.

• ATS-compatible by design: clean formatting and machine-readable text without tables or complex images.

• Explicit gap analysis: it shows the required keywords you are missing and suggests how to honestly address them without exaggeration.

• Privacy first: temporary data processing and automatic deletion, without selling user data.

• Dual-language support and genuine Arabic quality, not literal translation.

• A free start with no paywall blocking the user from seeing value first.

• The engine itself can run locally/partly open source to build trust and lower operating cost.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Upload/paste the CV and extract its text (PDF/DOCX).

2. Paste the job description and clean it of noise.

3. Match analysis and extraction of the list of required keywords and skills.

4. Gap report: what you have, what you are missing, and what needs highlighting.

5. Generate a smart CV with ATS-safe wording (unified template + reordering of experience).

6. Generate a tailored cover/introduction letter for each job from two or three templates.

7. A simple tracking dashboard for application status (sent/interview/rejected) for each job.

8. Export the final file in PDF and DOCX formats ready to send.

4) Pricing and profit model

• Free to start: 3 tailorings per month, full gap analysis, PDF export with a light watermark.

• Pro plan (monthly/yearly): unlimited tailorings, clean DOCX export, expanded tracking, a version history for each job.

• Career Coach plan (for coaches and universities): multiple seats, a student follow-up dashboard, per-group pricing.

• Additional revenue sources: optional paid templates and partnerships with employment centers — without selling data.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Practical SEO content: "How a CV passes ATS systems" in Arabic and English on a free blog.

• Presence on free product platforms (Product Hunt, relevant Reddit, LinkedIn/recruitment groups).

• Free lead-magnet tools: a quick match checker with no sign-up that generates automatic sharing.

• Partnerships with universities and career development offices (free seats in exchange for source mention).

• Referral program: one free Pro month for every friend who signs up.

• Brief documented success stories (case studies) published as text and short video.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal/privacy

• Protection of CV and description data (GDPR/CCPA): automatic deletion, encryption, and explicit consent.

• Terms of use of job sources: no scraping of sites without permission; reliance on user pasting instead of automatic pulling.

• Misleading claims: promising "guaranteed acceptance" is prohibited; the wording is "improves the likelihood."

• Intellectual property rights: formats and templates written from scratch, avoiding literal imitation.

Technical

• Model hallucination and fabricated skills: strict constraints prevent inventing any experience not present in the original CV.

• ATS variation in reading formats: rely on simple templates and test them across multiple engines.

• Prompt injection via the job description: sanitize the text and set clear model boundaries.

• LLM inference cost: caching, smaller models for simple tasks, and a usage cap in the free plan.

• Arabic language credibility: template review and periodic quality assessment.
