Bayanly — Complete Project Plan

Upload your file and ask in your own language, and get accurate answers and charts in seconds.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: The interactive AI data analyst

1) Target audience

• Small and medium business owners who have data (sales, inventory, customers) but are not proficient in SQL or advanced Excel.

• Business analysts and marketing officers who need quick answers and reports without waiting for the data team.

• Researchers, students, and journalists who deal with CSV/Excel files and need quick exploration.

• Non-technical teams that want to upload a file, ask in Arabic or English, and get a direct result.

2) Points of distinction (why choose our product)

• Full transparency: every answer is attached with the actual query executed on the data, so the user sees how we arrived at the number.

• Authentic Arabic support: understanding questions in Modern Standard Arabic and common dialects, with an Arabic-first interface.

• Privacy: file processing within the session, with the option to delete data automatically immediately without permanent storage.

• Smart automatic charting: suggests appropriate charts according to column types and distribution, not just manual buttons.

• Generous free start: no credit card required and no software download.

• Works offline after the first download (optional local mode) for those who want maximum privacy.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Upload a CSV/Excel file and read it automatically with detection of column types and simple errors.

2. Ask a question in natural language and get an accurate answer as a number or small table.

3. Display the executed query step by step for verification.

4. Generate automatic charts (bars, lines, pie, distribution).

5. Automatically compute performance indicators (sum, average, growth, highest/lowest).

6. Data cleaning: remove duplicates, handle empty values, unify formats.

7. Export results and charts to an image or PDF.

8. Local history of previous questions and reuse them.

4) Pricing and profit model

• Free to start: files up to a certain size, and a limited number of questions per month.

• Paid plan (monthly): larger files, unlimited questions, advanced export, saving projects.

• Team plan: multiple seats, collaboration, permissions, integration with cloud storage tools.

• Additional income: paid API, custom integration, and enterprise support with annual contracts.

• Principle: the free version suffices for light individual use to ensure organic growth.

5) Global distribution plan with free channels

• Educational content: short lessons and short videos on YouTube/TikTok showing "upload file → question → answer" within seconds.

• Product and developer communities: showcasing the product on product discovery platforms and tech communities with a live interactive demo.

• SEO: articles explaining CSV/Excel analysis in Arabic and English targeting long-tail questions.

• Content marketing on Reddit and LinkedIn: realistic use cases for local businesses.

• Partnerships: business accelerators, universities, startup incubators.

• Referral program: grant extra question credits in exchange for inviting colleagues.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Technical — answer accuracy: the model may err in generating the query; mitigation: result verification, clear limits, and always displaying the query.

• Technical — large data: slowness or high memory consumption; mitigation: sample processing, file splitting, and efficient query techniques.

• Technical — multiple file formats: unclean files or mixed columns; mitigation: a strong cleaning layer and clear error messages.

• Legal — data privacy: data protection laws differ between countries; mitigation: transparency of the privacy policy, automatic deletion, and the local processing option.

• Legal — content ownership: the user uploads data that may be sensitive; mitigation: not using user data for training without explicit permission.

• Legal — intellectual property rights: we write all the code and design from scratch, with license auditing for the libraries used.

7) Proposed name and marketing slogan

Proposed name: Bayanly (بَيَانلي)

Marketing slogan: Upload your file and ask in your language, and get accurate answers and charts in seconds.
