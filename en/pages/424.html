KeyLingo — Complete Project Plan

Write in Chinese and learn your second language with every word.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the rollout plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: A keyboard that teaches languages while you type

1) Target audience

• Chinese-speaking learners who type in Pinyin and want to strengthen a second language (mainly English) during everyday typing.

• Overseas Chinese language students (HSK), and e-commerce and technical support staff who use Chinese and English together.

• Windows/macOS users dissatisfied with current heavyweight or ad-laden keyboards.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Translation appears next to every candidate instantly with no extra click, with automatic saving of the word into spaced repetition (SRS).

• Lightweight engine built in Rust, using under 100MB of memory, and working offline to protect privacy.

• Personalized learning: it understands your typing pattern and suggests words at your language level, with a keyboard for multiple languages (Japanese/Korean later).

• Daily statistics export (new/reviewed words) to connect learning with tools such as Anki.

3) Scope of the first release (8 tasks max)

1. Pinyin core → Chinese candidates with local-context ordering.

2. English translation display next to each candidate, toggleable/hideable.

3. Desktop version (Windows/macOS) with lightweight installation.

4. Encrypted personal database for saved words.

5. Simple in-app spaced repetition.

6. Full offline mode + optional sync.

7. Weekly statistics and progress report.

8. Documentation, packaging (installer), and a free landing page.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free to start: core engine + 500 saved words + basic statistics.

• Pro monthly (~$5) or yearly (~$40): unlimited words, advanced SRS, reports, sync, extra language packs.

• B2B: licensing for schools/companies (~$3/user/month).

• Optional language-cycle content sponsorships (non-intrusive, optional).

5) Global rollout plan with free channels

• Reddit/Discord/Zhihu, HSK communities, and Chinese-learning platforms, with an early trial version.

• Short videos (TikTok/YouTube Shorts) showing typing + instant translation.

• SEO for comparison articles "best keyboards for learners" + a multilingual landing page.

• Affiliate program with language teachers on YouTube and Moodle/Coursera communities.

• Delivered as open-core for the core engine to build trust and free contributions.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: avoid infringing trademarks/patents of input engines; ensure translation dictionary licensing; comply with GDPR/CCPA for storing typing data (encrypted locally).

• Technical: multi-dialect Pinyin accuracy (zhuyin/mixed input), keyboard performance on legacy systems, input method switching on macOS/Windows, data protection against leaks.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: KeyLingo

Tagline: Write in Chinese and learn your second language with every word.
