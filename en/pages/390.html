Mawja — Complete Project Plan

Find your free seat before it disappears.

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Individual travelers who book award flights (Miles & Points) and want free seats (Award Seats) without browsing dozens of airline websites.

• Members of multiple loyalty programs (e.g., Flying Blue, LifeMiles, Miles+Bonus) who use more than one program.

• Families looking for 4 or more seats on the same route, making manual searches impossible.

• Travel planning consultants and points brokers who need flexibility in dates and routes.

• Frequent travelers (Digital Nomads) with flexible dates and destinations.

2) Unique Selling Points (Why choose our product)

• Parallel search across dozens of loyalty programs at once instead of switching from site to site.

• Smart filters: number of passengers, cabin class, alternative routes (Nearby Airports), and flexible date window.

• Instant alerts via Email + Telegram + Web Push when a seat matching criteria becomes available.

• "Date Flexibility" dashboard showing the cheapest day of the month visually.

• Integration with popular booking systems via ready-to-use search links, without booking on the user's behalf (non-intermediary model).

• Fully Arabic interface + support for both Gregorian and Hijri calendars, which is often missing for this audience.

• Free to start: at least one search and one alert with no credit card required.

3) Scope of First Version (max 8 tasks)

1. Visual identity design: logo, colors, Typography system, Arabic/English interface (RTL/LTR).

2. Basic data model: routes, dates, loyalty programs, cabins, and number of available seats.

3. Frontend search engine with basic filters and instant results.

4. Data collection/update layer (Backend Jobs) from public sources or API providers, with caching to protect quota.

5. Alerts system: create "saved searches" + send email and browser alerts.

6. User dashboard: saved searches, active alerts, and search history.

7. Landing page + sign-in (Magic Link) + free/paid plan.

8. Basic analytics (Analytics) and result quality measurement (timeliness and accuracy).

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free: unlimited searches, 1 saved search, 1 alert, daily update delay.

• Plus — $9/month: 20 saved searches, instant alerts, date flexibility, multi-channel alerts.

• Pro — $29/month: unlimited + API access + advanced Telegram alerts + nearby routes + priority support.

• B2B: email package for travel consultants ($59–$149/month) for multiple clients.

• Additional revenue: clearly disclosed referral links to wallets and welcome programs, without selling user data.

5) Global Distribution Plan with Free Channels

• Arabic/English SEO content: "How to book a free seat with points" + tables of popular routes.

• Loyalty program comparison articles and step-by-step guides for each program.

• Reddit, FlyerTalk/MileageRun forums + Facebook and Telegram groups for points.

• Short videos (TikTok/Reels/YouTube Shorts) showing live search results before/after.

• Free weekly newsletter: "Top 5 free award seats this week".

• Partnerships with Arabic and global travel content creators via a free referral program.

• Product Hunt + Slack/Discord groups for early adopters.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal: compliance with airline websites and loyalty program terms of use; avoid direct use of their logos without license; privacy policy (GDPR/CCPA) and explicit consent to store alerts; disclaimer that availability can change instantly.

• Technical: interface changes (Scraping) or quota limits (Rate Limits) ⇒ prioritize official/third-party APIs + caching + alerts on data quality drops.

• Accuracy: outdated data destroys trust ⇒ measure update time and valid result ratio as key metrics.

• Operational: provider call costs ⇒ gradual plan scaling based on usage.

• Competition: large player entry ⇒ differentiation via Arabic market focus, flexibility, and price.

7) Proposed Name and Tagline

Proposed name: Mawja

Tagline: Find your free seat before it disappears.
