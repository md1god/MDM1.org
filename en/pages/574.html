لُبَاب — Complete Project Plan

A local agent layer that makes your browser understand your context and complete your repetitive tasks securely and transparently.

Implementation plan: idea, audience, product, revenue model, launch plan, and risks. Under construction; not yet launched.

1) Target audience

• Knowledge workers in browsers: analysts, researchers, consultants, operations staff, content creators.

• Freelancers and small teams (5–50) repeating tasks across apps who need lightweight automation without technical setup.

• Arabic-speaking users needing bilingual, RTL experience; global products are English-first.

• Privacy-sensitive teams unwilling to upload full context to closed cloud services.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• We do not replace the browser: an extension layer in current browsers plus a local companion, lowering switching costs and working with existing tabs and apps.

• User-consented unified context: understands pages, files, and connected apps, with per-site permissions and limits on what the agent sees.

• Transparency before execution: action preview, audit log, undo; no hidden clicks; no financial or sensitive actions without approval.

• Privacy first: local processing or the user's own API key; no data sales or training on user data.

• Reusable recipes: turn repeated steps into a reviewable, shareable recipe, not a transient chat.

• Arabic and English from the start: RTL interface, mixed-content understanding, and sources in both languages.

• Free start, no lock-in: local model or user's key; recipes and data exportable.

3) First-version scope (max 8 tasks)

1. Tab summary/comparison: unified brief from multiple pages with citations and source links.

2. Multi-source research and verification: collect results, compare claims, separate fact from uncertainty.

3. Extract data to a table: turn page tables or lists into CSV or Google Sheets after preview.

4. Draft writing: email, post, or report from page content; save draft only, never auto-send.

5. Recurring automation recipes: record steps on allowed sites, then run with prior review and clear limits.

6. Page to task: create a task, reminder, or card in Notion or Calendar with the context link.

7. Document Q&A: summarize and question PDFs and open browser documents with citations.

8. Control/security center: action log, per-site permissions, preview before execution, and undo.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free forever: 100 actions/month, 3 recipes, 7-day history; local model or user's API key.

• Pro: $12/month or $96/year; 2,000 actions, unlimited recipes, sync, 90-day history.

• Team: $29/user/month; shared workspace, permissions, unified recipe library, admin support.

• Enterprise: custom pricing; private/on-prem, DPA, security audit, priority support.

• Additional revenue: 20% on recipe/connector marketplace; team customization; no data sales/ads.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Bilingual launch in Chrome and Edge stores first, then Firefox; clear landing page and simplified privacy policy.

• Launch on Product Hunt, Hacker News, Indie Hackers, and BetaList, with a “preview before execution” demo.

• Free, shareable recipe library and practical SEO pages such as “how to summarize multiple tabs” or “extract a table to Sheets.”

• Educational content on YouTube, LinkedIn, and X; free university and tech-community workshops.

• In-product growth loop: recipe links, colleague invites, public template gallery; English, then Arabic.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Site terms: automation may violate some policies; solution: allowed sites, user-initiated actions, respect robots/rate limits, no bypassing protections.

• Privacy and compliance: GDPR/CCPA risks; solution: data minimization, local processing by default, DPAs, no training on data.

• Extension store policies: broad permissions may be rejected; solution: least privilege, clear disclosure, pre-launch security review.

• Fragile interfaces: DOM changes may break recipes; solution: semantic selectors, failure self-detection, visual fallback, safe failure with alerts.

• Prompt injection: untrusted page content; solution: isolate page instructions, treat content as data only, explicit confirmation for sensitive actions.

• Account and data security: no password storage, use OAuth, encrypt tokens, audit log.

• Cost, latency, compatibility: local models for simple tasks, smart routing, caching, Chromium first before expanding.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: لُبَاب

Tagline: A local agent layer that makes your browser understand your context and complete your repetitive tasks securely and transparently.
