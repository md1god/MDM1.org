Mirsat — Complete Project Plan

An incident response automation platform that connects your tools and executes your plans in seconds without complexity.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• DevOps, SRE, and Platform Engineering teams at SaaS companies, banks, and e-commerce businesses.

• IT teams at mid-sized companies that lack an on-call team or a complete internal platform.

• Managed service providers (MSPs) managing multiple client environments.

• On-call engineers who need fast, repeatable incident response without writing fragile scripts.

2) Differentiators (Why choose our product)

• A deterministic rules engine + visual scenario builder combining simplicity and precise control.

• Simulation and replay mode to test automation against real events before enabling it.

• Policy-as-code guardrails that prevent arbitrary execution and enforce approvals and limits.

• Flexible deployment: managed cloud or self-hosted within the customer's network.

• Free, fast start in minutes, with no forced sales or licensing complexity.

• Complete audit log and traceability for every decision and execution, with support for multiple teams.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Receive events via Webhook and API and normalize their format.

2. Core rules engine: trigger, conditions, and actions.

3. Visual scenario builder supporting sequencing, branching, retries, and timeouts.

4. Initial integration pack: Slack, PagerDuty, GitHub, Jira, Kubernetes, Prometheus, HTTP, and email.

5. Secrets management and integration with common secrets managers.

6. Complete execution log with manual replay and simulation.

7. Basic multi-user permissions and separate workspaces.

8. Quick installation via Docker Compose and Helm, with a simple CLI.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free forever: 3 users, 1,000 executions per month, and community support.

• Team: $29 per month, including 5 users and 10,000 executions.

• Business: $199 per month, with SSO, advanced permissions, and 100,000 executions.

• Enterprise: custom pricing for self-hosting and SLA-backed support.

• Revenue sources: subscriptions, enterprise support, training and certification, and managed deployment services.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Open-source the core on GitHub and build a contributor community.

• Launch on Product Hunt, Hacker News, and Reddit (r/devops and r/sre).

• Educational content on Dev.to and YouTube and an indexable documentation guide.

• Free ready-made templates for every supported tool to attract long-tail search.

• Discord community and public support, with weekly Q&A sessions.

• Participate in DevOps conferences through speakers and volunteers, and collaborate with open-source monitoring tools.

• Transparent comparison pages and local editions in Arabic and English.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: Ensure code originality and licensing, avoid using proprietary trademarks, comply with GDPR and customer data protection, and review the licenses of third-party integrations.

• Technical: Changes to external tool interfaces, event duplication and lack of idempotency, security of action execution, tenant isolation, provider rate limits, and scalability under high load.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Mirsat

Tagline: The incident response automation platform that connects your tools and executes your plans in seconds without complexity.
