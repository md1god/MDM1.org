زينار — Complete Project Plan

Read only what matters, without distraction.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, monetization model, launch plan, and risks. The project is under development and not yet launched.

1) Target Audience

• Daily readers: those who want to follow multiple news or blog sources in one place.

• Researchers and students: those who need to organize their academic or technical sources without distraction.

• Privacy-conscious users: those who prefer a reading-only tool without tracking or ads.

2) Points of Distinction (Why Choose Our Product)

• Full focus on reading: an interface completely free of ad suggestions or forced discovery algorithms.

• Focus mode: an option that removes unnecessary elements to read articles with full concentration.

• Privacy first: no reading data is collected, and all settings remain local as much as possible.

• Fast and lightweight: runs smoothly even with hundreds of sources, without lag or clutter.

• Full user control: the user chooses each source themselves, with no predefined categories imposed.

3) Scope of the First Version (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. Add sources: the ability to add source links manually.

2. Article list: a clear and organized display of new articles.

3. Clean reader: display articles in clutter-free text for easy reading.

4. Mark as read/unread: simple tracking of what the user has read.

5. Save for later: add an article to a separate list.

6. Filter by source: display articles from a specific source only when needed.

7. Quick search: search within the titles of saved or displayed articles.

8. Simple display settings: change font size or reading style as desired.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free to start: all features of the first version are completely free.

• Optional freemium model: in the future, an optional paid feature such as sync between devices may be added, while keeping most features free.

• Ad-free: no ads will be introduced under any plan, as this is a core commitment of the product.

5) Global Launch Plan with Free Channels

• Simple landing page: create a single English page that clearly explains the problem and solution.

• Product Hunt: a free launch with a short description focused on "reading without distraction".

• Reading and tech communities: provide helpful contributions to relevant forums without direct promotion.

• Search engine optimization: target the term "read sources in one place" through free educational content.

• Open tool lists: add the product to free tool lists when the opportunity arises.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal risks:

• Linking to sources: avoid republishing full protected content without permission, and limit to displaying links and the text shown for the intended purpose of reading.

• Privacy: avoid any user tracking system or selling user data, and adhere to the simplest protection practices.

• Technical risks:

• Changing source formats: source structures may change, requiring simple updates to processing.

• Blocking or restriction: some sources may limit requests, so lightweight and respectful requests must be used.

• Performance with many sources: the design must include light and efficient updates from the start to avoid slowdowns.

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Slogan

Proposed name: زينار

Marketing slogan: Read only what matters, without distraction.
