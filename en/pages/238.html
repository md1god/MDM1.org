Clipora — Complete Project Plan

Turn any idea into a ready vertical drama in minutes.

Complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Content creators: those who want to produce micro-drama without directing or filming experience.

• Small brands: for companies looking for quick and simple promotional content.

• Educators and intellectuals: to publish educational or awareness stories in an attractive format.

• Non-English-speaking communities: focusing on local languages to fill the existing gap.

2) Points of distinction (why choose our product)

• Focus on local languages: broad support for non-English languages while maintaining their natural style.

• Greater user control: ability to edit scenes and scripts before production directly in the browser.

• Free-to-start model: daily free credit to encourage experimentation without any upfront cost.

• Enhanced character memory: a system that ensures character appearance and costumes remain consistent across all episodes of the series.

• Production speed: turning an idea into a ready episode in a short time with the fewest number of steps.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Idea input: a simple field to enter the idea in one line.

2. Series story generator: create the story summary and episode map with suspense points.

3. Character editor: create and edit the cast while maintaining their visual identity.

4. Scene editor: turn the story into individual scenes with a script for each shot.

5. Pre-production preview: a quick view of the scene before starting rendering.

6. Video producer: render a 9:16 vertical episode using suitable AI technologies.

7. Dashboard: to manage episodes, remaining credit, and follow up on production.

8. Quick publish: share the episode directly to popular platforms.

4) Pricing and profit model

• Free to start: daily free credit for each new user.

• Credit pack: buy additional credit as needed (small size and affordable prices).

• Optional subscription model: a monthly package with extra features for those who want it.

• Main profit: from selling credit or subscriptions, while keeping basic use free.

5) Global distribution plan with free channels

• Short video platforms: share sample episodes on TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels to attract creators' attention.

• Tech communities: engage in forums like Reddit and Indie Hackers to explain the idea and attract early users.

• Search engines: optimize simple educational content (how to make a micro-drama) to increase free visibility.

• Word of mouth: encourage users to share their work to increase spread naturally.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Intellectual property rights: ensure generated content does not infringe others' rights.

• Usage consent: clarify the terms of use regarding content produced by users.

• Inappropriate content: apply simple filtering based on predefined rules.

Technical:

• Rendering cost: reduce cost by optimizing the model and using credit wisely.

• Character consistency: gradually improve visual memory to ensure better results.

• Browser performance: focus on optimizing the interface to run smoothly on various devices.

7) Suggested name and marketing tagline

Suggested name: Clipora

Marketing tagline: Turn any idea into a ready vertical drama in minutes.
