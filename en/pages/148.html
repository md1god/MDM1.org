Wadah — Complete Project Plan

Know why your model made this decision, in a language you understand within minutes.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

An original product plan for explaining the decisions of AI models

An open platform/library that explains why the model made a particular decision, relying on cooperative game theory to distribute credit/accountability across inputs, with a human interface in natural language instead of abstract numbers.

1) Target Audience

• Data scientists and machine learning engineers who build models and need to understand their behavior.

• Risk, compliance, and audit teams in banks, insurance, healthcare, and education.

• Small startups that do not have a dedicated explainability team.

• Developers who integrate AI into their applications and want explanations that can be shown to end users.

• Regulators and auditors who require documented justifications for every automated decision.

2) Points of Distinction (why our product is chosen)

• Explanation in natural language: a readable textual explanation for every decision, not just charts and numbers.

• Bilingual with native Arabic support, not as a later add-on.

• No code: a ready interactive dashboard that produces a report in minutes.

• Runs locally or on the edge without uploading sensitive data to the cloud.

• Bias and fairness checking built in as a core function, not an add-on.

• Model-agnostic: works with any popular machine learning library.

• Free start and very low adoption cost.

3) Scope of the First Release (maximum of 8 tasks)

1. Load the model via a file or an existing inference service.

2. Compute each feature's contribution to a single decision.

3. Generate a natural-language textual explanation (Arabic/English).

4. Interactive chart dashboard to display contributions and differences.

5. Export a shareable report (PDF/link).

6. Compare the behavior of two models on the same data.

7. Detect bias and inconsistency in outputs.

8. A simple binding package for Python and a command line.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free forever: unlimited local operation, single user, local reports.

• Teams: collaboration, cloud storage of reports, history log — low monthly subscription.

• Enterprises: private deployment, single sign-on, permissions, audit log.

• Additional revenue: managed hosting, priority support, training and certification programs, custom integrations.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Open-source repository and excellent documentation as the first attraction point.

• Technical articles and tutorials on "model explainability" on free blogs and platforms.

• Developer and scientist communities, and publishing packages in public repositories.

• Academic and university partnerships to use the tools in courses and research.

• Hackathons and support for open projects, and a free starter package that drives adoption.

• Short visual content explaining "one decision" step by step.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: complying with the licenses of dependent packages, protecting privacy when processing customer data, clarifying that the explanation is an aid and not a legal certification, and avoiding exaggerated claims of accuracy.

• Technical: computation cost rises with the number of features, explanations may be approximate or sensitive to changes, difficulty dealing with fully closed models, in addition to teams' resistance to new tools. Mitigation through incremental optimization, clear confidence scores, and optional local deployment.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Wadah (وَضَح)

Tagline: Know why your model made this decision, in a language you understand within minutes.
