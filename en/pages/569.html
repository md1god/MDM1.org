مِيزان — Complete Project Plan

Improve your website with simple steps and without high costs.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, growth plan, and risks. The project is under development and has not yet been launched.

1) Target Audience

• Small store owners: those who do not have the budget to buy expensive tools.

• Content creators and bloggers: need to improve their visibility without deep technical complexity.

• Start-up agencies: looking for a practical and easy solution for their clients with limited budgets.

• Independent developers: want to improve their websites quickly with clear steps.

2) Points of Differentiation (Why Choose Our Product)

• Free to start: the most useful tasks are available for free without needing a credit card.

• Simple instead of complex: an interface focused only on top improvement priorities, not thousands of settings.

• Practical and actionable: each suggestion comes with a clear step to implement directly.

• Lightweight and fast: focuses on what actually matters and skips unnecessary reports.

3) Scope of the First Version (Max 8 Tasks)

1. [ ] Quick site check: detect the most common technical issues.

2. [ ] Keyword research: with a realistic and actionable difficulty estimate.

3. [ ] Direct competitor analysis: show strengths and weaknesses in brief.

4. [ ] Internal links check: detect gaps and the most important missing links.

5. [ ] Page titles check: check titles and descriptions with direct suggestions.

6. [ ] Basic speed issues check: identify the slowest elements that are easy to fix.

7. [ ] Smart priority list: rank suggestions by impact vs effort.

8. [ ] Export short report: a PDF report ready to share with one click.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

· Plan · Price · Content ·

· Free · 0$ · All tasks of the first version with fair use. ·

· Growth · 5$/month · Higher limits for more checks, saving reports, and tracking multiple websites. ·

· Professional · 12$/month · Higher limits for small agencies, with scheduled reports. ·

Revenue model: subscriptions only, with all essentials remaining free forever.

5) Global Growth Plan Through Free Channels

• Free tool directories: add the product to free tool directories with no fees.

• Educational content: publish step-by-step articles about website improvement aimed at beginners.

• Tech and creative communities: valuable participation in forums and among content creators with a focus on helping.

• Organic search: target very long and specific phrases that matter to new website owners.

• Community sharing: encourage users to share their experiences through non-paid channels.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

· Type · Risk · Mitigation Method ·

· Legal · Violating terms of use of some public sources · Rely only on allowed data and strictly comply with each source's terms of use. ·

· Technical · Service call limits or high load · Use caching, apply fair usage limits, and build lightly without unnecessary dependencies. ·

· Technical · Accuracy of estimates · Clearly label estimates as "approximate" and focus on suggestions that can be verified manually. ·

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Slogan

Proposed name: مِيزان

Marketing slogan: Improve your website with simple steps and without high costs.
