نَسَج — Complete Project Plan

Natural paraphrasing that preserves meaning and makes reading easier.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not yet launched.

1) Target audience

• Writers and editors: they need text that looks natural and fluent before publishing.

• Content marketers and SEO specialists: they want to paraphrase text without losing meaning or terminology.

• Students and researchers: they use early drafts and want to turn them into a more human-readable form.

• Small website owners: they are looking for a free, fast tool without needing to hire an editor.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Meaning preservation first: the absolute priority is preserving facts and terminology instead of radically changing them.

• Precise tone control: the ability to choose the tone appropriate to the specific reader, not just an arbitrary level.

• Terminology lock: protecting technical or proper names from any unwanted modification.

• Full transparency: displaying a direct comparison between the original and paraphrased text, while avoiding any misleading promises.

• Free to start: it can be used without mandatory registration, with a full trial of the core functions.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. [ ] Lightweight interface: a fast design that works smoothly on phone and computer.

2. [ ] Natural paraphrasing: turning text into a more fluent form while preserving meaning.

3. [ ] Tone list: adding tone options (formal, friendly, educational).

4. [ ] Terminology lock: a feature to protect specified words or phrases from modification.

5. [ ] Instant comparison: displaying the original and paraphrased text side by side.

6. [ ] Free daily quota: a daily credit system without requiring registration for first use.

7. [ ] Word count: a live display of the word count before and after processing.

8. [ ] Responsible use warning: adding clear text directing use toward lawful and ethical purposes.

4) Pricing and profit model

• Free to start: a daily quota sufficient for most ordinary users.

• Optional subscription: a larger quota or processing of longer texts when needed.

• Freemium model: profit from subscription only, without misleading ads.

• Complete privacy: not selling user data, and processing text with the least possible storage.

5) Global distribution plan through free channels

• Search engine optimization: creating educational guides in multiple languages that explain how to make text more natural to read.

• Free tool directories: adding the tool to open educational and free resource lists.

• Educational communities: contributing useful content in writing communities without direct promotion.

• Community translation: opening the interface for translation into different languages with user participation.

• Focus on speed: making the tool very lightweight, so that word-of-mouth spread becomes stronger than ads.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: the risk of misuse, so responsible use must be clarified in the terms of use and any explicit promises of "bypassing" any system must be avoided.

• Technical: the high cost of text processing, and the difficulty of fully preserving meaning while paraphrasing.

• Output quality: the possibility of unintentionally changing precise terminology, which calls for greater model accuracy and a terminology lock system.

• Server reliability: the need for a lightweight architecture to avoid delays with rapid user growth.

7) Proposed name and marketing sentence

Proposed name: نَسَج

Marketing sentence: Natural paraphrasing that preserves meaning and makes reading easier.
