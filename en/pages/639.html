CiteBid — Complete Project Plan

Turn the requirements file into a documented compliance matrix before the deadline.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target audience

• Small and medium-sized companies bidding on government or institutional tenders in technology, services, construction, and supply.

• Bid managers, pre-sales teams, and technical sales teams responding to long documents under time pressure.

• Independent consultants and bid-writing agencies managing multiple files for different clients.

• Later: large departments needing governance, review, and expanded collaboration.

The problem: teams lose track among hundreds of requirements, discover answer gaps shortly before submission, and rewrite similar answers from scratch.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Atomic extraction, not superficial summarization: every item becomes an independent requirement with page number and original excerpt.

• Explainable compliance: requirement status, reason, and linked evidence, not a black-box score.

• Gap detection before submission: unanswered requirements, conflicts, and missing evidence appear on one dashboard.

• Documented draft, not free generation: every sentence is linked to a source, and anything without evidence appears as a blank that must be filled by a human.

• Arabic and English ready: RTL support, table extraction, and handling of tender terminology.

• Distinct design and identity: an interactive matrix interface with a requirements heat map, and a logo based on a quotation mark that turns into a check mark.

• Privacy first: customer data isolation, no use of files for model training, and one-click deletion.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Create a project workspace and upload PDF/DOCX/images with Arabic and English OCR and page-ordered extraction.

2. Analyze requirements into atomic items and classify them: technical/commercial/legal, mandatory/optional, with an excerpt and confidence score.

3. Build an editable interactive compliance matrix: status, owner, due date, notes, and filters.

4. Detect gaps: unanswered requirements, conflicts, and missing evidence, with an alert before export.

5. Generate a draft answer for each requirement with the source quote and identify what needs human intervention.

6. A simple knowledge base for uploading certificates, policies, and success stories, tagging them, and linking them to requirements.

7. Light review: comments, assignment, change log, and a "ready for submission" status.

8. Export to Excel/Word/PDF while preserving citations, plus an executive compliance report.

4) Pricing and revenue model

· Plan · Price · Highlights ·

· Free · $0 · 3 projects per month, 150 pages per project, one user, 10 knowledge documents, basic export ·

· Pro · $17/user/month annually or $21 monthly · 20 projects per month, 1,000 pages, 100-document knowledge base, version history ·

· Team · $35/user/month annually · unlimited fair use, review, comments and roles, SSO, integrations, audit log ·

· Enterprise · custom pricing · data residency, API, SLA, training, advanced DPA ·

• Revenue model: per-user subscriptions + additional usage + setup and training services.

• Free start: no credit card, and the free plan is permanent to experience value before paying.

• Paid add-ons: extra pages, custom templates, knowledge base migration, and training workshops.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Bilingual SEO: free guides and templates such as "compliance matrix", "pre-submission checklist", and "tender readiness calculator".

• LinkedIn: de-identified breakdowns of public tenders, before/after comparisons, and an invitation to a free analysis.

• YouTube and webinars: short videos and practical steps hosted by tender experts.

• Niche communities: helpful posts in tender and procurement groups, not direct ads.

• Product Hunt, G2, and Capterra: initial launch, verified reviews, and comparison pages.

• Partnerships: bid consultants, chambers of commerce, business accelerators, and universities, with a clear referral commission.

• Integrations: Google Drive, Microsoft 365, and Slack/Teams to increase free discovery.

• Referrals: one free month for each party when a new team is invited.
