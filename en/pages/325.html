Nasaq — original product plan

Problem: Building enterprise systems (inventory, accounting, HR, sales) requires expensive development teams and months of work, while traditional platforms fail to support Arabic and become closed systems that are hard to customize or migrate.

Idea: A cloud platform that converts text descriptions (in Arabic or English) into complete enterprise applications — data models, permissions, workflows, reports, and interfaces — on top of a unified, extensible Metadata layer, with a free tier to get started.

1) Target Audience

• Small and medium enterprises in the Gulf, Levant, and North Africa (5–200 employees).

• Non-technical operations teams: finance, inventory, HR, sales.

• Consultants and implementation firms that build clients' systems and want to accelerate delivery.

• Small associations, government entities, and their local branches.

• Startups that need an early financial/operational system without in-house development.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Arabic-first: full RTL support in interfaces and reports, and natural-language Arabic command input.

• Unified generation engine: a single Metadata layer automatically generates tables, relationships, validation constraints, permissions, workflows, and REST API — not a fragmented system.

• Review before publishing: every generation goes through user review (human-in-the-loop) to reduce AI errors.

• Escapability: export data, templates, and API at any time; self-hosted option available, no data lock-in.

• Permanently free to start: a time-unlimited free tier for testing and small projects.

• Ready-made vertical templates: installable and adjustable conversationally instead of starting from scratch.

3) First Version Scope (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Engine to convert text descriptions (Arabic/English) into data models, relationships, and validation constraints.

2. Unified Metadata layer with auto-generated CRUD API and role-based permissions (RBAC).

3. Interface generator: lists, input forms, dashboards, and reports — with native RTL support.

4. Workflow engine: approvals, notifications, and scheduled tasks with editable rules.

5. Conversational editor: edit fields, reports, and rules via chat after publishing.

6. Four ready-made templates: inventory, invoicing and sales, purchase orders, and employee files.

7. Integrations: Excel and CSV import/export, REST API, Webhooks, and email linking.

8. Cloud hosting with a free tier + Docker package for self-hosting (open-core).

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

Revenue sources: monthly/annual subscriptions, additional AI credit packages, template and add-on marketplace (20% commission), implementation and training services.

5) Global Rollout Plan Using Free Channels

• Short video: YouTube, TikTok, and LinkedIn — "Build a complete inventory system in 3 minutes" in Arabic and English (3 videos per week).

• GitHub open-core: publish the self-hosting layer to build trust and organic growth (SEO).

• Community launches: Product Hunt, Hacker News, Indie Hackers with launch offers.

• Communities: Discord/Telegram, relevant Reddit forums (ERP, no-code), and Gulf LinkedIn groups.

• Partner program: train ERP consultants and certified accountants to become a sales channel.

• Comparison content: multi-language SEO articles comparing traditional systems by cost and time.

• Academy: Arabic university micro-courses and free accredited courses that issue certificates.
