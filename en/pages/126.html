Maslak — Complete Project Plan

A smart Arabic recruitment platform that connects you to the right job based on your skills, not the look of your CV.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

An original product plan: an AI-powered recruitment platform

1) Target audience

• Job seekers, especially in Arab and emerging markets, from recent graduates to holders of technical and non-technical skills.

• Small and medium-sized companies that do not have expensive ATS systems.

• Independent recruitment agencies and recruiters looking for fast screening tools.

• Technical and professional communities that need a job board embedded in their websites.

2) Points of distinction (why choose our product)

• Skills profile instead of a CV: the candidate is ranked by verified skills, not by a PDF file.

• Smart two-way matching: it suggests jobs to the candidate and candidates to the company, with a clear explanation of why they match.

• Arabic-first: native RTL support and stemming for Arabic search, not an afterthought translation.

• Free to start: basic posting and search are always free, and payment is only for advanced tools.

• Integrable: an open API and webhooks to connect the platform to companies' systems.

• Privacy-centric: control over profile visibility, and anonymity when applying.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. User registration with roles (candidate / company) with email authentication.

2. Structured skills profile (skills, years of experience, languages, work links).

3. Creating and publishing jobs with filtering (location, type, experience, optional salary).

4. Arabic search engine with stemming and simplified spell correction.

5. Initial matching engine (rules + vector similarity) with a match explanation.

6. Simple one-step application + an application status tracking dashboard for the company.

7. A company dashboard (managing jobs and candidates) and an admin dashboard.

8. Foundation package: email alerts, privacy settings, API documentation, and a containerized deployment environment (Docker).

(Post-first-release: skills verification, instant messaging, paid recommendations, paid integration.)

4) Pricing and profit model

• Free plan (Forever Free): two active jobs per month, unlimited search and profiles, basic matching.

• Growth plan: low monthly subscription — unlimited jobs, ad highlighting, advanced analytics, recruiting badges.

• Enterprise plan: custom pricing including SSO, ATS integration, support contract, private hosting.

• Supporting revenue: unobtrusive professional content ads, and fees on featured jobs for individual payments.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Open content: a public repository on GitHub with an open license, and a transparent feature list.

• Communities: posting in Python/Django communities, technical subreddits, and professional Discord/Slack.

• Ready-made embed boards: the ability to embed the job board for free on universities' and blogs' websites.

• Arabic SEO: skills, cities, and jobs pages optimized for search engines.

• Community partnerships: universities, coding bootcamps, and free technical groups.

• The product itself as a channel: a referral link and free job sharing on social networks.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: data protection laws (such as GDPR and local privacy regulations) — addressed through transparency, explicit consent, the right to deletion, and non-discrimination in screening.

• Legal: liability for fake or misleading job content — addressed through manual verification, reporting, and a clear removal policy.

• Technical: poor quality of Arabic matching — addressed with open training data and continuous human evaluation.

• Technical: scalability during growth — addressed with a modular architecture, task queues, and independent search indexing.

• Technical: profile manipulation and fake endorsements — addressed with rate limits, anomaly detection, and gradual verification.

• Commercial: acquiring the first companies in a two-sided market — addressed by starting in one narrow community and then expanding.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Maslak

Marketing tagline: A smart Arabic recruitment platform that connects you to the right job based on your skills, not the look of your CV.
