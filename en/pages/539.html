Zilal — Full Project Plan

Full local control of your AC via Home Assistant and Matter, no cloud, and setup in minutes.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target audience

• Homeowners with split ACs from families that use a local control protocol over UDP, who suffer from slow and interrupted cloud apps.

• The Home Assistant community and enthusiasts of local automation and privacy.

• Small offices, furnished apartments, and tourist units that need central control without subscriptions.

• Installation and maintenance technicians who want a tool for discovery, diagnostics, and managing multiple devices.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Fully local: works without internet or a mandatory account, and does not send data to the manufacturer’s cloud.

• Setup from the UI: automatic discovery and quick pairing without YAML or complex commands.

• Dual bridge: native Home Assistant and Matter integration for access to Apple Home, Google Home, and Alexa.

• Comprehensive control: power, mode, temperature, fan, swing, timer, and immediate state sync.

• Multi-device and multi-brand: support for families derived from the same protocol with detection of differences.

• Secure by default: local authentication, encrypted connection, and redacted logs.

• Core written from scratch: lightweight, fast, and extensible, without copying any prior code or design.

• Energy savings: local scheduling and consumption estimates based on runtime.

3) First release scope (maximum of 8 tasks)

1. Discover devices over the local network and verify their identity.

2. Basic control: on/off, mode, temperature, fan, and swing.

3. Setup UI inside Home Assistant via Config Flow and automatic creation of HVAC entities.

4. State sync, immediate response, and reconnection after network loss.

5. Lightweight local web UI (PWA) for device management.

6. Local scheduling and scenarios without internet.

7. Experimental Matter bridge for control through major platforms.

8. Diagnostics, energy consumption estimation, and export of redacted logs.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free forever: full local control for up to 3 devices, Home Assistant integration, and security updates.

• Pro: $29 one-time per home — unlimited devices, advanced scheduling, consumption reports, and local backup.

• Installer: $99 per year — fleet management, remote diagnostics, and priority support.

• Optional cloud: $2.99 per month for encrypted access outside the home, without disabling local functions.

• Optional hardware: a small ready-made bridge for those without an always-on server.

• No ads, no data selling, and the free version is not a temporary trial.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Launch the core on GitHub with HACS support and documentation in Arabic and English.

• Publish in Home Assistant Community, Reddit, Hacker News, and Product Hunt.

• Short videos showing setup in under 3 minutes, with before/after comparisons.

• Search engine optimization with pages such as “Local control of your AC without a cloud” and troubleshooting guides.

• Discord/Telegram community and an ambassador program for installers and technicians.

• Multiple translations and participation in smart home meetups and Maker fairs.

• Partnerships with local stores and smart home system integrators.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: reliance on a proprietary protocol may violate terms of use in some countries; open documentation, legal review, and not shipping protected assets are required.

• Trademarks: avoid manufacturer names or logos in branding and marketing.

• Privacy: comply with GDPR and CCPA, minimize data collection, and do not require users to use the cloud.

• Technical: multiple firmware versions, UDP packet loss, state conflicts with the original controller, and changes to Home Assistant and Matter.

• Security: protect local devices through authentication, network isolation, and signed updates.

• Hardware: any physical bridge requires FCC/CE certification and radio compatibility testing.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Zilal

Tagline: Full local control of your AC via Home Assistant and Matter, no cloud, setup in minutes.
