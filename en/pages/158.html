Siraj — Complete Project Plan

Your safe wallet into the world of Web3, simple, private, and free to start.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

Product plan: a native Web3 wallet for desktop browsers

1) Target audience

• Newcomers to Web3 who want a simple wallet inside the browser without complexity.

• dApp developers who need a reliable and fast connection.

• Privacy-conscious users who do not trust centralized or tracking solutions.

• Emerging markets suffering from weak virtual wallets and slow local support.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Simplicity first: set up the wallet in under a minute without complicated jargon.

• Privacy by design: no address tracking, no selling of data, and full open source for auditing.

• Free to start: download and core use at no cost.

• Multi-network: support for Ethereum and its compatible Layer 2 networks by default.

• Native Arabic experience: full Arabic/RTL interface with multi-language support.

• Transparent security: keys stored locally on the device only, with clear alerts for every sensitive action.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Create/import a wallet with a secure recovery phrase.

2. Local encrypted storage of keys with a password lock.

3. Display balances and main ERC‑20 tokens.

4. Send/receive transactions with fee estimation.

5. Connect to dApps via the standard wallet standard.

6. Network management (add/switch) with Ethereum Layer 2s.

7. Transaction history with live status and notifications.

8. Arabic/RTL interface and dual mode (light/dark).

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free option: all core features at no charge (wallet, sending, connecting).

• Optional advanced plan: productivity features (encrypted cloud backup, multiple wallets, price alerts) for a symbolic monthly fee.

• Partnership income: transparent, disclosed commission on asset swaps via external providers only, without hiding fees.

• Commitment: no hidden fees, no selling data, and one-click unsubscribe.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Publish the extension in official browser stores for free.

• Arabic/English educational content (lessons, short videos) on social networks.

• Open communities (chat server, forums) to provide support and receive feedback.

• Partnerships with dApp developers to list the wallet as a connection option.

• Building open source to attract contributors and community audit trust.

• Free referral programs and incentives for early users with digital badges at no cost.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: varying digital asset regulations between countries, anti-money laundering requirements, and disclosure liability. Solution: not holding user funds, and enabling regional compliance.

• Store compliance: extension store policies change; the need for ongoing compliance with their terms.

• Technical: key vulnerabilities, exposure to malware, and website phishing. Solution: local encryption, domain verification, and periodic security audits.

• Network dependence: provider node outages or high fees. Solution: backup node providers and smart fee estimation.

• Reputation: any security incident destroys trust; therefore transparency and independent auditing are essential.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Siraj

Marketing tagline: Your safe wallet into the world of Web3, simple, private, and free to start.
