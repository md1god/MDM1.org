LumaDeck — Full Project Plan

LumaDeck turns every video into a calm learning and focus experience under your control.

A full plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue, launch, and risks. Not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Students and self-learners watching long lectures and courses.

• Remote employees and creators needing focused, fast viewing.

• Users struggling with distraction or too many recommendations and notifications.

• People with accessibility needs and older adults needing better playback, audio, and captions.

• Teachers, families, and moderators wanting a clean, safe viewing experience.

• Users moving between multiple video platforms and devices.

2) Differentiators

• Intent first: three modes—Learn, Focus, Entertainment—instead of hundreds of switches.

• Local-first, privacy by design: core features need no account, no tracking, no data selling.

• Cross-platform: one adaptation layer works across major video platforms.

• Accessibility: keyboard support, screen readers, contrast, reduced motion, captions.

• Lightweight, transparent permissions: no heavy injection; every permission explained.

• Optional encrypted sync across devices without an account.

• New design: a calm command palette instead of crowded menus.

• User-aligned revenue: no ads, no data selling, no recommendation manipulation.

3) First Release Scope (8 Tasks Max)

1. Build a Chromium and Firefox extension on Manifest V3 with clear onboarding and permissions.

2. Create an adaptation layer that detects supported platforms' video players and unifies playback commands.

3. Implement three ready modes—Learn, Focus, Entertainment—with one-click switching.

4. Precise playback: granular speed, time jumps, resume, picture-in-picture, keyboard shortcuts.

5. Remove distractions: hide recommendations, comments, and sidebars; provide a clean reading mode.

6. Timestamp-linked notes, local search, and Markdown export.

7. Accessibility pack: contrast, font size, reduced motion, caption formatting, and clear screen reader labels.

8. A local dashboard for viewing and focus statistics, with export and full deletion, no remote analytics.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free forever: all core local features, no credit card, no blocking of essential features.

• Pro: about $4/month or $29/year: encrypted sync, advanced rules, themes, expanded export, faster support.

• Early offer: lifetime license at a reduced launch price.

• Teams and education: about $2 per seat/month with a minimum, plus admin dashboard and unified policies.

• Fixed principle: no ads, no data selling, no commissions for changing recommendations or results.

5) Global Launch Plan with Free Channels

• Publish on Chrome Web Store, Firefox Add-ons, and Edge Add-ons with an SEO-optimized description.

• Launch on Product Hunt, Hacker News, and BetaList with a short demo.

• Short before/after videos on TikTok, Reels, and Shorts, plus longer tutorials on video platforms.

• Genuine participation in productivity, accessibility, and learning communities, with direct help, not promotional links.

• A landing site with How it works, category comparisons, and guides, optimized for SEO.

• Translation into 8 languages by volunteers, with full Arabic and RTL support.

• Partnerships with teachers, educational creators, and accessibility organizations, plus specialized newsletters.

• Referral program granting one free Pro month, without cash incentives.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

Legal:

• Do not use platform names or logos in branding; detection is limited to descriptive compatibility statements.

• Comply with store policies: least privilege, privacy policy, no remote code, passing review.

• Do not violate platform terms: no ad blocking, no bypassing paywalls or DRM, no unauthorized scraping or downloading.

• Comply with GDPR and CCPA: local processing by default, explicit consent, full data deletion.

• Verify accessibility claims under WCAG 2.2 AA before publishing.

• Manage taxes and VAT for global sales.

Technical:

• Platform page changes may break features; use an isolated adaptation layer, monitoring tests, and fast patches.

• Manifest V3 service worker lifecycle and browser differences.

• Memory and performance use when injecting features into heavy pages.

• Sync security: end-to-end encryption, key management, and secure recovery.

• Feature creep; scope is frozen at 8 tasks until launch.

• Translation and long-term maintenance costs and the RTL experience.

7) Name and Tagline

Name: LumaDeck

Tagline: LumaDeck turns every video into a calm learning and focus experience under your control.
