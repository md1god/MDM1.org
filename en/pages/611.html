Molyn — Complete Project Plan

Draw molecules accurately and share them instantly from any browser.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, target audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is currently under development and has not yet been launched.

1) Target Audience

• Chemistry and pharmacy students in schools and universities.

• Researchers in laboratories, pharmaceutical companies, and biotechnology companies.

• Teachers who need interactive drawing and illustration tools.

• Scientific documentation teams that share molecular structures in reports and databases.

• Scientific software developers looking for an embeddable and extensible editor.

2) Key Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• Runs entirely in the browser with no installation, with offline support via PWA.

• Local-first data processing by default: structures are not sent to a server unless explicitly shared.

• Full Arabic interface with RTL support, alongside English and other languages.

• Fast editing with touch and stylus, with keyboard shortcuts for professionals.

• Open export to multiple formats (SMILES, MOL, InChI, SVG, PNG) with no paid restrictions.

• Real-time collaboration in the cloud version, with a free basic editor with no usage limits.

• A plugin architecture that allows the product to be extended without modifying the core.

• Full transparency: the client is open source, with no monopoly over data or file formats.

3) Scope of the First Release (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. 2D drawing canvas for atoms, bonds, and rings with drag and touch editing.

2. Library of ready-made templates for functional groups and common structures.

3. Undo/Redo and basic keyboard shortcuts.

4. Import and export of core formats: SMILES, MOL, SVG, PNG.

5. Structural validation and automatic layout cleanup before export.

6. Automatic local saving and offline functionality via PWA.

7. Read-only share link with permission controls.

8. Arabic RTL interface with basic accessibility features (contrast, keyboard navigation).

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free forever: basic editor, export, local saving, and individual educational use.

• Pro: around $6/month or $60/year; includes cloud sync, version history, and limited collaboration.

• Team: around $12 per user/month; includes shared workspaces and permission management.

• Education: free for schools and universities after institutional verification.

• Enterprise: custom pricing for self-hosting, SSO, and service level guarantees.

• Principle: no blocking of exports or core features; revenue comes from cloud, collaboration, and support.

5) Global Distribution Plan Through Free Channels

• Search engine optimization through tutorials and articles on drawing molecules and chemical file formats.

• Publishing the open-source client on code hosting platforms to attract contributors.

• YouTube channels and short educational workshops for chemistry students.

• Communities focused on chemistry, scientific programming, and specialized forums.

• Free university ambassador program and outreach to research laboratories.

• Free integrations with open-source lab notebook tools and scientific databases.

• Launch through tech product discovery platforms and open-source communities.

• Localization and targeting of Arabic scientific communities and scientific Mastodon networks.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal: avoid copying any interface, assets, or code from existing products; use a clear license (MIT or Apache) for the editor.

• Respect data privacy (GDPR) when enabling sync and collaboration.

• Review regulations related to sensitive or dual-use materials when sharing chemical structures.

• Verify usage rights for built-in molecular templates and functional groups.

• Technical: accuracy of bond and ring representation for large structures, and browser performance with complex structures.

• File format compatibility with different external tools.

• Resolving real-time editing conflicts and offline/online sync logic.

• Differences in touch and stylus behavior across devices and browsers.

• Protecting share links from leaks and managing access permissions.

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Slogan

Proposed name: Molyn

Marketing slogan: Draw molecules accurately and share them instantly from any browser.
