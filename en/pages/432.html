Haseen — Full Project Plan

Your AI agent runs inside your organization's walls; your data never leaves.

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: private AI agent architecture for regulated sectors

1) Target audience

• Hospitals and healthcare groups handling patient data under strict protection rules.

• Law firms and legal teams that refuse to upload documents to an external cloud.

• Financial entities (banks, lenders, insurers) tied to external auditors and audit trails.

• Government, sovereign, and security bodies requiring offline operation (Air-gapped).

• Compliance / security teams inside these organizations, and government contractors.

• Decision segment: IT director + security officer + compliance officer.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Fully local: the agent model is built inside the organization's network; no data ever leaves its boundaries.

• Optional isolated mode: one-tool Air-gapped deployment with feature parity with cloud mode.

• Private Endpoints: a fixed internal API address extending to on-prem servers or the customer's private cloud.

• Unified upgrade path: the same agent libraries and tool definitions run locally or in the cloud, no rewrite.

• Ready-made audit log: tamper-resistant record of every call, model, and data source, exportable to governance systems.

• Free tier: a free single-node layer to trial inside the organization before any purchase.

• Fair pricing and exit: no lock-in of customer files; full export of logs and settings anytime.

3) First-release scope (maximum 8 tasks)

1. One local runtime engine (small open-source model) with a unified REST interface.

2. One agent layer: plan → tool execution → response, with short-term session memory.

3. Private in-network endpoint + internal API key with project-level permissions.

4. Air-gapped deployment via a single container with a pre-bundled model index, offline.

5. Audit log + call tracing with JSON/CSV export.

6. One connector per sector: ready-made document templates (contextual / contractual / financial).

7. Lightweight web dashboard: usage monitoring, cap, and node status.

8. Free single-node license + guided self-installation in 15 minutes.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: one node, two projects, 10,000 calls/month, size-limited audit log — for internal trials.

• Professional: $79/month per production node, unlimited users, full audit log, model updates.

• Enterprise: from $999/month — Air-gapped deployment, multiple private endpoints, SSO, SLA-backed support.

• Optional add-on: adaptive fine-tuning on the organization's data; ready-made audit-report package.

• Revenue: recurring monthly subscription (self-operated SaaS) + integration services for regulated sectors.

5) Global rollout plan using free channels

• Short educational content in Arabic and English (LinkedIn, X, YouTube) on "what must never leave your organization".

• Technical comparison articles (On-prem vs Cloud) on the product blog and community newsletters.

• Presence in security and compliance communities (ISC², ISACA) and forums on Discord and Reddit.

• Open-source repository of the agent interface to attract security engineers (GitHub contributions).

• Short case studies with approved names (or anonymized figures if approval is unavailable).

• Guided program for partners and government contractors with a free registration page.

• Weekly newsletters + free monthly training workshops via webinar.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: healthcare and financial data rules may mandate certifications; claims must be limited to "capability" of compliance, not "guarantee".

• Legal: liability for generated outputs (professional practice / medical diagnosis / legal advice) requires an explicit disclaimer and human-review controls.

• Technical: a performance gap between local and cloud models may weaken the promise; mitigated with curated model lists per workload.

• Technical: securing Air-gapped deployment and updating models offline is complex and may lengthen the sales cycle.

• Technical: agent misuse to invoke tools touching sensitive data — mitigated with strict tool-level access policies.

• Commercial: long sales cycles with government entities; mitigated via the free tier and self-service model.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Haseen

Tagline: Your AI agent runs inside your organization's walls; your data never leaves.
