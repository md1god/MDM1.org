مِسْطَرة — Complete Project Plan

Draw, convert, and share your drawings from the browser — free.

A full implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the rollout plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

New CAD product plan

1) Target audience

• Beginner designers and engineers, and students who need a powerful tool without high licensing costs.

• Small workshops and craftspeople (carpentry, furniture, equipment) who rely on DXF/DWG files.

• Remote work teams that need real-time collaboration on drawings instead of sending files by email.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Runs directly from the browser with no heavy installation, with autosave and version history.

• Real-time collaboration on the same drawing (synchronized notes and edits) instead of file sharing.

• DXF/DWG import and export with smart checking for engineering errors before export.

• 3D orbit and display with GPU acceleration in the browser via WebGPU.

• Ready-made templates for the most common tasks (partitions, ribs, embossed letters) that save hours of work.

• A genuinely free tier at the start, with no credit card required.

3) Scope of the first release (8 tasks maximum)

1. 2D drafting: lines, circles, shapes, with precise trim, move, and rotate.

2. Converting 2D into 3D solids with adjustable depth.

3. DXF/DWG import and export with validation.

4. GPU-accelerated 3D display inside the browser.

5. Automatic cloud saving with a restorable version history.

6. Sharing a link for viewing and commenting on the drawing.

7. A library of reusable templates and blocks.

8. A web build optimized for desktop with keyboard and shortcut support.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: unlimited projects, DXF export, 3D display, view-only sharing.

• Professional (low monthly subscription): DWG export, real-time collaboration, extended version history, advanced templates.

• Teams and enterprises: workspace and permissions management, dedicated support.

• Additional revenue: a marketplace of templates and blocks where their creators receive a share of the sales.

5) Global rollout plan using free channels

• Short videos on YouTube and TikTok showing a part built from scratch in one minute.

• A free template shared weekly on Reddit and in engineering and industry forums.

• An SEO-optimized blog with articles such as "Convert DXF to 3D for free".

• A referral campaign: get a free team space when you invite a colleague.

• Participation in Fab Lab communities, engineering schools, and robotics clubs.

• An open community page for feature announcements and user feedback.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: the DWG file format is proprietary and may be classified as patent infringement; it is advisable to adopt an open standard export first and obtain legal clearance before full support.

• Legal: user ownership of artwork must be stated explicitly in the terms.

• Technical: precision of geometric modeling in the browser requires strict comparative testing against external sources.

• Technical: WebGPU performance is uneven across browsers; a WebGL fallback path is needed.

• Market: entry by large, well-funded players is possible; differentiation comes through cloud collaboration and ease of getting started.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: مِسْطَرة

Tagline: Draw, convert, and share your drawings from the browser — free.
