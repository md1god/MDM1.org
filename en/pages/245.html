Talfuz — Complete Project Plan

Improve your voiceover quality before rendering: spot and fix pronunciation, proper noun, and number errors in seconds.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the distribution plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: an AI-powered pronunciation-checking and Arabization tool for voiceover

1) Target audience

• Independent video creators (YouTube / TikTok / Reels) who rely on AI voices for voiceover.

• Localization and translation agencies that produce voiceovers in multiple languages and need quick review before delivery.

• E-learning and course teams that produce narrated content in large volumes.

• Podcast and audio marketing creators who handle brand names, prices, and numbers.

• Developers of TTS/audio tools looking for an embeddable quality assurance (QA) layer via API.

2) Points of differentiation (why choose our product)

• Based on linguistic rules, not statistics alone: precise detection of pronunciation-sensitive entities (names, prices, dates, units, numbers).

• Instant fix with alternative pronunciation: a ready-to-paste phonetic spelling suggestion for the engine.

• Per-line assessment: a pronunciation-risk score before rendering to reduce re-recording.

• Runs entirely in the browser: local-first processing = higher privacy and faster startup.

• Multilingual and multi-directional (RTL/LTR) without redesigning.

• Scalable as an API layer for existing production tools.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Upload plain text or an SRT file and automatically split it into numbered lines.

2. Detect sensitive entities: names/proper nouns, prices and currencies, dates, units of measurement, numbers, and identifiers.

3. A pronunciation-rules engine per language (starting with one language + English).

4. Generate a copyable phonetic spelling suggestion for each detected item.

5. Assess each line with a risk score and a suggested fix rate.

6. An interactive correction panel (accept/edit/ignore) with the reason highlighted.

7. Export corrected SRT/text + a pronunciation guide.

8. A multilingual, multi-writing-direction interface in the same editor.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free to start: a limited number of lines/minutes per month, full preview, basic accuracy.

• Pro (individual): near-unlimited lines, all languages, export without watermark, saved pronunciation lists — at a low monthly price with an annual discount.

• Team/Agency: multiple seats, shared workspaces, review log, roles and permissions.

• API/Enterprise: usage-based pricing (per line/per minute) with tiered rate limits.

• Revenue expansions: brand-specific pronunciation dictionaries (Brand Pronunciation Packs) and paid integrations.

5) Global distribution plan via free channels

• Launch: a free landing page + launch on new-product discovery platforms, and a live demo.

• Communities: Reddit (voice acting, video editing, localization, TTS), Discord and Slack for audio and video communities.

• Content SEO: "how to pronounce X" articles and lists of common AI pronunciation errors per language.

• Short video: before/after comparisons on YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Reels.

• Free integrations: a browser extension, SRT templates, open scripts for trial.

• Partnerships: educational content creators and course platforms to showcase the tool as a QA layer.

• GitHub + waitlist: a small free open tool to generate curiosity and collect emails.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: script privacy (unpublished client texts), personal data (names/prices), terms of use of external TTS engines, ownership of audio and subtitle files, compliance with data protection laws (GDPR) and data retention.

• Technical: pronunciation-detection accuracy (false positives/negatives), limited dialect and language coverage, browser performance with large files, response time, differing behavior of each TTS engine, and the need for continuous rule updates as voice models change.

7) Suggested name and tagline

Suggested name: Talfuz

Tagline: Improve your voiceover quality before rendering: spot and fix pronunciation, proper noun, and number errors in seconds.
