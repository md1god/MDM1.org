Parametrica — Complete Project Plan

Turn your text description into manufacturing-ready parametric CAD assemblies in seconds, and edit them geometrically in the browser without writing a single line of code.

Complete execution plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Mechanical engineers and product designers in SMBs who need to accelerate conceptual and detailed design phases.

• Prototype manufacturers and architects who want to turn text ideas into manufacturing-ready CAD models.

• R&D teams in hardware and robotics companies needing rapid iteration on complex assemblies with correct mates and constraints.

• Freelancers and engineering firms without expensive CAD licenses seeking a browser-accessible tool.

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• Full assembly generation with automatic Mates: not just separate parts, but correct geometric constraint relationships (coincident, concentric, parallel, tangent) that are editable.

• Explicit Parameters: every dimension and variable in the design tree is programmatically or manually adjustable with correct sequential updates.

• Browser-based CAD editor with no installation: direct geometric editing (sketch, extrude, revolve, fillet, pattern) with WebGPU-powered 3D viewport.

• Full STEP AP242 export: preserves precise B-Rep, PMI, and assembly structure for compatibility with SolidWorks, Onshape, and NX.

• True "Freemium" model: unlimited free tier with project count and STEP export, limits only on parallel compute and advanced features.

• Open API and plugin platform: automate design pipelines via Python/TypeScript SDK and integrate with PLM/PDM.

3) V1 Scope (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. Text-to-Geometry Inference Engine: LLM trained on structured engineering descriptions + geometric constraint solver to generate parametric feature tree.

2. Browser-based 3D CAD Editor: WebGPU renderer + draggable feature tree + basic sketch/extrude/revolve/fillet/chamfer/pattern operations.

3. Assembly & Mates System: Sub-assembly definition, 6 mate types, assembly constraint solver with interference detection.

4. Parameter Management & Associativity: Parameter table with expressions and design tables, sequential updates on change.

5. STEP AP242 Exporter: Write valid STEP files with precise B-Rep, colors, layers, and assembly structure.

6. Project & Collaboration System: Workspaces, version history (git-like), feature comments, member invitations.

7. Billing & Subscription Dashboard: Stripe integration, Free/Pro/Team tiers, compute usage meters.

8. API & SDK (TypeScript/Python): REST endpoints + Webhooks + client libraries for programmatic model generation and modification.

5) Global Go-to-Market Plan via Free Channels

• Deep technical content (SEO): 50+ "How-to" articles solving real design problems (e.g., "Design a parametric gearbox in 5 minutes") targeting long-tail keywords.

• Open Model Library (Community Library): Public repository of forkable, editable parametric models with contributor badges and monthly leaderboard.

• Monthly Design Challenges: Symbolic prizes (compute credits, swag) for best solutions to open-source problems; winners published on LinkedIn/Reddit/Hacker News.

• Student Ambassador Program: Free Pro licenses for engineering students in exchange for university workshops and documented capstone projects.

• Community Integrations: Official plugins for FreeCAD, Blender, and VS Code, published on their respective marketplaces.

• Weekly Educational YouTube: 8-12 minute videos showing real workflows from text description to manufactured part (CNC/3D print).

• Technical Forums: Detailed answers on GrabCAD, Engineering Stack Exchange, and Reddit (r/CAD, r/MechanicalEngineering) with links to platform solutions.

• Referral Program: 200 free compute hours per referred user who subscribes to Pro, plus referrer bonus.
