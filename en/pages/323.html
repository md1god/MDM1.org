Raqim — Complete Project Plan

Track your work intelligently, from order to accounting.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under development and has not been launched yet.

Product plan: A cloud-based ERP system for small and medium-sized businesses.

1) Target Audience

• Very small and small businesses (1–50 employees) in trade, wholesale, retail, services, and light manufacturing sectors.

• Entrepreneurs, e-commerce stores, and freelancers who have outgrown simple invoicing tools and need an integrated system (inventory, purchasing, accounting) without a technical team.

• First market: Middle East and North Africa (full Arabic support with native right-to-left orientation), followed by emerging markets in Africa and Asia.

2) Key Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• Set up in minutes, not months: pre-built workspaces by industry with sample data.

• Built-in AI assistant: create invoices, reports, and inventory forecasts using natural language (Arabic/English).

• Pricing starts free with realistic usable limits, not just a 14-day trial.

• Modern, mobile-responsive interface with native RTL support, not an add-on.

• Modular model: customers activate only the modules they need (invoicing, inventory, purchasing, accounting, simplified HR).

• Direct export to accountants and accounting firms, plus an open API.

3) Scope of First Version (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. User management and permissions (owner, accountant, salesperson, warehouse staff).

2. Customer, supplier, and contact records.

3. Product and inventory management with quantity tracking and reorder points.

4. Quotes and invoices with sequential numbering, VAT support, and QR codes for e-invoicing.

5. Purchase orders, goods receipt, and invoice matching with orders.

6. Basic general ledger with a chart of accounts and reports (Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, Cash Flow).

7. Dashboard with KPIs (sales, critical inventory, receivables/payables) and alerts.

8. AI assistant for search, summarization, report generation, and invoice drafts.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free Forever: 1 user, up to 500 invoices per year, 2 basic modules, community support.

• Professional: $29/month — 5 users, unlimited invoicing, full inventory and purchasing, advanced reports.

• Growth: $79/month — unlimited users, full accounting, workflow automation, e-commerce store integration, priority support.

• Enterprise: custom pricing + self-hosting option (On-Premise) with an annual license.

• Additional revenue streams: AI usage credits, extra workspaces, report templates, certified implementation partners (Affiliate/Partner program).

5) Global Go-to-Market Plan with Free Channels

• SEO: Arabic and English articles and comparisons targeting search intent ("free invoicing software", "ERP for small businesses").

• YouTube and TikTok: short tutorials "Create your first invoice in 3 minutes".

• Free launches on Product Hunt, Hacker News, Doola, and Middle East startup directories.

• Open community on Discord/GitHub with response within 24 hours (building trust).

• Partnerships with accounting firms and independent agents: free trial for their clients in exchange for referrals.

• Community Edition open-source as a magnet to attract developers and referrals.

• Referral program: free credits for each new customer.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal: Compliance with data protection laws (e.g. PDPL and GDPR), hosting data in the nearest data center to the target market; mandatory e-invoicing regulations changing in some countries may require continuous updates; ensure no encryption keys or customer data are included in the open-source version.

• Technical: Accuracy of accounting constraints and tax rules (thorough testing with licensed accountants); data breach protection as the system contains financial data; AI output quality must be subject to human review before reliance; complexity of data migration from legacy systems (ready CSV/Excel import tools).

7) Proposed Name and Tagline

Proposed name: Raqim

Tagline: Track your work intelligently, from order to accounting.
