Mujarrad — Complete Project Plan

Clean search that returns you to original links, with no AI summaries and no tracking.

Implementation plan for a new digital project: concept, audience, product, revenue, outreach, and risks. Under construction, not yet launched.

1) Target audience

• Researchers, students, journalists needing original, verifiable links.

• Privacy-focused users rejecting tracking and mandatory accounts.

• Users on slow connections or old devices.

• Institutions, libraries, and organizations with strict data protection.

• Arab and other users lacking clean tools supporting RTL and local languages.

2) Differentiators

• AI-free by design: no summaries or “smart modes,” only organic links.

• No engine scraping: official APIs and independent indexes reduce legal and technical risk.

• Extension + independent web interface: works without sign-in; per-site filtering is optional.

• Lightweight HTML mode: ~30KB target, works without JavaScript, fast on slow networks.

• Transparent ranking: merged, deduplicated results in a deterministic, explainable order, with no personalization bubble.

• Verifiable privacy: no query logs or tracking cookies, IP truncation, European hosting.

• Free at its core: basic search and filtering always free, with no ads or data sales.

3) First-version scope (8 tasks maximum)

1. Build a Chromium and Firefox extension hiding “AI Overviews,” “AI Mode,” Gemini elements, and paid results, with an on/off switch and per-site exceptions.

2. Build an independent web search interface fetching results from two or three official APIs, with language and region support.

3. Implement merging, deduplication, and transparent deterministic ranking.

4. Serve via server-side rendering (SSR), with JavaScript-free Lite mode and RTL support.

5. Implement the privacy foundation: no accounts or query logs, IP truncation, strict CSP, European hosting.

6. Add filters for language, region, time, and safe search; store preferences locally only.

7. Add a way to report wrong filters or missing results, with regular rule updates.

8. Release version one: full Arabic localization, documentation, basic monitoring, rate limiting, and open-source extension publication.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Always free: extension, organic search, Lite mode, basic filters, no ads.

• Optional Pro: $3/month or $29/year: custom filtering rules, multiple profiles, optional encrypted sync, early preview builds.

• Teams/Education: $5 per user monthly: unified policies, internal deployment, priority support.

• Self-hosting for organizations: annual license from $490 for universities and institutions needing full control.

• Supporting channels: donations, technology grants, paid support. No ads, no data selling, no paid ranking manipulation.

5) Global outreach plan with free channels

• Publish the client on GitHub, open issues and accept community contributions, then launch the extension in browser stores.

• Launch on Product Hunt, Hacker News, Reddit (r/privacy, r/degoogle, r/browsers), and Mastodon communities.

• Multilingual SEO pages targeting phrases such as “search without AI summaries” and “search without AI overviews.”

• Partner with libraries, universities, digital-rights organizations, and digital-literacy programs.

• Short videos, transparent comparisons with alternatives, and collaboration with privacy bloggers.

• Community translation of the interface and guides into Arabic, Spanish, Indonesian, and French.

• List the tool in alternative-search-engine and open-source directories, without spam or mass email.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Search engine terms: avoid HTML scraping entirely; use licensed APIs or independent indexes; review terms regularly.

• Content rights: limit excerpts, do not store full pages without a license, and honor takedown notices.

• Trademarks: avoid engine names in the brand or domain; use descriptive references only.

• Privacy: comply with GDPR/CCPA, minimize data, conduct an impact assessment, and clearly state that there is no tracking.

• Extension stores: follow user-data policies and Manifest V3 permissions, with clear disclosure.

• Technical: API volatility and costs, DOM changes breaking filters, rate limits, and automated blocking.

• Continuity: ranking quality, response time, browser updates, and denial-of-service attacks.

• Mitigation: automated filter-rule tests, cost monitoring, request limits, backups, and rapid security updates.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Mujarrad

Tagline: Clean search that returns you to original links, with no AI summaries and no tracking.
