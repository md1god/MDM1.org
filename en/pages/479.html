Veriq — Full Project Plan

Platform measuring your AI agent's impact on real work systems and proving success by readable state, not words.

Complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, monetization model, launch plan, and risks. The project is under development and has not yet launched.

Product plan: platform for evaluating and monitoring vertical AI agents

1) Target Audience

• Teams building vertical AI agents in law, finance, procurement, and supply chains, whose agents perform write-actions inside ERP/CRM or legal systems.

• Platform, QA, and SRE engineers responsible for launching and monitoring these agents after production.

• Consulting and integration companies deploying agents for enterprise clients and needing auditable proof.

• Governance and risk teams requiring a clear record of what the agent actually did in the systems.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• State verification, not text: after every operation, we read back the actual system state and compare it to the expected state, instead of relying on the agent's reply.

• Silent-failure detector: surfaces cases where the agent says "done" while the state did not change or changed incorrectly.

• Vertical rules engine: ready-made rules for currencies and exchange rates, units of measure, deadlines and SLA, tax rounding, and permissions.

• Deterministic replay: state snapshots and isolated test environments make results reproducible.

• Comprehensive production observability: track every tool call, payload, latency, cost, and the gap between the agent's claim and reality.

• CI gate: blocks deployment on any behavioral regression or rule violation, with a clear diff report.

• Privacy first: self-hosting and a data-residency option, with least-privilege write access.

• Open from the bottom: free and open SDK and CLI, lowering the trial barrier and speeding adoption.

3) Scope of the First Release (at most 8 tasks)

1. Connector SDK: universal REST/Postgres converter + two ready-made converters (such as Salesforce and HubSpot) + a mock ERP environment for instant testing.

2. Scenario runner as code: workflow definitions in YAML/TypeScript with seed data and state snapshots.

3. Read-back verification: compare state before/after the operation and validate the entities and fields that changed.

4. Domain rules library: currencies, units of measure, dates and deadlines, tax rounding, and permissions.

5. Silent-failure detector: match the agent's claim against actual state and issue a verdict (success/failure/ambiguous).

6. Trace view: every step, tool, payload, observed state, latency, and cost in a single view.

7. CI gate and alerts: run evaluations in the pipeline, compare regressions, and notify via Webhook/Slack/Email.

8. Evaluations dashboard: success and failure trends, the most dangerous silent errors, and shareable, auditable reports.

4) Pricing and Monetization Model

• Free to start: one project, 1,000 evaluations per month, one test environment, full SDK/CLI, and Discord community.

• Pro — $49/user/month: 5 projects, 20,000 evaluations, 3 connectors, custom rules, and alerts.

• Team — $299/month: unlimited users, SSO, 10 connectors, advanced permissions, and audit logs.

• Enterprise — custom pricing: self-hosting, data isolation, SLA, custom connectors, and priority support.

• Additional revenue: extra evaluation consumption, ready-made "evaluation packs" per industry, and initial setup services.

5) Global Launch Plan via Free Channels

• Open source on GitHub: SDK, CLI, and evaluation templates, with documentation and two-minute run examples.

• Technical SEO content: articles on silent failure in business agents, and comparison guides between text-based evaluation and state-based evaluation.

• Community launch: Product Hunt, Hacker News, Reddit (r/LocalLLaMA and r/devops), and AI communities on Discord.

• Free evaluation packs per industry: legal, finance, procurement, and supply chains, used as bait to attract teams.

• Free scanner tool: an "agent impact scan" that reads a demo system's state and issues an instant report.

• Partnerships: ERP/CRM consultancies and integration agencies that deploy the solution for their clients.

• Visual content: short videos demonstrating silent failure and how to detect it, and webinars with platform engineers.

• Academic and professional presence: speakers at AI/DevOps conferences and university hackathons.
