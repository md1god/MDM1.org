Madar — Full Project Plan

Where sustainable solutions meet real need.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Seekers of applicable sustainable solutions: municipalities, associations, green entrepreneurs, and energy and environment engineers.

• Funders and policymakers: climate funds, governments, and institutions looking for solutions with proven impact.

• Developers and researchers: those who want to compare open-source tools and choose the most suitable one for their project.

• Primary audience: the Middle East, Africa, and South Asia — where the need for climate-appropriate, affordably priced solutions is greatest.

2) Differentiators

• Smart matching instead of indexing alone: an engine that recommends the most suitable solution by country, climate, sector, budget, and level of technical readiness.

• Computed impact and readiness score: each solution gets a score combining technology maturity, measured impact evidence, and the number of documented use cases.

• Side-by-side comparison: up to 3 solutions together on standardized indicators (cost, energy, maintenance, impact).

• Live funding and policy tracker: weekly alerts on funding programs and policy calls related to the sectors.

• Bilingual Arabic/English with an interface designed for low connectivity and weak infrastructure.

• Open data, applicable via a free API with generous limits to build a developer community around it.

3) Scope of the First Release (8 Tasks Maximum)

1. Design a unified solutions database (sector, region, cost, impact, technical maturity) and data collection pipelines from public sources.

2. Automated enrichment layer: automatic classification, impact indicator extraction, and metadata cleaning (metadata).

3. Impact and readiness score filtering and ranking engine.

4. Web app for search and filtering with detailed pages for each solution (Next.js).

5. Side-by-side comparison tool (up to 3 solutions).

6. Community solution submission system + voting and lightweight verification.

7. Weekly newsletter with alerts (funding/policies/new solutions).

8. Free API interface + statistics dashboard for platform usage.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

· Plan · Price · What you get ·

· Free for individuals · $0 forever · Search, comparison, weekly newsletter, API with basic limits ·

· Pro · $12/month · Saved research, custom alerts, CSV export, advanced matching ·

· Teams · $49/month · 5 seats, shared dashboards, instant alerts, dedicated support ·

· Enterprise/API · Based on consumption · High API usage, integration with their systems ·

Additional revenue sources: sector sponsorships (climate fund, energy plant), paid custom research, non-intrusive ads from verified companies only.

5) Global Rollout Plan Using Free Channels

1. Practical SEO: comparison pages and "best X solutions in Y" in Arabic and English to capture long-tail search intent.

2. GitHub and tech communities: open part of the data and the API to attract developers and natural backlinks.

3. LinkedIn and X: daily threads on short impactful solutions + case studies with numbers.

4. Product Hunt, Hacker News, and forums: launch a beta version and ask for feedback.

5. Reddit and sector forums: r/sustainability and others — share useful content without advertising.

6. University and incubator partnerships: train students to use the platform for their projects = reach and users.

7. YouTube Shorts/Reels: explain one solution each week in simple language.

8. Discord/Telegram community: for users to suggest solutions and correct data.

6) Risks

Legal:

• Respect the licenses of collected content and attribution credits.

• User privacy and GDPR/equivalent compliance in email newsletters (one-click unsubscribe).

• Limited liability: disclaimer on "impact" data so it is not presented as an investment guarantee.

Technical:

• Data quality and duplication — solved with entry standards + community verification.

• AI model hallucination in impact indicators — solved by never publishing any number without a documented source.

• Fragility of reliance on external sources — solved with a caching layer and alerts when sources go down.

• Cold start problem: the platform begins with a manually curated set of high-quality solutions before scaling automatically.

7) Proposed Name and Tagline

Proposed name: Madar

Tagline: Where sustainable solutions meet real need.
