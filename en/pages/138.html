Laseeq — Full Project Plan

Design professional Arabic/foreign labels and print them precisely from your browser for free.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, launch plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a web-first label designer (Label Designer)

1) Target audience

• Owners of small shops, corner stores, bakeries, and pharmacies for printing price and barcode labels.

• Warehouses and online stores for printing shipping and inventory labels.

• Home users (kitchen organization, jam, handicrafts, gifts).

• Teachers, non-profits, and event teams (name tags and badges).

• A relatively neglected market: the Arabic user who needs native right-to-left (RTL) support.

2) Points of differentiation (why choose our product)

• Web-first with no installation: works from the browser, with lightweight desktop/mobile versions later.

• Native Arabic/RTL support: correct shaping, ready Arabic fonts, Arabic templates from day one.

• Precise print calibration: a margin calculation engine that ensures the label aligns on any home printer without drift.

• Batch printing from data: import CSV/Excel and automatically generate hundreds of labels.

• Built-in QR/barcode generator: EAN‑13, Code‑128, QR with print dimension control.

• Collaboration and cloud sync: share a design with a team via links and permissions.

• A real free start: all core features available without payment or a mandatory account.

3) Scope of the first version (max 8 tasks)

1. Drag-and-drop editor (texts, images, shapes, Arabic/Latin fonts) with an alignment grid.

2. Ready paper template identifier (A4, Avery equivalent, common roll files) + custom dimension editor.

3. High-resolution PDF export with precise print layers and crop marks.

4. Import CSV/Excel and map fields for batch printing.

5. QR and barcode generator with validity verification.

6. Initial template library (30 templates: prices, shipping, names) and in-library search.

7. Cloud project saving + read-only share link.

8. Test-print experience to calibrate the printer step by step.

4) Pricing and profit model

• Free forever (Free): design, individual printing, basic export, and a limited number of templates.

• Pro plan (monthly/yearly): unlimited batch printing, paid templates, professional fonts, collaboration roles, vector export.

• Team/enterprise plan: multiple seats, private brand library, version history.

• Additional income: commission from the template marketplace (revenue-sharing model with designers), referral partnerships with print shops/label material stores, and a paid API for automation.

5) Global launch plan via free channels

• SEO: landing pages for every "label size" and "printer type" and multiple languages, educational articles.

• Product Hunt / Hacker News / Reddit (r/smallbusiness, r/printers, r/EtsySellers).

• Local groups and communities for small shops and teachers on Facebook/WhatsApp.

• Partnership with e-commerce and handicraft influencers for reviews.

• Shareable free templates as a viral growth tool (a "Made with..." badge removable in Pro).

• Short content (repurposing, home organization) on TikTok/YouTube Shorts.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Writing all code from scratch and not copying the interface/assets/code of any existing product.

• Verifying trademarks before launching the name, and avoiding registered paper size/brand stamp names.

• Font, template, and image licenses (using open or commercially licensed fonts).

• Compliance with GDPR/CCPA when storing customer data coming from CSV.

Technical:

• Variation in home printer accuracy and print margins (we need intensive calibration and testing).

• Accuracy of Arabic font rendering and shaping in the browser versus the PDF file.

• Matching barcode/QR standards and verifying their validity before printing.

• Rendering and batch printing performance for thousands of labels.

• High storage/distributed cost on the free plan.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Laseeq (لصيق)

Marketing tagline: Design professional Arabic/foreign labels and print them precisely from your browser for free.
