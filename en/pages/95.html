Haraka — Complete Project Plan

Record once, run your procedure forever.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: the action recorder that turns into smart automation

1) Target audience

• Operations, support, finance, and HR teams that repeat manual tasks daily.

• Agencies and consulting teams that document client procedures (SOPs).

• Developers and automation builders who want to turn a manual procedure into a runnable agent skill.

• Small business owners who have no developer but want simple automation.

• Advanced users who want to archive their workflow and replay it later.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Self-healing: when the app interface changes, the system rediscovers the elements instead of failing.

• Multiple outputs: from a single recording we generate a human-readable SOP, a runnable automation, and a callable agent skill.

• Verification and testing: we turn the procedure into a repeatable test with explicit success conditions (assertions).

• Privacy first: local processing by default, with the option to use local models (local LLM).

• Comprehensive capture: screen + mouse + keyboard + desktop and web apps, with multi-OS support.

• Free starting tier with no bank card.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Automatic recording of screen, mouse, and keyboard.

2. Local analysis of the recording (computer vision + LLM) to extract the steps.

3. Visual step editor to edit/delete/merge steps.

4. Generate three outputs: a text SOP, an automation scenario, an agent skill.

5. Replay the automation with self-healing when the interface changes.

6. Add success conditions (assertions) to turn the procedure into a test.

7. Export and share (Markdown, JSON, public link).

8. A library of ready-made templates and basic collaboration (comments and sharing).

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: unlimited local recordings, basic outputs, limited export.

• Pro: ~$15/month — unlimited outputs, advanced self-healing, cloud, integrations.

• Team: ~$39/user/month — shared library, permissions, review, analytics.

• Enterprise: custom pricing — private deployment (on-premise), SSO, compliance.

• Additional revenue: paid cloud model consumption (credits), a paid template marketplace, custom integration services.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Launch on Product Hunt, Hacker News, and Reddit (r/automation, r/SaaS, r/smallbusiness).

• Open-core model on GitHub to attract developers (open capture engine).

• SEO content: "How to turn a manual procedure into automation" + practical guides.

• Short videos on YouTube, TikTok, and LinkedIn showing "record once, run forever".

• Discord/Slack communities for automation, and an integrations marketplace (Slack, Notion, Zapier-like).

• Ambassador and Founding Users program in exchange for free access.

• Contributions to Arabic and global productivity communities and documentation in Arabic and English.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Recording privacy and party consent (GDPR/CCPA) and the potential capture of sensitive data.

• Terms of use (ToS) of recorded apps and the prohibition of automated automation.

• Intellectual property rights and disclosure when exporting procedures containing third-party data.

• Compliance with protecting client secrets (data, passwords, payment data).

Technical:

• Interface fragility and sudden changes (we address this with self-healing and tests).

• OS permissions and screen and accessibility management.

• Risk of leaking secrets/credentials during recording.

• Model hallucination in understanding steps and generating wrong automation.

• Scalability, processing cost, and real-time performance.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Haraka

Tagline: Record once, run your procedure forever.
