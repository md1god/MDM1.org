Qayd — Complete Project Plan

Smart accounting and invoicing that starts free and grows with your business.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

Product plan: an ERP/CRM accounting and invoicing system — an original build from scratch

1) Target audience

• Small and medium-sized companies (5–200 employees) and startups.

• Freelancers and independent professionals (design, development, consulting, contracting).

• Agencies and e-commerce stores that need invoicing, expenses, and customer tracking.

• Accounting firms that manage several clients' books in one place.

• Initial focus on the Arab region (RTL interface, VAT, multi-currency), then global expansion.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Arabic-first: native RTL interface and reports, legally valid PDF invoices, Hijri/Gregorian calendar, and per-country tax.

• Built-in smart assistant: convert an invoice image/PDF into an automatic entry, categorize expenses, and forecast cash flow.

• Local-first: works offline and syncs later, so work never stops.

• Deployment freedom: one-click self-hosting or a managed cloud version, with full data export without lock-in.

• Genuine simplification: setup in minutes instead of weeks, with no complex accounting jargon for the ordinary user.

• Actually free to start with no time limit, and a clear upgrade path.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Login + multiple company workspaces + roles and permissions.

2. Contacts: customers and suppliers with account statements.

3. Invoicing: quotations, invoices, credit notes, and Arabic PDF export.

4. Expenses: manual entry/receipt upload with automatic categorization.

5. Payments and reconciliation: matching invoices with payments and bank accounts.

6. Core accounting: journal, trial balance, income statement, and balance sheet.

7. Dashboard and exportable reports (CSV/PDF) with alerts.

8. Smart assistant: invoice scanning, natural-language questions, and due-date notifications.

4) Pricing and profit model

• Free: two users, self-hosting, all core features with no time limit.

• Pro: about $12/user/month — automation, multi-currency, advanced reports.

• Business: custom pricing — SSO, API, advanced permissions, priority support.

• Managed cloud hosting: monthly subscription for those who don't want technical management.

• Commission on the add-on marketplace and integrated electronic payments.

• Paid services: installation, data migration, training.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Launch an open core to attract developers and contributions.

• Launch on Product Hunt, Hacker News, and specialized Reddit communities.

• Arabic/English SEO content: guides to issuing tax invoices, comparisons, illustrated explanations.

• YouTube and short clips: quick tours and hands-on training.

• Ready-made integrations: Stripe, PayPal, WooCommerce, Shopify, mail gateways.

• Referral program with ambassadors and rewards, and partnerships with local accounting firms.

• Localization and gradual compliance templates for each country to attract new markets.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: diversity of tax and e-invoicing laws across countries; compliance with GDPR and data protection; open-source license terms; ownership of customer data and backups.

• Technical: data security and encryption, multi-tenant isolation, AI accuracy in accounting entries, offline sync conflicts, and scaling as data volume grows.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Qayd

Tagline: Smart accounting and invoicing that starts free and grows with your business.
