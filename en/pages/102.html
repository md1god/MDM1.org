Sa3ati — Complete Project Plan

Track your hours and turn them into a paid invoice in a minute.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a time tracking and invoicing platform for small teams

1) Target audience

• Freelancers and small studios (1–10 people) working by the hour or by project.

• Workshops and small agencies dealing with multiple clients in different currencies.

• Technical and consulting teams that need accurate work-hour reports before issuing an invoice.

• Accountants collaborating with these clients who need clean data exports.

2) Points of differentiation (why choose our product)

• Instant start with no complex setup: log time in one click, then turn hours into an invoice automatically.

• Invoicing is tied to time from the start: every time entry can be linked to a project, client, and rate, so there is no manual data transfer.

• Fair pricing from day one: a generous free plan for individuals, and payment only when you need multiple teams or advanced customization.

• Privacy first: optional self-hosting with data encryption and open-format export, without locking users in.

• Modern, fast interface: clean design that works on mobile and desktop, with a progressive web app (PWA) for offline work.

• Invoice-ready reports: filter by project/client/period and export instantly to PDF/CSV.

3) Scope of the first version (max 8 tasks)

1. Time tracking (live timer + manual entry) with a description and task.

2. Manage clients, projects, and tasks with clear relationships.

3. Hourly or fixed-amount pricing per project/task.

4. Convert time entries into invoice line items in one click.

5. Generate a PDF invoice with a customizable header and automatic numbering.

6. Basic reports: hours by project/client/period.

7. Multi-user and roles (owner/member) with workspace sharing.

8. CSV export/import and optional self-hosting.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: one user, practically unlimited clients and projects, basic invoicing — free forever (freemium model).

• Pro (monthly/yearly): $6–9 per user/month — advanced roles, multiple currencies, custom reports, accounting export.

• Team/Business: volume-discounted pricing + SSO, managed backups, and priority support.

• Marketplace: a small percentage on invoice templates and third-party integrations.

• Value delivered: turn hours into cash faster and reduce leakage in invoicing.

5) Global distribution plan with free channels

• GitHub Releases + an open-source release of the core to attract contributors and build technical trust.

• Product Hunt, Hacker News, and Reddit (r/freelance, r/smallbusiness) with a gradual launch.

• Educational content: articles and short clips on "how to price your hours," linked to the product.

• Free invoice templates and time reports to attract organic search (SEO).

• Partnerships with freelancer communities and freelance platforms through free integrations.

• A referral program that grants Pro credit in exchange for inviting a colleague.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: invoicing and tax laws differ between countries (VAT, e-invoicing) — solution: local templates, a clear disclaimer, and not providing tax advice.

• Legal: data privacy (GDPR) — solution: encryption, regional hosting, and the right to export and delete.

• Technical: time differences and time zones when logging time — solution: store in UTC and compute locally.

• Technical: ensuring financial accuracy and rounding prices — solution: compute in minor units (cents) and automated tests.

• Technical: risk of strong, free competition — solution: focus on ease of starting and tying time to invoicing as a core differentiator.

• Operational: maintaining the development pace of the open-source project — solution: a stable core and organized contribution review.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Sa3ati

Marketing tagline: Track your hours and turn them into a paid invoice in a minute.
