Sanad — Complete Project Plan

Your tasks in your terminal, with full privacy and absolute control.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

A product plan for terminal-based task management

1) Target audience

• Developers and engineers who live inside the terminal and don't want to leave it.

• DevOps engineers and system administrators managing complex tasks across servers and repositories.

• Students and researchers interested in productivity and full control over their data.

• Small technical teams that want to sync tasks without a mandatory cloud service.

2) Points of distinction (why choose our product)

• Local-first storage with transparent text files, plus optional end-to-end encrypted cloud sync.

• Arabic-native interface with full right-to-left support in the terminal.

• Local intelligence to classify tasks and suggest priorities automatically (without sending your data to any service).

• Instant integrations: Git, SSH, shell scripts, and desktop notifications.

• Natural input processing: write a task in near-human language and have it broken down automatically.

• Lightweight and instantly responsive, with no heavy databases or large runtime.

• Open, extensible alternative via simple plugins.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Add/edit/delete a task with short, clear commands.

2. Classify tasks (tags, projects, priorities, deadlines).

3. Display lists with filters and flexible sorting, plus a compact in-terminal mode.

4. Simple natural input processing that turns a sentence into structured fields.

5. Local text storage with automatic backup.

6. Notifications and reminders via the scheduled system service.

7. Import/export commands (CSV/JSON) to avoid data lock-in.

8. Basic plugin system + customizable shortcuts.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Completely free to start for individual users (unlimited local use).

• Low-cost paid plan (Freemium) for encrypted cloud sync and multiple devices.

• Team plan to support collaboration, roles, and activity log.

• Additional revenue from premium plugins and open-source sponsorship.

• No ads, no selling data — trust is the core asset.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Publish the project as open source on public hosting platforms to attract contributors.

• Write technical articles and comparisons on platforms such as Hacker News, Reddit, and Dev.to.

• Short videos explaining the workflow on YouTube, TikTok, and X.

• Ready-made installation packages via package managers (Homebrew, apt, cargo, npm).

• Ambassador program for universities, tech communities, and local meetups.

• Bilingual documentation (Arabic/English) to serve two markets at once.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: avoid any code or trademark from existing products; the name, code, and design are entirely original.

• Licensing: choose a clear open license and review the licenses of all dependencies.

• Privacy: comply with data protection standards (GDPR) in cloud sync.

• Technical: sync conflicts between devices, data loss, and text file drift.

• Performance and compatibility: differences in terminals, operating systems, and command shells.

• Sustainability: maintaining development momentum and an active contributor community.

7) Proposed name and marketing slogan

Proposed name: Sanad

Marketing slogan: Your tasks in your terminal, with full privacy and absolute control.
