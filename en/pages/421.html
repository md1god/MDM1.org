Sahih Care — Full Project Plan

Accurate documentation, approved on the first try.

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout plan, and risks. The project is under construction and not yet launched.

Product plan: documentation and compliance assistant for disability and home care providers

1) Target audience

• Intellectual and developmental disability care agencies ([hidden source]): group homes, day programs, foundational support programs.

• Home Care / Home Health agencies that accept Medicaid payments.

• HCBS (Home & Community-Based Services) providers under Medicaid programs in the United States.

• Behavioral Health providers billed through Medicaid.

• Core segment: small and medium agencies (5-200 employees) with no dedicated compliance team that lose weekly hours to documentation.

2) Differentiators (why choose us)

• Field voice capture: the provider finishes their shift, speaks 30 seconds in English, Spanish, or Arabic, and AI converts it into an organized, compliant service note - no computer needed.

• Instant compliance scoring: every note receives a compliance score against Medicaid/HCBS rules with the note's specific gaps highlighted - not just a draft.

• Rule packs per state: Medicaid rules differ by state, and the product ships configurable packs for each state individually.

• Early billing denial sensing: monitors documentation patterns that historically led to payment rejections and warns before submission.

• Continuous audit readiness: periodic simulated audits showing the agency's documentation health score at any time.

• Human-in-the-loop: every note passes through supervisor approval before final sign-off - no blind automated delivery.

3) First release scope (max 8 tasks)

1. Convert voice into an organized service note (Transcription + Structuring).

2. Ready-made service note templates for [hidden source] and HCBS services.

3. Instant compliance scoring dashboard with gap explanations.

4. Two state rule packs at launch (chosen by market).

5. Export an audit-ready report in PDF format.

6. Dashboard of agency-level documentation health metrics.

7. Two-language support: English and Spanish.

8. Role-based access: provider / supervisor / administrator.

4) Pricing and revenue model

A free tier that expands with agency size:

· Plan · Price · Includes ·

· Free · $0 · Individual up to 2 users, 30 notes/month, one state template ·

· Professional · $99/month · up to 15 users, unlimited notes, full compliance scoring ·

· Agency · $299/month · up to 50 users, two-state packs, advanced audit reports, EHR system integration ·

· Enterprise · Custom · multiple states, SLA support, API integration, team training ·

Additional revenue streams: extra state packs ($50-150/state/month), setup and training, API access for integration agencies.

5) Global rollout plan (free channels)

• SEO and blog content: targeting long-tail search queries: "HCBS service note template", "Medicaid documentation checklist", "[hidden source] audit preparation".

• Free tool: a "documentation health score" calculator that collects agency managers' emails.

• LinkedIn: targeting [hidden source]/Homecare agency managers with short posts about common audit mistakes.

• Facebook groups: communities of home care agency owners and managers.

• Association partnerships: ARC, ANCOR, and Medicaid associations in each state.

• Free webinars: monthly series "How to pass a Medicaid audit with high scores".

• Podcasts: guest appearances on disability and home care podcasts.

• Reddit: helpful answers on r/homecare and r/medicaid without direct advertising.

• YouTube Shorts: correct documentation steps in 60 seconds.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Handling protected health information (PHI) requires full HIPAA compliance (encryption, access logging, processing agreements).

• Medicaid rules differ by state and change constantly - packs must be updated periodically.

• Legal liability if agencies adopt automatically generated notes without human review.

• No legal or regulatory advice provided - the product is an assistance tool only.

Technical:

• Risk of AI hallucination in clinical content - requires a mandatory human approval mechanism.

• Transcription accuracy across diverse accents and pharmaceutical names.

• Complex integration with different EHR/EMR systems.

• Data security and breach risks.

• Maintenance burden of per-state rule packs.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Sahih Care
