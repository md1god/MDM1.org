Raqeeb — Complete Project Plan

Log your dose, and stay organized without complexity.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, launch plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• New patients: those starting to follow a treatment protocol for the first time and needing quick, easy organization.

• Long-term patients: those following doses over a long period and needing a stable record without complexity.

• Older adults: those who prefer a simple, clear, and fast interface directly in the browser.

2) Differentiators (Why choose our product)

• Simplicity first: an interface oriented toward one step, away from long menus.

• Dual support: supports both injections and oral tablets in one place.

• Focus on organization, not advice: limited to personal logging, not diagnosis or prescription.

• Absolute privacy: priority on local storage, while minimizing any data collection as much as possible.

• Lightweight and fast: works entirely in the browser, with no need for installation or a mandatory account.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. [ ] Project structure: set up a lightweight web structure ready for development from scratch.

2. [ ] Dose log: record the date, time, dose, and type of use (injection or tablet).

3. [ ] Side effect tracking: a predefined list with a short notes field.

4. [ ] Injection site rotation: suggest a different site for each injection to avoid repetition.

5. [ ] Tablet schedule: a separate table for scheduling oral doses.

6. [ ] In-browser reminders: simple alerts for dose time.

7. [ ] Daily summary: a brief status view on one page.

8. [ ] Data export and import: save a manual backup in JSON format.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

· Item · Details ·

· Basic model · Completely free for personal tracking. ·

· Potential revenue source · Optional printable report templates, or non-medical educational resources. ·

· Revenue principle · Add-on and not mandatory, while keeping every organizational feature free. ·

5) Global Launch Plan with Free Channels

• Search engine optimization (SEO): focus on simple terms about dose organization in the most widely spoken languages.

• Support communities: participate in health support communities with general organizational tips and in a non-medical manner.

• Short content: produce short explainer videos that explain the organization method only.

• Gradual translation: start with the most requested user language, then expand gradually.

• Free tool directories: list the tool in open free tool directories without paying any fees.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

· Type · Risk · Mitigation ·

· Legal · The tool being classified as medical advice in some countries. · Display a clear disclaimer, limit it to a "personal log," and do not provide any diagnosis or prescription. ·

· Legal · Privacy obligations such as GDPR. · Prioritize local storage, do not collect sensitive data, and write a short, clear privacy policy. ·

· Technical · Data loss when the browser is cleared. · Provide a manual export and import feature in JSON format. ·

· Technical · Different browser behavior for reminders. · Rely on lightweight alerts, with a visible reminder instead of relying entirely on them. ·

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Tagline

Proposed name: Raqeeb

Marketing tagline: Log your dose, and stay organized without complexity.
