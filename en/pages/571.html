Mizan — Complete Project Plan

Balance your time, increase your focus step by step.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, expansion plan, and risks. The project is under development and has not been launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• University students: Suffer from distractions while studying and need simple support to maintain focus.

• Remote workers: Look for a clear separation between work time and leisure time without harsh restrictions.

• Parents and educators: Want to help their children build healthy digital habits gradually.

• Self-improvement enthusiasts: Those looking for solutions that motivate them internally rather than through direct punishment.

2) Points of Differentiation (Why Choose Our Product)

• Positive motivation instead of punishment: Instead of a painful "cost", the user earns free minutes by completing recorded productive tasks.

• Flexibility as needed: App blocking can be enabled for a set period or for a specific app, with rules customizable per context.

• Simple and pressure-free: No equipment required, no physical burden, and no social pressure.

• Focus on habit, not control: Helps build gradual self-discipline rather than creating a constant daily struggle.

3) Scope of the First Version (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. Blocked apps list: Easily select which apps to block.

2. Focus mode: Enable blocking for a specific time period with a countdown timer.

3. Productivity balance: Earn free minutes by completing short tasks recorded by the user.

4. Smart blocking screen: Display a gentle reminder and a practical alternative when attempting to open a blocked app.

5. Daily statistics: Show actual focus time vs. opening attempts.

6. Automatic rules: Block apps outside of designated work or study hours.

7. Limited emergency mode: Allow a small number of exceptional unlocks per day with usage tracking.

8. Completely free to start: All core features are available with no subscription in the first version.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free to start: All features of the first version are completely free.

• Optional subscription: A monthly/yearly license to add advanced features in the future (detailed weekly reports, ready-made templates for multiple routines, and cross-device sync).

• No ads: No ads will ever be displayed to maintain a distraction-free experience.

• Value-focused: Revenue will come from those who want to expand their capabilities, not from pressuring core users.

5) Global Distribution Plan Through Free Channels

• Productivity communities: Quietly participate in habit-sharing communities (by providing real value, not spam).

• Self-learning platforms: Collaborate with productivity bloggers to present the idea in the context of habit building.

• Organic search: Optimize the product description for keywords related to focus and digital habits.

• Short-form content: Showcase before/after usage screens in an educational format without exaggerated promises.

• Word of mouth: Focus on a simple user experience to make natural sharing among friends easy.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal:

• Data privacy: Do not collect detailed usage data; only keep local statistics on the device.

• Blocking permissions: Comply with device platform policies regarding app-blocking permissions and restrictions.

• Technical:

• System restrictions: Some systems impose restrictions on background app blocking, so the solution must be designed to work within available capabilities.

• Battery consumption: Avoid continuous monitoring; rely only on event-based notifications.

• Compatibility: Start with one operating system and test thoroughly before expanding to other platforms.

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Slogan

Proposed name: Mizan

Marketing slogan: Balance your time, increase your focus step by step.
