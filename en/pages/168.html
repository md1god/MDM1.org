Natiq — Complete Project Plan

Watch the video in your language while keeping its original character.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Non-native viewers: those who want to understand video content in different languages without needing to learn them.

• Students and researchers: those who rely on foreign educational clips in their studies.

• Content creators: those who want to expand their global audience quickly.

• Regular users: those interested in watching entertainment content in their native language.

2) Differentiators (Why choose our product)

• Tailored experience: a simple, clean design focused on viewing without cluttering the interface.

• Contextual accuracy: translation based on the meaning of the sentence rather than translating words individually.

• Lightweight performance: runs efficiently without heavy resource consumption on most devices.

• Free-to-start model: available for free so everyone can try it without barriers.

• Privacy first: processing data locally as much as possible, while minimizing the transfer of sensitive information.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Interface design: creating an original design that fits various screen sizes.

2. Browser extension development: building an extension that supports popular browsers.

3. Translation service integration: connecting a text translation tool to get accurate translations.

4. Voice generation: adding a TTS system that preserves the natural character of the voice.

5. Content synchronization: aligning the timing of the translated audio with video events.

6. Core language support: selecting a limited number of the most widely used languages.

7. Simple settings: providing control over audio speed and selection.

8. Basic testing: verifying performance on different types of short videos.

4) Pricing and revenue model

· Plan · Features · Price ·

· Free · A limited number of translation minutes per month, and support for core languages. · $0 ·

· Basic · A significant increase in the number of minutes, support for additional languages, and higher speed. · $5 / month ·

• Revenue model: Freemium (free to start, then subscription).

• Focus: growing the free user base first before promoting the subscription.

5) Global distribution plan using free channels

• Search engine optimization (SEO): publishing educational articles explaining the problem and solution in multiple languages.

• Online communities: sharing the value in forums and groups interested in education and translation.

• Short content: producing short videos that demonstrate the experience directly.

• Example library: providing free demo clips that everyone can watch.

• Word-of-mouth marketing: encouraging users to share their experience with others.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

· Type · Potential risks · Proposed solutions ·

· Legal · Infringement of intellectual property rights or platform terms of use. · Focus on personal use, and clarify usage limits in the product. ·

· Technical · Translation accuracy for specialized terminology, or slow performance. · Starting with popular languages, and improving lightweight models with continuous updates. ·

· Security and privacy · Leakage of user data. · Prioritizing local processing, and reducing the collection of personal data. ·

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Natiq

Tagline: Watch the video in your language while keeping its original character.
