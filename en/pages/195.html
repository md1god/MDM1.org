Qantara — Complete Project Plan

One window to connect to all your servers, regardless of protocol, securely and fast.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital product: idea, target audience, product, business model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under development and has not been launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• System engineers and server administrators who move daily between dozens of devices via RDP, SSH, VNC, and Telnet.

• Technical support and remote support teams that need to organize client connections into folders and tags.

• Software developers and testers who run multiple sessions on servers and different environments.

• Small and medium-sized teams that want to share their connection lists securely without expensive enterprise solutions.

2) Key Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• Native support for Linux, macOS, and Windows (unlike platform-limited solutions), with a modern and consistent interface.

• Locally encrypted secrets vault (AES-256) with integration to system key managers, instead of storing passwords insecurely.

• Share connection lists with the team with granular permission roles, without needing a VPN or heavy server.

• Session recording and audit logs (SSH/RDP) for compliance teams, with instant search across all connections.

• Open-core model that provides a fully complete free experience, charging only for team collaboration and enterprise integrations.

3) MVP Scope (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. Multi-session tabbed interface with split view support.

2. Initial client support for RDP, SSH, VNC, and Telnet protocols.

3. Organize connections into folders, tags, and instant search.

4. Locally encrypted secrets vault with master password and SSH keys.

5. Import/export connection lists in standardized formats (JSON/CSV).

6. Session manager with automatic reconnection and layout persistence.

7. Basic audit log for recording sessions and events.

8. Signed desktop client for Windows, Linux, and macOS with automatic updates.

4) Pricing and Business Model

• Free forever: all core features, unlimited number of connections, for individual use.

• Pro (Individual) — $4/month: end-to-end encrypted cloud backup, cross-device sync, custom themes and interfaces.

• Team — $8/user/month: shared connection lists, permission roles, centralized audit logs.

• Enterprise — custom pricing: SSO/SAML, self-hosted deployment, priority support, SOC2 compliance.

• Early funding through open-source sponsors and community support programs to avoid initial external investment.

5) Global Distribution Plan Through Free Channels

• Publish the open-source repository on GitHub with bilingual documentation (Arabic/English).

• Launch on Product Hunt, Hacker News, and Reddit (r/sysadmin, r/selfhosted).

• Technical content on YouTube and dev.to: "Manage 100 servers from a single window."

• Ready-made packages via Homebrew, winget, Flathub, and AUR to lower installation barriers.

• Build a Discord community with early access, a contributor program, and community-driven translations.

• Partnerships with developer tool bloggers and free sponsorship opportunities.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal: all code must be written from scratch and avoid any code, visual assets, or names that are similar; comply with third-party library licenses (GPL vs MIT) and conduct legal review before merging.

• Third-party licenses: audit the dependency list to avoid incompatible license contamination.

• Technical: complexity of implementing RDP/VNC agents across platforms, securing the vault, and protecting sensitive data in transit.

• Potential misuse: implement policies to prevent abuse of the client for unauthorized access.

• User adoption: gain the trust of users migrating from legacy tools by ensuring import compatibility and a frictionless experience.

• Sustainability: ensure long-term development under an open-core model without compromising the quality of the free version.

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Tagline

Proposed Name: Qantara (قنطرة)

Marketing Tagline: One window to connect to all your servers, regardless of protocol, securely and fast.
