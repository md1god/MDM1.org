Mishkat — Complete Project Plan

Your photos are understood, never uploaded — your memories stay on your own server.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, business model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under development and has not been launched yet.

Product Plan: AI-Powered Home Photo Management

1) Target Audience

• Owners of large photo collections (tens of thousands) who refuse to upload them to cloud services.

• Families and active mobile photographers who need fast search (faces, locations, objects, colors).

• Home server owners, fans of small devices (like Raspberry Pi), and privacy enthusiasts.

• Amateur photographers and users in countries with restrictions on exporting data abroad.

2) Key Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• Works entirely offline after installation — photos never leave your device.

• Natural language search in Arabic and English, such as: "photos of children at the beach in the evening".

• Lightweight: small ML models that run on CPU with no GPU required, and work on small devices.

• Smart automatic grouping: albums like "Travel", "Events", and "Similar Faces" without manual setup.

• Optional encrypted sync between your devices, while keeping the original copy on your home server.

• Simple and fast design: a single timeline, instant search, no ads, and no tracking.

3) Scope for the First Release (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. A simple self-hosted web server (Docker) with Arabic and English interfaces.

2. Import photos and videos from folders and mobile devices (optional encrypted uploads).

3. Automatic timeline based on date and location (from EXIF data).

4. Search by objects, colors, and time using a small local model.

5. Local face recognition with automatic grouping of people (with user consent).

6. Automatic smart albums and visual similarity grouping.

7. Share a temporary link for a single album without exposing the entire library.

8. Basic backup and sync between two devices over an encrypted connection.

4) Pricing and Business Model

• Free: full personal version for home use on a single device.

• Optional paid: annual license for advanced features (video scenes, improved search, priority support, small commercial license).

• Support services: ready-to-install packages for small devices, and setup consultation for families or small offices.

• No ads and no data sales ever — privacy is the product itself.

5) Global Distribution Plan Through Free Channels

• Articles and short videos about "how to protect your family photos on a home server" on YouTube, TikTok, and X.

• Participation in privacy and Self-hosting communities (forums and groups on Reddit and Mastodon).

• Trial copies and contests like "best photo library organization" on social media.

• Open documentation and interface translations for users in the Middle East, Europe, and Latin America.

• Partnerships with Arabic and international tech content creators focused on privacy and photography.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal: data protection laws (GDPR and others) impose strict rules on face recognition — it must be enabled manually and clearly explained, and usage without consent must be prohibited.

• Privacy: photographing people and creating albums for them raises concerns; the open source license must be documented accurately.

• Technical: CPU and memory usage for ML on small devices; inaccurate search results can erode trust.

• Storage and indexing: large libraries require efficient indexing; sync complexity over unstable networks.

7) Proposed Name and Tagline

Proposed name: Mishkat (Mishkat)

Tagline: Your photos are understood, never uploaded — your memories stay on your own server.
