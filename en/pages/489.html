Autovact — Complete Project Plan

Check your electronic invoices and correct their errors in seconds.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, expansion plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not yet been launched.

1) Target Audience

• Small and medium-sized enterprises: that issue invoices to foreign clients and need to comply with electronic standards.

• Accountants and accounting offices: that deal with multiple invoices from different clients.

• Exporters and international traders: that operate in markets requiring certified electronic invoices.

• Designers and developers: who need a quick tool to verify invoice formats before sending.

2) Points of Distinction (Why Choose Our Product)

• Extremely simple interface: verify an invoice in less than 30 seconds with no deep technical expertise required.

• Focus on smart correction: it not only reports errors, but also suggests direct, applicable corrections.

• Multi-language support: interface in Arabic, English, French, and others for easy global expansion.

• Secure local processing: verification can be done without uploading sensitive files to servers in the free mode.

• Smart conversion: convert PDF or CSV files to certified formats while preserving original data.

3) Scope of the First Version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Basic verification: check common XML formats and ensure all required data is complete.

2. Error detection: display a clear list of errors with line numbers and correction suggestions.

3. Tax ID verification: check tax identification numbers and country codes.

4. PDF conversion support: extract data from PDF files and convert it to the appropriate format.

5. CSV conversion support: convert simple data tables to the electronic invoice format.

6. Quick reports: display a summary of the result with a compliance percentage.

7. Offline mode: enable basic verification without an internet connection.

8. Fast interface: simple design that works on both mobile and desktop.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free starter model: verify and convert up to 5 invoices per day for free.

• Professional plan: low-cost monthly subscription (unlimited processing + advanced reports + technical support).

• API service for enterprises: sell usage licenses to accounting software providers for system integration.

• Licensing for large companies: custom solutions for integrating the tool into their internal systems.

• Clear freemium model: core features always free, with paid options for advanced requirements.

5) Global Expansion Plan with Free Channels

• Search engine optimization: create educational pages about electronic invoice requirements for each country for free.

• Free tool directories: register in free tool directories and relevant open-source product directories.

• Professional communities: participate in forums for accountants and international traders, providing real value.

• Short educational videos: create content that explains how to solve common problems without excessive direct promotion.

• Partner programs: collaborate with small accounting offices to recommend the tool for free.

• Content translation: translate the educational guide into the top 10 languages to increase global reach.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

Legal risks

• Changing standards: constant updates to requirements for each country requiring continuous monitoring.

• Verification liability: it must be clarified that the tool is an aid and not a substitute for legal advice.

• Data protection: compliance with international data protection laws when processing files.

• Differing interpretations: different interpretations of standards between countries may lead to inconsistent results.

Technical risks

• File format complexity: wide variety in PDF and CSV structures makes processing all of them difficult.

• Extraction accuracy: errors in reading data from complex or scanned PDF files.

• Processing performance: slow processing of large files without excessive resource consumption.

• Browser compatibility: ensuring smooth operation of the tool across different browsers and devices.

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Slogan

Proposed name: Autovact

Marketing slogan: Check your electronic invoices and correct their errors in seconds.
