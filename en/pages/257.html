Malameh — Complete Project Plan

Your professional headshot, in studio quality, in minutes.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the go-to-market plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: AI-powered professional headshot generator

1) Target audience

• Job seekers and professionals on LinkedIn who need a professional profile photo quickly and at low cost.

• Small business owners and freelancers building a personal visual identity.

• Content creators and influencers who need varied images of the same persona across multiple platforms.

• Students and recent graduates in markets where nearby or affordable photo studios are unavailable.

• Users in privacy-sensitive regions who prefer their photos to be processed without being shared publicly.

2) Points of differentiation (why choose our product)

• Privacy first: face detection and model training run in an isolated space (Private Workspace), with automatic deletion of the original photos and the model after a period set by the user.

• A real free tier to start: a full trial without a bank card, with a high daily limit of high-quality images.

• Identity consistency quality: generation that preserves facial features across lighting, backgrounds, and clothing (Identity Consistency) instead of relying on a single style.

• Linguistic and regional support: an Arabic/English interface and consideration of clothing and background tastes suited to different markets.

• Pricing transparency: no hidden subscriptions, and one-time payment when needed.

• Platform-ready export: presets sized for LinkedIn, Instagram, and company badges.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Upload 3–10 personal photos with automatic quality checks (lighting, sharpness, face angle).

2. Train a custom personal model (Fine-tuning / LoRA) for each user inside an isolated space.

3. Generate images in formal attire (suit, shirt, work uniform) that can be selected.

4. Generate multiple backgrounds (white studio, gray, office, outdoor).

5. A preview and download dashboard at high resolution with ready-made sizes for platforms.

6. A free credits system (Credits) with a daily limit and a clear status dashboard.

7. Secure deletion settings: delete the original photos and the model with one click and on an automatic schedule.

8. Simple login + a personal dashboard with an activity log.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: 8 high-quality images per month, optional watermark, limited backgrounds.

• One-time payment package (Starter): a small payment for 50 images + watermark removal.

• Monthly Pro subscription: practically unlimited images (fair-use limit), queue priority, advanced backgrounds and clothing.

• Teams package (Teams): multiple seats for companies with centralized management and unified visual identity standards.

• Additional revenue streams: upgrades for instant rendering (Priority Rendering) and API licensing for platforms.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Viral content on TikTok and Instagram Reels: "before/after" videos showing the transformation of an ordinary photo into a professional one.

• Referral program (Referrals): free credits for both parties for each successful invitation.

• Professional communities: useful posts on LinkedIn, Reddit, and Product Hunt at launch.

• Search engine optimization (SEO): articles on "how to get a professional profile photo" in multiple languages.

• Partnerships with employment centers and universities: a free offering for students and graduates.

• Content creator program: free access in exchange for an honest review.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Image misuse: generating images of people without their consent — addressed by a liveness check (Liveness Check) and blocking celebrities.

• Privacy and data protection: compliance with GDPR and local data protection laws through encryption and automatic deletion.

• Intellectual property and licensing: the need to verify the licenses of open models and the data used in training.

• Bias and representation: poor result quality for some skin tones and face shapes — addressed with diverse data and continuous evaluation.

• Compute cost: high GPU cost — addressed with smart queuing, caching, and batch generation.

• Technical quality race: rapid evolution of models — addressed by building an abstraction layer that allows models to be swapped easily.
