Wasla — Complete Project Plan

The shortest path between you and every customer.

Complete execution plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and not yet launched.

Product Plan: Wasla

1) Target Audience

· Priority · Segment · Size/Need ·

· Primary · Small and medium delivery companies (5–50 drivers) in MENA · Manage routes via Excel or paper ·

· Primary · E-commerce stores, pharmacies, and restaurants shipping daily within the city · Fluctuating orders throughout the day ·

· Secondary · Maintenance and home service companies (HVAC, plumbing, electrical) · Mobile teams with scheduled appointments ·

· Tertiary · Educational institutions, charities, and emerging logistics companies · Limited budget ·

Decision maker: Operations Manager / Business Owner. End user: the driver.

2) Differentiators (Why choose our product)

1. Complete interface from day one: no code or server integration needed — upload a CSV and get the route within two minutes.

2. Real-time re-optimization: new order or driver delay → instant automatic reordering of remaining routes only.

3. Human control over the solution: dispatcher pins or drags a stop between drivers, and the solver re-optimizes around those constraints (human-in-the-loop).

4. True closed loop: dispatch dashboard + driver app (PWA works offline) + notifications, not just an algorithm library.

5. Powerful open-core solver: modern algorithms (HGS, enhanced Clarke–Wright) with part of the solver published on GitHub to build technical community trust.

6. Your data, yours: free self-hosted version, and managed cloud version for those who want convenience.

7. Arabic-ready: bilingual UI and maps covering Arab city streets better than global alternatives.

8. Ready integrations: Shopify, WooCommerce, Odoo, and REST API + Webhooks.

3) MVP Scope (8 tasks maximum)

1. User, team, and billing system (Freemium).

2. Import orders from CSV/Excel with address validation (Geocoding).

3. Route optimization UI: load capacity, time windows, max drive time, single depot.

4. Interactive map (Leaflet + OpenStreetMap) with manual drag-and-drop adjustment.

5. Dispatch dashboard: pin stops, drag stops between drivers, quick re-optimization.

6. Driver PWA app: stops list, navigate to Google/Apple Maps, proof of delivery with signature, works offline with later sync.

7. Public REST API + Webhooks (new order, delay, completion).

8. Basic reports: execution time, route efficiency, estimated cost.

Deferred to v2: Multi-depot, multi-vehicle types, traffic prediction, POS integration.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

· Plan · Price · Limits · Audience ·

· Free · $0/month · 5 vehicles, 200 stops/month, light watermark · Trial and adoption ·

· Pro · $49/month · 25 vehicles, time windows, full API · Small businesses ·

· Business · $199/month · 100 vehicles, multi-user, reports, priority support · Medium businesses ·

· Enterprise / Self-Hosted · Annual license starting at $4,999 · Private hosting, SSO, SLA · Large organizations ·

• Add-ons: $4 per additional vehicle over limit, extra integrations ($99 one-time).

• Extended free: charities and educational institutions (free Pro plan) — builds reputation and social reach.

• Payment channels: Stripe + Paymob/e-wallet for emerging markets.

• Target margin: ≥ 80% (software + optimized cloud hosting).

5) Global Go-to-Market Plan via Free Channels

1. Bilingual SEO: Arabic and English articles on "delivery route optimization" and "last-mile cost" — targeting long-tail search questions.

2. Open-source solver core on GitHub: attracts developers and converts them to paid cloud users.

3. YouTube/TikTok: short tutorials "from Excel file to final route in two minutes" in Arabic, English, and Spanish.

4. Free launches: Product Hunt, Hacker News Show HN, Reddit (r/logistics, r/opensource) on the same day.

5. Specialized communities: Facebook and LinkedIn groups for logistics managers in Saudi Arabia, Egypt, UAE, Indonesia, and Mexico.

6. App marketplaces: Shopify App Store and Odoo Marketplace — free acquisition from platform traffic.

7. Agency partnerships: local ad agencies pitch "Wasla" to their clients for referral commission (30-day free trial).

8. Referral program: free month for both parties, free version for academics and teachers.

9. Monthly free live webinars with real case studies (with client consent).
