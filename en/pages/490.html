Mizan — Complete Project Plan

Before you buy... know your net profit in seconds.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, business model, growth plan, and risks. The project is under development and has not been launched yet.

Original Product Plan

1) Target Audience

• New sellers: Those going to clearance sales or auctions for the first time and need a quick evaluation.

• Collectors: Those looking for the best items in a batch before making a decision.

• Small sellers: Those who want to evaluate potential profit before buying, not after.

2) Points of Differentiation (Why Choose Our Product)

• Focus on net profit: Calculates fees and costs and displays net profit, not just the price.

• Fully mobile-oriented: Designed for use in the field, with a simple and fast interface.

• Smart filtering: Saves time and shows only items with potential profit, with an explanation of the reason.

• Support for regional markets: Provides price estimates closer to the local market, instead of an inaccurate global average.

• Lightweight and data-smart: Works efficiently on slow internet and reduces data consumption.

3) Scope of First Version (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. Quick capture: Take a photo or select one from the gallery for a batch.

2. Item detection: Identify the most visible items within the image.

3. Price range retrieval: Fetch a real price range based on the market.

4. Net profit calculation: Deduct suggested platform fees to display net profit.

5. Sort by profit: Display items sorted from highest net profit to lowest.

6. Ad draft: Suggest a title and short description for each item.

7. Filter unprofitable items: Hide items with negative profit, with the option to show them.

8. Temporary session save: Save results for a short time without requiring the user to create an account.

4) Pricing and Business Model

• Free to start: All features of the first version are completely free.

• Optional plan: A very small subscription for multiple batches per day or batch export when needed, while keeping the free tier sufficient for most users.

• Alternative revenue: Non-intrusive partnerships with selling platforms, without forcing ads on the user.

5) Global Expansion Plan with Free Channels

• Short practical video: Show a step-by-step evaluation process in seconds on short video platforms.

• Educational content: A simple guide titled "How to calculate net profit before buying" in the most widely used languages.

• Community participation: Contribute useful tips in collector communities without excessive direct promotion.

• Free tool directories: Submit to relevant free tool directories once the product is stable.

• Search engine optimization: Focus on local, field-specific search terms to increase organic visibility.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal: Comply with privacy regulations, avoid storing user photos for unnecessary purposes, and fully respect data policies.

• Technical: Rapid price fluctuations in markets, difficulty identifying rare items, and the need for compatibility with slow internet.

• Operational: Reliance on accurate pricing data while avoiding any closed or protected sources.

7) Suggested Name and Marketing Slogan

Suggested name: Mizan

Marketing slogan: Before you buy... know your net profit in seconds.
