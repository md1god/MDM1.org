Mirsad — Complete Project Plan

One interface to understand the web and track your brand's visibility across answer engines.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target audience

• Web and API developers and data teams who want one interface for extraction, crawling, and search instead of integrating multiple tools.

• SEO, GEO, and brand teams tracking their visibility inside ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google AI Overview.

• Marketing and PR agencies managing multiple clients and needing exportable reports.

• Early-stage SaaS companies and e-commerce stores monitoring competitors, prices, and content.

• Independent developers and researchers who prefer to start free before committing to a paid plan.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• A unified API that combines extraction, crawling, search, and executing actions on websites, with consistent JSON outputs.

• Measuring brand visibility inside answer engines with source and citation tracking, not just counting Mentions.

• Transparent Credit-based pricing, with a generous free tier and no mandatory keys or sales calls.

• Multilingual and multi-region support, with a special focus on Arabic and English content.

• Asynchronous processing, Webhooks, and SDKs for Python and JavaScript, plus MCP integration for modern development environments.

• A clear commitment to robots.txt, consent, and permissions, with audit logs and usage limits.

• A fast developer experience: interactive documentation, a Sandbox, and instant trial keys.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Unified extraction API: fetching pages with JavaScript Rendering and extracting structured fields via Schemas.

2. Multi-page crawler: Sitemap, specified depth, deduplication, and task scheduling.

3. Web search API: unified results with links, snippets, and sources.

4. Limited website actions library: about 30 templates for common actions, with the ability to add templates later.

5. AI query runner: sending Prompts to supported answer engines and collecting answers and citations.

6. Brand visibility dashboard: Share of Voice, Mentions, Sentiment, sources, and competitor comparison.

7. Accounts, limits, and consumption: API Keys, Credits, invoices, quotas, and usage logs.

8. Alerts and export: Webhooks, email alerts, and CSV/JSON export.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: 1,000 Credits per month, two projects, low rate limit, community support, no credit card.

• Pro: $29 per month, 25,000 Credits, 10 projects, Webhooks, and export.

• Growth: $99 per month, 150,000 Credits, 50 projects, processing priority.

• Scale/Enterprise: custom pricing, SLA, custom limits, private deployment or Self-hosted option.

• Revenue model: subscriptions + additional Credits on overage + enterprise services. Failed requests are not counted, with a 20% annual discount.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Strong technical documentation and programmatic SEO pages that compare use cases rather than naming competitors.

• Open GitHub repositories for SDKs, MCP Server, and ready-made examples.

• Launch on Product Hunt, Hacker News, Reddit, Indie Hackers, and developer communities.

• Attractive free tools: an AI answer engine brand visibility checker and an extractability checker.

• Regular educational content: tutorials, use cases, YouTube videos, and a newsletter.

• Free integrations with Zapier, Make, and n8n, plus ready-made agency templates.

• A referral program and partnerships with agencies and university communities, with documentation support in Arabic and English.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: website terms of use, robots.txt, database rights, and copyright. Mitigation: respectful crawling, honoring Opt-out, and explicit user permissions.

• Privacy: GDPR, CCPA, and data retention. Mitigation: minimized retention, encryption, and selectable storage locations.

• Technical: IP blocking, CAPTCHA, rendering cost, and changing website structures. Mitigation: multiple agents, caching, and quality monitoring.

• Technical: changes to AI providers' interfaces and inconsistent answers. Mitigation: Provider-agnostic adapters and documenting accuracy limits.

• Security: key leakage and Prompt Injection. Mitigation: session isolation, auditing, and strict usage limits.

• Commercial: cost of free calls. Mitigation: clear Free limits and measuring cost per Credit.
