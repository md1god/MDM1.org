NAFAS — Full Project Plan

Turn your text into speech in your own voice, right in your browser, free and private.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Content creators: YouTubers, podcasters, TikTok creators, and video makers who need voices for explanations or voiceovers.

• Freelance translators and dubbers: To sync videos in different languages without hiring a voice actor.

• People with disabilities or special needs: Those who prefer generating voices for their own purposes or for producing automated reading materials.

• Learners and researchers: To convert texts into audio files for listening anywhere.

• Small businesses: To produce training materials or audio ads on a limited budget.

2) Differentiators (Why choose our product)

• Guaranteed privacy: It works entirely in the browser without sending audio files or texts to external servers.

• Instant speed: It starts working immediately without creating an account or entering an API key.

• Very simple: One clear interface gives access to all features without complexity.

• Versatile: It focuses on the most important user needs instead of a clutter of multiple features.

• Completely free to start: It removes the cost barrier entirely for the average user.

3) Scope of the First Version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Interface design: Build a simple, clear interface using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

2. Audio import system: The ability to upload a short voice sample (10–30 seconds) from the user's device.

3. Voice pattern simulation: Generate a local voice model based on the uploaded sample.

4. Text-to-speech: Enter text and generate it in a cloned voice directly.

5. Built-in audio player: Play, pause, and download the audio file in .mp3 format.

6. Local saving: Save projects locally on the user's device without an external database.

7. Offline mode: Ensure it works without an internet connection after the first download.

8. Quick testing: Check performance on modern browsers before launch.

4) Pricing and Profit Model

· Source · Details · Expected estimate ·

· Free for all · All core features are completely free · $0 ·

· Professional extension · Add non-essential features such as advanced accuracy improvement or additional templates (optional) · $0 – $0 (not required) ·

· Partnerships · Optional integration with other open-source tools · No direct revenue ·

Profit model: It relies on free growth first while leaving the door open for crowdfunding or sponsorship only when needed, without obligating users to pay.

5) Global Distribution Plan with Free Channels

· Channel · Action · Goal ·

· Open-source platforms: GitHub/GitLab · Publish the project with a clear, free explanation · Attract developers and technical users ·

· Reddit · Participate in content creator communities by solving their problem without direct promotion · Build initial trust ·

· ProductHunt · Free launch with a description focused on privacy and ease of use · Global reach ·

· YouTube Shorts · Show a short video demonstrating text-to-speech in the browser · Attract content creators ·

· Discord/Forums · Help users and share the tool when requested · Natural organic growth ·

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

· Type · Risk · Mitigation plan ·

· Legal · Misuse of voice cloning for fraud or defamation · Add a clear warning at use and encourage ethical use only ·

· Legal · Voice rights issues in some countries · Focus on personal and creative use, and state that responsibility lies with the user ·

· Technical · Strong user device requirements for audio processing · Optimize models to run on mid-range devices, and support automatic accuracy reduction ·

· Technical · Browser performance inconsistency · Test on Chrome, Firefox, Edge first before expanding ·

· Technical · Model size inside the browser · Use lightweight, compressed models that run with WebAssembly ·

7) Suggested Name and Marketing Slogan

Suggested name: NAFAS

Marketing slogan: Turn your text into speech in your own voice, right in your browser, free and private.
