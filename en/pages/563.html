Nebulink — Complete Project Plan

Smart and secure marketing automation, designed specifically for developers and intelligent agents.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, expansion plan, and risks. The project is under construction and not yet launched.

1) Target Audience

• Software developers: Those who build apps or tools and want to automate marketing actions without integrating each platform separately.

• AI creators: Intelligent agents that need to perform marketing actions safely and accurately within their permissions.

• Small startups: With limited resources looking for a solution to save time without hiring a full marketing team.

• Independent developers: Looking for a quick and effective solution to integrate marketing automation into their projects.

2) Points of Differentiation (Why Choose Our Product)

• True focus on intelligent agents: Designed specifically to be used by intelligent agents, with precise permissions for each action.

• Greater simplicity: Instead of covering everything, we focus on the most commonly used actions in the first version to reduce complexity.

• Strict permissions: Every operation requires clear authorization, with the ability to restrict it by purpose.

• Transparent log: Display every executed action with its time for easy review and verification.

• Truly free start: Can be used without a credit card, with clear and understandable limits.

3) Scope of the First Version (Maximum 8 Tasks)

· No. · Task · Purpose ·

· 1 · Design a simplified API interface · Define basic endpoints with the fewest possible. ·

· 2 · Authentication and permissions system · Create secure accounts and connect platforms. ·

· 3 · Connect two basic platforms · Focus on the most used instead of covering many. ·

· 4 · Publish posts · Execute the most frequent action securely. ·

· 5 · Respond to interactions · Support simple interaction with followers. ·

· 6 · Usage limits system · Clearly apply free version limits. ·

· 7 · Event log · Display all operations to the user. ·

· 8 · Quick documentation for developers · A short and easy guide to get started in minutes. ·

4) Pricing and Profit Model

· Plan · Usage · Monthly Price · Notes ·

· Free · Reasonable action limits · $0 · Ideal for starting and testing without a credit card. ·

· Basic · Increased limits and limited support · $9 · For small startups. ·

· Professional · Higher limits and priority support · $29 · For growing projects with greater needs. ·

Profit model: Monthly subscriptions, while keeping the free model as a priority to attract developers initially.

5) Global Expansion Plan with Free Channels

• GitHub: Publish a short practical example showing usage, focusing on intelligent agents.

• Dev.to and Hashnode: Write short educational articles about secure marketing automation for developers.

• X and Bluesky: Share simple and short visual explanations to attract developers.

• Developer forums: Actively participate in discussions without direct advertising, sharing solutions.

• Free developer tool lists: Submit to lists dedicated to development tools with accurate and concise descriptions.

• Ambassador programs: Invite developers to use the tool for free in exchange for feedback and suggestions for improvement.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

· Type · Risk · Mitigation Method ·

· Legal · Platform policies prohibit some types of automation. · Respect terms of use, provide restricted settings, and enable full user control over each action. ·

· Legal · Data privacy issues. · Store as little data as possible, do not save passwords, and adhere to the principle of minimum data. ·

· Technical · Sudden changes to platform APIs. · Design a flexible and quickly updatable adapter layer with simple tests. ·

· Technical · Rate limits. · Smart queue system and internal limits per user to avoid exceeding. ·

· Technical · Security of tokens and permissions. · Use short-lived tokens, precise permission separation, and log all actions. ·

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Slogan

Proposed name: Nebulink

Marketing slogan: Smart and secure marketing automation, designed specifically for developers and intelligent agents.
