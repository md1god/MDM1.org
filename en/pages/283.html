Midad — Full Project Plan

Midad — publish your content freely, without complexity.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

«Midad» product plan

1) Target audience

• Independent publishers and blog writers who want a professional site without technical complexity.

• Small and medium businesses that need multilingual content with a small editing team.

• Developers and digital service agencies looking for a flexible, quickly customizable platform.

• Associations, schools, and non-profit communities with limited budgets (need-driven).

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Blocks content editor with instant preview and full right-to-left (RTL) support.

• Optional AI assistant for crafting headlines, summaries, and search engine optimization inside the editor.

• Data architecture fully decoupled from presentation: the same content is published to web, mobile app, and API interface.

• Fine-grained permissions system (roles and page/path-level permissions) for small editing teams.

• Local execution or our cloud hosting with one click, with full data export at any time.

• Completely free to start (open-source self-hosted edition), with payment for managed services.

3) Scope of the first release (at most 8 tasks)

1. Project setup: JSON Schema-based document storage + PostgreSQL, identity and sign-in.

2. Blocks editor: intro, image, quote, list, attachment — with autosave and versioning (history snapshots).

3. Permissions system: roles (owner, editor, contributor, reader) and path-based permissions.

4. Public API interface: content read/publish with access keys (API keys) and automatic documentation.

5. Site rendering: React Server Components templates + static page generation (SSG) and basic SEO optimization.

6. Language proofing: Arabic and English support with writing direction and localized dates.

7. Dashboard: basic visit statistics (without external tracking), publish scheduling, and drafts.

8. Shipping and releases: Docker package + 5-minute run guide, and automated tests for the core.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free forever: self-hosted edition with core functionality (up to 3 users and 2 subdomains).

• Pro — $19/month: managed hosting, custom domain, daily backups, 10 users.

• Teams — $59/month: AI assistant, advanced approval workflows, higher usage limits.

• Enterprise — on-request pricing: private hosting, service level agreement, and dedicated support.

• Additional revenue source: paid templates and plugins in a marketplace, with a 15% commission on store payments.

5) Global rollout plan using free channels

1. Public repository on GitHub with complete documentation and a live demo — build credibility first.

2. Technical articles and tutorials in Arabic and English (blog + Dev.to + Hashnode) on solving editing problems.

3. Short-form content accounts (X and LinkedIn and YouTube Shorts) showing before/after building a site in 5 minutes.

4. Community on Discord/Telegram for support and contributions, with releases posted on Product Hunt at launch.

5. Partnerships with web designers and free mentors (YouTube channel sponsorships and marketing courses).

6. Search engine optimization for auto-generated pages (landing page templates and comparison pages).

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: strict compliance with the carefully chosen MIT/AGPL license, and avoiding trademark infringement of names and logos; clear terms of use for the AI assistant and user data protection (GDPR) with data processing agreements.

• Legal: liability for content published by users — abuse policy and a rapid reporting mechanism.

• Technical: difficulty competing in a crowded market — addressed by focusing on Arabic and a first-run experience within 5 minutes.

• Technical: the first release's reliance on external frameworks (Django/React) may cause costly changes on upgrades — addressed by automated tests and thin abstraction layers.

• Technical: data leakage or content loss — addressed by encrypted backups and periodic restore tests.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Midad

Marketing tagline: Midad — publish your content freely, without complexity.
