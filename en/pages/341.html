Mithāq — Full Project Plan

Send and sign your document instantly.. Pay only when needed, with no subscription.

A full implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Freelancers: simple contracts, invoices, work forms.

• Small and medium business owners: agreements with clients or suppliers without taking on monthly subscription fees.

• Real estate agents: memoranda of understanding or quick signature forms.

• Small specialized companies: temporary hiring, internal approvals, service acceptance forms.

• Anyone who needs to sign a single document or only a few.

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• Pay only when needed: instead of buying a monthly seat, the user pays only for actual usage.

• Free to start: a limited free credit allows a real trial without a bank card.

• No account required to sign: the signer presses a direct link and completes signing within minutes.

• Extreme simplicity: the fewest steps to upload a document, add signature fields, and send the link.

• Clear audit trail: includes signing time, approximate IP address, and device type to preserve simple legal proof.

• Lightweight and fast design: focused on the "send and sign" spirit without complex menus.

• Price transparency: the price is shown clearly before purchase or before consuming any credit.

• Global readiness: a simple English and Arabic interface for ease of expansion.

3) Scope of the First Version (8 Tasks Maximum)

1. Document upload: PDF file upload only at the start, with a reasonable size limit.

2. Adding signature fields: specifying signature, name, and date locations for each signer.

3. Creating a signing link: generating a unique link for each document, valid for a limited period, without forcing the signer to register.

4. Instant signing page: a very clean interface for the signer on phone or computer.

5. Audit trail: storing Timestamp + IP Hash + User-Agent Hash for each signing step.

6. Free credit + credit purchase system: initial free credit, and very small credit bundles (no subscription).

7. Document status view: pending / signed / completed / cancelled status for the document owner.

8. Signed document + audit trail download: after signing is complete, the document owner can download the file and the audit trail together.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free to start: a very small number of free signatures granted on first use, with no bank card.

• "Credit purchase" model: instead of a monthly subscription. Practical example:

• Micro bundle: 5 signatures = a very symbolic price

• Medium bundle: 10 signatures = lowest per-unit price

• Larger bundle only when needed: for intermittent users

• Clear price per signature: automatically shown before consuming any credit.

• Revenue priority: a simple margin after storage, verification, and payment costs.

• No fees for the signer: only the document owner pays.

5) Global Rollout Plan Using Free Channels

• Search engine optimization (SEO): targeting long-tail phrases such as free pay per e-sign for one document and simple Arabic phrases such as sign one document without a subscription.

• Small forums and community building: useful participation on Reddit, Indie Hackers, and Dev.to focused on solving a real problem, with no paid ads.

• Small business groups: joining Facebook and LinkedIn groups aimed at small business owners, and answering questions about quick contracts.

• Micro educational content: creating very short posts explaining "how to sign one document without paying a monthly subscription."

• Free tools directory: listing the product in relevant free business tools directories.

• Real word-of-mouth: encouraging satisfied users to share the link with people who actually need it.

• Gradual regional focus: starting with one language, then adding others based on free search visits.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

A) Legal risks

• Acceptance of electronic signatures: laws differ between countries (ESIGN in the US, eIDAS in the European Union, and others). Therefore, at the start, we will settle for a clear statement about the nature of the service and its scope.

• Data privacy: the need to minimize data to the bare minimum. Avoid collecting unnecessary sensitive data.

• Audit trail as simple proof: focusing on proving the timing of the action rather than complex identity authentication in the first version.

• Terms of use and privacy policy: they must be written in clear language and in a form suited to the simple service.
