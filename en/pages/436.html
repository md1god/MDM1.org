Nabd — Full Project Plan

Write once, remember forever.

Full execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the launch plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: learning cards inside your writing space

1) Target audience

• Students and language/certification learners (IELTS, ACCA, medicine…) who write their notes in Markdown.

• Researchers and professionals who author a large reference knowledge base and need to retrieve it regularly.

• Users of personal organization apps (Obsidian, Logseq, Notion) looking for spaced repetition without manual copy-paste.

• Educators and educational content creators who build exercises for their audience.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• From note to card automatically: it captures questions straight from Markdown files (lists, tables, selected text) with no manual conversion.

• Modern spaced repetition engine (FSRS) instead of old mathematical schedules, with per-user time adaptation.

• Local-first with optional sync: files stay pure Markdown on your device; no data lock-in.

• Optional AI card generation from long text, with human review before saving.

• Lightweight web + desktop experience that works offline, instead of relying on an external host.

• Free to start with no limit on basic cards; you pay only for advanced cloud services.

3) Scope of the first release (8 tasks max)

1. Import single Markdown files and folders, and recognize common question formats.

2. Fast card editor (question/answer, list cards, true/false cards).

3. FSRS scheduling engine with simple retention statistics.

4. Daily review view: one card on screen, keyboard shortcuts.

5. Full local storage + JSON and CSV export/import.

6. Optional sync via a free account (encryption in transit and at rest).

7. Basic card generation from pasted text (manually triggered).

8. One desktop app (Electron/Tauri) + a read-only web version.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: unlimited cards locally, full scheduling, export anytime.

• Plus — $5 per month: multi-device sync, cloud backup, advanced statistics.

• Pro — $9 per month: AI card generation (with fair-use limits), academic templates, performance reports.

• Enterprise/education plan: dashboards for teachers and small schools, priced per seat.

• Initial revenue from subscriptions, later expansion with paid templates for content creators.

5) Global launch plan using free channels

• Open-source the core on GitHub (MIT-licensed) to build trust and attract contributors.

• SEO and long-form content: spaced-repetition tool comparisons, Markdown guides.

• YouTube/TikTok: 30-second clips showing the conversion from note to card.

• Reddit and Discord: participation in learning, language, and personal-organization communities.

• Product Hunt and Hacker News at launch, with a bilingual landing page.

• Partnerships with educational content creators: referral links and free accounts in exchange for reviews.

• Free webinars with universities and study clubs.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: ensure no infringement of trademarks or interface designs; comply with the FSRS license and encryption libraries; a clear privacy policy (GDPR) for sync data, and no training of AI models on user data without explicit consent.

• Technical: accurate recognition of varied Markdown formats (may require extensible rules); conflict-free concurrent sync; app performance on low-end devices; optional dependence on AI APIs whose pricing may change — these must be disableable.

• Operational: competition from established free tools; the solution: focus on the "note → card" flow with minimal effort.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Nabd — نَبْض

Tagline: Write once, remember forever.
