Watad — Complete Project Plan

Your notes turn into lasting memory.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the launch plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: Watad (Watad)

1) Target audience

• University and graduate students (medicine, languages, law) who study constantly.

• Self-directed learners and knowledge seekers who keep digital notes (Markdown).

• Language crammers and holders of global tests (IELTS, USMLE, JLPT).

• Users in the Arab world looking for a modern study tool with a ready Arabic interface.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• The modern FSRS algorithm enabled by default (saves 20–30% of review time at the same recall level).

• Works with any Markdown notes folder — not locked to a single tool.

• Original design with a fast Arabic/English interface and a one-tap review experience (installable PWA).

• "Exam" mode: schedule reviews against a specific exam date.

• Full data ownership: local-first storage, optional encrypted sync.

• Free and open-source core, with payment only for add-on services.

3) Scope of the first release (8 tasks maximum)

1. Import a Markdown folder and auto-generate cards from a simple ?/:: format.

2. Manual card editor (cloze, question/answer, image cards).

3. FSRS scheduler with a complete local review log (IndexedDB/SQLite).

4. Daily review interface (PWA works offline).

5. Statistics dashboard: recall rate, load forecast, heatmap.

6. Exam mode: enter an exam date and adjust the review schedule.

7. Full export/import (CSV + Markdown) and manual backup.

8. Landing page + optional email signup + basic analytics.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: unlimited cards, local review, FSRS, statistics, one exam mode.

• Plus — 4$/month: encrypted sync across devices, unlimited devices.

• Plus — AI add-ons: card generation from files/lectures (pay-as-you-go or within the subscription).

• Sponsorships of educational channels and group spaces (small B2B) — promising.

5) Global launch plan using free channels

• SEO content: comparisons of "best spaced repetition review tools 2026" in two languages (Arabic/English).

• Reddit and Discord: r/medicalschool, r/languagelearning, Arabic student communities.

• YouTube/TikTok: 60-second lessons on "how to study without forgetting".

• Product Hunt + Hacker News + highlight it in Markdown/Obsidian communities without naming competing products by name.

• Partnerships with Arabic educational content creators (language reviews, USMLE) in exchange for a Plus subscription.

• Weekly newsletter: learning statistics and study tips.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: do not copy any code or trademark; use FSRS libraries (MIT/BSD) under a proper license; rights of AI-generated content.

• Technical: sync conflicts (solve with CRDT or last-write-wins with a log), local data loss (periodic automatic export), scheduler accuracy (simulation tests against the FSRS reference), PWA performance on old iOS.

• Market: strong competition from free tools — we face it by focusing on Arabic + local ownership + operational simplicity.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Watad (Watad)

Marketing tagline: Your notes turn into lasting memory.
