Masarak — Full Project Plan

"Masarak — your project moves clearly, from idea to completion."

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout plan, and risks. The project is in development and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a simplified collaborative task board

1) Target audience

• Small teams (2–15 people) in startups and digital service agencies.

• Independent developers and designers managing multiple client projects.

• Educational teams and open communities that need simple planning without complexity.

• Those seeking self-hosted hosting for data privacy with a ready cloud option.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Instant performance: a fast interface that runs smoothly even on weak connections, with a mobile-first design.

• AI-powered customization: automatic generation of columns and boards from a text description, and summarizing project status.

• Fully flexible: support for Kanban, Timeline, and Calendar in the same project without restrictions.

• Privacy first: full data encryption and open export in a standard format that prevents vendor lock-in.

• Lightweight: runs on a small server (1GB RAM) or via a single container (Docker).

3) Scope of the first version (8 tasks maximum)

1. Boards, lists, and drag-and-drop tasks with custom columns.

2. Member invitations and role assignment (owner / editor / viewer).

3. Comments, attachments, and email notifications for each task.

4. Two views: Kanban and Timeline with filtering and quick search.

5. Full Arabic interface (RTL) with multi-language support.

6. One-click backup and restore + JSON export.

7. Responsive web app + installable web app (PWA).

8. Free self-hosted version via Docker Compose.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free to start: full version for individuals and teams up to 5 members (hosted or self-hosted).

• Pro – $6/member/month: unlimited members and projects, automation, integrations, activity log.

• Team – $12/member/month: SSO, advanced permissions, expanded storage, priority support.

• Additional income: our managed hosting (from $9/month) + training and data migration services for companies.

5) Global rollout plan with free channels

• Open launch on GitHub + a banner on Product Hunt and Hacker News (Show HN).

• SEO content: comparison articles and ready-made templates in (English, Arabic, Spanish, French).

• Communities: Reddit (r/projectmanagement), open Discord, indie hackers forums.

• Partnerships with Arab developer communities and programming education platforms (free courses).

• Weekly posting on X/LinkedIn and YouTube with "before/after" videos and short tutorials.

• Referral program: one free Pro month for every friend who signs up.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: choosing an explicit open-source license (such as MIT or Apache-2.0) from day one to avoid disputes; GDPR compliance in handling user data and European hosting contracts.

• Technical: intense competition from established free tools — we address it by focusing on speed, AI, and the Arabic language; data loss risk is mitigated by automatic backups and periodic restore tests; session and access security is ensured through continuous penetration testing.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Masarak

Tagline: "Masarak — your project moves clearly, from idea to completion."
