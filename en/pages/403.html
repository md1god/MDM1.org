Mantiq — Full Project Plan

Speak, and your voice becomes clean text on your device only.

A full execution plan for a new project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, launch plan, and risks. Under construction, not yet launched.

Product plan: local in-browser voice transcription

1) Target audience

• Writers and researchers: those who write research and reports and prefer voice dictation over typing.

• Arabic-speaking readers/listeners: lawyers, journalists, graduates, and university students writing heavily in Arabic.

• Editors and content creators: those who want filler cleaned while writing (emails, posts, scripts).

• People with disabilities or hand injuries, and anyone needing a fast, safe voice input method.

• Employees where cloud upload is forbidden (health/legal/financial sectors).

2) Differentiators (why choose us)

• Fully local processing in the browser: audio never leaves the device, no mandatory account, no file uploads.

• Works without internet: runs after the first visit, suitable for travel and weak networks.

• Smart text cleanup: removing filler (uh, I mean, like), splitting sentences, unifying punctuation, merging mispronounced words.

• Learns from your corrections: remembers your vocabulary and writing style and applies them automatically in future sessions.

• Bilingual with one click: switch input/output language (Arabic/English) with no complex settings.

• One-line interface: open the page, press a button, speak — no programs, no installation.

• Truly free to start, no credit card, no expiring trial.

3) Scope of the first version (max 8 tasks)

1. Voice recording from the microphone in the browser via Web Speech API / local Whisper (WASM).

2. Instant transcription for Arabic and English with common dialect support.

3. Automated text cleaner: removing filler, correcting repetition, adding punctuation.

4. Saving user edits locally (localStorage/IndexedDB) and learning from them.

5. Continuous dictation mode with a short pause for natural breaks.

6. Text export (copy, Markdown, TXT) and copying it into any app.

7. Status bar showing local processing + an audio quality indicator.

8. One landing page + waiting list + free activation without sign-up.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free (Free-to-start): 30 minutes of transcription per month, basic cleanup, two languages, text export — no card.

• Pro — $9/month: unlimited hours, advanced cleanup, learning from corrections, terminology glossary, faster speed.

• Pro annual — $69/year (incentive to subscribe).

• Teams — $12/user/month: shared glossary, strict privacy, unified billing.

• Extra revenue: licensing a desktop/browser extension to companies (per-device).

5) Global launch plan with free channels

1. SEO and language pages: one page per language pair/use case (Arabic→English, dictation for lawyers).

2. Communities: Reddit posts (r/speechrecognition, r/LanguageLearning), Hacker News "Show HN", Arabic forums.

3. Short content: Before/After clips of transcribed dialogue on TikTok/YouTube Shorts/X and LinkedIn.

4. Ecosystem: a free browser extension, ready-made templates for import into Notion/Obsidian.

5. Partnerships: content creators, university departments, disability associations — free educational version.

6. Product directories: productivity and privacy tool websites, free.

7. Growth contest: referral program with free minutes, plus a "publish your first text" week with prizes.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Consent for voice recording: some states/countries require parties' consent to recording — the user must be clearly notified.

• Data privacy: despite local processing, an explicit privacy policy and periodic security audits are required.

• Intellectual property of terms: avoid training models on protected texts without a license.

• Accessibility standards: comply with WCAG to ensure users with disabilities are not excluded.

Technical:

• Arabic transcription accuracy and dialects are the biggest challenge — quality may drop on weak devices.

• Browser limits: microphone constraints, memory use, and differing Chrome/Safari/Firefox behavior.

• Local model size: threatens the "open and speak" experience on slow networks.

• API changes: dependence on interfaces that may change or be restricted by browser providers.

• Free competition from giants: focus on the bilingual model and data locality as a clear niche.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Mantiq

Marketing tagline: Speak, and your voice becomes clean text on your device only.
