Rawaq — Complete Project Plan

A sovereign communication space that organizes every topic and turns it into a documented decision.

Complete plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market, and risks. The project is under construction and not yet launched.

Product plan for an original topic-based conversations product

1) Target audience

• Sensitive operations teams: emergency rooms, incident response, and field operations.

• NGOs and health and education sectors in low-connectivity environments.

• Governments and institutions requiring data sovereignty and on-premises hosting.

• Privacy-focused SMEs and development teams wanting self-hosting.

• Distributed communities and technical teams wary of surprise pricing or ads.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Topic-based organization, not just channels: every topic has a clear status (open, in progress, resolved), decisions, and tasks.

• Offline operation: differential sync, an outbox queue, and low-bandwidth performance for weak networks.

• Full sovereignty: one-command Docker or Kubernetes deployment, local storage, and end-to-end encryption for private rooms.

• Compliance-ready: audit log, retention policies, and structured export with no commercial lock-in.

• Optional local AI: summarize topics and extract tasks without external data transfer.

• Lightweight: a fast PWA and acceptable performance on old devices.

• Open from the start: API, Webhooks, integrations, a clear license, and protected trademark.

• Permanent free tier: no surprise billing or data selling.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Public and private topic rooms with threads, tags, and topic status.

2. Direct and group messages with @ mentions and presence.

3. Real-time WebSocket sync, offline mode, and an outbox queue.

4. File and link sharing with local or S3-compatible storage and size limits.

5. Permission-aware full-text search within rooms and topics.

6. Roles and permissions (owner, moderator, member, guest) and a basic audit log.

7. End-to-end encryption for private rooms with simplified key management.

8. Web and email notifications, an admin panel for deployment, configuration, and export, and a Docker package.

4) Pricing and revenue model

· Plan · Price · Contents ·

· Community · Permanently free · Self-hosting, unlimited users, core features, community support ·

· Cloud · Free up to 10 users, then $4/user/month · Managed hosting, daily backups, extended search ·

· Enterprise · $9/user/month · SSO/SAML, advanced retention, compliance export, SLA, priority support ·

· Managed services · Custom pricing · Sovereign deployment, data migration, training, custom integrations ·

• Revenue: cloud subscriptions, enterprise support, sovereign hosting, and training.

• No ads, no data selling, and no core features behind a paywall.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Publish code and documentation on GitHub, with SEO for comparisons and technical guides.

• Launch on Product Hunt and Hacker News; join r/selfhosted, r/sysadmin, r/privacy, and r/opensource.

• List the project in self-hosting directories, Docker Hub, and Helm repositories.

• Educational YouTube videos: deployment, migration, security, and use cases.

• Free partnerships with universities, NGOs, business incubators, and hackathons.

• Open community via Matrix or Discord, monthly Q&A, and a referral program with free months.

• Free GitLab, Nextcloud, and Jitsi integrations, and presence at open conferences such as FOSDEM.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• GDPR, CCPA, and data localization compliance, with clear data processing agreements.

• End-to-end encryption may conflict with lawful interception; a transparent policy is required.

• Choosing a license (Apache-2.0 or AGPL) with trademark protection and contributor review.

• Content liability, abuse, copyright, and clear retention and deletion policies.

• Encryption export restrictions and local laws in some markets.

Technical:

• Scaling WebSocket and presence with thousands of concurrent users.

• Offline sync conflicts and reliable merge design.

• Encryption key management and account recovery without breaking privacy.

• File security, malware scanning, and storage and cost limits.

• Search and indexing performance, support for old devices, and anti-spam.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Rawaq

Tagline: A sovereign communication space that organizes every topic and turns it into a documented decision.
