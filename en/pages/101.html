Nasaq — Complete Project Plan

All your company's work — clients, projects, employees, and invoices — in one simple system that starts free.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: A unified business management platform

1) Target audience

• Small and medium businesses (10–200 employees) that run their operations through scattered tools (a spreadsheet for client management, a file for employee management, another app for projects).

• Agencies and service firms (marketing, software, accounting) that need to link clients to projects and invoices.

• Emerging teams looking for a single system that starts free and then grows with them without complex data migration.

• Arab companies and emerging markets that need Arabic (RTL) support, a Hijri calendar, and local currencies.

2) Points of differentiation (why choose our product)

• Real unification without complexity: clients + projects + employees + invoices in one interlinked data model, not separate modules merged later.

• Native Arabic readiness: a fully RTL interface, Arabic and English support, Hijri and Gregorian calendars, local taxes and currencies.

• A truly free start: a permanent free plan for a small team with no time limit, no credit card.

• Operational simplicity: one-click installation on cloud hosting, with offline capability for essential tasks.

• Practical embedded intelligence: an assistant that suggests next tasks based on client and project activity, not just reports.

• Data ownership: full export in open formats (CSV/JSON) at any time, with no data lock-in.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Contact and client management: a unified record with tags and a timeline.

2. A simplified sales dashboard (CRM): deal stages, follow-up reminders, converting a deal into a project.

3. Project and task management: boards and tasks with an owner and a due date.

4. Time tracking: a timer linked to the task and client.

5. Invoices and quotes: create an invoice from time/a project, track payment, export PDF.

6. Simplified employee record: a basic profile, leave, manual attendance.

7. An initial reports dashboard: sales, project time, financial receivables.

8. Account and team management: users, roles and permissions, data export.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free (Free forever): up to 5 users and 3 active projects and basic features.

• Growth — $9/user/month: unlimited users and projects, advanced reports, automation.

• Business — $19/user/month: advanced permissions, integrations, priority support, custom scope.

• Additional revenue: managed hosting, paid data migration, customization/development on demand, an add-on marketplace with a commission.

• Self-host: a free community version + a commercial license for large companies.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Educational content (SEO): articles like "How to manage [a process] without scattered tools" in Arabic and English.

• Developer communities: publishing the open version on GitHub and package repositories, and inviting contributors.

• Product platforms: Product Hunt, Hacker News, Reddit, freelancer and agency communities.

• Partnerships: business coaches and accountants who offer the platform to their clients in exchange for a commission.

• Ambassador/referral program: free months in exchange for bringing in new teams.

• Short video channels: practical tutorials on YouTube, TikTok, and LinkedIn.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: compliance with data protection policies (GDPR/CCPA) and local invoice and tax laws; not copying any code, trademark, or design from any existing product; clear licensing for the open-source components used.

• Technical: the complexity of unifying systems may slow development; data security and breach points; the challenge of integrating with a changing accounting/tax system; scalability as teams grow; risks of data loss and backup recovery.

• Mitigation: a modular architecture built incrementally, automated tests and security review, multi-site backups, and ongoing documentation.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Nasaq

Marketing tagline: All your company's work — clients, projects, employees, and invoices — in one simple system that starts free.
