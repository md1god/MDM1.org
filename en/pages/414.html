AgentPenny — Full Project Plan

Every AI agent has a price on the bill — know your cost and profit before you execute.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the launch plan, and the risks. The project is currently being built and has not yet launched.

Product plan: An operating cost ledger for AI agents

1) Target audience

• Startups and product teams launching AI agents (Agent) who need to know the cost of every conversation or task.

• Independent developers building agents on LangChain, CrewAI, n8n, or MCP who want to see profit versus cost.

• Finance and operations (FinOps) teams inside companies that consume services like OpenAI, Anthropic, and Vertex and need to allocate the invoice across projects/clients.

• Agencies who bill their clients based on actual consumption, not estimates.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• A run ledger for every execution: token costs, tool calls, latency, success/failure, and realized revenue in a single auditable record.

• Framework-agnostic: one aggregation layer that captures events from any framework or via MCP without code changes.

• Realistic local-currency pricing: updated provider rates plus infrastructure and tool costs, not just a token counter.

• Profitability analysis: linking revenue to cost per client/path/model to know which agents close and generate profit.

• Open-source core (Core): a free self-hosted version with a paid managed service — a frictionless trial structure.

• Instant alerts: budget limits, cost spikes, or declining success rate via Slack/email.

3) First release scope (at most 8 tasks)

1. Build a lightweight SDK (Python + TypeScript) that captures execution events: tokens, tools, latency, status.

2. Ingest events through a standard HTTP/Claude-MCP receiver and store them in a time-series database.

3. A pricing engine that applies provider rates with support for custom models.

4. Dashboard: costs by run/path/day + success and latency metrics.

5. Aggregate costs across providers (Token Cost, Tool Cost, Infra Cost) in a single view.

6. Compare revenue to cost per client or path (basic profitability).

7. Budget alerts via Discord/Slack/email + CSV export.

8. Initial support for LangChain and n8n plus an MCP interface only, to reduce complexity.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free (Self-hosted / OSS): up to 50 thousand run events per month, open repository, all core features.

• Pro — $29/month: up to one million events, alerts, per-client profitability, advanced export, team up to 3 members.

• Business — $149/month: higher limits, SSO, permissions, 12-month retention, priority support.

• Add-ons: long-term retention (24+ months), custom integrations, ready-made tax-billing reports.

• Revenue: monthly SaaS subscriptions plus a margin on the managed hosting service, with a free tier to encourage trials.

5) Global launch plan with free channels

1. GitHub: publish the repository with a bilingual README (EN/ZH first) and a 5-minute quickstart.

2. Hacker News (Show HN) + Reddit (r/LocalLLaMA, r/OpenAI) with a candid technical article backed by numbers.

3. SEO content: tool cost comparisons ("Agent cost monitoring alternatives") and a free cost calculator.

4. Communities: Discord/Mattermost for LangChain, n8n, and MCP users + sharing in Arabic and Chinese DevRel communities.

5. Developer communities: articles on Dev.to, Hashnode, Zhihu, and Medium in the target audience's languages.

6. Content creator partnerships: providing ready-made hooks for influencers on YouTube/X to demo the dashboard live.

7. Ready-to-use downloads: templates for monitoring popular agents that deliver immediate value.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: run logs may contain sensitive content (Prompt/PII) — solution: capture metrics only by default, full encryption, and an option not to store text.

• Legal: frequent provider price changes require continuous updates and reliance on unofficial data may lag — solution: document the source and define an error margin.

• Legal: data protection regulations (GDPR) for log retention — solution: short retention options, a self-erasing policy, and regional hosting.

• Technical: high cost for high-volume releases — solution: sampling, event compression, and clear limits on every plan.

• Technical: difficulty integrating with frameworks that use in-memory state — solution: support for a Proxy mode as a second option.

• Technical: low switching costs since the pricing dashboard can be copied — solution: differentiate through profitability analysis and integration depth, n
