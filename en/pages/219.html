VitalMesh — Complete Project Plan

Unify your health data from all your devices into one AI-ready interface, while it remains yours alone.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: wearable health data unification platform

1) Target audience

• Developers and data engineers building health or fitness apps who need a unified data source.

• Digital health startups that do not want to build an integration layer from scratch.

• Training, fitness, and recovery (coaching) platforms that connect their clients' data from multiple devices.

• Privacy-sensitive entities: clinics, researchers, universities, institutions whose data must remain within their own infrastructure (self-hosted).

• Quantified Self users and performance analysts who want to aggregate and own their data.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• A unified data model (Unified Schema) that normalizes biometrics across all devices: sleep, heart rate, HRV, steps, workouts, body composition.

• Genuine AI readiness: a semantic layer + natural-language query endpoints + an MCP server for AI agents, and local LLM support.

• Privacy first and self-hosted by design: it runs locally without sending any data to a third party.

• Distributed connector architecture: adding a new device requires no changes to the core, via an open specification.

• Incremental sync + Webhooks instead of tiring periodic polling.

• Data ownership and full export at any time in open formats.

• Free frictionless start: a fully-featured community edition for self-hosting.

3) Scope of the first version (8 tasks max)

1. Connector engine + 4 core connectors (Apple Health import, Google Health Connect, Fitbit, Garmin).

2. Unified data model and its validation (activities, sleep, heart, HRV, body metrics).

3. REST API + Webhooks for incremental sync.

4. AI layer: normalized metrics + natural-language query endpoint + MCP server.

5. Authentication, multi-tenancy, API keys, and fine-grained permissions.

6. Self-host deployment package via Docker Compose with one-command setup.

7. A simple dashboard to view sources, sync status, and error log.

8. Full data export + audit log.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free forever (Community Edition): fully-featured self-hosting, no limit on the number of devices.

• Cloud Starter — $0: a free cloud tier up to a user/request cap to ease trial.

• Cloud Pro — $29/month: faster sync, longer retention, priority support, more smart queries.

• Team — $99/month: collaboration, roles, extended Webhooks, SLA.

• Enterprise (Open-Core License): self-host license + support, compliance, and security review, custom pricing.

• Add-ons: paid connectors for specialized devices, and custom support as a service.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Open Source on GitHub with the open connector specification to attract contributors.

• Product Hunt + Hacker News at launch to highlight the "your data is yours" idea.

• Developer communities: Reddit (r/QuantifiedSelf, r/healthIT), Discord, specialized Discourse.

• SEO and technical content: "how to unify X and Y data" articles, and tutorials on YouTube/Dev.to.

• Integration stores: Home Assistant, Low-code platforms, MCP marketplace for agents.

• Academic and research outreach: universities and labs get free self-hosting in exchange for case studies.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: compliance with GDPR and HIPAA and device terms (ToS), the liability of handling sensitive health data, Open-Core licensing, and avoiding any trademark conflicts.

• Technical: API rate limits, providers shutting down their APIs, data schema drift, sync reliability, security of health data storage, and scaling with Webhooks density.

• Adoption risks: bundled competition from providers, and the need to prove trust before adopting sensitive data.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: VitalMesh

Tagline: Unify your health data from all your devices into one AI-ready interface, while it remains yours alone.
