SetVault — Full Project Plan

Your workouts are saved here, not in the cloud.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: offline workout app

1) Target audience

• Gym-goers from beginners to pros (men and women).

• Those who want total privacy: no accounts, no cloud accounts.

• People in areas with weak connectivity, or those who ban the internet where they train.

• Personal trainers tracking multiple clients locally.

2) Differentiators

• Works fully without internet; data stays on the device only.

• Quick logging of lifts and sets in under 3 seconds during rest.

• Smart progression plan that suggests weight increases based on your log.

• Manual backup export (local file) with no cloud at all.

• Dark interface tuned for viewing while training, with visual performance comparison.

• Completely free to start, with no ads ever.

3) Scope of the first version (8 tasks max)

1. Create a free workout with exercise names, sets, reps, and weights.

2. "In progress" screen with a rest timer and instant weight editing.

3. Automatic log for every workout with date and volume.

4. Progress chart per exercise (best weight, total volume).

5. Simple progression plan suggesting the next weights.

6. Built-in exercise library, editable and exportable.

7. Backup/restore via a single local file.

8. CSV export for reports.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free to start: all core features with no limits.

• Paid later: a "Pro" plan at $4.99/month or $29/year including advanced plans, deep analytics, multiple workout types, additional UI themes.

• Never any ads; revenue comes only from subscriptions and in-app purchases.

• Extra opening: paid training-plan templates from certified trainers (revenue share).

5) Global rollout plan with free channels

• Google Play store: optimized listing (ASO) in English, Spanish, and Arabic.

• Short-form content on TikTok/Instagram Reels: before/after app screens and workout tricks.

• Reddit: fitness and workout communities, posting case stories and privacy features.

• Static-site blog (SEO) comparing the "best offline workout app".

• Partnerships with small creators in exchange for a free Pro plan.

• Posting in specialized Telegram and WhatsApp groups.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: exercise name and brand logo rights — use generic names only; app store privacy policy clarifying that data never leaves the device.

• Legal: local data protection laws when exporting off the device.

• Technical: data loss — enforcing periodic backups and confirmation before deletion.

• Technical: Android version fragmentation and small device screens.

• Commercial: market crowding and competition from many free apps — differentiate on privacy and speed.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: SetVault

Tagline: Your workouts are saved here, not in the cloud.
