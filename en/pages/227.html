Luqma — Full Project Plan

Plan your meals once… and cook calmly all week.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the rollout plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

New product plan: a meal planning and recipe management app

The idea and the problem: Self-hosted meal planning and recipe management servers are excellent, but they rely on unofficial clients spread across multiple platforms, and they often lack Arabic support, a cooking mode, a smart shopping list, and a unified experience across mobile and desktop. The problem: the user wants one fast, elegant, complete experience across all their devices, with full privacy for their data.

1) Target audience

• Users of self-hosted meal planning servers (privacy and open source enthusiasts).

• Busy families and individuals who want to plan meals weekly and reduce food waste.

• Diet and recipe followers (homemakers, students, travelers).

• Developers and Arabic-speaking users looking for genuine RTL support.

• Cooking and smart shopping communities worldwide (Android, iOS, and Desktop users).

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• A native app built from scratch with Flutter, running on Android, iOS, and desktop with a single codebase and high performance.

• An authentic Arabic experience (RTL, fonts, optional Hijri calendar) — a clear scarcity in this field.

• Cooking mode: interactive steps + multiple timers + touch/voice control.

• Smart shopping list: automatic ingredient grouping, duplicate removal, splitting by store sections, and sharing via WhatsApp.

• Full offline support for recipes and the saved plan, with instant sync upon return.

• Multi-account/multi-server support, standard export/import, and a calendar shareable with family.

• Privacy first: no tracking, no ads; data stays with the user or in local encryption.

3) Scope of the first version (8 tasks maximum)

1. Project structure with Flutter and Material 3 UI + Arabic/English support with full RTL.

2. API connection layer to any self-hosted server + multi-account and key management.

3. Recipe viewing and creation: images, ingredients, steps, tags, search, and filters.

4. Meal plan calendar (week/month) with drag-and-drop and day copying.

5. Smart automatic shopping list with section splitting and text sharing.

6. Step-by-step cooking mode + timers + offline storage.

7. Automated tests and CI, then Beta release on Google Play, TestFlight, and desktop builds.

8. User documentation + a closed prototype with 50 users to gather feedback.

4) Pricing and revenue model

Free to start: all core features (recipes, plan, shopping list, cooking mode) are free forever, with open source to build trust.

Revenue sources:

• Optional Pro subscription (~$3.99/month or $29/year): managed cloud sync, backups, nutrition analytics, advanced customizations.

• Community donations (GitHub Sponsors / Buy Me a Coffee).

• Paid template marketplace: packs of recipe plans and lists from certified chefs (20% commission).

• Community sponsorship from tooling and food companies, with no in-app advertising.

5) Global rollout plan using free channels

• Multi-language blog articles (Arabic/English) with SEO research on "meal planning" and "reducing food waste".

• Short-form content: TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels — quick recipes showcasing the app.

• Community posts: Reddit, Hacker News (Show HN), Mastodon, and LinkedIn.

• Open Discord/Matrix community + a volunteer translator program (Crowdin).

• Weekly email newsletters with a "ready-made meal plan" to build a loyal audience.

• Partnerships with healthy eating and balanced diet content creators in multiple languages (in exchange for a free mention).

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: the name and identity are original from scratch to avoid any commercial conflict; no use of third-party names or trade dress; GDPR/CCPA compliance for user data; Google Play and App Store policies; rights to images and recipes uploaded by users.

• Technical: API changes in servers (handled by a compatibility layer and automated tests); iOS storage and background restrictions; payment and subscription risks (via a trusted provider); maintaining 3 platforms with a single codebase; dependence on the hosted server's license.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Luqma

Tagline: Plan your meals once… and cook calmly all week.
