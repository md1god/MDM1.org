Rakeez — Complete Project Plan

The lightweight e-commerce platform that grows with you — start for free and scale without limits.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a lightweight, smart e-commerce platform (the new structure)

1) Target audience

• Small and medium merchants who want to launch a store quickly without a technical team.

• Independent developers and agencies looking for an API-first platform that is fully customizable.

• Emerging markets that need native support for Arabic, RTL direction, and local payment gateways.

• Multi-vendor marketplaces (Marketplaces) in their early growth stages.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Lightweight and fast: Headless architecture and API-first interfaces, without heavy administrative complexity.

• Smart by design: a built-in AI assistant for writing descriptions, improving SEO, and suggesting pricing.

• Free to start: can be self-hosted or use a free cloud, with no setup fees.

• Native support for Arabic and RTL instead of adding it later as a patch.

• One-click deployment: Docker and ready-made containers, with no complex configuration.

• An open plugin system with a clear API and a community plugin marketplace.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Product catalog system (create/edit, variants, inventory, media).

2. Simplified cart and checkout with two gateways (international + local).

3. Merchant dashboard with an Arabic RTL interface that is customizable.

4. Product and category pages optimized for search engines (SSR).

5. An API-first layer ready for Headless.

6. Basic order and shipping management.

7. One-click deployment via Docker/containers.

8. An AI assistant for generating product descriptions and titles.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: self-hosting or limited cloud + basic features.

• Pro (low monthly subscription): for cloud usage, custom domain, advanced reports.

• Growth: multi-vendor, team roles, priority support.

• An optional commission on payments through our configured gateways, and paid plugin sales in the plugin marketplace.

• Managed services for migration and customization for large clients.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Open source on GitHub to attract developers and contributions.

• SEO and educational content: store launch guides, comparisons, and case studies.

• Developer and commerce communities (Reddit, Discourse, Discord) through answering, not advertising.

• Product Hunt and Hacker News at launch.

• YouTube channels and free tutorials in Arabic and English.

• Integration with plugin directories for adjacent platforms and tools.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Choosing a clear license (open source) and avoiding infringement of registered trademarks.

• Compliance with privacy regulations (GDPR/CCPA) and managing data consents.

• Tax compliance for payments and protecting customer funds.

Technical:

• Security (data leaks, payment vulnerabilities, injection attacks).

• Scalability as the number of stores and orders grows.

• Dependence on external service providers (payment, AI) and their cost.

• Ensuring the quality of community plugins and preventing malicious code.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Rakeez

Tagline: The lightweight e-commerce platform that grows with you — start for free and scale without limits.
