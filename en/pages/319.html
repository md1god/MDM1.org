Fitora — Full Project Plan

Simple exercises, clear progress, real results.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, launch plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not yet launched.

1) Target Audience

• Beginners in exercise who do not know where to start.

• People who train at home and want simple plans without complex equipment.

• Busy employees looking for short exercises in limited time.

• Individuals interested in tracking their health progress without spending heavily on a personal trainer.

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• Simplicity: an extremely clean interface suited to beginners without the complexity of commercial apps.

• AI-powered customized plans: generates exercise plans based on the user's level, goals, and available time.

• Completely free to start: most core features are available for free without artificial restrictions.

• Works without mandatory registration: users can try creating an exercise plan instantly before creating an account.

• Focus on progress: simple tracking of exercises, repetitions, weights, and achievements with clear charts.

• Privacy first: user data stored locally as much as possible, minimizing collection of personal data.

3) First Version Scope (8 Tasks Maximum)

1. User authentication: email login with a guest option.

2. Exercise database: a basic library of exercises with illustrative images and brief descriptions.

3. Exercise plan builder: a simple tool to build a daily or weekly plan.

4. AI plan generator: creates a customized plan based on user inputs.

5. Progress tracking: a log of completed exercises, weights, and repetitions.

6. Simple dashboard: a summary of progress and the current plan.

7. Responsive application: works smoothly on phone and computer.

8. Usage guide: simple instructions for beginners.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free to start: full access to plans and tracking at no cost.

• Optional paid version: a modest monthly subscription for extra features such as advanced plans and extended tracking.

• Value-based model: focus on attracting a large number of free users first, then converting a small percentage of them to the paid version.

• No ads: to maintain a clean and fast experience.

5) Global Launch Plan with Free Channels

• Search engine optimization (SEO): writing content in Arabic and English about home exercises for beginners.

• Free communities: constructive participation in forums such as Reddit and Discord related to fitness.

• Social media: posting short instructional videos on TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels.

• Content marketing: creating free guides such as "How to Start Exercising at Home in 7 Days."

• Long-tail keywords: targeting simple terms such as "home exercises for beginners."

• Organic referrals: encouraging users to share their plans with others.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal: exercise plans are general and not diagnostic. A clear disclaimer must be added warning about special medical conditions.

• Health liability: avoid giving medical or therapeutic advice, focusing only on general exercises.

• Technical: ensure fast performance on low-end devices, especially with slow connections.

• Data growth: design the database to be lightweight and scalable later.

• Privacy: adhere to simple and clear data collection policies, especially in countries with strict data protection laws.

7) Proposed Name and Tagline

Proposed name: Fitora

Tagline: Simple exercises, clear progress, real results.
