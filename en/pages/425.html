سؤالَك — Full Project Plan

Write your questions once; let grading do the rest.

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout plan, and risks. Under construction, not launched yet.

Product plan: interactive question platform for teachers

1) Target audience

• Teachers in schools, universities, and training academies (especially those grading many multiple-choice and short-answer questions).

• Online course instructors who build short quizzes inside their platforms.

• Department supervisors who need instant reports on class-level student performance.

• Self-directed learners who want to test themselves on study material made as question flashcards.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Build a quiz in minutes: upload an Excel/CSV file or paste text, or generate draft questions from a PDF/Word file via AI, with teacher review before publishing.

• Instant grading and live reports: scores, answer distributions, questions most students get wrong — all in real time, no manual work.

• "Live session" mode: a short code students enter from any browser to start instantly, no sign-up.

• Multiple formats: multiple choice, true/false, short answer, and matching, with random shuffling of questions and options to reduce cheating.

• Works on mobile and low bandwidth: a lightweight page that loads fast, with local saving and later sync.

• Flexible integration: embed the quiz in a school website with one line (<iframe>), and export results to Google Sheets or CSV.

3) First version scope (8 tasks max)

1. Teacher dashboard: create/edit/delete quizzes and organize them into "packets."

2. Simplified question editor: five question types + bulk CSV upload.

3. Share a quiz via link or short code, run it with no student sign-up.

4. Instant automatic grading and score display to the student after submission.

5. Results dashboard: each student's score, per-question analysis, CSV export.

6. "Live session" mode with sequential questions, a timer, and a participation counter.

7. Embed page with customizable appearance (brand color).

8. User accounts and permissions (owner/teacher/viewer), plus a free plan with clear limits.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free forever: up to 3 active quizzes and 100 participations per month, basic CSV export.

• Pro — 9$/month: unlimited quizzes, advanced analytics, AI question generation (reasonable monthly cap), brand customization.

• Institution — 49$/month per school/department: shared management, SSO sign-in, audit log, dedicated support.

• Optional AI usage fees: question-generation credit sold when the plan limit is exceeded.

• Free for individuals and non-profit educational devices, to build community early.

5) Global rollout plan using free channels

• SEO with practical content: ready-made template pages (math quiz, language quiz, review flashcards) in three languages (Arabic, English, Spanish).

• Extensions marketplace: free extension in Google Workspace Marketplace and .directory for teachers.

• Teacher communities: Facebook and Telegram groups and Reddit/X teacher forums, with free templates posted.

• University/influencer-teacher partnerships: 20 influencer teachers creating free content for editor credit.

• Full Arabic localization: an Arabic RTL interface first to penetrate the Arabic market, then expand.

• Product Hunt / PH campaigns for teachers + listings in free tool directories (There's An AI For That, AlternativeTo).

• Referral program: one free month for every teacher who invites a colleague.

• Short video content: 60-second tutorials on TikTok/YouTube Shorts in Arabic and English.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: student data privacy (GDPR/COPPA/national data protection laws) — minimize data, collect no ages or sensitive info, sign data processing agreements with institutions. Content rights when generating questions from copyright-protected files (require ownership confirmation). Compliance with platform terms of service when integrating.

• Technical: performance under "Live session" spikes — solved with temporary rooms and request rate limiting. Ease of cheating (copy/paste, another window) — shuffling, reordering, timers. AI generation quality — keep human review mandatory. Dependence on cloud providers and AI APIs — use abstraction layers and a local-model option.

• Competitive: ease of copying — win through user experience, localization, and price.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: سؤالَك

Marketing tagline: Write your questions once, and let grading do the rest.
