Qistas — Full Project Plan

A research and trading platform that turns multiple perspectives into one transparent, testable decision.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, monetization model, expansion plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not yet been launched.

1) Target Audience

• Advanced individual traders who want a systematic research tool instead of ready-made recommendations, with a clear understanding of decision sources.

• Developers and quant-curious individuals who want an API and SDK to build and test strategies.

• Small research teams in fintech and early-stage funds that need automated evidence collection and hypothesis comparison.

• Educators and financial content creators who need explainable, well-documented reports in Arabic and English.

• Initial markets: Middle East and North Africa first, then global markets through an English interface.

2) Key Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• Auditable decisions, not a black box: Every conclusion is linked to a data source, time, and justification, with a full audit trail of versions and assumptions.

• Multiple perspectives without chaos: Agents with clearly defined roles (Lead, Technical, Sentiment, Bull/Bear team, Risk, Portfolio Manager), with a cost budget and a discussion cap.

• Transparency before profit: Strict backtesting that accounts for commissions, slippage, and future leakage prevention, with comparison against a benchmark.

• Arabic-first experience: Bilingual interface and reports, with precise financial terminology.

• No custody of assets: We start with paper trading and read-only access, with no access to user funds.

• Cost flexibility: Bring your own LLM key and your own data provider, with a generous free plan to get started.

• Built-in safety barriers: Risk limits, human approval before any execution, and news-based order injection filtering.

• Scalable by design: API, SDK, and strategy templates, rather than a rigid, closed product.

3) Scope of the First Version (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. Workspace and onboarding: Account, watchlists, risk definition, language and region selection.

2. Documented data layer: Prices, news, and reports with timestamps and clear sources (Point-in-time).

3. Agent council: Analytical roles, bull/bear discussion, risk agent, and portfolio manager, with cost and time limits.

4. Backtesting engine: Commissions, slippage, Walk-forward, benchmark, and future bias detection.

5. Explainable decision report: Hypothesis, evidence, confidence, decision invalidation conditions, and risks.

6. Paper trading and broker integration: Simulation environment, read-only connection or Paper API, with no real fund execution.

7. Safeguards and audit log: Exposure limits, human approval, full audit trail, cost controls, and key management.

8. Export and sharing: PDF/JSON, report link, and Arabic/English versions.

4) Pricing and Monetization Model

• Free Forever: One workspace, two strategies, 10 backtests per month, delayed data, bring your own LLM key, and paper trading.

• Pro — $29/month: More backtests, concurrent agents, full reports, alerts, and additional data sources.

• Team — $99/month: 5 seats, shared workspaces, permissions, audit log, and team templates.

• Enterprise — Custom pricing: SSO, private deployment, data connectors, and compliance support.

• Additional revenue: LLM and data usage credits, advanced data add-ons, strategy/template marketplace with commission, and White-label licensing.

• Principle: We sell software, infrastructure, and transparency, not recommendations or return guarantees.

5) Global Expansion Plan Through Free Channels

• Bilingual SEO content: Practical guides on building research agents, evaluating strategies, and backtesting mistakes.

• Build in public: Series on X, LinkedIn, and YouTube showcasing development and technical decisions.

• GitHub: Open SDK, templates, and integration examples to attract developers.

• Communities: Discord, Telegram, and specialized Reddit communities, focused on educational discussions rather than spam.

• Product launches: Product Hunt, Show HN, and Hacker News, with a clear free version.

• Universities: Workshops and paper trading competitions with finance and quant clubs.

• Partnerships: Integrations with data platforms, brokers, and TradingView, with ready-made templates.

• Referrals: One free month for each party, and shared reports with watermarks to drive user acquisition.

• Newsletter: Weekly analysis of a single agent decision and how it was tested.
