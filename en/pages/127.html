Riwaq — Complete Project Plan

All your properties, their tickets, bills, and maintenance, in one Arabic platform that starts with you for free.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan for shared property management

1) Target audience

• Residential building and shared complex management companies (small and medium-sized).

• Homeowners association (HOA) boards and housing cooperatives.

• Independent property managers and the maintenance companies contracted by them.

• Individual owners who rent out several units and want to track expenses and collections.

2) Points of distinction (why choose our product)

• A genuinely free start: we begin for free without the need for a payment card or complex self-hosting.

• Arabic-first experience: a native RTL interface, local calendar and terminology, and multi-language support.

• Handling debts and contracts as one unit: linking the contract to the unit to the invoice to the expense to the maintenance ticket.

• A maintenance Marketplace integrated with the bidding and offers system instead of being a separate add-on.

• A lightweight Plugins system (SDK) to extend functionality without modifying the core.

• Financial reports ready for daily operations, with CSV/PDF export and WhatsApp/Telegram notifications.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Managing buildings, units, owners, and tenants.

2. A unified ticketing system (reports, priority, statuses, attachments, SLA tracking).

3. Issuing invoices, claims, and arrears, and tracking collections.

4. Recording expenses against buildings and matching them to the budget.

5. Lease contracts and their lifecycle (renewal, termination, notifications).

6. Tracking payments, partial payments, and account statements.

7. Maintenance Marketplace: posting the request, provider offers, assignment, and rating.

8. Dashboard with reports and alerts + authentication, roles, and permissions.

4) Pricing and profit model

• Free plan: up to 2 buildings and 20 units, essential features, platform logo, community only.

• Starter plan: a monthly fee per unit (example: $0.99 per unit/month) with full invoices and reports.

• Pro plan: a higher fee that includes the Marketplace, the Plugins, integrations, and a custom domain.

• Additional profit: a 5–10% commission on all assignment transactions in the Marketplace, and managed hosting plans (Self-hosted Enterprise).

• The model: subscription + usage-based + commission, starting for free and expanding with the size of the portfolio.

5) Global go-to-market plan through free channels

• Publishing an open-source version on GitHub to attract contributors and build trust.

• Free educational content (YouTube, Blog) about property management and accounting.

• Product communities: Product Hunt, Reddit, Hacker News, and local owner communities.

• Referral program: one free month for each party when inviting another company.

• Partnerships with maintenance and cleaning companies as a mutual reach channel.

• Local SEO in Arabic and English + a free template directory for housing associations.

• Nasdaq for Business: participating in real estate exhibitions and incubation programs.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: residents' data privacy and GDPR/local laws, compliance with local billing and tax regulations, and owners' data ownership.

• Legal: content liability in the Marketplace (provider contracts, reputation, complaints).

• Technical: data migration from legacy systems, multiple currencies and languages, and role security.

• Technical: multi-tenancy without data leakage, and the stability of the Plugins.

• Operational: resistance to change among traditional management, and the need for ongoing technical support.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Riwaq

Marketing tagline: All your properties, their tickets, bills, and maintenance, in one Arabic platform that starts with you for free.
