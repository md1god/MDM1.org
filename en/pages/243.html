Zentra — Complete Project Plan

Manage your entire business in one place with complete ease.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Small and medium-sized companies: need to manage their daily operations without high cost or significant complexity.

• Freelancers and agencies: want to track clients, invoices, and expenses in one system.

• Entrepreneurs in emerging markets: looking for a solution that supports local currencies and different languages and runs on simple devices.

• Remote teams: need easy and secure access to data from anywhere.

2) Points of Distinction (Why Choose Our Product)

• Simplicity of design: an interface built on a clear user experience, reducing the number of steps to complete daily tasks.

• Free model to start: provides the core functions entirely free, with no need to pay upon first use.

• Module flexibility: allows the user to enable only the modules they need, without loading the system with unnecessary functions.

• Multi-language and RTL support: designed to suit users around the world, with a focus on markets that use Arabic.

• Self-hosting capability: allows users to choose cloud hosting or host it themselves according to their requirements.

• Assistive intelligence: provides simple smart suggestions such as inventory forecasts or payment reminders to improve decisions.

• Offline operation: supports some core functions when the internet is down, then syncs them automatically when it is restored.

3) Scope of the First Version (8 tasks maximum)

1. Authentication and roles system: creating accounts, logging in, and user permissions.

2. Relationship management: a simple module for contacts, clients, and suppliers.

3. Invoices and payments: issuing invoices, tracking payments, and payment status.

4. Sales and quotations: creating quotes and converting them into invoices easily.

5. Inventory: tracking items and quantities and alerts when stock runs low.

6. Expenses: recording and categorizing expenses to gain clear visibility.

7. Dashboard: displaying key indicators such as revenue, expenses, and receivables.

8. General settings: setting the currency, taxes, company, and basic preferences.

4) Pricing and Profit Model

• Free version: includes all core functions for a limited number of users, with no monthly fees.

• Paid version: a monthly or annual subscription that provides additional users, larger storage capacity, and priority support.

• Additional services: offering setup and custom hosting services for companies that want them.

• Freemium model: focus on attracting users through free value, then generate profit from expansion as their businesses grow.

5) Global Distribution Plan Using Free Channels

• Search engine optimization (SEO): creating simple educational content that explains business management for small companies.

• Open communities: participating in developer and business owner communities on free platforms to spread benefit.

• Ready-made templates: offering invoice and report templates that can be downloaded free to attract users.

• Referral marketing: a simple referral program that rewards users for inviting their colleagues.

• Educational platforms: publishing short clips or free guides focused on solving daily problems for business owners.

• Technical distribution: providing ready-made packages for self-hosting with clear and easy documentation.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal risks: differing tax and invoicing laws between countries, addressed by designing a flexible system that allows local customization.

• Data privacy: the need to comply with best data protection practices, especially when handling sensitive financial information.

• Technical risks: performance challenges as data grows, addressed through modular and scalable design.

• System security: the risk of unauthorized access, mitigated by using modern security standards and regular updates.

• User adoption: some may find it difficult to move away from manual methods, solved through a simple interface and a clear user guide.

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Slogan

Proposed name: Zentra

Marketing slogan: Manage your entire business in one place with complete ease.
