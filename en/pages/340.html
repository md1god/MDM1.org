ShelfSure — Full Project Plan

Check your store before Google suspends your account and get a ready fix plan within minutes.

Full execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, monetization model, rollout plan, and risks. The project is being built and has not launched yet.

Product plan: Google Merchant Center compliance inspector

1) Target audience

• Shopify and WooCommerce store owners whose products are Disapproved or banned from Google Shopping.

• Small advertising agencies (Freelancers/Agencies) managing Merchant Center accounts for multiple clients who need a quick check before submitting.

• Stores that received a Suspension notice and are looking for a reliable fix list before filing an Appeal.

• Defined initial segment: stores generating ≥ 100 orders/month (account fix value is high for them, and they are easy to reach via Shopify communities).

2) Differentiators (why our product)

• The check covers product rules and account policies together (misleading pricing, shipping, return policy, mobile data, mismatched Landing Page), because most suspensions come from account policies, not the product listing.

• Actionable output, not a report: prioritized fix list × ease of implementation, with copy-paste fix text for Shopify/Woo (e.g., SQL/ Liquid snippets and meta descriptions).

• Fix impact simulation: estimate how many products will be approved after applying fixes, before resubmitting.

• Appeal letter generator based on the actual suspension reason and official Google policies, with an evidence checklist.

• Works without installing a permanent app (Dry-run via URL + product file, or optional API permissions) — reduces installation resistance.

3) Scope of the first version (8 tasks max)

1. Enter store URL or upload products feed (CSV/XML) for an instant check.

2. Updatable rules engine (JSON rules) covering ~40 common errors per Google Merchant policy (data, pricing, shipping, language, images).

3. Compare landing page with each product: match of price/availability/shipping/return policy.

4. Site file check: robots.txt, return-policy, contact, shipping, SSL certificate.

5. Results dashboard: filter by severity (critical/warning) with fix guidance for each item.

6. Generate an editable Appeal letter with PDF export.

7. Shared report for agencies (public link + comparison log after re-check).

8. Free account + Stripe payment page for paid plans.

4) Pricing and monetization

• Free: 3 checks/month for one store, simple report without Appeal — for trust and distribution.

• Pro — $29/month: unlimited checks, error prioritization, Appeal letter, weekly monitoring and change reports.

• Agency — $99/month: 10 stores, API and white-label, before/after comparison.

• Additional revenue: optional "human review" for Appeals at $49 per case.

• Goal: convert Free → Pro during the first real failure (suspension), since willingness to pay at that moment is extremely high.

5) Global distribution plan using free channels

• Long-form SEO articles targeting purchase intent: "why are my products disapproved", "Merchant Center suspension appeal template", with English + Hindi/Spanish versions for the most affected markets.

• Free downloadable templates: appeal letter form, pre-submission checklist (screenshots of email vs inbox).

• Communities: r/shopify, r/PPC, Merchant Center Facebook groups, PrestaShop/WooCommerce forums — share specific solutions, not ads.

• Micro-agency partnerships: free agency plan in exchange for a public review + case study.

• Short screen clips (TikTok/YouTube Shorts/Reels) showing: store check → issue discovery → one fix → product approval.

• Localized landing page + highly accurate search-focused FAQ (Error 8xx / Policy violation codes).

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: must state that the tool is not affiliated with Google and does not guarantee approval; keep customer data compliant with GDPR/CCPA and Shopify policy; do not provide formal legal advice.

• Technical: continuous updates for Google rules (they change often each quarter); risk the tool is used to cover real violations (mitigate with human review); reliance on store API may fail due to permissions; landing page check accuracy affected by JS/Anti-bot.

• Operational: estimated check errors may weaken trust — so each result has a "confidence score" and a prompt to re-check after fixes.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: ShelfSure

Tagline: Check your store before Google suspends your account, and get a ready fix plan within minutes.
