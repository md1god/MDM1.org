Sijla — Complete Project Plan

Your notes are encrypted and private — privacy is a right, not a paid feature.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, business model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under development and has not been launched yet.

Encrypted Notes Product Plan

1) Target Audience

• Individuals who care about privacy (journalists, researchers, lawyers, volunteers).

• Markdown enthusiasts and users looking for a lighter, faster alternative to traditional note-taking apps.

• Small teams (2–10 people) who want an encrypted shared workspace without complex setup.

• Students and users in countries with strict digital privacy restrictions.

2) Key Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• Local-first: all functions work offline, and sync is optional and end-to-end encrypted.

• No mandatory account: start immediately; an account is only required for syncing across devices.

• Instant on-device search (Client-side search) across all notes without uploading any text.

• Flexible Markdown editor with ready-made templates and a native Arabic RTL interface from day one.

• Open-source and auditable: encryption and sync code in a public repository with an external security audit.

• Transparent, no-surprise pricing: full privacy is included in the free plan.

3) Scope of the First Release (Max 8 Tasks)

1. Local-first web app using Service Workers and IndexedDB.

2. On-device encryption layer (XChaCha20-Poly1305) with keys derived from a passphrase (Argon2id).

3. Markdown editor with RTL support and basic templates.

4. Tags and instant local search.

5. E2EE sync via a lightweight server with simple conflict resolution (Last-writer-wins + version history).

6. Lightweight desktop app via Tauri (Windows/macOS/Linux).

7. Import and export of Markdown and ZIP.

8. External security audit + public beta (Public beta).

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free: unlimited notes on one device, sync up to 2 devices, full export.

• Pro — $4/month or $36/year: unlimited sync, long version history, encrypted share links with expiration, priority support.

• Team — $6/user/month: shared spaces, permissions, unified billing.

• Complementary software: self-hosting (Self-host) with a paid license for businesses.

5) Global Distribution Plan via Free Channels

• Multilingual SEO blog: comparisons, migration guides, privacy explainers.

• Launch on Product Hunt, Hacker News, Reddit (r/privacy, r/selfhosted).

• Short video channels: 1-minute migration guides from other apps.

• Beginner translator program (Localization) for 10 languages in the first 3 months.

• Discord/Telegram community with a public roadmap board.

• Partnerships with Arabic and English privacy and tech pages.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal: strict export controls on encryption in some countries; avoid any marketing of “circumvention”, comply with GDPR and CCPA for billing data only.

• Legal: liability for shared links; clear abuse policy with automated reporting.

• Technical: losing the passphrase = losing data; provide an encrypted “recovery key” and clear warnings.

• Technical: sync infrastructure costs; start with one server and efficient diffing.

• Technical: data leaks via indexes or backups; encrypt indexes too and conduct regular penetration tests.

7) Proposed Name and Tagline

Proposed name: Sijla (Sijla)

Tagline: Your notes are encrypted and private — privacy is a right, not a paid feature.
