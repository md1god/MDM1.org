Watad — Full Project Plan

Your tasks anchored on your own server, with no subscriptions and no restrictions.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, growth plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

Product plan: an open-source task manager that you truly own

1) Target audience

• Individuals who control their data: those who refuse to store their tasks on a closed cloud.

• Small teams and startups: they need a lightweight tool that is self-hosted without complexity.

• Organizations with compliance requirements: education, health, public sector, where policies prohibit sending data to a third party.

• Developers and system administrators: they value a clean API, easy deployment packages (Docker), and CI/CD integration.

2) Points of distinction (why choose our product)

• Full data ownership: deploy with one command on your server, with export/import in open formats.

• Extreme lightness: a single low-resource package that runs on the cheapest server or even a home device.

• Arabic-first interface (RTL): native support for Arabic and other languages, not superficial translation.

• Built-in collaboration without a cloud: share tasks and projects over your own network.

• Free to start: no registration, no card, no limits on self-hosting.

• Extensible with components: optional plugins without bloating the core.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Create/edit/delete tasks with titles, due dates, and priorities.

2. Projects and task lists that can be reordered and named.

3. Tagging/classification, search, and quick filtering.

4. Local authentication (email and password login) and basic permissions.

5. Share a project between users on the same server.

6. Responsive user interface with full RTL support.

7. A documented REST API + import/export (CSV/JSON).

8. One-command deployment via Docker with basic backup.

4) Pricing and profit model

• Free forever: full self-hosting with all core features.

• Managed cloud (Cloud Pro): a low monthly subscription for those who do not want to manage a server.

• Team plan (Team): paid seats + advanced sync + audit log.

• Support and consulting: paid enterprise support, installation and management, and customization.

• Sponsorship and paid add-ons: a plugin marketplace with commission, without restricting the open core.

5) Global growth plan through free channels

• GitHub / GitLab: open repository, periodic releases, a strong README in English and Arabic.

• Reddit and Hacker News: "Show HN" posts and r/selfhosted and r/opensource with every release.

• Self-hosting communities: Product Hunt, Awesome-Selfhosted, AlternativeTo.

• Technical content: articles and tutorials on DEV.to and YouTube about "own your tasks."

• Open-source SEO: comparison pages and search-optimized documentation.

• Contributors: a contributor program, issues labeled "good first issue" to attract developers.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: respecting the licenses of the libraries used, and not copying any code or design from existing products; an original name and brand identity, and verifying there is no conflict with registered trademarks.

• Privacy and compliance: GDPR for European customers in the cloud version, and transparency in data processing.

• Technical: authentication security and preventing vulnerabilities (XSS/CSRF/privilege leakage), automated tests for updates.

• Sustainability: the risk of contributor decline and maintenance burden; addressing it with sustainable funding and clear governance.

• Competition: a crowded market; standing out through Arabic-first, lightness, and full ownership.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Watad — Watad

Tagline: Your tasks anchored on your server, with no subscriptions and no restrictions.
