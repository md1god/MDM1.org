Our Neighborhood — Complete Project Plan

Build your neighborhood in minutes and let your city's brands shine in it every day

A complete plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, profit model, rollout, and risks. Under construction, not yet launched.

1) Target audience

• Casual players 16-35: seek a light, no-download game for short 3-7 minute sessions while commuting/waiting; many have mid/low-end phones.

• Internet users in major Middle East, North Africa, and South Asia cities: long mobile use, need fast play even on intermittent 4G/limited data.

• Small local businesses: cafes, barbershops, clothing stores, delivery and local service apps needing cheaper ads than big platforms, targeting their own city.

• Emerging digital brands: payment apps, e-commerce, booking platforms testing messages with young daily users who ignore traditional ads.

• Small-agency marketing managers: want a measurable new channel — impressions, clicks, watch time — for clear weekly client reports.

2) Differentiators (why we choose our product)

• In-game ads, not interruptions: brands on 3D billboards in streets/squares feel part of the city, gaining more attention than skippable video ads.

• Instant, no install: browser game on phone/PC under 50 MB via one link, removing the main barrier on weak devices.

• Fully original art/city: fictional city inspired by public markets/neighborhoods, simple distinctive colors, unique identity, no copyright issues.

• Free to start: full play free; advertisers pay only for impressions, quickly building a large player base before profit.

• Clear advertiser measurement: dashboard with daily impressions, click rate, player geography, and in-game store visits, to renew confidently.

• Player rewards, not annoying ads: passing a billboard triggers a short challenge or in-game coins, benefiting player and brand together.

3) Version 1 scope (max 8 tasks)

1. Instant guest account: phone/email login in <30 seconds, progress auto-saved on server.

2. 12×12 grid city map: buy land, build house/store/garden, move by touch/keyboard between streets.

3. Simple economy: one currency from daily tasks like deliveries/repairs, spent on building/expansion, daily cap against cheating.

4. 24 in-city ad sites: billboards on large buildings, squares, bus stops; each with different price, size, location.

5. Advertiser panel: upload logo/image up to 2 MB, choose city/days, preview billboard before publishing.

6. In-game stats page: fixed page of top 10 billboards, with paid slot below for extra visibility.

7. Review/reporting: automatic image checks for prohibited text/content; report button stops billboard within 24h.

8. Analytics/trial payment: daily/weekly reports for both sides; card/local e-wallets; free trial first 7 days.

4) Pricing and profit model

• Player: fully free initially: no subscriptions/video ads; optional decorative items $0.99-$4.99, no gameplay-power effect.

• Neighborhood package: $9/week for residential-area billboard, up to 20,000 monthly impressions; ideal for one cafe/store testing.

• Downtown package: $49/month for large main-square billboard plus stats-page appearance; for steady presence.

• Capital package: $199/month for top 3 city sites, detailed report and trackable clicks; for national brands/agencies.

• Pay-per-impression: after packages, $0.80-$2.50 per 1,000 impressions by location; stops automatically when balance ends.

• Target at 10,000 active players: 100 Neighborhood + 20 Downtown buyers = ~$1,880/month before costs, covering hosting/support initially.

5) Global rollout plan with free channels

• TikTok/Reels shorts: 3 videos/week, 15-30s, showing a full neighborhood build or challenge win, play link in description, light-game hashtags.

• Gaming/developer communities: post in browser/indie game forums, showcase at free events like online indie exhibitions.

• SEO: Arabic/English pages like "browser city game no download", targeting 50 short gameplay articles for lasting visits.
