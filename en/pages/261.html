Mirqam — Complete Project Plan

All your books, on all your devices, with Arabic reading at its finest.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, business model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not yet been launched.

Product plan: a multi-device e-book reader

1) Target Audience

• Arab readers who suffer from poor support for right-to-left (RTL) and diacritics in existing applications.

• Those who own more than one device (phone + e-ink reader) and want to resume reading where they left off.

• Students and researchers who read PDF and DjVu alongside EPUB.

• Owners of low-resource legacy e-readers looking for a lightweight, open application.

2) Key Advantages (Why Choose Our Product)

• Authentic Arabic typography: diacritics, Kashida, correct line wrapping, and embedded fonts — a point competitors often overlook.

• Real-time syncing of reading position and notes across all devices with smart conflict resolution.

• A single lightweight rendering engine that works on weak hardware and e-ink devices.

• Privacy first: books and notes are local by default, and syncing is encrypted with user permission.

• Free to start with no intrusive ads, and no lock-in to a single platform.

3) Scope of the First Version (Max 8 Tasks)

1. A native rendering engine for EPUB and PDF (built from scratch).

2. A local library with indexing, metadata extraction, and cover art retrieval.

3. An Arabic/RTL layout engine with embedded fonts and diacritics support.

4. Reading position tracking, bookmarks, and table of contents.

5. Highlighting, notes, and export (Markdown/text).

6. Instant dictionary and translation on word selection.

7. Optional encrypted sync between devices with conflict resolution.

8. Reading modes (night/day/sepia) and simple reading statistics.

Platform release: Android + Linux for low-resource devices (other systems later).

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free tier: all basic reading features with no limits and no ads.

• Optional paid "Support" plan (monthly/annual or one-time) unlocks: larger sync space, cloud backup, family sync, and priority technical support.

• Non-monetized sales: donations, a "Patron" edition for institutions and libraries, and sponsor support without locking core features.

• No sale of reading data and no ad tracking.

5) Global Distribution Plan via Free Channels

• Publish the open-source repository to attract contributors and translators.

• Reach Arabic book and reading communities (forums, Telegram/Discord groups, Goodreads-like platforms).

• Leverage tech review channels on YouTube and RSS platforms.

• Distribute via app stores (Google Play and F-Droid) with descriptions optimized for Arabic keywords.

• Partner with e-book bloggers, reading challenge organizers, and run campaigns on Reddit and literary communities.

• Publish educational articles on repurposing old devices to drive organic adoption.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal: do not crack DRM or provide tools to bypass it; respect format and font licenses; avoid trademarks; pay attention to library licenses (GPL) to avoid contaminating proprietary code.

• Legal: comply with privacy laws (GDPR) when cloud syncing and require explicit consent.

• Technical: complexity of PDF rendering and layouts, hardware variability of e-ink devices.

• Technical: sync conflicts and version management, size of embedded fonts, and cost of testing across multiple devices.

7) Proposed Name and Tagline

Proposed name: Mirqam

Tagline: All your books, on all your devices, with Arabic reading at its finest.
