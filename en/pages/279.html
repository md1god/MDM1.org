Mizan Forms — Complete Project Plan

Smart forms built from data and published in minutes — even offline.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not yet been launched.

Product plan: Mizan Forms

1) Target Audience

• Startups and product teams that need quick surveys and registration forms without a large engineering team.

• Marketers and UX teams that run satisfaction studies and usability quizzes.

• Freelance developers and agencies that want a library of embeddable forms for React/Vue/Angular or static pages.

• Universities and small institutions that collect internal data with limited-budget tools.

2) Key Differentiators

• Visual Builder that exports JSON and ready-to-use code, with an extensible open schema.

• offline-first support with later sync, a common gap in similar form tools.

• Built-in accessibility by default: keyboard support, screen readers, color contrast, and full RTL support for Arabic.

• Optional AI assistant to draft questions and analyze text responses, with the ability to fully disable it for local deployment.

• Lightweight performance: small bundle, no heavy dependencies, lazy loading for fields.

3) Scope of First Version (max 8 tasks)

1. Core form engine that reads JSON and returns state and validation.

2. Basic fields: text, email, number, multiple choice, checkboxes, ranking, and file upload.

3. Multi-step forms with progress bar and local draft saving.

4. Visual drag-and-drop editor with live preview and JSON/TypeScript export.

5. Offline-first mode via Service Worker and IndexedDB with sync on reconnection.

6. Simple results dashboard: CSV/Excel export and basic charts.

7. Package as an npm package + embed with two lines (script or component).

8. Two ready templates: customer satisfaction survey and event registration form, in Arabic and English.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free to start: non-commercial use, open-source library, full self-host.

• Pro plan (monthly/yearly): hosted forms, unlimited responses, and AI assistant.

• Enterprise plan: SSO integration, private hosting, priority support — annual commercial license.

• Additional revenue: paid templates and professional forms in a marketplace, with 80% going to the creator.

5) Global Distribution Plan via Free Channels

• Technical content: explicit comparisons with alternatives, "build a form in 5 minutes" tutorials on YouTube and Dev.to.

• Community sharing: Hacker News, Reddit, React/Vue groups, and Arabic developer rooms on Telegram/Discord.

• SEO: free template pages for each use case (registration, support request, employee evaluation) with unique descriptions in Arabic and English.

• Ambassador programs: launch a form-building contest with symbolic prizes and media coverage.

• Partnerships with small agencies that use the product with their clients in exchange for attribution.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal: ensure no trademark or design infringement; review licenses of used libraries; clear user data policy and GDPR/CCPA compliance for hosted deployments.

• Technical: schema drift between versions — addressed with versioned schemas and automatic migration.

• Performance for very large forms — addressed with lazy loading of fields and clear limits in the free plan.

• Browser platform dependency on updates (IndexedDB/Service Worker) — covered by automated integration test suite.

• Competition from major players adding similar features — mitigated by differentiation in RTL, offline-first, and pricing.

7) Proposed Name and Tagline

Proposed name: Mizan Forms

Tagline: Smart forms built from data and published in minutes — even offline.
