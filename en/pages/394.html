GapRadar — Complete Project Plan

Know the opportunity before the market heats up.

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the launch plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: exploring demand before building

1) Target audience

• Small sellers and middlemen (dropshippers) aged 20–40 looking for a profitable product before having capital.

• Content creators (creators) who pick product offers in exchange for commission.

• Affiliate marketing agencies running multiple stores.

• Beginner users who have no paid analytics tools or experience reading data.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Three built-in readings: sales velocity, market saturation by content creators, and content gaps (rarely covered products) — instead of piling up scattered metrics.

• One decision report: instead of raw numbers, it gives the verdict "start / watch / avoid" with a confidence score.

• Free to start with a monthly operations cap, no credit card required for trial.

• Gap alerts: a weekly notification of products whose demand is rising but whose content coverage is weak.

• Affiliate-first mode: ranks results by the chance of getting a partnership offer, not by sales alone.

3) First version scope (8 tasks max)

1. Initial database of products updated manually/automatically on a weekly basis (500 products across 6 categories).

2. Single unified score engine: (sales velocity × growth) ÷ (competitor network) ÷ (content saturation).

3. Dashboard view sortable and filterable by category, score, and date.

4. One product detail page combining the three indicators + a simple chart.

5. "Content gaps" filter showing high-demand / low-coverage products.

6. Account creation by email + saved board (10 products for free).

7. Initial responsive mobile version (simple PWA).

8. FAQ page + disclaimer regarding data accuracy.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: 5 searches/day, 10 saved products, one report per week.

• Pro — 19$/month: unlimited search, gap alerts, CSV export, 6 months of history.

• Scale — 79$/month: API, team accounts, instant alerts, product comparison.

• Extra revenue: referral commission on e-commerce tools and integrated analytics tools (with explicit user opt-in).

5) Global rollout plan using free channels

• YouTube/Shorts: "How to pick a product in 60 seconds" videos using the report screens.

• TikTok/Reels: short series "Product X: start or avoid?" based on the same verdict.

• Reddit and Facebook Groups: posting value guides inside seller communities (no direct advertising).

• Weekly newsletter: the free "Gaps of the week" as a subscription lead magnet.

• Creator partnerships: a referral plan with free credits in exchange for mentioning the tool.

• SEO: "Best of what sells in [category/country]" pages with semi-automated content manually reviewed.

• Communities/Product directories: launch on free posting platforms and tool directories.

• Beta program: first 100 users free for life in exchange for documented feedback.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: relying on platform data may violate their terms of service; solution: public/licensed sources + purchasing an official API if available, and no republication of logos or brand images.

• Legal: accuracy claims about predictions → phrasing as "indicative estimate" + an explicit disclaimer.

• Legal: personal data protection (GDPR/CCPA) when collecting email and behavior.

• Technical: platform structure changes or scraping scripts breaking → avoid full reliance on scraping, with retry queues + alternative sources.

• Technical: data quality/bias → manual review of a weekly sample and a path for user error reports.

• Technical/commercial: competitors copying us → continuous differentiation in "content gaps" and update speed.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: GapRadar

Tagline: Know the opportunity before the market heats up.
