Ufuq Najm — Full Project Plan

Launch into space from your browser in one second.

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the rollout plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

A new product plan for space and astronomy visualization

1) Target audience

• Hobbyists and the curious: anyone who wants to explore space from the browser without installing heavy software.

• Teachers and students: they look for ready-made interactive educational material to present in class.

• Content creators and lecturers: they need high-quality shots and videos for their content.

• Sky observers (Amateurs): they want to know the positions of celestial objects and how to observe them at night.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Runs directly in the browser using WebGPU/Three.js, with no download or installation.

• Ready-made educational mode: pre-arranged tours with simple explanations and set durations.

• Quick time comparison: a slider that shows the evolution of celestial orbits over years and decades.

• Share with one click: any shot turns into a link or GIF that opens for the other side with the same scene.

• Light on weak devices: quality adapts automatically to the device's capability.

• Open source data with AI elements to automatically generate Arabic descriptions.

3) Scope of the first version (8 tasks maximum)

1. A 3D rendering engine that runs in the browser (WebGPU with a WebGL fallback).

2. An embedded database of orbits and astronomical positions (Solar System bodies + bright stars).

3. An interactive timeline for navigating between dates and times.

4. A search panel and quick jump to any object by name.

5. A simple account system + saving favorite scenes in the cloud (free).

6. Sharing shots through reproducible links.

7. A full Arabic interface with Latin support and direction switching.

8. A public beta (Public Beta) on free hosting + a landing page.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free to start: basic rendering, scenes and sharing — with no bank card.

• Free with limits: 3 saved scenes and GIF export at standard resolution.

• Pro — 5 dollars/month: unlimited scenes, 4K MP4 export, private educational tours.

• Ed/School — 15 dollars/month per class: teacher tools and student dashboards.

• Additional revenue: content sponsorship (crediting the data source) and a printed star map store.

5) Global rollout plan using free channels

1. YouTube/Shorts: 30-second "Did you know?" clips generated from inside the product.

2. Twitter/X and Reddit: weekly star maps + sharing shots on the same day.

3. TikTok: demos of the time slider with attractive scenes.

4. School and university forums: publishing free educational tools in education communities.

5. Product Hunt and Hacker News: launching a public beta with the product story.

6. Content partnerships: supplying Arabic astronomy content creators with ready-made scenes for free in exchange for a mention.

7. SEO: a page for every celestial object ("Mars from Mars") in two languages.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: making sure the astronomical orbit data is properly licensed (free usage rights are common) and avoiding any claim regarding the new trademark.

• Legal: avoiding copyright-protected images — relying only on programmatically generated visuals and open data.

• Technical: WebGPU support differs across browsers — making sure there is a WebGL fallback path.

• Technical: accuracy of long-term orbital computations — using verified equations and trusted sources.

• Technical: the hosting cost of shared scenes as we grow — setting the free limits from the very beginning.

• Competitive: existing free products — differentiating through speed, the Arabic language, and the education mode.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Ufuq Najm

Marketing tagline: Launch into space from your browser in one second.
