مسكنلي — Complete Project Plan

Turn your property listings into a fast, multilingual website in minutes—no developer needed.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

## 1) Target Audience

• Independent real estate brokers and small agencies (1–20 employees) without a technical team.

• Small project developers and property management departments that need a website to showcase units quickly.

• Markets with price-sensitive customers and heavy reliance on WhatsApp and phone calls, such as the Gulf, Egypt, Morocco, Turkey, Indonesia, and Latin America.

• Investors and expatriates looking for properties in multiple languages, with full Arabic and RTL support.

2) Differentiation (Why choose our product)

• Launch in minutes without technical knowledge: import listings from CSV/Excel or paste an ad link, then publish an instant website.

• Fast, low-cost website: static pages built automatically and distributed via CDN, instead of heavy servers requiring maintenance.

• Native Arabic experience: RTL, automatic content translation, and local SEO titles in Arabic and English.

• Focus on lead generation: contact forms, WhatsApp messages, and direct calls, with a unified inbox for requests.

• Data ownership and export: full export of listings and the website, without monopoly or technical lock-in.

• Pricing starting free: a permanent free subdomain, with upgrades only when a custom domain or more features are needed.

3) Scope of the First Version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Create a workspace and a free subdomain with a customizable template.

2. Manage property listings: add, edit, and delete, with image upload and automatic compression.

3. Bulk import from CSV/Excel, with error detection and preview before publishing.

4. Property search, filters, and maps, with detailed pages for each property.

5. Broker and agency profiles, plus contact forms linked to WhatsApp and email.

6. A writing assistant and automatic translation of property descriptions between Arabic and English.

7. Search engine optimization: sitemap, Schema structured data, and social sharing tags.

8. A simple analytics dashboard and a unified inbox for customer requests.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free: subdomain, up to 10 properties, platform logo, no credit card required.

• Starter — $9/month: custom domain, up to 100 properties, logo removal, 3 users.

• Pro — $29/month: unlimited properties, 5 users, automatic translation and AI credits, advanced export.

• Agency — $79/month: multiple websites, team roles, API, and priority support.

• Additional revenue sources: extra AI credits, domain resale margin, and fees on partner-designed templates.

• 20% annual discount, and a trial period for paid features with no commitment.

5) Global Go-to-Market Plan with Free Channels

• Programmatic SEO landing pages for every city and property type, with native Arabic and English content.

• Short videos on YouTube and TikTok explaining how to create a real estate website in under 10 minutes.

• Launch on Product Hunt and developer communities, with a free trial.

• Facebook, Reddit, and WhatsApp groups specializing in real estate, sharing value rather than intrusive ads.

• Partnerships with real estate photographers, local agencies, and marketing consultants.

• A free template gallery, and a referral program that grants free upgrades.

• A free migration tool from existing websites and spreadsheets to lower the switching barrier.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Privacy: compliance with GDPR and CCPA, and a clear policy for retaining customer data and disclaimers.

• Real estate content: verifying listing accuracy, preventing discrimination in ads, and respecting local real estate licensing laws.

• Images and rights: user image uploads with ownership confirmation, and a DMCA reporting system.

• Platform data collection: relying on official exports or CSV, and avoiding scraping websites in violation of their terms.

• AI errors: human review before publishing, labeling machine-translated content, and clear usage limits.

• Technically: image storage costs, spam and abuse prevention, tenant data isolation, and monitoring build and publishing limits.

• SEO: preventing duplicate content across websites, and ensuring consistent performance as the platform grows.

7) Suggested Name and Marketing Tagline

Suggested name: مسكنلي
