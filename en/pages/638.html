Dawrak — Complete Project Plan

Manage your waitlist and reservations from your phone, and notify your guest via WhatsApp that their turn is near.

A complete plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market, and risks. The project is under construction and not yet launched.

Product plan: a waitlist and reservation management system for independent restaurants

1) Target audience

• Small/medium independent restaurants (1–3 branches, 20–120 seats) heavily reliant on walk-ins.

• High-WhatsApp markets: MENA, South Asia, Latin America, Southern Europe.

• Owners, floor managers, reception staff, especially those without a tech team or enterprise-system budget.

• Problem: current booking systems are costly (per-customer fees), complex, English-first, and unfit for busy waitlists or WhatsApp.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Flat price per branch; no fees per customer or reservation.

• WhatsApp-first: confirmation, reminders, and “your turn is near” with two-way replies; SMS fallback.

• QR self check-in from a mobile browser, no app download; works on cheap Android tablets.

• Smart wait estimates from actual table-turn data, not a fixed guess.

• Arabic/English/Spanish/French UI and full guest-data export (no lock-in).

• Integrates with Reserve with Google and common POS; CSV export/import for easy migration.

• Works with unstable connections (PWA with later sync), critical on weak restaurant networks.

• Local payments: Mada, Fawry, Pix, UPI, and Stripe globally.

3) First-release scope (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Multi-branch account, staff roles, opening hours, capacity.

2. QR self check-in page + add walk-ins from reception dashboard.

3. Live queue with wait estimates and instant cross-device updates.

4. SMS/WhatsApp notifications with “confirm / cancel / arrived” replies and automated reminders.

5. Public booking page + Reserve with Google sync and simple reservation calendar.

6. Table map with automatic seating suggestions by group size and expected duration.

7. Basic analytics: turnover time, no-show rate, peak hours, customer source.

8. Billing and free-plan limits + CSV data export/import from an old system.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free forever (Starter): 1 branch, unlimited waitlist, 30 reservations/month, QR self check-in, 100 WhatsApp/SMS messages/month.

• Growth — $25/month per branch: unlimited reservations, more messages, Reserve with Google, full reports.

• Pro — $49/month per branch: multiple branches, advanced table map, roles/permissions, priority support.

• Extra revenue: WhatsApp message packs at cost + small margin, custom domain, paid data migration.

• Principles: no per-customer fees, no mandatory annual contracts, cancel anytime. Revenue from subscriptions and usage, not guest data.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Programmatic SEO pages per city/cuisine (“waitlist system for restaurants in Cairo,” “waitlist software for cafés in Lisbon”) with free practical guides.

• Free listing on Google Business Profile and Reserve with Google; visibility in POS integration marketplaces.

• Short TikTok, Reels, YouTube content: peak-time and wait-time tips, with trial offers.

• Restaurant-owner groups on Facebook, WhatsApp, Reddit (value first, no direct ads).

• Partnerships with POS vendors, accountants, local restaurant associations for referral commission.

• Referral program: free month for referrer and referred; free migration for first 100 restaurants.

• Launch on Product Hunt and AppSumo with limited-time offer; transparent pricing comparison page.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Privacy: GDPR and CCPA compliance, legal basis for processing, deletion/export rights, clear retention policy.

• Consent/messaging: explicit opt-in, honor unsubscribes, comply with TCPA, WhatsApp Business policies, approved templates.

• Payments/deposits: never store cards; use PCI-DSS provider; clear no-show fee terms to protect consumers.

• Accessibility/discrimination: WCAG compliance; no data use to discriminate or apply arbitrary pricing.

• Technical: WhatsApp API limits/costs, variable cross-country SMS delivery, offline sync conflicts, tenant data isolation.

• Reliability: time zones and DST, seasonal peaks (Valentine’s Day, Mother’s Day), fragile POS integrations via sync/monitoring layer.

• Platform dependency: SMS fallback if WhatsApp is down; don’t tie product value to one provider.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Dawrak

Tagline: Manage your waitlist and reservations from your phone, and notify your guest via WhatsApp that their turn is near.
