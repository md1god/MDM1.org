Mirqat — Complete Project Plan

Your career twin that explains your fit for every job, prepares your profile honestly, and opens your referral path.

A complete plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and not yet launched.

1) Target audience

• Job seekers in tech, business, and office roles, from recent graduates to 8 years' experience.

• People returning to the workforce and those seeking remote work or international relocation.

• Emerging markets first: Middle East, North Africa, India, Latin America, and Southeast Asia.

• Later: universities, career centers, and small and medium employers, without selling candidate data.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Explainable fit: a score out of 100 based on skills, experience, sector, location, and language, with evidence from the résumé and job and clear gaps.

• Truth guard: every sentence in the tailored résumé is linked to evidence in the user's profile, preventing exaggeration or fabricated experience.

• A career twin that learns: improves ranking based on interviews and rejections, while the final decision remains human.

• Consent-based referrals: an opt-in network and colleague and alumni paths, with permission requested before any contact.

• Assisted applications, not flooding: form filling, then human review before submission, and official integrations with recruitment systems.

• Privacy first: no data selling, full visibility control, and an audit log for every use.

3) MVP scope (max 8 tasks)

1. Import a résumé from PDF/DOCX or manual entry and convert it into a structured skills graph.

2. Collect jobs from public and official sources and add a job link manually, with deduplication.

3. A fit engine that explains the score, reasons, gaps, and recommendations for each job.

4. A short brief for each job: requirements, keywords, and expected interview questions.

5. Tailor the résumé and cover letter for each job, linking every claim to evidence.

6. An application tracker with reminders and follow-ups.

7. Suggest referral paths through a consent network and public data, with outreach templates.

8. Add a browser extension to fill application forms and pause for review before submission.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free forever for job seekers: one profile, 10 fit scores per month, 3 résumé versions per month, and a full tracker.

• Pro for job seekers: $9 per month or $79 per year, with purchasing-power pricing: unlimited fit and tailoring and advanced gap analysis.

• Employers: $299 per month for small companies, or $0.50–$1 per qualified candidate with explicit consent. No access to any profile without opt-in.

• Universities and career centers: $2–$5 per student per year or annual packages.

• Principle: we do not sell data, do not put jobs behind a paywall, and do not charge for applying.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Free SEO tools such as a résumé fit checker and résumé-to-job comparison, with pages for each role, city, and language.

• Launch on Product Hunt and Hacker News, and educational content on Reddit, Discord, and professional Telegram.

• Partnerships with universities, bootcamps, and community career centers in exchange for free student access and feedback.

• Ambassador program: one free Pro month for an active referral.

• Short content on YouTube, TikTok, X, and LinkedIn: job teardowns, résumé mistakes, and anonymized case studies.

• Multilingual support from day one: Arabic, English, Spanish, and Hindi.

• Free integrations with open ATS systems and job management tools.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: comply with GDPR and CCPA, with explicit consent and the right to deletion. Account for the EU AI Act because employment may be classified as high-risk, with transparency, human oversight, and documentation. Review anti-discrimination laws and prevent use of protected attributes. Assess whether the report provided to employers is subject to FCRA in the US. Avoid unauthorized scraping and rely only on official APIs.

• Technical: LLM hallucination and scoring variance, addressed with regression tests, rules, and human review. Job data quality and freshness and deduplication. Prompt injection through job texts, addressed by input sanitization. Inference cost and latency, addressed with caching and smaller models. Data security and tenant isolation. LLM bias and periodic evaluation.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Mirqat (مِرقاة)

Tagline: Your career twin that explains your fit for every job, prepares your profile honestly, and opens your referral path.
