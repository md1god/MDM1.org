مَودي — Full Project Plan

Tell your loved one how you feel with a single tap.

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the rollout plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

New product plan: sharing mental health with close ones

1) Target audience

• People going through psychological stress (anxiety, sadness, exhaustion) who want to explain their condition to family and friends without the difficulty of speaking directly.

• Families and close friends who want to know how to support the person in need without being asked to guess.

• Students and employees in high-pressure environments, and migrants far from their families.

• Arabic- and English-speaking users first, with additional languages supported later.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• "Ready-to-send message" model: the user creates a card explaining their condition in simple language and shares it via a single link, instead of writing everything from scratch.

• Ready-made templates by situation (for a hard day, I need space, I just need to hear from you) with the ability to edit.

• Privacy first: sharing without an account for the recipient, full control over links and their expiration, and content encryption.

• A follow-up dashboard for the recipient: gentle alerts and saved replies that help sustain the support.

• A calm, eye-friendly interface with mood-supportive colors, and high speed on mobile.

3) Scope of the first version (8 tasks at most)

1. Simple account creation (email + password) and secure login.

2. Card creator: choose a template, describe your condition, and set who can receive the share.

3. Sharing via a link with settings (expiry duration, disable resharing, revoke link).

4. A view page for the link visitor with a ready-made reply option (support, a question, later).

5. "Loved ones" dashboard: a list of those who supported you and the status of each message.

6. Recipient dashboard: simple statistics and a reply log.

7. Privacy, export, and full account deletion settings page.

8. Full Arabic/English experience with responsive mobile design.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free to start: all core features at no cost.

• Free with funding (Freemium): a nominal monthly/yearly subscription to remove limits (more updates, custom backgrounds, permanent links, advanced alerts).

• Family plan: a low price covering up to 6 members.

• Enterprise margin: licenses for care teams and non-profit communities (extra tiers for tools only, no data collection).

• No ads, no data selling — a voluntary, transparent subscription-based revenue model.

5) Global rollout plan using free channels

• Short practical content on TikTok and Instagram Reels and X: "how to tell your family you are struggling" in multiple languages.

• Blog and SEO targeting common search phrases about emotional communication and asking for support.

• Reddit and Discord communities and Arabic support groups; offering free shareable templates.

• Partnerships with content creators in the mental health field in exchange for using the product.

• Launch via Product Hunt and Hacker News and the open app directory.

• Referral program: every user who invites a friend gets a cosmetic feature.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: the content may be read as a sign of a mental crisis; an explicit disclaimer must be added that the product is not a substitute for emergency services or a doctor, and local emergency numbers must be enabled.

• Legal: protection of health/semi-health data (GDPR and its equivalents) — designed to be private from the ground up with clear privacy policy documentation.

• Legal: responsibility for content written by users and for sharing links with third parties.

• Technical: leakage of links or content — encryption, revocable links, security review, and rate limiting.

• Technical: scaling and media performance, and the risk of an external provider outage; reducing dependencies and adding backups.

• Ethical: risk of misusing the templates to pressure others — simple behavioral controls.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: مَودي

Marketing tagline: Tell your loved one how you feel with a single tap.
