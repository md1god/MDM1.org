Safa — Complete Project Plan

A cleaner internet with protection that starts from the very first request.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, growth plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a DNS filtering service for digital purity

1) Target audience

• Families and parents who want to protect their children from ads and inappropriate content at the level of the entire network.

• Privacy-minded users who want to reduce tracking and ads without installing extensions on every device.

• Small businesses, cafes, and schools looking for simple centralized protection without expensive hardware.

• Technical users and developers who want customizable lists and an API.

2) Points of distinction (why choose our product)

• One-click setup: activation via DoH/DoT without needing networking expertise.

• Arabic-first lists: categories built for Arab-region content and local ads, a gap competitors ignore.

• Family dashboard: profiles for each member with different policies.

• Full transparency: showing why each domain was blocked and its source, with precise control over exceptions.

• A genuinely free tier to start: the basic layer is free with no time limit.

• Multiple approaches: DNS-only filtering, plus an optional lightweight companion app.

3) Scope of the first version (max 8 tasks)

1. A DNS filtering engine supporting DoH, DoT, and plain DNS.

2. Aggregating blocklists from multiple sources with deduplication and validation.

3. Ready-made categories: ads, tracking, adult, malware.

4. A web dashboard for login and profile management.

5. Multiple profiles per network/device with independent policies.

6. A custom user allow/block list.

7. A simple statistics page (number of blocked requests and top domains).

8. A step-by-step setup guide for Windows, Android, iOS, and routers.

4) Pricing and profit model

• Free: basic filtering, one profile, limited statistics.

• Home (Pro): about $3 per month — unlimited profiles, advanced categories, longer history.

• Business: about $12 per month per site — team management, API, priority support.

• Pre-configured router: a physical sale with a profit margin plus an optional subscription.

• Sponsorship/donations for free users without ads, to preserve trust.

• A fully free first year to gather data and feedback.

5) Global growth plan with free channels

• Open publishing on GitHub/GitLab for the list sources and the generation tool to attract contributors.

• Educational content on Reddit, Hacker News, and privacy forums.

• Short videos on YouTube and TikTok explaining protection in a minute.

• Transparent comparisons with alternatives on the project site (long-tail SEO).

• Partnerships with parent communities, schools, and Arab tech blogs.

• An ambassador program with rewards for bringing new users.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: differing privacy and blocking laws between countries (GDPR and others); the risk of government blocking requests or misclassification blocking legitimate sites.

• Technical: encryption bypass via DoH from other browsers, and a static IP for our servers that could become a target for DDoS attacks.

• List reputation: blocking mistakes cause loss of user trust; solution: a complaints mechanism and instant exception.

• Sustainability: infrastructure hosting cost versus a generous free tier; solution: fair rate limits.

• Strong competition: big free players; solution: specializing in Arabic content and user experience.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Safa

Marketing tagline: A cleaner internet with protection that starts from the very first request.
