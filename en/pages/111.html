نَسَق — Complete Project Plan

A single workspace that brings together your tasks, documents, diagrams, and conversations with full end-to-end encryption.

Complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the go-to-market plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a unified workspace for teams

1) Target audience

• Small and medium teams (5–100 users) that want a lightweight alternative to heavy tools.

• Remote/Hybrid teams, agencies, and freelancers who manage projects and proposals together.

• Privacy-sensitive organizations (legal, healthcare, education, non-profits) that need full encryption or self-hosting.

• Developers and system administrators who prefer full control over their data without lock-in.

• The Arabic user who lacks high-quality native right-to-left interfaces.

2) Points of distinction (why choose our product)

• Privacy first: end-to-end encryption by default for conversations and files, with keys that never leave the user's device.

• Unified space: tasks + documents + diagrams (mind/flow) + chat + files in one app without switching between tools.

• Lightweight and fast: runs on modest devices, with an instant experience and a native RTL interface.

• Self-hosted or cloud: two equivalent options, with open data migration and no vendor lock-in.

• Assistive intelligence layer: summarizing conversations and turning them into tasks and auto-generating diagrams (a clear differentiator versus traditional tools).

• Generous free model: free forever for small teams without withholding core features.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. User accounts and workspaces with basic roles and permissions.

2. Task boards (Kanban + list) with assignment, due dates, and priority.

3. Instant group and direct chat with end-to-end encryption.

4. File storage and sharing with expiring links.

5. Collaborative real-time document editor (multi-user editing).

6. Simplified diagrams board (mind + flow) with PDF and PNG export.

7. In-app and email notifications + unified search across all content.

8. Self-deployment with a single command line + instant cloud sign-up.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free forever: unlimited users, a limited number of active projects, full encryption, and self-hosting.

• Pro plan (per user per month): advanced permissions, SSO, audit logs, more storage, integrations, and automation.

• Enterprise plan: priority support, SLA, training, managed deployment within the client's environment.

• Additional revenue: Managed Hosting, a plugin marketplace for a commission, and support/consulting.

• No selling of data, with transparent pricing and a discount for educational and non-profit associations.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• An open-source repository and building a community of contributors around it.

• Launching on tech product discovery platforms and developer forums.

• Content marketing via SEO: comparison pages, "open alternative" guides, and educational blog posts.

• Video tutorials on YouTube and free live workshops.

• Reddit, Discord, and specialized tech communities and forums.

• A referral program and rewards for contributors, plus participation in university hackathons.

• Integrations with popular developer tools to attract users from within their workflow.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: compliance with data protection laws (such as GDPR), encryption export rules, the open license and adherence to library licenses, and avoiding trademark infringement.

• Legal (operational): terms of use, privacy policy, and handling of minors' data.

• Technical: the complexity of encryption key management, instant sync consistency at scale, security vulnerabilities, maintenance of self-hosted versions, and competition from big players.

• Mitigation: regular security audits, penetration testing, scalable modular architecture, and transparent documentation.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: نَسَق

Tagline: A single workspace that brings together your tasks, documents, diagrams, and conversations with full end-to-end encryption.
