Wajeez — Complete Project Plan

From a single long video to a week of completed short clips, in your brand's style.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not yet been launched.

1) Target Audience

• Individual content creators: podcasters, YouTubers, streamers, coaches, or experts. They publish 1–3 long videos weekly and do not have an editor or an agency budget.

• Small agencies and marketing managers: manage 5–30 clients and need multiple visual identities, approvals, publishing calendars, and rights tracking.

• Corporate and educational content teams: lectures, seminars, internal training, corporate content; they want safe and fast reuse.

• Arabic and bilingual creators: need RTL support, dialects, translation, and switching between Arabic and English within the same video.

Core promise: From a single long video to a package of short clips that are complete in meaning, on-brand, and ready to publish in under 30 minutes.

2) Key Differentiators (Why choose our product)

• Legal-first import: connect channels via OAuth, upload files, or use podcast RSS with rights acknowledgment; no random downloads from public links.

• Story-aware clipping: transcript analysis, speaker identification, scene boundaries, and idea completeness; select clips with a beginning, context, peak, and end—not just loud moments.

• Interpretable Hook Score: explains why a clip was recommended, suggests 3 options for titles and openings, without viral reach guarantees.

• Deep Arabic and bilingual support: word-level captions, RTL support, translation, and language switching, with safe templates for visible areas.

• On-brand studio: reframing that respects the speaker, layouts for single or multiple speakers, fonts, colors, logos, and instant preview.

• Original and fast design: only three stages: Source, Filters, Export; native Arabic UI, shortcuts, and easy human review.

• Privacy and governance: no training on client content, source deletion after a set period, change logs, and approvals for agencies.

3) Scope of First Version (Max 8 Tasks)

· # · Task · Defined Scope ·

· 1 · Authorized import · YouTube/Twitch via OAuth, MP4/MOV/MP3/WAV uploads, or podcast RSS; up to 90 minutes, with file checks, signed links, and ownership acknowledgment. ·

· 2 · Audio analysis · Word-level transcription, speaker identification, filler and silence detection, Arabic and English support, and a correctable confidence score. ·

· 3 · Story filters · Split content into clips complete in meaning, show top 10 filters with recommendation reason, Hook Score, and adjustable clip boundaries. ·

· 4 · Vertical framing · Active speaker tracking, 9:16 layouts for one or two speakers, platform-safe zones, and manual override on tracking failure. ·

· 5 · Captions and identity · Word-by-word editing, RTL, translation, font/color/logo templates, and live preview. ·

· 6 · Lightweight editor · Cut, remove filler and silence, adjust pacing, sync captions, undo/redo, without becoming a full video editor. ·

· 7 · Export · 1080p, 9:16/1:1/16:9 ratios, SRT/VTT files, and watermark for the free plan. ·

· 8 · Publishing and scheduling · Publish via official APIs to TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels based on account eligibility, with calendar, manual approval, retry, and fallback download. ·

Out of scope: automated dubbing, voice cloning, full professional editor, advanced team analytics, LinkedIn/X, mobile apps.
