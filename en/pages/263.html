Mee'yar — Complete Project Plan

Free, source-cited accounting answers in seconds for every professional.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, target audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Licensed and certified accountants and auditors (primary users).

• Tax advisors and financial compliance officers in companies.

• Accounting and auditing students preparing for professional certifications (CPA / ACCA / CMA).

• Accounting and audit firms (as enterprise buyers later), which will be the paying customers.

2) Key Differentiators (Why Choose This Product)

• Source-cited answers first: Each answer includes the standard text, clause number, official source, and a link for verification; no black box.

• Free for licensed professionals: End users pay nothing, enabling rapid adoption within the profession.

• Built for one profession: Accounting terminology, financial statements, and disclosures, not a shallow general assistant.

• Regional regulatory coverage: Integrates local tax regulations with international standards, something general tools cannot provide.

• Declared neutrality: Advertising does not influence answers, with a clear separation between paid content and results.

• Ready for integration: API for firms and practice management software.

3) Scope of the First Version (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. Ask a question and get an answer with verifiable citations (standard text + clause number).

2. Semantic search across standards and regulations (IFRS, ISA, local regulations).

3. Summarize professional documents (contracts, financial statements, reports) and extract obligations.

4. Compare two standards or treatments with a comparison table.

5. Assist in preparing disclosures, financial statements, and draft notes.

6. Alerts for regulatory updates and standard changes.

7. Accounts and verification of professional licenses.

8. Internal dashboard to analyze frequently asked questions to improve coverage.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free forever for licensed professionals.

• Premium contextual advertising: From accounting software providers and training companies, without compromising answers.

• Sponsored Continuing Professional Education (CPE): Sponsored courses pay to reach a qualified professional audience.

• Enterprise contracts and API for firms and audit companies (annual subscription, higher margin).

5) Global Distribution Plan Through Free Channels

• SEO for accounting-related queries.

• Seeding in professional forums.

• Partnerships with accountancy bodies.

• University outreach.

• CPE webinars.

• Referrals.

• Social (LinkedIn).

• Free CPE credits.

6) Risks

• Legal: Unauthorized practice, liability for incorrect advice, licensing, data privacy/GDPR, IP of standards content, advertising influence on neutrality.

• Technical: Hallucination, source coverage, retrieval accuracy, keeping laws updated, latency/cost.
