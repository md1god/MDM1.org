LeaseLift — Full Project Plan

Renew your lease and compliance in minutes, without a lawyer.

A full execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the launch plan, and the risks. The project is being built and has not launched yet.

LeaseLift — Original PropTech Product Plan

1) Target Audience

• Primary focus: small landlords who own 1 to 5 residential units (retail sellers, partial investors).

• Secondary focus: independent real estate agents (Solo Agents) who manage a small client list and need renewals and compliance without corporate systems.

• Traits: they have no legal team, fear deadline mistakes and illegal rent increases, and pay fees to lawyers or offices for simple recurring documents.

• First geographic market: states with complex and changing local rental rules (cities/states with residential walls), then expansion to other countries.

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

1. Deliberate narrowness: we solve only one problem (lease renewal and compliance) instead of repeating a full property management system.

2. Updated knowledge base: increase caps and alerts built by city/state, with the last review date shown to the user.

3. Time to result: from unit data to a sign-ready document in minutes, not hours.

4. Free tier that works: truly free forever with two units, not just a 7-day trial.

5. Non-expert interface: simple language with no legal jargon, plus a clear notice "this is not legal advice."

6. Deadline automation: advance reminders (60/30/7 days) that prevent harmful auto-renewal for both parties.

3) First Version Scope (8 Tasks Maximum)

1. Basic unit file (address, current rent, lease start date, landlord/tenant).

2. Increase rules database: increase cap by state/city with last update date.

3. Renewal letter or rent-change notice generator (editable template + PDF export).

4. Reminders dashboard: automatic alerts 60/30/7 days before lease end.

5. Simple annual unit profitability account (revenue − costs − cash flow).

6. AI writing assistant: drafting replies to tenant questions about the renewal.

7. Secure document storage and notice history log per unit.

8. Single dashboard showing all units and each renewal status (up to 5 units on the free plan).

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

· Plan · Price · What It Includes ·

· Free · $0/month, forever · 2 units, rules database, 3 documents per month, basic reminders ·

· Pro · $19/month ($190/year) · Up to 5 units, unlimited documents, smart assistant, advanced alerts ·

· Portfolio · $49/month · Up to 25 units, profitability reports, priority rule updates, bulk export ·

• Expected revenue source: monthly subscriptions (the largest share) + additional revenue from electronic document notarization (e-signature) priced per document.

• Conversion funnel: free with no credit card → "your increase is illegal in your city" discovery → upgrade to Pro at the third unit.

• Limited-time launch: lifetime Early Adopter offer at a fixed price (limited seats) to fund development.

5) Global Launch Plan Using Free Channels

1. Local SEO: a page per city/state: "Can I raise rent in [City]?" — high search intent and endless content.

2. Standalone free tool: "Rent Increase Cap Calculator" as a landing page that collects emails.

3. Landlord communities: Reddit and Facebook Groups for small real estate investors, with educational non-advertising posts.

4. Short YouTube/TikTok: 60-second explainers "3 mistakes that end your lease."

5. Product Hunt + free tool directories: launch on Product Hunt and free tool listings.

6. Partnerships: real estate investor newsletters, small property accountants, and independent agents as referral channels (discount referral plan).

7. Comparative content: "full property management system vs. a single renewal tool" comparisons targeting those who don't want a heavy system.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

Legal:

• Risk of providing legal interpretation → explicit disclaimer + human review of documents before sending.

• Outdated and highly variable local rental rules → show review date and a "consult your lawyer when in doubt" alert.

• Tenant data protection (personal and financial data) → encryption, clear retention policy, GDPR/CCPA compliant.

• Liability for generated documents if submitted to court without review.

Technical:

• Updating the rules database manually or semi-automated: may lag or err → reference source plus a user alert when uncertain.

• AI reliance may produce inaccurate wording → verified templates and strict limits on assistant outpu
