Mizan — Complete Project Plan

Precise distinction between human and AI content with full privacy protection.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, growth plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Students: to verify the nature of texts before submitting assignments.

• Teachers and graders: to check the authenticity of work quickly and without complexity.

• Publishers: to confirm content authenticity before publishing.

• Hiring managers: to verify cover letters and written content.

2) Points of Differentiation (Why choose our product)

• Focus on context: analyzing the text as a whole, not just individual words, to get more accurate results.

• Privacy first: not storing any text that is checked, with priority given to processing it directly.

• Supporting both languages together: strong support for Arabic and English in the same tool.

• Clear presentation: dividing the result into segments with a simplified explanation, instead of just a single number.

3) Scope of the First Version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. [ ] Interface design: a simple homepage containing a text input and a check button.

2. [ ] Model integration: using a lightweight, open-source detection model.

3. [ ] Segment analysis: showing a probability for each paragraph separately.

4. [ ] Supporting both languages: enabling checking for both Arabic and English.

5. [ ] Privacy policy: applying the principle of not storing texts at all.

6. [ ] Speed optimization: ensuring fast results without the need to create an account.

7. [ ] Text limit: setting a safe length limit with a clear message when it is exceeded.

8. [ ] Initial launch: uploading the version to a free hosting domain.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Starting model: completely free for general checking, without restrictions that affect individual use.

• Future revenue sources:

• API: for entities with large needs, with a paid plan when the free limit is exceeded.

• Educational partnerships: non-advertising collaboration with educational institutions.

• Principle: keeping regular checking free always as much as possible.

5) Global Growth Plan with Free Channels

• SEO: creating pages targeting user questions about content authenticity.

• Educational communities: contributing real value in student and teacher communities around the world.

• Community translation: allowing users to translate the interface into other languages.

• Free tool directories: adding the tool to lists of open and free tools.

• Educational content: publishing short articles about the importance of verifying content authenticity.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

· Type · Risk · Proposed Solution ·

· Legal · Misleading claims about the result · Presenting all results as a "probability," and stating that it is a helping tool and not a final judgment. ·

· Legal · Privacy issues · Not storing texts, and writing a clear and concise privacy policy. ·

· Technical · Uneven accuracy between the two languages · Focusing on improving Arabic language accuracy in particular, and testing it continuously. ·

· Technical · High load · Setting a text limit, and using a lightweight model to handle high traffic. ·

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Slogan

Proposed name: Mizan

Marketing slogan: Precise distinction between human and AI content with full privacy protection.
