LongCast Studio — Complete Project Plan

Launch professional long-form YouTube videos with no face and no camera, from idea to export in one workspace.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: automating long-form YouTube videos without personal appearance (8–15 minutes)

1) Target audience

• Solo and beginner content creators who want to publish daily without a camera or personal voice.

• "Educational/story/finance niche" channels that rely on a high RPM ratio (10–15 minutes).

• Marketing agents and small agencies managing several channels for their clients.

• Creators in multilingual markets (Arabic, English, Spanish) looking for original localized production.

2) Differentiators

• Real content length: generation is tuned for 8–15 minute videos with interconnected sections, not short snippets.

• Natural voiceover tone and consistent pacing across the entire video.

• Source-backed research with point extraction and preliminary verification to avoid false claims.

• Smart visual synchronization: selecting scenes based on the context of each segment rather than randomly.

• Rewrite control for each section (Hook, body, conclusion) before export.

• Complete separation between the editing room and publishing to avoid risks before human review.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Choosing the idea and niche and entering the initial title.

2. Building a detailed outline divided by time for an 8–15 minute video.

3. Writing an original script optimized for SEO (title, description, tags, section separation).

4. Converting the script into a voiceover with multiple voices and adjustable pacing.

5. Generating/collecting the visual scene set and synchronizing it with the audio.

6. Adding background music and automatic subtitles.

7. Previewing the video in the browser with the ability to edit any segment.

8. Exporting an MP4 file and uploading it as a draft to YouTube (without automatic publishing).

4) Pricing and profit model

• Free to start: one production minute or one video per month with a watermark.

• Creator: ~$19/month — 8 videos, no watermark, additional voices.

• Studio: ~$49/month — 30 videos, 5 channels, multi-section composition.

• Agency: ~$149/month — practically unlimited channels + a discount on extra minutes.

• Additional income from consumption credit for overage packages when the quota is exceeded.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Organic content on YouTube and TikTok showing a "before/after" of producing a complete video.

• A community on Reddit, Discord, and Indie Hackers to offer the free version and receive feedback.

• Partnerships with emerging content creators in exchange for early access.

• Producing SEO articles on Medium/Dev.to explaining automation and RPM.

• Product Hunt and BetaList to launch the first free version.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Content rights: ensuring licensing of the voices, music, and scenes used.

• YouTube policies: complying with reused-content rules to avoid penalties.

• Hallucination and information: the risk of incorrect information in educational/financial videos.

• Cost collapse: cloud generation cost may exceed subscription revenue under intensive use.

• Audio/sync quality: variability of voiceover quality and synchronization across languages.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: LongCast Studio

Marketing tagline: Launch professional long-form YouTube videos with no face and no camera, from idea to export in one workspace.
