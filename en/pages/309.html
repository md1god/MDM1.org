Sijili — Full Project Plan

Your entire clinic in one place, ready in minutes.

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the rollout plan, and the risks. The project is under construction, not yet launched.

New product plan: a cloud-based digital clinic management system

1) Target audience

• Small and medium clinics (1–10 physicians): general practice, dental, dermatology, pediatric, and women's clinics.

• Resident physicians and independent practitioners who want to start operating within one day, without a server or an IT team.

• Aesthetics clinics, physiotherapy clinics, and labs connected to clinics.

• Emerging markets first (Middle East and North Africa, Southeast Asia), where affordable local systems are lacking.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Fully cloud and instant: create an account and start in minutes, with no installation or server maintenance.

• Native Arabic/English from day one (interface and bilingual report printing), not a later translation.

• Consultation first: a data model built around the visit, the diagnosis, and the prescription, not complex administrative tables.

• Offline mode: local storage and automatic sync when the network returns (important in areas with poor coverage).

• Automatic note generation: visit summary, prescription, and report from smart templates, with secure electronic publishing to the patient.

• Built-in security and compliance: full encryption, an audit log for every operation, managed backups, and a trial without a credit card.

3) Scope of the first release (8 tasks maximum)

1. Patient records: search, longitudinal medical file, protected sensitive fields.

2. Appointment schedule: daily/weekly calendar, SMS/email reminders.

3. Visit editor: complaint, examination, diagnosis, treatment plan.

4. Prescriptions and instructions: templates, dose calculator, printing/e-sending.

5. Billing and payments: invoices, partial payments, daily sales reports.

6. Basic reports: visit counts, revenue, most common diagnoses.

7. Bilingual (Arabic/English) mobile-responsive dashboard.

8. Documented REST API + CSV import/export to migrate from any legacy system.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free to start: a Free plan for one physician with 30 patients/month and limited visits — no credit card.

• Pro at 19$/month per physician: unlimited patients, reminders, advanced reports, billing.

• Clinic at 49$/month for a clinic of up to 5 physicians: roles and permissions, medicine inventory, aggregate reports.

• Paid add-ons: electronic invoicing for patients, extra SMS messages, lab and pharmacy integrations.

• 20% discount when billed annually, and a reduced introductory price for the first years to attract emerging markets.

5) Global rollout plan using free channels

1. Arabic/English content on "digital transformation for small clinics": articles and short clips on YouTube and LinkedIn.

2. Free community on Discord/Telegram: fast support + referral champions with free credit.

3. Presence in groups and places where clinic owners gather in each target market.

4. Public open trial, sign-up without an appointment + short video lessons.

5. Partnerships with medical colleges and training centers (free training in exchange for a product mention).

6. Ready-to-download templates (patient file, visit report, invoice) to generate organic visits.

7. Referral program: one free month for anyone who invites a clinic that completes sign-up.

8. Community presence in projects and open groups to build trust and credentials.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: health data protection requires specific standards in each country (such as HIPAA outside the US, and local privacy laws); solution: encryption, explicit consents, data processing agreements, and regional hosting when needed.

• Legal: we do not offer medical diagnosis — Sijili is an administrative/documentation tool only, with a clear disclaimer.

• Legal: drug and diagnosis lists must be updated according to the official authorities in each market.

• Technical: data loss or breach → managed backups, encryption, and periodic restore tests.

• Technical: conflicts between cross-border hosting laws → multiple hosting regions and explicit consent for transfers.

• Technical: weak adoption if the interface is complex → early user testing, only two languages at the start.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Sijili

Tagline: Your entire clinic in one place, ready in minutes.
