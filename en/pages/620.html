Kanz — Complete Project Plan

Everything you copy becomes organized memory, yours alone, ready to paste instantly.

A complete execution plan for a new digital product: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

A plan for an original clipboard management product on macOS

1) Target audience

• macOS users who copy a lot: developers, writers, researchers, designers, support teams, and operations staff.

• Freelancers and small teams that reuse texts, recipes, and recurring clips.

• Multilingual users and users of right-to-left interfaces.

• People in privacy-sensitive fields such as law, finance, health, and education.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Privacy first: encrypted local storage, no mandatory account, and no cloud in the first version.

• Operational speed: an instant access panel via a global shortcut, plus keyboard search and filtering.

• Smart organization: filtering by app, type, and date, and pinboards for recurring projects.

• A lightweight native experience: modern macOS design, low resource use, and full Arabic and RTL support.

• Commercial transparency: a permanent free core, an available one-time payment, no ads, and no data selling.

• Safe recovery: encrypted local backups and controllable export.

3) First version scope (at most 8 tasks)

1. Automatically capture text, images, and links, with sensitive apps excluded.

2. Encrypted local storage with the key in Keychain, with automatic deletion and a retention policy.

3. A quick access panel via a global shortcut, with instant search and filtering by type, app, and date.

4. Pinboards, saving frequently used items, and reusing them.

5. Snippets with simple variables such as date and time.

6. Basic transformations: plain text, letter case, whitespace removal, and link-to-Markdown conversion.

7. Settings: launch at login, shortcuts, retention period, and pause.

8. Signed and notarized distribution, with onboarding, a privacy policy, and secure updates.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free forever: 500 items, two pinboards, basic search, and basic transformations.

• Pro: $24 per year or $59 one-time; includes unlimited history, unlimited boards, advanced transformations, and encrypted backups.

• Later: optional encrypted sync for teams with a separate subscription.

• No ads and no data selling, with a full trial period before purchase.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• A bilingual landing page and SEO content that solves practical copy-and-paste problems.

• Launch on Product Hunt, Show HN, macOS communities, and productivity communities on Reddit.

• Short videos on YouTube and TikTok showing the workflow in under 30 seconds.

• Free listings in app directories and alternatives platforms, with honest reviews from content creators.

• A referral and rewards program, and community translation for English, Arabic, French, German, Spanish, and Japanese.

• Targeting universities and Arab and international tech communities through short free workshops.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Privacy: The clipboard may contain passwords and financial data; solution: encryption, app exclusions, pause, and instant deletion.

• Compliance: GDPR and CCPA, a clear privacy policy, and no selling or sharing of data.

• Intellectual property: trademark search, original interface and design, and review of open-source library licenses.

• Distribution: App Store restrictions, sandbox, and macOS permissions; solution: signed and notarized direct distribution with an alternative inside the store.

• Performance: slowness or memory use with large histories; solution: lightweight indexing and adjustable limits.

• Data loss: corruption of the database or key; solution: encrypted backups, recovery tests, and a security audit.

• Compatibility: differences between macOS versions and Apple Silicon and Intel; solution: an early test matrix and phased updates.

Proposed name: Kanz

Tagline: Everything you copy becomes organized memory, yours alone, ready to paste instantly.
