Mizan — Complete Project Plan

A multichain sovereign wallet that brings your assets and NFTs together in one place, with privacy by default and intermediary-free swaps.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a sovereign multichain wallet

1) Target audience

• Users in emerging markets who want to self-custody their assets without technical complexity.

• NFT collectors seeking a secure, curated display with fraud protection.

• Privacy-conscious users who reject tracking or data collection.

• Arab beginners: a native Arabic RTL interface and built-in education.

• Multichain traders who need one wallet instead of several apps.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Privacy by default: no tracking or external analytics, address isolation per account, and optional Tor/Proxy support.

• Local NFT screening: spam and fraud filtering on-device without sending wallet data.

• Transparent decentralized swap: liquidity aggregation from multiple protocols with MEV protection and disclosed fees.

• Human recovery: traditional backups + social recovery guardians to prevent wallet loss.

• Human experience: Arabic-first interface, gas simplification, Testnet mode, and hardware wallet support.

• Open source and auditable: full transparency and reproducible builds.

3) First version scope (maximum 8 tasks)

1. HD wallet core using BIP39/BIP44 with encrypted storage and biometrics/PIN.

2. Support core chains: Bitcoin, EVM, and Solana, with more chains added later.

3. Unified balances and NFT display with local classification and fraud protection.

4. Send and receive via QR, address book, fee estimation, and transaction speed-up/cancel.

5. Decentralized swap via liquidity aggregation with MEV protection and transparent fees.

6. Privacy features: separate addresses per account, tracking disabled, and a Tor option.

7. Backup with seed phrase verification, and social recovery via guardians.

8. Full Arabic/English experience, Testnet mode, and an external security audit before launch.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free forever for core features: wallet, chains, NFTs, privacy, and backup.

• Pro plan: $3.99 per month or $29 per year, including advanced analytics, multiple accounts, faster RPC, and encrypted cloud backup.

• Optional 0.2–0.5% swap fee disclosed before confirmation, without forcing the user.

• Partnerships and referral programs with NFT projects and hardware wallets, without ever selling user data.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Publish code on GitHub and F-Droid and attract contributors through beginner-friendly issues.

• Launch on Product Hunt, Reddit, X, and crypto forums with educational rather than promotional content.

• Short video series on YouTube/TikTok about security, privacy, and wallet recovery.

• Local translation and localization communities, university clubs, and free workshops.

• SEO through transparent comparison guides, and a podcast with security experts.

• Public bug bounty program and a referral program that grants Pro features instead of cash.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Classification of the activity as money transmission or a financial service in some countries.

• Sanctions and AML requirements even though the app is non-custodial.

• Tax and disclosure laws, and app store policies regarding cryptocurrencies.

• Privacy and data protection may conflict with local regulatory requests.

Technical:

• Key management and supply chain security for updates.

• Centralization of RPC providers and potential manipulation or outages.

• Chain upgrades and changing NFT/token standards.

• Phishing and malicious NFT attacks, and MEV and aggregation risks.

• Complexity of social recovery and its misuse.

Mitigation: non-custodial architecture, legal review for each market, geoblocking of sensitive features, external audit, multiple RPCs, reproducible builds, and withdrawal limits for new features.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Mizan (Mizan)

Tagline: A sovereign multichain wallet that brings your assets and NFTs together in one place, with privacy by default and intermediary-free swaps.
