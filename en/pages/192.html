Rasd — Complete Project Plan

Your site's numbers in full clarity, without tracking any visitor.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, growth plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: lightweight, privacy-respecting web analytics

1) Target audience

• Independent site owners, bloggers, and individual developers who want clear numbers without complexity.

• Small and medium businesses and agencies managing multiple sites that need client-ready reports.

• Educational and healthcare institutions and entities subject to strict privacy regulations (GDPR/CCPA) that want to avoid annoying consent banners.

• Marketing teams that need to know traffic sources and top pages without violating visitor data.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• No cookies and no personal tracking: fully aggregated measurement, so no consent banner is needed and no identifying data is collected.

• Extreme lightness: a very small JavaScript script that doesn't slow the page, plus the ability to collect visits without JS via a tracking pixel.

• Self-hosted or cloud: we offer the choice for those who want full control of their data without vendor lock-in.

• Instant, simple dashboard: metrics understood at a glance (visits, sources, pages, countries, devices).

• Multi-site support from a single account with permissions and client report sharing.

• Performance and economy: runs on a single small server, with low operating cost that translates into a cheaper price.

• Multilingual support and a native Arabic RTL interface from day one.

3) Scope of the first version (max 8 tasks)

1. Lightweight cookieless tracking script + alternate PNG pixel path.

2. Visit and pageview counter, unique per session, without identifying the visitor.

3. Source/medium/campaign classification + detection of visits from search engines and social networks.

4. Reports: top pages, sources, countries, browser/device, and peak times.

5. Instant dashboard + simple charts + customizable date range.

6. Multi-site management + tracking key per site + user permissions.

7. CSV export and read API + optional public report link.

8. One-click self-install (Docker) + backup and restore.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free to start: a Free plan including one site up to a generous monthly view limit, with data retained for a limited period.

• Starter plan (symbolic monthly price): more sites, longer data retention, and expanded export.

• Pro/Agencies plan: unlimited sites, white-label reports, advanced permissions, priority support.

• Paid self-hosting: annual license for the self-managed version with updates and support.

• Tiered pricing model based on view volume, with an annual discount and a trial period without a card.

5) Global growth plan via free channels

• GitHub + Product Hunt + Hacker News: launch the open-source version to build trust and a developer community.

• Content SEO: guides on "privacy-respecting alternative analytics", educational comparisons, and technical articles.

• Communities: Reddit, developer forums, small business communities, and Mastodon groups.

• A simple referral program that grants free upgrades in exchange for inviting users.

• Ready-made templates for integration with WordPress and popular frameworks to ease adoption.

• Partnerships with agencies through white-label reports that bring multiple clients per account.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Changes in privacy legislation (GDPR/CCPA) require periodic review of the definition of "aggregated measurement" and data retention.

• Risk of misuse for unethical visit counting or inflating numbers; we need clear usage policies.

• Data responsibility in the cloud version: a data processing agreement and transparency in storage and backups.

Technical:

• Tracking blockers (ad-blockers) reduce the accuracy of numbers; we address this with the alternate path and by clarifying the margin of error.

• The accuracy of estimating unique visitors without cookies via a lightweight aggregated fingerprint may be affected by changing networks.

• Scalability as views grow; we rely on batch processing and efficient caching.

• Self-installation requires maintenance by the user; we mitigate this with Docker automation and updates.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Rasd (رَصْد)

Tagline: Your site's numbers in full clarity, without tracking any visitor.
