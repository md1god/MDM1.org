Nabras — Complete Project Plan

Attract your client by writing one post, better than ten random drafts.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, dissemination plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not yet launched.

1) Target audience

• Founder entrepreneurs: those who own startups and need to increase professional visibility to attract clients or investors.

• LinkedIn content creators: such as coaches, consultants, and experts seeking to build their professional audience.

• Professional ghostwriters: those who write on behalf of others and need to produce coherent and fast content.

• Marketing teams in small and medium-sized companies: that do not have a large budget for writing content on LinkedIn.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Focus on the outcome, not just writing: instead of generating a single post, it guides the user to increase effective engagement according to their goal (attracting a client, building trust, etc.).

• Smart context-supported suggestions: analyzing the user's profile and professional activity to suggest topics genuinely relevant to them, not generic ones.

• LinkedIn reading-oriented optimization: automatic adjustment to suggest a strong opening, clear bullet points, and a call to action consistent with user behavior.

• Complete simplicity: a light interface without complex features, aimed at beginners writing on the platform.

• Priority on free cost: most core functions are available for free with a reasonable limit, without mandatory blocking of writing.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Registration and profile: create an account and link a LinkedIn profile or manually enter professional profile data.

2. Goal-based post generator: choose the post goal (attract client / build trust / share expertise) with initial draft generation.

3. Smart editor: improve the title, first paragraph, and tone with one click.

4. Topic suggestions: a list of suggested topics based on the user's field and context.

5. Engagement prompt list: questions or calls to action appropriate for the post.

6. Save drafts: ability to save, edit, and publish later without losing content.

7. Basic analysis: simple indicators such as post length and readability.

8. Free usage mode: activate a daily limit for free users with a clear display of the remaining limit.

4) Pricing and profit model

• Always free to start: a limited number of draft generations per day and a basic editor without payment.

• Monthly subscription: a low-priced package for active users granting a higher limit, unlimited saving, and broader analytics.

• Focus on value for price: a lower price than current alternatives, with clear justification by focusing the product on results, not unlimited features.

• Sustainable model: reliance on direct subscription, without selling user data, and without in-app ads.

5) Global dissemination plan with free channels

• Organic search optimization: writing very short articles on topics such as "How to write an effective LinkedIn post" with natural product links.

• Building a LinkedIn presence: sharing useful real experiences (without excessive direct promotion), focusing on the value of experience before the link.

• Simple email list: a free sample of relevant content attracts subscribers and broadcasts real use cases to them.

• Participation in professional communities: answering relevant questions in entrepreneur and coach communities, offering the solution only when needed, and respecting each community's rules.

• Referral program: a simple reward for a user who invites a friend and uses the product, in a system that integrates with the free version.

• Free tool lists: listing the product in "best free tools for entrepreneurs" lists when conditions are met, with an accurate description focused on the problem and solution.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal:

• Content ownership: ensuring no copying of protected templates or texts, and making all templates and content generated from scratch.

• Data privacy: adhering to the principle of minimum data, clear transparency about data collection and use, and taking into account general protection requirements.

• Third-party interface use: if linking to any future interface, terms and permissions must be verified before activation.

• Technical:

• Generation quality: risk of producing generic or inaccurate content; requires precise prompt tuning and continuous testing with real users.

• Performance measurement: maintaining fast response times with free usage
