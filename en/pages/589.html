Khayt — Complete Project Plan

Turn your interconnected ideas into organized data without losing context.

Complete execution plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market, risks. It is under construction, not yet launched.

1) Target Audience

• Researchers, writers, and students who collect interconnected notes and need to organize them without table constraints.

• Project managers and business analysts building task structures or multilevel taxonomies.

• Small product teams wanting one data source with multiple views (tree, table, board).

• Arab users wanting native RTL and good performance on mid-range devices.

• Those replacing mind maps, tables, and lists with one lightweight offline tool.

2) Differentiators (Why our product)

• Tree and table in one entity: every row expands into a subgrid, every cell links to another node, so an idea and its details stay linked.

• Boundless free canvas: flexible spatial organization with zoom and branch focus, not a rigid grid.

• Defined fields and lightweight formulas: text, number, date, select, status, node reference, with simple calculations, not enterprise complexity.

• Local-first: instant device saving, full offline use, optional later sync.

• Data ownership: an open, documented file format, full export to CSV, Markdown, and JSON, no lock-in.

• Speed and keyboard-first: fast entry and navigation for power users.

• Multiple views of the same data: tree, grid, and status board, without duplication.

• Native Arabic support: RTL, Arabic search, and ready Arabic/English templates.

3) First-version scope (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Tree/row editor with drag and drop, unlimited nesting, and keyboard shortcuts.

2. Expand any cell into a subgrid or link it to an existing node.

3. Basic field types (text, number, date, checkbox, select list, reference) with simple calculation formulas.

4. Three views of the same data: tree, grid, and status board.

5. Instant search and filtering within the tree and fields.

6. Automatic local saving, undo/redo, and restore a previous version.

7. Import/export CSV, Markdown, and JSON in an open, documented format.

8. Ready starter templates + Arabic/English interface + read-only share link.

4) Pricing and profit model

• Free forever: two workspaces, up to 10,000 nodes, local saving, full export, no ads.

• Pro: $5 per month or $40 per year — unlimited workspaces, cloud sync, version history, custom fields, page publishing.

• Team: $8 per user per month — collaboration, permissions, change log, priority support.

• Early offer: lifetime license at a reduced price for the first 500 users.

• Education and non-profits: 50% discount.

• Revenue from subscriptions and team seats; core stays free to attract users and lower the trial barrier.

5) Global go-to-market plan (free channels)

• SEO and practical content: articles and comparisons on hierarchical data, guides, and landing pages for each use case.

• Free template gallery: task, research, content planning, and study templates, one-click copyable.

• Product Hunt and Hacker News: launch an initial version with a "local-first" story and an open file format.

• Productivity communities: useful posts on Reddit, Discord, and forums, without promotional spam.

• YouTube and short lessons: 60-second videos for real tasks, in Arabic and English.

• Building in public: weekly development updates and a published roadmap for early trust.

• Universities and Arab tech communities: free workshops and educational accounts.

• Ambassador program: subscription rewards for bringing active users, with no initial cash payment.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Name or trademark similarity: check trademarks early in target markets and register the name early.

• Design or interface similarity with existing products: original design from scratch and documented decisions.

• Open-source library licenses: review each and comply with attribution and distribution terms.

• Privacy and compliance: clear data policy, GDPR compliance, no selling user data.

Technical:

• Performance with large nodes: early load testing, virtualization, and clear limits.

• Sync conflicts: a clear conflict model and manual resolution when needed.

• Local data loss: automatic backup and easy export.

• Browser and storage compatibility: local storage limits and an export/import alternative.

• Security: transport encryption, permission review, periodic penetration testing.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Khayt

Marketing tagline: Turn your interconnected ideas into organized data without losing context.
