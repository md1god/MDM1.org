Regulyx — Full Project Plan

Classify your systems, generate your evidence, face any audit with confidence.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the launch plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: an AI Act compliance tool

1) Target audience

• Medium and large companies in the EU (50,000+) that build or use AI systems and are preparing for the regulation's enforcement date in August 2026.

• Governance, Risk & Compliance officers in the sectors: finance, healthcare, hiring, automated marketing.

• Product developers building AI-powered features inside companies who need a quick check of their systems' classification before launch.

• Legal consultants and compliance firms serving multiple clients who want to automate file preparation.

2) Differentiators

• An AI-powered classification path that asks questions in plain language and then determines the risk category (prohibited/high/limited/minimal) with the reasoning behind each decision — instead of reading long legal texts.

• Automatic generation of a document package (risk management system, training data record, conformity assessment) in a format ready for upload and audit.

• Audit-ready evidence dashboard that tracks every change and version with a timestamp, so compliance verification is a moment, not a project.

• Continuous periodic checks: alerts when system data changes or new rulings are issued, rather than a one-time assessment.

• Free-to-start model: free instant classification check, then a subscription for documents and automation.

3) Scope of the first version (8 tasks maximum)

1. A guided multi-step form that asks about the system type, domain, and use.

2. A rules engine that classifies the risk and shows the rationale.

3. Generation of an exportable "System Card" document (PDF/Markdown).

4. A checklist of relevant materials by risk category.

5. Upload and linking of evidence (policies, test reports) to tasks.

6. A simple timestamped audit log per project.

7. A single dashboard for projects with the compliance status of each system.

8. Pricing plan and payment gateway (Freemium → Pro).

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: unlimited classification + one summary report.

• Pro — $99/month: 10 systems, full document generation, audit log.

• Business — $399/month: team seats, API integration, periodic checks and alerts.

• Additional revenue: partner consulting packages (Legal/Consulting) with a referral commission.

5) Global launch plan using free channels

1. A Free Scanner tool as a viral landing page: "Classify your system in 60 seconds", shareable.

2. Proactive SEO content: comparison articles on regulation materials and compliance paths in English, German, and French.

3. LinkedIn and X: short threads explaining common classification mistakes ahead of the August 2026 deadline.

4. Free templates on GitHub / Notion: a ready-made risk management system template in exchange for an email address.

5. Communities and groups: participation in AI Governance forums and local meetups.

6. Early partnerships: free trials with 10–20 pilot companies in exchange for case studies.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: the risk of providing advice that implies guaranteed compliance → an explicit disclaimer that the tool is "rule-powered and not a substitute for a qualified lawyer".

• Legal: changes to the regulation's details and individual rulings → quarterly rule reviews and a documented official source for every claim.

• Technical: classification accuracy errors → a mandatory "human-in-the-loop" pattern for high-risk classifications with human review.

• Technical: sensitivity of systems' and clients' data → encryption in transit and at rest, EU-only data paths, and no model training on user data.

• Market: saturation with free tools → differentiation through document quality and the audit experience, not price alone.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Regulyx

Tagline: Classify your systems, generate your evidence, face any audit with confidence.
