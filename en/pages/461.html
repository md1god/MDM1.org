RipplePost — A Complete Project Plan

One post… its ripple reaches every platform of yours.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the distribution plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: Multi-platform publishing for freelancers and solo marketers

1) Target audience

• The solo marketer who runs small brand accounts or their own personal accounts.

• The independent content creator (YouTuber, LinkedIn writer, Reels/TikTok maker) who wants faster reach with less effort.

• Consultants, coaches, and owners of small digital service businesses who need a consistent presence on several platforms.

• Startups and freelancers without a social media team and without an enterprise tools budget.

• Arabic-speaking creators in the Middle East and North Africa and the segment of expatriates seeking an easy Arabic interface.

2) Points of differentiation (why choose our product)

• The simplest possible dashboard: full focus on "one post → many platforms" without the complexity of team and enterprise workflows.

• Smart preview for each platform: text truncation, hashtag display, and image/video sizing right before publishing.

• A custom editor per network: an automatically tailored version for each platform's post length and tone.

• Visual content calendar and ready-made templates for recurring campaigns (ads, stories, updates).

• Arabic first: a complete RTL interface and support for writing posts in Arabic with templates suited to each platform.

• Free to start immediately with no credit card, with clear limits that are easy to exceed by upgrading.

• Simplified status tracking: success/failure per platform and suggested retry time.

3) Scope of the first version (max 8 tasks)

1. Log in and create a free account (email + OAuth).

2. Connect the first batch of platforms via official OAuth (for example: X, LinkedIn, Threads, Bluesky).

3. Single-post editor: text + image/video + hashtags.

4. Instant publishing to one platform or several platforms together.

5. Scheduling with a single unified time plus a list of upcoming posts.

6. Per-platform preview with automatic text truncation/editing.

7. Publishing status board (succeeded, failed, pending) with retry.

8. Account, subscription, and simple usage-limits dashboard.

4) Pricing and revenue model

· Plan · Approx. price · What you get ·

· Free · $0 · Up to 5 scheduled posts per month, two connected platforms ·

· Creator · ≈ $12–15/month · Nearly unlimited posts, all supported platforms, calendar, smart preview ·

· Studio · ≈ $29/month · Two brands/spaces, performance reports, priority support ·

• Main revenue: monthly/annual subscriptions (about 20% discount annually).

• No initial advertising budget: organic growth + referrals (one free month for every friend who purchases).

• We do not sell user data and do not serve ads inside the tool.

5) Global distribution plan through free channels

1. X and Threads: a short daily series on content creation and multi-platform publishing, with before/after examples.

2. LinkedIn: short weekly articles + case studies of small content creators (Arabic/English).

3. YouTube Shorts and TikTok: 30–60 second explainers: "Link your accounts and publish in one place".

4. Blog and SEO: general comparison pages (for example: best unified publishing tools for independent creators).

5. Reddit, Discord, and Telegram communities: sharing useful tips (without spamming) and collecting user feedback.

6. Product Hunt + free tool directories (launch list).

7. Arabic groups for content creators and freelancers: short free workshops and early beta offers.

8. Small influencer program: swapping a free trial for honest mentions and shared case studies.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Relying only on official interfaces/APIs; any change in platform terms may halt or limit features (especially Instagram/TikTok/YouTube).

• Respect each platform's policy on automated publishing and daily post limits to avoid account bans.

• Content, image, and trademark rights for users; the user is responsible for what they publish.

• Privacy policies and data protection when linking accounts and caching content.

Technical:

• Sudden changes in OAuth or app permissions on the platforms.

• Differences in post restrictions (text length, file types, hashtags) between platforms.
