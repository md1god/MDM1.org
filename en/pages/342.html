مَبْصَرة — Complete Project Plan

Your whole platform on one screen, at lower cost and faster.

Full execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

A new product plan in the field of software Observability

1) Target audience

• Software engineering teams (DevOps / SRE) in small and medium companies that do not want the expensive cost of observability tools.

• Startups looking for a production-ready solution that starts free and grows with them.

• Independent developers and high-traffic product teams that need to monitor logs, metrics, and request tracing.

• AI teams that need to monitor LLM model calls (indicators, errors, cost).

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Very low storage cost: smart compression and indexing reduce cost by up to 10× compared to traditional tools.

• Simple deployment: run in a single container or a single server, with the option to scale later without rewriting.

• Open standards compliant: direct OpenTelemetry support without installing locked agents (Vendor Lock-in).

• Instant log search through a fast web interface, with built-in Dashboards and Alerts.

• AI-powered: summarizing incidents and errors and suggesting the Root Cause in natural language.

• Full Arabic interface with right-to-left support, which is a clear gap in the current market.

3) Scope of the first release (8 tasks maximum)

1. Ingesting Logs over HTTP/gRPC and OpenTelemetry-compatible interfaces.

2. Storing logs with compression and text indexing and fast Full-text search.

3. Ingesting Metrics and displaying them on dashboards with timelines.

4. Distributed Tracing to show the request path across services.

5. Alerting system with simple rules + notifications via email and Webhook.

6. One web interface: search, dashboards, alerts, and source management.

7. Basic permissions system (users, API keys, multi-tenancy).

8. Easy deployment: a single Docker image + a Compose config file + quick documentation.

4) Pricing and the revenue model

• Free to start: up to 1 GB logs/day + 7 days retention + 2 users, no credit card.

• Team plan: Usage-based pricing per gigabyte stored/consumed monthly, starting at a nominal amount.

• Enterprise plan: Self-hosted (with a commercial license), SLA service level guarantees, and priority support.

• Add-on flows: LLM monitoring and long retention options as optional paid add-ons.

• Core revenue: paid consumption + commercial extension for self-hosting.

5) Global rollout plan using free channels

• GitHub: an open repository with an excellent README, screenshots, and good first issues to attract contributors.

• SEO and blog: practical technical comparison articles ("How to reduce observability cost...") in multiple languages.

• Developer communities: Reddit, Hacker News, Dev.to, and Discord/Telegram groups, plus Arabic communities (Ummah-forum, Arabic DevOps groups).

• Product Hunt and HN Show: official launch with a live demo.

• YouTube: short videos "From zero to first alert in 5 minutes" in Arabic and English.

• Partnerships: open templates, academies, and free Cloud Credits programs.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: comply with the license of the open-source repository we build on (its terms must be read before publishing); avoid infringing registered trademarks or domain names; comply with data protection laws (GDPR/SOC2) when hosting logs that may contain personal data.

• Technical: cardinality explosion in metrics may raise cost; maintaining search speed when data volume doubles; keeping up with OpenTelemetry developments; competition from well-established free tools.

• Commercial: difficulty converting free users into paying ones, and the need for clear differentiation from current alternatives.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: مَبْصَرة

Tagline: Your whole platform on one screen, at lower cost and faster.
