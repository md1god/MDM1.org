Nabra — Full Project Plan

Know your audience's opinion clearly… in minutes, not weeks.

Complete plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout, and risks. Not yet launched.

Product plan for "Nabra" — a platform for surveys and smart forms

1) Target audience

· Segment · Core need ·

· Researchers and academic teams · Reliable data with fast statistical analysis ·

· Small and medium-sized businesses (SMEs) · Customer satisfaction and employee experience with simple tools ·

· Marketers and product managers · Idea testing (MVP) and NPS/CES surveys ·

· Schools and non-profit associations · Free, easy, multilingual surveys ·

· Freelancers and content creators · Signup forms, waitlists, fast publishing ·

First geographic focus: Arabic-speaking world + English-speaking emerging markets.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

1. AI-powered build: write the survey from a one-sentence description, then get suggested questions and validation checks.

2. Smart no-code logic engine: advanced conditional branching (goto/quotas/pipe) via a simple visual interface.

3. Speed and lightness: responsive interface loading in one second, even on slow networks in emerging regions.

4. Arabic-first: full RTL interface, ready-made Arabic templates, bilingual analytics.

5. Built-in analytics: automatic impact summary (Insights Summary) instead of raw data tables.

6. A real free plan: permanent free version with no expiry, upgrade only when needed.

7. Privacy by design: multi-region hosting, explicit consent, and one-click permanent deletion.

3) Scope of the first release (8 tasks maximum)

1. Accounts system + basic dashboard (signup, team invites, roles).

2. Survey editor: question types (choice, multiple, Likert scale, visual scale, text, file, matrix) + ordering and reuse.

3. Conditional logic engine + conditional sections + quota-based pricing tiers (quotas).

4. Publishing: direct link, embed, and QR code.

5. Response collection: autosave, duplicate prevention, mobile-friendly and RTL-compliant.

6. Analytics: tables, charts, CSV/Excel export, and an early smart summary.

7. Ready-made templates + an AI question-writing assistant.

8. Landing pages + pricing pages + short docs + initial payment support (subscriptions).

4) Pricing and revenue model

· Plan · Monthly price · Limits ·

· Free · $0 forever · 3 active surveys, 100 responses/month, Nabra watermark, basic analytics ·

· Pro · $19/month · Unlimited surveys, 5,000 responses/month, branding removal, advanced logic and export ·

· Business · $49/month · Teams, SSO, regional hosting, higher limits, priority support ·

· Usage-based · As needed · Additional hosting, custom research, API packages ·

Extra revenue: paid template marketplace, commission on summary research (Option), university/non-profit plans at 50–80% off.

5) Global rollout plan using free channels

1. Arabic/English SEO: template pages per use case (employee satisfaction, NPS, event registration) with useful content.

2. Communities and forums: helpful posts on Reddit, Quora, LinkedIn, and university forums, no direct ads.

3. LinkedIn: a weekly content series (survey design tips) + short case studies.

4. University and open-community partnerships: a free educational plan giving students discounted hosting.

5. Product Hunt / launch sites: a prepared official launch with pre-launch content.

6. Open content: PDF guides and copy-paste templates published free to generate traffic and links.

7. Referral program: free credit for both the user and the host.

8. Site and product translation: 6–8 core languages for the widest geographic reach.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Compliance with data protection laws (GDPR and regional equivalents): consent, purpose, deletion rights, processing agreements.

• Potential regulations on survey content (privacy, elections, employment) in some countries.

• Ownership of user content and mutual licensing — must be clearly documented.

• AI assistant safety: avoiding biased questions or non-compliant probing.

Technical:

• Stability of survey collection at peak times and anti-manipulation/bot defenses.

• Quality of AI analysis and covering API costs without collapsing margins.

• Platform uptime, backups, and data integrity.

• Closing XSS/CSRF holes in the embed editor and open-ended questions.

• Reliance on payment/email providers that some regions may block.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Nabra

Tagline: Know your audience's opinion clearly… in minutes, not weeks.
