Mashhad Studio — Complete Project Plan

A lightweight, fast non-linear video engine that turns your idea into a ready scene in minutes, and starts with you for free.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, launch plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: an original non-linear video editing engine

1) Target audience

• Individual content creators (YouTube, Reels, TikTok) who need professional tools without complexity or expensive subscriptions.

• Students and editing beginners who want a free start and a simple interface.

• Small teams and marketing agencies that need fast collaborative editing on mid-range devices.

• Developers and educational institutions interested in open-source, extensible tools.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• High performance and low memory footprint thanks to a Rust core and GPU processing, so it runs on weak devices.

• Focus on simplicity: non-linear editing with a fast learning curve instead of competitors crowded with features.

• Instant collaboration by sharing small project files instead of bloated data.

• A free-to-start model with full export and an optional light watermark, removing the entry barrier.

• An open Plugins architecture that lets the community extend the tools.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. A non-linear timeline with cut/split/drag and multi-track ordering.

2. An import library for video, audio, and image clips with fast preview.

3. Basic transitions and color processing effects (correction and Look).

4. Multiple audio tracks with level mixing and fading.

5. Customizable text overlays and titles.

6. Export at multiple qualities and efficient encoding for YouTube and vertical platforms.

7. Auto-save and multi-project management.

8. An Arabic/English interface with customizable keyboard shortcuts.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free to start: all core features with no time limit, with an optional watermark and a 1080p export limit.

• Pro plan (monthly/yearly): watermark removal, 4K export, extra effects and template library.

• Team license: shared collaborative seats and an admin dashboard.

• Paid support and add-ons: an add-on marketplace with revenue sharing with developers.

• Continuing to keep the core open source to attract developers and build trust.

5) Global launch plan through free channels

• Publishing the open-source repository on GitHub with documentation and community contributions.

• An educational YouTube channel: short tutorials in multiple languages for search engine optimization.

• Discord, Reddit, Hacker News, and Product Hunt communities for a beta launch.

• TikTok/Reels content showing "from idea to export in a minute".

• Partnerships with educational content creators and editing instructors for a free trial.

• Writing technical comparisons and performance studies that attract a technical audience.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: licensing mistakes in dependency libraries, trademark conflicts, and rights to assets and demo video — addressed with strict license review and use of licensed assets.

• Technical: the complexity of video encoding across multiple platforms, graphics driver compatibility, and the performance of large projects — addressed with clear testing standards and continuous performance measurement.

• Adoption: the risk of weak adoption against free competitors — countered by building a strong community and add-ons.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Mashhad Studio

Tagline: A lightweight, fast non-linear video engine that turns your idea into a ready scene in minutes, and starts with you for free.
