Athar — Complete Project Plan

Smart agents that run on your device, with a documented trace for every step.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: Athar

1) Target audience

• Individual developers and small teams building smart agents on their own devices.

• Privacy-sensitive organizations (health, legal, finance, public sector) that do not allow data to be sent to the cloud.

• Researchers who need reproducible and auditable results.

• Users working offline or on edge devices, and owners of consumer graphics cards.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Guaranteed reproducibility: pin the model version and quantization precisely, with a verification fingerprint, so behavior does not change under your feet.

• Tamper-evident trace: a chained cryptographic record of every step and tool, exportable and auditable.

• Tool firewall: allow/deny policies for paths, domains, and commands, with human approval when needed.

• Automatic hardware calibration: automatically selects the appropriate model and quantization based on memory and speed.

• Privacy by design: no mandatory account, no remote telemetry by default, and works offline.

• Open orchestration: simple text-based run files, no lock-in, and extensible with third-party tools.

• Arabic-first experience: bilingual interface, logs, and documentation from day one.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Local installer for macOS and Linux (Windows later) with hardware check and model recommendation.

2. Model manager: download, fingerprint verification, pinning the model version and quantization, and caching for offline work.

3. Agent builder with a simple YAML file, with three built-in tools: files inside a sandbox, shell commands, and HTTP with an allowlist.

4. Policy engine for tool invocation: allow/deny, path and domain lists, and human approval requests.

5. Hash-chain trace log for every run, exportable as a standalone package.

6. Replay and comparison: run the same trace with the pinned model and compare outputs.

7. Local RAG over the user's folder, with locally running embeddings generation.

8. Local web interface + CLI, one-command run, bilingual.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free forever: the local core is open source (Apache 2.0), with no limit on runs or tokens.

• Pro: $12 per month or $99 per year per user — encrypted trace sync, shared policy packs, audit reports.

• Enterprise: custom pricing — self-hosted dashboard, SSO, priority support, and compliance reports.

• Revenue: team subscriptions, compliance, and support, not margin on tokens or calls.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• GitHub Releases with weekly early releases and a clear contribution guide.

• Hugging Face: compatible model cards and demo spaces.

• Developer communities (such as local-model communities) with a "value first" approach: guides and comparisons, not ads.

• Discord/Matrix, Show HN, Product Hunt, and dev.to.

• Short YouTube video series: installation, security, and reproducing results.

• Bilingual SEO documentation, university workshops, and talks at open conferences.

• An open benchmark dashboard that attracts contributors and transparent comparisons.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: open-model licenses may prohibit commercial use or require attribution; data-protection laws vary between countries; liability for agent actions; the need to review trademarks and avoid claiming compliance without an audit.

• Technical: hardware and quantization diversity threatens result consistency; possible sandbox escape; supply-chain risks in models and tools; the maintenance burden of model compatibility; the temptation to add cloud features that break the privacy promise.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Athar (أثر)

Tagline: Smart agents that run on your device, with a documented trace for every step.
