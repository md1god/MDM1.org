لَحْظة — Complete Project Plan

Analyze every moment… without tracking anyone.

Full implementation plan for a new digital product: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the rollout plan, and the risks. The product is in development and has not launched yet.

Product plan: privacy-friendly web analytics with session replay

1) Target audience

• SaaS companies, e-commerce stores, and mid-sized content sites, especially in Europe or with global customers (worried about data-protection fines).

• Freelance developers and small product teams that want tools instead of complicated GA4.

• Digital agencies managing dozens of sites and needing a single dashboard.

• Regulated sectors: healthcare, finance, education — where setting cookies before consent is forbidden.

• Sites facing visitors who reject cookies or use ad blockers.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• No cookies at all: an alternating identifier in localStorage + session estimation without a persistent fingerprint → no consent banner, no tracking disclosure.

• One tool instead of five: visits and sources + Journeys + Funnels + error tracking + session replay.

• Privacy by design: automatic masking of passwords, card numbers and sensitive fields, canvas blocking, with a server-side anonymisation option.

• Open Core: a free self-hosted version via Docker and a paid managed one — full code transparency.

• Lightweight: a single < 3KB script, does not slow the page or block rendering (asynchronous).

• Arabic and English dashboard, plus a weekly email report with no setup.

3) First release scope (8 tasks maximum)

1. Write a lightweight JavaScript SDK that collects events without cookies and respects Do Not Track.

2. Core analytics dashboard: live visits, traffic sources, pages, devices, countries.

3. Build Funnels and Journeys with a simple drag-and-drop interface.

4. Error tracking: JavaScript errors, console errors, failed network requests, with breadcrumbs.

5. Session recording and replay with a privacy mask for all inputs and compressed recording (gzip + DOM codes instead of video).

6. Easy deployment: docker compose up for self-hosting + an instant free cloud account.

7. Workspaces, share links, and weekly email reports.

8. Documentation + landing page + onboarding flow that finishes setup in 5 minutes.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: up to 100 thousand events/month, one project, 7-day replay, unlimited self-hosting (Open Core).

• Pro — 29$ per month: one million events, 3 projects, 90-day replay, funnel and journey templates, API integrations.

• Scale — 99$ per month: 10 million events, unlimited projects, SSO, SLA, EU local infrastructure.

• Revenue: cloud subscriptions plus support and training for the self-hosted enterprise edition, with free → paid growth (PLG).

5) Global rollout plan using free channels

• Comparative SEO content: "best alternative analytics tools" and "the cookie-free alternative" pages in English and Arabic.

• Launch Open Core on GitHub with good-first-issue for the community, then Show HN and Reddit (r/analytics, r/privacy) and developer communities.

• Product Hunt and Hacker News and template repositories on GitHub.

• Arabic channels: tech forums, Telegram/Discord groups, data-protection blogs.

• Short YouTube videos/tutorials: "connect your site in 5 minutes" and "delete GA4 and install the tool".

• Open integrations (Next.js, WordPress, Shopify, Laravel) spread automatically.

• Free newsletters + an ambassador program for freelancers and agencies.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: strict interpretation of GDPR/ePrivacy/CCPA for anything deemed a "fingerprint" or persistent identifier; minimizing IP address storage, defining retention periods, DPIA procedures, and hosting inside the EU.

• Legal: session recording liability — personal data may be recorded unintentionally → mandatory default masking and redaction before recording.

• Technical: accuracy of sessions and replayed sessions without cookies (bots, multiple devices) and within ad-blocker constraints.

• Technical: storage cost and replay of complex SPA sessions, and script performance on slow sites.

• Competitive: an open-source community may be copied → winning on quality, documentation, and speed, not features alone.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: لَحْظة

Tagline: Analyze every moment… without tracking anyone.
