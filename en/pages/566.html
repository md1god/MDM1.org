Wajiz — Complete Project Plan

Test your product before your first customer.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, growth plan, and risks. The project is under development and has not yet been launched.

1) Target Audience

• Solo entrepreneurs: Those building a product for the first time and do not have a budget for user testing.

• UI/UX designers: Those who want to validate their flow before handing it over to the development team.

• Small product teams: Those who need quick, actionable feedback before launch.

2) Competitive Advantages (Why Choose Our Product)

• Solution-oriented: It does not just display feedback, but suggests a direct fix and a priority level for each issue.

• Privacy-first: It does not require real customer data to run the first test, and only works with objectives and general behavior.

• Completely free to start: Allows actual testing without requiring a credit card.

• Super fast: Produces a short, ready-to-use report within minutes, focused on decision-making rather than lengthy reading.

• Extremely simple: Just upload a link or a screenshot, with no complex setup required.

3) Scope of the First Version (Max 8 Tasks)

• [ ] Original visual identity: Create a new, completely original design.

• [ ] Input upload: Allow uploading a page link or a screenshot of the product to be tested.

• [ ] Virtual tester: Generate a tester based on the product's purpose and target audience.

• [ ] Behavior simulation: Simulate clicks, navigation, and internal thinking step by step.

• [ ] Friction point detection: Extract real obstacles with their exact location.

• [ ] Fix suggestions: Generate 1-3 actionable suggestions for each friction point.

• [ ] Free credit system: Enable a limited number of free tests per account.

• [ ] Shareable report: Generate a short, text-based report that can be copied or shared via a link.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free — $0 — 3 tests per month, full report, no credit card required.

• Growth — $9 / month — 30 tests per month, unlimited report saving, priority processing.

• Small Team — $24 / month — 80 tests per month, share reports within the team, priority support.

Revenue model: Monthly subscriptions, while keeping the free plan as the main acquisition gateway.

5) Global Growth Plan with Free Channels

• SEO — Write an article "How to test your product before launch in free steps" — Attract long-term organic traffic globally.

• Indie Hackers — Participate in real discussions by presenting the problem and solution — Reach solo entrepreneurs.

• Dev.to — Publish a short article about reducing the cost of idea validation — Reach technical product builders.

• Reddit — Contribute to relevant startup communities without spamming — Build trust and attract interested visitors.

• Build in Public — Share weekly progress statistics on X — Organic growth at no cost.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal — Misleading claim about a "real user" — Explicitly use the term "virtual tester" and clarify that it is only a simulation.

• Legal — Privacy of uploaded content — Do not store screenshots or links longer than necessary, with a clear data policy.

• Technical — AI hallucinations leading to illogical suggestions — Restrict context to only the uploaded page and force suggestions to be based on observable points.

• Technical — High API call costs — Strict credit system for the free plan and minimize query size to the minimum required.

7) Suggested Name and Marketing Slogan

Suggested name: Wajiz

Marketing slogan: Test your product before your first customer.
