Flotilla — Complete Project Plan

Accomplish hundreds of Google Workspace operations in a few clicks, with preview and undo before execution.

Complete execution plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under construction and not yet launched.

1) Target Audience

• Independent professionals and large library owners: photographers, content creators, researchers, lawyers, accountants; they have 10,000+ emails in Gmail, 100+ GB in Drive, thousands of photos. They need periodic cleanup, export, and archiving.

• Virtual assistants and operations managers: execute daily tasks like downloading client attachments, uploading to Drive, organizing folders. They are the fastest segment ready to pay and can bring multiple clients with them.

• Small teams and agencies (2–20 people): need shared bulk operations and audit trails, but don't want the complexity of large enterprise tools.

• Secondary segment: Managed IT Service Providers (MSPs) for auditing public links and cleaning client accounts.

• Out of scope: Large enterprises with Google Vault or DLP; we leave them for a later phase.

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• One product instead of three scattered add-ons: one engine and one interface for Gmail, Drive, and Photos, with a single subscription.

• Recipes Engine: Savable, shareable recipes, e.g., "Save vendor invoice attachments from the invoices label to Drive/2026/Vendor". Works with one click or on a schedule.

• Security before speed: Dry-run preview before any change, typed confirmation for destructive operations, move to trash by default, and undo log.

• API-first, not DOM: We use official Google APIs, no UI simulation. Result: higher reliability, compliance with Google policies, stable operation when UI changes.

• Local-first privacy: Process files on the user's device where possible, no storage of email content or filenames on our servers.

• Fresh, fast design: Clean Side Panel, three-step Recipe Builder, command palette, full Arabic and RTL support.

• Transparent pricing: Generous free tier, no sales call, cancel anytime.

• Built from scratch: No legacy code, no wrapper over another product; everything built around the "Select → Preview → Execute" experience.

3) MVP Scope (8 Tasks Maximum)

Every task works in resumable batches, includes preview before execution, and undo log where Google API allows.

1. Save Gmail attachments to Drive: Filter by sender/label/date/extension/size, deduplicate, auto-create folders, save CSV index.

2. Download Gmail attachments locally: Bundle into a single ZIP with organized names, auto-split archive if it grows too large.

3. Bulk Gmail operations: Archive, add/remove labels, mark read/unread, move to trash; on defined search results.

4. Export Drive folder: ZIP preserving folder tree, convert Google Docs/Sheets/Slides to PDF/DOCX/XLSX, option to include shared files.

5. Rename/Move/Copy Drive files: Find & Replace rules, prefixes/suffixes, auto-numbering, dates, extension replacement.

6. Sharing audit: Show all public or link-shared files, restrict in bulk, remove external permissions, export CSV report.

7. Drive cleanup: Detect duplicates via md5Checksum and empty/orphan files, move to trash after preview.

8. Export Google Photos: Export user-selected items or albums via Picker API to Drive or ZIP, preserving date and description.

Intentional exclusion: No bulk delete for Google Photos in v1; API doesn't allow it, and UI simulation violates terms. We focus on safe export, re-evaluate if Google opens broader permissions.

4) Pricing & Revenue Model

Operation = a file, email, or image processed.

| Plan | Price | Limits & Benefits |

|------|-------|-------------------|

| Free | $0 | 150 ops/month, 3 saved recipes, full preview, 7-day undo, email support. |

| Pro | $6/mo or $49/yr | 5,000 ops/month (Fair Use), unlimited recipes, scheduling, 30-day undo, CSV reports, priority support. |

| Team | $4/user/mo (3+ seats) | Shared recipe library, audit log, sharing policies, billing management. Launches after v1. |

| Launch | $99 one-time (first 200 users) | Pro lifetime with 5,000 ops/month cap. |

• Payments: Stripe on web + license inside extension via custom license server. No Chrome Web Store payments.

• Purchase trigger: One hour of VA time costs more than the annual subscription, and the product reduces risk of accidental deletion or sharing.

• Principles: No data selling, no ads, no use of user content for training.

• Initial goal: Convert 3–5% of Free to Pro within 30 days, with Team growth later.
