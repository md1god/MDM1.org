DeltaBrief — Complete Project Plan

Know when your competitors change prices before your customers notice.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target audience

Founders of SaaS companies at the $500K–$10M ARR stage, and pricing, RevOps, and Product Marketing managers in small teams (5–50 employees). They do not have time to monitor competitor pages manually, but they make frequent pricing decisions and need early, reliable signals. The first entry point: companies that sell on a monthly/annual subscription model and rely on public pricing pages.

2) Differentiation (why choose our product)

• Structured pricing understanding, not just DOM diffs: extracting plans, prices, currencies, billing cycles, limits, and features into comparable entities.

• Noise reduction: distinguishing real change from A/B tests, temporary offers, or currency/region differences, with a confidence score for each change.

• Revenue impact estimation: linking change to the customer’s plans and ARR scenarios instead of settling for a text description.

• Practical weekly email + instant alerts: a clear-language summary, with a link to the source and a snapshot of the change.

• Free start with no credit card: value within minutes without technical setup or an agent.

• Transparency and compliance: public pages only, respecting site restrictions, and an audit log for every conclusion.

3) First version scope (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Self-serve signup and adding up to 3 competitor pricing pages with validation.

2. Scheduled crawling via Headless Browser with JavaScript support and storage of timestamped snapshots.

3. Structured extraction of plans, prices, features, currency, and billing cycle.

4. Change detection with noise suppression (A/B, geo-currency, temporary offers) and a confidence score.

5. LLM-generated summary in plain language + preliminary revenue impact estimate.

6. Sending a weekly email and an instant alert on high-confidence change.

7. A web dashboard showing the timeline, diffs, and CSV/PDF export.

8. Account management, free limits, basic billing, and usage analytics.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Plan · Price · Limits

• Free · $0 · 3 competitors, weekly summary, one user

• Starter · $29/month · 10 competitors, daily checks, Slack, 3 users

• Growth · $79/month · 30 competitors, hourly checks, API and export, 10 users

• Agency / Enterprise · Custom pricing · Unlimited competitors, SSO, white-label reports

Revenue comes from recurring subscriptions, with a 20% annual discount and usage add-ons for extra competitors. No credit card for the free plan, and clear limits prevent abuse.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Programmatic SEO: comparison pages and “How your competitor changed its pricing” guides in Arabic and English.

• Attractive free tool: scan a pricing page and provide an instant report that demonstrates the product’s value.

• Community launch: Product Hunt, Hacker News (Show HN), Indie Hackers, SaaS and RevOps communities.

• Building in public: publishing anonymized pricing changes weekly on LinkedIn and X to attract founders.

• Partnerships: accelerators, SaaS founder communities, and niche newsletters in exchange for free content.

• Referral loop: a share link for the change summary with a free invite for an extra competitor.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal: website terms of use, database rights, GDPR/CCPA, and risks of inaccurate claims about competitors. Mitigation: public pages only, respecting robots.txt and rate limits, no bypassing login or protection, minimal storage, human review for sensitive alerts, a disclaimer, and an objection/correction path.

Technical: anti-scraping, dynamic pages, currency and region variance, A/B tests, false positives, and LLM hallucination or cost. Mitigation: crawling from multiple regions, deterministic extraction then LLM for summarization only, confidence thresholds, a periodic evaluation set, caching, and cost caps.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: DeltaBrief

Tagline: Know when your competitors change prices before your customers notice.
