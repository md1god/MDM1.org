Nizam — Complete Project Plan

Your clinic, more organized, with less effort.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, business model, outreach plan, and risks. The project is under development and has not been launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Small clinics: especially those run by a single doctor or a small team.

• Rural medical centers: those suffering from weak internet or limited budgets.

• Pharmacies linked to clinics: those that need a fast connection between prescriptions and inventory.

2) Points of Distinction (Why choose our product)

• Free to start: all features of the first version are completely free with no mandatory fees.

• Mobile-friendly: a lightweight interface that runs smoothly on low-end devices and slow networks.

• Focus on speed: most tasks can be completed in a single step to avoid complexity.

• Ready to work offline: "Offline-first" architecture allows work during network outages with synchronization later.

• Privacy first: local storage is prioritized with encryption for sensitive data.

3) Scope of the First Version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Patient registration: add basic data with an automatic ID number.

2. Visit records: save each patient's visit with its date and notes.

3. Prescription module: create a quick prescription and link it directly to the patient.

4. Inventory management: track medicines, quantity, and expiry dates.

5. Link prescription to inventory: automatically decrease quantity when dispensing medicine.

6. Permission system: assign clear roles to users within the clinic.

7. Simplified invoices: issue a quick invoice without complex accounting.

8. Local backup: easily export a manual backup.

4) Pricing and Profit Model

· Plan · Price · Description ·

· Free · 0$ · Includes all features of the first version, for a single clinic. ·

· Professional · 9$ / month · Advanced reports, automatic backup, and priority support. ·

· Enterprise · 29$ / month · For multiple branches with centralized settings. ·

Profit model: only optional subscriptions, with no selling or sharing of user data.

5) Global Outreach Plan with Free Channels

• Free landing page: with a simple translation to Arabic and English for easy understanding.

• Health initiative directories: apply to free directories for non-profit clinics worldwide.

• Health volunteer groups: participate in communities that support rural medical centers.

• Search engine optimization: focus on local terms such as "free clinic system" in each region.

• Word of mouth: build a reputation based on ease of use to drive organic growth.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

· Type · Risk · Proposed Solution ·

· Legal · Different patient data protection laws between countries. · Do not collect unnecessary data, and support local storage as the default option. ·

· Legal · Confusing the system with an "official record" or local licensing requirements. · Clearly state that it is an organizational aid tool, not a substitute for official requirements. ·

· Technical · Frequent weak or interrupted internet connections. · Rely on "Offline-first" architecture with smart synchronization when connection returns. ·

· Technical · Risk of unauthorized access. · Apply precise permissions, local encryption, and simple, regular security updates. ·

7) Suggested Name and Marketing Slogan

Suggested name: Nizam

Marketing slogan: Your clinic, more organized, with less effort.
