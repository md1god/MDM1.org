Zafira — Complete Project Plan

Design your compliant forms securely and easily, and get started for free.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: Idea, audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

1) Target Audience

The product focuses on small psychiatric and mental health clinics, specifically:

• Private clinics with a limited number of doctors.

• Psychological counseling and behavioral therapy centers.

• Independent practitioners who need to comply with patient data protection regulations without hiring a technical team.

2) Competitive Advantages (Why Choose Our Product)

• Compliance by default: Automatically generates consent and assessment forms in line with patient data protection requirements, without complex setup.

• AI-powered: Allows users to write a simple description of the form, and AI generates it with the necessary fields and secure storage mechanisms.

• Extremely simple: Designed specifically for non-technical users, with the fewest steps possible to launch a working form.

• Built-in audit log: Every action is automatically recorded with timestamp and actor, eliminating the need for separate tools.

• Free-to-start model: Allows small clinics to use it for free, unlike general-purpose tools that become expensive when compliance features are enabled.

• Narrow focus: Saves time by focusing solely on this sector, rather than trying to support all regulated industries.

3) Scope of First Release (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. Secure authentication: Login system with two-factor authentication and user management.

2. AI form builder: Convert text description into a ready-to-use form with appropriate fields.

3. Ready-made templates: Basic templates for patient consent, assessment form, and consultation form.

4. Simple electronic signature: Secure signature linked to the form and user.

5. Complete audit log: Automatic recording of every creation, modification, view, and signature.

6. Role-based permissions: Separation between "clinic manager", "specialist", and "reviewer".

7. Secure data storage: Encryption in transit and at rest, with clear classification of sensitive data.

8. Simple dashboard: Display submissions, signature status, and latest clinic activity.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free-to-start: Up to a limited number of active submissions per month, with audit log and electronic signature enabled.

• Mid-tier plan: Fixed monthly fee based on the number of specialists in the clinic, with increased submission limit.

• Enterprise plan: Discounted annual fee for multi-branch clinics, with priority support.

• Revenue model: Monthly/annual subscriptions only, no per-file fees.

5) Global Distribution Plan via Free Channels

• SEO: Target phrases like "secure consent form for psychiatric clinic" in languages relevant to target markets.

• Shareable open templates: Publish compliance templates for free with a link to the product to increase organic visibility.

• Specialized communities: Actively participate in mental health professional forums by offering practical solutions without excessive direct promotion.

• AI tools directories: List the product in free directories dedicated to AI tools for small businesses.

• Educational content: Publish short articles that simplify compliance requirements for non-lawyers, driving natural traffic.

• Short-form video marketing: Explain real-life use cases in the local language of each target market.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

Legal:

• Regulatory differences: Data protection requirements vary between countries, so focus first on one market before gradual expansion.

• Compliance interpretation: Avoid giving explicit legal advice; limit to providing clear, sound technical mechanisms.

• Data privacy: Must adhere to the principle of data minimization—collect only what is necessary.

Technical:

• Sensitive data security: Risk of health data breaches; addressed through comprehensive encryption, regular security audits, and restricted access.

• AI accuracy: Risk of generating unnecessary fields; enforce human review before saving or publishing.

• Scalability: As the number of clinics grows, separate storage from business logic from the start to avoid costly rebuilds.

• Electronic signature reliability: Must ensure the integrity of the proof chain and audit log to prevent tampering.

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Tagline

Proposed name: Zafira

Marketing tagline: Design your compliant forms securely and easily, and get started for free.
