Vidmira — Complete Project Plan

From a single product photo to a testable video campaign in minutes, with no prompts or filming.

Complete execution plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue, launch, and risks. Under construction, not launched.

1) Target Audience

• Primary: small/medium Shopify, WooCommerce, and Etsy stores selling physical products, with no designer or video team.

• Secondary: small performance agencies, freelance marketers, social media managers, and local brands wanting fast ads without shoots.

• Need: a clear product photo but no time/expertise for a short ad; they dislike prompts and complex tools.

• Markets: US, Europe, the Gulf, Southeast Asia, and Latin America; Arabic and English interfaces from day one.

2) Differentiators

1. No prompt engineering: add product, choose ad angle, export; no prompt box.

2. Product identity protection: visual lock plus automatic packaging text/logo checks to prevent distortion or invented details.

3. Testable variants, not one video: 3–5 variants with different angles and hooks; ad success depends on testing.

4. Built-in compliance: blocks fabricated testimonials, flags unsubstantiated claims, discloses synthetic content.

5. Original identity: simplified studio design, RTL/LTR support, code and interface built from scratch.

6. Free start and transparency: free plan without a credit card; clear video usage counter.

3) MVP (max 8 tasks)

1. Product import: photo or link from Shopify/WooCommerce/Etsy; automatic name, category, and benefit extraction, manual entry on failure.

2. Brand identity: logo, colors, font, tone; reusable Brand Kit.

3. Automatic creative brief: 3 ad angles, hook, 15–30s script, shot list, CTA without prompts.

4. Asset generation: product isolation/enhancement, backgrounds/scenes, virtual presenter or voice only, voiceover, licensed music, captions.

5. Assembly/export: 9:16, 1:1, and 16:9 with safe zones, caption sync, audio normalization, free-plan watermark.

6. Product protection/compliance: shape lock, packaging text check, claims check, fake testimonial blocking, synthetic content disclosure.

7. Testing: 3–5 variants per product, hook/angle comparison, batch download, version history.

8. Account/billing/ops: sign-up, workspaces, usage limits, Stripe, cloud render queue, retries, data deletion.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

· Plan · Price · Includes ·

· Free · $0 · 3 videos/month, 15s, 720p, watermark, no card ·

· Starter · $29/month · 15 videos, 1080p, 3 variants, no watermark, one Brand Kit ·

· Growth · $89/month · 60 videos, 5 variants, 3 brands, store sync, variant comparison ·

· Agency · $249/month · 200 videos, 10 brands, API/white-label, priority processing ·

• Revenue: subscriptions, then add-on credits after limits, then API and white-label for agencies.

• Add-on credits: $12 for 10 videos, 20% off annual payment.

• Pricing principle: start free, pay for usage, not seats.

5) Global Go-to-Market Plan with Free Channels

• SEO/use-case pages: English, Arabic, Spanish, Portuguese, German, and French pages for cases like “turn a product photo into an ad video” by category and size.

• Free lead magnet: product photo readiness check with score and hook tips, no sign-up.

• Organic video: TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts with before/after, hook analysis, and real test results without paid ads.

• Communities: Product Hunt and Indie Hackers launch; valuable posts in Reddit, Facebook Groups, and e-commerce Discords, respecting their rules.

• Integration channels: free Shopify App Store and integration directory listings; quick store setup.

• Partnerships/referrals: free credits for agencies and freelancers, recurring commissions, joint case studies.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

Legal:

• Product photo and brand rights: user guarantees ownership; source log and fast takedown.

• Voices, faces, and music: licensed library only; impersonation prevention; synthetic content disclosure.

• Advertising claims: automated checks, fake testimonial prevention, final user approval before export.

• Privacy: GDPR/CCPA compliance, data processing agreement, actual deletion on request.

Technical:

• Product/packaging text distortion: visual lock, OCR check, confidence score before export.

• Generation cost/time: async queue, caching, spending limits per account.

• AI provider dependence: fallback paths and multiple providers to reduce disruption.

• Abuse: content checks, rate limits, review of high-usage accounts.

7) Name and Tagline

Name: Vidmira
