Mintfolio — Complete Project Plan

Snap your card, know its fair value, manage your collection with confidence.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, monetization model, growth plan, and risks. The project is under development and has not been launched yet.

Product Plan: Card Scanning and Financial Tracking

1) Target Audience

• Pokémon card collectors and sports card collectors (ages 16–40) who own 50–500 cards and primarily use their phones.

• Hobbyists returning to collecting after years, who are unaware of the current market value of their collection.

• Small sellers on eBay, Whatnot, and social media platforms who need fast pricing and a cost ledger.

• Local card shops and card show organizers looking to offer quick appraisal services to their customers.

2) Competitive Advantage (Why Choose Our Product)

• Batch scanning: Photograph multiple cards in succession with automatic cropping and identification of the set, number, and edition.

• Fair value, not just the last sale: Average and median of recent sales, adjusted by condition, grade, selling fees, and shipping costs, with a confidence score and transparent source history.

• Real cost tracking: Purchase price, realized and unrealized profit/loss, and portfolio distribution.

• Condition-first: Raw or graded, grading company and grade, borders and defects, with private images saved for the user.

• Privacy by default: The collection is private, with sharing via an optional link.

• Lightweight PWA: Works on mobile without an app store, with offline mode for the collection.

• Free to start, no intrusive ads: Core features are available before payment.

• Arabic/English interface: Multi-currency support, tailored to emerging markets.

3) Scope of the First Version (Max 8 Tasks)

1. Quick login (email or Google/Apple) and a collector profile, with a first-scan experience without an account.

2. Capture a photo or upload multiple photos, auto-crop, and identify the card via a vision model and a matching database.

3. Smart confirmation screen for weak matches: text search and filters (set, edition, number, language).

4. Fetch price comparisons from allowed sources, and display the price range, median, confidence score, and date of the last sale.

5. Record condition, grade, and photos, with a condition-adjusted value calculation.

6. Collection ledger: total value, cost, profit/loss, and distribution by set and player.

7. Alerts: price change for a card, drop in price of a desired card, or reaching a sell goal.

8. CSV/PDF export, optional public portfolio link, and PWA support for offline use.

4) Pricing and Monetization Model

• Free Forever: Up to 75 cards, limited monthly scans, basic comparisons, and simple CSV export.

• Pro: $4.99/month or $39/year — Unlimited cards, batch scanning, instant alerts, advanced analytics, and full export.

• Dealer: $14.99/month — Multiple users, fast shop interface, branded export, and higher limits.

• Optional referral commission upon transitioning to a partner selling marketplace, with clear disclosure.

• No selling of user data, and no ads targeting private content.

5) Global Growth Plan with Free Channels

• SEO pages per set/card: Approximate values, trends, and checklists, while respecting data rights.

• Communities: Reddit, Discord, and collecting forums, sharing free tools such as "Identify your card".

• Short videos: TikTok, YouTube, and Instagram showing a card scan and its value in seconds.

• Local presence: Card shows and game stores via QR code and a free store version.

• Referral program: One month of Pro free for both parties when inviting an active collector.

• Launch on Product Hunt and Hacker News, then optimize visibility for Arabic, Spanish, Portuguese, and Japanese markets.

• Weekly email newsletters on market trends, based on aggregated and anonymized data.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Image and trademark rights: Using card images and trademarks may require a license or limited use. Solution: Rely on user images and licensed metadata.

• Price data: Platform terms of use may prohibit scraping. Solution: Official APIs and partnerships, with source and date labeling.

• Privacy: Collection data and photos are personal. Solution: Encryption, limited retention, and GDPR/CCPA compliance.

• Misleading claims: No guarantee of authenticity or official appraisal is provided. Solution: Clear disclosure and labeling results as "estimates".
