Burhan — Complete Project Plan

Every sentence on your site has proof from your customers.

Execution plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue, go-to-market, and risks. The project is under construction and not yet launched.

1) Target Audience

Local businesses with good reviews but no site or an outdated one that doesn't attract customers: plumbing, electrical, cleaning, HVAC, clinics and beauty centers, salons, restaurants and cafés, repair shops, private tutors, and small professional services.

Start with Arab, Gulf, and Arabic-speaking diaspora markets, then English-speaking markets. Secondary: freelancers and agencies managing local clients' sites.

2) Differentiators

• Proof before decoration: every claim tied to a review quote or owner-verified fact, with source and date. No invented services or prices.

• Conversion-focused, not a brochure: WhatsApp, call, directions, and booking buttons, with strongest proof near decision point.

• Arabic/English from day one: native RTL, not later translation.

• Full ownership, no lock-in: export as static HTML and JSON, connect a custom domain; content remains the client's.

• Free, fast start: site in about 5 minutes without a credit card.

• Built-in compliance: official APIs or owner input, redacted personal data, no fake reviews.

3) MVP Scope (8 tasks max)

1. Verification and import: sign-in, phone/email verification, connect via Google Business Profile API or paste data/reviews manually with source.

2. Evidence engine: categorize reviews by service/topic, pick 5–8 short quotes, redact names and sensitive data, link each to its source.

3. Safe content generation: Arabic/English copy in owner-chosen tone, with locked facts preventing invented services, prices, or certifications.

4. Editor: ready sections for hero, services, proof wall, hours, map, contact, booking, and gallery, with live editing and RTL.

5. Templates: six original, fast, responsive designs with colors, fonts, logo, accessibility.

6. Publishing: free subdomain, SSL, CDN, one-click publish, custom domain on paid plans.

7. Updates and analytics: periodic review sync, stale-quote removal, and click tracking for calls, WhatsApp, and directions.

8. Accounts and billing: free/paid plans, usage limits, Stripe/Paddle subscriptions, HTML/JSON export.

4) Pricing and Revenue

• Free • $0 • 1 subdomain site, 10 quotes, 1 language, monthly updates, badge •

• Starter • $9/month • Custom domain, no badge, 30 quotes, weekly updates, 2 languages •

• Pro • $19/month • 3 sites, daily updates, forms and booking, deeper analytics, faster support •

• Agency • $79/month • 15 sites, white-label, reports, advanced export, agency account •

Primary revenue: subscriptions, paid setup ($99/site), agency referrals. No ads on client sites; static hosting keeps margins high and trust intact.

5) Global Go-to-Market: Free Channels

• Free diagnostic: shareable review-quality/local-presence report to collect emails and show value.

• Practical SEO: Arabic/English guides and real cases, not thin city pages.

• Communities: Reddit, Facebook Groups, Indie Hackers, Product Hunt, local business forums; useful analysis, not ads.

• Agency/freelancer partnerships: free agency plan, referral commission, workshops for chambers of commerce and coworking spaces.

• Short video: restaurant, salon, clinic cases on TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram, LinkedIn.

• Distribution loop: optional badge on free sites + referral credits + expat groups with permission.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

Legal:

• Review platform terms may ban scraping; use official APIs/owner input, cite sources, honor deletion requests.

• Review text is copyrighted; use short quotes with link/source, or explicit permission.

• Privacy/GDPR/CCPA: redact personal data, lawful basis, processing agreements, deletion on request.

• Advertising/consumer protection: no fake testimonials, owner pre-approval, AI disclosure.

• Accessibility, tax, and local compliance.

Technical:

• API changes/rate limits; use fallback sources and manual import.

• AI hallucination/cost; use evidence-linked claims, quality checks, caching, usage limits.

• Spam/impersonation; use ownership verification, moderation, publishing limits.

• Content/domain security; use input sanitization, XSS/SSRF protection, domain-hijacking prevention.

• Weak SEO from incomplete pages; use canonical/noindex for unfinished pages and original content.
