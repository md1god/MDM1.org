Shelflens — Complete Project Plan

Launch your app in the right place, at the right time.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under development and has not been launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Merchants: looking for the best app to solve a specific problem (shipping, reviews, upsell), and want proof of quality and price before paying.

• Marketing and development agencies: need reliable recommendations for their clients and want to compare multiple alternatives quickly.

• App developers: want to measure category size, find the optimal launch timing, and monitor competitors.

• Investors and entrepreneurs: looking for gaps in the e-commerce app ecosystem before building a new product.

2) Key Differentiators (Why choose our product)

• Smart alerts instead of static dashboards: instant notifications when a competitor grows, prices drop, or review consistency becomes unusual.

• Launch Score: a single metric that combines category size, competition intensity, and market pricing to help developers make a decision in seconds.

• Visual comparison: a single chart to compare 3–5 apps side by side (price, growth, reviews, support).

• Verified data with clear sources: every number is tied to a source and date to build trust rather than general claims.

• Instant free access without registration: an open trial for organic visits, with conversion to subscribers.

3) Scope of the First Version (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. Periodically collect data for ~1,000 apps via official APIs and public sources, with cleaning and standardization of fields.

2. A single database for categories, prices, and reviews with a weekly historical timeline.

3. Dashboard: growth rankings, price changes, and weekly review rate.

4. Comparison page for up to 4 apps in a table and a single chart.

5. Simple and explainable Launch Score.

6. Free weekly email alerts based on the category followed by the user.

7. Pricing page + login + Freemium plan.

8. SEO: category and comparison pages ready to be shared.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free: follow 2 categories, compare 2 apps, 1 weekly alert — to attract merchants and developers.

• Pro — $29/month: all categories, instant alerts, CSV export, 12-month history.

• Team/Agency — $99/month: multiple seats, custom comparisons, Slack/API alerts, white-label reports.

• Additional revenue: limited and transparent ad space for app companies (clearly labeled ads).

• Stripe payments, 14-day trial for paid plans.

5) Global Distribution Plan with Free Channels

• SEO: pages like "Best X app", "X alternative", and popular comparisons built automatically with unique insights.

• Weekly content: "Top 10 fastest-growing apps this week" report, ready to copy and share.

• Communities: share useful insights in developer and merchant groups (Reddit, Facebook Groups, Discord).

• Product Hunt and Hacker News: launch the free version with ready-to-use comparison pages.

• Content partnerships: short interviews with app developers and agencies in exchange for mutual mentions.

• Integrations: free templates and demos on publishing platforms (Notion, Google Sheets) as entry channels.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal: comply with store terms of use and official APIs, avoid verbatim content republishing, clearly separate trademarked elements owned by others, with a privacy policy and explicit consent for registration.

• Legal: respect review rights, autogenerated content, and cite sources when needed.

• Technical: API changes or shutdowns → modular extraction layer with swappable adapters + backup of the last valid dataset.

• Technical: data quality (removals and additions) → monitor historical completeness and automatic recalibration on anomalies.

• Conflict of interest: prevent selling rankings or reviews, and clearly disclose paid advertisements.

• Market: entry of a well-funded larger player → focus on speed, transparency, and alerts as a hard-to-copy advantage.

7) Proposed Name and Tagline

Proposed name: Shelflens

Tagline: Launch your app in the right place, at the right time.
