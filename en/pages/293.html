MawjTalk — Full Project Plan

Speak from minute one, and leave the silence to the teacher.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the rollout plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a conversational voice-language teacher powered by AI

1) Target audience

• Self-directed learners (18–40 years old) who want to speak a foreign language but cannot find an available conversation partner.

• Travelers and remote workers who need quick practical conversations (cafés, interviews, shopping).

• University students preparing to pass spoken tests such as IELTS Speaking.

• Corporate employees who need training in professional conversations in a foreign language.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Instant conversation in the browser via the microphone, with no app installation.

• Immediate pronunciation and grammar grading, with a clear written alternative for every sentence.

• Custom realistic scenarios (job interview, travel, doctor) instead of generic dialogues.

• "Gradual calm" mode: the teacher starts with simple sentences, then increases speed and complexity.

• A simplified weekly progress log showing new words and weak points.

• A free tier sufficient to start today without a credit card.

3) Scope of the first release (8 tasks maximum)

1. Simple sign-in (email or Google account).

2. Language and starting level selection via a short test.

3. In-browser voice conversation (Web Speech API + speech/text generation model).

4. Instant report: pronunciation + grammar + sentence translation.

5. Written mode as a fallback conversation for users with blocked microphones.

6. Only three ready-made scenario packs (travel, work, daily life).

7. "My progress" page: repetitions, saved vocabulary, weekly score.

8. Upgrade billing dashboard (Stripe) with monthly/yearly plans.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: 10 minutes of voice conversation daily + basic reports.

• Plus at $9.99/month or $79/year: unlimited conversation, extra scenarios, detailed pronunciation analysis.

• Pro at $19.99/month: professional settings, progress certificate, priority response speed.

• Additional revenue: school/team packs (B2B) priced per seat.

5) Global rollout plan using free channels

1. Short practical content (TikTok/Reels/YouTube Shorts): "Answer this question in English in 30 seconds."

2. Multilingual SEO articles: comparisons, vocabulary, pronunciation tips.

3. Discord/Telegram community for daily conversation exchange among users.

4. Partnerships with small educational content creators in exchange for free access.

5. "Invite a friend" version: 3 free Plus days for each side.

6. Presence on Product Hunt, Hacker News, and free educational platforms.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: compliance with GDPR/COPPA, microphone permissions, policy for deleting voice recordings, content translation rights.

• Technical: speech recognition accuracy in less common languages, cost of calling speech models, browser limits for Web Speech API, mobile network speed.

• Business: usage cost may exceed the subscription with heavy users → need for smart daily limits.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: MawjTalk

Marketing tagline: Speak from minute one, and leave the silence to the teacher.
