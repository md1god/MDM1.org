Product plan: 3D software design templates

1) Target audience

• Engineers and prototypers who want reusable, editable designs instead of repeated manual drawing.

• Developers and Makers in 3D printing, robotics and small workshop communities.

• Architects and students wanting a lightweight free tool that runs across systems without heavy install.

• Small product teams needing CAD files reviewable in a version control system (Git) with their team.

2) Differentiators (why choose us)

• Instant preview: any parameter change rebuilds the preview in fractions of a second, with before/after comparison.

• Dual editor: text and graphics fully linked — a code change appears at once, dragging a handle updates the code.

• Visual parameter system (Parameter Panel): sliders panel that builds the interface without writing any code.

• Parts library ready to install as Packages with one click.

• Direct export: STL/3MF/OBJ/DXF/SVG with Mesh Check before export.

• Runs in the browser, no installation, plus optional desktop version for offline work.

3) First-release scope (max 8 tasks)

1. Text editor with syntax highlighting, bracket matching and fast navigation.

2. Open-source programmatic geometry kernel for solids (CSG + Extrude/Revolve).

3. Interactive 3D preview in the browser (WebGL) with rotate, zoom and measure.

4. Sliding parameter bar with instant automatic rebuild.

5. Export to STL, 3MF and OBJ with mesh integrity check.

6. Save the project in the browser, then optional cloud sync (free account).

7. Units (mm/inch) and clear coordinate system with in-app help.

8. Arabic and English docs + 10 sample tutorials (phone stand, gears, variable box).

4) Pricing and revenue model

· Plan · Price · What you get ·

· Free-to-start · $0 · Full editor, preview, STL export, 3 cloud projects, core library ·

· Pro · $9/month · Unlimited projects, Git sync, DXF/SVG export, advanced library and checks ·

· Team · $25/user/month · Shared spaces, comment review, permissions, optional self-hosting ·

· Enterprise · Negotiable · Self-hosted deployment and annual support contracts ·

Extra revenue: paid template marketplace (creator 70% / platform 30%).

5) Global rollout with free channels

1. Blog and SEO: tutorials in English and Arabic targeting "parametric 3D design" and "browser CAD".

2. YouTube: "Designed in 5 minutes" series — full project from idea to print file.

3. GitHub (editor open source): publish examples and templates to encourage contributions.

4. Makers communities: sharing on 3D printing forums, Discord and Reddit.

5. Monthly challenges: free design contest with symbolic prizes on X, LinkedIn and Instagram Reels.

6. University partnerships: free teaching material for engineering and design departments.

7. Product Hunt and Hacker News: early beta launch to gather first users.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: confirm the license of every geometry kernel and graphics library used (prefer MIT/Apache/BSD) and list it in a licenses file; avoid registered trademarks belonging to others.

• Legal: protect user data and privacy (GDPR) when offering accounts and cloud sync.

• Technical: geometry accuracy and edge cases (intersections, holes, curved surfaces) — handled by automated geometry test suites.

• Technical: browser performance on large models — handled with progressive chunking and background building (Web Workers).

• Technical: competition from established free tools — softened by differentiation (dual editor, speed, Arabic experience) and avoiding direct confrontation.
