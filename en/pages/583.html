Nabra — Complete Project Plan

All your subscriptions in one calm reading space; read only what matters to you.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Knowledge professionals, researchers, and journalists who follow dozens of sources and need fast, organized reading.

• Students, developers, and content creators who want to follow blogs and newsletters without algorithm noise.

• Privacy-conscious readers, especially those who do not want their reader tied to an account or mandatory cloud services.

• Arab and global audiences: a native Arabic interface with full right-to-left support, alongside English.

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• Local-first: works without an account, subscriptions are saved on the device, with permanent export to OPML and JSON.

• Optional end-to-end encrypted sync: decryption keys are not stored on the server.

• Calm instead of algorithms: unified inbox, priority rules, deduplication, and a configurable daily digest.

• Comfortable reading: clean reading mode, offline saving, instant search, and native Arabic support.

• Transparent, optional AI: summarization and translation on demand, using the user’s key or a paid quota, without selling data.

• High performance: designed to follow hundreds of sources without excessive memory use.

3) Scope of the First Version (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. Import and export OPML/JSON, and add RSS, Atom, and JSON Feed feeds via link or auto-discovery.

2. Unified reading interface with folders, tags, read states, instant search, and filtering.

3. Clean reading mode with full-text extraction, offline saving, and RTL support.

4. Configurable background refresh, notifications, deduplication, and grouping of similar topics.

5. Encrypted local storage, backup, and secure restore.

6. Optional cross-device sync with end-to-end encryption and a local key.

7. Daily or weekly digest with calm rules, plus optional AI summarization and translation.

8. Arabic and English interfaces, keyboard shortcuts, and basic accessibility.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

· Plan · Price · Features ·

· Free · $0 · All local features, unlimited subscriptions, full export, sync for two devices, and AI with the user’s key ·

· Pro · $3 per month or $29 per year · Unlimited sync, hosted summarization quota, advanced rules, and priority support ·

· Team · $6 per user per month · Optional shared space and team-level rules, later ·

• 14-day Pro trial without a card, and appropriate regional pricing.

• No ads and no data selling. Payment through a provider acting as merchant of record for taxes, such as Paddle or Lemon Squeezy.

5) Global Go-to-Market Plan with Free Channels

• Launch on Product Hunt, Show HN, and RSS and privacy communities, with content that explains the problem, not just the product.

• A landing site and practical SEO pages: the best way to follow blogs, organize sources, and read offline.

• A free browser extension for discovering feeds and adding them with one click.

• Free Arabic and English guides, and ready-made OPML packs by field.

• Free partnerships with bloggers and newsletters via a “Follow in Nabra” button.

• Short video content on YouTube, Mastodon, and Bluesky, and community localization of the app.

• Gradual launch in English first, then Arabic, Spanish, German, French, and Japanese.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

· Risk · Mitigation ·

· Content rights and site terms · Display feed data and excerpts only, respect paywalls and robots, and do not republish content ·

· Privacy and GDPR · Local storage by default, end-to-end encryption, a clear privacy policy, and complete data deletion ·

· Trademark · Check the name and trademark before launch in target markets ·

· AI · Optional activation, clear disclosure, no training on user data, and usage limits ·

· Broken or malicious feeds · Robust parsers, SSRF protection, and an optional proxy only when needed ·

· Sync and key loss · Clear conflict versions, a recovery file, and periodic restore tests ·

· Cost and scaling · Quotas and limits, caching, and monitoring AI and sync costs ·

7) Proposed Name and Tagline

Proposed name: Nabra

Tagline: All your subscriptions in one calm reading space; you read only what matters to you.
