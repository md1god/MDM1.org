Zurfa — Complete Project Plan

Grow smarter with one square meter.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a data and knowledge platform for small home gardeners

1) Target audience

• Balcony and small home garden owners (beginners and intermediate).

• Members of community gardening clubs and urban container gardening operators.

• Teachers and community initiatives teaching home food security.

• Researchers and non-profit organizations interested in home gardening data.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Open structured data model (JSON API + CSV files) under a CC BY-SA license, ready for analysis and research.

• Local recommendation engine based on climate zone (USDA/Köppen), growing area, and season—not just crop names.

• Personal planting log with a harvest timeline and yield estimates that automatically converts to a per-area return dashboard.

• Auto-translated community (Arabic/English/Spanish/French interface) with documented contributions and human review.

• Offline-first via PWA for weak-connectivity areas, with later sync.

3) First-version scope (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Email/social login + public profile (climate, area, experience level).

2. Basic crop database (~300 crops) in Arabic and English: seasons, water/light needs, spacing, sizes.

3. PWA interface: recommended crops list based on climate zone and current season.

4. "What I plant" log with watering reminders and simple visual growth tracking.

5. Harvest documentation template (date, quantity, unit) + productivity calculator per m².

6. Public read-only API + Swagger documentation + rate limit.

7. Community contribution board: suggest new data + review + edit history.

8. CSV and JSON export/import for users and researchers.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free to start: all core features and the public API are free.

• Pro — $4/month: advanced productivity analytics, multi-layer export, smart alerts, priority community support.

• Institutions — $49/month: licensing for teaching teams, custom data, and reports.

• Secondary revenue: agricultural tool partners (transparent referral commissions) and newsletter sponsors.

• No in-app ads, to protect the practical gardening experience.

5) Global rollout plan using free channels

• Shareable content: PDF seasonal charts and short "how to grow X in one square meter" clips.

• Partnerships with local urban farming Reddit and Facebook communities and WhatsApp/Telegram groups.

• University and school club initiatives: ready-to-download educational activity packs.

• "Your data first" campaigns: invite gardeners to upload photos of their notes with Arabic/English tags.

• Arabic/English/Spanish SEO: crop pages for each major climate zone.

• Free podcasts and webinars with home gardening experts in exchange for a project mention.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: the data license (CC BY-SA) may deter some commercial contributors; adopting a clear Contributor Agreement solves this.

• Legal: local crop name data may conflict with commercial name ownership—use only common public names only.

• Technical: contributor data quality varies—require every edit to include a source reference and human review.

• Technical: changes to USDA/Köppen climate classifications require a tested annual update mechanism.

• Commercial: dependence on a small contributor community at first—organize self-funded internal seed content before launch.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Zurfa (زُرْعَة)

Tagline: Grow smarter with one square meter.
