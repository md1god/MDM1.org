Tarseem — Complete Project Plan

Know the final import cost of every SKU before you buy.

Complete plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, launch, and risks. Project is under construction, not launched.

1) Target audience

Small importers and online store owners buying from China, Turkey, or India, with 1–20 staff and 20–500 shipments/year. They lack a customs compliance team and need pre-purchase cost estimates plus a simple understanding of HS classification, duties, de minimis thresholds, and rules of origin. Secondary channel: customs brokers and small freight forwarders.

2) Differentiators

• Free start, no credit card: instant costs for a limited number of products.

• SKU-level cost before purchase: goods, shipping, insurance, duties, tax, and local fees combined.

• Explainable classification: HS suggestions with confidence score, reasons, and source, not an opaque number.

• Source and date for every number: time-stamped rules database, not a static price table.

• Proactive alerts when duties, classification, rules of origin, or de minimis thresholds change.

• User-friendly: CSV/Excel import, no ERP or complex integration.

• New, direct design: one cost card per shipment and a broker-ready report.

• Multi-country: starts with specific trade lanes, then expands globally.

3) First-version scope (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Import product catalog from CSV/Excel with validation of key fields.

2. Hybrid HS classification engine (rules + retrieval + LLM) showing top 3 suggestions with confidence and explanation, saving the decision.

3. Duties and taxes database for each destination/origin lane with source and effective date.

4. Final import cost calculator: goods value, shipping, insurance, duties, tax, and port/clearance fees.

5. Check de minimis thresholds and simplified rules of origin, with an explicit alert when human review is needed.

6. Email and in-app alerts for changes in duties, classification, or origin rules.

7. Export PDF/Excel report and share link, with a user account and clear usage limits.

8. Closed beta with 20 importers, measuring estimate accuracy and first-shipment completion.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: 20 saved SKUs/month, one trade lane, unlimited calculations, 3 alerts, limited exports.

• Pro – $19/month: 500 SKUs, 5 lanes, unlimited alerts, PDF reports, 3 users.

• Business – $79/month: 5,000 SKUs, API, 10 users, roles/permissions, audit log.

• Enterprise: custom pricing for brokers and forwarders with white label and integrations.

• Paid add-ons: extra API calls, new country coverage, optional human review.

• 20% annual discount, targeting gross margin above 80% and 3–5% free-to-paid rate. No ads or data selling.

5) Global expansion plan with free channels

• Programmatic SEO: “HS code for [product]” and “landed cost from [country] to [country]” pages with a live calculator and structured data.

• Free public tool: embeddable calculator for blogs and stores, bringing backlinks and organic traffic.

• Multilingual content: English, Arabic, Spanish, and French, with local examples per market.

• E-commerce and import communities: practical cases and real numbers on LinkedIn, Reddit, and store forums.

• Free partnerships: customs brokers, freight forwarders, and 3PLs offer a co-branded calculator to customers.

• App directories: free listings in Shopify, WooCommerce, and others, then gradual integration.

• Referral program: free usage credits for invitations, and a weekly email newsletter on duty changes.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Risk • Mitigation

• Classification may be considered binding customs advice • State that results are estimates, show confidence/source, and route ambiguous cases to human review.

• Duty errors or sudden rule changes • Versioning, official source per rule, change alerts, and a reviewable audit log.

• Data licensing and source terms • Use permitted official sources, review licenses, and do not republish protected data.

• Product and supplier data privacy • Encryption, data processing agreements, granular permissions, and GDPR compliance where applicable.

• Model hallucination or low accuracy • Confidence thresholds, rule-based validation, periodic testing, and automatic hold when uncertain.

• Slow updates and complex origin/de minimis rules • Monitored data pipeline, failure alerts, and gradual coverage of high-demand countries.

• Free-tier abuse • Rate limits, account verification, and usage monitoring.
