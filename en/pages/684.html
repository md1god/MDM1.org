KoraGraph — Complete Project Plan

Open football data, ready for analysis in minutes.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and not yet launched.

Product plan: KoraGraph

1) Target audience

• Performance analysts at small clubs and academies (about 70% lack a full-time analyst) need ready match reports within 10 minutes.

• Youth and amateur coaches who want to understand opponents without buying systems costing thousands of dollars.

• Journalists and sports creators seeking reliable, quotable numbers with a clear source.

• Data science students and researchers, especially in the Arab region, training models on real data.

• Fantasy and TikTok creators who need fast, shareable charts.

• Scouts and talent officers who want to compare players across competitions.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Structured data in PostgreSQL and Parquet instead of scattered raw files, with one unified schema for all competitions.

• A fully Arabic RTL interface alongside English, missing from most current analysis tools.

• One-click analytics: xG, PPDA, progressive passes, heat maps, and player comparison.

• Under two seconds for a query over 10 million events thanks to indexes, columnar storage, and caching.

• A generous permanent free tier and OpenAPI-documented API, lowering the trial barrier to zero.

• Full transparency in licensing and attribution on every page and export, with all code and design built from scratch.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. A Python import and cleaning pipeline for licensed open sources, with an attribution log per file.

2. Design a unified data schema (matches, events, players, teams, competitions) and store it in PostgreSQL and Parquet.

3. A web catalog of competitions and matches with filters by season, team, and player.

4. An interactive match dashboard: pass map, xG timeline, and heat map.

5. A player and team comparison tool with publish-ready PNG and CSV export.

6. A REST API with keys, usage quotas, and full OpenAPI documentation.

7. A Python client package and 5 educational Colab notebooks from querying to charting.

8. User registration, favorites, and responsive Arabic RTL interface.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Permanent Free plan: public data, 1,000 API requests/day, 3 saved dashboards, limited export.

• Pro plan at $9/month: 20,000 requests/day, unlimited dashboards, advanced comparisons.

• Club plan at $49/month: 5 seats, PDF reports with club branding, 100,000 requests/day.

• Enterprise plan with custom pricing: private hosting, service-level agreement, analysis team training.

• Additional revenue: $199 workshops, weekly newsletter sponsorship, and sales of ready-made reports.

• Realistic goal: convert 2% of 50,000 free users into 1,000 paid subscribers, about $9,000/month.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• SEO via a page per match and player (about 20,000 pages) with structured Schema data.

• An open SDK on GitHub and curated datasets on Hugging Face and Kaggle to attract researchers.

• Short videos on YouTube and TikTok explaining one analysis in 60 seconds with a direct link.

• Weekly participation in X, Reddit, and Discord football-analysis communities, without flooding or spam.

• Partnerships with universities and hackathons: free student access in exchange for published projects and reviews.

• Launch on Product Hunt and Arab football newsletters and podcasts, with a referral program for a free month.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Licensing: review every source before import, comply with attribution, do not redistribute what is not allowed, and withdraw data upon request.

• Privacy: collect no personal player data beyond what is published, comply with GDPR, and minimize data.

• Image and trademark rights: use only original charts and graphics, avoid club logos without permission.

• Betting: prohibit API use in betting products without a license, and set clear terms of use.

• Volume and performance: millions of events require partitioning, indexes, and caching to avoid slow queries.

• Data quality and schema changes: contract tests, schema versions, automated alerts, and source diversification to reduce dependence on one source.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: KoraGraph

Tagline: Open football data, ready for analysis in minutes.
