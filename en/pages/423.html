Faawra — Complete Project Plan

Your city's freebies… reaching you before someone else takes them.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the rollout plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• The bargain hunter: students, young families, and new residents looking for furniture, appliances, and home items without paying.

• The repeat browser: whoever opens the app daily searching for a free "deal" near them (the kilometer is the attractiveness metric).

• The quick-disposal owner: whoever wants to donate their items instead of selling them, and wants them out of the house today.

• The collector/reseller: whoever collects freebies and sells them (a segment that needs filtering to reduce friction).

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

1. Reverse request board: you write "what you are looking for" (bed 140×200) and a notification reaches you when one appears — instead of you chasing everything.

2. Extinction timer: each listing shows the remaining time before it closes/if it is taken, with an instant notification for saved searches (value within minutes, not days).

3. Map + actual walking-time sorting: sorted by perceived arrival time (walking/bus/car), not straight-line distance.

4. Protection layer: reliability signals for listings (pickup confirmation, listing age, member reports) and fewer spam posts.

5. Free for basic use (browsing + 3 alerts) and payment for speed and breadth, not for locking the door.

3) Scope of the First Version (8 tasks max)

1. Ingesting licensed sources + user submissions, with cleaning and unifying fields (name, image, location, pickup time).

2. Main feed with search and filtering (category, distance, status, pickup time) sorted by remaining time.

3. Map view of nearby listings.

4. User account + saving searches and alerting on them (email/Push).

5. "Request it" screen to publish user requests and send the notification to interested users.

6. Admin panel: reports, hiding, manual review.

7. Interactive PWA version for mobile (without a paid store at the beginning).

8. Paid subscription: payment gateway (Stripe) + a limited free plan and Pro plans.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

· Plan · Price · What it includes ·

· Free · 0 · Full browsing, 3 alerts, 15-minute delay on instant alerts ·

· Monthly Pro · 3$/month · Unlimited instant alerts, map, unlimited requests ·

· Annual Pro · 19$/year · Same features + ~45% savings ·

· Lifetime · 49$ one-time · For early adopters ·

• Model: subscription freemium (value layer = speed and alerts, not content).

• Additional revenue: local sponsored ads (second-hand stores) alongside the feed without cluttering it.

• Initial goal: 300 Pro subscribers = ~900$ MRR, with cost reduction through a free data source and host (Cloudflare Pages + Supabase).

5) Global Rollout Plan with Free Channels

1. Local SEO: a page for every city ("Freebies in [city]") with content updated hourly — a high-intent free channel.

2. TikTok/Reels: daily videos "I found this for free 500m away" (self-filmed if there is no content-creator budget).

3. Local groups and communities: useful (ad-like) posts in forums and Reddit and Telegram/WhatsApp groups for each city.

4. Referral program: "Invite a friend = one free week of Pro".

5. Campus channels: student clubs and hand-to-hand distribution at the end of the academic term (peak furniture disposal time).

6. Free listings: freebies catalogs, free newsletters, Product Hunt for the idea.

7. Collaboration with resell stores and associations: in exchange for mentioning them in the feed.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: respecting the terms of use of sources (avoiding aggressive extraction), copyright on images, liability for listings (we do not guarantee the safety of goods), location data privacy (GDPR/CCPA) with explicit consent, and preventing our app being used as a channel for selling prohibited items.

• Technical: outage or changes in data sources (low: we rely on direct user submissions as a fallback path), platform flooding with spam and fraud (need automated moderation), notification fatigue → one consolidated daily notification, growing storage/image costs, and the reliability of maps/free APIs.

7) Proposed Name and Tagline

Proposed name: Faawra

Tagline: Your city's freebies… reaching you before someone else takes them.
