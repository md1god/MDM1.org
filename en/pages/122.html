Riwāq — Complete Project Plan

A complete recruitment agency in one workspace, from the first message to the first day of work.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

A plan to build an original product for recruitment management and candidate relationships

1) Target Audience

• Small and medium-sized recruitment agencies (2–50 employees) that manage dozens of jobs and hundreds of candidates manually via email and spreadsheets.

• In-house recruiting teams at startups and small companies that do not need a heavy enterprise system.

• Freelancers and solo office owners in emerging markets who rely primarily on WhatsApp and the phone.

• Pain point: scattered candidate data, lost follow-ups, lack of team collaboration, and the high cost of enterprise systems.

2) Differentiators (Why choose our product)

• Built for agencies first: a complete workflow from receiving the job to hiring the candidate and earning the commission, not just a candidate database.

• A unified communication channel (email + WhatsApp) with reply tracking inside the system instead of switching between tools.

• Explainable smart filtering: ranks candidates based on criteria the user defines transparently, with the ability to adjust them manually to avoid bias.

• A collaborative team workspace with clear permissions, alerts, and task assignment.

• A truly free start with no bank card required, and instant cloud operation.

• Privacy first: isolation of each agency's data, a configurable retention policy, and full data export at any time.

3) Scope of the First Version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Sign up and create an agency workspace with member invitations.

2. Job management (posting, job status, client data, expected commission).

3. A unified candidate database with a profile, an uploaded résumé, and text search.

4. A customizable stage pipeline (Kanban board) and dragging the candidate through stages.

5. Add notes and ratings and track the communication history with each candidate.

6. Send emails with ready-made templates and link replies to the candidate.

7. A basic reports dashboard (number of candidates, conversion rate, active jobs).

8. Export data and bulk-import CSV/résumé files.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free forever (Free): one user, unlimited candidates, limited active jobs, full export.

• Pro per user/month: advanced stages and reports, email and WhatsApp integration, advanced permissions.

• Agency per workspace/month: multiple users and team permissions, field customization, priority support.

• Supplemental revenue: an add-ons marketplace (paid integrations), bulk-messaging fees, a managed data-import service, and annual plans at a discount.

• Principle: the free tier is direct marketing that makes acquiring small agencies cost almost nothing.

5) Global Go-to-Market Plan with Free Channels

• SEO and practical content: free guides in Arabic and English on candidate and commission management, targeting recurring job categories.

• Recruitment communities: helpful participation in LinkedIn, Reddit, and specialized Slack/Discord groups without spamming.

• Product Hunt and GitHub: a free product launch with open plans for integration components to attract developers and enthusiasts.

• YouTube and short videos: "from zero to first hire" walkthroughs that show value quickly.

• Integrations: listing the add-on in popular tool marketplaces (calendar, email, chat) to bring sustainable traffic.

• Referral and ambassador program: rewarding with free plans in exchange for inviting new agencies.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

Legal:

• Candidate personal data protection (GDPR/CCPA): the need for a legal basis, consent, and the right to deletion and copying.

• Employment discrimination regulation: preventing the use of sensitive criteria in automated ranking and documenting how shortlisting works.

• Terms of use of job-posting platforms (prohibiting unauthorized automated scraping).

• Ownership of résumés and intellectual property rights, with a commitment to writing all code from scratch and not copying any protected asset.

Technical:

• Email inbox deliverability and spam protection.

• WhatsApp integration and the limitations of its APIs and changing policies.

• Performance scaling with growing large candidate databases and fast search.

• Data security, encryption, backup, and service continuity.
