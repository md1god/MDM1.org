Maram — Complete Project Plan

Learn in small steps, remember for a lifetime.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a flashcard review app based on spaced repetition

1) Target audience

• Students (secondary and university) memorizing courses and definitions.

• Language and profession learners (medicine, law, technology) who need long-term retention.

• People preparing for accreditation and licensing exams.

• Self-directed learners building a personal knowledge library from their notes.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Adjustable spaced repetition engine (review intervals are computed from your actual performance, not a fixed schedule).

• One-step card generation from text, PDF files, or voice notes, with drag-and-drop editing.

• A "focus session" mode with a short duration and a progress bar showing only what remains for today.

• Offline-first priority: everything works locally; sync is optional and encrypted.

• A stats dashboard that answers one question: what will you forget in a week if you don't review it now?

• A modern, eye-friendly design with full Arabic support and RTL from the first line of code.

3) Scope of the first version (8 tasks maximum)

1. Build the spaced repetition engine with a modified, customizable SM-2 algorithm.

2. Card creation and editing (question-answer, multiple choice, image cards with occlusion).

3. Daily review screen with 4-level difficulty rating and instant rescheduling.

4. Initial card generation from pasted text with simple linguistic rules (no expensive external services).

5. CSV and Markdown import/export, and full local backup.

6. Optional cloud sync via a single account with encryption and simple conflict resolution.

7. Stats: day streak, recall rate, and an upcoming "forgetting risk" card.

8. Native Android app + PWA web version for early testing.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free to start: unlimited reviews, up to 500 cards, full export — no ads.

• Pro subscription: $4.9/month or $34/year — unlimited cloud, advanced card generation, detailed stats, audio cards.

• Lifetime purchase: $69 one-time (attracts users skeptical of subscriptions).

• Secondary income source: ready-made deck templates (languages, exams) at symbolic prices.

5) Global rollout plan using free channels

• Google Play store (ASO: keywords "spaced repetition", "flashcards" + localized pages in multiple languages).

• Short-form content on YouTube, TikTok, and Reels: 30-second tutorials on review techniques.

• Reddit, Discord, and Telegram communities around languages and exams, with a free trial version.

• SEO blog answering review and memorization questions in more than 10 languages.

• Partnerships with university clubs, study groups, and a referral plan (one free Pro month per friend).

• Launch on Product Hunt and Hacker News, and a micro-influencer program in exchange (free use).

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: secure the name and trademark before launch; comply with GDPR and learner data privacy (sensitive health/study data); avoid any claims of guaranteed academic results; minors' data (COPPA); licensing of any third-party training content.

• Technical: local data loss on updates (solution: always-on automatic export); sync conflicts; tuning the scheduling algorithm so it neither exhausts nor neglects the user; battery drain from background sync; app store policy changes; RTL and Arabic font support across all screens.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Maram

Tagline: Learn in small steps, remember for a lifetime.
