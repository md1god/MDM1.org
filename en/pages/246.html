Sitr — Complete Project Plan

Scan your app in one minute, and protect your keys before someone else finds them.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the go-to-market plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

An original product plan: a leak and misconfiguration scanner for apps built with AI

1) Target Audience

• "vibe coding" makers: solo founders and small teams building apps with AI and no-code and shipping them without a security background.

• Supabase / Firebase / Vercel / Netlify users who manage their own keys and data.

• Agencies and freelancers who deliver apps to clients and need a quick scan before handoff.

• Accelerators and coding schools that need a safe auditing tool for their students.

2) Differentiators (Why choose our product)

• Zero setup: just paste a URL or connect a repository, with no installation or configuration.

• Human language: a report that explains the risk, its impact, and the fix step in plain, simple language, not raw jargon.

• Built-in comprehensive scan: leaked secrets + missing RLS + open endpoints in a single report.

• Privacy first: we do not store keys or code; transient analysis and automatic deletion.

• Copy-ready fixes: every issue comes with a ready fix snippet (env, RLS policy, CORS config).

• Free to start, then pay as needed, with no credit card.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Published URL scan: headers, exposed sensitive files, common paths, development mode.

2. Connect a public GitHub repository via OAuth and read files without a full clone.

3. Secret detection: regex patterns + randomness scoring + provider key lists (OpenAI, Stripe, Supabase…).

4. Supabase audit: verify RLS is enabled and detect tables exposed via endpoints.

5. Test endpoints without authentication and report any data returned.

6. Illustrated report with ranked severity (critical/high/medium) and copy-ready fix steps.

7. Shareable report link with the project owner/client.

8. Technical privacy policy: no storage of inputs, immediate automatic deletion, respect for rate limits.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: one scan per month, basic report, URL scan only.

• Pro (monthly/yearly): unlimited scans, repository scans, shareable reports, periodic alerts.

• Team/Agency: multiple seats, batch scans for several clients, white label, PDF reports.

• Add-ons and B2B: CI/CD integration, scheduled scans, continuous monitoring, Enterprise plan.

• Additional revenue: referral commission from hosting/security providers, and a paid manual review service.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• GitHub Marketplace + an open-source repository for the detection engine to attract trust and contributions.

• Product Hunt / Hacker News / Reddit (r/webdev, r/Supabase, r/nocode) with an honest technical launch.

• Direct integrations with Vercel, Netlify, and Supabase in their dashboards and add-on stores.

• English SEO content focused on "Is my app leaking keys?" and real-world case studies.

• Short videos (YouTube Shorts / TikTok) showing a real leak discovered in one minute.

• Discord/Slack communities for no-code makers, offering a free scan as a value hook.

• Ambassador/student program in accelerators and coding schools in exchange for free credit.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Active scanning being considered "unauthorized access": we require the user to confirm ownership of the domain/repository (verification via DNS or a verification file or OAuth).

• Data privacy: no key storage, GDPR compliance, and EU data separation.

• Liability for "false negatives": a clear disclaimer that the report is not a complete security guarantee.

• Abuse: strict limits, prevention of scanning non-owned entities, and automatic banning.

Technical:

• False positives/negatives: continuous updating of detection rules and provider lists.

• Evasion and obfuscation (encrypted/split keys): progressive contextual analysis.

• Scalability, scan speed, and infrastructure cost: distributed queue processing and caching.

• API limits and rate limiting for providers and scanned sites.

• Protecting the tool itself from reverse leakage of users' keys during processing.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Sitr (سِتْر)

Tagline: Scan your app in one minute, and protect your keys before someone else finds them.
