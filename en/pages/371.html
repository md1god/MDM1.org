OrbitForge — Full Project Plan

Build extensions that achieve greater success with simpler steps.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, launch plan, and risks. The project is still being built and has not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Indie Builders: those who want to build a new extension without large investment.

• Startups and digital project owners: looking for quick opportunities to validate the idea before starting development.

• Development and marketing agencies: need to guide their clients toward areas with real opportunities.

• Global opportunity seekers: interested in tracking trends by country, language, or market.

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• Focus on actionable opportunities: instead of showing all data, the product highlights "opportunity spots" (areas with low competition and clear interest).

• Insights by local market: supports filtering data by countries and languages, helping target less crowded markets.

• Simple time-based analysis: tracks weekly changes (up/down) instead of only static numbers, to avoid outdated decisions.

• Ease of use: a clear beginner-oriented interface with explanations of analytics terms to lower the knowledge barrier.

• Free tier to start: completely free for the majority, with clear added value only for advanced users who need it.

• Methodology transparency: clearly shows data sources and calculation methods, building user trust.

3) Scope of the First Version (8 Tasks Max)

· # · Task · Goal ·

· 1 · Public data collection · Fetch publicly available extension data from official sources only, respecting usage limits. ·

· 2 · Simple data model · Design a schema focused on core metrics (rating, user count, category, last update, price/revenue model). ·

· 3 · Trend detection · Calculate simple opportunity indicators (such as weekly growth rate, active updates ratio, competition intensity). ·

· 4 · Smart filtering · Add filters by category, country, language, and opportunity range. ·

· 5 · Web dashboard · Build a lightweight web interface that displays opportunities clearly with fast browsing. ·

· 6 · Explanatory view · Add short texts explaining each metric and what it means in practice. ·

· 7 · Limited export · Provide CSV export of the opportunities list for free users with a reasonable limit. ·

· 8 · Trial launch · Deploy a usable version with a simple landing page to measure interest. ·

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Completely free to start: access to basic weekly opportunities, filters, and results viewing at no cost.

• Simple monthly subscription (optional): for advanced users such as development agencies. Includes: older archive (more than 4 weeks), broader export, custom opportunity alerts, and deeper insights.

• Moderate pricing: starts low to encourage beginners, and increases gradually only as value grows.

• No ads: focused on product value rather than ad revenue.

• Sustainable model: revenue from subscriptions only, while keeping the majority free.

5) Global Distribution Plan Using Free Channels

· Channel · How to Use · Notes ·

· Search engine optimization (SEO) · Publish short articles about "extension building opportunities" by market (example: opportunities in productivity, or opportunities for a specific language). · Long-term, attracts an audience actively looking for the solution. ·

· Developer forums · Provide valuable contributions in developer communities while avoiding direct ads, and answer real questions. · Builds trust slowly but strongly. ·

• Free product directories · List the product in free developer tools directories with a clear description of the core benefit. · Quick reach with no cost. ·

· Short educational content · Share practical tips on choosing a successful extension idea on suitable platforms. · Increases awareness and encourages returning to the product. ·

• Word-of-mouth recommendations · Encourage satisfied users to share the product with their peers. · The strongest growth channel in the long run. ·
