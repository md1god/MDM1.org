Hujja — Full Project Plan

Automatically collect compliance evidence from your cloud tools and hand your auditor a ready package in weeks.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target audience

• Primary: B2B SaaS startups and digital services companies (10–250 employees) at stages from post-seed to pre-Series C, whose enterprise clients have asked them for SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certification.

• Buyer/user: founder and CEO (the economic buyer), CTO/engineering lead (the technical owner), and GRC or security officer (the day-to-day owner).

• Professionals: independent SOC 2 auditors, ISO 27001 consultants, and small outsourcing offices that manage several clients and need a single customer portal.

• Priority sectors: SaaS, fintech, health-tech (HIPAA), e-commerce, AI as a service.

• Geography: the Gulf, Egypt, and Jordan first (Arabic policies, RTL, regional data hosting), then Europe and North America.

2) Differentiation points (why we choose our product)

• Tamper-evident evidence: every piece of evidence is stored with a hash fingerprint, timestamp, and clear source, so the auditor verifies its authenticity without requesting screenshots.

• Open control map: an engine that maps SOC 2 (TSC) controls to ISO 27001:2022 Annex A, so a single piece of evidence serves several frameworks without duplicate collection.

• Read-only permissions: agents read from AWS/GitHub/Okta without modifying any configuration, reducing operational risk for the client.

• Independent auditor room: a limited-permission portal instead of shared folders and passwords, with evidence request templates and response tracking — measurably reducing audit time.

• Drift detection: periodic comparison between the policy text and the actual state, with alerts before the audit rather than discovering gaps during it.

• Bilingual (Arabic/English) policy generator with templates written from scratch and linked to control identifiers instead of copying protected framework texts.

• Open-core from the ground up: CLI, a GitHub Actions Action, a Terraform Provider, and a self-hostable core, so it enters the developer's workflow.

• A genuine free tier with no card required, and generous limits on evidence by number of connectors.

3) Scope of the first version (max 8 tasks)

1. Accounts and workspaces: multi-tenant workspaces (each client gets an independent workspace), roles, team invitations, an audit log for every action.

2. Policy library: draft policy generator for SOC 2 Type I/II and ISO 27001 (access, identity management, encryption, incident response, backup, vendors), Arabic/English texts, DOCX/PDF export.

3. Control mapping engine: cross-framework mapping, assignment of an owner per control, statuses (implemented/missing/failing/not applicable) with a mandatory review log for every human change.

4. Connectors: GitHub, Google Workspace, AWS, Azure, Cloudflare, Okta — read-only via OAuth/limited roles, with periodic sync and retries.

5. Evidence collection: API snapshots + hash + timestamp + immutable (WORM) storage, with an evidence log filterable by control and framework.

6. Monitoring and alerts: scheduled rescans, configuration and policy drift detection, an "audit readiness" dashboard and an evidence coverage ratio.

7. Auditor room: secure read-only links, question and evidence request templates, comments and follow-up threads, reminder notifications.

8. Audit package export + integrations: ZIP/PDF export (audit scope, control matrix, indexed evidence, exception logs), API and CLI and Terraform, and a managed hosting plan later.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free (Starter): up to 3 users, one framework (SOC 2 Type I), two connectors, up to 50 evidence items, full policy generator, PDF export — no card required.

• Team — $29/user/month (annual $24): all frameworks, all connectors, evidence log, drift alerts, dedicated server.

• Firm — $79/user/month: for audit and consulting offices: multi-client workspace, question templates, white-label reports.

• Enterprise / self-hosting: custom pricing, with the core remaining free and open source while managed hosting and support are paid.

• Additional revenue: extra fees for advanced connectors or number of clients on the Firm plan, 20% discount for annual payment, a marketplace for integrations (70/30 revenue share), and a certified partner network (training, configuration reviews, bundle setup) paid by commission.
