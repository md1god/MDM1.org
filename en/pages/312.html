رَصْد — Full Project Plan

Know your service outage before your customer does.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target audience

• DevOps and SRE teams in small and medium companies (5–50 engineers) that do not want a dedicated monitoring team.

• Independent developers and SaaS product builders who need a public status page for customers without high cost.

• Development agencies managing dozens of client projects and needing one dashboard for all services.

• On-call teams looking for accurate alerts via instant channels.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Fast self-hosted setup with a free tier to start: one config file and one Docker container, running within minutes with no mandatory account signup.

• Alert intelligence: reduce noise by grouping repeated alerts, detecting degradation trends before an outage, and smart silence windows.

• Checks deeper than availability: verify page content, network flow, TLS certificates, SQL errors, and custom API paths with sequential steps.

• Customer-ready status page: modern templates, free subdomain, automatic incident log, and update subscriptions for users.

• Privacy first: outbound connections go through servers in multiple regions, and we do not store sensitive check content.

• Instant integrations: Slack, Telegram, Discord, WhatsApp, PagerDuty, and custom Webhooks.

3) First release scope (8 tasks maximum)

1. Core check engine: HTTP/HTTPS, TCP, DNS, TLS protocol with configurable interval and timeout.

2. Configuration read from a single YAML file and state management via simple SQLite.

3. Alert engine with conditional rules (state change only, retry after N failures) and at least one channel (Email + Webhook).

4. Single control panel showing service status and check history with simple time charts.

5. Public status page deployable on a free subdomain with integration keys hidden.

6. Manual incident logging with notes and resolution time.

7. One Docker container and ready docker-compose for local or cloud deployment.

8. Concise Arabic and English documentation with 5 ready configuration examples.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free to start: unlimited self-hosted for 25 services and one user — stays open forever.

• Pro SaaS — $19/month: managed hosting, unlimited services, private subdomain, and 3 alert routes.

• Teams — $59/month: 10 members, permissions, status page with custom domain, SLA and reports, and advanced integrations.

• Company — $199/month: out-of-network monitor, voice/phone alerts, SSO integration, and priority support.

• Additional revenue stream: private hosting support (annual licence) and Synthetics services as a paid add-on.

5) Global rollout plan with free channels

• Publish an open-source repository for the core version with a clear README and GIF demos.

• Presence on DevOps forums: Hacker News (Show HN), Reddit, Linux forums, and technical Telegram groups.

• Arabic and English content articles: "How to build a status page for your product in 10 minutes" and comparisons with hosted alternatives.

• Ready-made templates: YAML presets for monitoring WordPress, APIs, Minecraft games, and common databases.

• Participation in virtual community conferences and short educational YouTube clips.

• User referral program: one free month for each friend who subscribes.

• Regular releases with a public changelog to build trust.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: checking customer domains may be considered unauthorized monitoring — document explicit consent in the terms. Compliance with GDPR when storing visit data for status pages.

• Technical: double latency and false positives due to the check network itself — solved with multiple verification regions. Cloud costs for repeated checks — smart limit configuration. Protecting keys and Webhooks from leakage.

• Security: prevent the server from being used to check internal addresses (SSRF) by blocking private ranges.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Rasd

Tagline: Know your service outage before your customer does.
