Hams — Full Project Plan

Communicate quietly… without the noise.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, monetization model, rollout plan, and risks. The project is under development and has not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Mid-core Slack/WhatsApp-style users: aged 18–40, with accounts on major photo networks, but tired of Reels, ads, and endless scrolling.

• Those with digital anxiety and people interested in mental health (digital minimalism, detox).

• Small groups: family, close friends, small teams, teachers — anyone who just wants "photos, stories, and conversation."

• Developers and open-source communities looking for a lightweight, updatable alternative with a clear privacy policy.

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• No ranking algorithm, no Reels, no public explore page: simple chronological order for direct follows only.

• A "quiet room" instead of an endless feed: the interface opens on messages and nearby stories, not a content wall.

• A "clear hour" toggle: temporary pause of non-essential notifications and public counters.

• Full transparency: open source, updates installable manually or via an external store, no ad tracking.

• Privacy by default: app lock, auto-disappearing media, fine-grained control over who sees each story.

• Light on the device and battery, with a mode for older devices.

3) First Release Scope (Max 8 Tasks)

1. Build the core: login, profile, direct following between users.

2. Conversations: text and media messages, small groups up to 50 members.

3. Stories: photo/short video posts with follower circles and timed expiry.

4. Simple chronological feed: image posts from followed users only, no algorithmic filtering.

5. Configurable notifications system + "Clear Hour" mode.

6. Privacy: app lock, block/hide, media access settings.

7. UI: dark mode, customizable font size, calm colors, Arabic/English support from day one.

8. Distribution: Android (APK) package + lightweight app repository, with support pages and Arabic/English documentation.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Completely free to start: all core features available at no cost (free-to-start).

• Optional "Sakin+" subscription (around $3–4/month or $25/year): encrypted backup, advanced story templates, extra customizations, exclusive colors.

• Voluntary donations via BuyMeACoffee/Patreon from supporting users.

• Future: symbolic-price plans for teams and large groups, while keeping the individual plan free.

• No ads and no data selling, ever — this is part of the brand.

5) Global Rollout Plan Using Free Channels

1. Phased launch: closed beta via Discord/Reddit, then a public release.

2. Reddit, Mastodon, and Telegram: posting in privacy, mental health, and digital minimalism communities.

3. YouTube/TikTok (short video about the problem): before/after comparisons of "a day without Reels."

4. Blog and SEO: Arabic/English articles about digital addiction and the alternative.

5. GitHub: clear Readme, screenshots, beginner-friendly contributions, Hacktoberfest.

6. Open-source tech press: weekly outreach newsletter to sites and tech mailing lists.

7. Small partnerships: Productivity and Wellness influencers in exchange for honest usage.

8. Google Play store (if possible) + F-Droid/Droid-ify store for advanced users.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal: copyright on logos/images; risks of sharing download links outside Google Play; local data regulations (GDPR/PDPL) when storing accounts; app store content policies.

• Product: difficulty acquiring users from an existing giant platform; risk of stores changing their policies.

• Technical: protecting messages and media (encryption in transit and at rest), anti-harassment and anti-spam measures, story abuse (reports), background service and notification stability across multiple Android versions, server hosting costs and limits.

• Open source: possibility of modified copies or code misuse; addressed with a clear license and a review team.

7) Proposed Name and Tagline

Proposed name: Hams (هَمْس)

Tagline: Communicate quietly… without the noise.
