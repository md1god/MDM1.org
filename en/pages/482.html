Yusr — Full Project Plan

Your personal WhatsApp assistant gets your tasks done, and leaves the decision to you.

Full execution plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue, growth, risks. Not launched.

Product: Personal assistant in chat apps

Daily life means juggling email, calendar, travel, subscriptions, payments. Solution: an always-on WhatsApp or SMS assistant, no new app, completing multi-step tasks with approval and transparency.

1) Target audience

• Busy professionals 25–45 overwhelmed by apps and admin.

• Freelancers and small businesses needing low-cost help.

• Expats and multinational families with accounts and subscriptions across countries.

• Arabic speakers preferring Modern Standard Arabic or dialects, plus English.

• Users avoiding password sharing/extra apps; privacy matters.

2) Differentiation

• Existing chats: no separate number or app; verified WhatsApp/SMS contact.

• Approval first: sensitive actions shown before execution; reversible log.

• No passwords: OAuth, passkeys, official interfaces; revoke anytime.

• User-owned encrypted memory: export or delete; no lock-in.

• Transparent finances: no hidden booking margins; commissions disclosed.

• Arabic first: strong Arabic and dialect understanding, plus English.

• Human escalation: complex or high-risk requests go to a person.

• Pay per action: pay for completed work, not promises.

3) First-version scope (8 tasks max)

1. Calendar: create, edit, cancel appointments; detect conflicts; remind.

2. Email: summarize inbox, prioritize important mail, draft replies after approval.

3. Travel: compare flights and hotels; book via official partners after confirmation.

4. Subscriptions: find recurring charges, remind, cancel via official links/interfaces.

5. Bills: reminders and payment links; no card storage or held funds.

6. Orders: track shipments, alert delays, start returns.

7. Search: find products or services; shortlist features and prices.

8. Compound tasks: run steps like “arrange dinner, send invitations,” approving sensitive steps.

4) Pricing and revenue

• Free: 25 actions/month, one calendar and email, summaries/reminders, no card.

• Yusr Plus: $8/month or $80/year, 250 actions, all integrations, priority.

• Yusr Pro: $18/month, 1,000 actions under fair use, family of three, human escalation.

• Action packs: $4 for 50 actions, no subscription.

• Revenue: subscriptions, packs, disclosed travel/subscription commissions, team plans later.

• Principle: free start, instant cancellation, no hidden fees, no held funds.

5) Global growth via free channels

• In-chat virality: invite link and QR code; free actions per friend who joins.

• Programmatic SEO: Arabic and English pages for “how do I cancel a subscription” and “how do I plan a trip on a specific budget.”

• Short content: TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels showing a task done in one message.

• Communities: expat, student, traveler groups on WhatsApp, Telegram, Reddit; useful content, no spam.

• Partnerships: coworking spaces, universities, freelancer groups, ambassador programs for free actions.

• Public launch: Product Hunt, X, LinkedIn; waitlist and invite codes control cost.

• No paid ads initially: invites, content, partnerships until retention is proven.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Comply with GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and Saudi, UAE, Egyptian rules; processing agreements and transparency.

• Comply with WhatsApp Business Platform policies: consent, templates, no spam.

• Act as a travel or subscription intermediary per country; no licensed services without a licensed partner.

• Hold no funds; use a licensed payment provider; no financial advice.

• Define liability for wrong bookings/cancellations, with compensation and escalation.

• Protect vulnerable groups: age 18+, block unauthorized use.

Technical:

• Hallucination or wrong actions: restrict tools, double verification, approval gates, reversibility.

• Prompt injection via email/messages: isolate untrusted content; deny execution rights.

• Duplicate actions or double booking: idempotency keys, spending limits, caps.

• Access-token leaks: KMS encryption, rotation, least privilege.

• Partner API limits/outages: queues, smart retries, manual fallbacks.

• Call/message costs: route models by complexity; fair-use caps.

• Outages or lost context: persist state, apologize clearly, human handoff.

• Security: audit logs, penetration tests, incident response, instant “kill switch.”

7) Name and tagline

Proposed name: Yusr (يُسر)

Tagline: Your personal WhatsApp assistant gets your tasks done, and leaves the decision to you.
