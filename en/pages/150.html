Basira — Complete Project Plan

Turn your data into clear decisions in minutes, without technical expertise.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

Data Analytics Product Plan

1) Target Audience

• Small and medium-sized companies that lack dedicated data analysts.

• Product and operations teams that need fast reports without relying on engineers.

• Application developers (SaaS) who want to embed analytics dashboards within their products.

• Non-profit and educational organizations with limited budgets seeking an open-source solution.

2) Differentiators (Why choose our product)

• Setup in minutes, not weeks: connect to the database and generate the first dashboard via a smart wizard, without mandatory SQL writing.

• A built-in smart assistant that turns a question in Arabic or English into an instant report and chart.

• Lightweight embedding (Embeddable) via a single component and simple code, with full control over branding and colors.

• Works locally and online (Local-first): it can run entirely on the user's device to ensure privacy.

• No heavy data-model complexity: automatic discovery of tables and relationships.

• Optimized query speed via a smart caching layer (Cache) for repeated data.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. A connection engine for PostgreSQL, MySQL, SQLite, and CSV databases.

2. Building dashboards with drag and drop (Drag & Drop).

3. A generator from natural language questions to queries (Natural Language to Query).

4. Basic chart types: line, bar, pie, table, numeric indicator.

5. Interactive filters (Interactive Filters) at the dashboard level.

6. Sharing dashboards via a public link or specific permissions.

7. Embedding dashboards via a simple iframe/SDK component.

8. Exporting reports to PDF and CSV and scheduling their delivery by email.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free plan (Free forever): full self-hosting, unlimited data, one user, no paid support.

• Teams plan (Teams): monthly subscription per additional user + advanced permissions + report scheduling.

• Embedded plan (Embedded): usage-based pricing (number of dashboards or monthly views) for companies that embed it in their products.

• Paid services: managed cloud hosting, training, and on-demand feature customization.

• Principle: the core code is open source, and revenue comes from scaling, support, and hosting, not from locking vital features.

5) Global rollout plan with free channels

• GitHub + open-source community: releasing the code publicly to attract contributors.

• Educational content: lessons on YouTube and articles on Dev.to and Medium about easy data analysis.

• Product launch (Product Hunt / Hacker News): a community launch to gain the first thousands of users.

• Integration with developer platforms: publishing packages on npm/Docker Hub with clear documentation.

• Community programs: free "ready-made templates" shared by users to attract new domains.

• Collaborative translation: multilingual support via volunteers to cover new markets.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: the open-source license must be compatible with commercial use, and the privacy policy must be clear when processing customer data (especially data protection laws).

• Legal: protecting the trademark and the new name, and avoiding any similarity with existing products.

• Technical: query performance on massive data may slow the system; the solution is caching and indexing.

• Technical: accuracy of converting natural language into queries; the solution is showing the result for review before execution.

• Technical: security of connections to production databases and encryption of credentials.

• Technical: difficulty scaling (Scaling) compared to major cloud solutions.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Basira

Tagline: Turn your data into clear decisions in minutes, without technical expertise.
