Synqora — Full Project Plan

Gather everything about your customers in one place.. simple, smart, and free to start.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout plan, and risks. The project is being built and has not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Small and medium sales teams: especially teams that lack the budget for large, complex customer management systems.

• Agencies: such as marketing, design, or development agencies that handle multiple clients at once.

• Support and service teams: need to track customer communication and resolve their issues quickly.

• Entrepreneurs: working alone or with a small team, who need a simple tool instead of using several separate tools.

The problem the product solves: teams' tools are scattered across email, conversations, tasks, documents, and the customer system, which makes them lose work context and slows down decision-making.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Focus on context: connecting every element (email, conversation, document, task, customer) together, with a shared smart memory for the team, to automatically display the full context when needed.

• Design simplicity: a lightweight, fast user experience, with keyboard navigation, to reduce learning and resource consumption.

• Privacy first: fine-grained control over access permissions, with the ability to keep sensitive data local if teams prefer.

• Free plan to start: no upfront payment, enabling the smallest teams to use it immediately.

• Team-oriented intelligence: a smart assistant that leverages the team's shared memory and suggests replies or tasks based on the customer context, rather than providing generic answers.

3) Scope of the First Release (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Simple customer database: create and manage companies and people with custom fields.

2. Embedded conversations: a chat room for each project or client with support for the @mention feature to link elements.

3. Basic email: IMAP support to connect at least one mailbox.

4. Task management: simple task lists with a kanban view.

5. Internal documents: a Markdown editor for writing shared notes.

6. Element linking: link any task, document, email, or conversation to a specific customer or project.

7. Shared team memory: building automatic context for each project so the smart assistant can leverage it.

8. Responsive web app: an initial version that works on major browsers.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free: up to 3 users, with all first-release features, and suitable storage space to get started.

• Pro: $5 per user per month, including: additional storage space, greater smart assistant capacity, and a longer team context history.

• Revenue model: based on converting growing teams from the free plan to the pro plan when they need more capacity. No charges for core features.

5) Global Rollout Plan Using Free Channels

• Search engine optimization (SEO): creating a tutorial guide in simple language for each feature, in the following languages: Arabic, English, Spanish, Turkish, and Portuguese.

• Tech and product communities: sharing value in forums such as IndieHackers, Dev.to, and Reddit in sections for small teams and entrepreneurs.

• Ready-made templates: providing ready project templates (such as an agency template or a support team template) that teams can use for free, to attract organic visits.

• Bite-sized educational content: short videos or posts explaining how to solve the fragmentation problem for small teams, and publishing them across free platforms.

• Organic referrals: designing an experience that makes it easy for a team to invite others without expensive incentives, focusing on product quality as the strongest growth channel.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal risks:

• Data privacy: compliance with GDPR and CCPA according to the users of each region.

• Terms of service and privacy policy: the need to write them clearly before the global launch.

• Email connection: ensuring compliance with email providers' (IMAP) policies and not storing unnecessary data.

Technical risks:

• AI cost: managing the smart assistant's consumption carefully (limits in the free plan, and smart use of prompts).

• Email and conversation sync: dealing with IMAP limits while maintaining app performance.

• Shared team memory: organizing context to avoid data bloat while maintaining suggestion accuracy.

• Security: encrypting data in transit and at rest, and precisely verifying each user's permissions.
