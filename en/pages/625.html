Vetova — Complete Project Plan

Automated vaccination and follow-up reminders for your veterinary clinic via WhatsApp, with no client app.

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, launch plan, and risks. Project is under construction and not yet launched.

1) Target audience

• Small independent veterinary clinics (1–3 vets) without an integrated clinic management system.

• Mobile vets and home-visit practices relying on phone and WhatsApp.

• Pet grooming salons and pet food stores offering vaccination and seasonal immunization.

• First markets: Middle East, Southeast Asia, Latin America, Eastern Europe.

• Problem: vaccination appointments and recurring doses are forgotten, costing clinics repeat visits and trust.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• WhatsApp first: pet owners need no app or login; they receive and reply to reminders.

• No hardware: no cage QR codes or sensors; works from a phone or computer browser.

• Smart recurring reminders: vaccinations, deworming, chronic medications, post-surgery follow-up.

• Digital pet card: public link and collar QR code showing vaccinations and upcoming appointments.

• Revenue recovery dashboard: shows appointments that would have been lost and how reminders rebooked them.

• Pricing by active animals, not staff, with a genuinely usable free version.

• Language and RTL support and full data export with no lock-in.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Create clinic account, user roles, and import clients/animals from CSV/Excel.

2. Pet and owner record: vaccinations, medications, allergies, recurring appointments.

3. Automatic WhatsApp Cloud API reminders with SMS fallback and Arabic/English templates.

4. Public pet card with link and QR code showing vaccinations and next appointment.

5. Smart recall list with one-click rebooking.

6. Reply inbox: confirm, postpone, cancel, manage opt-in/opt-out.

7. Dashboard: due soon, no-shows, estimated recovered revenue.

8. Data export, basic audit log, Arabic/English RTL interface.

4) Pricing and profit model

• Free forever (Solo): 1 user, 100 active animals, 100 WhatsApp messages/month.

• Clinic – $19/month: 3 users, 1,000 animals, 1,000 messages, custom templates.

• Growth – $39/month: unlimited animals, 8 users, 3,000 messages, advanced reports/export.

• Add-on messages: $5 per 500, with a small margin over provider cost.

• Optional one-time migration and training: $49.

• Revenue: subscriptions + message margin + migration + future integrations. 20% off annual.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Local-language SEO pages: "dog vaccination reminder," "cat vaccination reminder template," free downloadable vaccination schedules.

• WhatsApp, Facebook, LinkedIn veterinary groups; share real use cases with owners' permission.

• Short YouTube/TikTok videos showing reminder creation in under a minute.

• Veterinary college partnerships, student ambassadors, free Zoom demos.

• Product Hunt, Reddit, IndieHackers, vet forums.

• Viral marketing via public pet card and "Created by" footer.

• Referral: one free month per active referred clinic.

• Free standalone "Vaccination Schedule Calculator" to drive sign-ups.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Pet owner data is personal data; comply with GDPR and local PDPL, prove consent, and provide a clear deletion policy.

• WhatsApp Business API: approved templates, prior consent, no promotional messages without permission.

• Clear medical disclaimer: reminder/management tool, not diagnosis or medication advice.

• "Missed reminder" liability: terms limit liability; final confirmation is the clinic's responsibility.

• Cross-border transfer/storage: choose a provider with clear hosting and a data processing agreement.

Technical:

• WhatsApp template approval delays/rejections and sending limits.

• Poor phone number quality and high undelivered rates.

• Duplicate import records and varied name/number formats.

• Scheduling across time zones and daylight saving time.

• Poor connectivity at some clinics; short offline operation needed.

• Account security: encryption in transit/at rest, 2FA, granular permissions.

• Single-provider dependence; easy export/exit path to reduce lock-in.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Vetova

Tagline: Automated vaccination and follow-up reminders for your veterinary clinic via WhatsApp, with no client app.
