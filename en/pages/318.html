PulseKit — Full Project Plan

Your health data in your app, in one hour.

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the rollout plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: an original alternative to the health-fitness data reading package in React Native apps

1) Target audience

• React Native app developers who need to read fitness data (steps, sleep, heart rate, calories) from iOS and Android devices.

• Healthcare and fitness companies (workout apps, corporate wellness programs) that require high compliance and privacy.

• Freelancers and MVP teams who want to integrate health data quickly without a high upfront cost.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Two platforms from a single path: iOS HealthKit and Android Health Connect through one TypeScript interface, instead of competing products limited to a single platform.

• Declared, clear permissions: the user sees why each data type is requested before approving, which raises acceptance and reduces store rejections.

• Offline-first mode: smart sync with conflict resolution and backfilling of missing data when the network returns.

• Testability: a built-in Mock so the developer can write tests without a real device.

• Built-in analytics tools: ready dashboards for sleep and activity data with threshold alerts — added value that a mere "data bridge" does not offer.

• Privacy by design: local encryption, full deletion with one tap, and compliance documentation (GDPR/HIPAA-ready).

3) Scope of the first version (8 tasks maximum)

1. A single TypeScript library to request permissions and read/write data on iOS HealthKit.

2. Equivalent support via Android Health Connect with the same API.

3. A local sync layer with caching and simple conflict resolution.

4. A Mock system for automated tests + ready-to-run examples (templates).

5. Ready UI components (activity card, sleep chart) that are customizable.

6. A free web dashboard for monitoring (Analytics).

7. Comprehensive documentation + learning wall (Quickstart in 15 minutes).

8. Publishing to npm and the stores with a first free tier.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free to start: individual use, open-source projects, and up to 1,000 monthly active users.

• Pro — $29/month: cloud sync, advanced analytics, alerts, priority support.

• Business — $149/month: compliance, SSO, SLA, enterprise reports.

• Additional revenue: paid integration consultations and ready-made app templates.

5) Global rollout plan using free channels

• SEO articles answering common questions: "integrating health data in React Native", "the difference between HealthKit and Health Connect".

• A repository on GitHub with practical examples + GIF images of the final result.

• Sharing in communities: Reddit (r/reactnative), Hacker News Show HN, Dev.to and Stack Overflow forums.

• Short videos on YouTube and X/Twitter showing the integration in minutes.

• Partnerships for content creation with app bloggers and developer clubs in Arabic and English.

• A small weekly newsletter (Email) on the state of health sync and privacy.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: health data is sensitive; Apple/Google terms and store policies must be followed, and a privacy policy and explicit disclosure must be provided. Review rules may change suddenly and delay publishing.

• Technical: system changes between iOS/Android releases, variability across watch and wearable devices, and the difficulty of testing every case.

• Competitive: difficulty distinguishing ourselves from free alternatives; we address this through our commitment to privacy and the developer experience.

• Operational: reliance on external platforms could temporarily disrupt sync.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: PulseKit

Tagline: Your health data in your app, in one hour.
