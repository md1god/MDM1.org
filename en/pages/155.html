Naseej — Complete Project Plan

Weave your intelligent agents' workflows and run them on your own infrastructure with full freedom and no execution limits.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a workflow automation platform for intelligent agents (self-hosted)

1) Target audience

• Platform/DevOps and software engineering teams at small and medium-sized companies.

• AI engineers building agents, RAG, and multi-step systems.

• Independent developers (indie hackers) and automation agencies.

• Institutions with privacy and regulatory requirements (financial, healthcare, government) that need on-premises operation.

• Entities that reject per-execution pricing and prefer to own their infrastructure.

2) Differentiators (why our product)

• Agent-native: ready-made contracts for agents, tools, memory, MCP, and RAG — not just a Webhooks runner.

• Robust and deterministic execution: automatic resume after interruption, replay, and time-travel debugging.

• Local-first: support for Ollama/vLLM and local vector databases without data leaving the premises.

• Native observability: per-step tracing, cost and latency accounting, OpenTelemetry export, and evaluation and guardrail contracts.

• Free for self-hosting with no execution limits, with a transparent base license and one-command deployment.

• Extensibility via a simple SDK for writing custom contracts in JavaScript/Python.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Visual workflow editor (Canvas) that saves flows as version-controllable YAML/JSON files (Git).

2. Core contracts: LLM, HTTP, Code, condition, loop, delay, Webhook, Cron.

3. Agent contracts: tools (tool calling), conversation memory, RAG vector store, MCP.

4. Durable execution engine with resume after interruption and deterministic replay.

5. Observability interface: step log, cost, latency, and OpenTelemetry export.

6. Support for multiple models: OpenAI-compatible, Anthropic, Ollama, vLLM.

7. One-click deployment via Docker Compose + secrets management and basic authentication.

8. REST API + Webhooks + a simple SDK (JavaScript/Python) for custom contracts.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free self-hosting: unlimited execution, an open base license, and separately commercialized enterprise modules.

• Cloud Starter: a free tier up to a monthly usage limit, then a subscription.

• Pro/Team: per seat or per execution + higher limits and integrations.

• Enterprise: SSO/SAML, RBAC, audit logs, air-gapped operation, SLA support, commercial hosting license.

• Additional revenue: contract/template marketplace commission, managed hosting, training, and consulting.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Open source on GitHub with a clear license and search-engine-optimized (SEO) documentation.

• Community on Discord/Matrix with incentives for external contributors.

• Educational content: YouTube, Dev.to, a technical blog, and tutorials in a "build in public" style.

• Launches on Hacker News, Product Hunt, and communities such as r/selfhosted, r/LocalLLaMA, and r/AIAgents.

• A gallery of ready-made templates and integration with ecosystem guides (MCP, LangChain, OpenAI community).

• University partnerships, hackathons, conference speakers, and an ambassadors program.

• A transparent comparison page and a managed trial version for a quick experience.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: license compliance, conformity with LLM model terms, data protection and geographic residency (GDPR), trademark or patent disputes.

• Technical: self-hosting security and secret leakage, code execution isolation (sandboxing), prompt injection and supply-chain attacks via contracts, scalability, version migration, and the maintenance cost of integrations amid competition from major players.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Naseej (نسيج)

Marketing tagline: Weave your intelligent agents' workflows and run them on your own infrastructure with full freedom and no execution limits.
