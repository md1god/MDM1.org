Platara — Complete Project Plan

From your dish ingredients to an appetizing, sales-driven menu in under a minute.

Complete execution plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, business model, launch, risks. Project under construction, not yet launched.

1) Target Audience

• Independent restaurants, cafés, cloud kitchens, and food trucks, especially without marketing staff or a content specialist.

• Multilingual menu owners in MENA, Latin America, and Europe, with Arabic/English or Spanish/English menus.

• Menu designers, consultants, and small agencies preparing menus for multiple clients.

• Delivery-platform sellers needing short, persuasive dish descriptions.

• Core need: turn dish names and ingredients into appetizing, clear, print-/QR-ready copy, without writing skills or mandatory subscription.

2) Differentiators

• Existing-menu start: import an image or PDF, extract dishes, no blank box.

• Extractive, not inventive: no unlisted ingredients; allergen tags require human review.

• Memorable brand voice: restaurant style on every dish; authentic culinary language, not literal translation; RTL support.

• Menu engineering: flags high-margin dishes; suggests order and sales phrasing.

• Ready outputs: PDF templates, QR link, no-registration trial.

• Privacy: no training on client menus; auto-delete afterward.

• Free tier: instant value, no card; pay for volume and collaboration.

3) First-Version Scope (Max. 8 Tasks)

1. Arabic/English landing page + instant no-registration generator: dish + ingredients → 3 versions; tone (warm, premium, simple, friendly); daily limit.

2. Image/PDF menu import via OCR with review/manual correction.

3. Arabic, English, Spanish, French generation with local culinary phrasing and RTL.

4. Allergen/diet detection (vegetarian, gluten-free…) with human-check warning and manual edits.

5. Brand voice: 3 texts or 3 questions → style profile applied to menu.

6. Menu editor + PDF/PNG export, three original templates, QR link.

7. Optional accounts, saved projects, view/edit links.

8. Usage limits, Stripe payment, simple analytics (generation/export/conversion).

4) Pricing and Profit Model

• No registration: 10 generations/day, one language, copy text.

• Free account: 30 descriptions/month, 1 project, 2 languages, lightly marked PDF.

• Pro — $9/month: 500 descriptions, 5 projects, all languages, brand voice, unmarked, QR.

• Business — $29/month: 3 seats, CSV import, custom templates, branch support.

• One-time credit — $15: 100 descriptions for events/seasonal use.

• White-label agencies — $99/month: agency brand and templates.

• Profit: subscriptions + credits + agency contracts; high margin, core cost LLM/OCR.

5) Global Launch Plan with Free Channels

• Programmatic SEO: “[dish] description in [language]” pages, menu-writing guides, downloadable templates for long-tail search.

• Community launch: Product Hunt, BetaList, Show HN, and restaurant/small-business subreddits.

• Visual: 20-second before/after clips on TikTok, Reels, Shorts; short YouTube tutorials.

• Pinterest: original menu templates as lead magnet, linking to the tool.

• Direct groups: Facebook, WhatsApp, Telegram for restaurant owners, especially MENA.

• Partnerships: printers, POS systems, food photographers, restaurant consultants; 20% referral commission.

• LinkedIn: “free menu audit” for F&B managers with 5 descriptions.

• Referral: 10 extra generations per successful invite; free embed widget for food blogs.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Allergies/health claims: liability if output seen as guarantee; use “guidance only,” ban medical/nutritional claims, require human review.

• Privacy/trade secrets: menus/images may be sensitive; no training, auto-delete, data processing agreement, GDPR/CCPA, data-residency option.

• IP/content: output ownership, text similarity, training-data rights; clear terms and pre-export similarity checks.

• Ads/transparency: no “guaranteed SEO” or ranking promises; realistic language and AI disclosure.

• Technical: OCR errors on poor images; confidence score and manual review.

• Ingredient hallucination: generate only from user inputs, blocked-ingredients list, automated tests.

• Translation/tone: culinary glossary and human preview.

• Cost/misuse: limits, caching, cheaper models for free users, multi-account detection.

• File security: document prompt injection; sanitize inputs, isolate processing, model-provider abstraction to avoid vendor lock-in.

7) Proposed Name and Tagline

Proposed name: Platara

Tagline: From your dish ingredients to an appetizing, sales-driven menu in under a minute.
