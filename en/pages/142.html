Maqam Studio — Complete Project Plan

Separate vocals from music in seconds, for free, on your device.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, business model, launch plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

Product plan: separating vocals from music (a completely new idea)

1) Target audience

• Music producers and mixers — they need Stems to make Remixes.

• Karaoke hosts and DJs — they need a version without Vocals for practice.

• Content creators and Reels/TikTok makers — they want an Acapella or Instrumental short clip in high quality.

• Teachers and language/phonetics students — they want to isolate speech from music for listening.

• Researchers and app developers — they want an API to integrate separation into their products.

2) Points of differentiation (why choose our product)

• Processing happens locally on the user's device first (full privacy, no file uploads, no waiting in cloud queues).

• A workflow of only 3 steps: upload → choose mode → export.

• Ready-made modes instead of complex settings: karaoke, Acapella, music practice, 4-stem separation (Vocals/Drums/Bass/Other).

• Instant A/B preview with a quick toggle switch for comparison before export.

• Works from the browser (WebGPU/WASM) + a lightweight desktop version, with no complicated installation.

• Free to start with no time limit in local mode, and payment only for the cloud and the API.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Import audio and video files (MP3/WAV/M4A/MP4) and extract the audio automatically.

2. Vocal separation engine locally in the browser/device with small-batch processing (Chunking).

3. A 3-step interface only with a progress bar and estimated wait time.

4. A/B preview with an instant toggle key between the processed and original version.

5. Ready-made modes: karaoke / Acapella / practice / 4-stem separation.

6. Export to WAV and MP3 with quality control and saving results to a local library.

7. A queue for batch processing (Batch) to process multiple files.

8. A landing page + creating a free cloud account + an in-product feedback form.

4) Pricing and profit model

• Free (Free-to-start): unlimited local processing, MP3 export, two ready modes.

• Free cloud: 60 minutes of cloud processing per month (no need for a powerful device).

• Pro — $6/month: unlimited cloud, 4-stem separation, WAV export, speed priority.

• Studio — $19/month: API keys, batch processing, commercial license to use the results in published content.

• Additional revenue sources: API credits for developers, an enterprise plan, and a donation/support page for professional users.

5) Global launch plan with free channels

• YouTube: short "before/after" videos (karaoke, Acapella) with SEO around phrases like remove vocals from song free.

• TikTok/Reels/Shorts: 15-second clips showing the result with a link to the free version.

• Reddit: helpful sharing in music production and karaoke communities (without direct advertising).

• Discord and Facebook Groups: DJ and karaoke-maker communities, with free replies to separation requests.

• Product Hunt + Hacker News: an official launch with a clear free version.

• Blog/SEO templates: guides ("how to make karaoke from any song") that attract organic visits.

• Small partnerships: music education and content creation channels in exchange for mentioning the product.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: vocal separation may be used to republish protected music; so we make clear that use must be for permitted purposes, and we prevent uploading protected content in the cloud layer via matching fingerprinting (Fingerprinting) and blocking it.

• Data and Models licensing: training or use must be on licensed data, with a review of the licenses of any component used to ensure commercial use is permitted.

• Trademark: check the name and logo in the target markets before launch to avoid trademark conflicts.

• Technical: separation quality is affected by dense mixes; the solution: multiple modes + optimization on small clip lengths.

• Performance: memory on weak devices; the solution: batch processing + a backup cloud version.

• Dependence on browser platforms: WebGPU changes may break functionality; the solution: a WASM fallback path and a desktop version.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Maqam Studio

Marketing tagline: Separate vocals from music in seconds, for free, on your device.
