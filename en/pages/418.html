Lamha — A Complete Project Plan

Upload your product link and get dozens of avatars to test it before wasting a riyal.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, monetization model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is currently under development and has not yet been launched.

Product Plan: UGC Ad Generator with Avatars

1) Target Audience

• Primary: e-commerce stores and small to medium-sized DTC brands that spend weekly on Meta/TikTok ads and need a large volume of clips for testing.

• Secondary: Performance advertising agencies and independent UGC creators who produce clips for clients at scale.

• Decision maker: Growth/Performance Marketers, aged 25–45, who complain about limited time and the high cost of human creators.

• Initial geography: Gulf + Egypt + Maghreb (Arabic), then Europe and North America (English).

2) Points of Differentiation (Why Choose Our Product)

1. Focus on hooks, not a single video: We generate a table of multiple hook variations (angle × persona × first 3 seconds) ready for testing, not just one clip.

2. Authentic Arabic support: Dialects (Khaleeji/Egyptian/Levantine) and unified dialects, with text written for Arabic-first, not machine translation.

3. Free hook templates as an acquisition magnet: A library of ready-to-use opening templates with SEO-indexable titles.

4. Built-in compliance: Automatic tagging of AI-generated content in accordance with Meta and TikTok policies, along with documented consent models for using avatars.

5. Faster and cheaper: First video in minutes with no credit card required, compared to weeks with clients or human creators.

6. Platform-specific interfaces: Different safe-area sizes for Reels/TikTok/Shorts, with auto-captions translated accordingly.

3) Scope of the First Version (Max 8 Tasks)

1. Input a product link or text script, and automatically extract product features.

2. Hook generator: Generate 10–30 varied opening lines across different angles using an LLM, with an estimated performance score.

3. Avatar selection: Choose from a library (face + voice + language/dialect) with a quick preview.

4. Vertical video generation: Produce a 9:16 vertical video of 15–30 seconds (lip-sync + auto-captions + subtle logo).

5. Hook comparison dashboard: Compare hooks and export MP4 files in bulk with standardized file names.

6. User account and dashboard: Save campaigns and access a video library.

7. Free credits: 3 videos per month with no credit card required.

8. Payment gateway and plan activation: Stripe/Paddle integration + a simple API key.

4) Pricing and Monetization Model

• Free to start: 3 videos per month with a light watermark + free access to all hook generation tools (the hook).

• Starter: $29/month — 20 videos, no watermark.

• Professional: $79/month — 80 videos, A/B hooks, bulk export, branding.

• Agencies: $199/month — 300 videos, multiple workspaces, API, client accounts.

• Add-ons: Extra credits (Pay-as-you-go), speed boost fees, custom avatar templates.

• Margin: Point-based pricing that links the actual GPU/TTS cost to the plan price with a target margin of 70–80%.

5) Global Distribution Plan Through Free Channels

1. SEO: Comparison pages and free hook template pages (Arabic and English) targeting long-tail purchase intent.

2. YouTube/TikTok/Reels: A weekly series titled "We turned a product link into 10 ads".

3. LinkedIn and X/Threads: Case studies with real numbers on CPA costs.

4. Communities: Reddit (r/PPC, r/ecommerce, r/Entrepreneur), Arabic e-commerce Facebook groups, and Telegram groups.

5. Product Hunt + directories: Weekly launch + listing in free AI tool directories.

6. Referral program: Free credits for both the referrer and the referred user.

7. Micro-partnerships: Independent marketers and small agencies in exchange for free credits.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal: Avatar likeness rights and voice signature rights (documented signatures), trademark use when mentioning competitors, mandatory AI disclosure in ads, GDPR/CCPA, music licensing, and platform policies that may reject or limit reach of AI-generated content.

• Technical: Lip-sync quality and rare dialect accuracy, volatility of generation providers and interfaces (vendor lock-in), processing speed under load, rising GPU bills, changes to platform algorithms, and ease of idea replication by larger competitors.

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Slogan

Proposed name: Lamha

Marketing slogan: Upload your product link and get dozens of avatars to test it before wasting a riyal.
