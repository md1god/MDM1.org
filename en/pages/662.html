Riwaq — Complete Project Plan

A lightweight community platform that turns your community's questions into organized knowledge you fully own.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a lightweight community platform for turning discussion into knowledge

1) Target audience

• Small and medium communities (open-source projects, tech teams, student clubs).

• Universities and institutes that need an internal knowledge base and Q&A without high cost.

• Content creators and Arab communities looking for a native, fast RTL experience.

• Organizations that want a lightweight alternative to heavy platforms with full ownership of their data.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Lightest and fastest: small initial load, works as a PWA and reads content offline.

• Discussion turns into knowledge: automatic summarization of the best answers and conversion into an organized, editable article.

• Data ownership first: full export in an open format at any time, with no migration lock-in.

• Arabic first: RTL support and Arabic semantic search from the ground up, not as an afterthought.

• Smart community management: duplicate, defamation, and spam detection before publishing to reduce moderation burden.

• A real free start: a small community can get started without a credit card.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Sign-in and profiles (email + optional OAuth).

2. Spaces and sections with question/discussion posts and tags.

3. Threaded replies and a lightweight Markdown editor.

4. Text and semantic search with suggestions for similar topics.

5. Moderation roles: report, block, pin, and assign accepted answer.

6. Email and Web Push notifications with a weekly digest.

7. Turning the best answer into an editable, publishable knowledge article.

8. Full data export + PWA for offline reading.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free to start: public community up to 300 members, subdomain, unlimited posts.

• Pro: custom domain, private spaces, analytics, and advanced AI tools (monthly subscription).

• Business: SSO, audit logs, higher limits, and priority support.

• Managed hosting for organizations that want an isolated environment.

• Additional revenue: self-hosting licenses for large teams and migration and support services.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Public, indexed Q&A pages that bring organic visits through search.

• Launch on Product Hunt, Hacker News, and specialized Reddit communities.

• A free program for open-source project maintainers and universities.

• Educational content and practical comparison guides (light vs. heavy) on the product blog.

• Integrations with chat tools, GitHub, and Slack to attract teams.

• A referral program that grants extra space for inviting a new community.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Content liability: defamation and copyright risks; addressed with a clear notice-and-takedown policy and audit logs.

• Privacy: compliance with GDPR and data protection laws, with a data deletion option and choice of storage location.

• Automatically generated content: risk of inaccuracy and misattribution; addressed with clear labeling and mandatory human review before publishing.

• Spam and abuse: protection through posting rate limits, verification, and automated account detection.

• Technical: scalability, email deliverability, and semantic search; addressed with a modular architecture, monitoring, and regular backups.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Riwaq

Tagline: A lightweight community platform that turns your community's questions into organized knowledge you fully own.
