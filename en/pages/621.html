Daffa — Complete Project Plan

Your TV's clean, ad-free interface, back in one click.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Owners of smart TVs (Google TV / Android TV, LG webOS, and Samsung Tizen) who are annoyed by the original interface being crowded with ads and recommendations.

• Families, older adults, and children who need a simple, fast, and clear interface.

• Privacy-conscious people who do not want viewing data collected or tracking.

• Owners of older devices that suffer from slow official interfaces.

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• No ads by design: there is no advertising SDK or paid recommendations.

• Instant startup and low memory consumption, suitable for weak devices.

• A single Back button instantly restores the original interface, with a safe restore point.

• Full customization of the grid, icons, backgrounds, and shortcuts.

• Works without the internet and collects no tracking by default.

• A unified experience across the three platforms with an original, lightweight design.

• Free to start without blocking core functions.

3) Scope of the First Version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Build a unified interface engine and a customizable app grid.

2. Detect installed apps, sort them, and pin favorites.

3. Implement an instant “Back” button that restores the original interface with a restore point.

4. Support the three platforms through an abstraction layer (Android TV / webOS / Tizen).

5. Local settings: background, theme, icon size, and remote shortcuts.

6. Fast search and launch with full remote navigation.

7. Offline and tracking-free mode, with manual updates only.

8. Prepare the release and signing package and a simplified installation guide.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Permanently free core functions with no ads at all.

• Optional Pro plan: cloud backup, multiple profiles, advanced themes, and settings sync — at a symbolic one-time price or a low annual subscription.

• Enterprise/OEM licenses for hotels, schools, and device retailers.

• Optional donations and community support, with no selling of user data.

5) Global Rollout Plan with Free Channels

• Direct distribution through GitHub, F-Droid, and APK files, while making official packages available to stores.

• Tech communities: Reddit, XDA, and Android TV, webOS, and Tizen forums.

• Short videos showing interface speed and the Back button, published on YouTube and Product Hunt.

• Comparison pages and SEO articles about “an ad-free TV interface” in multiple languages.

• Discord and Telegram channels and volunteer community localization support.

• Partnerships with privacy and free software communities.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal: app store restrictions on replacing the default interface, respect for trademarks, and compliance with GDPR/CCPA even without tracking.

• Technical: differences in platform interfaces, installation restrictions on some devices, system updates that may break compatibility, performance on older devices, and ensuring the reliability of the restore point if an update fails.

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Tagline

Proposed name: Daffa

Marketing tagline: Your TV's clean, ad-free interface, back in one click.
