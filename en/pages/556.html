Noor — Complete Project Plan

Local AI, simple, and private to you alone.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under construction and not yet launched.

1) Target Audience

• General users: Those who want to use AI without needing an internet connection or creating an account.

• Travelers and privacy-conscious users: Especially in areas with weak connectivity or when they want to keep their data on their devices.

• Students and academics: Those who need a smart assistant in places with unstable internet connections.

• Users with limited devices: Those who prefer local operation to avoid usage costs or monthly quotas.

2) Points of Differentiation (Why Choose Our Product)

• Greater simplicity: A very simple interface, with smart recommendations to automatically select the right model for the device's capabilities.

• Focus on experience: Clean design, clarity of options, and a smooth user experience even for beginners.

• Flexibility in downloading: Ability to download models only as needed, with a clear display of their size and requirements.

• Absolute privacy: All processing is done locally, with no data sent outside the device.

• Fast setup: "Click and start" experience with no complex steps or advanced settings.

3) Scope of the First Version (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. Basic application structure: Set up a new project with a clean, scalable structure.

2. Chat interface: A simple chat screen with support for local text interaction.

3. Model manager: A system to select and download models based on device capabilities.

4. Support for lightweight models: Run at least one local model to work offline.

5. Interface design: Apply a new visual identity and easy-to-use options.

6. Resource management: Monitor memory and processor to avoid freezing or overload.

7. Offline mode: Ensure all features work without any internet connection.

8. Initial testing: Test on two different devices to ensure stability.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Initial model: Completely free, no ads, no mandatory subscription.

• Potential revenue sources (optional): Optimized additional model packs, ready-made templates, or premium support for developers.

• Principle: Keep core functionality free forever, and generate revenue only from non-essential add-ons.

5) Global Distribution Plan Through Free Channels

• Free app stores: Publish on F-Droid with a focus on the privacy app category.

• Developer platforms: Upload open-source code under a clear license on an open platform.

• Tech communities: Participate in privacy forums, Reddit, and Mastodon with a focus on product value rather than direct advertising.

• Simple documentation: A quick image-based guide explaining step by step usage to attract general users.

• Community translation: Rely on the community to translate the interface into multiple languages to increase reach.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal: Ensure licenses of used models comply with distribution, and avoid including any copyrighted assets.

• Technical: Variation in device capabilities (memory/processor) may limit the experience for some users.

• Performance: Heavier models may slow down older devices, so lightweight models must be used for the first version.

• Maintenance: Supporting multiple platforms increases complexity, so it's better to focus on one platform initially.

• Community resources: If relying on community translation, quality must be managed to ensure a consistent experience.

7) Suggested Name and Marketing Slogan

Suggested name: Noor

Marketing slogan: Local AI, simple, and private to you alone.
