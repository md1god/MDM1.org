AssetScope — Complete Project Plan

Open your game packages, understand their assets, and export them safely and responsibly.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a desktop tool for analyzing and inspecting game files and extracting their assets

The idea in brief: a graphical application that opens game asset packages (Asset Bundles/PAK/content folders) and displays their contents — models, textures, sounds, text, data structures — with export in standard formats. It is built from scratch around a modern interface and a safe and responsible workflow.

1) Target audience

• Independent game developers: to diagnose their project files and compare versions.

• Modders: to understand content structure in games that officially allow modification.

• Researchers and students: to study asset engineering, compression, and packaging techniques.

• Game preservationists: to archive data of old, legally abandoned games.

• Visual inspiration artists (Moodboard): to study art trends within licensing limits.

2) Points of distinction (why choose our product)

• A modern and original interface: a completely new visual design (keyboard shortcuts, instant search, in-app 3D preview).

• Built-in responsible workflow: smart licensing alerts at export + an audit log of operations.

• Multi-engine: support for packages of several engines via plugins with a unified interface.

• Free to start: the base tier is open to use, and payment is only for advanced tools.

• Lightweight and fast: no heavy dependencies, runs locally without uploading any data.

• Localization and extensibility: a multilingual interface and an open plugin platform.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum of 8 tasks)

1. Open asset packages from one engine and support the basic container formats.

2. A content tree that is searchable and filterable by type.

3. Preview: textures, text, short sounds, and structural data.

4. Export in standard formats (PNG/WAV/JSON/OBJ) for a single file or a batch.

5. Detect dependencies between assets and link references.

6. File integrity checks and error reports.

7. An operations log (Audit Log) and a licensing alert at export.

8. A plugin system (Plugin API) extensible in one language, with documentation.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: analysis, preview, and single export — with no time limits.

• Pro (monthly/yearly subscription): batch export, 3D export, automation scripts, multi-engine support.

• Team/Studio: group licenses, unified configuration files, priority support.

• Paid third-party plugins: a small commission on the plugin store.

• Institutional (preservation and archiving): a special license for game preservation entities.

5) Global distribution plan through free channels

• An open-source repository for the core + a free binary release.

• Publishing on project-sharing platforms and community package managers.

• Visual educational content and technical series (profiles, short tutorials).

• Modding and engine communities: forums, discussion servers, mailing lists.

• A contributors and plugins program that grants badges and showcases contributors.

• Partnerships with game development education institutes (free educational copies).

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Copyright liability: prevent extraction from protected games; provide a licensing alert and do not distribute protected assets.

• Terms of Service (ToS): respect the restrictions of anti-tamper technologies and do not bypass them.

• Compliance with the licenses of used libraries (open source only).

Technical:

• Packaging formats change with every engine release; handle via fast-updating plugins.

• Performance on huge packages → progressive indexing and streaming processing.

• Challenges of 3D export and permissions.

• Extractability may be misused → built-in limits and a clear usage policy.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: AssetScope

Marketing tagline: Open your game packages, understand their assets, and export them safely and responsibly.
