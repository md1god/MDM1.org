Nojoum — Complete Project Plan

Explore the universe from your browser in real time, in Arabic with a captivating 3D experience.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, growth plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

Product plan: a real-time 3D visualization of space

1) Target audience

• Learners and astronomy enthusiasts among school and university students and observation hobbyists.

• Teachers and science professors looking for an interactive classroom tool.

• Science content creators (YouTube, podcasts, educational media).

• Museums, planetariums, and educational entertainment initiatives.

• Developers wanting to embed space scenes within their applications.

2) Points of distinction (why choose our product)

• Runs instantly from the browser with no installation, on any device, with a smooth mobile experience.

• Free model from the start lowers the entry barrier and accelerates adoption.

• Share scenes via instant links and direct collaboration between users.

• Open astronomical data sources updated automatically instead of relying on static files.

• Arabic-first interface with full right-to-left support.

• Modern architecture (WebGL/WebGPU) delivers high performance on modest devices.

3) First version scope (8 tasks maximum)

1. A 3D rendering engine for the solar system and planets in real time.

2. Geolocation and camera controls (rotate, zoom, track an object).

3. Star layer and basic constellations with search by name.

4. Timeline to control the date and the near past/future.

5. Simplified info cards for each celestial body (Arabic/English).

6. Share links for the current scene + exportable screenshots.

7. Built-in educational explanation (internal guided tours).

8. A basic free account with cloud sync of settings.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free for personal and educational use (Freemium).

• Pro plan for teachers/creators: custom tours, high display quality, watermark removal, presentation-quality export.

• Institutional licensing (schools, museums, TV channels) with an annual contract.

• Paid API for developers based on usage volume.

• Sponsorship and educational endowment from scientific bodies to support free Arabic content.

5) Global growth plan via free channels

• Short content on YouTube, TikTok, and Twitter: stunning space scenes with an open invitation.

• SEO optimization through educational pages for each planet and star.

• Open communities: Reddit, Discord, and teacher groups.

• Partnerships with schools, universities, and online curricula.

• Product Hunt / Hacker News at launch + an early waitlist.

• A student ambassador program granting certificates and free perks.

• User retention through challenges and image sharing.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: use of astronomical data must come from open sources with a clear license; risk of infringing external image rights → we rely on freely licensed maps and data and document sources.

• Legal: respecting privacy (GDPR) and storing minors' data cautiously.

• Technical: browser performance differences and weak devices → automatic quality scaling and detail reduction.

• Technical: accuracy of astronomical calculations over time → verification with accredited astronomical libraries.

• Technical: scaling with growing users → scalable cloud architecture and edge computing.

• Competition: standing out through Arabic, educational integration, and fast sharing.

7) Suggested name and marketing tagline

Suggested name: نُجوم (Nojoum)

Marketing tagline: Explore the universe from your browser in real time, in Arabic with a captivating 3D experience.
