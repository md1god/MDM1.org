Luqma — Complete Project Plan

Scan the barcode and know what's in your plate in a second, no internet and no complexity.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, growth plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

An original product plan: a barcode food-scanning app

1) Target audience

• The health-conscious consumer: those who read food labels and worry about sugar/additives/salt.

• Families: the mother/father who decides purchases for the children's sake (allergies, diabetes, diet).

• Dieters and athletes: those tracking calories, protein, and fats.

• Chronic patients: diabetes, blood pressure, wheat/lactose allergy, vegetarians.

• Volunteer contributors: data and nutrition enthusiasts who enrich the product database.

2) Points of distinction (why choose our product)

• Record speed: open the camera, scan, and get the result in under two seconds, with continuous scanning and no taps.

• Offline-first local database: works without internet and syncs later, important for markets with limited coverage.

• One clear health score per product based on a transparent, auditable formula, not a black box.

• Instant allergy alerts: a red warning customized per profile (gluten, lactose, nuts…).

• Real Arabic/regional support: RTL interface, recognition of local products, and translated ingredient names.

• Rewarding contributions: badges and direct impact for those who correct data, with no bank accounts.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Barcode scanning via the camera + alternative manual entry.

2. Display the product card: ingredients, nutritional values, additives, health score.

3. Allergies and diets profile with automatic matching and warnings.

4. Save products into lists (favorites / avoid).

5. Add a missing product or correct its data via a quick form + photos.

6. Work offline with a smart sync queue.

7. Search and classification (category, brand, health score).

8. Local history for each product (on any device) without mandatory cloud.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free to start for life: scanning and basic data unlimited and ad-free.

• "Luqma Pro" subscription (monthly/yearly): family reports, comparisons, food tracking, data export.

• B2B revenue source: an API for scores and product data for restaurants and health apps.

• Transparent sponsorship/partnerships: highlighting products that meet the standards, with a clear separation between sponsorship and rating.

• Optional donations and collaborative support (grants) to cover costs.

• Rule: the health rating is not sold and is not affected by payment.

5) Global growth plan through free channels

• Short content: "swipe the product and know its truth" videos on TikTok/Reels/Shorts.

• Wellness communities: Reddit, Facebook groups, diabetes and fitness forums.

• App store optimization (ASO): keywords about barcode, calories, allergy.

• Micro-influencers in nutrition: in exchange for reach, not cash payment.

• Seasonal campaigns: Ramadan, back to school, January (health resolutions).

• Community contribution: a "add 100 local products" challenge per country, with a global leaderboard for contributors.

• SEO: high-quality product pages that appear for free on search engines.

• A university ambassadors program to cover new languages and markets.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal:

• Data rights and licensing: the need for an open license permitting commercial use and integration without conflicting obligations.

• Privacy compliance: GDPR/CCPA, users' images and voices, explicit consent, and full erasure.

• Medical liability: presenting information as guidance, not as mandatory medical advice, with a clear disclaimer.

• Health claims and intellectual property: avoiding exaggerated claims and respecting company trademarks.

• Technical:

• Data quality: contributor errors, solved with a review threshold + voting + historical versions.

• Barcode matching accuracy: duplicate/missing barcodes, solved with multi-verification and record merging.

• Offline performance: database size, solved with gradual regional downloading and compression.

• Differences in dietary standards: varying standards between countries, solved with customizable local scores.

• Misuse: vandalizing contributions, solved with rate limits and anomaly detection.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Luqma

Marketing tagline: Scan the barcode and know what's in your plate in a second, no internet and no complexity.
