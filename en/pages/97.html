Nutq — Complete Project Plan

Speak in your language... and write clean, formatted text wherever you are.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a universal voice dictation tool that turns speech into formatted text

1) Target audience

• The individual Arabic and global user: students, journalists, bloggers, and developers who write long texts.

• People with special needs: those with hand injuries or difficulties writing/reading.

• Small teams: customer support, report preparation, and meeting notes.

• Bilingual users: Arabic and English in the same context (technical Latin terms inside an Arabic sentence).

2) Points of distinction (why choose our product)

• Bilingual by design: detects Arabic/English switching automatically without changing settings.

• A real free tier with a generous usage limit instead of an early paywall.

• Privacy first: on-device processing for sensitive texts, with an option not to store.

• Works in any text field: does not require special add-ons for each app.

• Smart text cleanup: removes filler ("I mean, umm") and fixes punctuation and formats lists automatically.

• Keyboard shortcuts and command templates: "correct", "convert to email", "summarize".

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Immediate speech-to-text (live streaming) in any text field.

2. Automatic detection of Arabic/English language switching.

3. Basic cleanup: filler removal + automatic punctuation.

4. Voice commands for formatting ("new line", "comma", "bullet list").

5. Terminology correction: a personal dictionary for terms and names.

6. Ready-made templates: email, meeting summary, short post.

7. A local text history with search, fully deletable.

8. A desktop app + web page with global keyboard shortcuts.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free, to start without a card: 2,000 words/month, dual language, basic templates, local processing.

• Pro, monthly/yearly subscription: fair-use unlimited words, optional cloud sync, custom dictionary training, advanced export.

• Teams: centralized seats, permission management, integration with work tools, and priority support.

• Additional revenue: API/Enterprise licensing and a developer API. A Freemium model with an optional early payment for intensive use.

5) Global distribution plan through free channels

• Effective short content: before/after clips showing speed and accuracy on TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Reels.

• Developer and professional communities: Product Hunt, Reddit, Hacker News, Discord/Slack.

• Bilingual SEO/ASO: keywords such as "voice dictation", "speech to text app".

• Free partnerships: productivity and writing influencers, universities, and support centers for people with special needs.

• Built-in viral growth: invite a friend grants free word credit to both parties.

• GitHub/open add-on: a free browser extension to attract developers.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: privacy protection (GDPR, CCPA), explicit consent for audio recording, defining liability for text accuracy, and respecting the rights of the models used.

• Technical: Arabic dialect accuracy, environmental noise, response time, processor consumption, and cloud inference costs.

• Consequences: strong competition and tools built into operating systems. Mitigation through bilingual specialization, privacy, and speed.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Nutq (نُطق)

Marketing tagline: Speak in your language... and write clean, formatted text wherever you are.
