Qudum — Complete Project Plan

Qudum.. manage your sales and inventory easily, and at no cost.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the distribution plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Owners of small and medium stores: those who do not have a large budget for expensive management software.

• New merchants: those who have just started their activity and need a simple and fast tool.

• Owners of workshops and warehouses: those who rely on tracking products, purchases, and sales daily.

• Entrepreneurs in emerging markets: those looking for a free and easy-to-use solution.

2) Points of Distinction (Why choose our product)

• Ease of use: a simple interface, designed to suit those without a technical background.

• Free to start: the user does not need to pay any fees to start working immediately.

• Supports weak devices: it runs smoothly on low-specification devices, making it suitable for emerging markets.

• Multilingual: it supports Arabic and English from the start, with the possibility of adding other languages.

• Fast and modern design: it focuses on the user experience and reduces the steps needed to reach any function.

• Reliable and secure: it provides precise permissions for each user and supports data backup.

3) Scope of the First Version (a maximum of 8 tasks)

1. Product management: add, edit, and delete products with unique codes for them.

2. Inventory: track quantities, out-of-stock statuses, and update inventory automatically.

3. Sales: issue simple and fast invoices with direct printing.

4. Purchases: record purchase invoices and link them to suppliers.

5. Customers and suppliers: create special profiles for them and track their balances.

6. Basic reports: display reports on sales, purchases, and inventory.

7. User permissions: define roles for each user and control access to every button and list.

8. General settings: set up company data, currencies, and the printing method.

4) Pricing and Profit Model

• Free to start: the basic version is completely free and covers all the tasks of the first version.

• Freemium model: advanced features can be added in the future in exchange for a low-cost monthly subscription.

• Additional revenue sources: dedicated technical support, template customization, or paid cloud hosting for users who want it.

• Focus on growth first: the goal is to build a large user base before imposing any fees.

5) Global Distribution Plan through Free Channels

• GitHub: publish the code for free with clear documentation, to attract developers and users around the world.

• Search engine optimization (SEO): create simple educational articles such as "How to manage your store for free" in both Arabic and English.

• Free communities: participate in small business communities, Reddit, and Dev.to, and trade communities in emerging markets.

• Educational visual content: create short and free videos that explain usage on platforms such as YouTube and TikTok.

• Word-of-mouth marketing: encourage users to share the product with their friends and other entrepreneurs.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal: differences in tax and invoicing laws between countries, so the system must be designed to support their customization easily.

• Technical: the need to ensure data security, encrypt it, and support automatic backup.

• Scaling the product: the number of users may grow quickly, which requires improving database performance.

• Competition: there are many solutions in this field, so the focus must be on simplicity and speed of use as a priority.

7) Suggested Name and Marketing Slogan

Suggested name: Qudum

Marketing slogan: "Qudum.. manage your sales and inventory easily, and at no cost."
