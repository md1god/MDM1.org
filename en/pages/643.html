CutPilot — Full Project Plan

Turn your episodes, articles and podcasts into viral short clips in minutes, free to start.

Complete execution plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, business model, launch plan, and risks. The project is under construction and not yet launched.

1) Target audience

• Content creators (1K–100K followers) on YouTube/podcast who want to repurpose their content

• Social media marketers in small businesses and agencies

• Trainers/teachers/experts (course creators) for promotional clips

• Micro-influencers on TikTok/Reels/Shorts

• Marketing teams without a dedicated video editor

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• 100% in the browser (FFmpeg.wasm + WebCodecs), no install

• free-to-start: 3 clips/week free, no card, no watermark

• Privacy: no upload of original video (local analysis + audio extraction)

• "Proven" templates updated weekly from public data

• Multilingual captions (Arabic/English/Spanish) + multilingual TTS

• Direct publishing (TikTok/IG/YouTube Shorts) via OAuth

• Brand Kit on paid plan

• 3-5× cheaper than competitors (SaaS credits-based)

3) First version scope (8 tasks)

1. URL import (YouTube/RSS) + PDF/text upload

2. Audio transcription + "highlight moments" detection (AI highlight detection)

3. Generate 3–5 clips (9:16) with auto-captions + B-roll from stock library

4. Simple editor (trim/crop/text/music)

5. Multilingual voiceover TTS

6. Library of 6–8 viral templates

7. Export MP4 (720p/1080p) + direct publishing

8. User account + free limits (3/week) + Stripe billing

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: 3 clips/week, 720p, no watermark, limited B-roll

• Pro $12/month: 30 clips/week, 1080p, brand kit, all templates, priority

• Team $39/month: 5 seats, 100 clips, shared brand, API

• Credit packs: $5 for 10 extra clips

• Revenue: freemium + credits + affiliates (30% recurring) + templates marketplace (20% commission)

5) Global launch plan via free channels

• Product Hunt + Hacker News (Show HN) launch

• Reddit: r/ContentCreators, r/NewTubers, r/podcasting, r/Entrepreneur

• TikTok/Reels/Shorts (dogfooding: "how I made this" clips)

• YouTube tutorials (SEO long-tail: "turn podcast into TikTok")

• Indie Hackers + Discord communities (creators)

• SEO landing pages (long-tail keywords)

• Programmatic templates marketplace (viral loop from users)

• Affiliate program for content creators

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: B-roll/music rights (licensed stock), YouTube ToS (scraping), GDPR, TikTok API policies, voice cloning consent

• Technical: FFmpeg.wasm performance on mobile, STT/TTS API cost, hallucinations in highlight detection, rate limiting, browser memory limits, B-roll scaling

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: CutPilot

Marketing tagline: Turn your episodes, articles and podcasts into viral short clips in minutes, free to start.
