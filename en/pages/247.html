Zinora — Complete Project Plan

Your digital subscriptions.. simpler than you think.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the distribution plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Startups: especially those building digital products and needing subscription invoicing without a large finance team.

• Independent software developers: who want to add subscriptions to their products quickly and at the lowest cost.

• Digital teachers and creators: who sell exclusive content or online courses.

• Small businesses in emerging markets: that need support for local currencies and payment gateways available in their region.

2) Points of Distinction (Why choose our product)

• Very simple: an interface designed specifically for beginners, requiring no accounting background.

• Free to start: anyone can launch their subscriptions without paying any upfront fees.

• Cost-smart: focuses on minimal complexity, making it lighter and faster than alternatives.

• Multi-currency and multi-gateway: supports local currencies and popular payment gateways across different regions.

• Modern design: a clean, fast user experience on all devices.

• Easily customizable: allows editing plans and invoices to fit any business model.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum of 8 tasks)

· # · Task · Its purpose ·

· 1 · Dashboard interface design · Create a simple and easy-to-use experience. ·

· 2 · Plans and subscriptions management · Enable creating paid or free plans with quick edits. ·

· 3 · Connecting multiple payment gateways · Support popular payment gateways to increase global reach. ·

· 4 · Automatic invoice generation and sending · Reduce manual effort and ensure accuracy. ·

· 5 · Customer management · Easily track customer data and their subscription status. ·

· 6 · Basic tax setup · Calculate value-added tax or simple taxes according to the settings. ·

· 7 · Smart notification system · Alert customers before subscription expiry or payment failure. ·

· 8 · Setting up quick educational documentation · Help users get started within minutes. ·

4) Pricing and profit model

• Completely free to start: no monthly subscription fees, which encourages everyone to try it.

• Shared success model: a symbolic fee is charged only when a payment succeeds (such as a small percentage of each transaction).

• Fair minimum: no fees on free subscriptions or trials.

• Full transparency: all fees are clear to the user before activation, with no hidden fees.

• Future paid option: additional packages for advanced features may be added later without affecting the free version.

5) Global distribution plan through free channels

• Search engine optimization (SEO): creating simple educational articles such as "How to start your digital subscriptions in 5 minutes".

• Open communities: participating in developer, digital producer, and entrepreneur communities on free platforms.

• Short visual content: publishing explainer videos on free video platforms to explain usage.

• Developer tool lists: listing the product in free lists dedicated to SaaS and developer tools.

• Word-of-mouth marketing: encouraging users to share the product with their networks through a simple, free referral program.

• Community partnerships: collaborating with digital teachers or small influencers with positive impact without large budgets.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

· Risk type · Details · Mitigation method ·

· Legal · Differing tax and data protection laws from one country to another. · Focus on minimal data, provide flexible tax settings, and follow general compliance guidance. ·

· Security · Handling payment data and sensitive personal data. · Do not store payment card data, use modern encryption standards, and conduct periodic security checks. ·

· Technical · Integrating different payment gateways and system stability as users grow. · Build flexible architecture, regularly test integrations, and use free monitoring systems. ·

· Market · Intense competition with existing solutions. · Focus on simplicity, user experience, and supporting emerging markets that are often neglected. ·

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Zinora

Marketing tagline: Your digital subscriptions.. simpler than you think.
