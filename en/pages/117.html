Mazn — Complete Project Plan

Accurate weather data in a single query, start free and build your product in minutes.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Original product plan: an open weather data platform for developers

1) Target audience

• Developers of small and medium apps and websites who need instant weather data without complicated signup or upfront billing.

• Student and academic teams and open-source projects.

• SaaS companies in the proof stage (MVP) building weather/logistics/precision-agriculture features.

• Embedded device and IoT developers who need lightweight, low-volume queries.

2) Points of differentiation (why choose our product)

• Instant free start with no credit card, with an API key working within a minute.

• Data aggregated from multiple open meteorological sources, with model blending to improve local accuracy.

• A single unified query returning current conditions, forecasts, historical, and climate data.

• Full transparency: open documentation, a published service status, and clear attribution data for the user.

• An initial SDK and ready-made examples (cURL/JavaScript/Python) and uncomplicated key generation.

• Low latency performance through geographic caching and request batching.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. An endpoint for hourly and daily forecasts up to 7 days by coordinates.

2. An endpoint for current weather (temperature, humidity, wind, precipitation, pressure).

3. A historical archive of simple daily data.

4. A caching layer (Time-series) and geographic indexing for queries.

5. An API key system with free quotas and a usage counter.

6. An interactive documentation page with runnable examples.

7. A simple developer dashboard (view usage, rotate key).

8. Service health monitoring and basic operational alerts.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free-to-start: up to 10,000 requests/month, current data and forecasts, non-commercial use.

• Pro: a nominal monthly subscription, higher quotas, historical and climate data, commercial use agreement.

• Scale/Enterprise: custom quotas, guaranteed latency (SLA), support and service-level agreement.

• Additional revenue sources: annual plans at a discount, custom data packages, paid support.

• Pricing principle: pricing by volume not by feature; no ads and no selling of user data.

5) Global distribution plan through free channels

• Publishing the repository and releases on GitHub with open documentation and a clear license.

• Writing examples and guides on Dev.to, Hashnode, and Medium.

• Posts on Product Hunt, Hacker News, and Reddit (r/webdev, r/gis, r/weather).

• Search engine optimization through indexable documentation pages and API references.

• Open integrations: community libraries, Postman Collection, and Jupyter examples.

• An ambassador program and open-source contributions to build a community around the project.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: the terms of data source licenses and their attribution; the need to clarify the limits of commercial use; geographic location privacy (GDPR). Mitigation: using open sources, reviewing licenses, an explicit privacy policy, and not storing user identities.

• Technical: the service's dependence on external data providers and their outages; storage and bandwidth costs as it grows; local model accuracy; key misuse. Mitigation: multiple sources and a fallback, smart caching, rate limiting, monitoring, and safe degradation.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Mazn — the weather data platform for developers

Marketing tagline: Accurate weather data in a single query, start free and build your product in minutes.
