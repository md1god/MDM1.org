Serviqa — Complete Project Plan

One platform that runs your hosting business from the first invoice to the last server, and starts with you for free.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

An original product plan: a platform for managing and selling hosting services

1) Target audience

• Small and medium hosting providers (VPS, shared hosting, domains, email).

• Tech agencies and DevOps teams that sell cloud services to their clients.

• System administrators (Sysadmins) who want a lightweight, customizable alternative to heavy closed solutions.

• Startups that begin with no budget and need a free model to get started.

2) Points of differentiation (why choose our product)

1. One-click installation via Docker with a setup Wizard that requires no server experience.

2. Open plugin architecture for infrastructure provider connectors (AWS, Hetzner, DigitalOcean, Proxmox, cPanel…).

3. Visual automation engine (Workflow Builder) that handles provisioning, invoicing, and tickets without writing code.

4. A genuinely free starting model that includes all core functions with no time limit, instead of waitlists or crippling restrictions.

5. A modern, faster interface (lightweight SSR + API-first) with full Arabic and RTL support from day one.

6. Transparent pricing with no hidden transaction fees, and full user-owned data export on migration.

7. Customer-centric operational support: client portal, auto-renewal, and ready-made profit reports.

8. Horizontal scalability via job queues (Queues) to prevent billing outages at peak times.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Customer and account management plus team permissions.

2. Product and plan catalog (recurring/usage-based) with discount codes.

3. Billing and recurring payments (Stripe/PayPal + extensible local gateways).

4. Order manager and automated provisioning via infrastructure connectors.

5. Customer dashboard: my services, my invoices, my support.

6. Built-in support ticket system with canned responses.

7. Simple automation engine (Triggers/Actions) for recurring tasks.

8. Revenue and inventory reports plus a public API.

4) Pricing and profit model

• Completely free (Community/Self-hosted): all core functions with unlimited counts, for contribution and adoption.

• Managed Cloud plan: tiered monthly subscription (free up to a limited number of clients, then $19/month, then $49/month) to cover hosting, backups, and updates.

• Enterprise license: private edition (White-label, SSO, SLA, priority support) at a fixed annual price.

• Add-on marketplace (Marketplace): 20% commission on paid add-ons from third-party developers.

• Principle: always free to start, and pay only when you scale or need support.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• GitHub as an open-source repository with an attractive README and a live demo (Demo sandbox).

• Product Hunt and Hacker News at the launch of the first version.

• Reddit (r/webhosting, r/selfhosted) and technical Discord/Matrix communities.

• Free educational content: a series "How to build a hosting company from scratch" on YouTube and a blog.

• Integration with Awesome Self-Hosted and open list repositories, and contributions to related open-source projects.

• Open-source ambassadors program: code contributions are rewarded with discounts on the managed plan.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• The responsibility of handling payment and customer data → PCI-DSS compliance by delegating to gateways (we do not store card data).

• Compliance with GDPR/privacy in cloud editions, and providing data export/delete tools.

• License disputes: clearly choosing AGPLv3 or MIT, and reviewing add-on licenses to avoid contamination.

Technical:

• Risk of provisioning task failures → queues, retries, and rate limiting.

• Security of server access and credentials → key encryption (KMS) and least-privilege principle.

• Multiple infrastructures and their changing interfaces → abstraction via a unified plugin interface.

• Backup and migration challenges → full export/import and clear documentation.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Serviqa

Marketing tagline: One platform that runs your hosting business from the first invoice to the last server, and starts with you for free.
