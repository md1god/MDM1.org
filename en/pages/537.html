Narviq — Complete Project Plan

From idea to publish-ready video, with no camera and no complexity.

New digital project plan: idea, audience, product, revenue, growth, risks. In development; not launched.

1) Target audience

Creators and small teams publishing camera-free videos, with little time or editing skill.

• YouTube, Shorts, TikTok, Reels: education, storytelling, summaries, explainers.

• Online stores, small marketing teams needing short multilingual ads at various ratios.

• Teachers, course creators, small agencies turning articles and lessons into structured videos.

• Priority: Arab markets, Latin America, Southeast Asia, where localization and local-language support are weak.

Problem: text-to-video needs editing, inconsistent visuals, unclear rights, unpredictable costs.

2) Differentiators

• Story first: mandatory, editable shot plan approved before export; no surprises or costly regeneration.

• Verifiable rights: license record for each image, clip, voice; clear commercial use; no celebrity voices without documented consent.

• Real localization, not literal translation: Arabic/RTL support, pronunciation dictionary, cultural adaptation.

• Reusable series identity: consistent visual/audio style, intro/outro templates, cross-episode branding.

• One project, many versions: 9:16, 16:9, 1:1, with platform-specific intros and captions.

• Transparent cost: balance and cost preview before export; no surprise charges.

• Fully original: name, identity, experience, code from scratch; licensed assets only; no other product reuse.

3) MVP scope (max 8 tasks)

1. Account/workspace: email or Google sign-up, auto-save, clear free quota.

2. Inputs: topic, script, or article link; choose language, duration, tone, ratio, audience.

3. Shot plan: auto-split intro/scenes/outro; edit/reorder text; flag claims needing sources.

4. Scene visuals: images/clips; regenerate one scene only; consistent style; filtering.

5. Assets/rights: licensed assets, user uploads, license/restriction records.

6. Voice/text: multilingual voices, pronunciation dictionary, speed/pause, auto translation/captions.

7. Export: 9:16, 16:9, 1:1 at 720p/1080p; SRT, identity templates, free-plan watermark.

8. Billing/controls: Stripe, usage limits, pre-export cost preview, history.

4) Pricing and revenue model

Permanent free tier, subscriptions, extra credits beyond quota. No card for trial.

· Plan · Monthly · Limits ·

· Free · $0 · 3 videos/mo, ≤60s, 720p, watermark, basic voices, 15 visuals ·

· Creator · $12 · 20 videos/mo, ≤3 min, 1080p, no watermark, 1 seat ·

· Pro · $29 · 60 videos/mo, ≤10 min, advanced voices, 3 seats, priority processing ·

· Agency · $79 · 200 videos/mo, 5 seats, agency identity, batch export, priority support ·

• Extra credits: $5 per 5 short videos; cost preview first.

• 20% annual discount; 25% recurring referral commission for 12 months.

• Target gross margin near 70% after model/storage; no ads or data selling.

5) Global growth plan with free channels

• Beta: waitlist, 200 free seats for interviews and feedback.

• Multilingual SEO: landing pages per use case; free tools: intro generator, script splitter, caption exporter.

• Organic: Shorts/TikTok/Reels showing text-to-video and tutorials; no ad spend.

• Communities: Product Hunt, Indie Hackers, Hacker News, BetaList, Reddit, Discord, Facebook, LinkedIn.

• Growth loop: free template gallery; optional “Made with Narviq” badge on public pages.

• Ambassadors: 25% recurring commission for one year; free credits for honest reviews.

• Early localization: Arabic, English, Spanish, Portuguese, Hindi, Indonesian; local support, translated tutorials.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Asset rights: licensed libraries only, license log; no celebrity voices/faces without documented consent.

• Synthetic content: disclosure/watermarking per YouTube, TikTok, Meta; review “reused content” rules.

• Privacy: GDPR/CCPA, encryption, deletion on request; no user-content training without consent.

• Billing: clear renewal, one-click cancellation, disciplined refunds.

• Name/trademark: check trademark and domain before launch.

Technical:

• Hallucination/errors: claim alerts, optional source review; no absolute accuracy guarantee.

• Model costs: multiple voice/visual providers, cost caps, priority queues.

• Quality/time: async processing, template quality checks, caching to cut export time.

• Abuse: multi-layer filtering, impersonation bans, human review of reports.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Name: Narviq

Tagline: From idea to publish-ready video, with no camera and no complexity.
