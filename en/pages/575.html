Nabra — Complete Project Plan

An original plan for a new digital project: audience, concept, revenue model, and go-to-market plan.

A complete plan for a new digital project: concept, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market, and risks. It is under construction and not launched yet.

1) Target audience

• Small and medium brands needing [source hidden] content fast without an agency/studio.

• E-commerce stores wanting multiple ad variants to test products.

• Marketing agencies managing multiple clients, needing scale and fast export.

• Creators and influencers wanting content variants that preserve voice and identity, with consent.

• Affiliate marketers needing short, high-converting videos without appearing.

2) Differentiators

• Consent first: every digital persona uses documented assets and explicit consent, with an auditable rights record.

• Brand identity memory: tone, vocabulary, offers, and rules are stored and applied automatically to every script and video.

• Built-in transparency: clear AI-generated labeling, with compliance options for each platform.

• Multi-platform from one draft: turn one idea into versions for TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.

• Arabic and English support: native writing, voice generation, and localization, which most global tools lack.

• Performance loop: link results to scripts and hooks to learn what works, not just generate content.

• Free start, no bank card, and lower cost than traditional [source hidden] production.

3) MVP scope (max 8 tasks)

1. Brand identity: upload logo, colors, tone of voice, and usage guidelines.

2. Documented persona: create one from licensed assets with a consent record and scope of use.

3. Script generator: write hooks and [source hidden] scripts in Arabic and English in brand tone.

4. Video generation: vertical templates, voiceover, subtitles, and licensed music.

5. Approval: pre-publish workflow with a customizable disclosure label.

6. Publishing: connect TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube; publish or schedule from one place.

7. Analytics dashboard: views, engagement, and click-through rate per variant and platform.

8. Asset library and rights log: store and export videos, voices, and licenses.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: 1 persona, 5 videos/month, watermark, manual publishing.

• Starter — $19/month: 30 videos, 3 personas, scheduling, no watermark.

• Growth — $49/month: 120 videos, 10 personas, analytics, multilingual versions.

• Agency — $149/month: 500 videos, client workspaces, approvals, white-label export.

• Extra revenue: credit packs, discounted annual plan, paid setup, referral commission.

5) Global go-to-market with free channels

• Programmatic SEO: industry pages like “[source hidden] for skincare products” and “ad video for an online store.”

• Free tools: hook generator, script quality meter, copyable template library.

• Product Hunt launch and founder communities with a free early-user offer.

• Partnerships with small creators who try the tool and publish results transparently.

• Referral program: free credits for inviting an active user.

• Short educational content on TikTok, YouTube, and LinkedIn on building an ethical [source hidden].

• Early localization: Arabic, English, Spanish, and Portuguese for less competitive markets.

• Shareable templates carrying the tool link, spreading in e-commerce communities.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Using a person’s image or voice without explicit consent; addressed by a consent and licensing record with defined scope and duration.

• Generated-content laws and misleading ads across markets; addressed by mandatory disclosure and human review.

• Music and asset rights; addressed by a licensed library and automated checks.

• Data privacy, GDPR, and CCPA; addressed by minimal data collection and clear deletion options.

• Changing platform policies on synthetic content; addressed by syncing rules and monitoring updates.

Technical:

• Weak face or voice realism; addressed by limited high-quality templates instead of full freedom at first.

• High generation cost; addressed by credits, usage limits, and temporary caching.

• Platform API changes or automated-publishing bans; addressed by a manual publishing fallback.

• Misuse and identity impersonation; addressed by identity verification, content review, and bans.

• Generation delays and slow UX; addressed by asynchronous processing and notifications.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Nabra

Tagline: Turn your brand identity into trustworthy, multi-platform [source hidden] content in minutes, with full consent and full transparency.
