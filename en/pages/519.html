Zennix — Complete Project Plan

Launch your campaigns across three platforms in two minutes, for free.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Small store owners: they want to launch their campaigns quickly without deep advertising expertise.

• Individual marketers: they work across multiple platforms and seek a unified tool to save time.

• Emerging marketing agencies: they need to organize their clients' campaigns without complex or expensive tools.

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• Launch speed: the user can launch their first campaign in just two minutes.

• Smart focus: suggestions are generated based on the user's product and price, instead of generic ready-made templates.

• Simplified interface: it removes duplication across platforms and shows everything needed on one page.

• Free to start: the core part always remains free, without gating a priority trial before subscription.

3) First Version Scope (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. [ ] Dashboard: a quick view of campaigns and their status.

2. [ ] Platform connections: support official OAuth connection for Meta, TikTok, and Snapchat.

3. [ ] Bulk upload: upload multiple ads in one step with data validation.

4. [ ] Smart generator: suggest the ad name and copy based on the entered product data.

5. [ ] Simple test creation: create an A/B test group with one click.

6. [ ] Unified preview: show the ad's appearance before publishing on each platform separately.

7. [ ] Publishing queue: manage upload operations while clearly displaying errors.

8. [ ] Free mode: enable all of the above for free for the user.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

· Plan · Price · Details ·

· Free · $0 / month · Covers all first-version needs, with no restricted trial period. ·

· Pro · $19 / month · For more campaigns and additional automation features. ·

· Team · $49 / month · For multiple members with simple organizational permissions. ·

Revenue model: monthly subscriptions, while keeping core functions free forever.

5) Global Rollout Plan with Free Channels

• Educational content: write articles in Arabic and English about how to successfully launch a first ad.

• Tool directory: list the tool in free emerging tool directories.

• Referral programs: reward a free month when inviting an active user.

• Niche communities: participate with helpful answers in entrepreneur communities without direct promotion.

• SEO optimization: target high-intent, low-demand keywords to increase organic visibility.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

· Type · Risk · Mitigation ·

· Legal · Changes to platform connection policies or API permissions · Rely only on official APIs and monitor updates weekly. ·

· Legal · Handling credentials or campaigns · Store the minimum possible data, use appropriate encryption, and maintain a clear privacy policy. ·

· Technical · Per-platform rate limits · Use a smart queue with retries and avoid excessive concurrent requests. ·

· Technical · Authentication failure or OAuth token expiration · Handle errors automatically and redirect the user to renew clearly and simply. ·

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Tagline

Proposed name: Zennix

Marketing tagline: Launch your campaigns across three platforms in two minutes, for free.
