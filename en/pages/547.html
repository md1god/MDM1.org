Nafadh – A Complete Project Plan

Scan and fix file accessibility easily, free to start.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, monetization model, growth plan, and risks. The project is under development and has not been launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs): Need to meet digital accessibility requirements without high costs.

• Regulated entities: Such as banks and insurance companies that are subject to strict digital accessibility requirements.

• Public sector suppliers: Required to submit files compliant with accessibility standards in tenders.

• Content and technical teams: Responsible for preparing documents before publishing or submitting them to official entities.

2) Key Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• Focus on fixing, not just diagnosing: Instead of just a list of errors, it provides practical step-by-step guidance to fix each issue.

• Prioritize by impact: Errors are classified based on their actual impact on users, so teams can fix the most important issues first.

• Complete privacy: Most of the scanning is done in the browser, minimizing file uploads to servers as much as possible.

• Generous free start: Free and practical scanning without needing a credit card or immediate subscription.

• Simplified interface: Designed for non-specialists in digital accessibility, with clear explanations in multiple languages.

3) Scope of the First Version (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. File upload: Support direct PDF uploads from the device with format validation.

2. Structure check: Verify correct sequence, headings, tables, and forms.

3. Text content check: Detect missing alternative text and unclear link texts.

4. Readability check: Check color contrast, reading order, and navigation elements.

5. Smart results display: Group errors by priority with quick links to each element in the file.

6. Interactive fix guide: Custom instructions for each error with clear, applicable examples.

7. Shareable report: Export a concise report in a format suitable for internal discussion or with partners.

8. Smart free usage limits: Track usage for the free version with clear display of limits without blocking educational scans.

4) Pricing and Profit Model

• Model: Free to start (Freemium).

• Free plan: Sufficient scans for most small businesses to test compliance and set priorities.

• Paid plan: Affordable monthly/annual subscription for small and medium-sized enterprises, including bulk fixes, custom compliance templates, and simple integrations.

• Potential additional sources: Licenses for educational or non-profit institutions at very reduced rates.

• Principle: Transparent pricing, no hidden fees, with real value proportionate to the organization's size.

5) Global Distribution Plan with Free Channels

• Search engine optimization: Create practical guides about digital file accessibility in easy-to-understand form targeting global search terms.

• Free tool lists: List the product in reputable free tool directories without paying fees.

• Technical communities: Meaningful participation in developer and UX communities, focusing on solving problems rather than direct advertising.

• Educational content: Publish free checklists and short success stories to attract compliance-responsible teams.

• Word-of-mouth: Encourage teams to share their publicly shareable reports, creating organic global reach.

• Engagement with non-profit organizations: Free collaboration with digital accessibility organizations to raise awareness and gain initial trust.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal - False claim that the file is "fully compliant" with standards: Use precise terms like "list of key violations" and add a clear disclaimer in every report.

• Legal - Differences in interpretation between international standards (WCAG / EN 301 549): Focus on the most common and widely accepted requirements, and note that the scan is advisory and not a substitute for specialized legal review.

• Technical - Complexity of some PDF structures or scanned documents: Support text-based files first, clearly warn about scanned files, and suggest alternatives when needed.

• Technical - False positives or false negatives in automated scanning: Allow users to report scan errors and improve rules gradually based on real feedback.

• Technical - Browser performance limitations for large files: Split processing, show clear progress, and set a reasonable file limit for the free version with explicit clarification.
