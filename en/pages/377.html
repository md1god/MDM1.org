Lahnix — Full Project Plan

Write your lyrics and hear your full song in a minute — free.

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a free in-browser music studio

1) Target audience

1. Short-form content creators (TikTok, Reels, Shorts) with no music production budget.

2. Hobbyists, songwriters, and music students in the Arab world, especially those who write lyrics but own no instruments.

3. Game developers, podcasters, and video creators who need royalty-free music.

4. Small agencies and solo projects seeking a sonic identity or ad jingles at minimal cost.

5. Developers and AI artists looking for runnable, editable open weights models.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Truly free, no credit card: local in-browser execution via WebGPU when available, otherwise a free daily cloud queue.

• Open weights models with open licenses: transparency, fine-tuning, and no commercial lock-in.

• Complete outputs: a full song or an instrumental, with stems separation (vocals/drums/instruments/bass) and WAV 24-bit and MP3 export.

• Precise control: style tags, duration, BPM, musical key, and segment editing after generation.

• Private work library with share links and a public artist page, with an optional no-storage mode.

• Arabic-first interface with full RTL support plus English, and two speeds: draft in seconds, high quality on demand.

• Outputs belong to the user, with usage rights clarified in the terms of service.

3) First release scope (max 8 tasks)

1. Single editor: lyrics field + style description field, text or audio generation only.

2. Connect two open weights models (first a free cloud service, then a local WebGPU option).

3. Task queue with a progress bar, plus regeneration and extension from the stopping point.

4. Clip library: save, search, tags, rename, delete.

5. MP3/WAV export + simple three-track stems separation.

6. Account by email or as a guest, with a clear free daily request limit.

7. "Chips" usage dashboard + chip purchases at token value via a ready-made payment gateway.

8. One public share page + Arabic/English localization + mobile responsiveness.

4) Pricing and revenue model

· Plan · Price · Includes ·

· Free · $0 · 10 draft generations per day, MP3 export, 50-track library ·

· Chips · $5 per 1000 chips · High quality, priority queue, WAV export — expires after 12 months ·

· Studio · $12/month · 300 chips + full stems + commercial use + long track uploads ·

· API/Commercial · $49/month or per audio minute · Integration for developers and production agencies ·

Additional revenue streams: paid ready-made music templates, tutorials, and sponsorship of free templates. Pricing covers GPU cost only, while light usage stays free.

5) Global rollout plan using free channels

• SEO templates: dedicated pages like "free music for podcasts", "congratulations song for occasion X", and "royalty-free music for YouTube".

• Short video: "I wrote lyrics → the result" clips on YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels, and Snapchat.

• Developer communities: Reddit, GitHub Discussions, Hacker News "Show HN", and Hugging Face Spaces for a live demo.

• Arabic channels: X, Telegram, Discord rooms, and songwriting groups on Facebook.

• Discovery directories: Product Hunt, the "There's An AI For That" directory, and Futurepedia.

• Small creator partnerships: free use in exchange for a credit in the description, plus monthly contests with chip prizes.

• Weekly newsletter featuring the top five generations + reposting the best user works.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Potential claims over training data of open models → rely on models with documented licenses and legal review at launch.

• Risk of imitating famous voices → explicit prohibition in the terms + voice fingerprint check on upload.

• Clarity of output ownership and compliance with model licenses (Apache-2.0 / MIT / CC).

• Data protection (GDPR), user content policy, and usage restrictions in some countries.

Technical:

• GPU cost volatility and its impact on the free plan → strict daily limits and abuse detection.

• Weak Arabic singing pronunciation in current models → continuous improvement and pre-generated templates.

• Uneven WebGPU availability → always keep a cloud fallback path.

• Slow generation and weak connectivity → a persisted queue with state that resumes after reload.
