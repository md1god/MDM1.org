SANAD — Complete Project Plan

From quote request to delivery of a traceable product, SANAD manages your entire factory on a single database.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the profit model, the rollout plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: An ERP/MES/QMS system for manufacturing (a new original product)

1) Target audience

• Small and medium-sized factories (SMEs) and manufacturing workshops that operate on a "Make-to-Order" basis.

• Startups in the fields of devices and materials, food, cosmetics, furniture, and mechanical parts.

• Quality and compliance teams that need lot and serial number traceability, in preparation for obligations such as ISO 9001, 21 CFR Part 11, and EU MDR.

• Emerging markets first (where current solutions are expensive or English-only), then global expansion.

2) Points of differentiation (why choose our product)

• A single schema on PostgreSQL: pricing, inventory, shop floor, quality, and traceability in one database, instead of linking 4–5 systems.

• Free to start and self-hosted, with no limits or setup fees.

• API-first + built-in MCP server to run intelligent agents and automate workflows.

• Native Arabic/English support with an RTL interface.

• Native lot and serial number traceability by design, not as an add-on.

• Works in low-connectivity environments with later sync for the factory floor.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum of 8 tasks)

1. Pricing and quote issuance (Quoting).

2. Sales and purchase orders and suppliers.

3. Inventory, warehouses, and minimum stock.

4. Bill of materials (BOM) and simplified requirements planning (MRP).

5. Work orders and shop floor management.

6. Quality (QMS): inspection and deviation records.

7. Lot and serial number traceability (Lot/Serial Traceability).

8. REST API + MCP server + authentication and multi-role permissions.

4) Pricing and profit model

• Community: fully free self-hosting (open license).

• Cloud Free: a limited free plan (one user, one factory, limited storage).

• Cloud Pro: monthly subscription per user or by capacity, with managed backup and support.

• Additional revenue: implementation and migration, training, custom integrations, enterprise support (SLA), private hosting.

5) Global rollout plan using free channels

• GitHub for the open repository, and Product Hunt and Hacker News for launch.

• SEO content and educational YouTube videos about digital manufacturing, traceability, and quality.

• Reddit and Discord communities specialized in manufacturing and open-source software.

• Publishing templates and documentation, an Arabic translation, and community contributions.

• Free listings on G2 and Capterra, and partnerships with industry chambers and universities.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: project licensing and contributor rights; data privacy (GDPR) and sensitive factory data; not claiming regulatory certifications (certification is the customer's responsibility); product safety and health liability.

• Technical: complexity of offline sync and security of API endpoints and the MCP server; multi-tenancy and isolation; PostgreSQL performance as data grows; migrating customer data from legacy systems; resistance to copying and impersonation.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: SANAD

Marketing tagline: From quote request to delivery of a traceable product, SANAD manages your entire factory on a single database.
