KayanDB — Complete Project Plan

"Ask your data and get the answer in an instant."

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

Product Plan: A Real-Time Analytics Database

1) Target Audience

• Product & Growth teams at startups and mid-sized companies that need real-time analytics dashboards.

• Data Engineering engineers at SaaS companies building reports for their customers (Multi-tenant Analytics).

• Business and marketing analysts in the Middle East, Africa, and Asia looking for a simple solution with a low entry cost.

• Game developers and e-commerce app developers tracking user behavior moment by moment.

2) Differentiation points (why choose our product)

• Sub-second queries across millions of rows with a vectorized execution engine written from scratch.

• One-click activation: a free cloud version that runs within two minutes without setting up servers.

• Simplified SQL + a natural-language query interface that generates SQL automatically.

• Ready-made integrations: Grafana, Kafka, Segment, mobile apps, and CSV/Parquet import.

• Transparent pricing and an open-core model: the local version is free forever.

• Excellent Arabic support in the documentation and interface, and data residency in Europe and the Gulf.

3) Scope of the first version (max 8 tasks)

1. A compressed columnar storage engine with indexing and automatic partitioning.

2. SQL layer: parsing and batch queries with vectorized execution and a compressed result schema.

3. Real-time data ingestion: Kafka + HTTP ingest, and batch import of CSV/Parquet.

4. Materialized Views and automatic rollups to speed up recurring reports.

5. Access interfaces: REST/gRPC + Python and Node.js libraries.

6. Web interface: a query editor + basic dashboards with sharing.

7. Security: authentication, roles, quotas, encryption in transit and at rest.

8. Deployment: a Docker image + a free cloud version (free tier) + full documentation.

4) Pricing and profit model

• Free to start: one node, 10 GB, one million queries per month, and community support.

• Pro: $29/month — 100 GB, backups, alerts, support within 24 hours.

• Business: $299/month — tenant isolation, SSO, SLA, specified data residency.

• Self-hosted Enterprise: annual license + support and engineering consulting (the main profit).

• Additional revenue: extra data hosting and automated report export.

5) Global rollout plan with free channels

• A core open-source repository with a README and a live interactive demo online.

• Writing performance-comparison articles and tutorials on Dev.to, Medium, Hashnode in multiple languages.

• Short clips on YouTube, X/Twitter, and LinkedIn showing query speed.

• Posting on Hacker News, Reddit (r/dataengineering), Product Hunt.

• A Discord community + contributions of ready-made templates (Grafana, Metabase).

• SEO on the documentation: pages like "How do I build real-time analytics dashboards".

• Participating in meetups and local conferences as speakers (with free virtual attendance).

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: compliance with an open-source license (such as AGPL) and avoiding any copying of code or conventions; trademark protection and early domain reservation; GDPR/PDPL compliance and documenting personal data processing.

• Technical: correctness of standard SQL results, handling failure and continuity, concurrency of ingestion and querying, the higher development cost of an engine built from scratch, and the difficulty of attracting distributed systems engineers.

• Commercial: entry of major players at low prices — competitiveness through ease of use, price, and the local market.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: KayanDB

Marketing tagline: "Ask your data and get the answer in an instant."
