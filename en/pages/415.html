WASL — Complete Project Plan

Your voice agent that answers, books, and connects — without writing a single line of code.

Complete execution plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product Plan: AI Voice Agent Builder for Small Businesses

1) Target Audience

• Primary sector: Clinics and local services (dental clinics, aesthetics, family clinics), salons and spa centers, auto repair centers, real estate offices, small transport and shipping companies.

• Decision maker: Business owner or operations manager (not IT team).

• Signals that make them ready to buy:

• They miss calls during peak hours or after hours.

• They pay a full salary for a receptionist or customer service.

• Appointments are frequently cancelled or double-booked.

• First geographic market: Gulf and Levant countries (local calls, common electronic payments), then expansion to English-speaking markets.

• Segment size: Businesses with 2 to 50 employees, where the cost of missed calls exceeds the monthly subscription value.

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

1. Arabic-first, not a later translation: Speech synthesis (TTS) and speech recognition (ASR) models trained on Gulf, Egyptian, and Levantine dialects, with natural mixing of Arabic and English in the same sentence.

2. Built by conversation, not templates: User describes their agent's function in one Arabic sentence ("I want a dental appointment, ask about insurance, transfer emergencies to doctor's number"), and the flow is built automatically.

3. Ready integration in two minutes: Direct integration with Google/Outlook Calendars, WhatsApp Business, and WhatsApp, and common booking systems, with zero code writing.

4. Full transparency: Dashboard logs every call with its transcript, customer satisfaction score, and reason for human transfer — no "black box".

5. Clear economics: Call cost far less than a receptionist, with a free plan covering a real monthly call volume, not just a trial.

6. Human control when needed: Smart call transfer to a real number with instant summary for the human agent before answering.

3) MVP Scope (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. Drag-and-drop agent builder + text description that auto-generates the flow.

2. Receive real phone calls via Twilio integration (local number).

3. Arabic voice understanding and response (ASR + TTS) with low latency.

4. Book, confirm, and modify appointments via single calendar integration (Google Calendar).

5. Capture lead info and save to simple table + CSV export.

6. Transfer call to human number with instant summary.

7. Dashboard: Call recordings, transcripts, and metrics (missed calls, booked appointments).

8. Free + paid plans with Stripe payment integration.

(Out of initial scope: Multi-agent, mobile app, multiple integrations, advanced analytics.)

4) Pricing & Revenue Model

| Plan | Price | Includes |

|------|-------|----------|

| Free | $0 | Shared number, 50 calls/month, 1 agent, basic recording |

| Growth | $49/month | 500 calls, dedicated local number, calendar, human transfer, no watermark |

| Pro | $149/month | 2000 calls, multi-agent, advanced reports, priority support |

| Add-ons | Usage-based | Extra minutes, extra numbers, extra languages/dialects |

• Revenue model: Recurring monthly subscription (SaaS) + share of call minutes after overage.

• Free to start: No credit card, to lower trial barrier.

• Target margin: ≥ 70% after deducting connectivity and voice costs.

5) Global Distribution Plan via Free Channels

1. Static demo video: Short clip recording a real call starting in Arabic then another dialect — posted on TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels three times weekly.

2. Indie communities: Participation in small business owner groups on Facebook, LinkedIn, and Telegram, with a "Try your agent in 60 seconds" free tool from the website.

3. SEO content blocks: Comparison and solution pages for each vertical ("Best booking system for dental clinic", "Auto-reply for restaurant calls") in three languages.

4. Referral program: Free month for both parties when inviting a business owner.

5. Opportunistic partnerships: With POS providers, local software companies, and accounting firms — mutual distribution with no first commission.

6. Public demo number: Publicly announced number anyone can call to try and discover the product instantly (virtual customer service front).

7. Product communities: Launch on Product Hunt and Arabic alternatives, with localized landing page per market.
