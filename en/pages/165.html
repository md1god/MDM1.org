Maabar — Complete Project Plan

Open internet for everyone, securely, fast, and without borders.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a decentralized relay network to bypass internet censorship

1) Target audience

• Users in regions with heavy internet censorship (the Middle East, Central Asia, parts of Asia and Africa).

• Journalists, activists, and researchers who need secure, anonymous access.

• Students and developers who rely on blocked educational and software resources.

• Expats and families who want access to services in their home countries.

• Small organizations and startups operating across borders.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Fully decentralized architecture: there is no single point of failure, as the network is built on distributed volunteer nodes.

• Advanced traffic obfuscation: modern protocols (WireGuard/QUIC) with an obfuscation layer that makes them hard to detect via DPI.

• Privacy first: no logs, and end-to-end encrypted connections.

• Truly free to start: the user connects within seconds with no payment card or complex setup.

• Smart routing (Split Tunneling): routing only blocked sites through the network, saving speed and battery.

• Open-source core: builds trust and accelerates adoption and contributions.

• Multi-platform support with a single, unified, easy interface for non-technical users.

• Rewarding node operators: free quota in exchange for sharing bandwidth, boosting self-sustaining network growth.

3) Scope of the first version (a maximum of 8 tasks)

1. Desktop client (Windows / macOS / Linux) with a simple one-click interface.

2. Basic Android app including connection and smart routing.

3. Smart obfuscation protocol with automatic switching when censorship is detected.

4. Volunteer node network with secure and easy registration and joining.

5. Distribution of connection points and automatic selection of the nearest/fastest.

6. Account and authentication system with a clear monthly free quota.

7. Network status dashboard with speed and connection measurement.

8. Basic mechanisms for anti-abuse and node protection.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free-to-start tier: a limited monthly bandwidth quota via public nodes, no payment card.

• Premium subscription (Pro): wider bandwidth, higher speed and priority, dedicated nodes, and technical support.

• Family/Team plan: multiple devices and users at a reduced price.

• Node sharing (Earn): the user gets additional free bandwidth in exchange for running a node.

• Revenue: paid subscriptions + licenses for teams/organizations + transparent community sponsorship.

5) Global rollout plan using free channels

• Publishing the open-source core on hosting platforms and attracting contributors.

• Launching on tech product communities (Product Hunt, Hacker News).

• Reddit and Telegram communities specialized in privacy and the free internet.

• Educational content in Arabic and English about privacy and bypassing censorship (SEO and blog).

• Partnerships with universities, media institutions, and civil-society groups.

• Full Arabization and multi-language localization to expand the base globally.

• An ambassadors and contributors program that rewards referring new users and nodes.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• The possibility of the app being banned or its use criminalized in some countries; we adopt a clear legal structure and transparent policies.

• Pressure to disclose data; we solve this with a no-logs architecture and information separation.

• Liability for node misuse; we set strict usage policies and terms of service.

Technical:

• Detection and blocking of network addresses via DPI; we handle it with obfuscation, switching, and flexibility.

• Uneven node performance and fluctuation; we compensate with smart distribution and redundancy.

• Scaling, security, and attack challenges; we rely on continuous auditing and periodic penetration testing.

• Protecting volunteer nodes from exploitation; we add rate limiting and abuse filtering.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline.

Proposed name: Maabar (مَعْبَر)

Marketing tagline: Open internet for everyone, securely, fast, and without borders.
