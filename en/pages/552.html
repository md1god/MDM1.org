مَطبَخ — Complete Project Plan

Organized cooking.. easier life for every family.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, business model, expansion plan, and risks. The project is under development and has not been launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Families: Those looking for an organized way to save and share home cooking recipes.

• Students and single people: Those who want to cook simple meals at minimal cost.

• Cooking enthusiasts: Those who collect recipes from multiple sources and need to categorize them easily.

2) Points of Distinction (Why choose our product)

• Very simple: A lightweight interface with no complexity, suitable for any user regardless of their experience.

• Smart to use: Ability to automatically create a shopping list from selected recipes.

• Family-friendly: Share folders and recipes between family members in one place.

• Works offline: Ability to save and use recipes locally, with the option to sync when needed.

• Privacy-focused: All data remains under the user's control, with no forced unnecessary sharing.

3) Scope of the First Version (Max 8 tasks)

1. Recipe management: Add, edit, delete, and search for recipes.

2. Categorization and organization: Sort recipes by category, cuisine, or favorites.

3. Shopping lists: Generate a smart shopping list from recipe ingredients.

4. Local storage: Full data storage on the device with no account required.

5. Reading mode: Clear display of steps and ingredients on a single screen.

6. Quick import: Add a recipe by pasting text or a simple link.

7. Language support: Bilingual interface with easy switching.

8. Backup: Export and import data in a secure and protected format.

4) Pricing and Profit Model

• Completely free to start: All features of the first version are available for free.

• Paid family plan: A small subscription for unlimited syncing between family devices and additional cloud storage.

• Template store: Sell optional shopping list templates or meal plan templates to users who are interested.

• Focus on value: No ads, and core features will remain free forever.

5) Global Expansion Plan with Free Channels

• Food communities: Participate in forums and home cooking groups with useful advice.

• Open source platforms: Publish the project with clear and attractive documentation for developers and users.

• Social media: Create short content about quick recipes using the product.

• Free app directories: List in relevant open and free app directories.

• Word of mouth: Focus on a simple experience that makes families recommend it to each other.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal:

• Content rights: Ensure no protected recipes or published images are used without permission.

• Privacy: Comply with data protection laws when adding any future cloud features.

• Technical:

• Poor performance: Avoid feature bloat to maintain the app's speed and lightness.

• Device compatibility: Ensure good performance on different devices and screens.

• Data storage: Provide a reliable backup mechanism to avoid losing recipes.

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Slogan

Proposed name: مَطبَخ

Marketing slogan: Organized cooking.. easier life for every family.
