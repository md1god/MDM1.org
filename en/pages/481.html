Riwaq — Complete Project Plan

Your community grows as knowledge, not noise.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target audience

• Small and medium communities (100 to 50,000 members) that want a permanent discussion space instead of fast chat.

• Open-source projects, product teams, educational organizations, and Arabic communities that lack strong RTL support.

• Content creators and trainers who want ownership of their data, templates, and visual identity.

• Teams that need lightweight self-hosting on a small server or simple managed hosting.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Arabic first: native RTL interface, optimized Arabic fonts, and search that understands Arabic morphology.

• Genuine lightness: one executable + SQLite by default, running on a 512MB server without Redis or extra services.

• Knowledge before noise: every discussion becomes a weekly summary documented with sources, plus pinned reference answers.

• Transparent moderation: trust levels, queues, public audit log, and spam controls without preemptive blocking.

• Data ownership: full export in an open format, automatic backups, and free self-hosting.

• Fair pricing: no per-seat pricing, no ads, and no data selling.

3) First-version scope (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Create sections, topics, and replies, Markdown editor, full RTL support, and basic attachments.

2. Email or OAuth registration, invitations, roles, and initial trust levels.

3. Moderation: reports, review queue, temporary hiding, muting, and audit log.

4. Full-text search + automated weekly summary with source links inside the discussion.

5. Email and Web Push notifications, and customizable digests.

6. Branding: logo, colors, landing page, and custom domain.

7. Admin panel: statistics, backups, and CSV/Markdown import.

8. One-click deployment: Docker, SQLite by default and optional PostgreSQL, and full data export.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free self-hosting: open-source core with unlimited members.

• Free cloud: up to 100 members, subdomain, 5GB storage, basic weekly summary.

• Growth — $19/month: up to 1,000 members, custom domain, 50GB, smart summaries, and integrations.

• Scale — $49/month: up to 10,000 members, SSO, advanced moderation, 250GB, and priority support.

• Enterprise: managed self-hosting, SLA, regional hosting, and white label.

• Additional revenue: paid support, extra AI usage, paid templates, and managed migration.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Programmatic SEO: comparison pages and guides in Arabic, English, French, and Spanish, with live interactive demos.

• Open source: public repository, excellent documentation, ready-made templates, and a contributor program.

• Launch: Product Hunt, Hacker News, Indie Hackers, and open-source product communities.

• Integrations: a bridge with Discord/Slack, WordPress plugin, and Webhooks and Zapier.

• Local communities: partnerships with universities, accelerators, and tech organizations, and ambassadors for each language.

• Content: YouTube tutorials, email newsletter, and case studies for small communities.

• Hosting: partnerships with VPS providers for one-click installation.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: GDPR/CCPA compliance, data processing agreements, a clear content policy, and DMCA/DSA procedures.

• Privacy and AI: explicit consent, no use of content for training, and the option to disable summaries entirely.

• Licensing and trademark: a clear open-source license, protection of the name and trademark, and review of external contributions.

• Technical: fighting spam and automated accounts, email deliverability, and Markdown security against XSS.

• Performance scaling: search and real-time subscriptions under load at thousands of members, with a clear upgrade path to PostgreSQL.

• AI: variable cost, possible hallucination, and the need for human review of summaries.

• Migration: reliable import tools from popular forums, and a tested rollback and backup plan.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Riwaq

Marketing tagline: Your community grows as knowledge, not noise.
