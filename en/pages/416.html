Mowasl — Full Project Plan

Aware of your prospect's timing, writes in their language, and books your meeting.

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout plan, and risks. The project is under development and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a self-serve sales agent platform ([hidden source])

1) Target Audience

• Primary: small and midsize SaaS and digital-services companies (10–200 employees) with a monthly/yearly subscription product and no full in-house sales team.

• Secondary: agencies and consultancies selling mid-value contracts ($2k–$20k) that rely on outbound.

• Buyer persona: the founder, Head of Growth, or sales lead, struggling with: high cost per SDR, poor data quality, time wasted on manual research, and impersonal messages that get ignored.

• First markets: the Gulf and North Africa and Arabic- and English-speaking audiences, then Europe and Latin America.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

1. An agent that speaks the prospect's language: bilingual message generation (Arabic/English) that respects professional communication norms in each market — not machine translation.

2. An intent-signals detection engine: job changes, hiring, new executive appointments, funding news, and blog activity to find the right outreach timing, not just static lists.

3. Automatic multi-channel sequences: email + LinkedIn + SMS, with smart channel rotation and automatic stop on reply.

4. Meeting booking and full reply handling: answers FAQs, offers available times, and updates the CRM with no human input.

5. Low deal threshold + human control: a "approve before send" setting for sensitive conversations, or a fully self-serve mode for small deals.

6. Transparency dashboard: every message and every data source is auditable, with a log of why the agent chose that prospect.

7. Genuinely free start: monthly free credits with no credit card, so growth comes from virality rather than upfront sales.

3) Scope of the First Release (max 8 tasks)

1. Enter company profile and define the Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) in natural language.

2. Import a prospect list (CSV) or pull one from a public data source.

3. Generate intent signals and score prospects.

4. Write and format personalized messages (email and LinkedIn) with A/B testing.

5. Multi-channel sending with scheduling, timing, and automatic stop on reply.

6. Automatic replies to FAQs + calendar booking.

7. Basic CRM integration (one CRM, HubSpot) with status sync.

8. Dashboard: reply rates, booked meetings, cost per meeting, and a full audit log.

Out of first release: financial deal closing, voice/video calls, multiple integrations, mobile app.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

· Plan · Price · Includes ·

· Free · $0/mo · 150 messages/mo, one channel, light watermark ·

· Growth · $49/mo · 1,500 messages, two channels, CRM integration, email support ·

• Professional · $199/mo · 6,000 messages, advanced intent signals, approve before send, priority support ·

· Enterprise · custom · dedicated servers, data compliance, success manager ·

• Revenue components: extra messages (500-message packs at $15), numbers/meeting booking, paid integrations.

• Cost of goods sold (COGS): LLM usage + data + email, covered with a margin of ≥75% on the Growth and Professional plans.

• Free-to-paid: conversion via higher message limits and unlocking advanced intent signals.

5) Global Rollout Plan with Free Channels

1. Comparison content: "human SDR team cost vs. agent" tables and downloadable digital case studies (SEO).

2. Free micro-tool: a "smart follow-up message generator" that works with no signup, watermarked with the brand.

3. Communities and groups: genuine participation in sales and growth communities on LinkedIn, Slack/Discord, and niche Reddit forums.

4. Content partnerships: interviews and podcasts about the changing role of the SDR, with referral links.

5. Referral program: message credits for each referred customer, plus a public profile page.

6. Free templates: message template packs in Arabic and English for industries (SaaS, real estate, services).

7. Events and competitions: startup and accelerator communities, and public product launches on launch platforms.

8. Organic growth through the product: every booked meeting produces a shareable public page.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Data protection laws (GDPR/PDPL) and data-subject consent for processing their data.

• Anti-spam laws (CAN-SPAM, CASL) and unsubscribe requirements.

• Telecom regulations such as TCPA and Do Not Call lists when using SMS.

• LinkedI
