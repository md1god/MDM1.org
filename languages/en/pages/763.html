Dallal — Complete Project Plan

Describe someone you love in plain words and get five suitable gifts in a minute with live prices, alternatives, and clear reasons.

New digital project plan: idea, audience, product, revenue, growth, risks. Not launched.

1) Target audience

• Hurried givers (25–45): 10–20 min before an occasion, unsure what fits a relative; "sister loves reading, 200 riyals" → five options, not 200 results.

• Families: a six-year-old's gift, no small parts, one line on fit for confidence.

• Expatriates: sending abroad; arrival 3 days pre-holiday, riyal/dirham/pound/dollar conversion, no manual math.

• Teams and associations: HR, 12–40 staff, 100–250 riyals each, one file of names, gifts, reasons.

• Mobile-first (~70%): 4G, average link, colloquial Arabic + English, no forced sign-up; list opens in <2 sec.

2) Why choose us

• Explained list, not a black box: three tags per gift ("Reading match", "Under budget", "Before holiday"), a reason from the request's words, not a polished blurb.

• Live prices, three alternatives: 15-min refresh; 20% cheaper, matching, 20% pricier; "Updated 3 min ago" stamp, no post-click surprises.

• Neutral ranking, public formula: 40% fit + 25% price + 20% availability + 15% shipping; no paid placement or hidden "sponsorship".

• Free from the start: ten monthly lists, no card; paid tier only adds alerts and export, no lockout for beginners.

• Explicit privacy: request kept 24 h then auto-deleted, no data sales, "Delete now" per list, clear usage policy.

3) Version 1 scope (8 tasks max)

1. Request parser: MSA, colloquial Arabic, English; extracts age, relation, interests, budget, occasion, exclusions; max 2 clarifying questions.

2. Catalog connector: 3 sources via official APIs or structured data, unified currency/price, 15-min cache, backup server on failure.

3. Ranking and explanation engine: score /100, three reasons from request words, reject <0.7 rather than guess.

4. Results UI: five gifts, two alternatives each, price/shipping filters, share link, "Last updated" bar, 360 px responsive.

5. Safety layer: block weapons/alcohol unless requested, filter counterfeits, warn if shipping nears the holiday, drop prices >30 min old.

6. Measurement and privacy: four events (list created, gift click, stale price, share), weekly Arabic dashboard, auto-delete after 24 h.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free (default): ten monthly lists, five gifts + two alternatives each, no account/card; main path, not a limited teaser.

• "Dallal Plus": 29 riyals/month (~$8) or 290/year; unlimited lists, price-drop alerts, PDF, family voting link.

• Team plan: 149 riyals per list of up to 25 recipients, invoice + CSV; companies/associations only, individual stays free.

• Commerce commission: 4%–8% on purchases within 24 h of a click, disclosed at page bottom; no higher commission for higher ranking.

• Trust first: no ads in results in version 1, no request-data sales, commercial links marked "Buy link" so not read as neutral advice.

5) Free-channel global growth

• Structured search content: 120 template pages (e.g. "Gifts for readers, 200 riyals", "Gift for a six-year-old before Eid"), English, FAQ tags, weekly price refresh; no stale pages.

• Sharing loop: WhatsApp/Telegram card of three gifts and "Make your list", reaching recipients who may use it later themselves.

• Community: five daily answers to real questions in Facebook groups and Arabic gift forums, with a tailored list, not generic ads.

• Unpaid content partnerships: five Eid/Christmas newsletters and blogs, co-branded results page naming both, for free reader access.

• Targeted launch: free startup-directory and launch-platform listings, plus "Free holiday list for associations" for 50+ recipients for a mention.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: disclose commission, don't use store names as our trademark, comply with Saudi/EU data rules, state deletion policy plainly.

• Price accuracy: prices change after display; timestamp, re-check at click, "Price may differ" beyond 30 min.

• Technical: store APIs change or blocked; independent data reconciliation layer, two backups, hourly auto failure checks.

• Recommendation quality: counterfeits or wrong age for a child; blocklist, seller filtering, 50 lists human-reviewed weekly, exclusion reason on request.

• Cost and latency: language model queries may top 3 sec; one-query summary, identical-result caching, 500-token cap per list.

7) Suggested name and tagline

Suggested name: Dallal

Tagline: Describe someone you love in plain words and get five suitable gifts in a minute with live prices, alternatives, and clear reasons.
