Nabdesk — Full Project Plan

Ask in a minute, understand your answers in a minute.

A full implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the rollout plan, and the risks. The project is in development and has not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Universities and schools: student research, instructor evaluations, short quizzes.

• Small and medium teams (10–200 employees): employee satisfaction, performance reviews, internal surveys.

• Marketers and content creators: audience polls, voting, product feedback.

• Nonprofits and local initiatives: field data collection on limited resources.

• Freelancers and consultants: anyone who needs a quick analytics dashboard for a client without building reports manually.

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• AI-powered creation: the user writes a single idea, and a complete survey is generated with questions, branching logic, and randomization.

• Analytics anyone can understand: instead of raw spreadsheets, the product presents a written summary (strengths, outliers, similar segments) with a ready-to-download chart.

• Quick-read mode: mobile-first experience, sub-second load time, and offline mode that saves answers and syncs them later.

• Ready-made templates by domain (education, HR, product, public opinion) in multiple languages.

• Open integrations: API, Webhooks, and a plugin installable inside popular websites.

• Optional user hosting: a fast cloud version, or self-hosting for those who require their data to stay in place.

3) Scope of the First Version (8 Tasks Maximum)

1. Survey builder: core question types (choice, Likert scale, text, multiple choice, categorization).

2. Conditional branching logic and random questions from a question bank.

3. Publishing via link, QR code, and embedding inside a web page.

4. Answer collection with offline sync.

5. Analytics dashboard: charts, sort/filter, and CSV export.

6. Team management and permissions + folders for projects.

7. Ready-made templates + AI question generator.

8. Accounts and automated billing (free plan and paid plan).

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free to start: 3 active surveys, 100 responses per month, light branding, basic analytics — no credit card required.

• Professional: $12/month billed annually (or $15 monthly) — 25 surveys, 5,000 responses, branding removal, advanced exports, expanded AI generator.

• Enterprise: from $79/month — higher limits, SSO, audit logs, optional self-hosting.

• Secondary revenue: fees on paid surveys (registration tickets / proctored results), a marketplace of free and paid templates, and a business API.

• First-year goal: convert 3–5% of free users to paid, and reduce hosting costs through smart limits.

5) Global Rollout Plan Using Free Channels

• SEO: multilingual articles on “how to write a good question” + 50+ free browseable templates targeting long-tail keywords.

• Product Hunt / Hacker News / Reddit: launch in both Arabic and English, with a weekly “what’s new” page.

• YouTube, TikTok, and Reels: 60-second explanations for each question type or analysis, in the local content language.

• User communities: Facebook and LinkedIn groups for teachers and marketers, plus an ambassador program (free credits in exchange for a review or template).

• Partnerships: colleges and statistics departments, open education platforms, and developer communities for the API.

• Product-led growth: public sharing of survey results as a report page that automatically carries the product’s branding.

• Marketplaces: listing in popular tool stores for passive acquisition.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

Legal:

• Personal data protection and local residency/storage requirements in each market (GDPR, CCPA, local data protection regulations) — adopting clear consent, a data deletion/export tool, and processing agreements with suppliers.

• Privacy when collecting sensitive health or educational data: data classification, encryption, and minimizing what is collected.

• Liability for user-generated survey content: acceptable use policy, reporting, and immediate suspension.

• Intellectual property for templates and AI rights over outputs, plus clarity on open-source library licenses.

• Taxes and cross-border billing when expanding.

Technical:

• Misuse of automated generation (cheating on tests or fraudulent surveys) → adding a human safety layer and usage limits.

• Generation and smart-analysis pricing efficiency under rapid growth.

• Browser and older-device compatibility, and weak connectivity.

• Self-hosting privacy
