Turn your vertical software into a mini-bank for its customers.

Complete execution plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and not yet launched.

Product plan: Embedded financial layer for vertical platforms

1) Target Audience

• Vertical B2B SaaS platforms in three initial sectors: maintenance and small contracting, clinics and health inspections, and property management companies.

• Companies listed on these platforms (contractors, clinics, unit owners) that conduct payments, supply, and payroll among themselves.

• Founders/product managers at these platforms (3–50 employees) as integration decision-makers.

• Avoid early stages: massive marketplaces, and traditional banks (long sales cycle).

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• True vertical customization: Ready-to-use SDKs and UIs with sector terminology (plumber invoice instead of "payment", inspection request instead of "transaction"). The vertical platform displays financial screens that look like part of its brand, not an external provider.

• Start with one wallet per sector: Instead of offering everything at once, we begin with a single high-value path (recurring payment collection for small facilities) then unlock financing and payroll as plug-in modules.

• Risk scoring engine tied to sector data: Because the vertical platform knows the customer's income and consistency, we use this operational data to offer short-term financing instead of generic credit models.

• Dashboard for the vertical platform: We give it tools to launch financial services, set its margin, and track revenue — it's a commercial partner, not just a technical client.

• Integration in days, not weeks: Full sandbox environment, ready webhook models, and Arabic/English documentation with ready-made examples for each sector.

• Customizable compliance per market: We build legal isolation (legal wrapper) for each region with a local partner bank, so the vertical platform expands geographically without rebuilding integration.

3) V1 Scope (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. Embedded payment gateway (Payments API + Checkout/Invoice element): Collect one-time and recurring payments, support cards and local bank transfers, and receipts in the vertical platform's name.

2. Web + Mobile SDKs + Two webhook sets with documentation and a quickstart example for each target sector.

3. Vertical platform dashboard: Activate payment UI, set fees (markup), track revenue and daily settlements.

4. Settlement accounts (ledger) per sub-merchant: Separate vertical platform customers' funds from its own (automatic commission split).

5. Short-term financing path first as a prepared waitlist: Early nomination flow built on platform payment data, with loans from a financing partner (we don't carry credit risk in V1).

6. Full sandbox environment + test keys + Mock Bank for testing.

7. Sales pages and internal platform ready for launch (self-serve signup + onboarding) with free-to-start model.

8. Initial support and ops team: SLA, status page, and simplified dispute resolution mechanism.

4) Pricing & Revenue Model

• Free to start: No setup fees or monthly fees initially; commission from payments only (e.g., 1.4% + 0.30 per transaction, per market). The vertical platform sets its margin on top of this price — its incentive to adopt.

• Financing revenue: Arrangement commission (referral fee) on facilitated short-term financing (percentage of the commission earned by the financing partner).

• Payroll and deferred payments (later): Flexible monthly fees after exceeding a certain flow volume (usage tiers).

• Optional Scale tier: Key rotation, SSO, additional compliance certifications, and dedicated support — monthly fees starting only when the platform requests.

• No setup fees or monthly minimums to keep the barrier to entry near zero.

5) Global Go-to-Market Plan via Free Channels

• Free sector catalog: Sector-specific solution pages (maintenance, clinics, real estate) in local market language + pages matched to search intent (SEO).

• Open examples and GitHub templates: Complete integration models ready to copy for each sector, attracting developers via search and sharing.

• Developer community and forum + Discord: Publicly archivable answers, build-in-public weeks, online hackathon events with partners.

• Partnerships with small vertical SaaS platforms: Mutual referral program, in exchange for featuring the platform file and marketer inside product meetings.

• Deep operational content: Repo
