مدى — Complete Project Plan

Book your hotel through مدى at a final price with no surprises, and track all your trip details in one WhatsApp chat.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, expansion plan, and risks. The project is currently under development and has not yet been launched.

1) Target Audience

• Primary: Arab travelers (Gulf/Egyptian/Levantine), aged 25–45, who book hotels 2–8 times per year and struggle with: hidden fees that appear after selecting a room, unclear cancellation policies, and difficulty communicating after booking.

• Secondary: Small independent hotels and apartments (1–30 rooms) that lack a marketing budget or a dedicated bookings team.

• Tertiary: Small travel agencies and companies that book travel for employees and need documented bookings with clear invoices.

2) Key Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• Transparent final price: The checkout screen displays the base price + fees + tax + total before confirmation, with no hidden items.

• Booking follow-up via WhatsApp: All updates (confirmation, changes, reminders, invoices) are sent in the same chat instead of switching between apps or email.

• Smart Arabic travel assistant: Recommends a full itinerary (city → hotel → activities) based on budget, departure date, and children's budget, showing alternatives only at real prices available on the platform.

• Empowering independent hotels: A free dashboard to manage rooms, prices, and bookings, allowing hotels to shift from agency clients to direct sellers.

• Understandable contractual flexibility: Cancellation, breakfast, and early check-in policies are written in simple Arabic, with a visual comparison between two options.

• Reliable reviews: Reviews are only shown from guests who have actually checked in, with independent hotels clearly distinguished from hotel chains.

• Authentic Arabic identity and experience: Full RTL support, Arabic fonts, Arabic customer support, and lightweight performance optimized for weak networks.

3) Scope of the First Version (Max 8 Tasks)

1. Advanced search and filters: Map, dates, number of guests, star rating, breakfast, amenities (pool/parking/WiFi), with sorting by final price.

2. Full hotel page: Photos and videos, amenities, location, cancellation/breakfast/hours policies, and hotel location map.

3. One-click booking: Select room ← View price breakdown (base/fees/tax/total) ← Instant confirmation with a booking number.

4. Flexible payment: Card, local wallets, and "Pay at Hotel" option, with a secure refund policy to minimize disputes.

5. Guest account: Favorites, booking history and invoices, saved address, and trip reminders.

6. WhatsApp channel: Confirmation message template, status updates, booking management link, and push/email notifications.

7. Smart travel assistant: Short Arabic chat that suggests a city, hotel, and daily plan based on budget, date, and preferences.

8. Free hotel dashboard (MVP): Manage rooms, prices, photos, accept/reject bookings, commission report, and an indexable hotel page (SEO).

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free to start: Users pay nothing. Hotels can register and list rooms with no subscription or listing fees.

• Core commission: 10–14% of the completed booking value only (no cancellation fees) — decreasing as hotel sales volume increases.

• "مدى+" subscription for travelers: 29 SAR/month (14-day free trial): price drop tracking, flexible cancellation, and quick consultation.

• Paid hotel subscription (optional later): 199 SAR/month for direct sales channel on the site + room management system integration + advanced reports.

• Additional revenue: Clearly labeled sponsored placements with "Ad" tag, corporate accounts for employee travel, and commission on insurance and rentals.

• Fixed principle: No hidden fees for users under any circumstances; every commission is clearly stated in the contract and invoice.

5) Global Expansion Plan Through Free Channels

• Arabic SEO content: Pages for each city/neighborhood/hotel in Arabic and English (Programmatic SEO) with structured Hotel/Offer data.

• Content creators: Collaborate with Arabic micro-influencers (2–10k) in exchange for a free stay + 6% commission code.

• WhatsApp: Weekly offers channel, travel groups, and "Gift a Night" program with discounts for both parties.

• Hotels: Direct visits in two target cities (Riyadh/Dubai) with a free listing package and a QR code at reception.

• Google: Google Business Profile for each hotel + price feed to Google Hotels later (high-intent free channel).
