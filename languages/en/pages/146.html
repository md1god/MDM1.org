Wajha — Complete Project Plan

From idea to a conversion-ready landing page in minutes, in Arabic and English.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, growth plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a landing page builder platform for SaaS

1) Target audience

• Pre-launch startup founders and independent SaaS makers.

• Front-end developers who want a fast start without building every section manually.

• Technical marketing teams and Indie Makers who are not proficient in code.

• Multilingual markets with a native need for right-to-left (RTL), a segment neglected in most current templates.

2) Points of distinction (why choose our product)

• A free-to-start model that delivers real value, with an optional upgrade and no export lock.

• Native support for two languages and both writing directions (RTL/LTR) with no manual adjustments.

• A visual drag-and-drop editor that builds sections and exports clean, ownable Next.js code.

• Built-in optimization for Core Web Vitals, SEO, Open Graph, and sitemap out of the box.

• A/B testing and conversion tracking integrated to measure the page's impact, not just its looks.

• A library of reusable composite sections that works as a unified design system.

• Full ownership export: the code is yours, with no permanent dependency on the platform.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. A visual drag-and-drop editor to assemble the page.

2. A library of 12 ready-made sections (Hero, features, pricing, testimonials, FAQ...).

3. An i18n system with full RTL support.

4. Setting up SEO, Open Graph, sitemap, and Robots.

5. A waitlist/email form with an email sending service integration.

6. Lightweight analytics and conversion tracking without heavy cookies.

7. Exporting a clean Next.js project with a standard folder structure.

8. One-click deployment to a hosting platform (Vercel/Netlify).

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: basic templates, code export, subdomain, no time limit.

• Pro (monthly subscription): all sections, custom domain, A/B testing, removal of the badge, multiple domains.

• Team: multiple seats, collaboration, and a shared design library.

• Additional revenue: a marketplace for paid templates from external makers with a commission, and a custom design service.

5) Global growth plan with free channels

• Publishing the core version as open source on GitHub to attract developers and build trust.

• Launching on Product Hunt, Hacker News, and specialized Reddit communities.

• Educational content on Dev.to and YouTube: "Build a landing page in 5 minutes."

• Leveraging long-tail SEO: comparisons, guides, best practices.

• Discord and Slack communities for SaaS, and a template designer program with a commission.

• Partnerships with hosting providers and the components marketplace.

• X/Twitter and Threads to build a following of independent makers.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: open-source dependency license obligations, data privacy and GDPR, clear terms for the template marketplace and designer rights.

• Security: managing secrets and mailing list data, and minimizing data collection.

• Technical: ensuring the exported code is clean, reliance on external hosting providers, and maintaining performance as section complexity grows.

• Competitive: the existence of strong open alternatives, which forces differentiation through RTL and visual generation rather than price alone.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Wajha (وَجْهة)

Marketing tagline: From idea to a conversion-ready landing page in minutes, in Arabic and English.
