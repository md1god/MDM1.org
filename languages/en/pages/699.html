Ulfah — Complete Project Plan

Ulfah is an Arabic-first human resources platform that enables small teams to manage employees, leave, and payroll in minutes at a fair price.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market, risks. The project is under construction, not yet launched.

1) Target audience

• SMEs with 5–200 employees, no dedicated HR, where the manager or accountant relies on Excel and WhatsApp.

• Best-fit sectors: clinics, medical centers, restaurant/café chains, digital agencies, e-commerce, contractors, training centers.

• First markets: Gulf, Egypt, Jordan, Morocco, then Arabic-speaking firms in Europe and Canada, where bilingual interfaces are in high demand.

• Secondary user: external accountant managing payroll for 3–10 small companies, needing a tab per company and fast accounting export.

• Year-one target: 300 active companies averaging 25 employees, 8% free-to-paid in 90 days.

2) Differentiators

• Faster setup: create company, add 10 employees, import CSV in under 15 minutes, no training or consulting.

• Arabic-first interface, full RTL, optional English, ready templates for contracts and leave policies matching local labor laws.

• Transparent pricing: no setup fees or mandatory annual contracts, permanent free plan up to 10 employees.

• Real mobile experience: employee submits leave and photographs documents from phone; manager approves via WhatsApp/email in seconds.

• One employee file for documents, leave, attendance, salary, promotions, with PDF/CSV export anytime, no lock-in.

• API/Webhooks integrations with accounting and payment tools, avoiding closed systems and monthly manual migration.

3) Scope of first version (max 8 tasks)

1. Employee files: data, contracts, ID, insurance, PDF attachments, access permissions.

2. Leave requests: customizable types, automatic balances, one- or two-level approval.

3. Attendance: manual or link/QR, monthly lateness/absence report, export.

4. Payroll: base salary, allowances, deductions, overtime, PDF payslips.

5. Onboarding checklists: tasks split between manager and employee, reminders, due dates.

6. Team directory and org chart: fast search, optional contacts, visual view.

7. Notifications and announcements: email, optional WhatsApp, read-receipt log.

8. Reports: headcount, monthly cost, leave balances, turnover, CSV export.

4) Pricing and profit model

• Permanent free plan up to 10 employees: files, leave, attendance, basic reports; builds trust, zero trial barrier.

• Growth: $2/employee/month, $19 minimum per company; payroll, onboarding, accounting export.

• Pro: $4/employee/month; advanced permissions, multiple branches, audit log, priority support.

• 20% annual discount; optional local payroll processing at $15/month per company.

• Setup and migration: $99 one-time up to 50 employees, $199 above.

• Target: 1,000 companies averaging 25 employees on Growth = $50,000/month, 65% target operating margin.

5) Global go-to-market, free channels

• Arabic/English SEO for “leave system,” “payroll,” “employee file,” with 30 educational articles in 6 months.

• Free templates: employment contract, leave policy, performance review, onboarding checklist; in exchange for email for the waitlist.

• Free communities: WhatsApp/Facebook manager groups, Reddit, Indie Hackers, accountant forums, solutions not ads.

• Accountant and HR consultancy partnerships: 20% recurring commission for one year per referral.

• Referral program: one free month each when the referred company upgrades, unique referral link.

• Launch on Product Hunt and YouTube with short lessons on managing team leave in 10 minutes, targeting 500 Q1 signups.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Data privacy: comply with GDPR, Saudi PDPL, local laws; document legal basis for processing employee data.

• Payroll data: encryption in transit and at rest, granular permissions, audit log, daily backups, 4-hour recovery.

• Attendance monitoring may count as surveillance: optional location check-in, employee notice, no tracking outside work hours.

• Tax/insurance compliance varies by country: keep accounts customizable; no automatic compliance promises before legal review per market.

• Technical risks: outages or payroll errors; mitigate with tests, staging, backup export before each payroll cycle.

• Reliance on a WhatsApp or payment provider: offer email/SMS alternatives; do not tie core functions to one provider.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Ulfah

Tagline: Ulfah is an Arabic-first human resources platform that enables small teams to manage employees, leave, and payroll in minutes at a fair price.
