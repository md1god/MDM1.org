Nafath — Complete Project Plan

Create your form in a minute, share it with a single link.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, growth plan, and risks. The project is under development and has not yet been launched.

1) Target Audience

• Small and medium-sized businesses: need to create forms for customer requests or bookings on a limited budget.

• Independent creators: such as individual sellers, bloggers, and designers who want to collect data without complexity.

• Non-profit organizations: need donation or registration forms without high costs.

• Teachers and trainers: want to quickly create surveys or course registration forms.

2) Points of Differentiation (Why choose our product)

• Completely free to start: most basic functions are available for free with no unnecessary restrictions.

• Focus on speed: build a complete form in less than a minute with the fewest clicks possible.

• Privacy-first: minimize unnecessary data collection, with clear options for users.

• Very lightweight: runs smoothly on low-powered devices and on mobile.

• Clean interface: focused on getting things done, with no complex menus or confusing surprises.

3) Scope of the First Version (Maximum 8 tasks)

· No. · Task · Purpose ·

· 1 · Drag-and-drop editor · Build forms quickly and visually ·

· 2 · Basic field types · Support text, email, select, and phone number ·

· 3 · Data validation · Prevent invalid inputs before submission ·

· 4 · Direct sharing page · Generate a single public link for each form ·

· 5 · Simple responses table · View and organize responses easily ·

· 6 · Responsive design · Works perfectly on mobile and desktop ·

· 7 · Free account system · Simple and secure sign-in ·

· 8 · Spam protection · Basic filter to limit automated submissions ·

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

· Plan · Price · Features ·

· Free · $0 · All tasks of the first version are fully available ·

· Pro · $9 / month · Custom domain connection, response export, and custom notifications ·

Revenue model: subscriptions only, while keeping the vast majority of features free.

5) Global Growth Plan with Free Channels

• Educational SEO: write short articles about creating free forms in multiple languages.

• Free tool directories: list the product naturally in free tool directories.

• Short-form content: publish tutorial videos under 30 seconds explaining usage.

• Relevant communities: participate helpfully in creator and entrepreneur communities without direct promotion.

• Word of mouth: focus on user experience to make it the sole reason for sharing.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

· Type · Risk · Mitigation ·

· Legal · Collection of personal data · Apply the principle of data minimization, and write a clear and simple privacy policy ·

· Legal · Differences in global privacy laws · Avoid collecting sensitive data by default, and add a warning when needed ·

· Technical · Spam and automated submissions · Use spam protection, rate limiting, and request throttling ·

· Technical · Unexpected surge in demand · Use a lightweight, scalable architecture from the start with effective caching ·

7) Suggested Name and Marketing Slogan

Suggested name: Nafath

Marketing slogan: Create your form in a minute, share it with a single link.
