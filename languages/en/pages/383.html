Riwaq — Complete Project Plan

Your digital library — collect, organize, and read offline.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: The idea, target audience, product, business model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under development and has not been launched yet.

Product Plan: Riwaq

A personal application for organizing and managing a digital manga and novel library. It relies on files imported from the device and legal free sources (public domain, Creative Commons, official free chapters via OPDS/RSS), converting them to EPUB/CBZ while syncing reading progress.

1) Target Audience

• Manga and Arabic web novel readers (especially ages 18–35) who collect local files.

• Readers who are not always connected (travel, limited data).

• Digital library collectors who want proper organization, metadata, and cover art for each title.

• Developers and designers interested in reading via OPDS and open-source materials.

2) Key Advantages (Why Choose Our Product)

• Legally designed: No copyrighted content is copied — only user files and open sources are used.

• Automatic metadata engine (cover, title, author, chapters) through local matching and Open Library/Gutenberg APIs.

• Built-in EPUB and CBZ/CR reader with Arabic support (RTL), chapter list, last page saved, and night mode.

• Batch conversion to organized EPUB/ZIP, while respecting robots.txt and request limits.

• Lightweight multi-device sync (web + desktop) with automatic fallback for offline use.

3) Scope for the First Version (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1) Desktop app (Tauri) + web PWA for management.

2) Import local files (CBZ/CBR/EPUB/PDF) with batch scanning.

3) Automatic metadata matching via Open Library and Gutenberg APIs.

4) CBZ/EPUB reader with RTL, reading progress, and night mode.

5) Batch exporter to organized EPUB/ZIP with a unified naming scheme.

6) Discovery of open sources (OPDS/RSS) with safe browsing and downloads.

7) Free account + reading progress sync (100 MB/month storage limit).

8) Welcome screen, privacy policy, and anonymous error reports.

4) Pricing and Business Model

• Free: Unlimited local library, full reader, open sources, sync for 1 device (100 MB/month).

• Pro: $4/month or $39 lifetime — unlimited sync, multiple devices, advanced auto-scan and metadata, and priority support.

• Additional revenue: Paid reading themes and cover templates, as well as optional referral commissions from publishers of free chapters.

5) Free Distribution Plan

• Reddit and Discord communities for reading and web novels (early access invitation).

• Short videos (YouTube Shorts/TikTok) explaining import and conversion in 30 seconds.

• Product Hunt and Hacker News (focusing on legal and privacy aspects).

• Bilingual blog (Arabic/English) about organizing libraries and reading offline.

• Telegram channels for digital libraries and open-source webmasters.

• Ambassador program: Free Pro for users who publish a helpful guide.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal: Accusations of facilitation if unauthorized sources are integrated — Solution: whitelist only open sources, explicit warning on external links, and a fast DMCA takedown policy.

• Platform terms: OPDS/RSS interfaces may change — Solution: adapter layers, source switching, and the user remains responsible for their files.

• Technical: Compressed CBR files, unsupported encodings, and server load — Solutions: rate limits, shared encoding, and local-first storage by default.

7) Proposed Name and Tagline

Proposed Name: Riwaq

Tagline: Your digital library — collect, organize, and read offline.
