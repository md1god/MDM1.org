Nebiq — Full Project Plan

From your website to your first customer, without a full sales team.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Startups: with a small or non-existent sales team, needing customers at the lowest cost.

• Entrepreneurs and freelancers: looking to find new customers without hiring a full-time salesperson.

• Small marketing agencies: needing to automate prospecting and follow-up for their clients on a tight budget.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Unprecedented simplicity: just enter the website and the system starts immediately, with no complex setup.

• Focus on the free tier: gives users real value for free, and only asks for payment when real need for scaling arises.

• Full user control: every automatic action requires explicit approval, ensuring complete control over the outreach process.

• Outcome-oriented intelligence: focuses on the smallest possible number of highly relevant prospects, rather than huge useless lists.

3) Scope of the First Version (8 tasks maximum)

• [ ] Website analysis: automatically extracting the company's identity and activity from its website.

• [ ] Prospect suggestion: showing a small list of the most relevant prospects for the company's activity.

• [ ] Message personalization: generating short, personalized messages based on each prospect.

• [ ] Smart filtering: automatically removing unlinked prospects before showing the list.

• [ ] Follow-up scheduling: creating simple follow-up steps that the user can edit.

• [ ] Pre-approval: showing each message before sending, with the option to edit or delete.

• [ ] Unified dashboard: tracking replies, opportunities, and status on a single screen.

• [ ] Clear free limit: enforcing a strict free-tier limit with a direct view of what remains.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

· Plan · Price · Features ·

· Free · $0 / month · Limited number of prospects and messages, with all first-version features. ·

· Growth · $29 / month · Higher limits, faster processing, with monthly or yearly extension available. ·

· Agency · $79 / month · Designed for multi-client use, with larger limits built for professional use. ·

Revenue model: direct subscriptions, focused on converting free users when they hit the natural limit rather than when core features are locked.

5) Global Go-to-Market Plan Using Free Channels

• Free tools directories: listing in free tools directories that are open to web search.

• Educational communities: contributing useful content about customer acquisition for small businesses, without direct links in every comment.

• Building in public: sharing daily practical lessons about the same problem, naturally attracting the target audience.

• Search engine optimization: creating short solution pages targeted at small businesses in various markets around the world.

• Relationship network: collaborating with unpaid educational content creators focused on entrepreneurship and self-funding.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

· Type · Risk · Mitigation ·

· Legal · Violating electronic marketing laws in some countries. · Enforcing an unsubscribe option, and warnings about the user's legal obligations according to their country. ·

· Legal · Collecting unnecessary data about prospects. · Minimizing data to only what is required, and refusing to collect sensitive data entirely. ·

· Technical · Messages being classified as spam. · Rate-limiting sends, requiring pre-approval, and keeping texts short and natural. ·

· Technical · AI accuracy in personalization. · Showing every message for human review before sending, with easy regeneration. ·

7) Proposed Name and Tagline

Proposed name: Nebiq

Tagline: From your website to your first customer, without a full sales team.
