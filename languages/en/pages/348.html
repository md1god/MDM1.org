Qubix — Full Project Plan

Simple analytics that protect your privacy and help you make more accurate decisions.

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, launch plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Small and medium website owners: seeking a simple alternative without complexity or heavy legal commitments.

• Bloggers and digital creators: want to know how their content performs without excess data collection.

• Independent developers: prefer lightweight tools that are easy to integrate into their projects.

• Privacy-focused teams: seeking analytics that comply with data protection requirements.

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• Privacy first: works without cookies by default and minimizes data collection to the essential minimum.

• Extremely lightweight: the tracking script is far smaller than traditional solutions, preserving site speed.

• New and simplified design: focuses only on the key numbers, without complex dashboards that distract the user.

• Legal readiness from the start: aligned with general data protection concepts, without imposing complex settings.

• Free model to start: completely free for many uses, with gradual growth only as needed.

• Completely original code: built from scratch with an independent identity, far from any porting or direct copying.

3) Scope of the First Version (8 tasks maximum)

1. Infrastructure setup: build a simple backend using a modern framework, with a small database.

2. Tracking script: write a lightweight JavaScript script from scratch to measure visits and basic events.

3. Dashboard: create a clear web interface showing the key statistics at a glance.

4. Project management: let users add one or more projects, each linked to a dedicated script.

5. Data minimization: filter bot requests and block sensitive data where possible.

6. Simple authentication system: login and secure session management for users.

7. Privacy settings: options to control whether IP addresses are stored or to reduce their precision.

8. Initial deployment ready for trial: deploy a working version to a simple environment for immediate use.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free to start: allows a reasonable number of monthly visits at no cost, reaching most of the target audience.

• Pay-as-you-go only: when the free visit limit is exceeded, it switches to a modest subscription proportional to scale, without inflated packages.

• Focus on simplicity: no core features locked behind expensive subscriptions; the difference is only capacity.

• Sustainable model: aims to earn small revenue from mature users while keeping entry free for everyone.

5) Global Distribution Plan Using Free Channels

• GitHub: publish a clear original repository with simple docs that ease contribution and experimentation.

• Dev.to: share short articles explaining the privacy problem and how the product solves it in simple ways.

• IndieHackers: share the build journey and updates to attract real users interested in alternative tools.

• Reddit: engage in privacy and web development communities, focusing on usefulness rather than advertising.

• Developer forums: join specialized web forums to present a practical solution to the same problem.

• Search engine optimization: create short pages targeting simple terms related to privacy analytics.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

· Type · Potential Risks · How to Mitigate ·

· Legal · Data protection requirements in different regions · Follow the "minimum data" principle, avoid collecting personally identifiable information, and review general requirements as laws evolve. ·

· Legal · Changes in browser policies or privacy guidelines · Follow general trends, and be able to modify the tracking script to match reality without a major rewrite. ·

· Technical · Bot traffic and fake visit inflation · Apply initial request filtering, with simple methods to identify non-human behavior. ·

· Technical · Data volume growth with increased usage · Rely on compact storage, automatic deletion of old data, and query optimization from the start. ·

· Technical · Simple security vulnerabilities · Follow basic security practices such as input validation, session management, and regular dependency updates. ·

7) Proposed Name and Tagline

Proposed name: Qubix

Tagline: Simple analytics that protect your privacy and help you make more accurate decisions.
