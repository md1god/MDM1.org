Darbi — Complete Project Plan

Track your outdoor workout on your device only, without an account and without ad tracking.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, profit model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not yet been launched.

1) Target Audience

• Runners and cyclists who exercise outdoors at least 3 times a week, and who need accurate measurement of distance, speed, elevation, and calories without paying an expensive monthly subscription.

• Android and iPhone users who avoid apps that require creating an email account or impose advertising, and who prefer to keep their data on the device itself instead of on cloud servers.

• Residents of cities and villages in the Arab region who practice brisk walking or running in neighborhoods and trails not documented on commercial maps, and who need full support for Arabic and a right-to-left interface.

• Privacy and open data enthusiasts, a segment estimated at about 15% of fitness app users, but who leave free apps when they discover that their locations are being sold or shared with third parties.

• Personal trainers and small groups of friends who train together at fixed times, and who need to share results manually via GPX files without a centralized social platform or public tracking.

2) Differentiators (Why We Choose Our Product)

• Privacy first in a real sense: no account, no email, and no servers to collect data, as all GPS data is stored locally in a database on the device, and can be permanently deleted with a single tap.

• Fully offline operation: download OpenStreetMap maps for an entire area once, then record routes in the desert, mountains, or rural areas without mobile network coverage.

• Low battery consumption: an algorithm to reduce the location update frequency when the user is stopped at traffic lights, aiming to reduce battery consumption by 25% compared to continuous recording every second.

• An authentic Arabic interface, not a literal translation: measurement names in kilometers and local time, clear weekly reports such as I walked 42 kilometers in 5 days, and support for Arabic numerals and miles when needed.

• Simple and fast design: open the app and start recording in just two taps in under 5 seconds, with large fonts readable while running, and clear buttons that do not require constantly looking at the screen.

3) First Version Scope (Maximum 8 Tasks)

• Build a cross-platform app using Flutter for Android and iOS, with original code written from scratch, and a single recording interface with only three activities: running, walking, and cycling.

• Accurate recording via GPS, the phone compass, and the altimeter when available, with live display of time, distance, current speed, average speed, and estimated calories burned.

• Save the activity log locally with a map for each activity and a weekly and monthly summary table, and the ability to add a short note such as road type, weather condition, or fatigue level.

• Export and import GPX and TCX files with a single tap, enabling the user to transfer their data to other platforms or keep a backup copy on their computer without uploading it to the cloud.

• Download offline maps for an area up to 500 megabytes maximum per area, showing the download percentage and storage space before downloading to avoid filling the phone's memory.

• Clear privacy settings in Arabic: choose location accuracy from 1 meter to 10 meters, hide the starting point from the map before sharing, and permanently clear an entire log or a single activity.

4) Pricing and Profit Model

• Free-to-start model: the basic version is free forever and includes unlimited recording, offline maps for one area, and exporting 10 activities per month, without ads and without needing a credit card.

• Pro package for advanced users at $3.99 per month or $29.99 per year, including unlimited maps, detailed elevation analysis, ready-made 8-week training plans, and unlimited export.

• One-time payment option at $39.99 for lifetime Pro, an option preferred by 20% of privacy-conscious users and gives us immediate income without a monthly commitment.

• Groups package at $9.99 per month for a single trainer for up to 10 trainees, allowing the trainer to send a training plan and track team commitment via imported files without centrally collecting trainee data.

• First-year profit goal: 100,000 free users, converting only 4% to paid, i.e., 4,000 annual subscribers at an average of $30, providing about $120,000 to cover development, hosting, and support.
