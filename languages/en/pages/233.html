Athar — Complete Project Plan

Your spatial log, yours alone.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a self-hosted alternative for spatial log tracking

1) Target audience

• Privacy-conscious individuals who want to record their daily places without handing their data to an advertising company.

• Advanced users and server administrators (homelab) who host their own services.

• Small families that want optional location sharing among their members.

• Small field teams (delivery couriers, field researchers) looking for a private route log.

2) Points of differentiation (why choose our product)

• Privacy first: all data stays on the user's device or server, with encryption at rest.

• Works without a real server: local operation on a single device versus an optional managed cloud version.

• Broad compatibility with location sources: receives data from tracking apps, importable phone networks (GPX/GeoJSON), and unified APIs.

• An understandable smart log: automatically converting raw points into "places" and "trips" instead of showing scattered routes.

• Full export without restrictions: the user takes their data in an open format at any time — no lock-in.

• A native Arabic interface with multilingual support, not an afterthought translation.

• Light on resources: runs on modest devices, unlike many heavy alternatives.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Import points from GPX/GeoJSON and a simple unified API for uploading a location.

2. Draw routes on an interactive map with a timeline.

3. Automatic classification into "places" and "trips" based on stopping and movement.

4. Basic statistics: distance, time, most frequent places.

5. Manually name places and save them (home, work, …).

6. Share a temporary link for a single trip with an access code.

7. Full data export and permanent account deletion from the interface.

8. Self-hosting with a single command via Docker Compose with storage encryption configured.

4) Pricing and profit model (free to start)

• Free (forever): full self-hosting with all core features, no limit on data.

• "Supported" cloud (paid monthly): managed tracking without server setup, backup, larger storage.

• Family plan: multiple seats and an expanded sharing link.

• Companies/teams: dedicated internal deployment, support, and SSO authentication.

• The model converges around paying for convenience and hosting, not for gating features.

5) Global distribution plan through free channels

• Release the code as an open-source project to attract contributors and trust.

• Appear in "self-hosted alternatives" and "awesome self-hosted" lists and specialized Reddit communities.

• Write practical guides and honest comparisons on a dedicated blog and Hacker News.

• Short videos on YouTube/PeerTube for a two-minute install.

• Mastodon and Telegram communities interested in privacy.

• Launch on Product Hunt with an offer to import user data from any source.

• An ambassador program for families and small teams with a symbolic commission.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: location sharing among members may be misused for surveillance; we address this with explicit consent, a permanent notice, and the ability to stop instantly.

• Legal: differing data protection laws between countries (GDPR and the like) during cloud expansion; we rely on data minimization and in-region hosting.

• Technical: the accuracy of identifying "places" varies with point density; we apply tunable algorithms.

• Technical: battery consumption in mobile apps; we optimize intervals and batching.

• Technical: the security of self-hosted servers exposed to the internet; we enforce HTTPS by default and setup warnings.

• Commercial: the difficulty of generating income from an audience that loves free; we focus on managed services and support.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Athar

Tagline: Your spatial log, yours alone.
