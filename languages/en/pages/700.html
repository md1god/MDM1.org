Nasaq — Complete Project Plan

Run your organization intelligently and without code, from the first request to the last report.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Nasaq Product Plan — A No-Code Enterprise Management Platform

1) Target Audience

• Hospitals and private clinics with 20–300 employees managing appointments, staff, and inventory with scattered tools.

• Schools and training centers with 15–200 employees needing admission forms, maintenance requests, and daily approvals.

• SMEs with 5–250 employees in trade, services, and contracting.

• Non-profit associations and local government offices with limited technology budgets.

• Operations and HR managers without an internal technology team, needing operations running within one business day.

• Emerging markets where mobile is the primary channel and internet is sometimes intermittent.

2) Differentiators

• Native Arabic right-to-left interface and a completely new design, with English and French support later.

• A visual form builder and native workflow engine supporting conditional branching, deadlines, and automatic escalation without code.

• A unified suite combining HR, attendance, inventory, CRM, projects, surveys, and documents in a single data model.

• An intelligent assistant that suggests form fields and approval paths from a text description, cutting setup time by up to 70%.

• A lightweight progressive web app (PWA) that works on weak connectivity, stores drafts locally, then syncs them.

• Transparent pricing starting free, with full CSV/JSON data export preventing vendor lock-in.

3) First-Version Scope (Max 8 Tasks)

1. Organizational structure and permissions: departments, roles, granular permissions, change log.

2. Dynamic form builder: drag and drop, 20 field types, instant validation, mobile preview.

3. Workflow engine: approval chains, conditions, deadlines, escalation, request suspension/resumption.

4. Core HR: employee profiles, leave, attendance via web and mobile.

5. Document management: upload, versions, comments, simple electronic signature requests.

6. Unified requests inbox: all requests and approvals in one list with filters and statuses.

7. Dashboards and reports: KPIs for attendance, approval time, request distribution, plus CSV export.

8. Notifications and integrations: email, in-app, Webhook, initial integration with Google Workspace.

4) Pricing and Revenue

• Permanent free plan: up to 10 users, 3 workflows, 1 GB storage — to attract small teams.

• Starter plan at $3 per user/month billed annually, $4 monthly: unlimited forms and 10 GB.

• Growth plan at $7 per user/month: advanced reports, SSO, 50 GB, support within 24 hours.

• Custom Enterprise plan: in-country hosting, 99.9% SLA, security audit, team training.

• Additional revenue: extra storage, SMS/WhatsApp credits, onboarding, and a template marketplace at 15%.

• First-year goals: 10,000 sign-ups, 5% paid conversion, annual ARPU of $45.

5) Global Go-to-Market with Free Channels

• Arabic and English SEO content: guides such as "How to Manage Leave for 50 Employees," comparison pages, sector solutions.

• Free template gallery for hospitals, schools, contracting, copyable with one click.

• Open community and public roadmap on GitHub Discussions with contributor badges.

• Partner program for accountants and IT consultants: 20% recurring commission in year one.

• Short educational videos on YouTube and LinkedIn, plus free weekly live demos.

• Referral program: one free month per party, free grants for universities and non-profit associations.

6) Legal and Technical Risks

• Data protection: GDPR and local-law compliance, data processing agreement, encryption in transit and at rest.

• Electronic signature: eligibility verification per country and an immutable audit trail.

• Patient data: exclude medical records from the first version and sign confidentiality agreements with clinics.

• Tenant isolation: separate logical database per organization, annual penetration tests, daily backups.

• Reliability: 99.9% continuity target, 15-minute RPO, one-hour RTO, real-time alert monitoring.

• Intellectual property and compliance: 100% original code, trademark check, open-source license review, full export preventing lock-in.

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Sentence

Proposed name: Nasaq

Marketing sentence: Run your organization intelligently and without code, from the first request to the last report.
