Kafaa — Complete Project Plan

Manage your team, attendance, and payroll from a single WordPress dashboard, simply and with no cost to start.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

Product plan: a resource and employee management system inside WordPress

1) Target audience

• Small and medium-sized companies (10–200 employees) that run their website or store on WordPress.

• HR and operations teams that rely on manual Excel spreadsheets.

• Agencies and site managers who provide their clients with ready-made internal management solutions.

• Emerging markets that prefer low-cost, easy-to-install solutions.

2) Points of distinction (why choose our product)

• A lightweight architecture written from scratch on modern WordPress interfaces (REST API + Blocks) without heavy bundles.

• Focus on HR and core operations instead of the complexities of a full ERP.

• Native Arabic/English interface with full RTL support.

• A free model to start, then upgrade based on the number of employees, without annoying restrictions.

• Data is stored on the client's site first (data sovereignty) with optional cloud sync.

• Instant reports (attendance, leave, estimated payroll) without needing a developer.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Employee registry with roles and departments.

2. Manual and QR clock-in/clock-out attendance tracking.

3. Leave management and approval workflow.

4. Generate exportable estimated payroll sheets (CSV/PDF).

5. A basic reports dashboard (attendance, leave, payroll cost).

6. Fine-grained permissions (manager, employee, HR).

7. Email notifications and in-WordPress alerts.

8. Data export/import and manual backup.

4) Pricing and profit model

• Free to start: up to 5 employees with all core features.

• Growth plan (monthly/yearly): 2–4 USD per employee per month via international payment gateways.

• Agency plan: annual license for an unlimited number of sites with a bulk discount.

• Paid add-ons: cloud sync, e-signature, payroll payments.

• Additional revenue from enterprise support and custom installation.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Publish the plugin in the official WordPress directory to capture free search.

• Educational videos on YouTube and short channels showing setup in minutes.

• SEO content: "How to manage your team from WordPress" and comparison guides.

• WordPress communities on Reddit, Facebook, LinkedIn, and developer groups.

• Collaborate with WordPress hosts and agencies through a free partner program.

• An open-source release on GitHub to attract contributors and build trust.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: employee data privacy, GDPR regulations, and payroll protection; we need explicit consents and a clear retention policy.

• Licensing: ensure a GPL license compatible with WordPress and avoid using protected code.

• Technical: conflicts with other plugins, payment security, and difficulty of WordPress upgrades.

• Operational: data fragmentation between local and cloud copies and multilingual support.

• Competition: clear differentiation through simplicity and price to avoid fading amid huge solutions.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Kafaa (كفاءة)

Tagline: Manage your team, attendance, and payroll from a single WordPress dashboard, simply and with no cost to start.
