Wishah — Complete Project Plan

Sync your bookmarks encrypted across all your devices, and store them in your own private place alone.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, growth plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: Syncing bookmarks (Bookmarks) with full privacy

The idea: An open-source tool that syncs bookmarks across all browsers and devices, stores them encrypted in the user's personal cloud, and cleans them up automatically with AI. We do not copy any existing product; we build an original solution from scratch.

1) Target audience

• Users concerned with privacy and unwilling to tie their data to browser accounts.

• Self-hosting enthusiasts and Nextcloud/WebDAV users.

• Professionals who work on 3 or more devices (work/home/phone).

• Small teams and agencies that share a common link library.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• End-to-end encryption by default: the provider sees no links.

• Storage freedom: WebDAV / Nextcloud / Google Drive / Dropbox / local folder, without lock-in.

• Automatic smart organization: classification, tags, and folder suggestions via AI that can be disabled.

• Unified instant search across all devices with page texts stored locally.

• A free model from the start and open source that can be audited.

• No vendor lock-in: full export always available.

3) First version scope (8 tasks maximum)

1. A browser extension for Chrome, Firefox, and Edge with basic two-way sync.

2. Connect personal storage (WebDAV and Nextcloud first, then Google Drive).

3. E2E encryption with a local key derived from a recovery phrase.

4. Conflict resolution with a clear strategy (newest / merge / saved copy).

5. Automatic organization with AI (suggested tags and folders that can be reviewed).

6. A web interface / mini app to view and search links.

7. Import/export in HTML, JSON, and CSV + backup.

8. A simple settings panel and a sync health check.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free forever (Free): unlimited sync via the user's storage, two devices, limited monthly AI organization.

• Pro (monthly/yearly subscription): unlimited devices, unlimited AI organization, conflict log, priority support.

• Teams: shared libraries, roles and permissions, and an admin panel.

• Open-source support (Sponsorships): GitHub sponsorships and an additional commercial license for enterprises.

5) Global growth plan with free channels

• Publish the open-source code on GitHub and list it in awesome-privacy and awesome-selfhosted lists.

• Official extension stores: Chrome Web Store, Mozilla Add-ons, and Edge Add-ons.

• Community platforms: Reddit (r/privacy, r/selfhosted, r/firefox), Hacker News, Product Hunt.

• Alternative sites: AlternativeTo and Slant.

• SEO content: tutorials on "how to sync bookmarks without selling your data", and neutral comparisons.

• YouTube and open technologies: setup walkthroughs with Nextcloud.

• Nextcloud and Home Assistant integrations and communities.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: compliance with GDPR when processing user data, browser store policies, storage providers' terms of use, and trademark rights for the name.

• Technical: browser API changes (Manifest), accuracy of conflict resolution with encryption, cloud provider rate limits, key and sync security, and AI running costs.

7) Suggested name and tagline

Suggested name: Wishah

Tagline: Sync your bookmarks encrypted across all your devices, and store them in your own private place alone.
