Nabra — Complete Project Plan

Practice job interviews with realistic questions and get clear feedback that boosts your confidence before you enter the room.

Plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market, risks. Under construction; not launched.

1) Target Audience

• New graduates (22–26): 5–15 interviews in 6 months; no practice network; need realistic questions and instant feedback, not generic advice.

• Professionals in first 5 years: switch jobs every 1.5–3 years; want evening practice under 20 minutes daily.

• Career changers (28–40): change fields (e.g., education to data analysis); must link past experience to a new role persuasively.

• Second-language applicants: immigrants/students interviewing in English or German; need wording/tone practice, not just content.

• Universities/career centers: B2B2C channel for cohorts (200–2,000 students); need aggregated reports.

2) Differentiators

• Assessment tied to CV and job: questions come from specific job-description paragraphs; answers are scored on fixed criteria (STAR + 4 criteria) from 0–100, quoting and correcting the weak sentence—not generic replies.

• "Explicit consent" live coach: listens only after "Start," with red bar, 60-second audio alerts, instant stop, exportable consent log.

• Structure cards, not scripts: 3 short points appear and vanish after 10 seconds, keeping user effort, reducing verbatim reading.

• Privacy first: no-recording default, 24-hour deletion, local processing for sensitive sessions, no training use.

• Languages: 3 at launch (Arabic, English, German) with fluency/tone scoring; Spanish/French later.

• Measurable progress: dashboard compares first/last sessions (e.g., 58 to 79 in two weeks), driving retention and subscriptions.

3) First-Version Scope (max 8 tasks)

1. Sign in, upload CV (PDF/DOCX), extract/edit skills and experience.

2. Paste job description; generate 15 weighted questions (5 behavioral, 5 technical, 5 scenario).

3. Audio/text practice, 90-second timer, automatic transcription.

4. Score each answer on 5 criteria (0–100), with quote, rewrite, example.

5. Personal answer bank, retries, version comparison (best answer, score gap).

6. Live coach, explicit consent: 3-point cards vanish after 10 seconds; permanent stop bar.

7. Weekly PDF report: averages, recurring weaknesses, 3 recommendations, progress chart.

8. Privacy settings: one-click deletion, no-recording, block illegal personal questions.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free forever: 3 sessions/month, 10 questions, basic report, no card, breaking the trial barrier.

• Pro: $9/month or $79/year (27% off), unlimited sessions, live coach, advanced reports.

• Seasonal pass: $4.99/7 days for upcoming interviews; highest expected conversion (5–7%).

• Enterprise: $3/user/month, minimum 200 users; admin dashboard, aggregated reports.

• Unit economics: ~$0.02 model cost/session, 80% target margin, 60-minute cap.

• Year-one goals: 50,000 free users, 5% paid conversion, $7 average revenue/paid user.

5) Global Free-Channel Go-to-Market

• Long-tail SEO: 200 pages of "interview questions + job title" with free sample answers; target 100,000 monthly visits in 9 months.

• Short video: 3 weekly TikTok/YouTube Shorts videos, 45–60 seconds ("rewrite this answer"), link in description.

• Niche communities: free guides on Reddit, Quora, jobs Discord; 9 valuable posts per product mention.

• Product Hunt: one-week free version; target 800 upvotes, 2,000 sign-ups.

• Universities: 20 university/career-center partners in 6 months; free one-year license for a case study/testimonial.

• Referrals: one month of Pro per successful referral; shareable LinkedIn report with branding.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Recording without consent: laws vary by state/country; require consent mode, audible notice, no auto-recording, documented consent.

• Data protection: comply with GDPR/CCPA via encryption in transit/at rest, 30-day deletion, no data sales, institutional processing agreements.

• Deceptive use: ban undisclosed use; vanishing cards, no full scripts reduce cheating risk.

• Model hallucination in assessment: lock criteria, test 500 reference answers, optional human review, confidence score.

• Cost and latency: stream responses, cache common questions, usage limits, plan for switching model providers.

• Product security: tenant data isolation, quarterly code reviews, annual penetration testing, 72-hour incident response.

7) Name and Tagline

Proposed name: Nabra

Marketing line: Practice job interviews with realistic questions and get clear feedback that boosts your confidence before you enter the room.
