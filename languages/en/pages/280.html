Miḥwar — Full Project Plan

Miḥwar: what actually matters to you in the world of computing.

A full execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, launch plan, and risks. The project is being built and has not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Software engineers, computer students, and tech explorers looking for the top news and tools in one place.

• Tech content creators (bloggers and video makers) seeking an audience that values substance over noise.

• Arabic- and English-speaking developers who want deep technical discussion in their own language.

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• Smart duplicate detection: an embedding model groups similar links and prevents discussion fragmentation.

• Transparent ranking: a published-factor voting algorithm (quality, freshness, voter experience) instead of a black box.

• Discussion over count: reputation points awarded for contribution quality, not posting volume.

• Arabic/English bilingual with a UI supporting both directions and search in both languages.

• A free daily digest delivered by email, tailored to your interests.

• Clearer categories with branches for systems, languages, AI, and security.

3) Scope of the First Release (8 tasks maximum)

1. Login with email and OAuth with verification.

2. Link submission with automatic duplicate detection and category suggestion.

3. Single story page + nested comments with voting.

4. Core front-page algorithm (votes + time + quality).

5. Category pages with "Newest" and "Most Discussed" tabs.

6. Personal dashboard: submission history, points, interest settings.

7. Moderation panel: hiding, duplicate merging, bans, and a decisions log.

8. Full Arabic/English UI + a first version of the daily newsletter.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free to start: all core features at no cost (posting, voting, discussion, categories).

• Pro plan at $5/month: advanced newsletter customization, real-time alerts, analytics dashboards, early feature access.

• Category sponsorships: sponsors back a specific category (AI, security, games) for a transparent monthly fee.

• University/team margin: optional institutional accounts for weekly reports.

5) Global Launch Plan Using Free Channels

• Product Hunt + Hacker News + free launch sites on launch day.

• Weekly valuable content (threads and educational threads) on X, Reddit, and LinkedIn.

• Discord and Telegram channels for the product community and feature requests.

• SEO blog targeting "best tools/news" queries in Arabic and English.

• Partnerships with newsletters and Arabic tech news sources for citations.

• GitHub for the brand page, icons, and open-source interface.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal: liability for links and comments → clear content policy, fast reporting, DMCA compliance.

• Legal: personal data and cookie protection → privacy policy and consent notice.

• Technical: vote stuffing and bots → verified accounts, rate limiting, abnormal behavior detection.

• Technical: hosting costs at scale → lightweight architecture and CDN caching from day one.

• Slow growth: a value loop starting with a focused early community by language/domain.

7) Proposed Name and Tagline

Proposed name: Miḥwar

Tagline: What actually matters to you in the world of computing.
