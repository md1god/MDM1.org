Lunsa — Complete Project Plan

Make your voice speak in every viewer's language within minutes.

Plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout, risks. Under construction; not launched.

Product plan: AI dubbing of YouTube videos

1) Target audience

• Independent creators: Arabic cooking channel wants Spanish/French viewers without reshooting; owner pays $200-$500/hour for dubbing, a small channel's monthly profit.

• Teachers/professors: 40-min English lecture, students in 5 countries; audio in student's language + bilingual subtitles to follow slide and term.

• Small businesses/shops: 90-sec product intro in 6 markets; reshooting needs 6 workdays, studio, actors.

• Viewers: German learner at 60% comprehension wants translated audio with original text to learn, not lose context.

• Low-budget creators in emerging markets: time and online presence, no agency budget; free tool that scales with channel.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Keep the video's spirit: split speech/music, remix translated audio at matching level; music stays, speech isn't flat.

• Word-level sync: per-word timestamps, auto-shorten for lip movement, drag any segment 200 ms left/right.

• User glossary: enter product/character names once; "Klimat" stays "Klimat" in every video.

• Partial editing: fix word 12, regenerate only that audio in 2 sec, not the 12-min video.

• Full ownership: MP4, separate audio, SRT, VTT; watermark off on upgrade; no publishing-platform lock-in.

• Verifiable privacy: no training on user videos, 24 h auto-delete, "Delete now" per project.

3) Scope of the first version (8 tasks maximum)

1. Import public video link or upload up to 200 MB/15 min, real progress bar.

2. Transcribe with word timestamps; edit original text before translation.

3. 12 languages at launch: Arabic, English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Turkish, Italian, Hindi, Indonesian, Korean, Japanese.

4. 6 licensed natural voices (3 male, 3 female); rate 0.9-1.2.

5. Separate music/speech and remix; original and translated level sliders.

6. One-click bilingual subtitles; YouTube-compatible SRT/VTT export.

7. Free 30-sec preview before payment to check quality and timing.

8. Dashboard: name search, 24 h auto-delete, 7-day download log.

4) Pricing and business model

• Free forever: 3 min/month, small watermark, 12 languages, full preview; without a credit card.

• Starter $9/mo: 60 min, no watermark, 6 voices, subtitle export; weekly channel.

• Creator $19: 300 min, glossary, priority queue, unlimited partial edits; daily channel.

• Team $49: 5 seats, 1,500 min, shared history, API to connect to a content management system.

• Economics: $0.02-$0.06/min processing; profit after ~120 paid min; 3%-5% conversion target, 70% stay as acquisition channel.

• Payments: credit card and PayPal; local methods in India, Egypt, Indonesia in Q2; annual prepaid 20% off.

5) Global rollout via free channels

• Free search content: 30 posts, e.g. "How to dub a video into Spanish in 5 minutes"; per-language page with steps and internal links.

• Short YouTube videos: 6 x 90-sec clips in first 6 languages, audio "before/after", education channels, mutual promotion not money.

• Communities: weekly help in Reddit, Telegram, Facebook creator groups; answer first, tool last, avoid self-promo bans.

• Small partners: 20 teachers on Patreon/Gumroad get referral link + free Team copy for one trial video.

• Free template: Google Sheets + Notion planner for one video in many languages; free, tool in footer only.

• Referrals: accepted invite gives both sides 10 min, max 10/month, small cost per user.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Content rights: only user files/links, consent before processing, report path removes content in 72 h.

• Voice rights: commercially licensed voices only; no famous-voice imitation; store used voice fingerprint to block abusive reuse.

• Sensitive terms: medicine/law show "Human review recommended"; publish limits, no full-accuracy claim.

• Partial lip sync: admit gap; 50-ms timeline editor; post-export satisfaction question.

• Load/cost: subscriber priority queue, cache repeated sentences, strict free caps, alert at 80% quota.

• Privacy: transit/at-rest encryption, 24 h deletion, GDPR compliance, 3-min privacy policy.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Lunsa

Tagline: Make your voice speak every viewer's language in minutes.
