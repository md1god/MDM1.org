مَدار — Complete Project Plan

Your community speaks clearly, and stays yours.

Plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout, risks. In development; not launched.

1) Target Audience

• Admins of small/medium communities (50–5,000 members): organized dialogue without ads or tracking: reading clubs, neighborhood groups, dev teams.

• Creators, writers, teachers: a permanent archive making discussions searchable, quotable references, not lost on fast social platforms.

• Startups and product teams: internal support forum, API-linked to their tools, no build from scratch.

• Limited connectivity (under 3 Mbps): lightweight preloaded interface, under 200 KB per page.

• Schools, universities, zero-budget nonprofits: free start with members' data kept private.

• Non-Latin languages, especially Arabic: RTL and Arabic fonts from day one.

2) Why Our Product

• Deliberate restraint: no addictive algorithms or fake trends; chronological, transparent order; "Calm" hides engagement counters for quality.

• Smart moderation with human approval: a local model only flags abusive messages for review; moderators get a transparent reason log; false positives under 2%.

• Privacy by default: no ads, tracking, or data sale; one-click export of all data as standard JSON.

• Fast on weak devices: first content under 1.5s on a mid-range phone; data-saving mode cuts size by 70%.

• True multilingual: Arabic, English, French, Spanish UIs, full RTL, optional one-tap translation.

• Portability and ownership: one-click Docker install, or move all data to any host, no lock-in.

3) First Version Scope (6 Tasks)

• Core engine: forums, sections, topics, replies nested to 5 levels; Markdown, quotes, attachments up to 10 MB.

• Accounts and roles: email or OAuth sign-up, member/moderator/admin permissions, optional 2FA.

• Moderation: review queue, word rules, bans/temporary restrictions, dashboard with 10 daily metrics.

• Search and indexing: full-text search with section/date filters, clean SEO pages, RSS per section.

• Responsive, accessible UI: RTL/LTR, screen readers, dark/data-saving modes, hot reload for developers.

• Install and migration: official Docker image, daily backup script, JSON import/export tool, open API.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free forever: 5 sections, 1,000 members, 5 GB storage, no credit card, no commitment.

• Community ($9/month): unlimited sections, 100 GB, custom domain, advanced moderation, no branding.

• Pro ($29/month): audit logs, 30-day auto backups, 12-hour priority support, multi-moderator management.

• Enterprise (from $99/month): dedicated hosting, unified SSO, 99.9% uptime, signed privacy agreement.

• Ethical extras: add-on store (themes, translations) at 15% commission, one-time migration/setup services $49–$199.

• Conversion target: 3% of free users paid in 6 months; 300 subscribers at $9 = $2,700/month.

5) Global Rollout via Free Channels

• Content: 4 weekly articles on "building a healthy community" and "running a forum without addiction", with examples and volunteer translation into 5 languages.

• Community presence: active in 10 open tech events; release on free code and app platforms, no paid ads.

• Ambassadors: 50 active members get a free Pro license for local support, translation, and workshops in their countries.

• Institutional partnerships: work with library/school associations seeking a free alternative; ready-to-install "education community" kit.

• Membership incentives: one-click install template, optional "Powered by مَدار" badge, auto invite links for members.

• Metrics: 1,000 installs in 6 months, 40% weekly activity, 200 responses via an open quarterly survey to guide development.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal: laws like GDPR require explicit consent and the right to be forgotten; privacy-by-default, deletion tools, no personal-data logs.

• Legal: content liability; clear terms, a 24-hour reporting channel, immediate removal of violations.

• Legal: IP for themes and translations; explicit MIT and CC licenses, verify each asset's source before store listing.

• Technical: injection and forgery flaws; ORM, input sanitization, security tests per pull request, 48-hour security updates.

• Technical: downtime or data loss; encrypted daily backups, a quarterly-tested recovery plan, 99.9% availability target.

• Technical: weak adoption; beta with 10 pilot communities 8 weeks before public launch, then adjust to feedback.

7) Proposed Name and Tagline

Proposed name: مَدار

Tagline: Your community speaks clearly, and stays yours.
