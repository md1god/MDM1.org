Recurra — Complete Project Plan

Discover your forgotten subscriptions, cancel with confidence, and negotiate to save every month.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a web tool to discover hidden subscriptions and negotiate bills

1) Target audience

• Middle-income individuals in major cities (25–45) who pay for multiple digital subscriptions.

• Households that manage more than one card/account and lose track of recurring expenses.

• Freelancers and those with a limited monthly budget keen to reduce financial leaks.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Privacy first: we don't require mandatory bank account linking; manual pasting or uploading a statement is enough, and processing happens in the browser as much as possible.

• Action-ready outputs: copy-ready cancellation texts and negotiation emails, not just a report.

• Cheaper alternatives suggested for each subscription with an estimate of actual annual savings.

• Native, lightweight Arabic/English interface that works on mobile without an app.

• Free start with no paywall, and upgrading is optional.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Enter subscriptions manually or paste a text list/simplified statement.

2. Automatic categorization of expenses (entertainment, software, health, sports…) and extraction of recurring ones.

3. A dashboard highlighting "forgotten subscriptions" and "close to renewal" and their annual cost.

4. A generation engine for a customized cancellation text for each service.

5. A generation engine for an email/script to negotiate a bill reduction (internet, mobile, annual subscriptions).

6. A database of cheaper alternatives with a savings calculator.

7. Reminders (email/notification) before the renewal date.

8. Export the report (PDF/CSV) and keep a personal savings log.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: up to 5 subscriptions, basic texts, limited export.

• Paid (Freemium): monthly/annual plan for unlimited subscriptions + advanced negotiation + smart reminders.

• Referral commission: earn from converting users to cheaper alternatives via declared affiliate links.

• B2B later: licensing to digital banks and budget management platforms (White-label API).

• Suggested price: free, then ~$4 per month / $30 per year (multi-regional pricing).

5) Global distribution plan with free channels

• SEO: "how do I cancel X subscription" and "cheaper alternatives to Y" pages for each popular service.

• Reddit/Forums: personal finance and budgeting communities (value posting, without being annoying).

• Product Hunt/Hacker News at launch with a free offer for early adopters.

• TikTok/Shorts: "how much are you paying without knowing?" challenges with shareable content.

• Deep marketing: free integration with savings bloggers and personal finance email newsletters.

• Referral loop: credit/free feature in exchange for inviting a friend.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: accuracy of legal texts (we don't provide legal/financial advice), data protection (GDPR/CCPA), and clear disclosure of affiliate links.

• Security: sensitivity of bank statements; encryption in transit and at rest and an option not to save.

• Technical: accuracy of parsing diverse data formats and reducing errors in detecting recurrence.

• Commercial: user resistance to paying after the free tier, and heavy reliance on volatile SEO.

7) Suggested name and tagline

Suggested name: Recurra

Tagline: Discover your forgotten subscriptions, cancel with confidence, and negotiate to save every month.
