Qiwam — Complete Project Plan

Arabic-first accounting that explains your numbers in plain language and lets you start for free.

Complete plan for a digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue, go-to-market, risks. Under construction, not launched.

Independent accounting plan

Problem: Current systems are costly, complex, and often unfit for Arab markets in language, taxes, and e-invoicing; data is locked in and exports are hard.

Idea: Original Arabic-first product: free self-hostable core, smart plain-language reports, new design, simpler UX.

1) Target audience

• SMEs (1–50 staff) in trade, services, e-commerce, clinics, agencies.

• Freelancers/small owners needing simple invoices, expenses, reports.

• Small accounting firms with multiple clients, needing portal and permissions.

• Budget-limited associations, cooperatives, startups.

• Emerging markets/Arab diaspora: multiple currencies, native RTL, low cost.

2) Differentiators

• Arabic-first, not translated: native RTL, precise terms, tax-ready reports.

• Free start, no lock-in: open-source self-hostable core, free cloud plan, full open-format export.

• Verifiable reports: natural-language questions, each figure linked to invoice/entry.

• Compliance add-ons: VAT, e-invoicing, country tax calendars; simple core.

• Accountant collaboration: invitations, comments, granular permissions, audit log.

• Light/fast: weak-connection ready, quick setup, hides complexity from non-specialists.

• Transparent pricing: no surprise volume fees, never sells customer data.

3) First version (max. 8)

• 1. Company setup/chart of accounts — company, fiscal year, currencies, tax, editable chart.

• 2. Invoices/expenses/taxes — sales/purchase invoices, tax, payment status, Arabic PDF.

• 3. Double-entry/auto-posting — balanced entries; unsafe deletion blocked.

• 4. Bank reconciliation — CSV import, matching, reviewable categorization.

• 5. Core statements — trial balance, P&L, balance sheet, cashflow.

• 6. Smart assistant — "Why did expenses rise?" concisely answered, each figure referenced.

• 7. Permissions/audit/export — roles, audit log, CSV/JSON/PDF, accountant portal.

• 8. Deployment/backup — Docker, scheduled backups, documented restore, free cloud.

Out of scope: payroll, advanced inventory, point of sale, complex multi-branch.

4) Pricing and revenue

• Community — Free — self-hosted, unlimited companies, full export, support.

• Cloud Free — $0 — 1 company, 2 users, 50 invoices/mo, basic reports.

• Growth — $12/mo — unlimited invoices, 10 users, reconciliation, 100 smart queries/mo.

• Firm — $49/mo — 20 clients, accountant portal, consolidated reports, advanced permissions.

• Enterprise — Custom — private hosting, data residency, SSO, service agreement, priority support.

Revenue: cloud subscriptions, compliance modules, payroll, extra AI, migration, training, paid support; purchasing-power pricing; free is a permanent entry channel.

5) Global go-to-market: free channels

• Open core on GitHub: docs, demo data, screenshots.

• Arabic/English SEO: guides, invoice templates, tax calendars, comparisons.

• YouTube shorts/lessons: invoice to statement in 10 min.

• Communities: Telegram, WhatsApp, Reddit, Indie Hackers, Hacker News.

• Accountant ambassadors: free Firm plan for feedback/referrals, no cash commission.

• Community templates/compliance modules; volunteer translators.

• Partnerships: coworking spaces, incubators, universities, trade groups.

• Referral: free month both sides; free country landing pages.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Data protection/privacy: encryption, granular permissions, processing agreement, storage choice.

• Tax/e-invoicing changes: updatable modules; disclaimer: not an auditor substitute.

• Open-core licensing: clear license (e.g. AGPL), separate commercial modules; trademark review.

• Never hold customer funds or offer payments; avoids extra financial/regulatory duties.

• AI: no training on customer data without consent; liability limits.

Technical:

• Double-entry errors: automated balance tests, no silent edits, audit log.

• AI hallucination: read-only, references required, human approval before action.

• Bank diversity: CSV import first, not fragile links; add integrations gradually.

• Security: transit/at-rest encryption, restorable backups, penetration tests.

• Multiple currencies/tax rounding: clear policies, edge-case tests.

• Migration/growth: early export/import, performance monitoring before cloud expansion.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Name: Qiwam

Tagline: Arabic-first accounting that explains your numbers plainly and lets you start for free.
