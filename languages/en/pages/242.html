Raff — Complete Project Plan

Organize your kitchen and home smartly, offline, with complete freedom.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: managing pantry and home furniture inventory (original alternative)

1) Target audience

• Organization-minded households: families who want to track what they have in the kitchen and home and reduce waste and duplicate purchases.

• Power users (Self-hosters): those who want to host their data on their own devices without mandatory subscriptions.

• People on a limited budget: students and employees who plan meals and purchases smartly.

• Small shops and small cafés: they need simple inventory management without a heavy and expensive ERP system.

2) Points of distinction (why choose our product)

• Works offline first (Offline-first PWA): the user enters their data in the market or kitchen, then it syncs later.

• Instant barcode scanning via the phone camera with product data fetched from free open sources.

• Arabic voice input: add an item by a spoken phrase instead of manual typing.

• Very simple setup: one run command via Docker or one-click cloud hosting, no technical experience needed.

• Suggestive intelligence: it automatically suggests a shopping list based on consumption and expiry dates.

• Family integration: sharing inventory among family members with clear roles and permissions.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Inventory management: add/edit/delete an item with quantity, unit, and location.

2. Barcode scanning via the camera and product recognition.

3. Expiry and shortage date alerts with browser notifications.

4. A shared shopping list synced in real time.

5. Weekly meal plans with automatic shopping list generation.

6. Offline-first work with sync upon return.

7. Family accounts with roles (owner/editor/viewer).

8. Self-deployment with one command via Docker and backup/CSV export.

Excluded from the first version: advanced invoicing, thermal printing, peripheral device interfaces, complex ERP reports.

4) Pricing and profit model

• Always free to start (Open-Core): the full self-hosted version is free and open source.

• A cloud "Pro" plan: managed hosting at a symbolic monthly price (for example $3/month individual, $6/month family) including cross-device sync, backup, and family collaboration.

• Support and services: installation and customization for small shops (subscription or one-time fee).

• Sponsorship and donations: via GitHub Sponsors and open-source funding platforms.

• No gatekeeping: core features are never placed behind a paywall in order to build trust.

5) Global rollout plan with free channels

• GitHub + self-hosted lists: appearing in awesome-selfhosted and Awesome Privacy lists.

• Discussion communities: Reddit (r/selfhosted, r/homelab, r/mealprep) and Hacker News forums.

• Product Hunt and AlternativeTo: an initial launch and an alternative page for similar products.

• Educational content: SEO articles and short clips about "reducing food waste" and "inventory organization".

• Translating the interface into Arabic and English to attract both the Arab and global markets.

• An open contributor program: badges and recognition for supporters to motivate project sustainability.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal – barcode data: full compliance with the licenses of open data sources (such as ODbL) with source attribution and no resale of aggregated data.

• Legal – privacy: applying a clear data retention policy and compliance with GDPR (right to deletion and export) and not selling user data.

• Legal – trademark: choosing a fully original name and identity with a similarity check before launch.

• Technical – sync conflicts: adopting a clear conflict resolution strategy (Last-Write-Wins with a change log) to prevent data loss.

• Technical – offline work: ensuring robust local storage (IndexedDB) and a reliable sync queue.

• Technical – scalability and operations: providing Docker and a lightweight database (SQLite with an optional PostgreSQL) to reduce maintenance overhead.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Raff (رَفّ)

Marketing tagline: Organize your kitchen and home smartly, offline, and with complete freedom.
