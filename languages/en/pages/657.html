Nabra — Complete Project Plan

A full marketing studio for your clinic, powered by AI every day.

New digital project implementation plan: idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market, risks. Under construction, not yet launched.

1) Target audience

• Primary: small/medium dental clinic owners (1–5 chairs, 1–2 branches), plus clinic managers/in-house marketing coordinators.

• First markets: Gulf, Egypt, Jordan, then English-speaking markets; full Arabic/English support.

• Need: lower-cost agency alternative; one system for content, ads, lead capture, reviews, booking.

• Core pain: inconsistent content, lost patient messages, unmanaged reviews, no-shows; agency invoices lack measurable results.

2) Differentiators

• Real vertical depth: dentistry templates/content (implants, orthodontics, whitening, cosmetics) respecting medical ad rules.

• Outcomes, not likes: dashboard for leads, appointments, return, not just engagement.

• Native bilingual: native-quality Arabic/English content and messages, not machine translation.

• One unified channel: content + ads + Lead Capture + WhatsApp + reviews + booking in one workspace.

• Transparent pricing, no agency contract: starts free, grows with clinic.

• Compliance: patient consent, audit log, sensitive-data masking before generation.

• Integrates with existing systems: API/Webhooks sync with calendars and PMS, not replace them.

3) MVP scope (max 8 tasks)

1. Clinic onboarding wizard: profile, services, brand tone, visual identity, target areas.

2. Content engine: monthly calendar + posts, email, SMS in Arabic/English.

3. Ads generator: copy/static creative for Meta and Google, with medical claim checks before publishing.

4. Lead capture: landing page, forms, automated WhatsApp/SMS replies, unified inbox.

5. Review management: automated requests, sentiment analysis, reviewable reply drafts.

6. Booking and reminders: appointments, confirmation, no-show-reducing reminders.

7. Results dashboard: leads, appointments, response time, best converting channel.

8. Operational basics: invoicing, user permissions, simple audit log.

4) Pricing and profit model

• Free: one site, one user, 30 AI credits/month, platform branding on pages.

• Starter — $99/month: content + reviews + booking + 200 credits, two users.

• Growth — $249/month: all above + ads + WhatsApp + unified inbox + 800 credits.

• Scale — $499/month: multiple branches, advanced permissions, ROI reports, priority support.

• Add-ons: extra AI credits, managed setup (one-time), agency white-label.

• Profit: subscription + credit consumption + onboarding + pass-through channel fees.

• Growth incentives: annual discount = two months free; referral credits both sides.

5) Global go-to-market with free channels

• Programmatic SEO: landing pages per specialty/city (e.g., dental clinic marketing in Riyadh) + practical guides.

• Free tool: “Clinic Marketing Score” assesses digital presence, creates shareable report.

• Video: YouTube Shorts and short lessons on attracting patients and managing reviews.

• Niche communities: dental groups on Facebook, LinkedIn, professional forums; useful, non-spammy input.

• Partnerships: dental labs, equipment suppliers, professional associations, clinic consultants.

• Product Hunt + Reddit + Directories: initial launch, tool-directory presence.

• Referral and Affiliate program: rewards for consultants and clinic owners.

• Free monthly webinar with a medical marketing expert; attendees converted to free trials.

• Localization: Arabic/English first, then Spanish/Portuguese for expansion.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: medical ad rules and results claims vary by country; solution: per-market validation, human review before publishing.

• Privacy: patient data subject to HIPAA/GDPR/PDPL; solution: encryption, data minimization, local storage, clear consent policies.

• Consents: “before/after” images and marketing messages need explicit consent; solution: mandatory consent log, instant opt-out.

• Technical: Meta/Google/WhatsApp API changes can break integrations; solution: abstraction layer, fallback providers, monitoring.

• AI hallucination: medical-content risk; solution: constrained templates, fact-checking, no treatment promises.

• Delivery/integration: email issues, PMS differences; solution: managed domains, Webhooks, tech partnerships.

• Model cost/churn: inference price volatility, high churn; solution: credits, caching, customer success plan.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Nabra (نبرة)

Tagline: A full marketing studio for your clinic, powered by AI every day.
