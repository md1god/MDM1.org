Nabra — Complete Project Plan

An intelligent media player that turns any movie or video into an interactive language lesson with dual subtitles and full privacy.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Self-directed learners who want to learn languages through films, series, podcasts, and videos.

• Students, immigrants, and professionals who need to understand original content quickly.

• Teachers and content creators looking for a bilingual presentation and explanation tool.

• Arabic speakers learning English, and learners of Arabic who are not native speakers.

• Privacy-conscious users who prefer local processing instead of uploading their files to the cloud.

2) Differentiators (Why choose our product)

• Local-first: playback, transcript translation, and subtitle generation run on-device by default, without uploading media.

• A learning experience, not just watching: dual subtitles, sentence repetition, instant dictionary, and vocabulary saved in exportable cards.

• AI flexibility: support for local models and multiple translation providers, with the option to use the user's own key.

• Original design: RTL-first interface, unified timeline, and a synchronized, clickable text panel for navigation.

• Free to start: core functions are always available; payment is only for cloud capabilities and advanced add-ons.

• Broad language support: initial focus on Arabic and English, with an architecture that can add other languages quickly.

3) Scope of the First Release (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Cross-platform desktop player (Windows/macOS/Linux) supporting local files and direct links.

2. Dual subtitle engine with customization of position, size, and colors, plus playback and repetition shortcuts.

3. Synchronized text panel with click-to-navigate, sentence repetition, speed control, and A-B loop.

4. Automatic subtitle generation via local ASR models compatible with Whisper, with language detection and timing editing.

5. Pluggable instant translation via external providers or a local model, with caching of results.

6. Instant dictionary and vocabulary saving into lists, with export to Anki and CSV.

7. Strict privacy mode: local processing by default, key masking, and explicit consent before any cloud transmission.

8. Onboarding, Arabic/English interface, automatic updates, and optional anonymous metrics.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free plan: full player, dual subtitles, dictionary, vocabulary cards, and local ASR models.

• Pro: $4.99 per month or $39 per year, including limited cloud hours, synchronization, and advanced translation.

• Optional AI credit: pay-as-you-go for cloud processing, with support for "Bring Your Own Key".

• Education license: discounted price for institutions and teachers, with no ads or data selling.

5) Global Go-to-Market Plan with Free Channels

• Search engine optimization with pages such as "Learn English from movies" and "Dual subtitles for video" in ten languages.

• Short videos on YouTube, TikTok, and Reels showing improved comprehension in 30 seconds.

• Launch posts on Product Hunt, Hacker News, Reddit, Discord, and Anki and language-learning communities.

• Free collaboration with teachers, educational influencers, and universities to try the tool in classrooms.

• Referral program that grants cloud credit for inviting new users.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Content rights: prohibit hosting or distributing protected material, rely only on user files, with clear terms of use and a takedown procedure.

• Subtitles and generated texts: make clear that machine translation can be wrong, and do not present it as an official or certified substitute.

• Privacy and compliance: minimize data collection, encrypt keys, and comply with GDPR and CCPA when cloud features are enabled.

• Accuracy and synchronization: address subtitle drift, model hallucination, and variations in audio quality and accents.

• Performance and compatibility: test different encodings, low-end devices, and memory and battery consumption.

• Cost and dependency: avoid reliance on a single provider, set usage limits, and provide local alternatives.

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Tagline

Proposed name: Nabra (نبرة)

Marketing tagline: An intelligent media player that turns any movie or video into an interactive language lesson with dual subtitles and full privacy.
