Adswarm — Full Project Plan

From a single product link to a test-ready ad swarm in minutes.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the launch plan, and the risks. The project is being built and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a one-shot UGC ad generator for testing creatives in direct-to-consumer stores

1) Target Audience

• DTC (direct-to-consumer) stores on Shopify and WooCommerce running Meta and TikTok ads.

• Creative teams (Creative Buyers / Media Buyers) that need a high volume of tests weekly.

• Small agencies and independent marketers running multiple brand accounts on a limited budget.

• Solo store owners with no filming or editing team.

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• From a single link to a full batch: copy, voice, avatar, editing, and platform cuts in one step.

• A Hooks library built on proven testing patterns with an expected score for each hook.

• Multiple versions for structured testing: generate 20–40 similar copies where only one variable changes (scientific, not random).

• Direct export in Meta and TikTok formats and sizes, with a tracking log for each version.

• Works in Arabic, English, and other languages, with local voices.

3) Scope of the First Version (8 Tasks Max)

1. Enter a product link or image set and automatically extract the name, images, and description.

2. Generate 10 hooks + 10 short scripts in UGC format.

3. Choose an avatar (image + voice) from a ready library, supporting Arabic and English.

4. Automatically assemble one video: product shot + avatar + on-screen text + music.

5. Cut and export in 9:16, 1:1, and 16:9 for each version.

6. A dashboard showing the whole batch with export links and testing labels.

7. User account with a Credits system and free limits.

8. Credit-based pricing with a payment gateway (Stripe) and a monthly plan.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free to start: 5 ads per month with a light watermark and one size.

• Basic plan: $29/month — 50 ads, all sizes, no watermark.

• Pro plan: $79/month — 200 ads, advanced voices, batch export, version log.

• Pay-as-you-go credits: $0.5 per extra ad (for those who don't want a subscription).

• Additional revenue: programmatic hook templates (Add-ons) and API access for agencies.

5) Global Launch Plan with Free Channels

1. Content on LinkedIn/X: post hooks and immediately actionable testing ideas, 3 times a week.

2. TikTok/Reels: before/after showcase (product link → 20 ads) as short video.

3. Community groups: Shopify/Meta Ads and Reddit (r/shopify, r/PPC) sharing helpful guides, not ads.

4. Referral program: free credits for every friend who creates an account.

5. Multilingual landing page and SEO around "UGC ad generator" and "creative testing."

6. Early partnerships with small content creators who get credits in exchange for case studies.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal: avatar and actor rights (written releases), image and trademark rights for the client's product, transparency of "AI-generated ad" per platform policies, DMCA risks if copyrighted text is generated.

• Technical: changes to Meta/TikTok export APIs, cost of video/voice model calls and its high level, inconsistent Arabic pronunciation quality, generation speed during peak times, idea theft by competitors.

7) Proposed Name and Tagline

Proposed name: Adswarm

Tagline: From a single product link to a test-ready ad swarm in minutes.
