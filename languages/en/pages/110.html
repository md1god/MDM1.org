Qirtas — Complete Project Plan

Your notes and tasks in open files you own alone, with no mandatory subscription and no locks.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, launch plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not yet launched.

An original product plan to solve the problem of note-taking and managing tasks locally

1) Target audience

• Individuals who care about privacy and refuse to upload their notes to a mandatory cloud.

• Writers, researchers, and students who work in Markdown and need full ownership of their files.

• todo.txt users and fans of simple text files that can be copied and controlled.

• Developers and self-hosting enthusiasts who want optional encrypted sync.

• Users fed up with note-app subscriptions and looking for a free alternative to start.

2) Points of distinction (why choose our product)

• Local-first: everything is stored as plain text files owned by the user, with no data lock-in.

• Multi-platform: Android app + web version (PWA) + desktop app, with one editor and a unified experience.

• Optional end-to-end encrypted sync, with a free self-hosted server for those who want it.

• Powerful built-in tasks: due dates, recurrence, reminders, and tags — working on top of todo.txt with a modern interface.

• Superior Markdown editor: live preview, tables, math equations via KaTeX, and full RTL/LTR support.

• Instant local search via a lightweight index that works offline and fast on low-end devices.

• Free to start with no ads and no mandatory account, with full transparency in the code.

3) Scope of the first version (8 tasks maximum)

1. Markdown editor with live preview, syntax highlighting, and quick commands.

2. Local storage as text files in a folder chosen by the user, with open/save via SAF.

3. Task management from todo.txt with a due date, tag, and recurrence interface.

4. Instant search across all files via a local index.

5. Biometric lock and optional encryption for sensitive files.

6. Export/import and local backup in ZIP format.

7. Interface customization: dark mode, fonts, text size, text direction.

8. Sharing files with other apps and home screen shortcuts.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free-to-start tier: all core functions open, no ads and no account.

• Optional Pro plan (annual subscription or one-time purchase): encrypted sync, PDF export, advanced themes, file version history.

• Free self-host server: for those who want full sync without paying, which builds trust and adoption.

• Donations and sponsorship: via open support platforms, and B2B channels for licensing and enterprise support.

• Principle: no blocking of data, and no payment for access to the user's files.

5) Global growth plan using free channels

• Publish the code on GitHub and F-Droid to build trust among privacy-conscious users.

• Host the web version as a PWA for free on Cloudflare Pages.

• Launches on Show HN, Product Hunt, and Reddit (r/androidapps, r/selfhosted, r/plaintext, r/todotxt).

• Free educational content on YouTube and technical articles about local-first and todo.txt.

• Community translation via Crowdin to open new markets at the lowest cost.

• Build an open community for contributions and add-ons to accelerate growth.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Risk of license contamination or copying code from similar projects — solution: write all code from scratch and document sources.

• Trademark conflict with a similar name — solution: check names and trademarks before launch.

• GDPR compliance when offering sync — solution: end-to-end encryption and minimizing data collection.

• Licenses of third-party libraries such as KaTeX — solution: review licenses and provide the required notices.

Technical:

• SAF and Android version differences may cause file access errors — solution: extensive testing and alternative paths.

• Sync conflicts and data loss — solution: clear conflict resolution and version history.

• Performance on low-end devices and large files — solution: partial loading and incremental indexing.

• Background reminders and battery consumption — solution: WorkManager and schedule optimization.

• Managing encryption keys securely — solution: rely on the system keystore and never store exposed secrets.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Qirtas

Marketing tagline: Your notes and tasks in open files you own alone, with no mandatory subscription and no locks.
