Sila — Complete Project Plan

From the first message to lasting loyalty, Sila brings your customer relationships together in one place and grows with your team.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market, risks. Under construction, not yet launched.

1) Audience

• SMEs (5–200 employees) running sales teams of 2–20, needing clear follow-up without an enterprise budget.

• Agencies and service studios (marketing, design, development) handling 50–500 clients yearly linking projects to deals.

• SaaS startups gathering hundreds of leads monthly, needing simple tracking without $50/user.

• Freelancers and consultants in the Arab region starting with one contact, needing a free tool that grows with them.

• Emerging markets using WhatsApp and Telegram as their primary sales channel, needing native Arabic RTL, not later translation.

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Us)

• Truly free: self-host with unlimited seats, and cloud up to 3 users and 1,000 contacts, unlike competitors' 14-day trials.

• No per-seat pricing: flat price per workspace, cutting costs 60–80% for a team of 10.

• Arabic-first: full RTL, Hijri/Gregorian dates, Arabic-Indic numerals, ready WhatsApp and email templates, and Arabic reports.

• Native integration with WhatsApp Business and Telegram, where 70% of target-market sales conversations happen.

• Data sovereignty: one-click full CSV/JSON export, and on-premise deployment for regulatory needs.

• Optional Arabic AI assistant to summarize conversations, suggest replies, and classify leads; fully disableable.

3) First Version Scope (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Contact and company management with custom fields, plus CSV/Excel import/export.

2. Customizable Kanban pipeline (stages, close probability, deal value) with drag and drop.

3. Two-way email sync via IMAP/SMTP, templates, and open/click tracking.

4. Unified activity timeline (call, email, note, meeting), tasks, and reminders.

5. Web lead capture forms, and WhatsApp/Telegram integration to turn conversations into deals.

6. Dashboard and reports: conversion rate, sales cycle length, pipeline value, and rep performance.

7. Granular roles, permissions, and an Audit Log for changes.

8. One-command Docker deployment, automatic backups, and data migration from CSV or other tools.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free for life: open-source self-host + cloud up to 3 users, 1,000 contacts, and 500 messages/month.

• Growth: $19/month per workspace (up to 10 users and 10,000 contacts), instead of $30–40 per user at competitors.

• Business: $49/month (unlimited users, automation, SSO, advanced permissions).

• Usage revenue: AI credits and WhatsApp messages at cost + 20% margin, covering expansion without raising the base price.

• Managed hosting and support: $99–299/month for teams not wanting to manage servers; highest margin.

• Conversion funnel: 5–8% of free users to paid within 6 months, with a growth lever via transparency and referrals.

5) Go-to-Market Plan with Free Channels

• Publish the repository publicly with a clear license and bilingual README, aiming for GitHub Trending within 90 days.

• Launch simultaneously on Product Hunt and Hacker News with a free cloud version and "start in 5 minutes" offer.

• Bilingual SEO content: "best free [hidden source]," "open-source alternative," with 3 weekly articles and short YouTube tutorials.

• Present in entrepreneurship communities: Reddit, LinkedIn, local WhatsApp/Telegram groups, and a product Discord.

• List integrations in WhatsApp Business, Google Workspace, and Zapier marketplaces to capture automatic demand.

• Ambassador and local implementation partners: free training for case studies and referrals.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal – Data protection: comply with GDPR and local privacy laws, provide a data processing agreement (DPA), and permanent deletion on request.

• Legal – Intellectual property: write code from scratch, review library licenses, and register a new trademark and domain.

• Legal – Platforms: use only official WhatsApp/Meta APIs, and prohibit unauthorized bulk sending.

• Technical – Email deliverability: set up SPF, DKIM, and DMARC and monitor domain reputation to avoid spam.

• Technical – Security: encryption, two-factor authentication, tenant data isolation, and pre-launch penetration testing.

• Technical – Dependency and cost: multiple AI and email providers, and clear usage limits to avoid unexpected bills.

7) Name and Tagline

Proposed name: Sila

Tagline: From the first message to lasting loyalty, Sila brings your customer relationships together in one place and grows with your team.
