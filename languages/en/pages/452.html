NURS — Complete Project Plan

First compliance platform for fire protection contractors: every inspection documented in real-time, every deficiency turns into a ready-to-send quote.

Complete execution plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market, and risks. Project is under construction and not yet launched.

Product Plan: Compliance and Operations Platform for Fire Protection Contractors

1) Target Audience

Primary: Fire protection system maintenance contractors and subcontractors (sprinklers, alarms, pumps, valves, specialized suppression systems) with 3 to 50 technicians — the segment ignored by enterprise FSM solutions whose cost is prohibitive:

• Owner-operator: wants compliance without an admin team.

• Service Manager: responsible for scheduling, response, margins.

• Field Tech: works in basements and shafts without network — needs an app that works offline.

• Office Coordinator: issues quotes, tracks invoices, collections, and change orders.

• End Client: Property/Facility Manager — needs documented proof of compliance when visited by AHJ or insurance company.

Secondary/Pull: AHJ inspection agencies, insurance agents, material distributors who bring customers.

Geographic Expansion: Phase one United States (Texas/California/Florida/Gulf) because code and jurisdiction density is clear and AHJ is digital, then Gulf (Saudi Arabia/UAE) via WhatsApp, then UK/EU via BS/EN packs.

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

1. Code-Aware Asset Library: Every device type carries test frequency (weekly/monthly/quarterly/annual), test steps, and acceptance criteria per jurisdiction (NFPA 25/72/10 + local packs). Horizontal tools store free text; we auto-generate the visit and exceptions.

2. Proof-of-Compliance Packet: One click produces a dossier per system/building: log for every device, photos, test readings, timestamp, tech signature and credentials, with QR for verification, shareable with AHJ/insurance. This is the "asset" that turns us from a scheduling tool into a proof system people pay for.

3. Deficiency → Quote Engine: From inside the field inspection: classify result (critical/major/minor) → auto-match with price list → generate quote with margin, materials, duration, and convert to work order. Specialists turn inspections into revenue; we make this cycle one minute instead of two days.

4. Persistent Device Identity (QR/NFC tags): Track device across building and previous owner; record stays attached to equipment not the customer, so value stays with assets.

5. True Offline-First: Inspection in a basement with no signal — scan QR, forms, photos, signature, then conflict-aware sync when network returns. Most tools are cloud-only and fail at the worst point on site.

6. Compliance Calendar + Auto-Renewal: Auto-generate visits, reminders for cert/insurance expirations, price escalation per CPI, and multi-year maintenance contracts.

7. Arabic RTL UI + Multi-currency/Language + WhatsApp: Serve mixed workforce (local and expat workers) and Gulf/LatAm markets where WhatsApp is the primary ops channel.

8. Interoperable with Client Systems: CSV/JSON export, API, webhooks, and integration with accounting and ERP — without replacing their system.

3) V1 Scope (Max 8 Tasks)

1. Quick Onboarding: Create company, branches, teams, price lists and labor rates, report templates, and Code Packs per state/city, with RTL and multi-language support.

2. Asset Registry: Buildings, systems, and devices with serial/QR, location within building, install date and warranty, linked to photos and floor plans.

3. Compliance Scheduler: Auto-generate visits per device frequency + maintenance contracts, prevent duplication, distribute to crews by proximity and skill.

4. Offline Field App: Ready-to-use inspection forms per device type, record readings (pressure/current/response time/volume), linked photos, signature, QR scan, and secure later sync.

5. Deficiency & Quote Engine: Classify result and severity, auto-match part/service from price list, generate quote with margin, duration, materials, and convert to work order or schedule maintenance.

6. Compliance Packet & Documentation: Generate signed PDF per system, visit, or year, full searchable archive, and secure share link for client with verification QR.

7. Billing & Collections: Quote → Work Order → Invoice → Collections, collection reminders, field expenses, and margin calc per order.

8. Admin Dashboard & Alerts: Revenue and margin per contract/site/device, on-time %, compliance pass rates, and alerts for cert/insurance/regulatory expirations.
