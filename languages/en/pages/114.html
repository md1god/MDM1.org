Murasil — Complete Project Plan

Your own email with your own name and domain, in one click on your own server.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a one-click self-hosted email service

1) Target audience

• Individuals concerned with privacy who do not want to hand their data to major email companies.

• Small and medium businesses and freelancers who want email under their own domain at low cost.

• Developers and technical teams who want full control over the server.

• Organizations in regions that require local data sovereignty or regulatory compliance.

2) Points of differentiation (why choose our product)

• One-command installation on any cheap VPS, with container support (Docker) instead of assumptions.

• A smart deliverability assistant that reduces the problem of landing on blocklists, which is the biggest technical obstacle facing self-hosted solutions.

• A modern, simple control panel managed from the browser and phone, not dependent on the terminal.

• Fully automatic DNS setup (SPF / DKIM / DMARC / MTA-STS).

• Email + calendar + contacts in one package, with easy migration from your current provider.

• A model that starts free (self-hosted) with an optional upgrade to managed administration.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. One-command installation on Ubuntu/Debian or via Docker Compose.

2. Automatic DNS setup (SPF, DKIM, DMARC, MTA-STS, and TLSA).

3. SMTP/IMAP server + responsive Webmail interface.

4. A control panel to manage domains, users, and mailboxes.

5. Built-in Spam/Phishing filtering that updates automatically.

6. Automatic encrypted backup with one-click restore.

7. Monitoring of delivery health, blocklists, and instant notifications.

8. A data importer to migrate mail and calendar from the previous provider.

4) Pricing and profit model

• Free to start: self-installation and the core code are completely free.

• Managed plan: low monthly ready-made hosting per domain/user (no technical knowledge needed).

• Relay service: a simple subscription to ensure delivery via an IP with a high reputation.

• Support and consulting: paid packages for companies (installation, migration, priority support).

• Add-ons: extended backup, archiving, and extra storage.

5) Global distribution plan using free channels

• Publish the code on GitHub with an open-source badge and a quick-start guide.

• Launch on Product Hunt, Hacker News, and Reddit (r/selfhosted, r/homelab, r/privacy).

• Free guides and articles: "How to get your own email in 10 minutes."

• Short video content on YouTube/TikTok explaining installation step by step.

• Partnerships with privacy, Open Source, and VPS hosting communities.

• An ambassador program for tech bloggers in exchange for free usage credit.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: liability for misuse and spam, compliance with GDPR and data retention laws, and liability for user content → a strict Anti-Abuse policy and clear Terms of Service.

• Technical: port 25 being blocked by cloud providers, poor IP reputation, landing on blocklists, the complexity of DNS setup, and security vulnerabilities in email services → solutions: delivery relay, automatic monitoring, continuous security updates, and encryption by default.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Murasil (مراسل)

Marketing tagline: Your own email with your own name and domain, in one click on your own server.
