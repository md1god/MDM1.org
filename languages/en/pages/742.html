Nabra — Complete Project Plan

Rewrite your text in your own tone, not the machine's tone.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, launch plan, and risks. The project is under construction and not yet launched.

1) Target audience

• Non-native English professionals (35% of target market): writers/translators in MENA and South Asia selling on Upwork and Fiverr, needing natural English that preserves meaning.

• Content teams at SMEs: 10–50 AI-assisted articles monthly; suffer uniform tone and weak coherence; need group review and change log.

• Agencies/localization teams: 5–20 clients; separate voice profile per brand; Word export with version comparison.

• Individual creators: newsletter or 8–20 LinkedIn posts monthly; AI drafts, then phrasing with their personality.

• Researchers/academic writers: self-improve drafts for clarity and consistency; explicit ban on assessment cheating.

• Product teams: short UI copy (200–800 words) where tone errors are costly, e.g. error messages and feature intros.

2) Differentiators

• Personal voice fingerprint: analyze 3–5 samples (~500 words) for sentence length, vocabulary variety, punctuation; require ≥85% similarity before export.

• Meaning first: auto-lock names, numbers, quotes, terms; NLI check rejects edits changing a fact, so "grew 12%" never becomes "grew 21%".

• Arabic-first, bilingual: MSA, some dialects, RTL, diacritics, Arabic–English paraphrasing; most competitors are English-first.

• Transparency over absolute promises: report explaining each edit and guidance-only "naturalness" indicator; no claim of 100% detector bypass.

• Free start, private: 1,500 words daily without a card; texts deleted after 30 days; not used for training by default.

• Speed and cost: 300 words in under 3 seconds; estimated $0.02 per 1,000 words via small models and linguistic rules.

3) MVP scope (max 8 tasks)

1. Web editor: paste/upload .txt and .docx; word count; automatic language detection.

2. Voice profile: 3 text samples and tone questionnaire (friendly/neutral/professional) saved to profile.

3. Paraphrase engine: light/medium/strong intensities with instant preview and side-by-side comparison.

4. Entity locking: highlight names, numbers, quotes; prevent changes during generation.

5. Meaning check: semantic similarity ≥0.90; alert on deletion or drift.

6. Authenticity report: colored diff, reason per edit, guidance-only naturalness indicator.

7. Export/versions: Word, PDF, Markdown; two-version comparison; change log.

8. Account/limits: email signup; 1,500-word daily limit; simple usage dashboard; payment next phase.

4) Pricing and profit model

• Free forever: 1,500 words daily, 3 voice profiles, Markdown export, no credit card.

• Pro $9/month ($84/year): 50,000 words monthly, unlimited voice profiles, Word/PDF export, 10,000 API calls.

• Team $29/seat/month: shared tone guide, audit log, SSO, minimum 5 seats.

• Usage credits: $5 per 100,000 words, optional auto-renewal.

• Enterprise API: $0.50 per 1,000 words, volume discounts up to 40%.

• Target economics: 85% gross margin, 4% free-to-paid, CAC under $12, LTV ~$90, 25% recurring referral commission.

5) Global launch plan with free channels

• Long-tail SEO: 150–200 pages in 6 months targeting "Arabic AI text paraphrasing" and "make AI text sound human"; goal 60,000 monthly organic visits by month 12.

• Free no-signup tools: "Sentence Rhythm Meter" and "Text Tone Checker" to attract visits and links; share results as an image.

• Free distribution: Chrome and Google Docs extensions, tool-directory listings, open-source style analyzer to build trust.

• Niche communities: 5 weekly helpful contributions on Reddit, LinkedIn, X, and translator groups, without spam.

• Coordinated launch: Product Hunt, Hacker News, "Show HN", then referral campaign giving 500 free words per friend.

• Gradual localization: Arabic first in MENA, then English, Spanish, Indonesian via local translators and university writing-center partnerships under an integrity charter.
