I'tidal — Complete Project Plan

Your home's climate calibrates itself and saves your energy, without touching the valve.

A complete plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market, and risks. The project is under construction, not yet launched.

1) Target Audience

• Homeowners with smart thermostatic radiator valves (TRV) facing inaccurate readings near the radiator.

• Home Assistant users wanting local control without cloud or mandatory subscription.

• Tenants and small landlords wanting lower heating bills without changing the heating system.

• Families with uneven room temperatures, frequent window opening, or hours away from home.

• Heating technicians and small installations needing scalable calibration and diagnostics.

• European and North American markets first, with full Arabic support and RTL UI.

2) Differentiators

• Local-first: runs entirely on the home network, with no mandatory cloud account and no data selling.

• Multi-sensor fusion: combines room sensor, external sensor, window/door, presence, and weather forecast into one decision.

• Adaptive calibration: learns each valve's offset and corrects it automatically instead of the radiator reading.

• Decision explanation: why it heated or stopped, with an auditable log instead of a black box.

• Broad compatibility: Zigbee, Matter/Thread, and Wi-Fi via Home Assistant, with automatic valve behavior detection.

• Heating safety: lower/upper limits, frost protection, and fallback when a sensor is lost.

• Free to start: local core always free, payment only for optional services.

• Native Arabic interface: not a later translation, with energy-saving reports and clear terminology.

3) First release scope (max 8 tasks)

1. Local control core (Add-on/Container) and setup with Home Assistant.

2. Room and zone model, multi-sensor fusion (median/weighted average), and outlier rejection.

3. Adaptive valve calibration and offset learning, with limits and safe failover.

4. Window/door and presence detection, pausing heating temporarily then resuming.

5. Weather forecast, preheating, and use of sun and thermal load.

6. Weekly scheduling, manual override, and temporary boost.

7. Visual dashboard with decision explanations, diagnostics, and exportable logs.

8. Setup wizard, Arabic/English docs, and compatibility tests on popular valves.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free-to-start: local core, unlimited rooms, basic dashboard, security updates. No credit card.

• Pro: about €3.99/month or €34.99/year: optional cloud backup, remote access without VPN, advanced energy analytics, priority support.

• Lifetime: about €89 for personal use.

• Installer/Partner: about €199/year per installation: multi-site management and white-label reports.

• Optional hardware: sensors and installation kits at cost + small margin, no obligation.

• Principles: no data selling, no per-valve fees, open API for the local version.

5) Global go-to-market with free channels

• Publish the open-source core on GitHub and list it in HACS.

• Active presence in the Home Assistant forum, Reddit, and Discord/Matrix with technical answers, not ads.

• Short YouTube tutorials: valve calibration, saving 20% on the bill, connecting a window sensor.

• Multilingual SEO around “TRV calibration” and “energy-saving smart heating.”

• Transparent comparisons and home case studies with real numbers.

• Show HN and Product Hunt launch; participate in Home Assistant meetups and FOSDEM.

• Community translation via Weblate, and referrals for free Pro months.

• Free partnerships with heating technicians and local energy groups via a shared savings calculator.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Comply with GDPR and data minimization; ensure privacy policy transparency.

• Verify trademark and freedom to use before launch.

• Use compatible open-source licenses; avoid code or assets from other products.

• Clear disclaimer for heating failure or frozen pipes.

• If selling hardware: CE/UKCA, RED, RoHS, WEEE compliance and consumer warranty.

Technical:

• Valve protocol/behavior differences: compatibility layer and community testing.

• Sudden Home Assistant changes: pinned versions and automated tests.

• Sensor loss or delay: fallback data and safety limits.

• Frost and valve failure: independent protection that works offline.

• Cybersecurity: signed updates and limited permissions.

• Cloud/support costs: optional, limited cloud features.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: I'tidal (اعتدال)

Tagline: Your home's climate calibrates itself and saves your energy, without touching the valve.
