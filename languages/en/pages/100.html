Sanad — Complete Project Plan

Manage your clients, invoices, inventory, and finances in one place, and start for free in minutes.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Freelancers and self-employed people who manage their own invoices and expenses.

• Small and micro businesses (1–20 employees) with no technical department or full-time accountant.

• Small institutions and non-profit organizations that need simple financial transparency.

• Geographic focus: the Middle East and North Africa first (RTL and Arabic support), then emerging markets globally.

• Sectors: services, e-commerce, contracting, consulting, clinics.

2) Differentiators (Why choose our product)

• Fully cloud-based, runs in the browser and on mobile as a PWA with no server installation or maintenance.

• Arabic-first (RTL) with multi-currency and taxes (VAT) and e-invoice outputs.

• A smart assistant that fills in data, categorizes expenses, and drafts invoice line items.

• Setup in minutes with a generous free plan and clear limits instead of enterprise complexity.

• Modular: enable only the modules you need to lighten the interface and resources.

• Open API and ready-made integrations, and exportable ownership of your data.

• Works on weak internet or offline, then syncs automatically.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Account and multi-tenant workspace, with team invitations, roles, and permissions.

2. Contacts (clients and suppliers) with simplified CRM and interaction tracking.

3. Quotations and invoices, payment recording, and collection status.

4. Simple expenses and income with a journal and basic accounting.

5. Products and inventory (basic balance and low-stock alerts).

6. Dashboard and reports (sales, cash flow, top clients).

7. PDF export + e-invoice + multi-currency and tax.

8. Smart assistant + PWA, offline work, and sync.

4) Pricing and profit model

• Free to start (Free): one user, monthly invoice limit, basic inventory, no card.

• Pro: per-user monthly subscription, unlimited invoices and reports, and automation.

• Business: multiple companies, API, advanced permissions, and priority support.

• Additional revenue sources: payment processing fees, an integrations and add-ons marketplace, managed hosting, and a discount on the annual subscription.

• Optional open-core model: an open core and paid cloud features.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• SEO with Arabic/English content about invoicing, taxes, and freelancing.

• Free tools as marketing bait (invoice generator, tax calculator), with a network effect.

• Product-led growth (PLG): instant trial without a card and in-product team invitations.

• Communities: Reddit, Indie Hackers, freelancer groups, and local chambers of commerce.

• Educational videos on YouTube and short clips on TikTok/Instagram.

• Free channels: GitHub (open-core), app stores, and distribution via PWA.

• Partnerships with accountants, co-working spaces, and a referral program.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Data protection and compliance (GDPR and local laws) and data residency requirements.

• Differences in e-invoicing and tax systems between countries (such as ZATCA) and their continuous updates.

• Liability for accounting accuracy: a clear disclaimer and defined service limits.

• Trademark verification of the name before launch to avoid conflicts.

Technical:

• Multi-tenancy and scaling with secure separation of client data.

• Data import from spreadsheets and competitors to avoid the switching barrier.

• Security, encryption, backup, and disaster recovery.

• Sync conflicts in offline mode.

• AI accuracy in financial data and the need for human review.

• Complexity of the multi-currency and tax engine.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Sanad

Tagline: Manage your clients, invoices, inventory, and finances in one place, and start for free in minutes.
