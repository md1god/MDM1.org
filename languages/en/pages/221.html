Tabaqat — Complete Project Plan

Open interactive maps in Arabic, with high performance and no restrictions.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

Product plan: an original interactive maps engine

1) Target audience

• Web developers who want interactive maps without being tied to a closed commercial provider.

• Small and medium businesses that need maps for their website or app on a limited budget.

• Governments and non-profit organizations in the Arab region that need maps supporting Arabic and RTL direction.

• Data and IoT teams displaying live locations and custom layers.

• University students and instructors in geographic information systems and graphics courses.

2) Points of distinction (why choose our product)

• Open from the root: the engine and styles in standard open formats, with full self-hosting capability.

• No data lock-in: works directly with OpenStreetMap and any open vector tiles source.

• Higher performance: rendering on WebGL2/WebGPU and a small bundle with tree-shaking.

• Native Arabic support: Arabic labels, letter shaping, and RTL direction out of the box — a feature competitors lack.

• Built-in offline support via Service Worker.

• Easy integration: official wrappers for React, Vue, and Svelte and a flexible plugin system.

• A genuinely free start for the individual developer without a credit card.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. A WebGL2 rendering engine that draws vector tiles layers in MVT format.

2. A style reader with an open JSON specification (layers, colors, labels).

3. Basic interactions: pan, zoom, rotate, tilt, and clicking on features.

4. An Arabic labels engine supporting RTL and shaping.

5. Official wrappers for React, Vue, and Svelte.

6. Offline support via Service Worker and caching.

7. A CLI tool to convert GeoJSON data into tiles.

8. Full documentation, ready-made examples, and an open community gallery.

4) Pricing and profit model

• Free tier (Free): the engine is fully open source + free cloud hosting up to a monthly limit (for example, 50 thousand tile loads). No credit card.

• Small Pro tier: a fixed monthly fee with a higher limit, analytics tools, and a custom domain.

• Pay-as-you-go: when the free limit is exceeded, the user pays per additional thousand loads.

• Self-hosting: always free; profit comes from support and training contracts for companies.

• Enterprise: service level agreements SLA, priority support, and private deployment.

5) Global distribution plan with free channels

• GitHub and npm: making the engine open source available to attract contributors and users.

• Developer communities: technical posts on Dev.to, Hacker News, Reddit, and Product Hunt at launch.

• Educational content: a tutorial series on YouTube and a blog explaining how to build maps step by step.

• Open maps community: participating in OpenStreetMap initiatives and mapping competitions.

• Universities: free workshops and courses, building a generation of users.

• Developer conferences: talks and demo sessions to attract users and contributors.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal — Data: compliance with data source licenses (attribution to OSM), and not reusing any protected code or assets.

• Legal — Brand: using a completely new name and visual identity and avoiding any similarity with existing products.

• Legal — Privacy: compliance with GDPR and specifying storage locations and processing of location data.

• Technical — Performance: GPU memory management and rendering on weak devices, with progressive degradation.

• Technical — Compatibility: differing WebGL2/WebGPU support across browsers, with a fallback path.

• Technical — Dependence on external sources: tile provider outages, addressed through self-hosting and caching.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Tabaqat

Marketing tagline: Open interactive maps in Arabic, with high performance and no restrictions.
