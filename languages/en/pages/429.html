Atlas — Complete Project Plan

Save world geography with a click on the map.

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, launch plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: interactive geography flashcards

1) Target audience

• School and university students (especially geography and social studies curricula).

• Competitors in geography quizzes and general knowledge contests.

• Geography teachers who need ready-made training tools for their students.

• Self-learners and fans of mental shortcuts (spaced repetition) on their phones.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Interactive map learning: the answer is tied to a real location on a clickable map, not just a text card.

• Level-based learning paths (beginner → pro), Arabic-first coverage with multilingual support.

• Smart card generator: builds new cards from reliable sources and automatically fixes repetition.

• Visual progress tracking: heatmaps for weak spots and short weekly reports.

• Works offline (offline-first) with optional cloud sync.

• Free to start instantly with no external software: works in the browser and on the phone.

3) First version scope (max 8 tasks)

1. Basic web interface (React + TypeScript) with a question/answer card mode and "Knew/Didn't know" buttons.

2. Spaced repetition engine (simplified SM-2) to schedule daily review.

3. Interactive map (Leaflet) showing destinations and linked to the cards.

4. First content pack: ~500 cards in 3 categories (capitals, countries, landmarks).

5. Simple user account (email + password) with progress saved to cloud storage.

6. Progress dashboard: daily/weekly stats and mastery percentage per category.

7. Full Arabic support (RTL interface) with the ability to switch language.

8. Deploy to free hosting (Vercel/Netlify) with a subdomain and a share link.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free to start: all core features (cards, map, spaced repetition) stay open permanently.

• Pro – $4.99/month or $39/year: advanced paths, detailed analytics, import/export, unlimited AI-generated cards, and cross-device sync.

• Schools/groups plan – $99/year: for classrooms and teachers with an admin dashboard and group reports.

• Future additional revenue: card packs from partners and custom content.

5) Global launch plan using free channels

1. SEO and content: a blog and articles like "The best way to memorize the world's capitals" targeting long-tail keywords.

2. Reddit and forums: sharing free tools in geography and learning communities (r/geography, r/Anki-replacement in a general style).

3. YouTube/TikTok: short videos "Do you know where this country is?" showing the product naturally.

4. Twitter/X and LinkedIn: daily educational threads (geography fact + map image).

5. Arabic-language learning communities: Telegram and WhatsApp groups for students and geography contests.

6. Referral program: a user invites a friend = one free month of Pro.

7. Launch on Product Hunt and Horror.io and education tool sites: with a clear landing page and an instant experience with no signup.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: verify the licensing of geographic sources and maps (some base maps have commercial use restrictions); rely on open sources (Natural Earth, for example) and document the license.

• Legal: protect learner data (children's privacy if minors are involved) and comply with GDPR/COPPA: either collect no data or obtain explicit consent.

• Legal: trademarks — check that the name and logo do not conflict before registration.

• Technical: accuracy of geographic content (changing borders and names) — need for periodic human review and a content update system.

• Technical: user retention — spaced repetition needs free reminders (email/notification) or usage drops.

• Technical: hosting cost as you grow — start on a free budget, then plan a migration.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Atlas

Tagline: Save world geography with a click on the map.
