Celluna — Full Project Plan

Your scientific diagrams complete in minutes, with consistent icons that suit your research.

Full execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the rollout plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target audience

• University and graduate students and professors in biology, chemistry, and medicine.

• Researchers and scientists who need precise diagrams for posters, research papers, and conference talks.

• Science communication specialists, poster designers, and presentation designers.

• Science content creators (YouTube, Instagram, newsletters) and educational material editors.

• Website and platform developers who need an icon library embedded in code.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

1. Visual style unity: all icons on a single grid with consistent stroke weight and corner angles, so drawings look professional even when mixing different categories.

2. In-browser editor: change color, stroke, and labels, and group icons into a complete scene without external software.

3. Multi-format export: SVG, PNG, PDF, LaTeX/Overleaf, PowerPoint, React/Vue/Tailwind/Iconify from one button.

4. Unified, clear licensing: commercial use permitted on one understandable page, with no ambiguity.

5. Smart bilingual search: Arabic/English search and accurate categorization (cells, molecules, devices, lab, plants...).

6. Community contribution: submitting new icons with unified review and quality control, plus a contributors thank-you board.

3) First version scope (8 tasks maximum)

1. Build the design system (grid, stroke, angles) and produce 250 icons across 6 main categories.

2. Fast static website with search, filtering, and instant preview.

3. Free download in SVG and PNG with a clear license page.

4. Publish the package via npm and Iconify for integration into React/Vue/Tailwind.

5. Basic browser editor: change color, stroke, and labels, then download.

6. Export to LaTeX, PowerPoint, and PDF.

7. Receive community icons through a programmatic form with manual review.

8. Documentation + Arabic/English support + free ready-made presentation and poster templates.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free-to-start: the full core icon library in SVG with commercial licensing — an attractive entry point.

• Pro — 6$ per month or 48$ per year: advanced editor, combined scenes, PDF/PowerPoint/Overleaf export, Figma files, dark/transparent colors.

• Teams — 29$ per month: shared workspace, unified visual identity, enterprise billing.

• Additional revenue sources: a paid template store (posters, social carousels), open donations and sponsorship, custom scientific design services on demand.

5) Global rollout plan using free channels

1. SEO: a dedicated page for every icon and common icon (bilingual search) + articles such as "how to draw a cell diagram".

2. GitHub: a repository with an excellent README and listing in awesome lists to attract developers.

3. Community launch: Show HN and Product Hunt and r/biology and r/labrats and r/chemistry.

4. Social content: short videos (Reels/TikTok) showing a scientific diagram being built in 30 seconds, plus threads on X and LinkedIn.

5. Partnerships: science content creators and university professors in exchange for a free mention; institutes and laboratories.

6. Academic groups: educational communities on Facebook, Discord, and Telegram in Arabic and English.

7. Free weekly newsletter: new icons + a scientific design tip.

8. Free catalogs: list the library on template and free-asset websites.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: similarity of the icons to benchmark works could raise claims; originality must be proven for every drawing and the license documented for every line or element, and GDPR respected for user data.

• Technical: quality drift when accepting contributions, difficulty unifying the style across contributors, and site performance with thousands of files.

• Commercial: how easily the open library can be copied, and the rise of AI image generation tools as an alternative — we face them with the editor, the export, and the community, not with content alone.

• Operational: keeping up regular updates and spending unpaid time on review.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Celluna

Tagline: Your scientific diagrams complete in minutes, with consistent icons that suit your research.
