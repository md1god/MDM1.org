Dawra — Complete Project Plan

Track all your subscriptions, stop the leaks, and save smartly from one place.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, launch plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

A new product plan for tracking recurring subscriptions

1) Target audience

• Budget-conscious individuals: those who have 5–20 recurring digital subscriptions (entertainment, software, cloud, fitness).

• Shared households: those who pay shared subscriptions and need a unified view of who pays and when.

• Freelancers and small business owners: to separate business expenses from personal ones.

• Privacy enthusiasts and self-hosters: those who refuse to link their bank accounts to closed cloud services.

• Students and those with limited income: looking for free ways to reduce waste.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Automatic detection of subscriptions from account statements (CSV) and email invoices, instead of tedious manual entry.

• Cancellation assistant: explains the cancellation steps for each service with direct links, not just a reminder.

• Price history tracking: detects price increases and alerts before the hike.

• Cash flow forecasting: shows the impact of each subscription on the coming months.

• Family sharing: shared spaces with clear roles (owner/member).

• Privacy first: works via self-hosting or cloud with encryption, without selling any data.

• Multi-currency and multi-language: native Arabic interface (RTL) alongside English.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Add a subscription manually with the billing cycle (monthly/yearly/custom), category, and currency.

2. A visual dashboard for monthly, yearly, and upcoming spending.

3. Renewal alerts via email and Webhook/Telegram.

4. Import CSV statements with automatic detection of recurring subscriptions (rules + name similarity).

5. Track price changes and record the date of each increase.

6. Cancellation assistant with templates, links, and steps for each service.

7. A shared family space with permission roles.

8. Support for Arabic/English and multiple currencies with updatable exchange rates.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free-to-start: up to 10 subscriptions, alerts, and CSV import, with full self-hosting capability.

• Pro plan: unlimited subscriptions + advanced automatic detection + cash flow forecasting.

• Family plan: up to 6 members and shared spaces.

• A one-time paid self-hosting license for those who want Pro features on their own server.

• Revenue model: freemium (monthly/yearly subscription) + optional sponsorship for the open-source community.

5) Global launch plan with free channels

• GitHub + self-hosting communities: an open-source release as an attractive base version.

• Product Hunt and Hacker News: launch with the "privacy first" story.

• Reddit: budgeting and privacy communities (r/personalfinance, r/selfhosted).

• SEO content: articles on "how to cancel a subscription" and "subscription tracking" in Arabic and English.

• YouTube/TikTok: short explainers on saving money.

• App stores and Webhook interfaces for integration with automation tools (such as n8n).

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: financial data privacy, transaction record keeping, and GDPR compliance; formal "financial advice" must not be provided without a license, and attention must be paid to service-name trademarks.

• Security/technical: sensitivity of bank data, accuracy of CSV statement parsing, exchange rate volatility, self-hosting security, and reliance of maintenance on an open-source community.

• Mitigations: full encryption, not storing bank secrets, open transparency, and clear boundaries between "information" and "advice".

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Dawra (دَورة)

Tagline: Track all your subscriptions, stop the leaks, and save smartly from one place.
