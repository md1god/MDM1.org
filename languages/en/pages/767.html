Insight — Complete Project Plan

Know who is preparing to buy before your competitor does, and send them the right message in two minutes.

Execution plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue, distribution, risks. Under construction, not launched.

1) Target Audience

• Mid-sized company sales teams (10–200 staff): manager with 5–15 reps needs 40 new prospects/week, not lists everyone buys. Sectors: software, logistics, manufacturing; buying decisions precede market requests by weeks.

• Pre-seed to Series A founders: 3–12 people, no dedicated sales; first 20 customers in 90 days. Start free, upgrade after first contract; budget may be under $500/month.

• Marketing managers and req gen: need to know which accounts merit extra ad budget this week; tie signals to campaigns, not mass messages.

• Agencies and independent consultants: consultant with 8–20 clients needs one "ready signal" each for a 10-minute talk, not a prospecting system per account.

• Customer success and expansion (Account Expansion): track 100–300 accounts; learn when a client hires a data manager or raises funds, then pitch a package 20%–40% higher.

• Non-technical users in Arabic-speaking regions: salesperson who knows neither SQL nor CSV; needs a simple UI, WhatsApp/email alerts, and a ready professional-tone message editable in one click.

2) Differentiators (Why choose our product)

• Signal before noise: instead of "buzz," an action in a short window: job post in 72 hours, funding in 30 days, tech provider change, or profile update. Each has date, source, confidence 1–100.

• Explain, don't just collect: one line why: "3 data engineers hired = likely analytics launch in 6 weeks." Data becomes a decision; research 25 min -> under 2/account.

• Conversation draft, not a repeated message: 80–120 words opening with the signal, one editable field, auto follow-ups at 3 and 7 days. No sending before approval; no identical mass templates.

• Source transparency and right to object: each alert links to source, capture date, "Not accurate" button; disputed signals lose score and leave recommendations. Trust: let you verify, not hide the source.

• Privacy from day one: no email permissions or client data up front; public data only. CRM/calendar optional later; deletion wipes all settings in 24 hours.

• Meetings, not signal counts: dashboard: signals viewed, replies, meetings, one deal in 60 days. Zero meetings after 30 days of weekly use adds a free month.

3) First Version Scope (8 tasks max)

1. Signal capture engine: 5 public sources (job pages, announced funding, domain records, company news, public work profiles) via tasks every 6 hours; store source and timestamp.

2. Signal classifier: simple rules + light language model sort signals into 5 types (hiring, funding, technology, expansion, leadership change) and score relevance 1–100 by sector and size.

3. Simple account profile: 3 sectors, size (1–50 / 51–200 / 201+), region, 10 editable keywords; no uploads.

4. Daily/weekly digest: one email at 9:00 a.m. user time, max 5 signals, or a Monday summary for quiet users.

5. Draft generator: 80- to 120-word message + subject line (Subject) + one follow-up; "Copy" and "Adjust tone" (formal / direct / concise).

6. Tracking board: statuses—new, contacted, replied, meeting, excluded—with weekly meeting counter.

7. Settings/privacy: optional WhatsApp or email link, CSV export, 1-click deletion, 30-day change log.

8. Free start: 25 signals + 10 drafts/month, no card; 1-click upgrade at limit, in-app note not pop-up.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free: 25 signals + 10 drafts + one daily email alert, no card, no time limit; first real meeting before any payment ask.

• Starter – $19/month: 200 signals, 60 drafts, WhatsApp alerts, 3 sectors, CSV export. For one founder or solo rep; 10x cheaper than a database subscription.

• Team – $79/month: 5 seats, 1,000 signals, unlimited drafts, optional CRM, shared board. ~$16/seat, paid for by two extra meetings/month.

• Agency – $249/month: 15 seats, client workspaces, weekly White-label report, extra seats $12.

• Annual: 20% off (Starter $182/year, Team $758/year); 14-day refund if unused.

• Later revenue: "Custom signal" pack ($5/alert outside sectors) + $49 monthly workshop on turning signals into meetings; never sell user data.

5) Global Expansion Plan, Free Channels

• Verifiable content: 3 weekly articles, titles like "How do you know a company is preparing to buy a data tool within 45 days?", public-signal screenshots, figures (e.g., 62% of software funding rounds lead to hiring in 60 days). Share on LinkedIn, X, sales communities.
