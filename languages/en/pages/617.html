Manazir — Complete Project Plan

Watch browser agents work side by side, compare their performance transparently, and export ready-made code in minutes.

A complete plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, launch, and risks. The project is under construction and not yet launched.

1) Target audience

• Software developers and AI agent engineers testing ideas before building infrastructure.

• QA, automation, and agency teams running repetitive browser tasks like search, extraction, and forms.

• Product teams and startups comparing automation engines before adoption.

• Students and hackathon participants, especially Arabic speakers seeking RTL support.

• Shared need: cloud browser setup, keys, and monitoring are costly and complex; engine comparisons are unfair and unreproducible.

2) Differentiators (why choose us)

• Instant start with no signup or card, plus a clear free daily quota.

• Fair comparison of two engines on the same task, start point, and browser profile.

• Transparent monitoring: live stream, screenshots, DOM, console, and network logs, with each step explained.

• Export TypeScript or Python with replayable trace JSON and no platform lock-in.

• Native Arabic/English support with an RTL interface and Arabic task/site templates.

• Secure by default: full isolation, allowed-domain list, secret blocking, and PII redaction.

• Original design and identity focused on “watch and compare,” not copying existing interfaces.

• Per-step cost and time transparency for data-driven engineering decisions.

3) MVP scope (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Anonymous guest session with daily quota and anti-abuse limits, no signup.

2. Bilingual task builder with templates: search, data extraction, comparison, and forms.

3. Isolated cloud browser with live screen streaming and DOM/console/network logs.

4. Run one agent with pause, resume, and step-by-step control.

5. Side-by-side comparison of up to two engines from the same start.

6. Results card: success/failure, step count, time, cost, and errors.

7. Export TS/Python code, trace JSON, and a 24-hour public replay link.

8. Security controls: domain list, credential-entry blocking, PII redaction, and content classification.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: 8 runs/day, up to 3 minutes each, two-engine comparison, code export, and 24-hour public replay.

• Pro: $19/month; 300 runs, longer sessions, 30-day private traces, three-engine comparison, and API.

• Team: $99/month; 5 seats, shared library, roles and permissions, SSO, and 90-day retention.

• Enterprise: custom pricing; VPC or on-prem deployment, SLA, security audit, and priority support.

• Revenue: subscriptions + margin on overage + enterprise contracts.

• Economics control: cap Free browser minutes, separate queues, and strict usage limits.

5) Global launch plan with free channels

• Bilingual SEO for task and integration pages, with an optional redacted replay gallery.

• GitHub: SDK, CLI, examples, and open benchmarks, inviting users to try the product.

• Launch via Product Hunt, Hacker News, Reddit, and Dev.to, plus a weekly browser-task challenge.

• Short videos on YouTube, X, and LinkedIn, and Arabic and international developer communities.

• Partnerships with university clubs and hackathons, plus a referral program with free credit.

• List integrations in developer tool directories and add-on marketplaces, without paid ads.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Violating some sites’ terms or automated-access limits; solution: fair-use policy, domain list, and blocking CAPTCHA or payment bypass.

• Screenshot/log privacy may include personal data; solution: automatic redaction, short retention, and immediate deletion on request.

• Model provider terms and exported code licenses; solution: legal review, keeping code ownership with the user.

• Platform misuse for fraud or spam; solution: limits, content classification, suspicious-session suspension, and credential blocking.

Technical:

• Browser isolation, SSRF, malicious pages, and secret leaks; solution: isolated containers, restricted network, and input scanning.

• Bot detection, CAPTCHA, and site changes; solution: clear expectations, smart retries, and documented limitations.

• Free usage cost and abuse; solution: strict quotas, anomaly monitoring, and free queues.

• Comparison consistency and changing provider interfaces; solution: fixed configuration snapshots, replay, and a swappable integration layer.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Manazir

Tagline: Watch browser agents work side by side, compare their performance transparently, and export ready-made code in minutes.
