Naqaa — Complete Project Plan

Turn any cluttered page into a clean reading experience focused on the words alone.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, launch plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a light, comfortable web reader

1) Target audience

• Daily readers: those who consume long articles and reports on the web.

• Researchers and students: those who collect sources and need saving, highlighting, and exporting.

• Developers and technical writers: those who want to convert articles into clean Markdown.

• Non-English speakers: those who need easier reading with interface language support.

• Users of slow devices: those looking for a light extension that does not consume memory.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Smart content detection that runs on a local engine inside the browser without uploading the user's text to any server.

• A fully customizable focus mode: fonts, spacing, line width, paper/dark theme.

• Saving and highlighting without a mandatory account: fully functional at first without logging in.

• Clean export to Markdown, HTML, PDF, and note-taking tools.

• Optional end-to-end encrypted sync only when the user wants it.

• Lightness and performance: no injection of heavy scripts, and near-zero performance impact on the page.

• Multilingual from day one with full Arabic support and a right-to-left interface.

3) Scope of the first version (max 8 tasks)

1. Core content extraction engine and hiding of clutter elements.

2. Focused reading mode with controls for font, spacing, width, and theme.

3. Saving articles to a local "Read later" list.

4. Highlighting and adding notes with the ability to revisit them.

5. Exporting the article/excerpts list to Markdown and PDF.

6. Localizing the interface and full RTL support.

7. Optional encrypted sync via a free account.

8. Performance improvements and a light settings panel.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free forever: all core features, unlimited local saving, Markdown export.

• Paid plan (Pro, low monthly/yearly subscription): expanded cloud sync, search in excerpts, advanced PDF/documents export, ERP/wikis support, and reading statistics.

• Lifetime license as an alternative option for those who dislike subscriptions.

• Early founder pricing (a permanent discount for the first subscribers).

• No ads in the reader at all, to preserve clarity.

5) Global launch plan with free channels

• Publishing the extension in browser stores (Chrome Web Store, Firefox Add-ons, Edge).

• An open-source repository + community contributions + a strong README file.

• Educational content on Product Hunt, Hacker News, and Reddit (r/productivity, r/webdev).

• Partnerships with productivity blogs and publishing transparent comparisons.

• A fully Arabic version for a globally neglected market.

• Inviting tech influencers to try it for free without paid compensation.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Respecting copyright: displaying content to the user only, and preventing public re-publishing.

• Compliance with password/privacy policies (GDPR/CCPA) and transparency of data collection.

• Not imitating the identity, name, or interface of any existing product.

Technical:

• The diversity of modern page architectures (JS frameworks) may cause extraction to fail → a solution with updatable heuristics.

• Browser store updates may break permissions → the fewest possible permissions.

• Sync security → end-to-end encryption and local keys.

• Performance on heavy pages → running on demand only.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Naqaa

Tagline: Turn any cluttered page into a clean reading experience focused on the words alone.
