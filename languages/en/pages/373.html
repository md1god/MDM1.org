Milkaba — Full Project Plan

Your invoices get reminded until they're paid.

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout plan, and risks. The project is being built and has not launched yet.

1) Target audience

• Freelancers: designers, developers, content writers, and translators who get paid late.

• Small business owners and consultants who issue long-term monthly invoices.

• People with 3–20 open invoices per month who lose time chasing payment manually.

• People who don't know how to word professional dunning messages or feel embarrassed when following up.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Automatic time-based escalation ladder: friendly reminder ← formal demand ← final warning — it stops the moment payment arrives.

• Professional message wording that adapts to the client's language and prior relationship with them, not rigid templates.

• One status board for every invoice: current stage, next message due date, communication history.

• Notifications via email and WhatsApp/Telegram with a cooling-off window preventing more than one message per day.

• Auto-generated ready-to-use final warning draft for an amicable settlement or a collection path without needing an expensive lawyer.

• Completely free to start with unlimited access to core invoices.

3) Scope of the first version (8 tasks maximum)

1. Enter a single invoice (client name, email, amount, due date, currency).

2. Upload the freelancer's contract and the original agreement as attachments linked to the messages.

3. Three automatically scheduled escalation stages (3 days / 7 days / 14 days after the due date).

4. Message template editor with variables filled automatically (amount, number, days overdue).

5. Automatic email sending with message open-tracking.

6. Payment receipt: a public payment link or a "Paid" button that stops the ladder immediately.

7. Dashboard showing all invoices, their current stage, and upcoming message schedule.

8. Smart notifications for the freelancer before each message, with a pause/cancel option.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: two active invoices, three escalation stages, email open tracking.

• Pro — $9/month: 20 invoices, full template customization, multi-channel reminders, PDF export of the communication log.

• Team — $24/month: a small team (up to 5 members), unlimited invoices, dedicated sending numbers.

• Additional revenue: a small optional commission on invoices collected through the built-in payment links.

• Hypothesis: conversion from Free to Pro when a customer exceeds two overdue invoices in the same month.

5) Global rollout plan using free channels

• Core SEO content: "payment reminder message" pages in English/Arabic/Spanish + free copy-paste templates.

• Reddit and Forums: sharing useful tools and templates in freelancer communities without direct advertising.

• X/LinkedIn: short threads with real examples of successful collection messages before/after editing.

• Discord and Slack communities for freelancers: a free bot that estimates days overdue and drafts the first reminder.

• Product Hunt and Hacker News with a ready MVP and a limited free launch plan.

• Partnerships with Arab designer and developer communities in exchange for free discount codes.

• Publish small helper tools (late fee calculator, demand message generator) for recurring traffic.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: dispute authorities differ between countries; messages must be limited to professional civil demands without threats or blackmail, plus a disclaimer that the tool is not legal advice.

• Legal: client data privacy controls and GDPR/CCPA compliance for storing emails, attachments, and invoice confidentiality.

• Legal: preventing use of the tool for fabricated or undue claims that could expose the platform to liability.

• Technical: sending-domain reputation suffers from spam; solved by gradual sending, SPF/DKIM, and strict daily limits.

• Technical: email open detection may break due to image blocking; rely on multiple signals and reduce dependence on it.

• Technical: automatic sending may fail and send a message after payment; needs double verification and an emergency stop button.

• Technical: account theft and users being flooded with unintended messages; two-factor authentication and an audit log for every send.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Milkaba

Marketing tagline: Your invoices keep being reminded until they're paid.
