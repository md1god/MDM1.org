Raseen — Complete Project Plan

A polished, job-aligned resume, ready for applicant tracking systems.

Complete plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, growth, and risks. The project is under construction and not yet launched.

1) Target audience

• Online job seekers, especially recent graduates, mid-career professionals, and career changers.

• Applicants for international or remote roles who write resumes in English or Arabic.

• Price-sensitive users: intensive job seekers, people returning to work, and residents of emerging markets.

• Secondary channel: university career centers, training bootcamps, and career coaches offering the tool to students.

2) Differentiators

• Privacy first: local in-browser analysis by default, no mandatory account, immediate deletion.

• Native Arabic and English: RTL support and proper tokenization, not just translated UI.

• Explainable score: every deduction has a clear reason, not a black box.

• Actionable suggestions: specific rewording with a “why” rationale and professional synonyms, not dry keywords.

• Safe ATS export: cleanly structured .docx, no complex tables or columns, with preview.

• Real free tier: core score and export without registration, with fair cost limits.

• Calm original design: Arabic-first editorial interface, high contrast, fast, no stock images.

3) First-version scope (maximum 8 tasks)

1. No-registration flow: upload or paste resume and job description on one page.

2. Local PDF/DOCX text extraction, optional OCR for scans, with Arabic, English, and RTL support.

3. Transparent 0–100 scoring for match, skills, experience, education, keywords, and writing quality.

4. Keyword gaps: missing, repeated, or low-importance words, with synonyms and importance per word.

5. Copyable rewordings with clear reasons, plus a free daily API-call limit.

6. Safe .docx export for ATS with preview, preserving heading and bullet structure.

7. Privacy: no storage by default, immediate deletion, transparency about external-interface processing.

8. Responsive, fast interface with basic accessibility, and anonymous failure/usage metrics without personal data.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Permanent free tier: 3 full checks daily, score, gap analysis, basic suggestions, and one export daily, without registration.

• Pro: $5.99/month or $39/year: fair-use checks and exports, cover letters, interview prep, version history, job offer comparison.

• Occasion credits: $3 for 10 extra checks for non-subscribers.

• B2B later: $199/month for career centers and universities up to 500 users, with an admin dashboard.

• Regional pricing by purchasing power, local payment methods, easy cancellation, no ads on free tier.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Long-tail SEO: useful “ATS score for [job title]” pages in English and Arabic, with real examples.

• Short-form content: before/after clips of anonymized resumes on YouTube, TikTok, and LinkedIn, plus career-creator collaborations.

• Practical communities: Reddit, Discord, Telegram, and Facebook groups, sharing value first without spam.

• Universities and bootcamps: free workshops and dedicated links for career centers and coaches.

• Launch platforms: Product Hunt, Hacker News, Indie Hackers, and BetaList.

• Referrals: friend invites give both parties extra checks, with a share link to an anonymized result.

• Localization: Arabic and English first, then Spanish, Portuguese, and Indonesian based on usage data.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• GDPR and CCPA compliance: data minimization, no resume use for training, API processing agreements, clear deletion policy.

• No employment promises: the score is guidance, not an official result from any ATS.

• Discrimination and AI risks: keep decisions with the user, review suggestions, audit results periodically.

• Check trademark and domain before launch, use licensed fonts and libraries, and original designs.

• Transparent billing, cancellation, and refunds under consumer laws.

Technical:

• Inconsistent PDF/DOCX reading: extensive testing, fallback OCR, careful handling of scans.

• ATS ambiguity: avoid claiming a perfect match; rely on an explainable guidance score.

• LLM hallucination and cost: structured templates, output validation, usage limits, smaller models where possible.

• Prompt injection via resume or job description: separate data from instructions and sanitize inputs.
