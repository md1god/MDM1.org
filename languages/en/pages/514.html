Nabra — Complete Project Plan

Turn one idea into a full presence in your own tone, without losing your voice.

Complete plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue, launch, risks. Under construction; not launched.

1) Target audience

• Individual creators—podcasters, YouTubers, newsletter writers, skills teachers—publishing long-form with no time to repurpose.

• Experts/consultants—coaches, doctors, lawyers, financial advisers—needing daily presence without new production.

• Small agencies/marketing teams (2–10 people) managing client accounts, needing approvals and workflows.

• Arabic-speaking creators underserved by global tools, especially dialects and RTL.

• Zero-budget beginners wanting a free start with no credit card or watermark.

2) Differentiators

1. Idea engine, not clipper: extracts units (idea, story, quote, statistic) and rebuilds each output from scratch.

2. Multilingual tone fingerprint: learns style from 3 samples, preserves it in Modern Standard Arabic, dialects, and English.

3. Mandatory timestamp attribution: quotes/numbers link to source timestamps to prevent hallucination; human review before publishing.

4. Native per-platform output: independent hook, length, captions, CTA, aspect ratio; no copy-paste.

5. Free without watermark: limits on exports and source duration, not branding.

6. Clear rights: source ownership attestation, licensed assets, no processing others' content.

7. Performance learning loop: creator logs results; engine recommends closer formats.

8. Collaboration/approvals: shared workspace with pre-scheduling review for small agencies.

3) MVP scope (max 8 tasks)

1. Sign-in and "tone fingerprint" setup from 3 text/audio samples, with language and dialect.

2. Import one owned source: file, YouTube/RSS link, or long text.

3. Transcription with timestamps and speaker separation; extract ideas, quotes, stories.

4. Generate 3 short-video scripts with different hooks, a carousel outline, posts/threads.

5. Unified RTL/Brand Kit editor showing quotes beside timestamps.

6. Export 9:16 video with burned-in subtitles + SRT + text, 720p free, no watermark.

7. Account, billing, limit metering, project history.

8. Feedback: rate versions and log performance for later recommendations.

4) Pricing and revenue

Freemium, no credit card; upgrade when limits are exceeded.

· Plan · Price · Core limits ·

· Free · $0 · 3 exports/month, 15-min source, 720p, 1 Brand Kit ·

· Creator · $9/month · 30 exports, 90 min, 1080p, 3 Brand Kits, scheduling ·

· Pro · $24/month · 100 exports, 300 min, all dialects, analytics, priority processing ·

· Team · $59/month · 5 seats, 250 exports, approvals, shared library ·

· Credit packs · $5–15 · occasional users, valid 12 months ·

• Annual discount = two months free; 30% recurring referral commission for 12 months.

• Target margin >70% via free quotas, caching, batch processing.

• No ads or data sales; revenue only from subscriptions and credits.

5) Global launch with free channels

• Long-tail SEO: 150–200 Arabic/English pages, e.g. "turn a podcast episode into short clips," with embedded free tools.

• No-signup tools: hook generator, article-to-thread converter; collect emails and build a waitlist.

• Community launch: Product Hunt, BetaList, Indie Hackers, Show HN, per each community's rules.

• Build in public: weekly X/LinkedIn series on development, numbers, lessons.

• Short video: YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Reels tutorials showing one source becoming a full package.

• Partnerships/audience swaps with podcast hosts and small newsletters via free mutual promotion.

• Referrals: friend invites give both sides extra exports.

• Copyable Notion/Canva templates under the product name; optional "Made with" share pages.

• Free Zapier, Make, Notion, YouTube integrations to list in their marketplaces.

• Localize first, then expand: Arabic/English, then Spanish, Portuguese, Indonesian, Hindi, with local ambassadors and Discord/WhatsApp communities.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Source rights: mandatory ownership attestation, no downloading others' content, DMCA policy, approval log.

• Platform terms: official APIs only, no scraping/limit bypass; comply with YouTube, Meta, etc.

• Audio/image: explicit consent before voice cloning, disclose generated content, no impersonation.

• Privacy: GDPR/CCPA, encryption, deletion on request, team DPA, EU processing option.

• Regulatory transparency: align with EU AI Act disclosures and platform policies; review model provider terms.

• Output ownership: clear user rights to outputs, reasonable indemnity limits.
