Kashf — Complete Project Plan

Issue your first invoice in two minutes and track your collections smartly.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the distribution plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: an invoicing, projects, and time platform — an original version

1) Target audience

• Freelancers and small service providers (designers, developers, consultants, photographers) who want to issue an invoice and track their hours without accounting complexity.

• Small agencies (2–15 people) that need quotes, projects, time tracking, and recurring invoicing across multiple teams.

• Emerging markets that lack easy payment gateways and need support for local currencies and simplified tax invoices.

• Developers and small companies that want self-hosting to protect their clients' data.

2) Points of distinction (why choose our product)

• Instant start with no payment wall: issue your first invoice in under two minutes, with no credit card.

• An authentically Arabic interface from the ground up (full RTL, local numbers and currencies), not just a later translation.

• Automatic collection intelligence: due alerts, friendly automated reminders, and ready-made follow-up messages.

• Time tracking tied directly to the invoice: press start, and the time turns into billable line items.

• Hosting transparency: a fully managed cloud option or self-hosting with the same core features.

• Lightweight and simple: fast loading and a less complex experience than bloated all-in-one systems.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Contact management (clients/suppliers) and multi-currency support.

2. Invoice creation (draft → sent → paid) with PDF and export.

3. Quotes and one-click conversion to an invoice or project.

4. A reusable catalog of items/services with tax and discount.

5. A time tracker tied to the project and task and billable.

6. Automated reminders and payment-status tracking with a collections dashboard.

7. A simple payment link + manual logging of partial payments.

8. Basic reports (sales, receivables, hours) with numbering and PDF sharing.

4) Pricing and profit model

• Free forever (Free-to-start): unlimited invoices, up to 5 clients, time tracking, basic payment link.

• Pro plan (monthly/yearly): unlimited clients and projects, automated reminders, recurring invoicing, advanced reports, custom branding.

• Team plan: multiple users, roles and permissions, audit log, integrations.

• Paid self-hosting with a single annual license including support and enterprise features.

• Additional income: a small commission on payment gateways, add-on domains/services, and priority support.

5) Global distribution plan with free channels

• Arabic/English SEO content: "how to issue an invoice" guides, free templates, and practical comparisons.

• Attractive free tools: tax calculator, invoice-number generator, PDF templates — driving sign-ups.

• Communities: Reddit, freelancer communities, Arabic Facebook/Telegram groups, Indie Hackers.

• Product Hunt and GitHub: open launch + a contributor repo path for self-hosting.

• Referral program: a free month of Pro for each side upon upgrade.

• Partnerships with co-working spaces and freelance hubs to use the tool for free in exchange for promotion.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: differing tax invoicing laws between countries (such as mandatory e-invoicing) — solved via local settings, customizable templates, and a clear disclaimer.

• Data protection: GDPR and privacy protection — encryption, choice of hosting, and data processing agreements.

• Copying no protected asset: all code, design, and the name are original from scratch, with a trademark review.

• Technical: attacks and sensitive financial integrations — multi-factor authentication, security audits, and delegating payment to certified providers.

• Retention and competition: adopting full data export and flexibility to move between plans to reduce churn risk.

• Single-provider dependency: avoiding technical lock-in by abstracting the payment layer and supporting multiple providers.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Kashf

Marketing tagline: Issue your first invoice in two minutes and track your collections smartly.
