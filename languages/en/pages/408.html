Nazam — Full Project Plan

Design your board in Arabic, and launch it to production in an hour.

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: An original alternative to electronic circuit design software

1) Target audience

• Individual engineers and programmers building IoT devices, robotics projects, and wearable products.

• Students and researchers in engineering colleges and technical institutes who need a free tool to learn PCB design.

• Startups and small workshops that need fast design and online collaboration without expensive licenses.

• Independent designers (Makers) in Maker and open-hardware communities worldwide.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Full Arabic interface with RTL support, which is rare in EDA tools.

• Integrated database engine: schematic, PCB layout, and parts library in one application without complex linking.

• Smart checking with customizable Design Rules, with illustrated explanations for each error.

• Real-time collaboration on the same project between multiple designers, with full change history.

• Open export with no lock-in: open Gerber, BOM, and STEP files readable by any other tool.

• Lightweight cloud platform that runs in a modern browser, plus a desktop version for offline use.

3) Scope of the first version (8 tasks maximum)

1. Schematic Editor with a basic symbol library.

2. PCB Layout editor with autorouting and manual layout.

3. Central shareable parts library (symbols + 3D models).

4. Design Rule Check (DRC) and Electrical Rules Check (ERC) engine.

5. One-click export of Gerber / Drill / BOM / Pick-and-Place.

6. 3D preview of the final board inside the browser.

7. Cloud saving and full project version history.

8. Full Arabic interface with embedded documentation and tutorial videos.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free to start: unlimited projects, full export, and a public parts library — no credit card required.

• Pro at $12/month: real-time collaboration, private projects, extended parts library, priority in cloud processing.

• Education plan: $50/year per educational institution (multiple student accounts + dashboards).

• Additional revenue: small commission on PCB Fabrication through partners, plus non-intrusive ads in the free edition.

5) Global rollout plan using free channels

• YouTube: "Zero to First Board" tutorials in Arabic, English, and Hindi.

• Reddit and Discord: electronics, embedded, and Maker communities; direct links to the project.

• GitHub: publishing open project templates and attracting contributors.

• Twitter/X and LinkedIn: short GIF clips of designing a board in 60 seconds.

• Universities and clubs: "a workspace for every team" campaigns and free tickets for student initiatives.

• EEVblog and Hackaday forums: sharing use cases and real problem threads.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: avoid any code or library with a closed license; rely only on documented GPL/MIT licenses, and document the source of every step.

• Legal: trademark issues — check domain and trademark names before launch.

• Technical: accuracy of the layout engine and performance with large boards (10,000 traces); early load tests.

• Technical: compatibility with different fabrication houses in Gerber settings.

• Competitive: established free tools; differentiation comes from collaboration, Arabic, and the cloud — not from copying features one by one.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: نَظَام — Nazam PCB

Tagline: Design your board in Arabic, and launch it to production in an hour.
