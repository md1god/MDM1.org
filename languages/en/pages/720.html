Mudrik — Complete Project Plan

Turn every lecture into quizzes, flashcards, and a voice tutor that remembers what you learned.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, growth plan, and risks. Under construction, not launched.

1) Target audience

• University students in content-heavy majors (medicine, pharmacy, law, engineering), especially with 15–25 weekly lecture hours needing pre-exam revision.

• Students preparing for USMLE, MCAT, IELTS, GRE, paying $30–100 monthly for courses and question banks.

• Independent learners and professionals buying Coursera and Udemy courses, forgetting 70% in a week without review.

• Teachers and small institutes (5–50 instructors) wanting automatic quizzes and flashcards without complex tools.

• Non-English speakers needing native-language audio with original terminology.

2) Differentiators

• File to mastery: extract concepts, build a knowledge map, generate source-linked questions with page or timestamp citations, not generic summaries.

• Quality over quantity: reject questions lacking clear source answers; classify by Bloom's level (remember/apply/analyze) to avoid blind memorization.

• Voice tutor with memory: remembers weak points, follows up, explains in your dialect with English terms, simulating oral exams.

• Explainable spaced repetition: FSRS with a dashboard showing "why this card appeared now" and full schedule control.

• Arabic and multilingual: reasonably accurate dialect speech conversion and native RTL, not after-the-fact translation.

• Better economics: a 90-minute lecture in under 4 minutes at under $0.25 inference, enabling a generous free tier.

3) First-version scope (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Import: PDF, MP3, MP4, YouTube links, with speech-to-text (Whisper) and slide extraction.

2. Concept map: 20–40 key concepts, similarity links, 5-point summary.

3. Quiz generator: 10–30 questions (multiple choice, fill-in, true/false), explanations and citations.

4. Auto flashcards with FSRS scheduling, daily review under 20 minutes.

5. Voice tutor: voice/text dialogue, "test me" mode, instant pronunciation/terminology correction.

6. Dashboard: mastery per concept, weak points, seven-day pre-exam plan.

7. Shared space: class/group link, teacher dashboard, export to Anki and PDF.

8. Free account without credit card, autosave, learner-owned data export.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: 3 lectures monthly (up to 180 minutes), basic quizzes, 15 voice minutes—no card.

• Pro: $8 monthly or $72 yearly (25% off): 60 processing hours, unlimited voice, export, smart review.

• Teams/Campus: $4 per seat monthly at 20+ seats, teacher dashboard, reports, SSO.

• Regional PPP: $3 monthly for Egypt, India, Nigeria, lowering payment barriers, preserving margin.

• Extra revenue: ready-made package marketplace (USMLE, law), institutional API at $0.02/question.

• Targets: ~$1.2 monthly inference cost per Pro user, >80% margin, 4–6% free-to-paid conversion.

5) Global growth plan with free channels

• Programmatic SEO: 3,000 long-tail pages "[subject] questions + [university/test]" plus a free tool generating 5 questions from short text.

• TikTok and YouTube Shorts: "study 10 minutes" challenge and before/after one-week comparison, plus bio link.

• University ambassadors: 200 students at 40 universities get free Pro for 10 active referrals at near-zero cost.

• Communities: answer study questions on Reddit, Discord, Quora; launch a free open-source Anki add-on as a channel.

• Product Hunt and Hacker News launch: "lecture to exam in 4 minutes," plus lifetime deal for first 500 users.

• Referral loop: one Pro week per activated friend, plus a shareable "weekly recap."

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Content rights: recorded lectures may infringe university/professor rights; use personal-use pledge, encryption, no public sharing.

• Privacy: sensitive voice and educational data; GDPR/FERPA compliance, 30-day deletion, no model training on user data by default.

• Academic integrity: prohibit "solve the exam for me"; focus on generation, explanation, citation, not ready answers.

• Speech-to-text errors: low dialect and medical-term accuracy; add quick correction, flag low-confidence spots.

• Hallucination: every question passes "is the answer in the source?" or is excluded/marked.

• Cost and single-provider dependence: route across models and cache; cut cost 30% via smaller models for simple tasks.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Mudrik (مُدرِك)

Tagline: Turn every lecture into quizzes, flashcards, and a voice tutor that remembers what you learned.
