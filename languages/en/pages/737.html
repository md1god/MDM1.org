YieldAtlas — Full Project Plan

Find below-market property deals in the UK, evaluate transparently, start free.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout plan, and risks. The project is under construction and not yet launched.

1) Target Audience

• First-time Buy-to-Let investors in Midlands/North cities (Manchester, Leeds, Birmingham, Liverpool): age 25-40, budget £120k-250k, target rental yield ≥6%.

• Portfolio owners (5-30 properties) seeking steady deal flow without broker fees (typically 2-3%).

• Independent sourcers needing proof (score + comparables) to sell a deal on commission.

• Foreign investors (Gulf/Europe) buying remotely wanting area data due diligence.

• HMO/turnkey investors needing planning and licensing alerts before purchase.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Transparency: 0-100 score with visible weights (discount vs median, yield, days on market, EPC, crime, transport) not a "black box".

• Free-to-start: search + 3 alerts/month without a card, reducing signup friction.

• Data integration: Land Registry + EPC + crime + transport + council tax + planning (where available) instead of listing-only.

• Adjustable "below market" threshold (e.g. ≥12% discount) + median per postcode (LSOA) to avoid outlier skew.

• CSV/PDF export + audit trail to present deals to partners/funders.

• No long contracts: cancel with one click, with pause subscription for seasonal investors.

3) First version scope (max 8 tasks)

1. Ingest: import listings + sold prices (Land Registry/feeds) with daily update and dedup.

2. Postcode analytics: median, yield, discount, 12m trend per postcode/LSOA.

3. Scoring v1: explainable factors + risk flags (flood, crime, low EPC).

4. Responsive web app: filters (price, yield, beds, type), map, deal cards.

5. Alerts: saved searches + email/push + quiet hours.

6. Accounts: saved deals, notes, tags, shared link.

7. Exports: CSV/PDF report with comparables.

8. Admin: data quality dashboard (stale, missing EPC, duplicates).

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: 10 searches/day, 3 alerts/month, basic score.

• Starter £19/month: unlimited search, 25 alerts, export.

• Pro £49/month: advanced scoring, HMO/turnkey filters, bulk export, limited API.

• Team £149/month: 5 seats, shared watchlists, audit.

• Annual discount (2 months free), referral credits, extra alert packs (pay-as-you-go).

• Goal: 1,000 free → 8% conversion → ~80 paid × £45 = £3,600/month; at 5,000 free → ~£18,000/month.

5) Global rollout plan via free channels

• SEO: pages "[city] below market property deals" and "average yield [postcode]" with aggregated data.

• Reddit/forums (r/UKProperty, r/propertyinvesting): valuable posts (case studies) not spam.

• YouTube Shorts: deal analysis walkthrough (screen recording) 60-90 seconds.

• Product Hunt + Indie Hackers: build in public, weekly updates.

• LinkedIn: outreach to mortgage brokers/accountants/investors, referral affiliate.

• Partnerships: local property meetups, sourcers (referral commission).

• Monthly report "UK Below-Market Deal Index" for PR/organic backlinks.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Data licensing: follow feed/API terms; avoid prohibited scraping; keep provenance.

• GDPR: consent, retention, cookie policy, right to deletion.

• Accuracy: stale listings/asking vs sold; disclaimers; verify with Land Registry.

• False signals: scoring "not investment advice" + risk labels + manual verify.

• Technical: rate limits, geocoding costs, map tiles; caching + queues + monitoring.

• Competition/churn: differentiate via transparency + community; annual plans + pauses.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: YieldAtlas

Tagline: Find below-market property deals in the UK, evaluate them transparently, start for free.
