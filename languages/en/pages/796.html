Mi'yar — Complete Project Plan

Snap your card and know its price and grading value before you pay.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Hobby collectors (ages 18–40) in the Gulf, Egypt, Morocco, and the Levant: each owns between 200 and 2,000 cards spread across drawers and binders, and does not know which card deserves official grading and which should be sold as-is. They need a tool that answers in under a minute instead of two hours of searching.

• Sellers on online platforms: 20 to 50 sales per month, losing money when they list a card at a high "listed" price instead of the actual "sold" price. They want a number they can defend to the buyer.

• New buyers: a $100–300 budget, hesitating over a card priced at $120 because they do not know whether it is a rare version or a recent reprint. We give them an alert: "The price is 35% above the 90-day average."

• Small shop owners and trading clubs: they receive 3–20 customers daily and need a quick counter check without making the customer wait for manual research.

• Content creators and presenters: they produce "How much is this card worth?" episodes and need a figure documented with a source and date so they do not publish incorrect information.

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• Fast visual matching: the camera creates a visual fingerprint of the card and reads small letters and numbers via OCR, showing the match within 2–3 seconds with a confidence score. If the score is below 85%, we show two options for manual selection instead of forcing a wrong answer.

• Sold prices, not listed prices: we show the last 90-day range (low–high–median) with the number of actual transactions. Example: a $74 median from 18 sales, instead of a $99 listed price that has not sold yet.

• Grading feasibility calculator before paying: the user enters the expected grade, fees, shipping, insurance, and wait time, and the result is shown: expected sale $180 − ($65 grading + $12 shipping) = $103 net, and with a 60% probability of the desired grade, the expected profit becomes about $30. We also show "How much must the price rise for the process to be worthwhile?"

• Collection management, not just inventory: a dashboard shows "You completed 87 of 102 cards" and ranks missing cards by the value of completing the set, not alphabetically, so you know that a $40 card completes a $600 series.

• Privacy first: images stay on the device by default, are not used to train external models, and collection data is not sold to any party. The user can export everything with one click.

3) First Version Scope (8 Tasks Maximum)

1. A mobile-first responsive web app, with a capture button, image upload, and manual scan by code or name.

2. An initial matching engine covering about 15,000 cards from common editions, with a confidence score and correction option.

3. A weekly importer for sales data from licensed sources, with cleanup of duplicate listings and unaccounted shipping.

4. An ROI calculator with grades, fees, shipping, and time, with a report shareable as an image.

5. "My Vault": add/delete a card, grading status, and original purchase cost.

6. Portfolio dashboard: current value, profit/loss, and completion percentage for each set.

7. Free alerts: a card in your list drops by 15% or more, or a matching deal appears.

8. An "Incorrect match?" button that sends a correction from one user to improve the engine in the next batch.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free plan (free forever): 50 scans per month, 2 sets, 2 alerts, and one CSV export. Enough to experience the real value before any payment.

• Hobbyist plan: $5 monthly or $49 yearly (two months free). Unlimited scans, 20 sets, 20 alerts, and full price history for every card.

• Pro plan: $14 monthly for traders: invoices, a simplified tax report, API access at 500 requests/day, and inventory label printing.

• Club subscription: $9 monthly for 3 members (family account or school club) with a shared dashboard and defined editing permissions.

• No commission on sales: we refuse to take a percentage of user transactions so recommendations stay neutral. Any referral to a grading or shipping provider is clearly disclosed and is a fixed, limited amount.

• Ads deferred: no ads in the first version; later, at most one contextual ad per day in the free plan only, removable by upgrading.
