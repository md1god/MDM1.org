Wadih — Complete Project Plan

From a vague idea to clear, testable instructions with any AI model.

A complete plan for a new digital product: idea, audience, product, revenue model, launch plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target audience

• Freelancers, marketers, and content creators who use language models daily without deep technical expertise.

• Support, education, and small product teams that need consistent, reusable prompts.

• Researchers and developers who compare results across several models before relying on them.

• Arab users and multilingual teams, with native support for Arabic and English and RTL/LTR interfaces.

• Privacy-sensitive organizations that avoid sending data to tools with unclear retention and training policies.

2) Differentiators

• Diagnosis before optimization: we first reveal ambiguity, missing context, constraints, and required outputs, not just rewrite.

• Reusable context: brand identity, audience, tone, constraints, and examples are saved once and applied to all prompts.

• Testing instead of guessing: compare models against clear evaluation criteria instead of relying on impression.

• Model-agnostic: works with different models through an abstraction layer, without tying the product to one provider.

• Privacy first: sensitive data redaction, clear retention controls, and no use of content for training.

• Native Arabic experience: not an afterthought translation, but a design that understands Arabic phrasing and cultural differences.

• Free, frictionless start: core value available immediately without a payment card or installing an extension.

3) First-version scope (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Enter the prompt and define the goal and output.

2. Build reusable context: audience, tone, constraints, examples.

3. Diagnose the prompt and show issues with an explainable clarity score.

4. Rewrite in three formats: concise, detailed, and structured.

5. Compare two models against specific evaluation criteria.

6. Prompt library with versions, tags, and export.

7. Free account with usage limits, privacy controls, and data redaction.

8. Arabic/English interface with RTL/LTR support and quick-start guidance.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: 25 optimization runs monthly, two test models, 3 versions, limited library, no payment card.

• Pro: about $8 monthly or $72 yearly; broader fair use, advanced tests, brand tone, full history, and export.

• Team: about $15 per user monthly; shared library, roles, audit log, and usage analytics.

• Additional usage: optional credit for heavy testing with cost transparency.

• B2B/API: licensing for teams and educational platforms later.

• No ads, no selling data, and no core feature hidden behind payment.

5) Global launch plan with free channels

• Original Arabic and English SEO content: diagnosis guides, checklists, and “before/after” examples we produce.

• Community launch via Product Hunt, LinkedIn, X, and Reddit, sharing value first rather than ads.

• YouTube Shorts and TikTok videos showing a real prompt improvement in under a minute.

• A free weekly “prompt clinic”: improving participants’ prompts publicly with consent.

• A referral loop: share a read-only “prompt recipe” with a free link.

• Free partnerships with newsletters, universities, training bootcamps, and professional communities.

• Local Arabic/English landing pages and a built-in free “prompt clarity score” tool.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Unintentional copying of prompts or interfaces: build code and design from scratch, review intellectual property, and use original templates only.

• Protect the brand and name before scaling.

• User data may include personal information: GDPR/CCPA compliance, encryption, deletion on request, and no training on content.

• Model provider terms, usage limits, and fair use.

• Output ownership and content liability: clear terms and a disclaimer on final decisions.

Technical:

• Data leakage via prompt injection: isolate inputs, sanitize content, and enforce strict access limits.

• Volatile model quality, prices, and limits: abstraction layer, alternative providers, and periodic quality tests.

• Inference cost: caching, usage quotas, and economical models for simple tasks.

• Multilingual quality: an Arabic and English evaluation set, and human review of sensitive outputs.

• Dependence on an external service: availability monitoring and safe degradation plans.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Wadih

Tagline: From a vague idea to clear, testable instructions with any AI model.
