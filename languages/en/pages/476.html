Qayd — Complete Project Plan

Turn your bank statement into a clean, accounting-ready spreadsheet in minutes.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Accountants and auditors who receive monthly PDF statements and retype them manually.

• Small and medium business owners, freelancers, and e-commerce store owners.

• Accounting firms and financial operations teams in MENA, Europe, and Latin America.

• Users who upload Arabic and English statements from multiple banks and need clean Excel/CSV quickly.

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• In-browser preprocessing to protect sensitive files, with an optional cloud processing option when needed.

• Automatic bank and language detection, with support for Arabic and English statements without prior setup.

• Smart review table: every field has a confidence score and highlighting, so the accountant reviews only errors.

• Automatic detection of duplicate transactions and reconciliation of opening and closing balances.

• Export to Excel, CSV, and Google Sheets, with the option to add an API later.

• Free start with no card, and no charges if conversion fails.

• Operational speed: a 10-page file is ready in under a minute.

3) Scope of the First Version (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. Drag-and-drop PDF upload, with support for password-protected files.

2. Transaction extraction via OCR and parsing rules, with automatic bank and language detection.

3. Editable review table with highlighting of low-confidence fields.

4. Initial template library for ten banks (Arabic/English), updatable.

5. Export to Excel, CSV, and Google Sheets.

6. Free account with a monthly quota and a simple conversion history.

7. Encryption in transit and at rest, and automatic deletion of files after 24 hours.

8. Balance reconciliation check and a brief error report.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free: 3 files per month up to 20 pages, no credit card.

• Pro: $9 per month or $90 per year, up to 50 files and 200 pages per file.

• Business: $29 per month, 5 seats, API, and custom templates.

• Additional credit: $0.5 per additional file (Pay-as-you-go).

• The revenue model is based on subscription + usage, with a guarantee of no charges if conversion fails.

5) Global Go-to-Market Plan with Free Channels

• Search engine optimization through pages for each bank and formats such as “PDF to Excel” and a free balance reconciliation checker tool.

• Short content on YouTube and LinkedIn showing the before/after workflow.

• Practical sharing on Reddit, Facebook bookkeeping groups, and accounting communities.

• Launch on Product Hunt and Show HN.

• Free partnerships with accounting communities, universities, and referral programs.

• Early Arabic content for MENA markets, then Spanish for LATAM markets.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

Legal:

• Sensitivity of financial data: compliance with GDPR/CCPA and local protection laws, and clear data processing agreements.

• Liability for extraction errors: disclaimer and notice that human review is mandatory.

• Prevention of misuse: statement ownership verification and limits on new accounts.

• No claim of official integration with banks without an agreement.

Technical:

• Diversity of statements and changing formats: template engine + continuous automated testing.

• OCR errors in numbers and tables: combining rules with OCR, confidence scores, and review.

• Leakage security: encryption, isolation, immediate deletion, and audit logs.

• Cost and scaling: local processing first to reduce cloud load.

• Open Banking competition: future integration instead of confrontation.

7) Proposed Name and Tagline

Proposed name: Qayd (قَيْد)

Tagline: Turn your bank statement into a clean, accounting-ready spreadsheet in minutes.
