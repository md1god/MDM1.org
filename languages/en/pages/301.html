Mishkhat — Full Project Plan

Your photos at home — a private gallery run on your own server, no subscription, no surveillance.

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the rollout plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a self-managed personal photo gallery

1) Target audience

• Owners of home servers (Homelab) and defenders of their privacy and data.

• Amateur and professional photographers with large archives (tens to hundreds of thousands of images).

• Families and friends looking for a shared private album without accounts on foreign services.

• Anyone who recently moved away from closed cloud services and wants their files back under their control.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Fully local processing: face, object, and location tagging happens on your server, with no image sent to any third party.

• Speed with huge archives: pre-generated WebP/AVIF thumbnails and lazy loading, with a timeline that stays smooth across hundreds of thousands of files.

• Backup from your phone: automatic upload of the camera folder via scheduling, and hash matching to avoid duplicates.

• Secure sharing: temporary password-protected links, albums with limited permissions, no guest registration.

• Simple deployment: one container and one config file; runs on modest hardware (Raspberry Pi) and bare metal.

• No lock-in: full export of your data (files + tags) in an open format at any time.

3) Scope of the first release (8 tasks maximum)

1. Docker image and a single compose file for startup within minutes.

2. Scanner for the photo/video archive with a SQLite database and background jobs.

3. Timeline + albums + automatic tags (objects, places, dates).

4. Thumbnail cache with size optimization and automatic generation on addition.

5. Lightweight web app installable as an app + phone upload/sync with scheduling.

6. Temporary password-protected sharing links with a time limit.

7. Interactive map and navigation by date and location.

8. Scheduled backup + one-command update (update) + open data export.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free for individuals: near-unlimited use of all core features (starts out completely free).

• Family plan: around $4 per month or $39 per year — multiple users, public links, priority access to features.

• Voluntary support: crowdfunding (Sponsors / Buy Me a Coffee) for the open-source community edition.

• Ancillary revenue: ready-made display templates, paid integration packages, commercial support for small businesses.

5) Global rollout plan using free channels

• Public release on code hosting platforms + sharing in developer communities and "Show HN".

• Posts in self-hosting, DataHoarder, and digital privacy groups.

• Blog and short videos (YouTube/TikTok) explaining installation in 3 minutes and comparing "cloud vs your server".

• Documented package on the container registry with a comparison page to improve search engine visibility (SEO).

• Support groups on Telegram and Discord, and invitations to contribute translations and modified releases.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: face analysis counts as biometric data in many countries (such as GDPR) — we make it optional, processed locally, with explicit consent and permanent deletion available.

• Liability: content and files are the user's responsibility; we neither copy nor upload images to any third party.

• Licenses: verify the licenses of AI libraries and databases before inclusion to avoid commercial-use restrictions.

• Performance: indexing giant archives may consume memory/disk — solutions include streaming processing, multi-pass indexing, and background jobs.

• External dependency: changes in container registry or app store policies — direct distribution as a backup option.

• File duplication: syncing may create duplicate copies — solved through hash matching and smart merging.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Mishkhat

Tagline: Your photos at home — a private gallery run on your own server, no subscription, no surveillance.
