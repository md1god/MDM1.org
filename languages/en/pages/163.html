Athar — Complete Project Plan

Recover digital evidence and analyze what happened in minutes, not in weeks.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the go-to-market plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

Product plan: a digital evidence analysis platform

1) Target audience

• Independent digital investigators and small and medium-sized companies that do not have a full forensic lab.

• Information security and incident response (DFIR) teams inside companies.

• Evidence experts in law firms, financial audit, and professional review offices.

• System administrators who examine employee devices upon departure or when an incident occurs.

• Investigative journalism and human rights organizations that verify evidence.

• Students and researchers at universities.

2) Points of distinction (why choose our product)

• A web interface that runs directly in the browser with no heavy installation, with real-time collaboration between team members.

• Local-first analysis: device images are not uploaded to any server, so evidence stays inside the client's premises.

• An intelligent assistant to summarize the timeline, group similar evidence, and reduce noise.

• Built-in chain of custody management with a tamper-proof audit log.

• Automatic support for Android/iOS devices, camera cards, and memory cards.

• Full support for Arabic and multiple time zones, which most competing tools lack.

• A free tier to start that allows actual use, not a trial version.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Import a disk, device, or memory card image and link it to a case.

2. Automatic extraction of files, images, and video with full indexing.

3. Unified search by keywords, tags, extensions, and dates.

4. An interactive timeline that can be filtered and sorted.

5. Detection and recovery of deleted files.

6. Reading metadata (EXIF) and geographic information.

7. Chain of custody management with a timestamped audit log.

8. Export a report in PDF/HTML format in multiple languages.

4) Pricing and profit model

• Free to start: one seat, one device, unlimited local cases, and the eight core features.

• Pro: about 29 USD per month per seat — multi-device analysis, collaboration, advanced reports.

• Team/Business: about 79 USD per seat per month — self-hosted deployment, SSO, roles and permissions.

• Enterprise/Forensics: custom pricing — integrations, SLA support, training.

• Permanently free for students, universities, and non-profit organizations to build early adoption.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• An open-core plugin model on GitHub to attract contributors.

• Free educational content: YouTube channels, simplified DFIR courses, and technical blogs.

• Partnerships with universities and security labs to use it in courses.

• Participation in conferences, hackathons, and open-tool communities.

• A Discord/Forum community and a student ambassador program to produce guides and translations.

• Presence in specialist forums, Reddit, and Stack Exchange with a technical answer, not an advertisement.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: privacy protection and GDPR compliance, dual-use (legitimate/malicious) liability, limits of evidence admissibility in courts, and the licensing terms of the components used.

• Technical: ensuring the original source is not modified, the accuracy of extracting various disk formats, the security of the local database and attack surface, performance on huge cases, and maintaining the integrity of the chain of custody across backups.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Athar

Marketing tagline: Recover digital evidence and analyze what happened in minutes, not in weeks.
