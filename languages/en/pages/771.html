SAJIL — Complete Project Plan

Record your screen with one tap and get a polished, share-ready video in seconds.

Plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, business model, distribution, risks. Under construction, not launched.

1) Target Audience

• Arabic creators: post 3-6 videos weekly on YouTube and TikTok; want polished screen clips with no separate editor or monthly fee.

• Developers/designers: record 5-15 minutes to document a bug or demo a UI prototype; want auto-zoom on every click.

• Teachers/trainers: give 30-60 minute slide-and-audio lectures; need auto chapters and captions to jump back in a second.

• Support/product teams: make 10-20 short tutorials monthly; want one template adding title/logo and hiding private data before publishing.

• Mid-range users (8GB RAM, quad-core CPU): want a light program with no complex settings or cloud permissions.

2) Differentiators (Why Our Product)

• Intent-aware zoom: detects clicks, adds smooth 130-160% zoom with a 300 ms transition, resets after 1.2 seconds; looks pro-edited.

• Privacy by design: blurs phone numbers, emails and notifications locally before saving; no upload unless the user shares a link.

• Editor in the same window: trim, remove silence, merge clips, add chapters on screen changes, with instant preview.

• Auto captions/chapters: synchronized Arabic/English text from small local models, exported as editable SRT; target error under 8% on clear speech.

• Flexible export: MP4, WebM, GIF up to 12 seconds, plus a "sticker image" titled from the first spoken sentence, ready for social posting.

• Light and fast: install under 120MB, memory under 350MB at 1080p/60, ready to record 3 seconds after launch.

3) First Version Scope (6 Tasks)

1. Native Windows engine: screen, system audio and microphone at 1080p/60 with real-time compression.

2. Region/window/full-screen selector, timer, pause and stop.

3. Auto click-zoom and cursor highlight, disableable in settings.

4. Simple timeline editor: trim, split, remove silence, add SRT captions.

5. Local smart blurring of sensitive data and encrypted on-device storage.

6. MP4/GIF export, installer with auto-updates, opt-in anonymous usage measurement.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free forever: 5 minutes, 720p export, small watermark; enough to experience the value before paying.

• Pro $4.99/month or $39/year: unlimited time, 4K, no watermark, smart blurring, advanced zoom.

• Team $9/user/month: 20GB shared space, unified logo templates, view stats for internal clips.

• Pay-per-use: remove the watermark after export for $0.99 once; converts 3-5% of free users with low friction.

• 14-day trial, no credit card, one-click cancellation; 30% student/teacher discount to lift retention.

• Ancillary revenue: template and sound-effect packs at $2.99; referral program paying 20% for 12 months.

5) Global Distribution Plan with Free Channels

• Phased launch: Product Hunt and Hacker News, then Reddit within 3 weeks; 90-second video focused on click-zoom.

• Free technical content: weekly Dev.to and Hashnode articles in English/Arabic on building the engine, with a download link at the end.

• Short clips: 30 videos of 45-60 seconds, one solution per problem (notifications, zoom, captions), 3 per week.

• Education communities: partner with 5 Arabic training centers and 3 developer groups to distribute it as an approved free workshop tool.

• Sharing campaign: weekly hashtag for "best screen clip" posts, product in each video's final message.

• Localization: translate site and UI into 6 languages via volunteers, plus local-currency pricing per market to lift conversion 15-25%.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Encoding licenses: H.264 and HEVC patents; use open-source WebM/AV1 or document FFmpeg compliance before launch.

• Branding: check name and domain in 4 international registries 6 weeks before launch; register trademark in Arab and EU markets.

• Data protection: GDPR via local processing; privacy policy stating audio is not saved or sent without explicit consent.

• OS changes: Windows and macOS updates may break capture; compatibility layer with two alternative interfaces, test each release within 48 hours.

• Performance on weak devices: auto-drop to 720p/30 below 24 fps, with a gentle notice instead of freezing.

• Dialect accuracy: small local model may err with dialects; add manual correction and an optional, clearly labeled cloud option.

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Line

Proposed name: SAJIL

Marketing line: Record your screen with one tap and get a polished, share-ready video in seconds.
