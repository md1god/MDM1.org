Nahj — Full project plan

Your fleet's smartest routes, start free and launch in minutes.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the go-to-market plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: an original cloud fleet routing solution

1) Target audience

• Delivery and food companies: small and medium fleets (5–200 vehicles) without an operations research team.

• E-commerce merchants: managing "last mile" delivery in-house.

• Field service providers: maintenance, meter reading, home care.

• Developers and data teams: via Python SDK and REST interface for fast integration.

• Emerging markets: where cost and intermittent connectivity are decisive factors.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Start free immediately: a ready cloud layer with no installation or hardware setup.

• Bilingual first: full Arabic/RTL support in interfaces and reports.

• Multiple practical constraints: time windows, capacities, driver skills, rest periods, customer priority.

• Explanatory transparency: the system explains the reason for every route and rerouting decision.

• Offline operation: a driver app that works offline and syncs later.

• Performance: near-optimal solutions in seconds for medium fleets.

• Practical integration: Excel and CSV import/export and open-source maps to reduce data cost.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Upload orders and addresses via CSV/Excel or API.

2. A basic routing engine with time-window and capacity constraints.

3. An interactive map to review and manually adjust routes.

4. Initial calculations for cost, distance, and expected time.

5. A simplified REST interface + Python SDK for integration.

6. A web driver app with task instructions and offline mode.

7. Export routes to printable and navigable files.

8. A basic dashboard (delivery time, time-window compliance, total distance).

4) Pricing and profit model

• Free to start: up to two vehicles and 50 tasks per day, no bank card.

• Pro tier (monthly/per vehicle): practically unlimited vehicles, advanced constraints, priority support.

• Enterprise tier: annual contract, private hosting, SSO, success manager.

• Additional revenue: usage fees per thousand solutions via API, and paid integration and setup services.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Technical content: tutorials, comparisons, and SEO articles in Arabic and English.

• Communities: Reddit, Discord, Stack Overflow, and logistics forums.

• PyPI + GitHub: publish the client library for free to attract developers.

• University ambassadors: graduation projects and hackathons in operations research.

• Integration partnerships: with store platforms and small fleet management systems.

• Referral program: one free month for bringing a new customer.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: location data privacy (GDPR and the like), map data terms of use, and ownership of solution results.

• Legal: avoid copying the code or logos of any existing product; the new code is entirely ours.

• Technical: solution quality versus speed as the fleet grows, and the diversity of real-world constraints.

• Technical: geographic data accuracy and changing congestion, and the reliability of offline mode and synchronization.

• Technical: cost scalability of computation as the number of users grows.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Nahj

Tagline: Your fleet's smartest routes, start free and launch in minutes.
