Awn — Complete Project Plan

Turn an insurance denial into a clear, documented appeal in minutes.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Patients: those who received a denial for a treatment request or prior authorization from an insurance company.

• Doctors and medical staff: in small and medium clinics that need quick help writing an appeal.

• Patient support organizations: that help others face insurance denials without high cost.

2) Points of Distinction (Why choose our product)

• Free to start: focuses on delivering core value for free without the need to pay immediately.

• Simple with clear steps: designed for a patient or a non-specialist employee to use in minutes.

• Shows sources: cites the plan or policy language it relied on in the letter to increase credibility.

• Serves everyone: supports multiple languages with a focus on simplifying medical terminology.

• Transparent: shows the user what must be clarified or attached before sending the appeal.

3) Scope of the First Version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Simple input form: allows entering only the denial code and request details.

2. Denial code explanations: a database explaining the most common denial codes in simplified language.

3. Letter generator: creates an organized, editable appeal letter.

4. Policy quotes: displays relevant excerpts from the plan policy with their source.

5. Ready-made templates: templates customized for patients and clinics.

6. Letter export: the ability to save it as PDF or copy it.

7. Practical tips: a quick list of next steps after writing the letter.

8. Privacy check: a mode for handling data with minimal storage unless necessary.

4) Pricing and Profit Model

• Completely free: all features of the first version are available for free.

• Freemium model: a symbolic subscription for clinics that need a large number of users or reports.

• No ads: to maintain a clean and focused experience.

• Later growth: paid features can be added without compromising the free core version.

5) Global Go-to-Market Plan with Free Channels

• SEO optimization: creating content that answers questions like "How do I appeal my insurance denial?".

• Patient support organizations: collaborating with non-profit associations to direct patients to the platform.

• Online communities: participating in patient help forums while offering useful value.

• Educational content: publishing simplified guides in different languages.

• Word of mouth: relying on the product's value for automatic growth.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal:

• Privacy: the need to comply with data protection laws (such as HIPAA where applicable) and not collect sensitive data without justification.

• Content liability: it must be clear that the platform helps with writing, and is not legal or medical advice.

• Technical:

• Accuracy of quotes: the risk of displaying inaccurate quotes, so their sources must be verified.

• Policy coverage: the difficulty of covering all insurance plans globally, so we start with a limited scope.

• Security: protecting any data entered by minimizing its storage and encrypting it.

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Tagline

Proposed name: Awn

Marketing tagline: Turn an insurance denial into a clear, documented appeal in minutes.
