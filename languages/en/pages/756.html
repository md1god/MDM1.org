Mirsad — Complete Project Plan

Know what's missing from your inventory before a customer does.

Plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout, risks. Under development; not launched.

New product plan: purchase order and replenishment planner for small Shopify stores

1) Target audience

1. Owners of small Shopify stores (10 employees or fewer) managing inventory in Excel, losing weekly hours on quantities and reorder points.

2. Emerging stores in the Gulf, Egypt, and North Africa needing accurate landed cost for imports (shipping, customs, fees), absent from default tools.

3. Procurement managers with partial suppliers: a supplier ships only 60% of an order, so receipts need installments and a clear remaining quantity.

4. Distributors and e-commerce professionals managing several stores who want one dashboard for all inventory and reorder alerts.

5. Freelance consultants seeking a free tool for clients without costly monthly licenses.

2) Why our product

1. Free to start: one store, up to 500 SKUs, no credit card, so stores can try before paying.

2. Real partial receiving: purchase orders received in installments, remaining quantity auto-updated, timeline log per receipt and shipment number.

3. Landed cost: shipping and customs allocated proportionally across order items for accurate cost of goods and margins.

4. Supplier file rebuild: import suppliers, prices, lead times from CSV or store data in minutes.

5. Smart reorder points: auto-calculated from daily sales, lead time, service level, with "Reorder Now" list, suggested quantity, email alert.

6. Bilingual UI (Arabic and English) with short step-by-step docs, as part of the audience is not confident in English.

3) First-version scope (max 8 tasks)

1. Shopify integration via OAuth: sync products and inventory levels every 6 hours.

2. Supplier management: profile per supplier with prices, lead time, currency, contacts.

3. Purchase order creation: pick items, quantities, cost preview, PDF print, Draft link sharing.

4. Partial receiving: log quantities, date, shipment number; update remaining quantity.

5. Landed cost: add external expenses (shipping/customs) and distribute across order items.

6. Reorder points and alerts: daily priority list with suggested order quantity.

7. Stocktake: mobile session with variance recording and auditable adjustment log.

8. Overview dashboard: inventory value, out-of-stock items, open orders, CSV export.

4) Pricing and revenue model

1. Free: one store, 500 SKUs, 30 purchase orders/month, one user, knowledge-base support.

2. Pro: $19/month — unlimited orders, two users, advanced alerts, full landed cost, advanced export.

3. Agencies: $49/month — up to 5 stores, team permissions, unified report for all stores.

4. Optional paid add-ons: demand forecasts, WhatsApp alerts at $5/month each.

5. Permanent free access for support programs and small-shop education initiatives to build reputation and reach.

6. Year-one target: 1,000 free stores, 150 paid subscribers ≈ $3,000 monthly recurring revenue.

5) Global rollout via free channels

1. SEO content in Arabic and English: comparison articles and free purchase order templates driving targeted traffic.

2. Communities: practical tips in the Shopify forum, Discord, Reddit, Twitter/X, no direct ads; mention the tool only at the end of the answer.

3. Short videos: TikTok and YouTube Shorts tutorials on reordering and landed cost (one problem + solution in 45 seconds).

4. Partnerships: 20% commission affiliate plan for e-commerce micro-influencers.

5. Weekly newsletter: three inventory tips + one case study; target 2,000 subscribers in 6 months.

6. Launch on free software directories (Product Hunt, alternative-tool directories) for the first user wave.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

1. Legal: no trademarks of major platforms in the name or ads; use only official public APIs.

2. Platform policies: follow app store terms and review policy before publishing to avoid suspension.

3. Data privacy: encryption in transit and at rest, clear privacy policy, full deletion on request.

4. Technical: API changes may break sync; use automated tests, failure alerts, manual-export fallback.

5. Poor CSV files: preview before saving; check duplicates, missing codes, differing currencies.

6. Competition: free tools may copy features; respond with fast releases, 24-hour support, monthly updates.

7) Suggested name and tagline

Suggested name: Mirsad

Tagline: Know what's missing from your inventory before a customer does.
