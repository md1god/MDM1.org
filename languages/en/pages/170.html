Mirshid — Full Project Plan

Turn your documents into a ready explainer video in minutes with no editing experience.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Development teams: they need to explain new releases quickly without complex video setup.

• Customer success teams: they use it to clarify how to use the product.

• Teachers and content creators: they want to turn written content into video.

• Sales and marketing teams: they need short explainer videos, fast.

2) Differentiators (Why choose our product)

• Focus on quality with simplicity: a single interface, where uploading a file or pasting text is enough to get started.

• Cost reduction: it relies on open-source models as much as possible to lower the video cost per user.

• Speed: it generates the video in minutes, not hours.

• Multilingual support: automatic caption and voice translation generation to facilitate global reach.

• Suitable for mobile and web: it produces clips in formats that fit different platforms.

• Freedom to start: anyone can use it for free without paying an initial subscription.

3) Scope of the First Version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Content upload: support uploading text, a PDF file, or a short screen recording.

2. Content analysis: automatically split content into ordered, logical segments.

3. Script generation: convert each segment into text that reads naturally.

4. Voice generation: use natural synthetic voices for narration.

5. Video generation: combine segments with audio to create a ready video.

6. Simple editing: the ability to edit the text or change only the voice.

7. Video export: export it in appropriate quality and in more than one format.

8. Free limit system: enforce a free usage limit per user.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

· Plan · Features · Price ·

· Free · A set monthly video limit, with basic quality and a light watermark. · $0 ·

· Pro · Higher limit, watermark removal, higher quality, and priority support. · $25 / month ·

· Enterprise · Custom limit, team workspaces and roles. · On request ·

Revenue model: based on a freemium model, where users pay when they exceed the free limit.

5) Global Distribution Plan via Free Channels

• Outreach: posting short explainer examples on platforms such as Reddit, X, and LinkedIn with real solutions to problems.

• Search engines: optimizing product pages for keywords such as "converting a document into an explainer video" to increase free traffic.

• Communities: participating in global developer communities with useful content, without ads.

• Educational content: creating short free lessons on YouTube Shorts to showcase the capabilities.

• Smart tool lists: listing the product in free AI tool directories.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

· Type · Potential Risks · Mitigation Method ·

· Legal · Rights to uploaded content, rights to synthetic voices, or model licenses. · Using properly licensed voices, user consent for content, and not storing sensitive documents. ·

· Technical · The high cost of processing, slowness, or poor video quality. · Using open-source models when possible, creating processing queues, and enabling an economy mode in the free plan. ·

· Other · Misuse or generation of unwanted content. · Setting controls for uploaded content and basic filters before processing. ·

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Tagline

Proposed name: Mirshid

Marketing tagline: Turn your documents into a ready explainer video in minutes with no editing experience.
