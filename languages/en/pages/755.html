لَقْطة — Complete Project Plan

From your spoken words to a clip that stops the scroll in seconds.

Complete plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, distribution, and risks. Under construction; not launched yet.

1) Target audience

• YouTube and podcast creators: record 30–90 minutes weekly, publish one clip; goal: 3–5 clips/episode with no editing program.

• Entrepreneurs and small brands: bakery or clinic owners with under $200/month marketing, turning a customer testimonial into a vertical clip in under 10 minutes.

• Influencers in emerging markets: mobile-first, speaking Arabic, Spanish, or Portuguese; want a local interface and exports ready for Reels and TikTok.

• Marketing teams of 2–5: managing 3–8 accounts, needing shared templates for one identity regardless of who edits.

• Remote teachers and trainers: record a one-hour lecture, extract the best 5 questions and answers for absent students, with a caption per clip.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Start from the transcript, not the timeline: search «the biggest marketing mistake» and the playhead jumps to that second; 5 times faster than dragging.

• Transparent 0–100 engagement score per clip from speech rate, tone changes, and questions, with the reason and an option to reject the suggestion.

• Auto-cropping follows the speaker's face in a 9:16 frame, with one button for manual cropping when two people appear; no reshooting.

• Smart captions in Modern Standard Arabic, colloquial Arabic, and English: 12 font templates, animated word highlighting, 20-caption bulk fix via shortcut.

• Free start, no credit card: 3 exports/month at 720p; upgrade at the limit, not via a surprise-ending trial.

3) First-version scope (6 tasks)

1. Import/transcription: file up to 2 hours and 2 GB, or direct link; word-level timestamps in Arabic and English.

2. Moments engine: text search + engagement score + top-10 clips, reorderable or dismissible.

3. Vertical editor: draggable in/out points, split/merge, 9:16 crop with face tracking and saved manual adjustment.

4. Captions: auto-generation, Arabic templates, animated words, bulk correction, text saved as SRT.

5. Audio cleanup: remove silence over 0.4 seconds and filler words, with preview and per-clip on/off button.

6. Export: MP4 1080×1920, presets for TikTok, Reels, Shorts, render queue, final preview before saving.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: 3 exports/month, 720p, small watermark, processing within 10 minutes; enough for a real trial.

• Pro at $12/month or $96/year: 30 exports, 1080p, no watermark, priority queue, 20 extra templates.

• Studio at $29/month: 5 seats, shared template library, approval before publishing, API for team tools.

• One-time $2/hour of processing, plus font and effect packs for $5–15 and a 20% referral commission keeping free as the entry gate.

5) Global distribution plan using free channels

• SEO: 15 articles/videos on «podcasts to Shorts» and «auto silence removal», plus a free 30-second cutter with no sign-up; goal: 10,000 monthly visitors in 6 months.

• Communities: 3 useful weekly contributions in r/podcasting, r/YouTube, Discord, and Facebook groups, with examples and ready templates instead of promo links.

• 20 creator collaborations (10K–100K followers): one-year Pro and a clip in the template gallery for an honest review; 8% conversion target.

• Official account: 5 «before/after» clips weekly, showing raw text to final clip in under 15 seconds.

• Localization: Arabic, English, Spanish, Portuguese interfaces; community translation for 3 months of Pro; a separate landing page per language.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: content belongs to the user; acknowledgment on upload, deletion within 24 hours of a complaint, and protected-music checks before export.

• Privacy: first-pass processing in-browser via ffmpeg.wasm for the free trial, server encryption and deletion after 24 hours, and a no-training pledge for clips.

• Transcription accuracy: target error rate below 8% with Arabic, dialects, and noise; manual correction and ready SRT upload when needed.

• Face tracking may fail with multiple speakers or poor lighting; detect face count, notify gently, and save the manual crop as a reusable template.

• Cost and time: free cap of 2 hours/day per user, plus WebGPU and low-res preview before final render, wait under 10 minutes.

7) Suggested name and tagline

Suggested name: لَقْطة

Tagline: From your spoken words to a clip that stops the scroll in seconds.
