Khutwa — Complete Project Plan

From a big goal to one step today.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, launch plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target audience

• Freelancers and small business owners who manage themselves without project management experience.

• Students, researchers, and people completing long-term tasks.

• Small teams of 2 to 10 people that need simple organization without complexity.

• People with neurodivergence, especially attention, reading, and visual sensitivity difficulties.

• Nonprofits and educational teams with limited budgets.

• The Arab market first, then English-speaking global markets.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Energy-based planning, not time-based: we choose tasks according to available energy, not empty hours.

• Daily focus limit: a maximum of three core tasks to reduce distraction and exhaustion.

• Calm interface: low-saturation colors, limited motion, no pressure notifications or exaggerated motivation.

• Native accessibility: WCAG 2.2 AA compliance, dyslexia-friendly font, adjustable contrast, screen reader support.

• Arabic first: full RTL design, not an afterthought translation.

• Goal breakdown: turn a big goal into small actionable steps without complex conversation.

• Free to start: no ads, no data selling, and full data export.

• Speed and reliability: works smoothly on mobile, with basic offline support.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Quick setup: enter one goal and get the first three steps suggested.

2. “Today” screen: three core tasks with an energy tag: low, medium, high.

3. Simplified board with only three states: Later, Now, Done.

4. Quick capture inbox for text and voice to unload ideas.

5. Gentle customizable reminders with full notification muting.

6. Accessibility mode: line spacing, dyslexia font, reduced motion, high contrast.

7. Ready-made goal templates: study, product launch, personal routine, team project.

8. Free account, basic link sharing, and data export in an open format.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free forever: unlimited personal projects, the three tasks, and accessibility mode. No ads.

• Pro: about $4 per month or $36 per year: unlimited goal breakdown, advanced templates, integrations, smart reminders.

• Teams: about $8 per user per month: shared spaces, simple roles, completion reports.

• Supporter pack: optional one-time payment for a supporter badge and experimental features.

• Discounts: 50% for nonprofit and education, and free for early-stage teams for a limited time.

• Principles: transparent pricing, instant cancellation, no data lock-in, and no selling personal information.

5) Global launch plan with free channels

• Short videos showing “from goal to three steps” in under a minute.

• Authentic participation in neurodivergence and productivity communities, respecting each community’s rules.

• Arabic and English SEO content about task organization and reducing burnout.

• A free template gallery that can be copied and shared to attract organic visits.

• Free listings in product directories, comparison platforms, and integration marketplaces.

• Free integrations with calendar, email, and chat tools to generate daily use.

• An ambassador program for students, coaches, and nonprofits in exchange for early access.

• Partnerships with organization coaches, universities, and community centers.

• Free collaboration invites that create spread within teams without paid ads.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Comply with GDPR and CCPA on privacy, consent, and data deletion.

• Avoid any medical or diagnostic claims; the product is an organization tool, not treatment.

• Meet European accessibility requirements and WCAG before commercial expansion.

• Verify the trademark and name in target markets.

• Write the code from scratch and avoid any borrowed licenses or assets.

• Regulate AI use, content rights, and transparency.

Technical:

• Scope creep and adding features that complicate the user experience.

• Sync and offline conflicts across multiple devices.

• AI cost and consumption of the free quota.

• Accessibility regressions with every interface update.

• Account security and link sharing.

• Dependence on external APIs that may change or stop.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Khutwa

Tagline: From a big goal to one step today.
