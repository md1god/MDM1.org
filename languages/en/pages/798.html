مُقاطِع — Complete Project Plan

Know what's being decided near you before it's decided.

A complete plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, growth, risks. Under construction, not launched.

1) Target audience

• Residents near proposed sites: families wanting a 3-minute answer: will a data center be 2 km from their child's school within 12 months? Not 200 pages of minutes—one map, one clear alert.

• Municipal planning officers: 5–20 people tracking 8–15 files at once; need an official link citable in a public session in 2 minutes, not 6-site government searches.

• Journalists and neighborhood associations: 2–4 reports/month; need a neutral reference—decision no., session date, minutes link—keeping news separate from opinion.

• Investors and utility engineers: decide 12–24 months before building; ask: can the grid carry 40 MW more, or is the substation at 92% capacity? Researchers want 50 districts in one CSV file.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Evidence before opinion: each map point links to 2+ sources (agenda + minutes/decision) with date and page; target 90% coverage in v1.

• Alerts without noise: max 2/week, only on real change—session date, vote result, capacity change; no cosmetic updates.

• A map that explains the constraint: 4 toggleable layers—electricity (MW), water withdrawal (million gallons/day), land classification, 1 km from schools; a district whose substation is at 92% capacity glows a warning color.

• Instant-use export: one button gives CSV, JSON, and a permalink preserving file state and date, attachable to a report or memo with no re-search.

• No-account start: viewing and RSS alerts work instantly; sign-up only to save lists; no ads or user-data sales.

3) First-version scope (max. 8 tasks)

• Foundation: React UI with open-source Leaflet map, PostgreSQL, cron every 6 hours; free hosting at first.

• Tasks 1–2: collector reads 20 local sources, extracts 6 fields; matcher links decisions to districts and coordinates within 500 m.

• Tasks 3–4: map with 4 layers and district-name search; RSS/email alerts for only 3 change types.

• Tasks 5–6: watchlists capped at 3 districts for free users; CSV, JSON, permalink per record.

• Tasks 7–8: "How we know" page with source, timestamp, confidence score; full Arabic with RTL plus English and French translations.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free forever: map, search, RSS alerts, 3 watchlists, 100 pages/month export, no credit card; entry point for all.

• Individual (ميزان+): $7/month or $60/year for unlimited lists, PDF export, 1,000 API calls/day, alerts in 15 min instead of 6 hours.

• Teams and associations: $49/month for 10 users, shared dashboard, ready monthly report; 20% off nonprofits and planning colleges.

• Company data: API package $199–$499/month with service-level agreement and 3 years of history; no personal data sold.

• Financial target: 1,200 individuals ≈ $100k/year, 40 teams ≈ $23k, 10 API clients ≈ $36k; ≈ $160k recurring in year one.

5) Global distribution plan using free channels

• Open content: 500 static district pages in Arabic, English, French, Spanish, targeting district name + "near you"; free search indexing.

• Public repository: data collector open source on GitHub, four-language README; volunteers fix it and add district sources, credited by name.

• Zero-budget partnerships: 20 local newspapers and 5 planning colleges get a weekly quotable RSS alert, in exchange for a source mention.

• Digital communities: 5-line weekly summary in 30 specialized Facebook, Reddit, and Discord groups, linking the map, not sign-up.

• Events: monthly Zoom workshop, "Read meeting minutes in 10 minutes", recorded on YouTube; attendees become free users.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal — sources: only public minutes and agendas; robots.txt at 1 request/2 seconds; no paid-portal copying or republishing protected text.

• Legal — translation and advice: mark translations as machine-generated; state tool is information only, not legal or investment advice; official decision governs.

• Legal — privacy: encrypt alert links, delete user data in 30 days on request, no ad tracking or third-party sharing.

• Technical — source quality: 15% of files scanned; OCR, then human-review a 10% sample; show a confidence score.

• Technical — conflicts and stability: log versions on cancellation/amendment; cache 24 hours, 3 retries, so stale data never looks new.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: مُقاطِع

Tagline: Know what's being decided near you before it's decided.
