Revocly — Complete Project Plan

Clear withdrawal button, instant confirmation, and an audit-ready evidence log.

Plan for a new digital project: concept, audience, product, revenue, go-to-market, risks. Under construction, not launched.

1) Target Audience

• EU SMB consumer stores, especially Shopify/WooCommerce with 10–500 monthly orders, no legal team.

• DTC brands, digital/subscription stores, and sellers with withdrawal exceptions.

• Non-EU merchants targeting EU consumers without an EU entity.

• Design agencies/legal consultants as sales/support; multi-store access.

• Buyer: owner, operations/compliance lead—not a large tech team.

• First: Germany, France, Netherlands, Spain, Italy, Poland, Sweden, then rest of EU/EEA.

• Exclude regulated financial services initially; focus on goods, services, digital products.

2) Differentiators

• Withdrawal mirrors purchase: persistent button, two-step confirmation, under a minute, no forced login/retention offers/extra steps.

• Full lifecycle: order verification, full/partial withdrawal, deadlines, confirmation, refund tracking.

• Audit-ready log: content, date, time, time zone, verification hash, immutable log, structured export.

• Rules engine: updates rules, deadlines, national exceptions without reinstall; handles DST/business days.

• Continuous monitoring: periodic button/information checks, change alerts.

• Multilingual/embeddable: widget, hosted page, email/PDF templates, branding.

• Accessibility/privacy: WCAG 2.2 AA, data minimization, EU hosting.

• Pricing: free start, flat subscription, no sales commission; suits stores/agencies.

3) MVP Scope (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. Store/regulatory setup: domain, countries, languages, product types, exceptions, deadline start.

2. Button/UI: clear button/link, hosted/embeddable page, data review, final confirmation; mobile/WCAG.

3. Order verification/partial withdrawal: email or one-time code, guest checkout, product choice, duplicate prevention.

4. Deadline engine: 14 days by product type and delivery/time, missing-info extensions, national business days.

5. Confirmation/evidence: email/PDF with content, date, time; verification hash, immutable log, CSV/PDF export.

6. Operations dashboard: statuses, notes, deadline alerts, return/refund tasks, roles, search/filter.

7. Integrations: Shopify, WooCommerce, REST API, Webhooks, generic button; test environment, short guides.

8. Audit/monitoring: free scanner for missing button/information, compliance report, EU-language templates.

4) Pricing and Revenue

• Free forever, no card: one store, unlimited withdrawals, basic form, email, 30-day log, one language, “Powered by” badge.

• Growth — €19/month: no badge, custom domain, all EU languages, email customization, 12-month log, CSV/PDF export, partial withdrawal.

• Pro — €49/month: 3 stores, monitoring/alerts, API, advanced permissions, compliance reports, 24-month log.

• Agency — €149/month: up to 20 stores, white label, client portal, priority support, client reports.

• Assisted setup — €199 one-time: installation/settings review, optional.

• Prices exclude VAT; no per-withdrawal fee; annual discount equals about two months.

5) Go-to-Market with Free Channels

• Free tools: compliance scanner, button generator, deadline calculator, templates; language pages with hreflang.

• App stores: free listings on Shopify, WooCommerce, PrestaShop, BigCommerce; short guide, early reviews.

• Growth loop: “Powered by” badge, referral link, agency recurring-commission rewards.

• Agency/consultant partnerships: partner portal, sales materials, monthly button workshop.

• Content: short guides, comparisons, YouTube/LinkedIn videos, legal-update newsletter.

• Communities: useful participation in e-commerce forums, founder groups, Reddit, Indie Hackers; no spam.

• Launch: Product Hunt, BetaList, Hacker News, European startup sites; local chamber/e-commerce campaigns.

• Optimization: language landing pages, social proof, interactive demo; email only on save.

6) Risks (Legal/Technical)

Legal:

• Implementation/documentation tool, not legal advice; review with local counsel in key markets.

• Rules vary by member state; interpretations/rulings change, requiring rule/release updates.

• Data protection: processing agreement, minimization, retention, subprocessors, EU hosting.

• EU accessibility rules: WCAG gaps may create legal/reputational risk.

• Avoid dark patterns: no forced registration, retention offers, confirmation delays; design changes need compliance review.

• Email as permanent medium: delivery/blocking may weaken evidence; offer signed-copy download.
