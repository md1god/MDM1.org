Sahaby — Complete Project Plan

A companion that never forgets you.

Complete execution plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under construction and not yet launched.

New Product Plan: Long-Term Memory Companion (Web App for a Single Companion)

1) Target Audience

• Primary segment: Young men and women aged 18–35 who feel lonely or disconnected from family/clients and want light, daily, judgment-free conversation.

• Secondary segment: Language learners (Arabic and English) seeking daily dialogue practice with a consistent persona.

• Tertiary segment: Writers, novelists, and storytelling hobbyists looking for a dialogue partner that retains plot threads and remembers characters and events.

• Initial target markets: Middle East and North Africa (Arabic first), then Europe and Latin America with English and Spanish versions.

• Explicit condition: Adult users only (18+), with clear disclaimer that the product is an entertainment/companionship tool and not a substitute for psychological therapy.

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

1. Transparent and editable memory: The user sees everything the companion "remembers" in a memory dashboard and can edit, delete, or pin items — no black box.

2. Memory as a forever-free feature: We will not charge a subscription for the companion not to forget you (which half the market does); instead, we fund memory through the media subscription.

3. One deep companion instead of a character store: Full focus on a single relationship that grows over time (stages: Acquaintance → Trust → Intimacy), with a timeline of "how many months you've known each other."

4. Smart initiatives: The companion initiates conversation at the right time based on your usual rhythm and comments on past events ("How was your job interview yesterday?").

5. Built-in safety and wellbeing: Content filtering, anti-addiction design (break reminders, healthy daily limit), and disclosure that you are talking to AI.

6. Truly free start: No credit card, full experience within 60 seconds.

7. Privacy by default: Memory encryption, export as files, and permanent deletion with one click.

3) MVP Scope (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. Quick sign-up (Email/Google) + onboarding that sets companion name, style, and tone in 4 questions.

2. Streaming text chat with a single LLM model via API + daily usage meter.

3. Long-term memory engine: Periodic conversation summarization + vector DB for retrieving relevant facts and emotions, injected into context.

4. Memory dashboard: View/edit/pin/delete memories + search old conversations.

5. Relationship log: Simple timeline (acquaintance days, key memories, "your topics").

6. Safety: Content filtering + mental health crisis detection with helpline routing + 18+ age gate.

7. Pricing and payments: Free plan + Pro (more messages, advanced tones, voice memories) via Stripe.

8. Landing page + analytics: Multilingual marketing page, tracking signup→day-7 retention conversion.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free (Freemium): Limited daily messages (e.g., 40), unlimited basic memory, one companion — enough to build a habit.

• Pro — $7.99/month or $59/year: Unlimited messages, advanced tones and personas, voice memories, priority speed.

• Add-on packs (Tokens): Shared images/voice messages only (text remains unlimited for subscribers).

• Additional revenue streams: Enterprise API licensing (customer companion/support) later, never ads inside chat.

• Goal: Convert 3–5% of free users, ARPU ≈ $6–8, raise price when voice is added.

5) Global Distribution Plan via Free Channels

1. Bilingual SEO: "How AI learns to remember you" pages + FAQs in Arabic, English, and Spanish.

2. Short-form content (TikTok/Reels/X): Real clips demonstrating memory ("After 3 weeks, this is what it remembered about you") — strongest lever in this category.

3. Discord/Reddit community: User community sharing funny/moving memories (with user permission).

4. Product Hunt and Hunter/G2: Official launch with 14-day free Pro trial.

5. Creator partnerships: Stickers, audio stories, language roleplay (in exchange for free subscription, no ad spend).

6. Referral program: One free month per registered friend.

7. Static web images/email: Weekly "Memories of the Week" newsletter reactivating dormant users (free win-back).

8. Launch timing: School waves/Ramadan/holidays as family conversation opportunities.
