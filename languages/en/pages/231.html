PaceVault — Full Project Plan

Your run stays saved. Your privacy stays yours.

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, launch plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan for a new tracker for outdoor sports activities

1) Target Audience

• Amateur runners and walkers who log their runs via GPS (GARMIN / Strava / phone app).

• Cyclists and hikers who have GPX files from their devices.

• Families and friends who want to share their sports activity and follow each other privately, without huge social platforms.

• Beginner coaches who want to track progress with numbers (distance, pace, elevation, weekly frequency) without complexity.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Privacy first: private accounts and sharing limited to selected people, with data hosted transparently.

• Simple smart analysis: a smart read of the run (effort, pace, stress zones) instead of raw tables.

• Broad file support: import GPX/FIT/TCX and sync with common devices.

• Family/team dashboard: shared weekly comparison and friendly challenges.

• Truly free tier: permanently free basic use with no early paywall.

• Fast on mobile: a lightweight interface that works well on weak networks.

3) First Version Scope (8 tasks maximum)

1. User registration and small family/team accounts.

2. Upload GPX files and view them on a map with distance, time, and speed.

3. Bulk import from common tracking services.

4. Personal progress page: week/month, day streak, distance trends.

5. Limited sharing: invite a person and see only their activity.

6. Responsive web app (PWA) installable on mobile.

7. Data export in open formats (GPX/CSV).

8. Gentle reminder notifications and a weekly email report.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free: individual account + almost unlimited uploads + basic reports.

• Pro — $5/month or $40/year: advanced analytics, heatmaps, training plans, multi-sport comparisons.

• Family — $9/month: up to 6 members.

• Sponsor (B2B) — club/company sponsors: private team dashboards at an annual subscription price.

• Additional revenue: custom developments for coaches.

5) Global Launch Plan Using Free Channels

1. SEO and content guide: articles like "How to read a GPX file" and "5K couch-to-run plan" in English and other languages.

2. Sports communities: forums, subreddits, Facebook and Telegram groups for runners (useful posting, not overt ads).

3. YouTube/Shorts: short tutorials on importing a run and reading the analytics.

4. Creator partnerships: free trials with small coaches in exchange for an honest mention.

5. Localized landing pages: Arabic, Spanish, French, Hindi — gradual geographic targeting.

6. Referral program: one free Pro month for every friend who signs up.

7. Inclusion in sports tool directories and comparative app reviews.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal: personal data protection (GDPR/local laws), GPS location privacy that can reveal home — start/end points should be hidden by default, and hosting terms must be clear.

• Legal: respect map data licenses and third-party service trademarks when importing.

• Technical: variable GPS data quality and error handling (speed spikes).

• Technical: server load when uploading large files and high-resolution maps.

• Business: strong competition and user retention — addressed by focusing the product on privacy and simplicity.

7) Proposed Name and Tagline

Proposed name: PaceVault

Tagline: Your run stays saved. Your privacy stays yours.
