Pixora Studio — Full Project Plan

Create your world with a single pixel and share it in any game or anywhere.

Full execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, launch plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: animated pixel art editor

1) Target audience

• Independent game developers (indie game devs) who need character and enemy graphics in animated Sprite Sheet format.

• Professional and hobbyist Pixel Art artists in the itch.io, DeviantArt, and X communities.

• Short motion designers for GIFs and stickers for messaging and social media.

• Teachers and students in digital drawing and game design courses, an audience that always looks for free and open-source tools.

2) Differentiators

• Arabic-first interface with full right-to-left support and Arabic labeling tools.

• Built-in programmatic drawing engine (Scripting API in JavaScript) for repetition and automation, with ready-made examples to download.

• Live preview in the browser with immediate Sprite Sheet and Tiled Animation support, without initial export.

• Smart export in multiple formats: PNG, GIF, animated WebP, JSON (TexturePacker-compatible), and Unity/Godot presets.

• Simple collaboration via a Share Link for review and commenting without account registration.

• Simplified physics engine (Squash & Stretch, Bounce) that suggests in-betweening frames automatically.

3) Scope of the first version (8 tasks maximum)

1. Multi-layer Pixel drawing engine with core tools (pen, eraser, fill, Eyedropper).

2. Timeline for arranging frames and controlling playback speed and loops.

3. Basic transformation tools: Flip, Rotate, Onion Skin.

4. Complete color system: Palettes, Swatches, Eyedropper, and HSL/RGB support.

5. Export to formats: PNG Atlas + JSON, GIF, animated WebP.

6. JavaScript scripting engine with a built-in console.

7. Save and share projects in the browser (IndexedDB) with offline support.

8. Sign-up page + browsable public projects page (Community Gallery).

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free to start: all core drawing and export tools are open for personal and commercial use.

• Freemium based on cloud services, not on tools:

• Free: unlimited local projects, PNG/GIF export, sharing via public links.

• Pro — $5/month: cloud sync, private projects, WebP/Unity/Godot preset export, managed private scripts.

• Studio — $15 per seat/month: shared workspaces, review with comments, team permissions, and invoicing.

• Additional revenue: paid pixel art templates and libraries (Marketplace takes a 20% commission), and short training courses.

• Legal: basic license free (such as GPL/MIT) while cloud services remain paid.

5) Global launch plan using free channels

• Launches on Product Hunt and Hacker News (Show HN) with an interactive demo ready to share.

• YouTube / TikTok / Reels: a video series "Make a Game in 60 Seconds" showing the full workflow.

• Twitter/X and Mastodon: daily GIFs of user creations with a dedicated hashtag.

• Discord Community: servers for discussion, weekly Pixel Art Challenges, and featuring the best works.

• itch.io: publishing free tools/extensions integrated with the project to attract indie developers.

• Reddit: sharing in r/pixelart, r/gamedev, and r/IndieDev with short tutorials.

• Educational partnerships: free provision to university courses and platforms such as itch.io jams and Ludum Dare.

• Ambassadors program: symbolic rewards for artists publishing tutorials in Arabic, English, Japanese, Korean, and Chinese.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Verify licenses for fonts, icons, and typefaces used.

• Do not imitate existing visual identities or trade names; stay entirely clear of registered trademarks.

• Copyright issues in pixel art libraries and reference databases.

Technical:

• Engine performance on high-resolution drawing tablets with Pen Pressure via Pointer Events and WebGPU/WebGL.

• Export consistency across browsers (especially Safari) and saving large projects without data loss.

• Strong competition from established free and commercial tools; differentiation must come from the Arabic interface, scripts, and cloud collaboration.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Pixora Studio

Tagline: Create your world with a single pixel and share it in any game or anywhere.
