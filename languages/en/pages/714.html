Daw' — Complete Project Plan

Edit your RAW images in seconds, free and on your device.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, business model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not yet launched.

Software product plan: Daw' (Daw')

1) Target audience

1. Amateur and professional photographers who shoot RAW with Canon, Sony, Nikon, and Fujifilm cameras, and want a free alternative to tools costing $10/month.

2. Users in emerging markets (Middle East, Southeast Asia, Latin America) who lack international bank cards, and need a free start with no financial commitment.

3. Batch image processors: product designers and content creators who process 300–1000 images per week and want to save two hours of manual work daily.

4. Linux users (developers, researchers, designers) who lack a native, fast, compatible RAW editor on their machine.

5. Students and photography clubs worldwide (about 50,000 clubs) who need a free educational tool with sample RAW files.

6. Preset creators who sell processing templates and want a platform to showcase their products under a 70/30 revenue model.

2) Key differentiators (why choose our product)

1. GPU-built speed: using Vulkan and Metal for live preview; target of opening a 45-megapixel image within 1.5 seconds and exporting a JPEG within 0.4 seconds per image, about twice as fast as commercial tools in our initial tests.

2. Fully local AI: automatic masking of sky, faces, and frames with over 95% accuracy without uploading any image to the internet, a crucial feature in privacy-conscious markets.

3. Full openness: editing and exporting work without an account or internet, with support for Linux, macOS, and Windows from a single codebase in Rust.

4. Standards compatibility: one-click import of standard XMP preset files, with a library of 200 free templates at launch.

5. Authentic Arabic interface: full RTL support with translated interface and shortcuts, something rarely offered by any competing RAW editor.

6. Pricing transparency: no watermark, no image dimension limits, and no locked camera formats in the free version.

3) First release scope (8 tasks max)

1. RAW loading engine supporting 5 main formats: DNG, CR3, ARW, NEF, and RAF via licensed open libraries.

2. Non-destructive editing pipeline with 12 essential adjustments: exposure, contrast, saturation, temperature, sharpness, noise reduction, cropping.

3. GPU-accelerated live preview with before/after modes and side-by-side comparison with one click.

4. Preset system: import and export XMP with a library of 200 free templates.

5. Batch mode: process 500 images by applying one template with naming templates and JPEG/TIFF export.

6. Bilingual interface (Arabic RTL + English) across all three systems at once.

7. Landing page and direct download with 20 free sample RAW files.

8. Internal metrics dashboard: time to open 10 images, memory usage, export error rate.

4) Pricing and revenue model

1. Free forever: unlimited editing and 300 exports per month with no bank card, to attract the first 10,000 users in 6 months.

2. Pro — $6/month or $49/year: unlimited exports, batches, advanced smart masks, and 50 GB cloud sync.

3. Studio — $19/year per user: shared preset libraries, license management, and tax invoices.

4. Creator marketplace: 30% commission on preset sales (average template price $8, i.e. $2.40 per sale).

5. Free educational license for 100 universities and schools in the first year to build reputation and loyalty early.

6. Payments via Stripe, PayPal, and Paddle to support 40+ countries with automatic local tax discount.

5) Global launch plan through free channels

1. Reddit and Hacker News: publish speed comparison tables with numbers (10 images, 100 images); target 500 votes in launch week.

2. YouTube and TikTok: 12 "before/after" videos in English and Arabic and 3 speed comparison videos, with zero production budget (screen recording only).

3. Product Hunt and AlternativeTo: two spaced launches (beta then 1.0 after 90 days); target 1,000 downloads in the first week.

4. Arab photographer groups: 5 Facebook and Telegram groups totaling about 80,000 members, with 4 comparison posts per week for a month.

5. Referral program: one free Pro month per successful invitation, with a monthly leaderboard of the top 10 referrers displayed in the app.

6. Bilingual SEO: 50 articles like "Best RAW editor for Linux 2026" and "Convert RAW to JPEG high quality" targeting 300 organic visits per day after 6 months.
