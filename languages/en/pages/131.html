Naqsh — Full Project Plan

Draw precisely, export freely, and start for free.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a 2D engineering drawing tool

1) Target audience

• Architects, civil and mechanical engineers who need fast 2D drawings.

• Craftsmen and workshop owners (carpentry, metalwork, aluminum) who design simple parts and schematics.

• Engineering and design students in universities and technical schools.

• Hobbyists and model makers (3D printing, CNC, laser cutting) who need clean DXF files.

• Small teams and freelancers who want a lightweight, free alternative to heavy paid software.

2) Points of distinction (why choose our product)

• Completely free to start, with fast performance on weak devices (built on a modern interface and vector graphics).

• Simple, clear user experience instead of crowded interfaces, with customizable workspaces.

• First support for the open text formats DXF/SVG with smooth import and export, and a plan to support DWG later.

• Works locally without internet, with full privacy for user files (no mandatory cloud upload).

• Direct collaboration and sharing preview links for drawings in the paid plan.

• Partially open-core or fully open-source software to attract contributors and build community trust.

• Works on Windows, Linux, macOS, and Web (the same work session).

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Basic drawing engine with elements: line, circle, arc, polyline, and spline.

2. Editing tools: move, copy, rotate, scale, trim, offset.

3. Layers with colors, line styles, and show/lock control.

4. Snapping tools (Snap/Osnap): endpoint, midpoint, center, intersection, perpendicular.

5. Basic measurements and dimensions: linear, angular, radius.

6. Import and export DXF and SVG, and export PDF for printing to scale.

7. Undo/redo, session management, and saving files locally.

8. Customizable interface with keyboard shortcut support and Arabic (RTL) language localization.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free forever: all drawing, editing, and basic export tools, no limit on the number of files, works locally.

• Pro (monthly/yearly subscription): ~$7 per month or ~$59 per year: cloud collaboration and sharing, version history, professional templates, block libraries, DWG export.

• Teams & Education: ~$10 per user per month: member management, permissions, unified billing, discounts for universities and schools.

• Additional revenue streams: sponsorship, a templates and symbols marketplace (developer shares the revenue), and enterprise support/priority support.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Publish the repository on GitHub with roadmap transparency and open community issues.

• Launch on Product Hunt, Hacker News, Reddit (r/cad, r/engineering, r/opensource), and Lobsters.

• Free educational content on YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram: short tutorials "draw a part in two minutes."

• Articles and documentation in Arabic and English plus SEO for inputs like "free DXF program."

• Partnerships with universities and trainers and providing free educational licenses.

• A crowdsourced translation community (Crowdin/Weblate) to increase international adoption.

• Showcases in digital manufacturing communities (CNC, laser, 3D printing) and workshop forums.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal:

• Writing the code from scratch (clean-room) and avoiding any copying of code, interface, or icons from any existing product.

• Verifying licenses: DXF is an open specification, while DWG is a protected format and requires a license agreement with a technology provider (such as ODA) or delaying its support.

• Avoiding similarity in names and trademarks, and checking the trademark before launch.

• Respecting the licenses of the open-source libraries used and documenting their attribution.

• Technical:

• Accuracy of DXF import/export and handling old versions of it.

• Drawing performance with large files and a 60fps target.

• Compatibility across the three systems + the web version.

• Stability of PDF export at precise scales.

• Attracting contributors to an open-source project and continuity of maintenance.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Naqsh

Marketing tagline: Draw precisely, export freely, and start for free.
