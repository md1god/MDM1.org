Sijil — Complete Project Plan

Record your expenses in five seconds, and own your data alone wherever you go.

Full plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue, distribution, risks. Not launched.

1) Target audience

• Small firms/self-employed: shopkeeper, barber, freelance designer, 30-50 invoices/month; simple income/expense ledger, no accounting skill, like a juice seller knowing daily profit, not tables.

• Privacy-focused: employees, doctors, journalists avoiding strange servers; data on device first, full CSV/JSON export anytime.

• Weak connectivity: daily drops; record offline, sync on return, no loss or duplication.

• Advanced users/developers: use automation and MCP, e.g. ask an assistant "this month's expenses" or add an entry via short script.

• Families/multi-currency: remittances abroad, local currency and USD together; daily conversion, shareable reports.

2) Why our product

• Device first, cloud optional: save locally before upload; offline instantly, user owns data, no cloud account needed.

• Sync freedom: no imposed server; iCloud, WebDAV, S3, any self-hosted server. NAS owner points app to it; others pick a provider in one click.

• Smart input: camera reads receipt text, amount, date, suggests category; tap to confirm. Under 5s per entry, human corrects.

• Open MCP automation: assistants read summaries/add entries in natural language; revocable permissions, per-operation log.

• Native Arabic, fresh design: full Arabic UI, own identity, same on iOS, Android, web, no translated feel.

• Honest free start: no ads, data selling, or sudden trial end; basic free forever, export always on.

3) First release (max 8 tasks)

1. Local DB (e.g. SQLite): income/expense in under 5s, auto-classify.

2. Arabic entry UI: add, edit, delete, search by name, amount, date.

3. Monthly summary: income, expenses, net profit, category pie chart.

4. Sync with iCloud, WebDAV, S3; ask user on two-device conflicts.

5. Camera receipt capture + AI extraction, mandatory confirmation before saving.

6. Full CSV/JSON export, password-encrypted backup.

7. Web in browser plus iOS and Android apps, unified UI, independent local storage.

8. Self-hosted server, one config file (Docker), MCP interface for read/add.

4) Pricing and revenue

• Free forever (Free): 100 ops/month, basic categories, one server, full export, no credit card.

• Pro (Pro): ~$39/year or $5/month; unlimited ops, advanced reports, multiple servers, auto backups. 10,000 subscribers ~ $390,000/year.

• Optional AI credits: 50 free/month, then 500 for $4; heavy camera users only.

• Paid support/hosting: self-hosted server always free; $99/year support for small firms, no data lock-in.

• Trust rules: no ads, no selling/sharing financial data, pricing page explains cost.

5) Free global distribution

• GitHub open source: clear license, practical README, Arabic/English tags; attracts developers reviewing finance tools before install.

• App stores: App Store and Google Play, free listing, reply to reviews within 24h, target "offline expense ledger", "self-hosted accounting".

• Communities/forums: real guides in privacy/self-hosting communities, Reddit and Hacker News; value, not ads; 30 posts in first two months.

• Content/education: 12 articles like "sync your ledger between phone and home server"; 90-second offline camera-entry videos.

• Gradual localization: Arabic and English day one, French, Spanish, Turkish in six months via volunteers; local date/currency formats.

• Privacy partnerships: install guide for small server hosts in exchange for mentions in their lists; high-trust free channel.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Finance/tax rules: no tax advice; clear disclaimer, no "tax-ready" claims, review EU consumer rules pre-launch.

• Data protection (GDPR etc.): local storage still needs privacy rules for cloud sync; clear policy, permanent deletion, upload consent, storage-region choice.

• Sync complexity: same entry edited on phone and server may lose data; numbered logs, backups, conflict choice screen, no silent decisions.

• AI accuracy: wrong amounts possible; user saves, field sources shown, weekly error checks, blurry images rejected.

• Security/trust: keys must never reach our servers or privacy fails; end-to-end encryption, independent review before launch.

• Sustainability/competition: free rivals pressure subscriptions; diversify Pro, AI credits, support; listen to users, skip random features.

7) Suggested name and tagline

Suggested name: Sijil

Tagline: Record your expenses in five seconds, and own your data alone wherever you go.
