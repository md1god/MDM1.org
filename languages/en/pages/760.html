NAJDAH™ — Full Project Plan

Smartly control ad spend and increase ROI effortlessly.

Complete execution plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, business model, rollout, and risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

1) Target Audience

1. Small advertisers (1-10 employees) with monthly ad budgets not exceeding $5,000 who need to improve return on spend without hiring a specialized marketing team.

2. Independent digital agencies managing campaigns for a limited number of clients and looking for a unified tool to reduce time spent on manual analysis.

3. Entrepreneurs selling products via e-commerce stores targeting social channels to drive quick sales.

4. Small non-profit organizations relying on paid ads for fundraising that need to maximize impact with limited budgets.

5. Freelance marketers working on multiple ad accounts simultaneously who want to consolidate data into a single dashboard.

2) Points of Differentiation (Why Choose Our Product)

1. Free start model: A free basic version allowing up to 3 ad account connections and weekly optimizations at no cost, creating a very low entry barrier.

2. Smart analysis of wasted spend using machine learning algorithms trained on open-source industry data, identifying inefficient CPC ads and recommending bid reductions or pausing.

3. Automatic execution of decisions after user approval via a single button, where commands are sent to platform APIs without manual login.

4. Simplified visual dashboard displaying KPIs with clear colors and enabling real-time tracking of optimizations.

5. Multi-language support focusing on Arabic and English to expand the user base in emerging markets.

6. Data privacy protection via TLS-1.3 encryption and temporary data storage only during the analysis session, without storing sensitive data on servers.

3) First Version Scope (Max 8 Tasks)

1. Connect ad accounts from Google Ads, Meta Ads, TikTok Ads, LinkedIn Ads, Reddit Ads, and X (Twitter) using OAuth.

2. Collect campaign data (spend, impressions, clicks, conversions) for the last 30 days and analyze it in a temporary store.

3. Identify ads with CPC > average + 20% and recommend bid reductions or targeting adjustments.

4. Detect unprofitable keywords in search campaigns and provide replacement or deletion lists.

5. Generate weekly PDF reports and send them to the user's email.

6. Enable “Execute Suggestion” button to apply selected changes directly via API after user confirmation.

7. Set up Slack/Telegram alerts for unusual spending or sharp drop in conversion rates.

8. Arabic user interface with a step-by-step guide to activate and connect accounts.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

1. Free version: Connect up to 3 accounts, weekly reports, and manual execution only (no automation).

2. “Starter” Plan – $19/month: Unlimited connections, automated execution of changes, daily reports, and email technical support.

3. “Growth” Plan – $49/month: All “Starter” features + instant alerts, advanced ROAS analysis, and ability to add extra users (up to 5).

4. Annual subscription model with a 20% discount to encourage long-term commitment.

5. Optional 5% success fee on the net increase in ROI (ROAS) achieved due to optimizations, paid only upon proven results.

5) Global Rollout Plan via Free Channels

1. Educational content on YouTube (series “Optimize Your Ads with AI”) targeting Arabic and English speakers, with free template downloads.

2. Technical articles in blogs like Medium, Dev.to, and Hashnode explaining account connection steps and success examples from pilot projects.

3. Participation in Facebook and Telegram groups specialized in digital marketing, offering free Zoom workshops every two weeks.

4. Launching the “Ambassador” program allowing early users to get lifetime free subscriptions in exchange for publishing reviews and explanatory videos.

5. Leveraging SEO by targeting keywords such as “free ad analysis,” “AI ad spend optimizer,” and “Facebook ads optimization.”

6. “Open Data Challenge” initiative inviting developers to test the product's open API and provide improvements, creating an open community that raises product awareness.
