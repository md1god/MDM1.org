BasaltOps — Complete Project Plan

A solid foundation for running Odoo: inspect, back up, and recover with confidence before issues escalate.

A complete plan for implementing a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, expansion plan, and risks. The project is under development and not yet launched.

1) Target Audience

• Odoo administrators and technical supervisors managing self-hosted instances on servers or Docker.

• Integration companies and agencies managing multiple Odoo environments for different clients.

• IT and compliance teams that need to audit changes and prove successful recovery.

• Managed hosting providers that want to add a reliable operations service for their clients.

2) Key Differentiators (Why Choose This Product)

• Provable trust: every sensitive operation goes through a pre-check, a dry run, approval, and an audit log.

• Recovery, not just backup: verify backup integrity and actually restore it in an isolated environment before relying on it.

• Independent layer (Sidecar): no modifications to Odoo core and no reliance on a modified version, reducing conflicts with upgrades.

• Privacy-first: the agent runs locally and data stays on the server; the cloud dashboard is optional.

• Actionable results: not just warnings, but the cause, impact, and safe remediation steps.

• Independent design and identity: a clear Arabic-English operations interface with a unique visual identity that does not mimic the Odoo UI.

• Free to start: the basic self-use version is free forever, with no credit card required.

3) Scope of the First Release (Max 8 Tasks)

· # · Task · Tangible Output ·

· 1 · Lightweight server agent · Monitor CPU, memory, disk, database connections, and Odoo workers with minimal impact ·

· 2 · Upgrade readiness check · Risk report: incompatible addons, risky settings, insufficient space, and incomplete backups ·

· 3 · Scheduled backups · Encrypted backups with checksum verification and a clear retention policy ·

· 4 · Isolated restore · Automated restore in a test environment with a success/failure report and failure reasons ·

· 5 · Job and queue monitoring · Monitor scheduled actions, workers, and queues, with alerts and guidance for safe retries ·

· 6 · Configuration change audit · Log who changed a setting, when, the previous value, and the new value, with compliance exports ·

· 7 · Dashboard, reports, and alerts · Unified web dashboard, PDF/CSV reports, and email and Webhook alerts ·

· 8 · Secure CLI · Commands for inspection, backup, restore, and maintenance mode, with limited permissions and dry-run by default ·

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free: $0, 1 Odoo environment, 1 user, basic checks, 1 test restore per month, 7-day log retention.

• Pro: $39 per environment per month, unlimited checks and restores, 5 users, 90-day log retention, advanced alerts and scheduling.

• Business: $199 per month for up to 10 environments, SSO, compliance reports, priority support, 99.9% SLA.

• Enterprise: custom pricing for on-premises and isolated deployments, with a security review and training.

Revenue comes from subscriptions, installations, and enterprise support, with a commitment not to sell customer data or use it for any purpose other than operations.

5) Global Launch Plan with Free Channels

• Publish the free version via GitHub Releases, Docker Hub, and PyPI, with clear installation documentation.

• Create bilingual SEO content covering upgrade checks, backup verification, and safe Odoo restores.

• Share practical solutions in Odoo forums, Reddit, and Stack Overflow without spam or direct ads.

• Create short video tutorials on YouTube showing real checks and successful restores in minutes.

• Run monthly newsletters and webinars, with anonymized case studies.

• List the product on Product Hunt and open-source tool directories, with a free trial partner program.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

Legal:

• The Odoo trademark is owned by another party: use only descriptively, make no official claims, and include a clear disclaimer.

• Comply with Odoo licenses and dependencies, build code from scratch, and review licenses before each release.

• Protect customer data: use encryption, a data processing agreement, a retention and deletion policy, and a defined storage location.

• Backups may contain personal data: require explicit consent, enforce least privilege, and maintain an auditable access policy.

Technical:

• Odoo interfaces and database schemas change across versions: maintain a compatibility matrix and automated tests for each supported version.
