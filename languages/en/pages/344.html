Bīʿlay — Full Project Plan

List your product once; it appears for sale everywhere.

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the launch plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a multi-marketplace listing tool for independent sellers

1) Target audience

• Individual sellers on thrift and discount marketplaces (thrifters) who handle 50–500 items per month.

• New sellers starting from a home room who want to reach customers across different platforms without manually recreating each listing.

• Small shop owners on Vinted, eBay, Depop, Poshmark, Mercari, and Etsy.

• Sellers in North American and European markets (United States, United Kingdom, France, Germany, Italy) where these platforms are concentrated.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Smart, compliance-aware listing: one bulk upload with automatic conversion of title, description, sizes, and tags according to each platform's rules — not just simple copy-paste.

• Instant status sync: when an item sells on one platform, it is automatically delisted from the others within minutes, with a clear log of every operation.

• Live inventory tracking: a single dashboard showing each item's status, view counts, and sales per platform.

• AI listing assistant: generates a title and description from the item's photos and dimensions alone.

• Free to start: a free plan sufficient for daily use without a credit card.

• Arabic and English interface from day one to also serve the Middle East.

3) First-version scope (8 tasks maximum)

1. User account, email verification, and a basic dashboard.

2. Manual single-product add (title, description, photos, price, condition).

3. Platform account linking (eBay first, then Vinted and Depop as a next step).

4. Publish one listing to two linked platforms with publishing status saved.

5. Detect sales via platform updates and auto-delist the listing from the other platform.

6. Inventory page showing all products and their status on each platform.

7. Free plan (up to 25 active listings) and a payment page for upgrading.

8. Email/push notifications for sales, failed publishes, and expirations.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: up to 25 active listings, two platforms, auto-delist after sale, help-center support.

• Pro — $19/month: up to 300 listings, 6 platforms, bulk publishing, AI description assistant, performance reports.

• Store — $49/month: effectively unlimited, team of up to 3 users, simplified API, priority support.

• Additional revenue: a small commission (1–2%) on optional tools such as professional templates and barcode printers.

• Expected profit margin: 70–80% after covering infrastructure costs, because the marginal cost of syncing is low.

5) Global launch plan with free channels

• Short-form content on TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts: before/after listing an item on 4 platforms within a minute.

• Seller groups and communities on Facebook and Reddit (r/Poshmark, r/Flipping, Vinted groups).

• SEO blog in Arabic and English: comparisons, profit calculators, product photography tips.

• Partnerships with resale and selling content creators in exchange for free subscription credit.

• Free weekly newsletter: used-goods price statistics per platform.

• Free 14-day trial of the Pro plan without a credit card.

• Referral program: one free month for each friend who completes their first listing.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: frequent changes to each platform's terms may invalidate unofficial APIs; relying on available official APIs and an approved seller agreement reduces the risk.

• Legal: image and description intellectual property belongs to the seller, with a clear disputes policy.

• Legal: personal data protection in line with GDPR in Europe and CCPA in California, and card data storage via a certified payment provider (PCI).

• Technical: platform API changes can break syncing; addressed with error monitoring, alerts, and listing backups before every publish operation.

• Technical: rate limits and suspicious-account blocks; addressed by implementing cooldown periods and gradual rollouts.

• Operational: reliance on user accounts may lead to their suspension; addressed by documenting safe-use guidelines and providing rapid emergency support.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Bīʿlay

Tagline: List your product once; it appears for sale everywhere.
