Risala — Full Project Plan

Discover newsletters worth your inbox, nothing more.

Full execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the distribution plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: A Guide to Proven Newsletters

1) Target Audience

• Arab readers looking for high-quality newsletters on technology, marketing, design, and economics.

• New newsletter creators who want to reach an initial audience without paying for ads.

• Marketers and analysts who follow weekly content trends.

2) Points of Difference (why choose our product)

• Human review of every newsletter before it is listed, with a quality score out of 5 and a clear reason.

• Search and filtering by language, category, and sending frequency, with inactive newsletters filtered out automatically.

• A "Growth of the Week" board showing rising newsletters before they become well known.

• Newsletter submission and user voting live, with no technical account required, plus a free newsletter-owner dashboard.

3) Scope of the First Release (8 tasks maximum)

1. Newsletter database: name, description, language, category, subscription link, status.

2. Manual review process: accept / reject / request changes, with a written reason.

3. Index page with search and filters (language, category, frequency).

4. Unified newsletter card: quality score, content sample, date of last send.

5. Form to submit a new newsletter + an "I want to subscribe" vote.

6. Simple editor dashboard to manage reviews and hide dead newsletters.

7. Automated link checks and last-send-date checks via a periodic script.

8. Public, linkable (SEO) pages for each category and for each newsletter.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Completely free for readers and basic newsletters (free-to-start model).

• Pro for owners (9$ per month): newsletter highlighting, click-through and click statistics, a "Reviewed" badge.

• Category sponsorship (from 79$ per month): a labeled sponsored placement inside a single category page.

• Early revenue from weekly featured-newsletter sponsorship (always clearly labeled).

5) Global Distribution Plan Using Free Channels

• Long-tail SEO articles: "Best X newsletters in English/Arabic" for each category.

• Accounts on X, LinkedIn, and Reddit: a weekly "3 Rising Newsletters" post.

• A free PDF guide to the top 50 newsletters, given away in exchange for subscribing to our own mailing list.

• Partnerships with newsletter creators: a free badge for joining, in exchange for being listed in their guide.

• A Discord/Telegram community for users, with weekly voting.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: no copying of descriptions or ready-made lists — every description is written from scratch; respect the trademarks of registered newsletters; clear disclosure of paid content.

• Legal: quoting from newsletter pages is limited to links and public information only.

• Technical: newsletters change their interfaces and subscription links — handled by periodic automated checks and watchdog alerts.

• Technical: vote abuse (bots) — handled by a voting limit per user / IP address.

• Technical: dependence on a single email provider — reduced by a swappable queue-based system that allows changing the provider.

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Tagline

Proposed name: Risala

Marketing tagline: Discover newsletters worth your inbox, nothing more.
