Market Pulse — Complete Project Plan

Write your idea in one sentence, and wake up to a working store and a report telling you the next step.

Plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue, distribution, risks. Under construction; not launched.

1) Target Audience

• Solo Builders: clear idea, no time or team; e.g. full-timer with 10 hours/week wanting a store live in 72 hours, not months.

• Artisans/local services: carpenter, photographer, trainer; know their craft but can't build a payment page or organize orders; 30% of time lost to unpaid admin.

• Intrapreneurs: 2–5 staff testing an idea with zero budget before seeking approval; need tangible proof in one week.

• Digital learners/trainers: course/ebook creators selling via manual payment links; need one place for orders, payments, reports, not 5 tools.

• Arabic-speaking markets: 400m+ users, half preferring full Arabic UI and local payments; goal: first "write your idea in Arabic, get a running business" experience, no language barrier.

• Subscription-wary: refuse to pay before trying; users must see a working product within 5 minutes, before any payment decision.

2) Differentiators (why choose us)

• Result before setup: skip 12 fields and account setup; one sentence like "a Shakshuka store selling weekly meals in Riyadh" yields a real page and working test payment link in 90 seconds.

• Transparent "Today's Tasks": daily 3 actionable items (e.g. "reply to 2 orders", "update meal price", "thank a customer") plus one "Done" button — progress without complex dashboards.

• Full ownership: export site and customer data anytime as a standard-format compressed file; no rights retained, ending "platform lock-in" fears.

• Local payments first: instant STCPay/Fawry, Stripe on activation, pre-prepared tax invoice; rivals start with Stripe only, missing half the market.

• Low operating cost passed on: lightweight engineering (static pages + short workflows) keeps each store under $0.5/month, so 3 free stores per user are affordable.

• Optional "Human Review": stop any automatic action (e.g. messaging a customer) and require approval, earning trust where unsupervised AI agents are rejected.

3) First release (8 tasks max)

1. Prompt Intake: one Arabic/English field, 3 examples; sentence → "idea file": name, audience, suggested price, 3 keywords.

2. Page generator: responsive HTML/CSS template; landing page with open-library images, correct RTL Arabic text, click-to-edit.

3. Inbox: web-form orders in a table: name, email, product, status (new/in progress/done), instant search.

4. Test payment link: "Test Mode" from day one, no card; one click to a real gateway.

5. Daily task worker: 8 a.m. user-time job reads Inbox, creates 3–5 prioritized tasks; email alerts only.

6. "Activity Pulse": one screen, 4 numbers: today's orders, test revenue, tasks done, last activity; no complex charts.

7. Export/backup: "Download your project" outputs compressed page + data in JSON, PDF invoices.

8. Simple login: email + password or Magic Link, basic protection, 10 login attempts/day per IP.

4) Pricing and revenue

• Free Forever: 1 store, 25 orders/month, 1 page, 1 daily report; not a "trial gimmick": enough to sell 25 real units.

• Growth ($19/month): 5 stores, 500 orders, custom domain, no logo, invoice export; below two separate subscriptions.

• Team ($49/month): 5 seats, change audit log, human approval for every automatic action; for small in-company teams.

• Optional 1% fee: only on payments via our integrated gateway; none if users use their own; transparency is competitive, not marketing.

• Marketplace 80/20: designers sell ready-made page templates for $9, keep 80%; we add none by hand — community supplies them.

• Conversion: per 1,000 free users, 60 paid (6%), 120 gateway orders/month; ~$1,740 recurring revenue per thousand, service cost under $500.

5) Global reach via free channels

• Share-by-Output: each page gets a "made with one sentence" link bringing visitors to it with a ready copy of the idea; output is the ad, no paid creative.

• Long-tail content: 30 Arabic/English articles answering real questions — "How to start a store with no website?", "How to write a tax invoice for a small project?" — each with a 45-second video.

• Entrepreneur communities (organic, no spam): solve specific problems in 5 communities (e.g. Facebook groups, Discord, Reddit's r/Entrepreneur); one case study weekly with real numbers, not promises.

• Free education partnerships: free "classroom license" for 20 instructors in schools and institutes; students use it hands-on, becoming 2,000 users in month one, no ads.
