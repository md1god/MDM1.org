مِدَار — Complete Project Plan

مِدَار turns your doses, protein, and symptoms into a single page your doctor understands at your next visit.

Plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue, rollout, risks. Under construction, not launched.

1) Target audience

• First 90 days: beginners on a weekly injection or daily pill; need a 3-minute routine. Example: employee injects Sundays 8 a.m., fears a repeat dose.

• Missed doses/travel: many users miss one dose every 6–8 weeks from travel or time changes. Reminders at 24 h and 2 h with a large "Done" button.

• Early symptoms: nausea, constipation, fatigue in weeks 1–2; users want dose vs low fiber/water link, not generic tips.

• Low protein: with low appetite, may eat only 35 g vs 82 g needed for 68 kg at 1.2 g/kg; split into 3 meals: 30 g breakfast, 25 g lunch, 27 g dinner.

• 4-week doctor visits: 15 minutes is too short; one page shows adherence, average protein, top 3 symptoms.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Drug-level curve: estimated daily steps, assumed 7-day half-life. After 4 weekly doses level accumulates then plateaus, explaining week-3 symptoms.

• Cause to effect: nausea 4/5 after 8 g fiber and 1.1 L water shows card: "Fiber 12 g under target in last 24 h," no medical judgment.

• Site rotation: 6-site body map, no repeat within 28 days, least-used suggestion; after left thigh, "right abdomen" next week.

• One-page report: PDF, 4 small charts: adherence, sites, protein/fiber averages, symptoms; printed or protected link.

• Privacy: on-device by default, upload only with explicit consent, AES-256 backup, "Delete everything" under 10 s.

3) Scope of the first version (8 tasks max)

1. Dose logging: one button injection/pill, adjustable timing, 90-day log, 2 reminders; "Missed dose," not "Failed".

2. Rotation map: 6 clickable sites, 28-day no-repeat, auto weekly suggestion, manual override with reason.

3. Level curve: 12-week weekly view, daily steps; "educational estimate" on axis; plateau after 4–5 doses.

4. Needs calculator: weight + goal (1.2/1.6 g/kg) → protein, fiber, water; 200 common items, e.g. "cooked lentils 18 g/cup".

5. Symptom log: 6 symptoms, 1–5 rating, time of day, optional meal/dose link; weekly top 3.

6. Export/backup: PDF, JSON, optional encrypted backup, PWA offline on 5 test devices, load under 2 s.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: unlimited doses, reminders, map, targets, 1 PDF/month; no ads, no trial limit.

• "Full follow-up": $4.99/mo or $39/yr; unlimited reports, 52-week curve, CSV, encrypted backup, one-tap restore.

• "Pay what you can": $0–$2/mo in low-income markets, one setting, no income proof.

• Care: $9.99/mo for 5 profiles managed by family/nurse, one comparative report.

• Economics: 1,000 × $39 = $39,000/yr; covers servers, light upkeep; no ads or health-data sales.

5) Global rollout plan using free channels

• 20 groups: free 7-page guide "How to rotate injection sites over a month"; moderator permission; 3 daily Arabic/English answers.

• 12 search articles: "Enough protein with low appetite: 3 practical meals", "Reading the drug-level curve without guesswork"; each ends with free-app link.

• 30 videos, 30 s: rotation, injection vs pill, 82 g protein as meals; Reels, Shorts, TikTok; Arabic/English.

• 30 pharmacy/nutrition clinics: ready-to-print PDF sample, pharmacist input on warnings; no paid patient referrals.

• Tech launch: PRODUCT HUNT page, Hacker News post on curve and offline mode; direct app link, no store download.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• No diagnosis: every screen says "Consult your doctor before changing your dose"; no calculated doses or dose-change advice; generic terms, not brand names, in identity/logo.

• Data rules: sensitive health data; local first, consent before upload, AES-256, deletion within 30 days for GDPR/privacy.

• Ad rules: educational, not drug ads; review all content; ban "best medicine", "guaranteed results" in app description.

• Curve accuracy: educational, ±20% error; put "Educational estimate, not for medical decisions" inside chart; block lab-measure export.

• Data loss: JSON export reminder every 7 days, local copy per edit, restore tested on 2 devices per release.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: مِدَار

Tagline: مِدَار turns your doses, protein, symptoms into one page your doctor understands next visit.
