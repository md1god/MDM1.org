Mizanly — Complete Project Plan

One dashboard for your net worth that helps you understand what changed and why, while your data remains yours alone.

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: concept, audience, product, revenue model, launch, and risks. Under construction, not launched.

1) Target audience

  • Individuals and small families, not banks or accounting teams, with accounts at multiple banks, brokers, and digital wallets who want one net-worth view.
  • Expats, multi-currency earners, and owners of assets in several countries.
  • Self-hosting/privacy communities and early financial-independence supporters.
  • Freelancers separating personal and business assets.

2) Why our product

  • Privacy first: offline local operation; encrypted on-device database. Optional cloud sync exposes only encrypted data.
  • No bank credentials or screen scraping. Optional read-only licensed-provider access; immediate CSV, OFX, and CAMT alternatives.
  • Explains wealth: why net worth changed via deposits, markets, currencies, fees, expenses, and inflation.
  • Multi-currency real return: chosen-currency valuation, purchasing power, and after-fee, after-inflation return.
  • Family privacy: separate profiles, optional per-account sharing, hidden details with visible totals.
  • Calm original design: paper-ledger identity, editorial type, warm colors—not loud platform colors or exaggerated gamification.
  • No lock-in: full export, free local version forever, optional paid services only.

3) First-version scope (max 8 tasks)

1. Secure storage: encrypted local SQLite, Argon2id keys, backup, recovery.

2. Data/family model: accounts, assets, liabilities, categories, profiles, per-asset sharing.

3. Import: CSV, OFX, CAMT, QIF; column mapping, rules, duplicate detection, preview, undo.

4. Read-only connectivity: one licensed bank provider via switchable interface, BTC/ETH/SOL addresses, platform files.

5. Dashboard: total, allocation, timeline, per-account breakdown, multiple currencies.

6. Change explanation: monthly split into contributions, market, currency, fees, expenses, plus summary.

7. Goals/scenarios: savings, emergency fund, debt repayment, "what if" simulations without personal investment advice.

8. Security/distribution: Passkeys/TOTP, Tauri desktop app, PWA, Arabic/English, docs, automated tests.

4) Pricing and profit model

  • Free forever: local version, unlimited accounts, import, dashboard, reports, export, self-hosted sync.
  • Plus: $5/month or $45/year: encrypted cross-device sync, mobile app, daily updates.
  • Family: $8/month for up to 6 members, advanced sharing.
  • Early Lifetime: $99 one-time for first 500 users.
  • Transparent banking: own provider key free, or actual cost passed through without markup.
  • Profit: hosting, sync, and support subscriptions. No ads, data selling, or holding data hostage.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

  • Publish open-source core on GitHub with one-click docs and Docker images.
  • List in Awesome Self-Hosted, AlternativeTo, and privacy/open-source directories.
  • Launch on Show HN, Hacker News, Product Hunt, r/selfhosted, r/personalfinance, r/privacy, r/FIRE.
  • English, Arabic, Spanish, and Portuguese SEO comparisons and guides targeting "local alternative for wealth tracking."
  • Short YouTube demos of import and monthly explanation, lightly edited.
  • Free partnerships with financial creators, newsletters, and FIRE communities.
  • Community translation; invite early users to test and improve import.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

  • Financial data: GDPR/CCPA, data-protection impact assessment, deletion/export rights, storage location.
  • Banking: licensed providers only; no stored bank credentials; respect bank terms, PSD2, screen-scraping ban.
  • Not financial advice: clear disclaimer; indicative tax calculations only, varying by country.
  • Price data: respect provider licenses and attribution.
  • Brand/license: pre-launch name check; open license compatible with paid hosting.

Technical:

  • Provider interfaces/costs: adapter layer, immediate import alternatives, usage limits.
  • Lost key/sync conflicts: recovery phrase, backups, auditable conflict log.
  • Categorization/duplicates: mandatory preview, editable rules, undo.
  • Self-hosting support: rigorous docs, independent security audit, free-version support limits.
  • Supply chain/devices: signed releases, automatic updates, least privilege.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Mizanly

Tagline: One dashboard for your net worth that helps you understand what changed and why, while your data remains yours alone.
