AriaForge — Complete Project Plan

Scan your site, get copy-ready fixes, and track compliance automatically.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, launch plan, and risks. The project is under construction and not yet launched.

1) Target audience

• Founders of SaaS/no-code sites (Webflow, Bubble, Framer) with teams of 5–50 employees and $10k–$200k monthly revenue; 40% of their pages contain accessibility issues.

• Web development agencies (20–200 employees) needing compliance reports for clients (ADA/WCAG 2.2 AA).

• Product/marketing managers at Shopify stores wanting to avoid ADA lawsuits (average settlement $25k).

• Independent developers using v0/Bolt/Lovable to build AI-generated interfaces.

• Educational institutions and nonprofits needing WCAG 2.2 AA compliance on registration pages.

• Figma designers wanting to check accessibility before writing code.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Copy-ready fixes (diff for React/Vue/HTML) instead of just a report, reducing fix time from 8 hours to 15 minutes.

• Prioritized by legal risk + user impact (ADA Title III lawsuits, screen readers).

• Free tier: 5 scans/month, 10 pages each, no credit card, no watermark.

• AI trained on real WCAG failures from Common Crawl, 95% alt text accuracy.

• Continuous monitoring via GitHub Action + Slack alerts, detecting hallucinated ARIA in AI code.

• On-prem/GDPR option, no PII storage, respects robots.txt.

3) First version scope (max 8 tasks)

1. URL scanning engine (Playwright + axe-core) detecting 50+ rules.

2. AI fix generator (LLM) for alt text, labels, contrast, focus order, button names.

3. Web dashboard with prioritized issue list + diff viewer.

4. Free tier signup + magic link auth.

5. Stripe billing for Starter/Pro/Team.

6. GitHub Action for CI scanning.

7. Weekly monitoring report via email/Slack.

8. Documentation + onboarding tutorial (5 steps).

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: 5 scans/month, 10 pages, basic fixes.

• Starter $19/month: 50 scans, 100 pages, AI fixes, monitoring of one site.

• Pro $79/month: unlimited scans, 500 pages, 5 team seats, CI integration.

• Team $249/month: SSO, on-prem, SLA, dedicated support.

• Enterprise: custom pricing (annual contract).

• Revenue: 60% Pro, 25% Team, 15% add-ons (audit PDF reports at $9).

• Goal: 1000 free users → 5% conversion = 50 paid = ~$3950 MRR by month 6.

5) Global launch plan via free channels

• Launch on Product Hunt (target top 5 of the day).

• Show HN post with before/after demo.

• Reddit: r/webdev, r/accessibility, r/SaaS, r/noCode (3 posts/week).

• Twitter/X threads with visual demos (2/week).

• YouTube tutorials (accessibility for AI-generated sites) 1/week.

• Dev.to/Hashnode articles (SEO: "WCAG scanner free", "accessibility checker AI").

• Partnerships with no-code communities (Webflow forum, Bubble forum).

• Open-source axe-core wrapper on GitHub (target 1000 stars).

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: ADA lawsuits; disclaimer (tool is not a legal certification); WCAG not mandatory in all jurisdictions; avoid claiming "compliant".

• Privacy: scanning third-party sites may capture PII; respect robots.txt, rate limiting, 30-day retention.

• Technical: ~15% false positives from axe-core; AI fixes may break UI; human review + preview mandatory.

• Performance: scanning large sites is costly; queue + caching + 500-page limit.

• Competition: AudioEye/accessiBe/UserWay; differentiation through developer-ready patches.

• Model drift: providers change; abstraction layer + fallback.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: AriaForge

Tagline: Scan your site, get copy-ready fixes, and track compliance automatically.
