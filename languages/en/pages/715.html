Spreadly — Complete Project Plan

Read manga like a real book—two pages at a time, anywhere, offline.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, launch plan, and risks. Project is under construction and not yet launched.

1) Target audience

• Web manga/comics readers (16–35 years), 60% on phones, frustrated by page-by-page scrolling.

• "Spread" (double-page view) fans who want a paper-book-like experience on aggregator sites.

• Scanlation teams needing quick visual review before publishing.

• Tablet users (iPad/Android tablets) reading in landscape.

• RTL/LTR content readers (Japanese, Korean, Chinese, English) needing smooth direction switching.

• Accessibility users (magnification, high contrast, keyboard navigation).

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Smart "double-page" detection (gutter detection) with auto-crop removes empty margins and aligns panels.

• Offline-first (PWA + IndexedDB) stores 200–500 pages (~150MB) for offline reading.

• General adapter layer + per-site plugins: works on 5+ major sites without per-site forks.

• Performance: page switch <100ms, prefetch next 2 pages, virtualized list for long chapters.

• Privacy: no tracking; reading progress exported/imported as encrypted file (no server required).

• Accessibility: ARIA landmarks, keyboard focus, high contrast, screen reader support.

3) First release scope (max 8 tasks)

1. Reader engine (spread/single toggle, RTL/LTR, zoom, keyboard/gesture nav).

2. Adapter layer for 5 sites (heuristics + per-site selectors).

3. Offline storage (Service Worker + IndexedDB) with quota handling.

4. Prefetch and caching (prefetch next 2 pages, virtualization).

5. Export/import reading progress (encrypted JSON file).

6. Settings panel + onboarding (3 steps, <60s to first read).

7. Accessibility pass (WCAG AA) + keyboard tests.

8. Closed beta (200 users) + fixes + extension store listings.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free-to-start: basic reader (spread/single, offline 50 pages, 3 sites) free forever, no ads.

• Premium $3/month or $24/year: offline 500 pages, sync across devices, themes, unlimited sites, priority support.

• Lifetime $49 one-time for early adopters (first 1,000 users).

• Donations (GitHub Sponsors/Patreon) for community translators.

• Target: 5% conversion from 50k MAU → ~$7.5k MRR at $3 avg; break-even at ~$1.2k/mo infra.

5) Global launch plan via free channels

• GitHub releases + Chrome/Firefox/Edge stores (ASO keywords: manga reader, two-page).

• Valuable (not spam) posts in r/manga, r/manhwa, r/ComicBook (case studies with screenshots).

• Discord: outreach to 20 scanlation groups for beta testing + testimonials.

• Product Hunt launch with demo GIF + "Show HN" post.

• YouTube/TikTok: 60s demos by creators (barter: free premium for review).

• Translate UI into 6 languages via community (Crowdin free for OSS).

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: we don't host images; client-side enhancement only. Risk: extension store takedowns. Mitigation: DMCA contact, no hotlinking changes, respect robots.txt, clear ToS.

• Technical: DOM changes break adapters. Mitigation: heuristic fallback, weekly automated smoke tests on 5 sites, community plugin updates within 48h.

• Stores: MV3 policy rejections. Mitigation: minimal permissions, no remote code, documented privacy policy.

• Privacy: sync data. Mitigation: E2E encryption, opt-in, local-first default.

• Performance: low-end devices. Mitigation: quality tiers, disable animations option.

• Competition: native apps. Mitigation: web-first zero-install, works on any site.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Spreadly

Marketing tagline: Read manga like a real book—two pages at a time, anywhere, offline.
