Malath — Complete Project Plan

Your files in your own shelter, in just ten minutes.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the rollout plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: Malath, a private digital shelter

1) Target audience

• Individuals and families who want to store their files and photos behind a wall of privacy, with no reliance on advertising companies.

• Freelancers and small teams (2–20 people) in sensitive fields: legal consulting, clinics, photography, engineering.

• Universities, small schools, and associations that need shared space with clear rules.

• Regions that require data to remain inside the country (data sovereignty).

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Focus on "self-hosting in 10 minutes": a single installer (Docker) and a friendly setup page, with no server expertise required.

• Optional end-to-end encryption, enabled by default for sensitive folders, with the key staying with the owner.

• Smart syncing over a P2P protocol between your devices when the network is available, reducing your server usage and giving higher speed.

• "File time machine": automatically archived copies with one-click restore, and a restore bin with a clear expiry period.

• No ad tracking, no data selling, and an interface with no marketing noise.

3) Scope of the first release (8 tasks at most)

1. File sync engine for web and desktop (with WebDAV support and open standards).

2. Modern web app: folders, instant search, document and image previews inside the browser.

3. Sharing: public links with a password and expiry date, and invitations to folders with permission levels.

4. Optional end-to-end encryption for selected folders + simple key management.

5. Version history + restore bin + full restore with one click.

6. Admin panel and server delivered as Docker with guided step-by-step setup.

7. Secure login: 2FA, and identity federation via OIDC.

8. Responsive mobile experience with instant notifications for shares and new sessions.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free forever: one user, 10 GB of your own space (self-hosted), for personal users.

• Family: up to 5 members, 4 dollars per month (shared hosting or commercial license).

• Team: 3 dollars per member per month with an admin panel, reports, and SSO.

• Optional managed hosting for those who do not want to run the server themselves.

• Software license: free for personal use, licensed fees for commercial use and managed hosting.

5) Global rollout plan using free channels

• Multi-language SEO content hub: guides on "how to protect your files", self-hosting comparisons, annual cost calculator.

• Launch on Product Hunt and Hacker News, and enthusiast communities on Reddit, Telegram, and Discord.

• Practical tutorials on YouTube and Bilibili: full run in 10 minutes, family backup.

• Partnerships with privacy blogs, routers, and embedded systems (NAS) to build ready-made images.

• Referral program: a free month for every friend who activates, and sharing ready-made setup templates.

• Presence at open-source conferences and user communities speaking Arabic, German, and Japanese.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: compliance with data protection standards (GDPR and its equivalents), a clear definition of the "data processor" role, and a notice that self-hosting makes the customer responsible for their content.

• Legal: risks of sanctions and export controls on encryption technologies, and choosing a license that prevents commercial re-hosting without a license.

• Technical: losing the encryption keys requires a clear recovery path or means permanent data loss.

• Technical: sync conflicts, denial-of-service attacks on installers, and package supply chain security.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Malath (Malath)

Tagline: Your files in your own shelter, in just ten minutes.
