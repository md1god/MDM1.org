IntelliCanvas — Full Project Plan

Turn your words into an interactive interface in moments

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, go‑to‑market plan, and risks. The project is under development and not yet launched.

1) Target Audience

1. Professionals in research and analysis – researchers, consultants, data analysts who need to present complex results interactively and quickly.

2. Designers and content developers – graphic designers and educational content creators who want to embed text with animations, diagrams, and maps within a single interface.

3. Technical support and customer service teams – teams responding to user inquiries who need to provide visual explanations (e.g., device setup guides, flowcharts).

4. Students – especially in educational programs that rely on interactive explanations (such as physics, geography, economics).

5. Small and medium enterprises – those lacking budgets for expensive commercial software and seeking an open‑source, customizable solution.

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

1. Interactive UI driven by text flow – each sentence is turned into a UI element (map, chart, image) without writing additional code.

2. Extensible ready‑made component library – includes timelines, interactive maps, chart generators, hand‑draw tools, with easy addition of custom components.

3. Fully free‑to‑run model – based on a lightweight stack (Node.js + React) and can be hosted on free servers such as Render or Railway.

4. Compatibility with any language model – supports OpenAI, Anthropic, Llama‑2 via a unified API, giving users freedom to pick the most suitable model.

5. Privacy protection – no content is stored locally unless the user chooses; all data is processed only in temporary memory.

3) Scope of the First Version (Maximum 8 Tasks)

· No. · Task · Practical Description ·

· 1 · Convert text to chart · When the user types “sales 2022‑2024” a Chart.js chart is generated automatically. ·

· 2 · Insert interactive map · Sentence “service center locations in Saudi Arabia” displays a Google Maps map with custom markers. ·

· 3 · Manual drawing tool · User can drag to add lines or shapes, then export them as SVG. ·

· 4 · Generate illustrative image · Prompt “draw a login flowchart” creates an illustration via DALL·E or Stable Diffusion. ·

· 5 · Generate short code snippet · Sentence “Python function to calculate average” shows a code window with a run button. ·

· 6 · Insert interactive table · Sentence “product price list 2024” turns into a sortable, editable table. ·

· 7 · Launch calculator tool · Request “compute compound interest for 5 years” adds a live‑input calculator widget. ·

· 8 · Add voice note · Button to record a voice note attached to the text, displaying a playable waveform. ·

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

1. Freemium – core version free with a limit of 10 requests per day per user.

2. Paid Pro plan – $9.99 /month, raises the limit to 1 000 requests daily and adds cloud‑storage of projects.

3. Enterprise subscription – $49 /month for 5 users, with LDAP/SSO support and component customization.

4. Additional revenue – Marketplace where developers sell paid add‑ons; 20 % of sales returns to the platform.

5. Sponsorship and statistical data – provide anonymized analytics reports to research bodies for an annual subscription fee.

5) Global Free‑Channel Distribution Plan

1. Share on developer forums – publish a quick‑install guide on Reddit r/webdev, Hacker News, and Dev.to with a practical example.

2. Educational YouTube channels – produce 5 short videos (3‑5 min) demonstrating each major feature, inviting contributions on GitHub.

3. Arabic communities – post Arabic articles on Medium (Arabic), and share in specialized Telegram and WhatsApp groups for programming.

4. Write for open tech blogs – articles showing how to integrate the platform with free LLMs (e.g., Llama‑2 7B).

5. Hackathon contests – organize a “Best Interactive App” competition on Devpost with symbolic prizes (certificates, site promotions).

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

1. Image copyright infringement – external image generators may produce protected content; include a warning against commercial use without a license.

2. Dependency on third‑party APIs – outages of OpenAI or Anthropic keys could break the service; mitigate by offering local alternatives (Llama‑cpp).
