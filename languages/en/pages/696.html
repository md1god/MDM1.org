Qirtas — Complete Project Plan

Create your invoice in a minute and collect your money before it's due.

Full plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue, rollout, risks. Under construction, not launched.

Product: smart invoicing/collections for small teams

1) Target audience

• Freelancers and makers (design, development, photography, translation): 3–15 clients, 5–20 monthly invoices, manual follow-up via WhatsApp/email.

• Small firms (2–20 staff) with recurring services (maintenance, consulting, marketing); need overdue tracking without complex accounting.

• Agencies and consultancies with many projects; want one view of revenue and receivables per client.

• Accountants with 5–30 small clients; need fast exports and tax-ready reports.

• Emerging markets: Arab region, South Asia, Latin America; bank transfers and local wallets dominate, connectivity is weak.

• Local service teams (clubs, training centers, clinics) needing recurring invoices and subscriptions.

2) Advantages

• Offline-first: create, edit, save invoices on-device; auto-sync online, resolving conflicts by last documented edit.

• One app, all platforms: web, Android, iOS, Windows/macOS from one codebase, cutting development cost up to 60%.

• Collect via client's channel: invoices/reminders by email, WhatsApp, SMS with a direct payment link, no account needed.

• Local payments and auto-matching: record transfers and wallets, match by amount/reference; smart suggestions cut manual work by 70%.

• Native Arabic, not translated: full RTL, optional Arabic/Indic numerals, multiple currencies, Hijri/Gregorian calendars, equal English.

• Free start, no card: try the full product within limits, keep data, export anytime as CSV/PDF.

3) First release (6 tasks)

1. Clients and items: contacts, currency, tax, reusable item catalog, default prices.

2. Invoices and quotes: one-click quote-to-invoice, auto-numbering, item/invoice discounts and tax, draft saving.

3. PDF templates: 3 customizable designs with logo/colors, PDF/CSV export, browser printing.

4. Automated reminders: pre/post-due reminders (e.g., 3 days before, 7 and 14 after) by email and WhatsApp, stopping on payment.

5. Payments: record full/partial payments, attach receipts, show balances, report overdue by client.

6. Sync and offline work: encrypted local storage, sync queue, simple conflict resolution, last-sync status panel.

4) Pricing and revenue

• Free forever: 5 invoices monthly, 10 clients, small PDF watermark. Goal: 10,000 users year one, 4–6% paid.

• Starter, $6/month: 50 invoices, unlimited clients, no watermark, email reminders.

• Pro, $15/month: unlimited invoices, WhatsApp reminders, advanced reports, one gateway.

• Business, $29/month per user: agencies; 5 seats, permissions, client white-labeling.

• Extra revenue: 20% annual discount, $19/month white-label add-on, 0.5% collections commission via our gateways (optional).

• Pricing: no setup fees, no mandatory annual contracts, cancel anytime; trust drives growth.

5) Free-channel rollout

• SEO: 40 Arabic and English articles on "invoice template," "payment reminder," and "tax invoice," targeting long-tail keywords.

• Community launch: Product Hunt, Hacker News, Indie Hackers; 3 months of Pro free for first 500 users.

• Niche communities: Reddit (r/freelance, r/smallbusiness), Facebook freelancer groups, Discord maker servers; educational, not promotional, content.

• Accountant and accelerator partnerships: 20% recurring commission for accountants, free monthly collections workshop.

• Referrals: one free month each, trackable link, rewards dashboard.

• Free templates and integrations: 10 downloadable invoice templates as a lead magnet, plus Stripe, WooCommerce, Zapier.

6) Legal and technical risks

• Local tax compliance: e-invoicing rules vary (e.g., ZATCA in Saudi Arabia, Egypt e-Invoicing); start generic, add local packages later.

• Data protection: GDPR, encryption in transit/at rest, clear retention (5–10 years by country), no selling customer data.

• Sync conflicts: offline work can cause conflicts; use a change log, daily backups, network-outage tests.

• WhatsApp dependence: Business API policy or fees may change; keep email and SMS fallbacks, avoid one-channel reliance.

• Price competition: large players with similar features; focus on emerging/Arab markets and offline experience.

• IP: write code and design from scratch, review licenses, avoid borrowed assets, text, interfaces.

7) Name and tagline

Proposed name: Qirtas

Tagline: Create your invoice in a minute and collect your money before it's due.
