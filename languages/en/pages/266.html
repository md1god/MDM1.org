Loopmark — Full Project Plan

A digital passport for every product, from factory to recycling, without complexity or startup cost.

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: Digital Product Passports (DPP) platform

1) Target audience

• Small and medium manufacturers and brands within the European Union in sectors being phased in: batteries, textiles, electronics, furniture, construction materials, detergents.

• Exporters to the European market from outside it (the Gulf, Egypt, Turkey, Morocco, China, India) who lack an in-house compliance team.

• Compliance consultants, sustainable design agencies, and BOM/PLM/ERP system providers looking for a ready-made compliance layer.

• Circular economy startups that want to prove the repairability and recyclability of their products.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Genuinely free to start: create basic passports without a bank card or prior subscription.

• At item level, not batch level, with a unique identifier for each piece.

• A ready-made data model generator that matches ESPR regulation requirements and updates automatically when they change.

• No technical expertise needed: import data from Excel/CSV and map fields automatically instead of writing code.

• An authentic Arabic interface alongside European languages, serving Arab exporters.

• A carbon calculator and repair guide embedded within the passport itself, not as separate add-ons.

• Open to integration via API and exportable data mapping templates, so no customer lock-in.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Account registration and creation of a brand workspace with roles and permissions.

2. A customizable "product data model" editor compatible with ESPR classifications.

3. Import of BOM and materials from Excel/CSV with automatic field mapping.

4. A basic carbon footprint calculator and a simple repair/durability indicator.

5. Generation of unique identifiers (UID) for each product unit.

6. Generation of a printable QR and a ready link for NFC tagging.

7. A public multilingual passport page (Landing Page) that works on mobile.

8. Export/import via API and a pilot connection to the identifier registration interface.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: up to 50 passports, basic data, basic QR.

• Starter — €29/month: up to 1,000 passports, import, two languages, ESPR templates.

• Growth — €149/month: up to 10,000 passports, full API, multiple users, carbon calculator.

• Enterprise — custom: massive volumes, SLA, data migration, ERP integration.

• Additional sources: fees per registration transaction with the official registry, NFC/QR chip printing, data migration and setup services, paid compliance consulting.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Bilingual SEO around ESPR and DPP terms in Arabic and English (guides, templates, FAQs).

• A blog and free templates to attract exporters looking for an explanation of the requirements.

• Open-source components for reading QR/GS1 Digital Link on GitHub to attract developers.

• LinkedIn and compliance and sustainability groups with regular educational content.

• Joint webinars with Arab and European chambers of commerce and export centers.

• A referral and ambassadors program with rewards for every paying customer, and integrations with PLM and designer platforms.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: changing ESPR implementing regulations and their timelines, and differing requirements between product categories; liability for the accuracy of declared data; personal data protection when including owner data.

• Technical: the evolution of data schemas and their need for continuous updating; API security and access integrity; dependence on the availability of the official registration infrastructure; supplier data quality; scalability at millions of QR reads.

• Mitigation: separating rules logic from the regulation engine for easy updates, documented template versions, and self-auditing before publication.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Loopmark

Marketing tagline: A digital passport for every product, from factory to recycling, without complexity or startup cost.
