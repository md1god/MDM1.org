My Battery — Full Project Plan

Towards a digital battery passport at the touch of a button, for fast and transparent compliance.

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, profit model, go‑to‑market plan, and risks. The project is under development and has not been launched yet.

1) Target Audience

1. Small battery importers in EU member states (companies with 5‑10 employees) who need to demonstrate compliance with Annex XIII without legal complexity.

2. Mid‑size manufacturers producing batteries for consumer devices (phones, e‑bikes) who need to compile technical documents into a standardized template to submit to the regulator.

3. Consultants and medium‑size firms offering compliance services to multiple clients and looking for a fast tool to generate digital product passport drafts.

4. Non‑profits monitoring sustainable supply chains and needing to store environmental and social evidence for batteries.

5. Educational and research institutions studying battery life cycles and needing a testing platform to experiment with digital passport requirements.

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

1. Drag‑and‑drop interface that converts documents (PDF, Excel, images) into ready‑to‑use passport fields within minutes, no technical expertise required.

2. “Trust Point” system that rates each piece of evidence based on its source (approved supplier, lab certificate, independent report) and shows a confidence level to reviewers.

3. “Gap Text” library that proposes ready‑made wording explaining the gap between requirements and available documents, customizable to reduce writing time.

4. Exportable versions in the formats required for the EU Digital Product Passport (JSON‑LD, CSV) with a trusted electronic signature.

5. Version history tracking that lets you review every change, who made it, when, and restore a previous version with a single click.

3) Scope of the First Release (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. Create a new project and select battery type (rechargeable, non‑rechargeable) and manufacturing date.

2. Upload supplier documents (CE certificates, safety test reports, material declarations) and insert them into the template via drag‑and‑drop.

3. Automatic file analysis to identify missing sections according to Annex XIII requirements and display a “gaps” list.

4. Generate gap texts with customization options to explain why a specific evidence is unavailable.

5. Add a QR‑Code that generates a static web page showing the product’s digital passport when scanned.

6. Export a regulator‑ready version in signed JSON‑LD using a free API key from EU‑Trust.

7. Version management: save every edit, display confidence scores, and export the change log as a PDF.

8. “Trusted Evidence” area showing confidence indicators (0‑100 scale) and allowing the importer to add internal comments or review notes.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

1. Free tier allowing creation of only two projects, with a maximum of 5 documents per project; suitable for a small importer to try the product.

2. “Starter” plan (9 €/month) offering unlimited projects, storage up to 100 documents, and unlimited exports.

3. “Professional” plan (29 €/month) includes API support, integration with ERP systems (SAP, Odoo) and the ability to attach an electronic signature from a trusted service provider.

4. “Pay‑as‑you‑go” for multi‑use QR‑Codes: 0.02 €/scan after 10 000 free scans per month.

5. B2B partnerships with industry associations to provide group licenses (30 % discount for groups larger than 50 members).

5) Global Free‑Channel Go‑to‑Market Plan

1. Educational content: produce Arabic and English video series on YouTube explaining digital passport requirements and platform usage; target 10 000 views within 3 months.

2. Tech communities: share tutorials in Stack Overflow and Reddit (r/RegTech) with links to the trial version.

3. Virtual conferences: host a free workshop in EU‑Commission webinars on “Digital Product Passport”, offering a demo account.

4. University partnership: provide a free license for academic research, generating scholarly documentation that enhances reputation.

5. Email marketing: build a list of 5 000 subscribers from public European trade organization directories and send a monthly newsletter outlining regulatory updates and our solutions.
