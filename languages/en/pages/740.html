قصة‑نقطة — Complete Project Plan

From idea to storyboard in one minute

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, go‑to‑market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and not yet launched.

1) Target Audience

1. Independent screenwriters – who write original ideas and need a tool to support them in drafting text and completing scenes quickly.

2. Small‑scale directors – who manage teams of 5‑10 people and need visual planning (Storyboard) without hiring external artists.

3. Film and theatre students – at universities and institutes studying screenwriting and needing automatic constructive feedback to evaluate their work.

4. Digital content producers – creators of web‑series and short videos who want to turn a short text into a complete script with scene breakdowns.

5. Small advertising agencies – that produce short ads and need a fast pipeline from concept to storyboard.

2) Unique Selling Points (Why choose our product)

1. “Free‑to‑Start” model with open‑source AI – we rely on open language models (e.g., LLaMA‑2‑7B) to lower operating costs and provide a free version that requires no upfront subscription.

2. AI‑driven story structure analysis – the text is automatically examined against six known narrative structures (three‑act, hero‑obstacle‑turning‑point, …) and weaknesses are shown in real time.

3. Intelligent character generation – the character model (goal, conflict, arc) is filled based on a simple writer description, with suggested dialogue and traits that complement the character.

4. Instant Text‑to‑Image storyboard – using Stable Diffusion 2.1, key frames are rendered from the script within seconds, with style options (cartoon, realistic, cinematic).

5. Offline capability – a desktop version (Electron) stores models locally and supports offline mode for writers in low‑bandwidth regions.

3) Scope of the First Release (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Script editor supporting industry‑standard formats (Final Draft, Fountain).

2. Automatic scene‑completion suggestions based on the text written so far.

3. Story‑structure diagnosis and display of a three‑act outline with improvement recommendations.

4. Automatic generation of a Character Sheet from a brief description.

5. Visual storyboard generation from selected scenes (limit 5 scenes per story).

6. Export project to PDF, Final Draft (.fdx) and HTML.

7. Free cloud saving (up to 3 projects) via Google/Apple account.

8. Multilingual writing support (English, Arabic, Spanish).

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

· Tier · Description · Price · Additional Features ·

· Free · Unlimited editor and basic AI access · $0/month · 3 active projects, storyboard limited to 5 scenes, 500 MB cloud storage ·

· Pro · Remove project limits, unlimited storyboard, updated language models · $9.99/month · Export to all formats, 24/7 support, integration with production tools (ShotGrid). ·

· Team · License for teams up to 10 people, permission management, custom template library · $39.99/month · Admin dashboard, productivity reports, integration with Slack/Discord. ·

Revenue relies on monthly subscriptions, plus additional sales (story templates, premium storyboard lines) and income from API partnerships with video distribution platforms (Vimeo, YouTube) to provide direct links to final products.

5) Global Go‑to‑Market Plan with Free Channels

1. GitHub Open‑Source community – publish core code and documentation to encourage contributors to improve models, with a “Star” count indicating popularity.

2. YouTube and TikTok channels – produce a series of 30‑minute tutorials showing how to write a script, improve structure, and generate a storyboard, inviting viewers to try the free version.

3. University competitions – sponsor screenplay contests in art colleges, offering free Pro licenses to winners, creating rapid academic adoption.

4. Partnerships with learning platforms (Coursera, Udemy) – offer a free course backed by live tool examples, with a discount coupon for upgrading to Pro.

5. Facebook/Telegram groups for writers – create support and idea‑discussion groups where updates and polls are posted to guide product development.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

1. Copyright issues for generated images – text‑to‑image models may produce content similar to protected works; clear CC‑0 licensing policies and periodic database updates are required.

2. GDPR and CCPA compliance – user data (scripts, characters) must be encrypted, with the ability to delete data on request.
