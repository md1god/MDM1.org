NebulaDial — Complete Project Plan

Build a smart phone agent for your project in minutes, start for free with no coding experience.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, target audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

1) Target Audience

The project targets small and medium-sized businesses in specific sectors that face pressure from daily phone calls:

• Small healthcare centers: Clinics and dental centers that need to schedule appointments constantly.

• Maintenance and operations services: HVAC companies, plumbing, and electrical companies that receive emergency requests and daily maintenance calls.

• Law offices: For initial inquiries, case screening, and booking consultations.

• Local craft and service workshops: Such as plumbers, carpenters, and home service providers who do not have a full-time receptionist.

2) Key Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• Unprecedented ease of use: Instead of creating complex scenarios, business owners simply describe their activity in plain words, and the agent is automatically created based on the description.

• Privacy-first focus: Designed with customer data protection as a top priority, with options for local storage based on each market's requirements and support for gradual compliance by sector.

• Built for underserved markets: Multi-language and multi-dialect support, with a focus on emerging markets that are often neglected by similar solutions.

• Truly free model: Allows users to start for free with full access to core features without being forced to pay for a trial.

• Flexible integration: Focus on simple, open-source CRM tools (such as Google Sheets or open-source Airtable alternatives), rather than limiting integration to expensive platforms.

• Contextual intelligence: Understands the context of Arabic and English conversations, automatically filtering serious customers from general inquiries.

3) Scope of the First Version (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. AI-powered activity description: Automatically generate a conversation flow from the business owner's description of their activity.

2. Simple visual editor: Quickly edit questions, answers, and transfer options without writing code.

3. Ready-to-use voice agent: Connect to a text-to-speech engine to answer calls, with natural conversation timing and hold behavior.

4. Smart call transfer system: Route calls to the appropriate number when needed, or record a message for follow-up.

5. Basic dashboard: Display calls, requests, and simple statistics for the current day.

6. Spreadsheet integration: Automatically save records to Google Sheets or an open-source alternative.

7. Free credit system: Grant free trial credits upon registration, with a clear display of credit usage.

8. Basic compliance settings: Simple privacy notice, options to delete records, and a ready-made structure to support future requirements for sensitive sectors.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free: Sufficient call credits to get started, ideal for small shops and solo entrepreneurs.

• Starter: Suitable for small businesses with moderate call volume, with a monthly fee and a reasonable call limit, plus a clear per-call rate for overage.

• Growth: For high-activity businesses, with benefits such as multiple agents, longer call history, and priority support.

• Pay-as-you-go: Flexible option for those without predictable call volume, with full price transparency and no hidden fees.

• Revenue model: Primarily based on call consumption (credits/usage), with potential to add optional services in the future such as advanced analytics or voice customization.

5) Global Distribution Plan via Free Channels

• SEO: Create educational content targeting each sector (e.g., "How to automatically schedule your clinic appointments?") in the language of each target market.

• Free educational content: Publish practical guides, ready-to-use templates, and sector-specific use cases to drive organic traffic.

• Professional communities: Provide valuable contributions in Facebook, Reddit, and LinkedIn groups for target sectors, focusing on solving problems rather than direct promotion.

• Free libraries and templates: Offer ready-to-edit conversation templates for each sector, shared on developer and entrepreneur platforms.

• Word of mouth and free trials: Rely on real free experiences to encourage users to recommend the product, especially in small local communities.
