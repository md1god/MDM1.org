A11yCompass — Complete Project Plan

Scan, fix, and certify your EU website for EAA compliance with PR-ready code and a 24-language accessibility statement—free to start.

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, business model, go-to-market, and risks. Project is under construction and not yet launched.

1) Target audience

• EU e-commerce stores (Shopify/WooCommerce/PrestaShop) — over 250k mid-size stores (10–200 employees) exposed to EAA fines (up to €100,000/violation in Germany).

• Digital marketing agencies (15–60 employees) serving 20–80 European clients, seeking a deliverable "compliance" report (white-label).

• B2B SaaS teams (50–500 employees) with EU users needing EN 301 549 clause 9.x coverage for procurement.

• Freelance web developers building sites for European clients needing copy-paste-ready fixes.

• Compliance/legal officers at mid-size companies (500–2000 employees) requesting a local-language accessibility statement.

• Product managers at European startups (Seed–Series B) needing quarterly audit evidence for investors.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• PR-ready fixes: before/after snippet for React, Vue, Angular, HTML (e.g., <img alt="...">, aria-label, 4.5:1 contrast) instead of PDF report only.

• Automatic accessibility statement in 24 EU languages (EN/DE/FR/ES/IT/PL/NL/...) with editable legal template.

• Legal risk classification: each issue gets a "score" (potential fine + user impact) — e.g., checkout form without labels = high (WCAG 1.3.1, 4.1.2).

• CI/CD GitHub Action (free, limited in Free tier) prevents regression: a11ycompass scan --fail-on high.

• Truly free-to-start: 1 site, 5 pages, weekly scan, no card — target 6% conversion to Starter within 30 days.

• Plain-language explanation + highlighted screenshot of the issue — suitable for non-developers.

3) First version scope (≤8 tasks)

1. Headless crawler (Playwright) — up to 100 pages/site, handles SPA (React/Vue) via networkidle wait.

2. Scan engine (axe-core + custom rules) — WCAG 2.2 AA + EN 301 549 clauses 9.x (9.1.1.1, 9.1.3.1, 9.1.4.3, 9.2.1.1, ...).

3. Fix generator (top 20 issues): alt text, contrast, ARIA roles, form labels, heading order, focus order, skip links, language attr.

4. Accessibility statement generator (24 languages) + HTML/PDF export + hosting on static URL.

5. Dashboard (Next.js) — issues classified (high/med/low), filtering, CSV/PDF export, highlighted screenshot.

6. GitHub Action (Docker) — scan on PR, comment with diff of new issues.

7. Weekly email digest (Resend) + Slack webhook alert.

8. Billing (Stripe) — Free/Starter/Pro/Agency + team seats + white-label domain.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: 1 site, 5 pages, weekly scan, no CI — $0 (target 70% of signups).

• Starter: €19/month — 1 site, 100 pages, daily scan, code fixes.

• Pro: €79/month — 5 sites, 1000 pages, CI/CD, API, 5 seats.

• Agency: €299/month — 25 sites, white-label, SLA 99.5%, unlimited seats.

• Enterprise: negotiable — SSO (SAML), on-prem scan, DPA, audit support.

• Additional revenue: "Manual audit" upsell €499/audit (partner with certified auditors), affiliate 20% (EU consultants).

• Goal: 1,000 paying customers × €45 ARPU = €540k ARR by month 12; 3,000 customers = €1.6M ARR by month 24.

5) Global go-to-market via free channels

• Product Hunt launch + "EAA deadline 2025" angle — target 500 upvotes, 2k signups.

• Hacker News Show HN: "I built an EAA fixer that outputs PR-ready code" — target 200 points.

• Reddit: r/accessibility, r/webdev, r/ecommerce, r/shopify — 1 post/week with case study (before/after).

• Dev.to / Hashnode: 12 articles (EAA checklist, "fix alt text in React") + canonical links → SEO.

• SEO: "EAA compliance [country]" pages (27 EU states) + "EN 301 549 checklist" — target 50k organic visits/month by month 9.

• Partnerships: Shopify App Store listing (free), WooCommerce plugin directory, Webflow integrations page.

• Affiliate: 20% recurring for 200 EU accessibility consultants (LinkedIn outreach).

• Open-source: publish a11ycompass-cli (MIT) on npm/GitHub — acquisition funnel.

• Webinars: 1/month with EU chambers of commerce (free) — 200 attendees each.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: EAA enforcement varies by member state (Germany: BFSG fines up to €100k; France: RGAA); a "scan" tool ≠ legal compliance — clear disclaimer "not legal advice" + recommend manual audit for high-risk.

• Technical (false positives): axe-core detects only ~57% of WCAG issues; mitigate with manual audit upsell + confidence score per issue + "needs human review" flag.

• SPA rendering: heavy Playwright cost (~€0.02/page on cloud); limit free tier to 5 pa
