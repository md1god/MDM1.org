An original plan for a new digital project: audience, idea, revenue model, and launch plan.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, launch plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

Original product plan: a diagramming and prototyping tool

1) Target audience

• UI designers and web developers who want to draw wireframes and interactive prototypes quickly without complexity.

• Product teams and startups that need to quickly document ideas and flowcharts.

• Teachers and students in technical tracks.

• Users who prefer a free, open, or lightweight solution that runs directly in the browser.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Runs entirely in the browser (offline-first via PWA) with no complex installation, with support for desktop and tablet surfaces.

• A drawing engine built on SVG with ready-made components (stencils) that are searchable and extensible.

• Real-time collaboration and local/cloud backup, with export to interactive PDF/PNG/SVG/HTML.

• A "boards" system (workspaces) and a shared library within the team with simple permissions.

• Extensibility via community component packs in a unified format, with right-to-left (RTL) writing support.

• Free to start with no limits on local individual work.

3) Scope of the first version (max 8 tasks)

1. Basic SVG editor: shapes, lines, arrows, text, align and distribute, layers, undo/redo.

2. Ready-made electronic component library (buttons, fields, windows, quick diagram icons).

3. Multiple pages/boards within one document with navigation and transition linking between them to build an interactive prototype.

4. Offline work + automatic local saving in the browser (IndexedDB).

5. Export: PNG, SVG, PDF, and shareable interactive HTML.

6. Ready-made templates (flowchart, simplified UML diagrams, application wireframe).

7. Simplified collaboration: read-only share link + importable version (versioned document).

8. Arabic/RTL support and a UI with light/dark theme.

4) Pricing and profit model

• Free forever: local editing, all drawing tools, basic export, community templates.

• Personal plan (monthly/yearly): cloud sync and favorites, historical version copying, unlimited real-time collaboration.

• Teams plan: shared library, permissions, comments, change log, signed export.

• Additional sources: advanced third-party component packs via an internal marketplace (commission fee), dedicated enterprise support (on-premises hosting), and educational sponsorship.

5) Global launch plan via free channels

• Publish the open-source repository on hosting platforms with a clear license and contribution guide.

• Free educational content: short lessons, ready-to-copy templates, videos on visual platforms.

• Posting in technical communities (developer forums, Reddit, Hacker News, Product Hunt at launch).

• Free integrations with common development tools (embedding in wikis and documentation platforms).

• A university ambassador program and periodic template competitions with symbolic prizes.

• SEO via "ready-made templates" and "free alternative to..." pages in an ethical, non-misleading way.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Avoid any copying of assets, code, or icons from any other product; write everything from scratch and provide a clear license for included components.

• Verify the licenses of templates, icons, and third-party components and record them accurately.

• Comply with data protection (GDPR) in cloud sync and student data.

Technical:

• Engine performance with huge documents and high density of SVG elements.

• Sync conflicts and concurrent editing (solved by merging and temporal versions).

• Export consistency across browsers and Arabic/RTL fonts.

• Sustainability of the open-source project and balancing income with free usage.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Mirsam Nuqta

Marketing tagline: Design diagrams and prototypes freely in your browser, and start for free.
