Najwa — Complete Project Plan

Turn client DMs into confirmed appointments in minutes, under your brand, no fees to start.

New digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue, rollout, risks. Building; not launched.

1) Target Audience

• Performance agencies (5-40 staff, 3-25 accounts): 30-50 daily messages via Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, website; no clear buying intent.

• Local businesses (clinics, real estate, schools): 20-80 weekly inquiries, half after hours; 60-minute reply loses the customer; target: 90 seconds.

• Freelancers/creators (1-3) selling by private messages: one inbox, not 4 apps, max $29 when needed.

• New sales teams (Gulf, Egypt, Morocco, India, Brazil): Modern Standard Arabic, dialects, English, Portuguese, Spanish; customer hours, not staff hours.

• Founder/ops manager decides on client-facing numbers: 60% faster replies, 20% more bookings, report in 2 minutes.

2) Why Choose Our Product

• Four-channel inbox, sorted by priority, readiness score, last activity; no app switching or copy-paste.

• Assistant: 3 versions, formal/friendly/quick, customer's dialect; human sends only, avoiding false promises.

• Qualification: 5 in-chat questions, 0-100 score, hot/warm/cold tag, three-line manager summary before calls.

• Client first: logo, colors, name, subdomain per agency; full data isolation; our brand absent from client UI.

• One place: notes, teammate mentions, handoff summary, booking link synced with Google Calendar or CalDAV, 24-hour reminder.

• Your data: CSV/JSON export, 7-day deletion, no contract or setup fee, free plan for real use.

3) First-Version Scope (8 Tasks Max)

1. Official connectors: Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp Business, website tool; 5-minute trial link, permissions screen.

2. Suggestion engine: 3 versions, tone control, price/guarantee alerts before sending.

3. Editable qualification: 5 questions, 0-100 rules, instant list tags.

4. Client workspaces: logo, color, subdomain, separate account-manager permissions.

5. Internal notes, teammate mentions, handoff summary in chat, not a separate email.

6. Per-employee booking link, calendar sync, time zones, 30-minute slots, auto confirm and reminder.

7. Track reply time, qualified leads, booking rate, channel performance; PDF/CSV client report.

8. Subscription/security: free and paid plans, 3 roles, audit log, 6-hour backups, data-deletion page.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free: 1 workspace, 2 channels, 100 chats, 2 seats, booking link, 300 credits; no card, no trial expiry.

• Pro: $29/workspace/month: 4 channels, 2000 chats, 5000 credits, PDF reports, our brand off client UI.

• Agency: $99/month up to 10 workspaces, then $9/workspace; 15 clients = 99 + 45 = $144/month, under $10/client.

• +1000 credits for $10, used only on generation; yearly billing saves 20% ($948/year for Agency).

• Recurring subscriptions; no deal commission, no setup fees, processing fees on invoice.

• Year 1: 200 agencies × $99 ≈ $19,800 MRR; 85% retention at 6 months via reports and support.

5) Global Growth via Free Channels

• 12 reply templates, 8 guides in Arabic, English, Portuguese, Spanish; 3 articles/week, e.g. "Replying to a real estate inquiry in 60 seconds".

• Free no-signup tools: reply-time calculator, qualification generator; sign-up prompt in result; 25% conversion goal.

• 30 agency groups on Facebook, LinkedIn, Discord, Slack: 2 questions/day, no ads, link only when asked.

• 20 design studios, 10 consultants: free Agency plan for 6 months for a case study and workshop; no commission.

• Launch in 5 free software directories; 90-second video: message to booked appointment.

• Referral: 3 active sign-ups earn a free month; goal 1000 sign-ups, 150 active workspaces in 90 days, 30% paid by month 6.

6) Legal and Technical Risks

• Platform rules: WhatsApp 24-hour window, approved templates, no unsolicited bulk messages, 1 message/hour/user, 3-month reviews.

• Privacy: consent log, source per message, 30-day deletion, DPA, GDPR/CCPA docs before Europe and US.

• Model accuracy: no auto-send, human review, 3 suggestions, price/promise alerts, error rate target <2%.

• Downtime: connector per platform, 3 retries, local queue, instant alerts, 99.5% uptime, 6-hour backups.

• Security: transit/storage encryption, 3 permissions, 12-month audit log, pen test 4 weeks pre-launch, 24-hour incident response.

• Backup model provider, full export, closure within 7 days; no client lock-in.

7) Name and Tagline

Suggested name: Najwa

Tagline: Turn client DMs into confirmed appointments in minutes, under your brand, no fees to start.
