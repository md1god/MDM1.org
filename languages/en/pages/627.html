DuskDesk — Complete Project Plan

Turn your website visitors after business hours into confirmed appointments, automatically.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market, and risks. The project is under construction, not yet launched.

Product plan: capturing leads after business hours

1) Target audience

• Small and medium service businesses (1–50 employees): plumbing, electrical, HVAC, cleaning, locksmithing, auto repair, clinics, beauty salons, law firms.

• Business owners without 24/7 reception who lose evening and weekend inquiries.

• Local marketing agencies managing websites and services for dozens of clients seeking a white-label resellable tool.

• English-speaking markets first, then Spanish, Portuguese, and Arabic.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Focus on "after hours" rather than a generic chatbot: welcome message, qualification, then appointment booking or callback request.

• Free start and setup in under 5 minutes via a single embed line working on WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, and Shopify.

• Automatic multilingual support based on visitor language, with a business-appropriate tone per sector.

• Conversion, not just conversation: integration with Google Calendar and Outlook, time zone handling, and customer confirmation.

• Multiple follow-up channels: email, SMS, WhatsApp, and instant owner notification.

• Seamless human handoff when the visitor requests it or for sensitive questions.

• Privacy first: optional regional hosting, sensitive data redaction, and no use of customer conversations to train models.

• No contractual lock-in, transparent pricing, and full data export.

3) MVP scope (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Widget embed tool with color, text, and logo customization.

2. Qualification question editor ready for each sector (service type, location, emergency status, approximate budget).

3. Automatic business hours detection and after-hours mode with a fallback form when chat is unavailable.

4. Appointment/call booking with Google Calendar and Outlook sync and time zones.

5. Lead inbox with priority classification and email/SMS notifications.

6. CSV and Webhook export and basic HubSpot integration.

7. Analytics dashboard: conversations, qualified leads, booking rate, peak times.

8. Trust basics: privacy consent, disclosure that the party is automated, spam protection, and multilingual support.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: 50 conversations per month, one site, our branding, no credit card.

• Starter – $19/month: 300 conversations, two sites, branding removal, calendar integration.

• Growth – $49/month: 1,000 conversations, 5 sites, WhatsApp, advanced reports.

• Agency – $99/month: 15 sites, white-label, team permissions, API access.

• Additional revenue: conversation credits, WhatsApp numbers, custom domains, and a white-label version for agencies.

• 20% annual discount, and a full trial on the paid plan without a card.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Long-tail SEO with articles such as "after-hours answering service," "missed call text back," and "AI receptionist for plumbers" in 4 languages.

• Free tools to attract traffic: a "How many customers do you lose after hours?" calculator and a conversation scenario generator.

• Launch on Product Hunt and BetaList, and listings on G2, Capterra, AlternativeTo, and SaaSHub.

• Integration marketplaces: WordPress, Wix, HubSpot, and Zapier for free distribution.

• Reddit, Indie Hackers, and specialized Facebook groups, with educational rather than promotional content.

• Partnerships with local marketing agencies, vocational schools, and chambers of commerce, and a referral system with free credits.

• Short YouTube and TikTok videos explaining 5-minute setup, and ready-made templates per sector.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: compliance with GDPR and CCPA (consent, data deletion, DPA), TCPA and WhatsApp rules on marketing messages, disclosure that the conversation is automated, avoiding medical or legal advice, and clear terms that the service is not for emergencies.

• Technical: model hallucination and difficulty tuning qualification, prompt injection, unexpected token costs, calendar API outages, rate limits and spam, data security, and high availability.

• Mitigation: response rules restricted to approved sources, automatic handoff to a form/human, strict usage limits, cost monitoring, backups, and periodic security testing.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: DuskDesk

Tagline: Turn your website visitors after business hours into confirmed appointments, automatically.
