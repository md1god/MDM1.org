NutriVault — Complete Project Plan

Log your food with complete privacy — your data and nutrition stay in your hands alone.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, dissemination plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

Product plan: a privacy-focused food tracking app

1) Target audience

• People interested in health and fitness (weight loss/gain, muscle building).

• People with diabetes and blood pressure, and those following a food allergy or a specific diet.

• Privacy-conscious users who refuse to upload their food data to an external cloud.

• Markets with weak or limited internet, where the app must work offline.

2) Points of distinction (why choose our product)

• Privacy first: data stays on the device by default, no mandatory account, and no ad tracking.

• Full offline operation with optional end-to-end encrypted sync on demand.

• Fast barcode and label scanning with an open food database expandable locally.

• Support for multiple diets (calories, macros, intermittent fasting, allergies) with a simple interface.

• Free to start with no paywall, with full data export to prevent lock-in.

• Cross-platform with a single technology to reduce maintenance cost.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Log meals manually with quick search in a local food database.

2. Scan barcodes and add the product automatically.

3. Calculate calories and macronutrients daily.

4. Weekly/monthly reports dashboard with simple charts.

5. Set personal goals (weight, calories, macros).

6. Export and import data (JSON/CSV) locally.

7. Full offline operation + optional encrypted sync.

8. Support for at least two languages with a light/dark interface.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free to start: all core functions are free without limits.

• Optional paid plan (Premium): encrypted cloud sync, automatic backup, advanced reports, smartwatch integration.

• Low individual pricing (monthly/yearly subscription) + family license.

• Additional sources: commercial license for custom databases, optional donations.

• No ads and no selling of data whatsoever to preserve trust.

5) Global dissemination plan through free channels

• Publish an open-source version on public Git repositories to attract contributors and trust.

• Privacy and free software communities (Reddit, Mastodon, open-source forums).

• App store listings with keyword optimization in multiple languages.

• Short content on social platforms (nutrition tips + feature showcases).

• Collaboration with fitness and health bloggers and offering free early access.

• Participation in hackathons and open-source software conferences.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: privacy regulations (GDPR/CCPA), accuracy of nutrition data, and health advice liability (a clear disclaimer is required).

• Licensing: ensure the licensing of the food databases and images used.

• Technical: barcode recognition accuracy, testing on varied devices, and sync consistency without data loss.

• Security: encrypting sync and protecting keys, and reducing the attack surface.

• Market: strong competition; the solution is excelling in privacy and offline operation.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: NutriVault

Marketing tagline: Log your food with complete privacy — your data and nutrition stay in your hands alone.
