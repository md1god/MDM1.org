قَافِلَة — Complete Project Plan

From an AI agent's idea to a post on every channel, with one caravan and under your supervision.

Plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout, risks. Under construction, not launched.

1) Target Audience

• Entrepreneurs/small teams: 1-5 people running 3-7 channels (X, LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook), no time for manual posts; e.g. a store needing 5 posts/week without a full-time writer.

• Mid-sized marketing teams: 10-50 staff, 10-20 channels across products/sub-brands, conflicting posts; need audit log of author and approver.

• AI agent developers: build agents (research assistant, customer service agent) that output content but lack a unified "pipe" to push to social channels without per-platform publishing code.

• Agencies/freelancers: 10-30 clients or 5 accounts, need client separation and one-click monthly reports.

• Emerging-market users: prefer Arabic and low-bandwidth UI, want free-to-start on own server or a plan under $10/month.

2) Differentiators

• "Agent-first": one endpoint (POST /drafts) accepts JSON drafts from any agent and previews every platform; no learning four APIs or per-platform tokens.

• Default human gate: mandatory queue before publishing with "Approve / Edit / Reject"; aim: 70% fewer hallucination-driven wrong posts vs direct publishing.

• Auto adaptation: one idea becomes a <=280-char tweet, short-line LinkedIn post, Instagram caption with 30 hashtags.

• Smart scheduling: best time by time zone/history; block two posts in the same minute per account.

• Self-hosting: Docker + SQLite on any 2 GB server; full data ownership, free start, no credit card.

• Decision analytics: links posts to clicks/conversions, shows top agent, not just likes.

3) First Version Scope (at most 8 tasks)

1. Publishing engine for 4 channels (X, LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook) via official APIs, extensible adapter layer.

2. Arabic/English draft editor, live preview, review queue (approve/edit/reject), change log.

3. Time-zone scheduling, auto-retry on connection failure, weekly status calendar.

4. Editable per-platform adaptation templates in settings.

5. OAuth account linking, token encryption, tenant data isolation by tenantid.

6. Docker package + API docs: any external agent connects in 5 minutes; daily automated tests.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free forever: 1 user, 2 channels, 30 posts/month, unlimited drafting agents; real trial, no card.

• Starter $9/month: 5 channels, 300 posts, full review queue, 30-day analytics; small store.

• Team $29/month: 3 seats, 20 channels, 2,000 posts, approver audit log.

• Agency $79/month: 10 seats, 100 channels, white-label client reports.

• Self-hosting free: core open source; revenue from support/managed hosting.

• Extra: Salesforce and HubSpot integration $19/month; enterprise API fees over 100,000 requests/month.

5) Global Distribution Plan with Free Channels

• Developer communities: Product Hunt, Hacker News, Reddit (r/SaaS, r/LocalLLaMA) post + 90-second video: agent drafts, approves, publishes to 4 channels.

• Build in public: founder's X and LinkedIn daily progress, weekly poll for next platform; 500 followers in 60 days.

• GitHub lists/repos: add to "awesome-ai-agents", "awesome-social-media"; "best agent using the interface" contest, symbolic prize.

• Free integrations: n8n and Zapier nodes in community stores for workflow discovery.

• Free education: 6 SEO articles on "safe AI-agent publishing" and "best Arabic posting times", with copyable code.

• AI communities: Discord servers and Slack groups, weekly "hands-on" integration Q&A.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal - platform terms: API rules may change; official APIs only, adapter swaps any platform in one business day.

• Legal - data protection: GDPR and CCPA deletion on request; "Delete my account" in 24 hours, tokens encrypted with AES-256.

• Legal - violating content: agent may make medical/financial claims; default keyword filter, mandatory human review on free plan.

• Technical - API outages: one failure must not stop others; isolate attempts, 3 retries, exponential backoff.

• Technical - multi-tenancy: tenantid in every table, automated isolation tests, no cross-client draft leaks.

• Technical - rate limits: 20 requests/minute per-account queue, alert at 80% of limit.

7) Proposed Name and Tagline

Proposed name: قَافِلَة

Tagline: From an AI agent's idea to a post on every channel, with one caravan and under your supervision.
