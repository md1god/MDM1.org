Auralis — Complete Project Plan

Train your ear, shape your voice, master English speaking offline.

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, business model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target audience

• 1.5 billion English learners worldwide, 40% cite "listening comprehension" as the biggest obstacle (approximate figures from EF/IELTS reports).

• Employees (25-40 years) in Germany/Japan/Brazil needing spoken English for meetings: 15-25 min/day.

• University students preparing for IELTS/TOEFL (target 7.0+): 3-6 months.

• Self-directed learners on the go (metro/car): 60% of sessions <20 minutes.

• Language teachers needing a classroom tool for 30 students.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Offline-first operation with on-device ASR (Whisper tiny ~75MB) → privacy + speed.

• Phoneme-level pronunciation assessment with a personal error map.

• Spaced repetition (SRS) based on actual errors, not on "any day".

• Realistic dialogue scenarios (interviews/travel/medicine) generated by local LLM.

• Unlimited free tier for basic episodes (3/day) + Premium for advanced.

• Audio data stays on device (zero cloud by default).

3) Scope of first version (max 8 tasks)

1. Import/record audio + automatic segmentation (VAD).

2. Shadowing mode (waveform + pause/repeat).

3. Blind listening test (fill in the blanks).

4. Retelling + AI feedback.

5. SRS scheduler (modified SM-2).

6. Progress dashboard (streak, accuracy, minutes).

7. Offline model packs (download manager).

8. Subscription + paywall (StoreKit/Play Billing).

4) Pricing and business model

• Free: 3 episodes/day, basic ASR, 7 days of progress.

• Pro: $6.99/month or $49/year (42% discount).

• Teacher: $12/month (30 students).

• Lifetime: $99 (30-day launch offer).

• 70% of revenue from subscriptions, 20% lifetime, 10% B2B.

• Goal: 10,000 paid subscribers in 12 months → ~$838k ARR.

5) Global launch plan through free channels

• TikTok/Reels: 30s "before/after" shadowing (3/week) → target 1M+ views.

• Reddit: r/languagelearning, r/EnglishLearning (value, not spam).

• YouTube: tutorials "fix your listening in 30 days".

• Product Hunt launch + Hacker News "Show HN".

• SEO blog: 20 long-tail articles ("how to improve English listening").

• Micro-influencer partnerships (10k-50k) with 20% commission.

• ASO: localized keywords (10 languages).

• Discord community for challenges (retention).

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: rights to imported audio (user bears responsibility; we do not collect protected content).

• GDPR/CCPA: data deletion + opt-in telemetry.

• COPPA: block <13 or parental consent.

• Technical: model size/battery; ASR accuracy for accents; offline sync.

• Mitigation: only user-provided audio, clear ToS, optional crash reporting, A/B accents.

7) Proposed name and marketing sentence

Proposed name: Auralis

Marketing sentence: Train your ear, shape your voice, master English speaking offline.
