Kadr — Complete Project Plan

A simple Arabic HR system that starts with you for free and grows with your team without complexity.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, growth plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

HRM product plan

1) Target audience

• Small and medium-sized companies (10–250 employees) that manage HR through Excel files and scattered spreadsheets.

• HR teams made up of one or two people who lack the time for complex systems.

• Fast-growing startups that need instant setup without a technical team.

• Agencies, service providers, and seasonal projects that rely on temporary contracts and attendance.

• Initial geographic focus: the Middle East and North Africa and South Asia markets, then global expansion according to local localization.

2) Points of distinction (why choose our product)

• Instant start: account activation within minutes with no server installation or consultations.

• A unified mobile and web experience: everything works mobile-first (attendance, requests, approvals).

• Visual workflow engine: build approval paths with drag and drop without programming.

• Built-in smart assistant: drafting job descriptions, summarizing candidates, and answering employee questions.

• Works on weak networks (Offline-first): automatic sync when the connection returns.

• User-owned data: full export in open formats and a public API.

• Flexible localization: multiple languages, calendars, currencies, and local leave rules.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Employee management: a unified record of basic data, documents, contracts, and the organizational structure.

2. Attendance and leave: check-in/check-out, leave balances, and work schedules.

3. Approval paths: a requests engine (leave, expense, advance) with multi-level approvals.

4. Simplified recruitment: posting jobs, receiving applications, and a candidate board with clear statuses.

5. Onboarding: automatic task lists to prepare the new employee.

6. Reporting dashboard: attendance indicators, employee turnover, and workforce costs.

7. Roles and permissions: precise control for each role (manager, HR, employee) and an audit log.

8. Export and API: full data export and basic integration with external systems.

(Everything else: performance reviews, payroll, and training — is deferred to later releases.)

4) Pricing and profit model

• Free to start: up to 15 employees with all core features and no time limit.

• Growth plan: a monthly fee per active user (starting low) that includes automation and advanced reports.

• Enterprise plan: custom pricing with SSO, custom compliance, and priority support.

• Optional add-ons: the AI assistant module, local localization, and private hosting.

• Additional revenue streams: an add-ons marketplace (Marketplace) with a commission, and migration and data setup services.

5) Global growth plan with free channels

• Educational content: a series of articles and videos on simplifying HR for small companies.

• Developer and product communities: a public launch via product platforms and specialized forums.

• Partially open source: an open core that builds trust and pulls in community contributions (Open-core).

• Accounting and legal partnerships: firms serving small companies as a referral channel.

• Multilingual local SEO: targeting keywords such as "attendance and leave system" and "HR for small companies".

• Referral program and local communities: internal rewards and free online workshops.

• Free templates: downloadable contract and policy templates that lead to registration.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Personal data protection (such as GDPR) and compliance with changing local labor laws.

• Liability for processing payroll data and sensitive documents, and the need for data processing agreements.

• Risks of data migration from competitors' systems and liability for data accuracy.

Technical:

• Sync conflicts in offline mode across multiple devices.

• Scalability with complex permission models and multi-tenancy.

• Privacy and accuracy of the smart assistant's outputs and preventing data leakage.

• Access security and penetration testing to prevent leakage of sensitive data.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Kadr

Marketing tagline: A simple Arabic HR system that starts with you for free and grows with your team without complexity.
