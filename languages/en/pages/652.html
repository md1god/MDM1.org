Madar — Complete Project Plan

Publish your releases with confidence, and roll back with one click.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Small and medium development and operations teams that want self-hosted deployment without the complexity of large enterprise tools.

• Software companies that need multiple environments (development, testing, production) and clear release tracking.

• Managed service providers and agencies that manage deployments for multiple clients from a single dashboard.

• Teams that prefer to keep their data and infrastructure in their own environment rather than relying entirely on SaaS.

2) Differentiators (Why choose our product)

• Operational simplicity: one-command installation and a clear Arabic/English interface with no steep learning curve.

• Native release management: every deployment is tied to a version that can be rolled back and audited, with a readable changelog.

• Free-to-start model: the self-hosted edition is completely free for individual projects and small teams.

• Vendor independence: support for Docker, Kubernetes, and any container environment without technical lock-in.

• Secure by default: isolated secrets management, granular permissions, and a full audit log from the first release.

• Gradual scalability: starts as a simple deployment tool and grows into an integrated delivery platform without rebuilding.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Self-hosted installation with an initial web interface and quick setup.

2. Connect a Git repository and automatically build a container image.

3. Define deployment environments (development/testing/production) and their policies.

4. Create numbered releases with a changelog and metadata.

5. Deploy a release to a specific environment with status tracking.

6. Instantly roll back to a previous release with one click.

7. Manage secrets, basic permissions, and an audit log.

8. A service status monitoring dashboard and simple alerts via email or Webhook.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Always free: a self-hosted edition with all core features, no limit on the number of releases, for personal projects and teams of up to 3 users.

• Team plan (paid): advanced permissions, longer logs, additional integrations, and email support — at a low monthly price per user.

• Enterprise plan: single sign-on (SSO), advanced auditing, managed backups, and priority support.

• Optional managed services: ready-made cloud hosting for organizations that do not want to manage the server themselves.

• Revenue comes from upgrades and managed services, not from withholding core functionality.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Publish the open-source repository on GitHub with bilingual documentation and ready-made examples.

• Write a series of technical articles on Dev.to, Medium, and the product blog about safe deployment and release rollbacks.

• Short YouTube videos showing installation and deployment in under 10 minutes.

• Share the build journey in Reddit, Hacker News, and DevOps Discord communities.

• List the project in Awesome Self-Hosted, AlternativeTo, and Product Hunt.

• Launch a free edition on platforms such as GitHub Releases and Docker Hub with starter templates.

• Build a contributor program and rewards for early contributors to drive community growth.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: Ensure compliance with the licenses of the libraries used, choose a clear license for the project, and avoid any trademark or design similarity with existing products.

• Legal: Comply with data protection (GDPR) when storing deployment logs and secrets, and clarify the privacy policy.

• Technical: Supply chain security (SBOM, image signing, vulnerability scanning) to prevent secret leakage or compromised images.

• Technical: The complexity of supporting multiple Kubernetes environments and versions, with the risk of incompatibility.

• Technical: Ensure tenant isolation in the cloud edition and prevent data leakage between teams.

• Operational: Competition from mature tools, so focus must be on simplicity and user experience as a differentiator.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Madar

Marketing tagline: Publish your releases with confidence, and roll back with one click.
