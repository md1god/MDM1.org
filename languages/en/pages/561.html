نبض — Complete Project Plan

One alert, at the right time, without distraction.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, growth plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

1) Target Audience

• Busy professionals: People with tight schedules who find it difficult to track their appointments and bills.

• Frequent travelers: Those who travel between cities and need quick alerts before trips or deliveries.

• Small families: Those who need simple reminders for bills, deliveries, or important appointments without complexity.

2) Key Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• Focus on priority only: Sends one critical alert instead of overwhelming the user with messages.

• Minimal data: Requests very limited permissions and does not collect the entire mailbox or all contacts.

• One-click action: Offers quick action options via a single response, without needing to open a complex app.

• Privacy by default: Every requested permission is explained to the user, with the ability to disable it immediately.

3) Scope of First Version (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. [ ] Calendar integration: Monitor upcoming appointments in the next few hours.

2. [ ] Bill alerts: Remind before the due date of any predictable bill.

3. [ ] Travel alerts: Alert before departure or when a trip time changes.

4. [ ] Delivery alerts: Alert when a delivery is approaching or its time changes.

5. [ ] Priority filtering: Filter alerts to show only the most important ones for each period.

6. [ ] Quick action suggestion: Show a ready-to-execute response option directly.

7. [ ] Do Not Disturb mode: Pause alerts for a specific time with one click.

8. [ ] Short morning summary: Send only the top three items in a single morning message.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

· Plan · Price · Description ·

· Free to start · 0$ · Includes all tasks of the first version with a reasonable daily alert limit. ·

· Basic · 4.99$ / month · Higher daily limit, broader alerts, with no change to the privacy policy. ·

· Annual · 49.99$ / year · Same features as the Basic plan with a clear discount, while keeping the free plan available permanently. ·

Revenue model: Monthly or annual subscriptions only, with no sale of user data.

5) Global Expansion Plan Through Free Channels

• Free tool directories: Add to reputable free tool directories.

• Short educational content: Publish short videos or posts explaining how to avoid missing urgent tasks.

• Word of mouth: Simple user invitation system without expensive cash rewards.

• SEO FAQ: Create FAQ pages in English and Arabic to appear in free search results.

• Community participation: Provide helpful answers in productivity communities, focusing on the problem rather than direct promotion.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

· Type · Risk · Mitigation ·

· Legal · SMS laws and privacy regulations such as GDPR · Obtain explicit consent, minimize data, and have a clear privacy policy. ·

· Legal · Calendar and email provider restrictions on permissions · Request the narrowest scope of permissions and fully comply with each provider's policies. ·

· Technical · Delay or failure to send messages during peak times · Retry system, real-time monitoring, and a backup sending plan. ·

· Technical · Abuse or mass sending attacks · Rate limiting, phone number verification, and automatic detection of suspicious activity. ·

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Slogan

Proposed name: نبض

Marketing slogan: One alert, at the right time, without distraction.
