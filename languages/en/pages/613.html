Molingua — Complete Project Plan

One platform that converts and validates molecular data without losing meaning.

Complete plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market, and risks. Under construction, not yet launched.

1) Target audience

• Computational chemistry and cheminformatics researchers working daily with multiple molecular formats.

• Data and AI teams cleaning molecular datasets before training or analysis.

• Pharmaceutical labs and startups needing reliable conversion without building internal tools.

• University students and instructors in chemistry and informatics.

• Developers of ELN, LIMS, and scientific applications needing a ready-made API.

Problem: Molecular data spans dozens of formats, and conversion can silently lose stereochemistry, charges, or isotopes; traditional tools also require installation, programming, and expertise.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• In-browser, no-install conversion: drag a file, get results instantly, with optional local processing for privacy.

• Conversion safety score: reports what was preserved and lost, not a silent result that may be wrong.

• Chemical quality checker: detects bad valence, missing bonds, duplicate molecules, and unbalanced charges.

• Audit trail and versioning: track each conversion's source and changes, unlike traditional desktop tools.

• API-first: simple interface with usage keys and Python and JavaScript SDKs.

• Free to start: experience full value before paying, with a permanent free plan.

• Secure collaboration: expiring links and team-shareable reports.

• Built from scratch: all code and design are new, without copying code or interfaces from existing products.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Core engine converting among SDF, MOL, SMILES, InChI, PDB, and CSV while preserving stereochemistry, charges, and isotopes.

2. Drag-and-drop web interface and 2D molecule preview before/after conversion.

3. Quality checker showing potential errors with actionable fixes.

4. REST interface with API keys, usage limits, and an initial Python SDK.

5. Audit trail per operation: source file, format, version, and result.

6. Large-batch processing with downloadable error reports.

7. User account and dashboard showing usage and past reports.

8. Secure, expiring links for sharing conversion reports.

4) Pricing and revenue model

· Plan · Price · Limits ·

· Free · $0 · 300 operations/month, files up to 10 MB, basic conversion ·

· Pro · $15/month · 5,000 operations, larger batches, full audit trail ·

· Team · $79/month · 5 users, report sharing, permissions, priority support ·

· Enterprise · Custom · Internal/private deployment, SSO, SLA, training ·

Revenue: subscriptions, API overage, internal deployment licenses, and custom support/training. The free plan stays permanent to attract users and measure paid conversion.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Programmatic SEO: pages for common format pairs like “SDF to SMILES conversion,” with real examples and common-error explanations.

• GitHub: new SDKs and helper tools with Jupyter examples and integration guides.

• PyPI and npm: lightweight packages enabling integration in minutes.

• Scientific communities: practical guides in chemistry and data forums, without spam or mass messages.

• Universities: free educational edition and workshops for students and researchers.

• Public launch: Product Hunt, Hacker News, and specialized Reddit communities, with a live demo.

• Content: articles on conversion data loss, transparent comparisons, and short YouTube videos.

• Partnerships: free integrations with Jupyter tools and open-source lab systems.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Clean-room implementation from scratch; no copying code, docs, or protected interfaces from other products.

• Compliance with licenses for formats such as InChI and scientific database terms of use.

• Verify the name's trademark before launch and avoid medical or regulatory claims.

• Protect customers' private molecular data and comply with GDPR and export policies for sanctioned parties.

Technical:

• Maintaining full accuracy across formats, especially stereochemistry and complex organics.

• Browser performance with huge files and memory use.

• Evolving specifications and new formats.

• Upload security and prevention of malicious files or resource abuse.

• Extensive benchmark tests before trusting conversion results.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Molingua

Tagline: One platform that converts and validates molecular data without losing meaning.
