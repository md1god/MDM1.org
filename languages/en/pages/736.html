Sutoor — Complete Project Plan

Convert your bank statement into a clean Excel spreadsheet in your browser, without your file ever leaving your device.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, business model, distribution, risks. Under construction, not launched.

Product plan: bank statement converter to clean spreadsheets

1) Target audience

• Freelancers/small businesses (1–20 staff): 2–6 PDF statements/mo; export for accountant/invoicing. E.g. designer, 180 txn/mo, 25 min/statement.

• Accounting offices/bookkeepers: 15–40 clients, 30–80 statements/mo; save 20 min each = 10–25 h/mo.

• SMBs without ERP: one finance staff reconciles statement/invoices; needs unified file for Excel/Sheets.

• Budget users/expats: multi-currency (USD/EUR/AED); categorize spend without cloud.

• Loan/visa applicants (read-only): income/liability summaries; no changes to official doc.

• Automation teams/devs: local conversion library; no financial files to external servers.

2) Distinctive points

• 100% local browser: pdf.js + WebAssembly; no upload, offline PWA, lower compliance, no server cost.

• Verifiable accuracy: per-row confidence; auto reconciliation; alert on 1-cent diff/duplicate.

• Fast review grid: editable, highlight suspicious; bulk fix dates/decimals/negative parens in seconds.

• Multi-language/direction: EN/FR/AR (RTL); unify dates; extract currency/balance from varying columns.

• Free from start: 3 statements or 30 pages/mo, no card, full export; pay for volume/templates/advanced.

• Speed: 20-page (≈400 txn) in 10–30 s vs 15–25 min manual.

3) First-version scope (max 8)

1. PWA drag-drop PDF UI, local parsing, "file never leaves device" indicator.

2. Column detection + templates (8–10: Chase, HSBC, Barclays, Gulf/European).

3. Schema: date, description, deposit, withdrawal, balance, currency; merge multi-line descriptions.

4. Review/edit grid, confidence, highlight unmatched.

5. Reconciliation, duplicate, date-sequence; exportable error report.

6. XLSX/CSV export + clipboard, preserve decimals/symbols.

7. Local free usage portal (counter) + offline paid licensing via signed key.

8. Trilingual landing (EN/AR/FR) with anonymized samples pre-signup.

4) Pricing and profit model

• Free: 3 statements or 30 pages/mo, full export, no card/watermark.

• Pro: $9/mo or $79/yr (27% off); unlimited, batch, custom templates, local settings.

• Accounting office: $29/mo for 5 seats/client workspaces; usage logs.

• Enterprise/API: $199/mo library license or $0.02/page.

• Economics: local → near-zero marginal; margin >90%. Target 3–5%: 20k users, 4% = 800 × $10 ≈ $8k/mo; team growth/annual plans.

• Friction: 14-day Pro trial locally; 30-day refund.

5) Global distribution via free channels

• Programmatic SEO: 150+ pages "Convert [Bank] statement to Excel/CSV" + comparisons/guides; high intent, low competition.

• Free tools: anonymized sample gen, balance calc, first 10 pages free; upgrade CTA.

• Communities: Show HN, Product Hunt, Reddit (r/Bookkeeping, r/smallbusiness, r/excel) helpful not ads; answer Qs with links.

• Short video: 30–60 s YouTube Shorts/TikTok/LinkedIn "messy PDF → clean spreadsheet" 30 s, two langs.

• Partnerships: accounting/bookkeeping influencers; open-source analysis lib on GitHub.

• Localization/referrals: AR/FR/ES pages; free month per friend; 20% commission accountants.

6) Risks (legal/technical)

• Sensitive data: clear privacy, anonymized stats off, GDPR/CCPA, no file/desc storage.

• Misuse: block PDF editing, issue marked "converted copy", strict terms/legal warning.

• Trademarks: bank names descriptive only, no logos/sponsorship.

• Accuracy: scanned needs Tesseract.js OCR, lower; warn + quality score; review before tax.

• Performance: 100+ pages may use memory; batch + Web Worker; test Chrome/Safari/Firefox.

• Competition: low barrier; moat via templates, review UX, trust, open lib.

7) Name/tagline

Proposed name: Sutoor. Tagline: Convert your bank statement into a clean Excel spreadsheet in your browser, without your file ever leaving your device.
