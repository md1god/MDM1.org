VoyageHub — Complete Project Plan

A single platform that turns every travel consultation into a profitable and organized journey.

Full execution plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, go‑to‑market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and not yet launched.

1) Target Audience

• Independent travel consultants managing 10‑30 clients per month who need a single tool for inquiries, itineraries, and bookings.

• Small travel agencies (2‑5 employees) looking to automate commissions and track payments without high costs.

• Custom trip organizers (weddings, adventure, medical tourism) who need a white‑label client portal bearing their brand.

• Remote travel professionals wanting real‑time data sync across phone and computer.

• Newcomers to the field who want a 14‑day free trial before committing financially.

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• Arabic‑English RTL‑ready interface with support for local currencies (SAR, AED, BHD).

• Dynamic pricing engine that automatically calculates commission by provider type (flights, hotels, insurance).

• Self‑service client portal allowing schedule changes, document uploads, and payment via Stripe/PayPal.

• Daily financial reports generated as PDF/Excel and emailed automatically.

• Native integration with Gmail/Outlook to turn inquiry emails into tasks with one click.

3) Scope of Version 1 (Maximum 8 Tasks)

• Central dashboard showing open inquiries, pending bookings, and due payments.

• Step‑by‑step trip creation wizard (select destination, dates, providers, pricing).

• Client management module with custom fields, communication log, and automated follow‑up reminders.

• White‑label client portal supporting Magic Link login and passport uploads.

• Configurable commission engine (flat rate, flat + tiered) with monthly report export.

• Secure payment integration (Stripe, PayPal, Apple Pay) with automatic invoice reconciliation.

4) Pricing & Revenue Model

• “Free Forever” plan for a single user: up to 5 trips/month, basic client portal, PDF reports.

• “Professional” plan $19/month/seat: unlimited trips, advanced commissions, email integration, priority support.

• “Team” plan $49/month for 5 seats: shared analytics dashboard, role permissions, limited API (1,000 requests/day).

• Transaction fee 0.5 % on each portal payment (deducted from commission).

• Partner program: 20 % recurring commission for each referred user who subscribes to a paid plan.

5) Global Go‑to‑Market Plan Using Free Channels

• Publish weekly SEO articles in Arabic and English on “Travel Consultation Automation” with case studies.

• Short 60‑second videos on TikTok/Reels showing trip creation in 3 minutes.

• Participate in specialized forums (TripAdvisor Community, Travel Agent Forum) and answer technical questions.

• “Product Ambassador” program granting lifetime free subscription in exchange for 3 genuine reviews on G2/Capterra.

• Partner with local tourism associations (e.g., Gulf Travel Agents Association) to deliver free Zoom workshops.

6) Risks (Legal & Technical)

• Compliance with data protection laws (GDPR, Saudi PDPL) → AES‑256 encryption, audit logs, automatic deletion policy after 24 months.

• Payment provider licensing in each target country → quarterly legal review and terms‑of‑service updates.

• Dependence on external APIs (airline booking systems, hotels) → SLA monitoring, Circuit Breaker pattern, local backup storage.

• Payment fraud risk → integrate 3D Secure, fraud prevention rules (Stripe Radar), manual review for transactions > $10,000.

• Scalability at 10,000+ users → micro‑services architecture on Kubernetes, PostgreSQL with Read Replicas.

Proposed Name: VoyageHub

Tagline: A single platform that turns every travel consultation into a profitable and organized journey.
