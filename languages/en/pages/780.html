Luqma — Complete Project Plan

Photograph your dish and know your calories and ingredients in three seconds — free, from your browser.

Plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout, risks. In development; not launched.

1) Target audience

• Beginners (18–35): want meals understood without tables/scales; ~60% of manual trackers quit in 3 weeks due to tedious logging.

• Eating out: 5–10 meals/week at restaurants/universities, no reliable calorie data, no time to calculate ingredients.

• Amateur athletes: aim ~1.6 g protein/kg/day; need fat and carb breakdown, not one number.

• Medical patients: diabetes/hypertension under specialists; estimate is an aid only, with in-app notice: not medical advice.

• Limited-data users and trainers: one browser suffices, no install/account; trainers explain portions via photo.

2) Differentiators (why choose us)

• No account: 3 scans daily from home page; email only for long-history saving or export.

• Speed: under 3 s from upload to result, with visible progress indicator against perceived waiting.

• Transparency: confidence % (e.g. 78%), per-item detail, "smaller/larger" slider adjusting portion and numbers at once.

• Improves with use: consented corrections refine that dish next time, avoiding repeat errors.

• Privacy and locality: photos deleted in 24 h, no training by default, plate-size calibration per region, 3 languages.

3) First-release scope (max 8 tasks)

Ten weeks, three people, eight tasks in six packages. Out of scope: watch syncing, social network, medical guidance.

1. Upload: camera, drag-and-drop or paste, 8 MB limit, 24 h deletion notice (Task 1).

2. Vision engine: (a) identify up to 100 common items with probability each (Task 2); (b) convert items to calories/ingredients (Task 3).

3. Portion: visual reference (spoon/plate) and ±30% slider with instant recalculation (Task 4).

4. Result card: calories, protein, fats, carbohydrates, confidence %, "incorrect item" button (Task 5).

5. Correction and history: (a) three-click edit saved (Task 6); (b) local 7-day history via IndexedDB, no account (Task 7).

6. Subscription: pricing page, payment gateway, three-scan daily limit, two-click cancellation (Task 8).

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free forever: 3 scans daily and 7-day on-device history; tryable without a bank card.

• Plus: $6.99/month or $49/year (~$4.1/month): unlimited scans, 12-month history, CSV export.

• Family: $11.99/month for five separate accounts, shared portion dashboard, no photo sharing among them.

• Billing transparency: 7-day trial, renewal reminder 24 h before, two-click cancellation with email confirmation.

• Revenue mix: 70% subscriptions, 20% sponsorship of educational content outside results, 10% disclosed health-tool commissions.

• Example: 1,000 subscribers at $6.99 = $6,990/month; break-even at ~350 subscribers if a scan costs $0.002.

5) Global rollout via free channels

• Free search: 60 "How many calories in..." pages for popular dishes, from corrections, in Arabic, English, French.

• Short video: 30 15-second "before/after" clips, three weekly on short-form platforms; no competing brands.

• Small partners: 20 trainers with 5,000–50,000 followers, for free Plus and a tool mention in their content.

• Communities: answer nutrition questions in Telegram and Reddit groups and trainer forums; link only when needed.

• Web directories: list the product on launch platforms and alternative directories, with one screenshot and a "works in browser" note.

• Retention: weekly newsletter and free "Read Your Plate" webinar biweekly, targeting 200 sign-ups and 5% conversion to Plus.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Medical/legal: fixed phrase "approximate estimate, not medical advice" on result card and usage page; no therapeutic or guaranteed-number claims.

• Privacy: explicit consent for image processing, deletion in 24 h, export/deletion rights, GDPR and CCPA compliance via auditable log.

• Children: block use under 13; parental consent for 13–16 in strict regions.

• Accuracy: expected 20–30% error in mixed dishes or poor lighting; show an approximate range, not a final number, with user correction.

• Bias: model bias toward one cuisine; addressed with regional item library and local calibration for at least five regions pre-launch.

• Technology and cost: compress images to 800 pixels and 150 KB for 3G, check file type/size, fallback provider layer against inference outages.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Luqma

Tagline: Photograph your dish, know calories and ingredients in 3 seconds — free, from your browser.
