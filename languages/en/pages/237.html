Raseef — Complete Project Plan

Deploy your open-source apps on your own server in minutes, without cloud complexity.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a self-hosting platform for running open-source applications

The idea in brief: a single simplified dashboard that lets anyone put an open-source web app online on a private server (VPS) within minutes, without needing deep expertise in DevOps or Docker.

1) Target audience

• Individual developers and side-project owners who want simple, cheap hosting.

• Small teams and startups that cannot bear the costs of managed cloud.

• Web agencies and freelancers who manage sites for multiple clients.

• Non-technical users who want to run WordPress or open-source tools themselves.

• Educational institutions and associations that prefer full control over their data.

2) Points of distinction (why choose our product)

• One-command install: a full install on any ordinary VPS via a single container, with no need for Kubernetes.

• Ready template store: a catalog of open-source apps (blogs, stores, analytics tools) deployed with one click.

• Fully Arabic interface with RTL support: a priority for a market neglected by competitors.

• Free to start: a fully open-source free version for self-use.

• Works without external connectivity: user data stays on their server, with no traffic passing through our servers.

• Focus on simplicity: a clean dashboard that hides the complexity of networking, certificates, and backups.

• Light on resources: runs on a small-memory server (1–2GB), unlike heavy alternatives.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. One-command installer via Docker Compose that sets up the dashboard and the agent on the server.

2. Web control panel with secure authentication and user and role management.

3. Running apps from ready Docker templates, stopping them, and updating them.

4. Automatically linking domains with HTTPS certificates via Let's Encrypt.

5. App environment variables and secrets through a secure graphical interface.

6. Periodic backup and restore for containers and databases.

7. Simple monitoring indicators (CPU, memory, container status, domain status).

8. An initial template store with 15–20 popular open-source apps.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free tier (Free/Self-hosted): open source, unlimited on the user's server — a growth and trust tool.

• Cloud tier (Cloud Starter): a monthly subscription for managed hosting on our servers for those who do not want to manage the server.

• Agencies plan: tiers based on the number of sites/clients with billing and white-label capability.

• Enterprise support (Enterprise): SLA technical support, SSO integration, and paid security auditing.

• Template store: an optional commission on templates and add-ons from external developers.

5) Global rollout plan with free channels

• GitHub: an open-source repository with Arabic and English documentation and a license attractive to contributors.

• Product Hunt and Hacker News: a coordinated launch with a filmed demo.

• Reddit and communities: r/selfhosted, r/DevOps, Arabic groups on Discord/Telegram.

• YouTube and blogs: "Deploy your app in 5 minutes" tutorials in Arabic and English for search engines.

• Long-term SEO: pages for each app template + hosting comparisons.

• Ambassadors and rewards program: badges and perks for those who create tutorials or free impressions.

• Dev.to / Medium / Arabic platforms: writing practical experiences and real use cases.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Respecting the licenses of apps and templates listed in the store (GPL/Apache/commercial).

• Responsibility for hosting user content; the need for clear terms of use and a privacy policy.

• Compliance with GDPR and data protection laws when providing the cloud tier.

• Not using trademarks or the names of other projects without permission.

Technical:

• Complexity of container version conflicts and app upgrades; solution: exported, version-pinned templates.

• Security of exposed servers and vulnerabilities; solution: automatic updates and hardening of default settings.

• Resource consumption on weak devices; solution: resource limits per container.

• Backup continuity and data conflicts; solution: periodic restore testing.

• Resource burn against the free model; solution: a clear separation between self-hosting and the paid cloud tier.
