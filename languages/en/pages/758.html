Loom — Complete Project Plan

One platform that weaves your sales, inventory, and accounting into a clear system that grows with your business.

Execution plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, rollout, risks. Under development; not yet launched.

1) Target Audience

• SMEs (5–200 staff): trade, distribution, professional services using scattered tools; e.g., food distributor, 25 staff, 800 items, 120 invoices/month.

• Traditional and digitally native store owners: sales, inventory, accounting in one place; e.g., new online store, 300 orders/month, 8–10% manual count errors.

• Ops and finance teams in growing firms: accountants and ops managers spend 10+ hours/week reconciling spreadsheets and siloed systems; need instant reports.

• Multi-branch or multi-currency firms: 2–5 branches, one daily performance view, different access per team.

• Low-cost, easy-to-adopt buyers: tech budget up to $200/month; start free, scale gradually without tech complexity.

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• Fast setup, simple UX: guided wizard, first account in under 30 minutes; clear UI cuts new-hire training to two hours.

• Modular design: enable Sales, Purchases, Inventory, Accounting as needed—start with one or two, expand later.

• Real-time visibility: one screen for daily sales, cash flow, critical stock items; alerts below a limit (e.g., 10 units).

• Cloud + self-hosting: start in the cloud, move data to a private server later—speed or control.

• Spreadsheet migration: smart CSV/Excel import, common-error checks; transition cut from two weeks to 2–3 days.

• Modern, secure stack: scalable, encryption in transit and at rest, granular role permissions, audit log for sensitive actions.

3) First Version Scope (Max 8 Tasks)

1. Accounts, companies, permissions: users, one company on free plan, basic roles (Manager, Accountant, Sales Employee).

2. Customer and supplier directory: contacts, addresses, payment terms, simple transaction history.

3. Product catalog and inventory: items, units, prices; one warehouse; minimum-stock alerts.

4. Sales cycle: quotations to invoices, partial/full payments, invoice status.

5. Purchasing cycle: purchase orders, goods receipt with auto inventory update, supplier invoices.

6. Automated simplified accounting: invoice-linked entries, general journal, basic monthly profit and loss report.

7. Main dashboard: total sales, expenses, available cash, best-selling items; daily updates.

8. Import, export, initial migration: customers, products, opening balances from CSV/Excel; export core reports.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free (Free): 3 users, one company, 1,000 documents/year (invoices/orders), 2 GB storage; trials and very early startups.

• Growth ($15/user/month): min 5 users, advanced reports, email support within 24 hours, basic integrations, flexible monthly bill.

• Business ($29/user/month): up to 5 branches, advanced permissions, API access, dedicated daily backup, priority support within 8 business hours.

• Self-hosting ($499/year): annual updates and maintenance license for own servers, install guide, initial support for one setup session.

• Professional services: remote training $200/session (2 hours); custom migration from $300 by data volume and complexity.

5) Global Outreach via Free Channels

• SEO and educational content: 2 articles/week, 4 guides/month in Arabic and English targeting "small store inventory management" and "simplifying customer invoicing".

• Professional communities and social platforms: weekly tips in LinkedIn, Facebook, Reddit owner groups; short case studies (save 5 hours/week).

• Referral program: one free month for referrer and new customer after first paid subscription; in-product tracking dashboard.

• Incubator and association partnerships: free monthly online workshop (45 minutes) for entrepreneurs, with a ready demo.

• Educational video channel: 20 short videos (5–8 minutes) in 6 months on setup and daily tasks; build trust, lower entry barrier.

• Software and launch communities: release updates, feedback in global tech communities, monthly FAQ page.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Tax and invoicing rules vary by country: mitigate via configurable tax engine and local templates released after specialist review in each target market.

• Data protection and privacy: GDPR and similar duties; comprehensive encryption, defined retention (e.g., delete inactive data after 12 months on request), regional storage when expanding.

• Information security: hacking or data leaks; two-factor authentication, encrypted daily backups, penetration tests every 3 months.
