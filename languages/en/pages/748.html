Fiscora — Complete Project Plan

Turn your tax return in Luxembourg from overwhelming complexity into clear steps in minutes.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under development and has not launched yet.

An original product plan to simplify tax filing for individuals in Luxembourg

1) Target Audience

• Resident employees: Individuals with annual salaries between €35,000 and €90,000 who need to file their annual tax return without accounting expertise and want to know the amount due or refund in less than 10 minutes.

• Newcomers and multilingual users: Professionals who recently moved for work and have difficulty understanding official forms, benefiting from a simplified interface in English, French, and German with clear explanations of terms.

• Families and couples: Families with children, housing obligations, or care costs who need to compare individual versus joint filing scenarios to determine the less costly option.

• Mixed-income earners: Employees who combine a fixed salary with limited freelance income (e.g., up to €15,000 extra per year) and need organized input for sources and basic deductions.

• First-time filers: Graduates and new employees who need a step-by-step guide that reduces common mistakes such as forgetting transport deductions or pension contributions.

2) Key Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• Smart, concise wizard: Transforms the complex tax return into just 15–25 questions through conditional logic that shows only the questions relevant to the user's situation instead of displaying long forms.

• Instant and transparent estimate: Displays a real-time estimate of the amount due or refund with a simplified breakdown (e.g., €500 transport deduction or €1,200 pension contribution) to increase confidence before filing.

• Multilingual experience: Full support for EN/FR/DE from launch, with short instruction formats and clear numerical examples suitable for non-specialists.

• Privacy and security to European standards: Encryption in transit and at rest (TLS 1.3 and AES-256), hosting within the European Union with adherence to GDPR principles and minimized data retention periods.

• Proactive error checking: Automatic checks for inconsistencies and missing fields (such as a missing identification number or inconsistent income) before generating the final file.

• Frictionless free start: Ability to begin and calculate an estimate for free without a payment card, with upgrades only when advanced features such as full export are needed.

3) First Version Scope (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. Interactive filing wizard: Covers the most common cases (resident employee, single/married, standard deductions) through a sequential question flow.

2. Tax calculation engine: Applies current-year tax brackets and basic deductions with a structure that allows annual parameter updates.

3. Secure account system: Login via email or Magic Link and encrypted data storage with the ability to delete the account within 30 days.

4. Draft saving and resumption: Automatic progress saving with the ability to return and complete the filing across multiple sessions.

5. Formatted PDF export: Generates a clear summary and a data file ready for printing or personal archiving.

6. Multilingual interface: Provides content and interface in EN/FR/DE with a simplified glossary of tax terms.

7. Automatic validation: Immediate alerts for errors or illogical values before final export.

8. Landing page and privacy analytics: Measures registration and completion rates using privacy-friendly analytics.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free plan (€0): One filing per year including estimate calculation, draft saving, and simplified summary export, to attract new users and lower the barrier to trying.

• Personal plan (€19 per filing): Full PDF export, unlimited draft saving, and priority email support within 48 hours.

• Family plan (€29 per joint filing): Support for joint filing with a quick comparison between individual and joint scenarios to clarify the financial difference.

• Launch offer: 50% discount for the first 1,000 paid filings during the first season to build a user base and early reviews.

• Additional revenue later: Optional referrals to certified advisors for complex cases with a 10–15% commission, without selling or sharing user data for advertising purposes.
