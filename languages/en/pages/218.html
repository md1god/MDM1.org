Aklti — Complete Project Plan

Your calories in your pocket, and your data is yours alone.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, growth plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

An original product plan: a privacy-respecting calorie tracker

1) Target audience

• Individuals interested in fitness and weight management who do not trust apps that collect their data.

• Users in privacy-sensitive markets (Europe, Canada) looking for a free, ad-free alternative.

• Beginners who want a simple interface: quick meal logging without complexity or mandatory subscriptions.

• Developers and open-source enthusiasts who want to self-host their data.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Privacy first: works fully without an account, data is stored locally on the device by default, with optional end-to-end encrypted sync.

• No ads and no tracking: no spyware analytics and no data selling; revenue comes from optional features, not from the user.

• Faster experience: log a meal with a single gesture, and a local, searchable food database that works offline.

• Free import and export: an open format for user data that prevents lock-in and allows migration without loss.

• Self-hostable: a lightweight server version for those who want family or private sync without a third-party cloud.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Set up the profile, calorie goal, and macronutrients (protein/fat/carbohydrates).

2. Search a built-in local food database and add an item in under 5 seconds.

3. Log daily meals by category (breakfast/lunch/dinner/snacks).

4. A daily dashboard showing remaining calories and macronutrient distribution.

5. Create custom foods and recipes and reuse them.

6. Barcode scanning to auto-fill nutrition values.

7. Simple weekly progress indicators (weight + average calories).

8. Export/import data in an open format, and full offline operation.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Completely free to start: all core features are free with no usage limits.

• Optional "Supporter" plan: luxury features only (themes, encrypted cloud sync, multi-device backup, advanced export) at a symbolic monthly/yearly price.

• A lifetime offer for early users to build an early revenue base.

• An explicit pledge: absolutely no ads and no data selling; revenue is transparent and comes from optional features and donations.

5) Global growth plan through free channels

• Publishing on open-source software platforms and free community app repositories.

• Short video content (logging a meal in seconds) on social networks.

• Privacy and fitness communities on discussion platforms, with honest participation without promotional spam.

• Collaboration with privacy and digital fitness bloggers for independent reviews.

• Educational content in Arabic and English that improves search engine visibility.

• A university ambassador program and fitness clubs in exchange for paid plan benefits.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: the sensitivity of health data requires compliance with data protection regulations (such as GDPR); solution: a design that minimizes data collection and sync with explicit consent.

• Food database accuracy: nutrition value errors may mislead the user; solution: trusted sources and clear labeling of community-contributed data and its review.

• Misuse of barcodes and recipes: incorrect content; solution: community moderation and correction reports.

• Technical: device differences and limited local storage; solution: a compressed database and lazy sync.

• Financial sustainability: difficulty relying on donations; solution: diversifying revenue sources through optional features without breaching privacy.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Aklti

Tagline: Your calories in your pocket, and your data is yours alone.
