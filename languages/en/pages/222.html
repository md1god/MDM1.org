Naqi — A Complete Project Plan

Know what's in your food at a glance, and decide with confidence.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, target audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is currently under development and has not yet been launched.

Product Plan: Smart Food Scanning App

1) Target Audience

• Parents who want to choose better food for their children.

• People with diabetes, high blood pressure, and food allergies (gluten, lactose, nuts).

• Athletes and those interested in building a healthy body and tracking their protein and sugar intake.

• Vegetarians and those following special diets.

• Shoppers in emerging markets where food data transparency is limited.

2) Unique Selling Points (Why Choose Our Product)

• Built from scratch using modern technology (Flutter/Dart), with a native interface that supports Arabic and RTL.

• Offline-first: works without an internet connection using a local database on the device.

• Scans food labels using the camera and OCR technology when a barcode is missing or damaged.

• A personalized health indicator that adapts to the user's allergies and dietary needs, rather than a generic one.

• Privacy by design: no mandatory account, and no selling of user data.

• Reliable crowdsourcing with user reviews and corrections.

• A free-to-start model that lowers the barrier to entry and accelerates adoption.

3) Scope of the First Version (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. Scan barcodes using the camera, with a manual entry option as a fallback.

2. A local food database with fast search functionality.

3. Product card: nutritional values, ingredients, and allergens.

4. A personalized health indicator with custom alerts for allergies and dietary restrictions.

5. Community-based product additions and corrections with a simple review process.

6. Offline functionality with automatic synchronization when back online.

7. A bilingual interface (Arabic/English) with full RTL support.

8. Favorites, history, and quick product sharing.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Freemium: unlimited scans, full access to the database, and the ability to add products for free.

• Optional Pro subscription: advanced nutritional reports, family tracking, data export, and expanded offline scanning.

• Anonymized data sponsorship: aggregated, non-personal insights for researchers and food organizations, but only with explicit consent.

• Community donations and support from health institutions.

• No intrusive ads and no sale of personal data.

5) Global Distribution Plan Through Free Channels

• Publish the source code as open-source on GitHub with regular releases.

• Distribute for free via Google Play, App Store, and F-Droid.

• Create SEO content and articles about reading food labels in Arabic and English.

• Engage with nutrition and health communities on Reddit and specialized forums.

• Launch a community challenge ("Data Challenge") to enrich the product database.

• Collaborate with health bloggers, nutrition colleges, and schools.

• Launch on Product Hunt, Hacker News, and independent tech channels.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

Legal:

• Health advice liability: a clear disclaimer must be provided, stating that the app is for informational purposes only and not a substitute for medical advice.

• Data protection (GDPR/CCPA) when collecting any data, with explicit consent and data minimization.

• Compliance with licenses for any open data used (Open Database License), as well as product trademarks.

• Avoid misrepresenting medical expertise or making unsubstantiated health claims.

Technical:

• Database coverage and quality, as well as accuracy of automatic classification.

• OCR accuracy for labels in low-light conditions or with different languages.

• Complexity of syncing in offline mode and conflict resolution.

• Performance on low-end devices.

• Ongoing maintenance and prevention of maliciously added incorrect data.

7) Proposed Name and Tagline

Proposed Name: Naqi

Tagline: Know what's in your food at a glance, and decide with confidence.
