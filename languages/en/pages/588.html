Rawi — Full Project Plan

We turn every customer conversation into a completed deal, with AI that respects your privacy and runs on your infrastructure.

Digital project implementation plan: concept, audience, product, revenue, go-to-market, risks. Under construction, not launched.

1) Audience

• SMBs selling via chat, mainly Middle East, Latin America, South Asia, Africa; sales teams 3–50.

• Agencies/BPOs with multiple clients' WhatsApp numbers; one multi-tenant white-label deployment.

• Privacy-sensitive healthcare/education/finance: self-hosting, local data residency, GDPR/LGPD/PDPL.

• API/MCP teams rejecting per-seat pricing/lock-in.

2) Advantages

• Free start: open-source, self-hostable core, unlimited seats, own LLM key.

• Arabic-first: RTL UI, dialects, templates, multilingual.

• Channels: official WhatsApp Cloud API first; later Instagram, Telegram, email, site widget, no rebuild.

• AI agents: knowledge-base answers, sources, confidence limits, block policies, human handoff.

• MCP-native: scoped CRM tools, approvals, audit logs, external agents.

• Multi-tenant day one: one deployment for agency/clients; isolated data/permissions.

• Lightweight: one-command Docker Compose on a small server; no Kubernetes.

• Ownership: full export, published pricing, compliance logs/approvals.

3) MVP

1. Multi-tenant workspaces: email/OAuth login, roles/permissions, invites, audit log.

2. Unified inbox: WhatsApp Cloud API text/media/templates; assignment, tags, notes, SLA timers.

3. CRM core: contacts/companies, custom fields, pipelines, deals, tasks; CSV import/export, deduplication.

4. AI agent studio: RAG knowledge base, tone/policies, sources, confidence threshold, human handoff, testing.

5. MCP gateway: scoped tools, sensitive-action approvals, rate limits, audit logs.

6. Automation: triggers/actions, sequences, business hours, templates, opt-outs.

7. Analytics/compliance: sales funnel, response time, resolution rate, approvals, retention, export/deletion.

8. Ops: Docker Compose install, backup/restore, upgrades, health checks, demo data, docs.

4) Pricing/Revenue

• Community: free forever, unlimited seats, own LLM key, community support.

• Cloud free start: 1 workspace, 2 seats, 1 WhatsApp number, 500 AI replies/mo.

• Starter — $29/workspace/mo: 3 numbers, 5 users, 2,000 AI replies, basic automation.

• Growth — $79/mo: 5 numbers, 15 users, 8,000 AI replies, advanced reports, integrations.

• Scale — $199/mo: fair-use numbers/users, SSO, compliance logs, priority support.

• Agency White-label — $299/mo: 10 client workspaces, white label, billing.

• Enterprise: supported self-hosting, SLA, training, security review; custom pricing.

Revenue extras: AI usage margin, managed WhatsApp onboarding, SLA support, paid integrations, affiliates. 20% annual discount; PPP pricing.

5) Global Go-to-Market: Free Channels

• GitHub/open source: AGPLv3 core, commercial license, README, demo video, one-click deploy on Railway/Render/Coolify.

• Multilingual SEO: Arabic, English, Spanish, Portuguese "self-hosted WhatsApp CRM," comparisons, templates, ROI calculators.

• MCP registries/awesome lists: server, tools; n8n, Make, Zapier.

• Communities: Meta developer groups, WhatsApp Business forums, r/selfhosted, r/sales, Indie Hackers, Product Hunt, Show HN.

• Visual: YouTube/TikTok tutorials, workshops, Q&A.

• Agency program: white-label demo, recurring commission, partner growth.

• Local: incubators, chambers of commerce, university clubs, Arabic/Spanish seminars.

• Referral loop: lead-magnet templates, referrals, public roadmaps.

6) Risks

Legal

• Meta/WhatsApp: official Cloud API only, consent, approved templates, sending limits. Unofficial bridges risk bans; isolate/disclose.

• Privacy: DPAs, transit/rest encryption, regional residency, subprocessors. No training on customer data.

• AI liability: human handoff, banned uses, content filters, reviewable logs.

• Licensing/IP: AGPL/commercial compatibility, contributor agreement, trademark check.

• Anti-spam: consent, sending limits, stop policy.

Technical

• Tenant isolation: logical isolation, separate encryption keys, leak tests.

• LLM cost/latency: provider abstraction, caching, fallbacks, tenant limits.

• WhatsApp outages/API changes: resilient integration, retries, quality checks.

• Agent security: prompt-injection defense, tool limits, Webhook SSRF defense, central secrets.

• Deployment/upgrades: backups, rollback, migration tests.

• Competitor migration: import, cleanup, docs.

Name: Rawi
