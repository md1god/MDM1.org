Qirtas — Full Project Plan

From idea to a book ready for publishing on KDP and Etsy, with professional Arabic formatting.

Full execution plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, launch plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

1) Target audience

• Arab authors and beginners wanting to turn an idea into an organized book without formatting or publishing experience.

• KDP and Etsy sellers producing low-content books, coloring books, and practical guides.

• Teachers, trainers, and consultants turning their content into books or training guides.

• Small publishers and marketing teams needing fast PDF, EPUB, and DOCX output with a unified visual identity.

• Non-Arabic-speaking creators wanting multilingual templates with strong Arabic and RTL support.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Arabic first: diacritics, RTL, and professional chapter formatting — not just a translated interface.

• Publish-readiness check: automated verification of bleed, margins, image resolution, font embedding, page count, spine, and barcode before export.

• One source, multiple outputs: export the same book to print-ready PDF, EPUB, and DOCX without reformatting.

• Truly printable coloring books: pure line art with no grayscale, with resolution and margin control.

• Integrated cover studio: templates, typography, generated images, 3D preview, and ready-made Etsy ad files.

• Rights transparency: content ownership, commercial use, and AI-generated content disclosure.

• Real free start: first project and basic export available without a payment card.

3) First-version scope (8 tasks max)

1. Sign-up, login, projects dashboard: create a book, autosave, version history, restore copies.

2. Book outline generator: set audience, tone, language, chapter count; produce editable title and chapter elements.

3. Arabic chapter editor: RTL, word count, paraphrasing, paragraph expansion, terminology consistency.

4. Cover and coloring studio: cover templates, text editor, image generation, page-to-line-art conversion.

5. Print-ready PDF export: KDP and Etsy settings, ready sizes, bleed, margins, font embedding.

6. EPUB and DOCX export: automatic TOC, page breaks, compressed images, content consistency across formats.

7. Pre-publish checker: dimensions, resolution, fonts, spine, barcode area, with an actionable error report.

8. Free plan and billing: project limits, generation credits, removable watermark, instant upgrade.

4) Pricing and revenue model

· Plan · Price · What you get ·

· Free · $0 · One active project, 30 monthly generation credits, PDF export with a small badge, basic templates ·

· Author · $12/month · 5 projects, 300 credits, watermark-free PDF/EPUB/DOCX export, commercial license, publish checker ·

· Studio · $29/month · 25 projects, 1500 credits, coloring books, advanced covers, priority processing, KDP readiness reports ·

· Book ticket · $19 · One complete book in all formats, no subscription, for one-time payers ·

• Revenue: recurring subscriptions + extra credit bundles + paid template licensing.

• Controlled variable costs: credit caps, queues, right-sized model per task.

• Free-to-paid conversion: triggered by watermark removal, multi-format export, or print checks.

• Later: a template marketplace with designer revenue share, without affecting version one.

5) Global launch plan with free channels

• Programmatic SEO: pages per book type, language, and KDP size, e.g. "how to publish a coloring book" and "EPUB formatting mistakes."

• Standalone free tools: cover size calculator, bleed checker, page-to-coloring converter, title generator.

• YouTube and TikTok: short tutorials from idea to ready file, before/after comparisons, no guaranteed earnings claims.

• KDP, Etsy, and author communities: practical sharing on Reddit, Facebook, Discord, Telegram; free templates, no spammy email.

• Product Hunt, Indie Hackers, Show HN: present the "publish-readiness checker" as an instantly tryable free tool.

• Free partnerships: universities, writing clubs, teacher groups, educational creators, for a limited free license.

• Referral loop: free credit for each friend who activates an account, plus a weekly newsletter with templates and publishing tips.
