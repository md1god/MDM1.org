Mifyar — Complete Project Plan

Know what AI sees in your store, and fix it before your customer does.

Plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue, rollout, risks. Building; not launched.

1) Target Audience

• Mid-sized independent stores (5 to 500 products): owners on WooCommerce or BigCommerce or Magento, selling in at least one marketplace. No tech team, yet expected in AI answers. Biggest data gap, fastest fix impact.

• Company e-commerce teams (2 to 10): catalog/channel managers updating prices/stock manually across 3 to 6 feeds (feeds). Weekly report flags missing fields before a customer sees a wrong answer.

• Agencies (100 to 2000 clients): manage 10 to 50 stores; want white-label recurring revenue, no custom tool. One dashboard, reports in their name.

• Multi-marketplace brands: Amazon, eBay, and own-store sellers, hurt by spec mismatches. Example: size "M" in store, "Medium" in feed; AI reads two products.

• Pre-launch startups: 20 products, incomplete policy pages. Get a "pre-launch check" in 10 minutes, not weeks.

• AI-search marketers: asking "How does AI see my store?" and needing a free tool to start and upgrade. Our first adoption channel.

2) Why Choose Our Product

• 6-platform scan: 18 queries to ChatGPT, Google AI Mode, Gemini, Copilot, Meta AI, Shop vs real product data. One report, not 6 tables.

• Explainable score, not opinion: 100 points / 6 axes (structured data 25, policies 15, options 15, stock 15, feed 20, identity 10). Deductions cite page text; users know where to click.

• Instant fixes: not "add schema" but full JSON-LD in store language/currency with gtin, sku, availability, priceCurrency. Paste into product template in minutes.

• Platform-neutral: no assumed structure; read public HTML, RSS, XML, CSV; 7 platforms, no commercial bias.

• Measures results: rescan every 7 days; change curve: "Visibility +12 after updating 14 fields." Links work to revenue.

• Free start, no card: 1 store, 20 pages weekly, PDF. No lock-in, hidden trial, or plugin.

3) Version 1 Scope (8 tasks max)

1. Light crawler: Python + Playwright, max 50 pages/20 sec, honors robots.txt and crawl-delay, logs user agent.

2. Structured data analyzer: scan JSON-LD, Microdata, RDFa for Product, Offer, AggregateRating, BreadcrumbList; flag missing/conflicting fields.

3. Policy checker: find shipping, returns, payment, privacy within 3 clicks of home; score return-window and shipping-fee clarity with a number.

4. Options/stock checker: compare 30 variants (color/size) across page and feed; alert on price or stock differences.

5. Feed Watcher: read XML/CSV on 4 paths, validate 12 required fields, alert after 48+ hours without update.

6. Dashboard: score, top 5 impact issues, "Copy fix" per issue, PDF/CSV export.

7. Fix generator: ld+json templates, llms.txt rules, editable return policy text, store language/currency, no direct client file edits.

8. Free account, weekly cycle: email signup, 1 free scan, rescan every 7 days, email alert at 5+ point score change.

4) Pricing & Revenue

• Free ($0): 1 store, 20 pages, weekly scan, PDF. Goal: 1,000 users in 3 months, zero ad spend.

• Starter ($19/month): 3 stores, 200 pages, daily scans, email alerts, 30-day history. For artisans and single-store owners.

• Growth ($59/month): 10 stores, 2,000 pages, scan per feed update, API, 3-competitor report. For marketing teams.

• Agency ($179/month): 50 stores, branded reports, 2 extra seats, support in 8 hours. Expected 35% of recurring revenue.

• Supporting revenue: 20% commission for 12 months on recommended hosting/feed tools, disclosed, no ranking or score impact.

• Profit rule: ~$0.01/scan (crawl + queries), >90% margin at $59/month. Goal: 400 paid subscribers by end of year 1, ~$21,000 annual recurring revenue.

5) Global Rollout, Free Channels

• Early-intent content: 30 articles on "Why doesn't my product appear in AI answers?" in 6 languages: Arabic, English, French, Spanish, German, Portuguese. Each starts with the free tool.

• Shareable mini-tools: "free schema checker for one page" and "feed/product page comparator", no signup, result link at report bottom. Best free acquisition channel.

• Communities/forums: 5 weekly answers in developer and store-owner communities, full advice, tool only last line. Goal: 200 answers in 6 months.

• Free channels: free listing in app directories of 4 store platforms, 5 referral deals with e-commerce consultants at 15%, no featured score placement.

• Open source for trust: simple CLI on GitHub scanning a local page, docs, MIT license. Try before sharing store data.
