Kawin — Complete Project Plan

Turn your idea into a playable, shareable game in minutes, no coding.

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market, risks. Under construction; not launched.

1) Target audience

• Hobbyists/content creators (16–34): turn quick ideas into short games for video audiences. Example: 60-second challenge tied to weekly trend; 1,000 shares in 48h.

• Teachers/trainers: turn lessons into interactive experiences without coding. Example: science teacher makes 10-question quiz in <15 min for 30 students.

• Marketers/small businesses: lightweight promotional games (Advergames) to boost campaigns. Example: points game with 10% discount coupon in 14-day campaign.

• Students/beginner devs: build prototypes to test gameplay ideas. Example: prototype time from 3 days to <30 min.

• Event organizers/digital communities: quick interactive activities for virtual/in-person events. Example: live competition via one link for 200 participants with real-time leaderboard.

2) Differentiators

• Precise conversational editing: modify elements (speed, enemies, win condition) via later text commands without rebuild; undo up to 20 steps/project.

• Consistent visual/audio identity: generate simple assets (characters, backgrounds, SFX) in one style (Pixel/Flat); coherent; file ≤2 MB.

• Instant publishing/smooth UX: public share link works in browser desktop/mobile no install; load <3s on 4G.

• Forkable/learnable templates: ready templates for common patterns (platformer, puzzle, racing, collection), customizable/forkable, with simplified logic explanation for beginners.

• Clearer ownership/control: export standalone HTML5; private projects in paid plans; transparent UGC rights policy.

• Frictionless free start: core features without card; limits allow publishing first 3 games and testing before upgrade.

3) First version scope (max 8)

1. Guided text creation: idea field + quick options (type, difficulty, duration) + examples in 3 languages (Arabic, English, Spanish).

2. HTML5 generation engine: description to playable browser game for two launch patterns: platformer and collection.

3. Initial asset generator: auto simple graphics/SFX in 2 styles (Pixel, Flat) with file-size optimization.

4. Instant preview/testing: built-in player with restart and basic runtime errors.

5. Iterative conversational editing: follow-up commands (e.g. "make jump higher", "add second level") applied <20s.

6. Accounts/project saving: email/Google sign-up; save up to 5 free projects; simple version history.

7. One-click sharing: public play link with landing page (title, description, "Play Now"), mobile-compatible.

8. Starter template library: 8 ready templates previewable and instantly customizable.

4) Pricing/revenue

• Free (always): 30 generations/month, 5 public projects, public links, small brand badge on play page.

• "Maker" ($8/mo): 300 generations/month, 30 projects, private projects, HTML5 export, priority queue.

• "Team/Education" ($25/mo per 5 seats): shared workspace, simple permissions, custom class/team templates, basic reports (sessions, avg play duration).

• "Studio" ($49/mo): higher generations under fair use, badge removal, custom subdomain.

• Future: template/asset marketplace; 70% creator / 30% platform; core features stay free.

5) Global go-to-market (free channels)

• Product communities: trial on discovery platforms with 60s idea-to-game video; target 500 registrations week 1.

• Repeated short content: 3 clips/week on YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels with "turn idea into game" challenges.

• Education partnerships: contact 50 teachers/bloggers; free lesson templates for documented experiences/case studies.

• Search visibility: 20 guide articles in 3 months targeting "create browser game without coding" and "design educational game quickly."

• Specialized communities: free templates + monthly challenges in indie dev/game creator communities; digital badges for best creations.

• Built-in referral: 10 extra generations per invited friend who publishes first game; max 50 extra/month/account.

6) Risks (legal/technical)

• IP infringement: users may imitate protected brands/characters; mitigate with filters for known names/trademarks and reporting/removal within 48h.

• Inappropriate/harmful content: excessive violence/abuse; mitigate with auto classification, block public publishing of violations, report button on play page.

• Data privacy/minor protection: GDPR/COPPA; mitigate by minimizing data, deletion within 30 days, appropriate consent.
