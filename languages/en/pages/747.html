Namaa — Complete Project Plan

Namaa unites farmers and researchers to measure each field's economic, social and environmental impact in its owners' hands.

New digital project plan: idea, audience, product, revenue, rollout, risks. In development, not launched.

1) Audience

• Small/medium farmers (1–5 ha): villages like Damietta, Bekaa, Kerala; 3 fields, paper notebook. Cost/ha unknown; cannot prove 20% less irrigation raised quality. Simple tools turn daily notes into comparable numbers.

• Researchers/graduates: soil researcher, 200 samples over 2 seasons, needs daily field data, not old tables. Volunteers collect samples under one protocol, saving 6 months of manual work.

• Cooperatives/rural bodies: 150-member cooperative wants an "improved seeds" program's effect on income and soil in one report, not 150 scattered views.

• Local decision-makers: municipality/agriculture directorate distributing 5,000 seedlings needs a map of biggest gains for next season's support.

• Consumers/local markets: city-market buyer wants proof of measured impact via a pack code opening a three-line summary.

2) Why our product

• Data stored in farmer's name; they choose who sees it. E.g. researcher gets "read without copy" 6 months, revocable in one tap.

• Before the season, farmer and researcher set 5 indicators, e.g. "price per kilo", "organic matter %", not blind templates.

• One dashboard: economic (profit/ha), social (work hours, women's share), environmental (water, biodiversity). Two same-crop fields: 18% profit, 25% water gap.

• Offline/local language: 500 readings on phone, synced later; 6 languages incl. Arabic, Swahili, Hindi; large buttons for sun use.

• Every formula published and explained; no black box trusted by one side, unclear to the other.

• Farmer pays zero; beneficiaries fund it; simplified annual budget published to keep trust.

3) First version (max 8 tasks)

1. Register farmer: phone, village, up to 3 free fields.

2. Custom "measure": 5 short weekly phone questions.

3. Link researcher to 50 farmers by invite code; agree indicators pre-season.

4. Offline collection; auto-sync on reconnection.

5. 3D dashboard: two-field comparison; profit per hectare.

6. Aggregated-location map + two-page local-language PDF; permissions center: who sees what, how long.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Farmers: free. 3 fields, 500 readings, unlimited reports, no card. Target 10,000 in year one, no paid ads.

• Researchers/universities: 1 account, 50 farmers, 30 days free; then $25/month or $240/year per 5-account lab.

• Institutions/companies: $1,200/year for custom dashboard and aggregate reports; 50% off non-profits.

• Training: one-day workshop, 20 farmers, $300 (grant-funded); cooperative indicator design, $500.

• Knowledge marketplace: with consent, anonymous aggregate reports sold; 70% to farmers' fund, 30% to platform.

• Grants/blended finance: 60% grants, 40% subscriptions by year three; audit every 6 months.

5) Free-channel global rollout

• 20 WhatsApp/Facebook groups in 5 countries; 60-second weekly lesson per measurement, not long newsletters.

• 10 agriculture faculties host "Design your indicator"; 10 ambassadors bring 20 farmers each.

• App packages and docs on GitHub; volunteers add languages or improve models via simple weekly issues.

• 30 YouTube/TikTok videos, "How do you know your field made a profit?", with direct sign-up links; 5,000 organic views by month six.

• 100 volunteers, 12 countries, 3 villages each; 10-page guide, monthly free Zoom.

• Free weekly "seasonal indicator" to farm sites and local radio for a mention; no ad spend.

6) Risks (legal/technical)

• GDPR/local law: consent every 12 months, exportable deletion log, same-region server where possible.

• Ownership agreement keeps rights with farmer; collective publication needs consent. Lost trust outweighs any loss.

• Round GPS to 5 km before public display, or a farm is traceable from an "aggregated" map.

• Sync must survive 3 days offline with no loss, or farmer returns to paper in week two.

• Reject "900 mm rain in one day"; flag values repeated 7 times, else comparisons mislead.

• Encryption at rest/in transit, role-based permissions, daily backups, 9-month reserve against grant gaps.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Namaa

Tagline: Namaa unites farmers and researchers to measure each field's economic, social and environmental impact in its owners' hands.
