Laqta — Complete Project Plan

Learn Japanese from one scene a day, with a tap and no dry memorization.

Complete execution plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, distribution, and risks. In development; not launched.

1) Audience

• Beginners (16–30, ~55% of demand): watch 3–5 anime episodes weekly, know under 20 words. Goal: grasp one spoken sentence, no full subtitles, in 30 days at 7 min/day while commuting.

• Active viewers (18–35): want a heard phrase's meaning in that same scene, live. Message: "Search what you heard, not what you recalled." Voice, Arabic, and English search.

• Travelers/students (20–40): trip in 3–6 months, need 300 daily phrases (restaurant, train, hotel, shopping). "90-Day Trip": 10 scenes/week, short Sunday quiz.

• Teachers/clubs: classes of 8–20 need ready material; dashboard assigns 5 clips/week, shows each student's completion, no complex accounts.

• Mid-range device users: 3 GB phone, 3G connection; offline after downloading 20 clips under 60 MB, auto-deletes old content.

2) Why our product

• Motion, not stills: 6–12 s clips carry voice, face, context. Sound binds to expression, so cards become memories, not bare definitions.

• 3 contexts per word in week 1: "eat" at restaurant, home, market. "Acquired" only when recognized in a new clip.

• One tap: tap a subtitle word: mini-card with audio, furigana, meaning, one example; video plays on, no pause or settings.

• Error-aware: 2 misses: review after 24 h, then 3 days; 3 hits: 14 days. Review drops to 6–8 min/day.

• Clean content, clear privacy: original or education-licensed clips, no protected-series copies; no real name, no history sale, no in-clip ads.

3) First-version scope (max 8 tasks)

• Task 1 — Structure: iOS in Swift + SwiftUI, full RTL Arabic, 4 screens: clip bank, player, review, progress.

• Task 2 — Player: tappable subtitles, 0.75×/1×/1.25×, one-tap sentence repeat, auto-stop after 3 repeats.

• Task 3 — Cards: word tied to audio, furigana, Arabic meaning, source clip; auto-created on first tap.

• Task 4 — Scheduling: spaced repetition: 10 min, 1 d, 3 d, 7 d, 21 d, difficulty-adjusted.

• Task 5 — Content: 60 original 8 s clips, 6 topics (greetings, food, market, transport, home, feelings), 400 linked words.

• Task 6 — Progress: weekly panel of acquired words, streaks, and comprehension from 5 questions; one dismissible daily notification.

4) Pricing and revenue

• Always free: 10 clips/day, 20 review cards, 5 offline clips, no credit card or trial; first understood sentence within 48 hours.

• Pro $3.99/month or $23.99/year: unlimited clips, 100 downloads, 90-day track, teacher dashboard. Annual ≈ $2/month.

• Student $1.99/month with university email; Family $5.99 for 5 separate accounts.

• Targets: 2,000 actives × 4% = 80 subscribers, $1,920/year; 10,000 × 6% = 600, $14,400. Servers covered in month 7.

• Later: topic packs e.g. "Work Terms" $4.99 once, dashboard license $19/class, never ads. No rise for current subscribers; 14-day refund.

5) Global reach via free channels

• 21 clips/week: 7 daily on TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts; each 9 s ends with "What does it mean?", fixed caption "The app is free to start."

• Communities: 3 posts/week in Japanese-learning forums and anime groups on Reddit and Discord, contrast two words, no hard promo; profile link only.

• Store: 12 low-competition phrases like "learn japanese with scenes"; test 3 icons, 4 screenshots in 6 weeks to lift installs to 18%.

• Weekly article: 800–1,200 words on e.g. "How do I understand what I hear in an episode?"; ends with a free download link for organic traffic.

• Partnerships/referral: 10 university clubs, 5 teachers in 90 days for free dashboard and short certificate; each user invites 3 friends: 2 subscribe = free month, 5 = topic pack.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Rights: no protected-work clips; original/licensed library, license log per clip; outside material only as an external link, not a stored file.

• Privacy: 12+, no under-13 data; GDPR and CCPA, export/deletion in 72 h, local encryption, no history sharing.

• Language: human review of all 400 cards at launch; "Report an error" fixed in 48 h on weekdays.

• Performance: test on 6 devices with 2–3 GB RAM; open under 2 s, memory under 120 MB, video compressed to 480p.

• Competition: Android in month 9 with same design and backend, own email list reaching 20% of actives, 8 new clips/week, public changelog monthly.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Laqta

Tagline: Learn Japanese from one scene a day, with a tap and no dry memorization.
