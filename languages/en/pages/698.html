Midad — Complete Project Plan

An offline-first knowledge workspace that puts your team's data under your control.

Full plan: idea, audience, product, business model, GTM, risks. Not launched.

Product plan: local-first team knowledge space

1) Target audience

• Small/medium product & engineering teams (5–50) needing internal wiki/specs/decision docs; avoiding high per-user fees.

• Agencies/studios with per-client docs needing isolated spaces and precise permissions without complex ops.

• Universities, research labs, nonprofits with limited budgets preferring self-hosting on own servers/local cloud.

• Field teams in low-connectivity areas (humanitarian, farms, construction) needing offline work + later sync.

• Compliance-bound orgs (health, legal, public sector) keeping data in-boundary w/ audit logs & local backups.

• Individuals & technical writers wanting a lightweight alternative combining notes & docs in portable Markdown.

2) Differentiators

• Offline-first: edits saved locally instantly, merged on reconnect via CRDT; no lost writes in outages; recoverable per-page history.

• One-command deploy: single Docker Compose runs app, DB, storage; target <10 min on 2GB VPS.

• Truly free to start: full-featured community edition, self-hosted, no page/duration limits.

• Multi-tenant from day one: isolated space per team/client with roles (owner, editor, commenter, reader) and query-level isolation.

• RTL/Arabic as core, not add-on: alignment, direction shortcuts, sound Arabic search.

• Data ownership: full export to Markdown+JSON anytime, scheduled backups to S3-compatible storage, encryption in transit & at rest.

• Identity: optional OIDC/SAML in paid edition, email invites, fine-grained perms.

3) First-release scope (max 8 tasks)

1. Rich Markdown editor: headings, lists, tables, tasks, code blocks w/ highlighting, live preview, RTL/Arabic.

2. Spaces & page tree: create team, per-client space, nested pages, tags, internal links.

3. Permissions & roles: owner/editor/commenter/reader, email invites, revocable share links.

4. Local-first sync: browser/desktop local storage, auto-merge on connect, simple conflict resolution.

5. Instant search: full-text within space, filter by tag, author, date.

6. Import/export: Markdown & ZIP, batch import of Markdown files w/o losing links.

7. Scheduled backups: daily job to S3/MinIO, one-click restore, documented restore test.

8. Single-deployment ops: Docker Compose + one config file, basic admin panel.

4) Pricing & business model

• Community: free self-hosting, 50-user limit, all core features; build user base & trust.

• Free cloud: 3 users, 1GB storage; real trial, lower entry barrier.

• Pro: $6/user/mo (or $60/yr), 20GB, full version history, advanced search.

• Business: $12/user/mo with SSO/OIDC, audit logs, longer retention, priority support.

• Extra revenue: enterprise support & data migration ($500–$3000/project), managed backups, region hosting.

• Discounts: 50% for universities/nonprofits; 14-day trial for paid plans, no card.

5) Global go-to-market via free channels

• Open source on GitHub: clear license, regular releases, "awesome self-hosted" listing, deploy guide AR/EN.

• Show HN, Product Hunt, Reddit (r/selfhosted etc.) launches, angle "lightweight offline-first alternative."

• Educational content: 12 articles & 6 short videos comparing real scenarios, no competitor attacks.

• Long-tail SEO in Arabic & English: "offline team wiki", "self-hosted alternative", neutral comparison pages.

• Local communities: university meetups, Arabic programmer groups, student ambassador trials.

• Convert free to paid via soft limits (storage, SSO, audit logs), not by blocking core features.

6) Risks (legal & technical)

• Privacy/compliance: GDPR, CCPA, health/legal data; solution: EU hosting, DPA, encryption, clear retention.

• IP & licensing: open license compatible with commercial hosting, trademark review, clarify content ownership.

• Data loss during sync: CRDT conflicts, restore misuse; solution: periodic restore tests, immutable backups, audit log.

• App security: session leaks, malicious uploads, SSO vulns; solution: code review, dependency scan, rate limiting, secret encryption.

• Cost & competition: pricing pressure from big tools; solution: focus local market, small teams, self-hosting, monitor storage.

• Dependence on identity/email providers: OIDC outage or email rejection; solution: owner fallback login, multi-provider email.

7) Proposed name & marketing tagline

Proposed name: Midad — tagline: An offline-first knowledge workspace that puts your team's data under your control.
