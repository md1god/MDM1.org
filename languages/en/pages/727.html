Zabad — Complete Project Plan

We keep the cream for you and expose the scum: know who really visits you and who drains your budget.

Complete digital-project plan: idea, audience, product, revenue, outreach, risks. Under construction, not launched.

1) Audience

• Small advertisers: e-commerce/local-service owners spending $500–$10,000/month on Meta, Google, TikTok, without a fraud analyst.

• Small agencies/freelancers: manage 5–30 accounts; need fast client reports without in-house tools or costly platforms.

• Lead-gen: coaches, clinics, insurers, and real estate firms with duplicate/fake phone numbers wasting sales time.

• Emerging markets first: the Gulf, Egypt, Morocco, Turkey, India, and Brazil; Arabic/English content needed, while enterprise rivals serve only large firms.

• Solo founders: campaigns under $2,000/month; need a clear “Is my budget being wasted?” signal in minutes, not weeks.

2) Why Us

• No-code start: paste a landing-page URL or upload a lead CSV for a report in under 3 minutes—no SDK/site changes.

• Human language: instead of “IVT ratio 14.2%,” say “About $1,400 of $10,000 went to suspicious visits, led by repeat clicks from one device.”

• Waste in money: suspicious-visit rate × cost per click (e.g., $0.35 × 4,000 clicks) gives one figure owners understand.

• Impact-ranked fixes: each report ends with 3–7 ordered actions, e.g., “Exclude countries with 0.5% of sales and 22% of clicks.”

• Privacy first: no ad-tracking Cookies; lead data kept at most 30 days—a selling point against heavy platforms.

• Free start: one free monthly scan removes the trial barrier, aids referrals.

3) MVP (8 Tasks)

1. Landing-page scan via URL: light crawl, header analysis, and a small JavaScript script for behavioral signals (cursor, load time, real browser).

2. Lead forms: Webhook or CSV upload; validate international email and phone formats.

3. Bot detection: known UA lists and event timing (e.g., 40 submissions in 20 seconds).

4. Duplicate/fake leads: Fuzzy Matching for names/phones; flag disposable/temporary email domains.

5. Geography/device: compare visit and payment countries; flag abnormal device/browser concentration (e.g., 68% from one browser version).

6. Waste estimate: suspicious-visit rate × cost per click × clicks; confidence low/medium/high.

7. Shareable Arabic/English report: one-page PDF plus password-protected client/auditor link.

8. Dashboard/alerts: recent scans on one page; email alert when suspicious rate exceeds a set threshold (default 15%).

4) Pricing

• Free: one scan/month, 500 events, HTML report only, no card—builds trust and spread.

• Pro $19/month: 3 sites, 20,000 events, unlimited PDF reports, email alerts.

• Growth $49/month: 10 sites, 100,000 events, monthly trends, up to 5 lead forms.

• Agency $149/month: 30 clients, White Label reports, read-only API.

• Extra revenue: one-time deep audit $99; “tracking setup review” $199; high margins on limited human work.

• Growth logic: free-to-paid conversion 4%–7%, average customer value $35/month; focus on retention over price hikes.

5) Global Outreach

• Long-term SEO: 30 articles on “how to know your clicks are fake” with numerical examples and an embedded free scan tool at the end for links/traffic.

• Copy-ready templates: free Google Sheets lead-audit template shared in advertiser groups, product link at top.

• Advertiser communities: real anonymous cases on Reddit, Indie Hackers, and LinkedIn, “what we found in 50 accounts” style, not ads.

• Freelancer partnerships: referral program giving 30% of subscriptions for three months to consultants who bring a client, via a tracking link.

• Product Hunt and YouTube Shorts: six 40-second videos, each on a striking finding (e.g., “We detected 1,200 leads from a temporary email domain”).

• Small nonprofits: Pro free to five yearly for a usage testimonial; provides social proof and stories.

6) Risks

• GDPR/CCPA: minimal data, deletion on request within 7 days, and a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) with each paid customer.

• Sensitive lead data: encrypt in transit/at rest, auto-delete after 30 days, never sell or share with third parties.

• Marketing claims: avoid “we prove fraud”; use “suspicion indicators” with a clear disclaimer to cut legal risk from ad platforms.

• False Positives: VPN/privacy-browser users may be misclassified; show confidence and allow manual exclusions.

• Advanced bots: disguised bots bypass simple checks; use behavioral signals plus weekly-updated rules, not one list.

• Script blocking: ad blockers may stop collection; offer CSV/log analysis with no site code.

7) Name

Proposed name: زَبَد (Zabad)
