Niyya — Complete Project Plan

Know purchase intent before it turns into a deal for someone else.

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, launch plan, and risks. The project is under construction, not launched.

1) Target audience

• B2B startup founders and small sales teams seeking customers without a large ad budget.

• Marketing and growth agencies managing several client accounts.

• RevOps and Product Marketing teams needing early purchase signals.

• Independent consultants in Arabic- and English-speaking markets.

The product scans public conversations on Reddit, X, Hacker News, and niche forums, detects questions showing real pain and need, and turns them into an organized review queue with human-approved reply drafts.

2) Differentiators

• Explainable purchase intent: beyond keywords, we analyze the question, pain, urgency, budget, and buying authority, and explain each score.

• Human-first decision: every reply is an editable draft; no auto-publishing, reducing risk and protecting reputation.

• Compliance by design: official APIs only, no LinkedIn scraping, data minimization, clear retention.

• Fast value: setup in minutes, clear free plan, no credit card.

• Learn from decisions: "lead / not a fit" labels reorder results and improve accuracy.

• Multi-source coverage: Reddit, X, Hacker News, Stack Exchange, and RSS forums, with deduplication and conversation linking.

• Transparent pricing: no selling customer data, no content lock-in, export anytime.

3) MVP scope (max 8 tasks)

1. Sign up, create a workspace, set keywords, competitors, and language.

2. Connect permitted sources: Reddit API, X API, Hacker News, and RSS for niche forums.

3. Normalize and deduplicate data; link replies to conversations.

4. Multi-signal purchase-intent classifier with an explanatory score per lead.

5. Review queue with filters, statuses, tasks, notes, assignment.

6. Reply drafts with brand tone and compliance limits; human approval and manual copy.

7. Email and Slack alerts; CSV export.

8. Initial analytics dashboard, feedback loop, plan limits, billing.

4) Pricing and revenue model

· Plan · Price · Core limits ·

· Free · $0 · 1 workspace, 3 topics, 50 leads/mo, 10 drafts, 7-day history ·

· Starter · $29/month · 5 topics, 500 leads, 100 drafts, Slack, 30-day history ·

· Growth · $79/month · 20 topics, 2,000 leads, unlimited drafts, CRM export, 3 seats ·

· Agency · $199/month · 50 topics, 10,000 leads, 10 seats, white-label reports ·

• Annual discount equals two months free.

• Extra revenue: managed setup, advanced CRM integration, and consulting packages.

• Referral program grants usage credits, without selling data.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Build in Public on X and LinkedIn: weekly best purchase signals and experiment results.

• Free weekly public report on "most frequent buying questions" by sector to attract search and subscriptions.

• Programmatic SEO for glossaries, comparisons, and "how to find B2B customers in communities" guides.

• Launches: Product Hunt, Show HN, Indie Hackers, BetaList, Uneed, Microlaunch.

• Niche communities: genuine Reddit and Slack participation, no automated posting or spam.

• Integration directories: Slack App Directory, HubSpot, Pipedrive.

• Podcasts/newsletters: guest appearances and a free report for republishing.

• Arabic/English content: bridge the Arabic content gap in Social Listening and B2B Lead Generation.

• Referral loop: free credits for inviting a team or customer.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Platform terms and APIs: respect licensing and limits; no content resale.

• Privacy: GDPR and CCPA, clear legal basis, data minimization, deletion on request, 90-day retention.

• Anti-spam: human approval required, sending limits, no auto-publishing.

• Draft liability: prevent false claims and defamation; review before use.

• IP: trademark checks; no copying another product's design or text.

Technical:

• X API price and limit volatility: multiple sources and caching.

• False positives: hybrid classification, human feedback, confidence threshold.

• Multilingual quality: specialized models and periodic Arabic/English tests.

• Model cost: cheap initial filtering before expensive analysis.

• Prompt Injection from public content: isolate untrusted text; ignore its instructions.

• Deduplication and identity linking: clear rules and manual review of critical cases.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Niyya (نيّة)

Tagline: Know purchase intent before it turns into a deal for someone else.
