Wisal — Complete Project Plan

An open layer that gives your apps location and mapping services with power, privacy, and complete freedom.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not yet been launched.

Product plan: an open compatibility layer for location and mapping services

1) Target audience

• Android app developers who want to run their apps without relying on closed services.

• Users of alternative Android systems and old or low-spec devices.

• Institutions and government entities that impose privacy and local data storage requirements.

• Developers of mapping, navigation, fitness, and delivery apps.

• Device manufacturers in emerging markets seeking a lightweight and free solution.

2) Points of distinction (why choose our product)

• A complete alternative written from scratch: a Compatibility Layer that reimplements the closed device services interfaces, plus an independent SDK for developers.

• Privacy first: no tracking and no advertising identifiers, and data stays on the device by default.

• Light and economical: reduces battery and memory consumption compared to heavy alternatives.

• Flexibility of map providers: supports multiple map sources and swappable location providers.

• Fully open and auditable, with stable and documented API interfaces.

• Works offline for essential tasks, and runs on old devices.

3) Scope of the first version (8 tasks maximum)

1. The core of the basic compatibility layer and its loading inside apps.

2. Implementing the geographic location service (GPS + network + approximation).

3. Implementing the basic maps interface (display, zoom, markers).

4. A route, layers, and swappable map sources editor.

5. Geographic search and reverse geocoding (Geocoding) across multiple providers.

6. A developer dashboard: keys, quotas, and usage.

7. An SDK for Android with documentation and ready-made examples.

8. A publishing package on GitHub and F-Droid and an initial free release.

4) Pricing and profit model

• Free to start: the full core is open source under a free license, with no fees and no limits on personal projects.

• The professional package (monthly subscription): direct technical support, higher update frequency, and enterprise features.

• Managed cloud hosting: for those who do not want to manage the infrastructure themselves.

• Corporate sponsorship and support: integration and licensing contracts for companies.

• No ads and no selling of data, to ensure the credibility of privacy.

5) Global distribution plan via free channels

• Publishing the repository on GitHub with an open license and clear documentation.

• Making the package available via F-Droid and free app stores.

• Developer communities: Reddit and XDA and open Android forums.

• Free educational content: articles, workshops, and technical channels.

• Launching on Product Hunt and Hacker News and the open source audience.

• Conferences and hackathons (Hackathons) and prize packages for contributors.

• Partnerships with alternative system projects and device providers.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: the possibility of claims regarding intellectual property rights or cloning of interfaces; solution: completely new design and interfaces and documenting independence.

• Legal: providers' terms of use; solution: relying on legal and transparent sources and servers.

• Technical: continuously tracking changes in closed systems; solution: a flexible abstraction layer and automated tests.

• Technical: variability of device compatibility; solution: a broad test matrix and a contributor community.

• Technical: battery and accurate location performance challenges; solution: continuous optimization and adjustable alternatives.

• Reputation: security concerns about a new attack surface; solution: security reviews and open auditing.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Wisal (وصال)

Marketing tagline: An open layer that gives your apps location and mapping services with power, privacy, and complete freedom.
