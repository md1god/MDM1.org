Echo of the Neighborhood — Complete Project Plan

Know where your brand appears in AI answers, why competitors win, and what to fix this week.

Full plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue, rollout, risks. Not launched yet.

1) Target audience

• Local owners: restaurants, dental clinics, salons, repair shops, retail stores in cities of 50k–1M. Customers ask AI "best kidney dialysis center near me?" — is their name there?

• Multi-branch chains (5–50 branches): need branch-by-city mentions; general visibility fails if the customer's branch is absent.

• Small digital agencies: 2–10 staff, 10–40 local clients; one tool for ready reports instead of manual answer tracking.

• Independent SEO consultants: sell $300–800/month plans; need digital proof the work is needed, not opinion.

• Local directories and booking platforms: "best in the city" lists wanting sources AI trusts so they rank first.

2) Why us

• Free, no card: 1 domain, 3 competitors, 20 questions, weekly check. Real result in under 5 minutes, before paying.

• Explains "why," not just "where": instead of "you are absent," shows the winning paragraph — government source, recent reviews, or clear FAQ? Each reason has quote, link, source date.

• Gap map: 5 pages doable in a week, e.g. "transparent pricing," "certifications and licenses," with time per page.

• Sentiment and trust: each mention positive/neutral/negative, with 0–100 trust score from source type, recency, question fit.

• Owner report: 2-page PDF in Arabic or English, 3 big numbers on top, fixes ranked by impact, plus CSV for analysis.

• No site changes: no code, plugins, or hosting access — just a public domain name.

3) First version scope (max 8 tasks)

• Goal: prove value in one 7-day cycle, domain in → fix list out.

• Eight tasks:

1. Email sign-up; dashboard with 3 screens.

2. Input: domain, 3 competitors, country, 20 ready-made or custom questions.

3. Collect answers from 4 AI assistants via official APIs; save quote, source, timestamp.

4. Detect mentions and compute visibility: 6 of 20 = 30%.

5. Classify winning reason: source type, recency, intent match.

6. Extract 5 source gaps → suggested page tasks.

7. Sentiment analysis and trust score per mention.

8. Weekly report export as PDF and CSV, ranked fixes.

• Out of scope: daily monitoring, agency workspaces, Google Analytics integration, call tracking.

• Quality: run each question 3 times for a stable average; drop answers without verifiable sources.

• Architecture: light web app, collection separate from analysis, DB keeps 30 days on free.

4) Pricing and revenue

• Free: 1 domain, 3 competitors, 20 questions, 1 weekly check, 30-day history. Enough to try and decide.

• Starter – $19/mo: daily checks, 5 domains, 50 questions, email alerts on lost mentions, 90-day history. For one owner.

• Growth – $49/mo: 15 domains, 150 questions, 5 countries, 12-week comparison, client report exports. For in-house marketing.

• Agency – $149/mo: 5 workspaces, up to 50 clients, agency logo, client read-only access.

• Low-friction: 14-day paid trial without card, 20% annual discount, cancel in 2 clicks, no fees.

• Extra revenue: $99 setup for those without time to write 20 questions; tool stays free to start.

5) Free-channel global rollout

• General templates: 30 free pages, e.g. "20 questions for a Riyadh dental clinic," "questions London restaurant owners ask." Each starts with a real tool result, then invites a trial.

• Communities: weekly posts in owner groups and SEO communities on LinkedIn, Reddit, Facebook groups — a useful number, not an ad.

• Unpaid partnerships: chambers of commerce, incubators, restaurant associations — "free check for our members" monthly.

• Product as channel: each free report links to an anonymized aggregate result, so another owner sees their own gaps.

• Languages: Arabic and English first, then Spanish and Portuguese via community volunteers; translate the interface, not only results.

• Content: 1 weekly article + 1 monthly case study + 1 monthly template update = 3 assets only.

6) Risks (legal, technical)

• Legal: only officially available brand APIs, respect terms, no unauthorized collection; short quote with link and source, not full text.

• Privacy: client/competitor data encrypted, auto-deleted after 30 days on free, plain-language privacy policy.

• Technical: assistant changes or a closed interface can break collection; separate adapter per source, internal alert at 20% result drop.

• Accuracy: answers vary per run; repeat each question 3 times, show average with variation range, label figures estimates, not guaranteed visibility.
