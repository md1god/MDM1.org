Wamd — A Complete Project Plan

Everything you need starts with a single letter.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the rollout plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: an Android launcher focused on search

1) Target audience

• Android users who have dozens of apps and waste time navigating between screens.

• Professionals, students, and those who work heavily on their phones and need instant access to apps, files, and contacts.

• Users who care about privacy and minimal tracking, and want a fast, simple, customizable interface.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Search first: everything starts from a single search field that instantly indexes apps, contacts, messages, files, and settings.

• Offline, on-device operation: all indexing happens locally, so no user data is sent to any cloud.

• Simple contextual intelligence: suggestions based on time, place, and frequent use, without complicated setup.

• Open customization: themes and plugins (Plugins) that the community can build, with lightweight performance and low battery consumption.

• Free from the start: core functions are complete without payment, and revenue comes from optional, non-intrusive channels.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. A home screen with a unified search field and instant indexing of apps and contacts.

2. A local fuzzy search engine that handles spelling errors and Arabic/Latin characters.

3. Smart suggestions based on frequency and time of day (on-device).

4. Quick actions on results (open, call, share, pin).

5. Simple appearance customization: icons, grid, dark/light theme.

6. A preliminary plugin system with a documented API for at least one skill.

7. Transparent privacy settings + local export/import of settings.

8. Support for at least two languages (Arabic and English) with full RTL layout.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free-to-start: all core functions are available without an account or payment.

• A supported version (Pro): a cheap monthly/yearly subscription for advanced plugins, settings sync via encrypted export, exclusive themes, and automation tools.

• Optional one-time support through in-app donations.

• A plugin store: revenue sharing with skill developers to attract participation, while keeping the core free.

5) Global rollout plan using free channels

• F-Droid and GitHub Releases for free, open distribution, with a focus on the privacy community.

• Product Hunt and Reddit (channels dedicated to Android and privacy) for a deliberate initial launch.

• Short content on TikTok/YouTube/Reels showing the speed of accessing apps, and tech blogs in Arabic and English.

• Developer communities (Telegram, Discord) to build plugins and grant badges to contributors.

• A crowdsourced translation program to localize and add new languages, expanding global reach at no cost.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: respect the licenses of the libraries used and avoid any copied code or assets; Google Play policies require legitimate, justified permissions, especially for accessing notifications or files.

• Technical: index performance limits on weak devices, modern Android restrictions on background permissions, the variety of manufacturer interfaces, and inconsistent plugin performance across devices.

• Risk mitigation: building everything from scratch, auditing permissions, testing on a wide range of devices, and isolated plugin interfaces (Sandbox) for security.

7) Proposed name and marketing tagline

Proposed name: Wamd
