Markhunt — Complete Project Plan

Find your counterfeiters and take them down before they sell.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under construction and not yet launched.

1) Target Audience

• Top priority: Small and medium D2C brands selling on Amazon, TikTok Shop, and Temu, with limited legal budgets and no monitoring team.

• Second: IP agencies and brand agencies managing multiple clients who need a single dashboard for all enforcement campaigns.

• Third: Mid-size companies with in-house legal teams (2–5 people) wanting to automate DMCA and Vijay/Brand Registry takedowns instead of manual work.

• Fourth (later): Content creators and photographers tracking stolen image and video copies.

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

1. Focus on takedown speed, not just reporting: Our core metric is "Time-to-Remove" and accepted notice rate, not number of pages scanned.

2. Multi-signal visual matching engine: Combines product fingerprint (logo, label, packaging pattern) + page fingerprint (title, description, prices) + seller fingerprint, reducing false positives compared to name-only matching tools.

3. Ready-to-send notice draft: Generates legal request text per platform type (DMCA, Amazon Report Infringement, Meta IP Form) with attached evidence and timestamped storage (Evidence Chain) for reuse during counter-notices.

4. "Gradual Automated Enforcement" model: Friendly receipt phase → formal warning → formal notice, with full audit trail exportable for lawyers.

5. Single workflow dashboard for agencies: Multiple brands, per-client permissions, scheduled white-label client reports — turning the tool into recurring revenue for IP agencies.

6. Pricing: Tailored for SMBs with transparent notice caps (Free-to-start) instead of opaque enterprise pricing.

7. Data privacy: Regional EU/US storage and hosting region selection — critical selling point against US SaaS tools.

3) MVP Scope (Max 8 Tasks)

1. Single Dashboard: Brand management, upload reference product catalog (images + SKU + ASIN).

2. Source Reader: Scanner supporting only two sources in MVP — Amazon + Google Shopping/Search (using official APIs where possible, limited ethical crawling where no API exists).

3. Detection Engine: Image matching (Embeddings via open model like CLIP/SigLIP) + text matching (title/description/price/seller) with adjustable thresholds.

4. Results Inbox: Match list with confidence score, comparison image (reference vs. infringing), and classification reason.

5. Notice Generation: Automatic DMCA template + Amazon IP Report template with timestamped evidence log.

6. Follow-up Status: Manual states (New / Sent / Accepted / Rejected / Removed) + link picker to submit notice via platform.

7. Email/Telegram Alerts: Instant alert on high-confidence infringement detection.

8. Free + Paid Plans: Signup, monthly free scan limit, Stripe payment gateway integration for plan upgrades.

Out of scope until v2: TikTok Shop and Meta and fine links, automated API warnings, white-label reports, Shopify integration.

4) Pricing & Revenue Model

• Free (Free-to-start): 1 brand, 100 pages scanned/month, 3 generated notices/month, email alerts.

• Pro — $49/mo: 3 brands, 2,000 pages/mo, unlimited notices, evidence log, Telegram alerts.

• Business — $149/mo: 10 brands, 10,000 pages/mo, scan priority, lawyer-ready audit export, unlimited users.

• Agency — $399/mo: Unlimited clients, scheduled white-label reports, API, permissions, selectable storage region.

• Additional Revenue: Custom market reports (Price/Brand-Infringement Report) at $199/report, and paid integrations (Shopify App Store).

• Revenue Model: Monthly subscription SaaS + usage layer (extra scanned pages) — high margin because automated scanning cost is low.

5) Global Distribution Plan via Free Channels

1. Long-tail SEO: Comparison pages and practical guides: "How to report counterfeit product on Amazon", "DMCA takedown template", "counterfeit vs replica" — in English then translated to Arabic, Spanish, German.

2. Attractive Free Tool: "Free Check: Is Your Product Sold on Copycat Sites?" — single page driving traffic and signups.

3. Reddit & Forums: Genuine participation in r/ecommerce, r/FulfillmentByAmazon, r/smallbusiness, Shopify forums, BHW (non-promotional).

4. LinkedIn: Digital case studies "We filed a notice and the link was removed within X hours" + founder content for marketing team.

5. Communities & Users: Referral program (free month per successful referral) + Open Playboo
