Mihfaz — Full Project Plan

Save your data once, retrieve it on any device — with one tap.

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, the audience, the product, the revenue model, the rollout plan, and the risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

مِحفظة — Mihfaz

1) Target audience

• Android users who root their devices or try custom ROMs and need robust backups for their apps and data.

• People who keep changing phones or use more than one device and want to transfer their settings and data quickly.

• Privacy-conscious users who want an encrypted backup that stays on their own devices or their own private storage.

• Professionals and small maintenance teams that need batch restore across multiple devices.

2) Differentiators

• Fast incremental backup: saves only what changed since the last backup, so the size shrinks and the duration is cut.

• Dated snapshot chain: you can go back to any point in time instead of relying on a single file.

• Selective restore: choose a specific app, folder, or data type rather than everything at once.

• Local end-to-end encryption (E2EE) with a key only the user owns.

• Device-to-Device transfer over Wi-Fi Direct, with no internet.

• Automatic backup scheduling with smart alerts when the last run did not complete.

3) Scope of the first release (8 tasks maximum)

1. Simple main screen: show the latest backup, its size, and its status.

2. Choosing the packages and data to back up (an in-app transactional system for sources).

3. Full backup with compression and local encryption.

4. Full or selective restore with file integrity verification (Checksums).

5. Multiple snapshots with old-backup management and smart deletion.

6. Direct connection between two devices over Wi-Fi Direct to transfer the backup.

7. Simple CLI for advanced use and automation.

8. Complete Arabic/English documentation and sample test files.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free-to-start: unlimited local backup and restore, device-to-device transfer, two snapshots, basic encryption.

• Pro (monthly/yearly subscription): unlimited snapshots, scheduled backups, E2EE encrypted cloud storage, multi-device sync, priority support.

• Lifetime licence (one-time): for those who hate subscriptions — unlocks all Pro features for the life of the product.

• B2B / Fleet: licensing for multiple devices for small teams and service centers.

• Goal: earn revenue only from convenience and sync, while keeping the core base entirely free in order to build a wide community.

5) Global rollout plan using free channels

• A detailed post on XDA Developers and Reddit (r/Android, r/LineageOS, r/privacy) explaining the idea and its advantages.

• Telegram and Discord channels for users and direct support.

• Short YouTube videos: usage walkthroughs, speed comparisons, and data protection tips.

• Posts on Medium/Dev.to and shares on X/Twitter with hashtags (#Android #Privacy #Backup).

• Listing the product on free directories (GitHub topic pages, alternativeto-style listings).

• Ambassador program: symbolic rewards and Pro access for anyone who publishes tutorials or translations.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Root and system modification may void the warranty with some manufacturers; this must be explicitly warned about in the documentation.

• Personal data protection (GDPR/PDPL) when offering cloud sync: commitment to E2EE, a clear privacy policy, and no filtering of user content.

• App stores may impose restrictions on tools that work with Root; distribution through direct channels (F-Droid/GitHub) is preferred, with a dual inventory.

Technical:

• Constant changes in Android APIs (Scoped Storage, BackupAgent, SELinux) require continuous updates.

• Risk of data corruption during restore: verification via Checksums and a separate initial copy before any sensitive operation.

• The huge size of app data requires smart compression and efficient memory management to avoid OOM errors on weak devices.

7) Name and tagline

Proposed name: مِحفظة — Mihfaz

Tagline: Save your data once, retrieve it on any device — with one tap.
