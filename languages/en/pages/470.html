Mimariq — Complete Project Plan

Renovate your rooms smartly and on a smart budget, in seconds.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, business model, growth plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

New Product Plan

1) Target Audience

• Home renters: Want to change their home decor without high renovation costs.

• New homeowners: Want to visualize how their rooms will look before buying or arranging.

• DIY enthusiasts: Want practical ideas that can be applied on a limited budget.

2) Key Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• Precise element control: The ability to lock any element in the room to keep it as is, and suggest changes only to the rest.

• Cost focus: Display cheaper alternative materials that can actually be purchased for each generated suggestion.

• Lightweight and fast: Works on low-end devices and does not require mandatory registration for the demo.

• Multi-purpose: Supports interior rooms, gardens, and outdoor areas in a single experience.

3) Scope of the First Version (Max 8 Tasks)

1. Image upload: A simple interface to upload a single room image.

2. Element locking: A manual tool to select elements to keep in the image.

3. Style selection: A limited list of common decorative styles.

4. Realistic generation: Generate at least one image with a natural and applicable result.

5. Material suggestions: Display a list of alternative materials with an approximate cost estimate.

6. Image compression: Optimize image size to reduce waiting time.

7. Local saving: Save results on the user's device without permanent storage of images.

8. Smart free limit: Activate a daily free credit to reduce costs from the start.

4) Pricing and Profit Model

• Free to start: A limited daily free credit for trial use.

• Paid credits: Purchase additional credits at a very low price when free credits run out.

• Conversion partnerships: Earn a commission when users click through to decor material stores, without intrusive ads.

5) Global Growth Plan with Free Channels

• Search engine optimization (SEO): Create educational pages about renovating rooms on a limited budget in the most widely used languages.

• Short-form content: Show "before / after" videos on short video platforms to demonstrate value directly.

• AI tool directories: List the product in popular free AI tool directories.

• Specialized communities: Share practical tips in DIY and home decor communities without aggressive direct promotion.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal:

• Privacy: Images must not be stored for long periods, and the principle of minimal data must be followed.

• Usage rights: It must be clearly stated that uploaded images are used only for the purpose of generating results.

• Technical:

• Cost: High generation costs, so free credits must be controlled precisely.

• Accuracy of locked elements: Difficulty keeping selected elements exactly as is, requiring continuous improvement.

• Poor internet: Performance may be slow, so image compression and reduced request sizes must be used.

7) Suggested Name and Marketing Slogan

Suggested name: Mimariq

Marketing slogan: Renovate your rooms smartly and on a smart budget, in seconds.
