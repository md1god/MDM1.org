Nabih — Complete Project Plan

A voice agent that understands your dialect, answers your calls, and turns every missed call into an appointment or lead.

Plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue, launch, risks. Under construction; not launched.

Voice receptionist for small businesses

1) Target audience

• Small service firms (1–20 staff) earning from appointments: dental/dermatology, salons, workshops/home maintenance, vets, law, real estate.

• Pain: 20–40% of calls missed after hours or when busy → lost appointments and competitor wins.

• First markets: Gulf/Egypt/Levant (Arabic dialects), then Spain/LatAm and Southeast Asia, then US/Canada local services.

• Buyer/channel: owner or branch manager, not IT; found via search, peer referral, or small agency/Twilio consultant.

2) Differentiators

• 5-minute browser setup: import website/PDF, test chat/voice, publish.

• Try before pay: free trial + call simulator, no card/commitment.

• Arabic first: Gulf/Egyptian/Levantine; auto Arabic-English switching, not literal.

• Vertical templates: dental, salon, home maintenance, not from scratch.

• Outcome-focused: confirmed appointment or lead, with automatic SMS/WhatsApp follow-up.

• Bring Your Own Twilio: user’s number/account cuts marginal cost; near-free start.

• Transparent: transcript, summary, action log, consent notice, instant takeover.

• Usage pricing, not per seat: small teams, no setup fees or lock-in.

3) First-version scope (max 8 tasks)

1. Template agent: 4-step form (sector, hours, tone, transfer number) → editable agent.

2. Knowledge: website/FAQs/PDF/text, with source preview and quote fix.

3. Twilio: SID/Token, buy/connect number, limited free trial.

4. Simulator: browser text/voice with price, booking, complaint, transfer scenarios.

5. Lead capture: answer from knowledge; collect name/phone/reason/consent; end with summary.

6. Booking: Google Calendar read/write, conflict prevention, SMS/WhatsApp confirmation/reminder.

7. Transfer/recording: warm transfer on request/failure; consent-gated recording/transcription.

8. Dashboard: calls, transcript, summary, status, CSV, Webhook/email lead alerts.

Launch: 20 calls, >90% capture, 30% booked/lead.

4) Pricing and profit model

· Plan · Price · Limits · For whom ·

· Free · $0 · 30 min/month, trial number, own Twilio, logo · Trial/proof ·

· Starter · $19/month · 120 min, managed number, one calendar, SMS follow-up · Solo owner ·

· Growth · $49/month · 400 min, 3 users, Webhook, reports, transfer · One clinic/branch ·

· Agency · $149/month · 1,200 min, 10 sub-clients, White-label, API · Agencies/marketers ·

• Overage: $0.12–0.15/min after limit, transparently published.

• Revenue: subscriptions + usage margin (Twilio/STT/TTS/LLM) + managed numbers + paid templates/setup/migration.

• Free-to-start: no card/setup fees; near-zero marginal cost with own Twilio.

• 20% annual discount and referral credit per paying customer.

5) Global go-to-market, free channels

• Programmatic SEO: sector × city/language pages, comparisons, setup guides, hreflang (e.g. “phone agent for dental clinics in Dubai”).

• Free tools: missed-call calculator, reception scenario generator, number readiness checker for email/backlinks.

• Launches: Product Hunt, AlternativeTo, G2/Capterra (free), GitHub open templates, Indie Hackers.

• Communities: Reddit (r/smallbusiness, r/dentistry), Facebook groups, real estate forums; value, not ads.

• Short video: YouTube Shorts, TikTok/Instagram “real call before/after” demos in Arabic, English, Spanish.

• Partnerships: Twilio consultants, independent developers, chambers of commerce; 20% referral commission.

• Organic: templates, referral links, “free month for a success story,” quarterly missed-calls report for press.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Recording: some jurisdictions require two-party consent; GDPR needs legal basis/transparency. Fix: voice notice, recording off by default in sensitive markets, short retention.

• AI disclosure: California bot law and EU AI Act proposal. Fix: identify as automated within 5 seconds.

• Privacy/localization: GDPR, CCPA, Gulf laws, patient data (HIPAA/BAA). Fix: no US healthcare before BAA; optional EU storage.

• Outbound: TCPA, STIR/SHAKEN, anti-fraud. Fix: no marketing outbound in v1; verify numbers.

• Payments: no phone card collection in MVP; avoids PCI.

• Twilio dependency: terms/price risk. Fix: abstraction layer for a later alternative.

Technical:

• High latency/choppy audio → fast STT-TTS, time limits, instant transfer.

• Weak dialect/noise accuracy → multiple models, dialect tests, human escalation at low confidence.
