Bayyin — Full Project Plan

Reveal any website's technologies in seconds, with proof and confidence, not guesswork.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, monetization model, growth plan, and risks. The project is under development and has not yet been launched.

1) Target Audience

• B2B sales teams and entrepreneurs building prospect lists based on technologies.

• Web and marketing agencies that inspect potential clients' websites before pitching.

• Growth marketers and market researchers tracking competitors' tech stack changes.

• Freelancers and digital transformation consultants looking for opportunities to update or replace systems.

• Security and compliance teams needing a quick inventory of visible technologies without collecting personal data.

2) Key Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• Proof over guesswork: Every detected technology comes with supporting evidence (Header, Cookie, Script path, DOM fingerprint) and a clear confidence level.

• No sign-up required for the first scan: Instant results with a shareable link, lowering the barrier to trial.

• Actionable buying signals: Classify domains by technology stacks and recent changes, not just a technical list.

• Change monitoring: Get alerts when a site switches CMS, payment platform, or marketing tool — high-intent buying moments.

• Privacy-first: Only analyze domains, no personal data collected, GDPR/CCPA compliant.

• Native Arabic experience + English interface: Cover an overlooked regional market with global scalability.

• Generous and transparent free model: Clear limits, simple export, upgrade only when scale is needed, not for core features.

3) Scope of First Version (Max 8 Tasks)

1. Build a signature-based detection engine (Headers, HTML, JS globals, Cookies, DNS/ASN) covering 150 core technologies.

2. Instant scan page with no registration and a shareable result link.

3. Display evidence for each detection, confidence level, and technology category.

4. Export results for a single domain to CSV/JSON.

5. Limited free monitoring (10 domains) with email alerts on tech stack changes.

6. Buying signals calculator: Filter domains by tech stacks (e.g., Shopify + Klaviyo).

7. Simple dashboard to save, name, and share lists.

8. Usage limits, abuse protection, and a basic performance dashboard.

4) Pricing & Monetization

• Free Forever: 20 scans/day, 1 user, 10 monitored domains, single export.

• Pro — $19/month: 2,000 scans/month, batch export up to 500 domains, unlimited alerts, API up to 1,000 requests.

• Team — $79/month: 10,000 scans, 5 seats, Webhooks, CRM export.

• Enterprise — Custom pricing: higher limits, SLA, historical data, custom integrations.

• Additional revenue: Extra API batches, prepaid scan credits, and referral commissions. No ads in the first version.

5) Global Growth Plan with Free Channels

• Programmatic SEO: Comparison and category pages like "Sites using X" and "Switching from X to Y".

• Embeddable free tool: A technical scan widget to embed in blogs and communities.

• Free browser extension: A "Scan this site" button to drive daily usage.

• Public launch: Product Hunt, Hacker News, IndieHackers, and relevant Reddit/LinkedIn communities with helpful content, not ads.

• Build in public: Share interesting scan findings weekly on LinkedIn and X.

• Free partnerships: Listings on Zapier/Make, sales tool directories, and Arabic tech communities.

• Educational content: Short tutorials on reading tech fingerprints to drive long-term organic traffic.

6) Risks (Legal & Technical)

• Legal: Respect site terms of use and robots.txt, avoid bypassing technical blocks; stay neutral on personal data and comply with GDPR/CCPA; avoid using tech logos without license; provide a clear disclaimer on result accuracy.

• Technical: False positives, constantly evolving fingerprints, server IP blocks/rate limits, high scan costs at scale, API abuse, and SSRF risk when fetching user-provided URLs.

• Mitigations: Versioned signature database with human review, strict per-IP/account limits, isolated fetch requests with URL validation, result caching, and clear confidence classification.

Proposed Name: Bayyin
