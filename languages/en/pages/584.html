Athar — Full Project Plan

Know which channel pays your invoice, not which channel fills your reports.

A complete execution plan for a new digital product: concept, audience, product, revenue model, distribution plan, and risks. The project is under construction and not yet launched.

Product plan: revenue analytics attributed to channels

1) Target audience

• SaaS and small digital product founders (1–20 people) using Stripe, Paddle, or Lemon Squeezy, spending on ads and content without a data team.

• Growth and marketing teams at startups (pre-seed to Seed) needing to prove return on channel spend.

• Freelancers, indie hackers, and small agencies managing more than one project/client.

• Privacy-focused teams in Europe and California that reject heavy tools or personal data collection.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Connect revenue to cause: every subscription or payment is attributed to a visit source with a clear confidence score, instead of misleading absolute numbers.

• "Today's priorities" instead of crowded dashboards: 3–5 tasks ranked by expected revenue impact, phrased as: fix, increase, or stop.

• Minutes to set up: read-only OAuth connection to Stripe, GA4, Search Console, and ad platforms, with no frontend SDK.

• Privacy first: aggregated data with no PII, EU storage, and a "cookieless" mode where legally permitted.

• Model transparency: show inference limits and gaps instead of claiming full accuracy.

• Pricing starts free and grows with tracked revenue, a lightweight alternative to expensive BI tools without a data engineer.

3) MVP scope (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Sign in, create a workspace, and one project.

2. Connect Stripe with read-only access and sync subscriptions, payments, and refunds.

3. Connect GA4 and Search Console via OAuth and pull sessions and sources.

4. Transparent attribution engine: last non-direct touch + first touch, with UTM and referrer grouping.

5. "Revenue by channel" dashboard showing MRR, new revenue, refunds, and confidence score.

6. "Today's priorities" list: rule-based recommendations (high-traffic low-conversion channel, budget-draining campaign, leaking page...).

7. CSV export, read-only share link, and weekly email notification.

8. Usage limits and billing via Stripe Billing, plus a trial mode.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free: one project, tracked revenue up to $2,000/month, last 90 days, no card.

• Starter — $19/month: up to $10,000 tracked revenue, 3 projects, export and email report.

• Growth — $49/month: up to $50,000, 10 projects, deeper recommendations, ads integration, team members.

• Scale — $99/month: up to $250,000 + usage-based pricing on overage, SSO and API.

• Additional revenue: one-time "revenue audit" service and referral partnerships with complementary tools. We never sell data.

5) Global distribution plan with free channels

• Free web tool: "Revenue Leak Calculator" giving an instant report in exchange for email.

• Programmatic SEO: "... alternative" pages, comparisons, UTM guides, and revenue attribution.

• Integrations as a distribution channel: Stripe App Marketplace and SaaS tool directories.

• Open-source content: a repository of connectors and report templates.

• Communities: Indie Hackers, Reddit (r/SaaS, r/analytics), Hacker News "Show HN", Product Hunt.

• YouTube and blogs: "Find your profitable channel in 10 minutes" tutorials.

• Referral program for agencies and freelancers with commission.

• Weekly newsletter and free webinar.

• Multilingual pages (Arabic, English, Spanish) to reach broader markets.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• GDPR/CCPA/ePrivacy: legal basis for processing, cookie consent, data processing agreements (DPA), and data transfers.

• Stripe and Google API terms: usage limits, no storage of sensitive payment data, and OAuth policy compliance.

• Advertising privacy: no personal profiles and respect opt-out signals.

• Data ownership: no selling, plus export and deletion.

Technical:

• Attribution accuracy: attribution gaps, ad blocking, ITP in Safari, multiple devices, and direct visits.

• Differences between attribution models and their effect on decisions; we address them with confidence scores and ranges.

• API limits, sync delays, data quality variance, multiple currencies, and refunds.

• Scalability and cost at large volumes.

• Security: token encryption, least privilege, and continuous monitoring.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Athar

Tagline: Know which channel pays your invoice, not which channel fills your reports.
