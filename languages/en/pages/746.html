Meeting Point — Full Project Plan

Turn your meeting audio into a clear summary in seconds, without any technical intervention.

Full implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, go‑to‑market plan, and risks. The project is under development and not yet launched.

AI Product Plan for Recording Meeting Summaries and Transcriptions

1) Target Audience

1. Small and medium teams – need fast meeting minutes to reduce follow‑up time.

2. Executives – prefer instant summaries for quick decisions without reading full transcripts.

3. Consultants and researchers – rely on call recordings with clients or participants and need to extract main ideas.

4. Students and faculty – benefit from converting lectures or study‑group discussions into searchable text.

5. Non‑profit organizations – need to document fundraising meetings or workshops at minimal cost.

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

1. No bot required – audio is extracted from a recording file or a live cloud‑call link, preserving participant privacy.

2. Free‑to‑start model – the first 30 minutes of transcription per user are free, with upgrade options later.

3. Smart summaries – uses a “summarization‑by‑topic” algorithm to generate action points, decisions, and follow‑up questions.

4. Easy integration with work tools – export texts to Google Docs, Notion, or Slack with one click.

5. Multilingual support – includes Arabic, English, and Spanish in the initial trial version.

3) Scope of the First Version (Maximum 8 Tasks)

1. Import recordings from MP3/MP4 files or Zoom/Google Meet links.

2. Convert speech to text with ≥ 92 % accuracy for Arabic and English.

3. Generate a short summary (5‑7 bullet points) highlighting the most important statements.

4. Extract Action Items with assignment of participant names.

5. Add timestamps to the transcript for easy navigation to specific sections.

6. Export the text to PDF or DOCX with presentation‑ready formatting.

7. Share the link with the team with read/edit permissions.

8. Simple web interface supporting dark/light mode and compatible with all modern browsers.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

1. Free plan – 30 minutes of transcription per month + one summary, to encourage trial.

2. Monthly plan – 9 USD/month for 5 hours of transcription, unlimited summaries, and API integration.

3. Enterprise plan – usage‑based pricing (starting from 500 USD/year) including team management, analytical reports, and dedicated technical support.

4. Pay‑as‑you‑go subscription – charges customers precisely for each additional minute (0.02 USD/minute).

5. Additional revenue streams – sell “template” add‑ons for summaries (fixed price list) and non‑intrusive ads in the free version.

5) Global Growth Plan Using Free Channels

1. Educational content on YouTube & TikTok – short lessons on meeting note‑taking with a live product demo.

2. Posts on Reddit and Quora – answer questions about improving meeting productivity and include the trial link.

3. Partnerships with tech bloggers – provide a free trial account in exchange for a detailed review on the blog.

4. Launch an “Ambassador” program – users earn extra transcription hours by inviting friends.

5. Listing in productivity‑tool directories on platforms like Product Hunt and AlternativeTo to generate organic traffic.

6. Targeted LinkedIn ads – focus on managers of small and medium teams with valuable free content.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

1. Data protection laws – must comply with GDPR and CCPA; all files are encrypted in transit and at rest.

2. Audio ownership rights – users must obtain consent from all participants before transcription.

3. Speech‑recognition accuracy – dialects or background noise may cause errors; a manual correction mechanism is required.

4. Cloud server reliance – dependence on providers such as AWS or Azure could cause downtime if a service outage occurs.

5. Security breaches – conduct regular penetration tests and enable multi‑factor authentication for the admin console.

6. Competition – emerging similar solutions could erode market share; focus on unique features like no‑bot requirement.

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Tagline

Proposed name: Meeting Point

Marketing tagline: “Turn your meeting audio into a clear summary in seconds, without any technical intervention.”
