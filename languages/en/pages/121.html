Ladan — Complete Project Plan

Every crash reshapes the road, and every vehicle tells its story through physics.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, growth plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not been launched yet.

Product Plan: Soft-Body Physics Simulator for Vehicles

1) Target Audience

• Players and car enthusiasts who want a realistic driving experience and physics-based destruction without buying powerful hardware.

• Content creators and commentators looking for crash clips and competitions that are easy to share.

• The modding community that wants open tools to build vehicles and maps.

• Physics and engineering students and teachers who need an interactive simulation of flexible systems and collisions.

• Indie developers who want a platform extensible through add-ons.

2) Differentiators (Why choose our product)

• Runs instantly in the browser via WebGPU/WASM with no download, plus optional desktop versions.

• A soft-body engine written from scratch with a hybrid approach (mass-spring + position-based physics) for higher stability and faster performance.

• Accurate cumulative destruction and deformation with gradual repair that actually affects driving behavior.

• An open, documented vehicle format that makes it easy to move work between users and encourages creativity.

• Link sharing: deterministic crash replays (Deterministic Replay) watched directly in the browser.

• Community first: a free add-on store with a fair quota system for creators.

3) Scope of the First Version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Build the core soft-body physics engine from scratch with stability tests.

2. Vehicle assembly model: chassis + joints + wheels + suspension system.

3. Procedural terrain generation and ground-vehicle collision detection.

4. Cumulative damage and deformation system + in-game repair.

5. Controls and cameras (chase/free/cockpit) with keyboard and hand support.

6. Simplified vehicle editor + import/export in an open, documented format.

7. Browser play, local save, and optional cloud sync.

8. Deterministic replay system and clip sharing via a public link.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free-to-start edition: full play in the browser, basic vehicles and maps, local save, and clip sharing.

• Creator plan: a low monthly subscription that unlocks monetization tools, cloud storage, and add-on featuring.

• One-time purchase (Founder): a perpetual license for the desktop version + an asset pack.

• Add-on marketplace: a small commission on paid sales while keeping free items free.

• Educational/institutional licenses and integration into physics classrooms.

5) Global Growth Plan Using Free Channels

• Launch an instantly playable web version as a primary goal to lower the entry barrier.

• Short clips (Shorts/Reels/TikTok) of funny crashes + weekly competitions under a fixed title.

• Creator communities on Discord, Reddit, and Steam Workshop, plus an open format that encourages derivation.

• Campaigns with car and physics content creators using shareable replays via a single link.

• Free school and university challenges to use the simulation in teaching flexible systems.

• Partially open source (the core and the format) to attract contributors and build trust.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal: avoid any borrowed names, assets, or logos; all assets and code are original, and licenses are reviewed when accepting contributions. A clear policy is needed for ownership rights of user add-ons and paid content.

• Technical: achieving stable real-time physics simulation in the browser; mitigated through position-based physics, fixed time steps, and regression tests.

• Technical: WebGPU performance varies across browsers and devices; mitigated with automatic detection and a gradual simulation-quality scale.

• Technical: determinism risk across different platforms; mitigated with fixed-point numbers and unified positional calculations.

• Legal: originality matters — using any code or data from existing products is prohibited, and an independent development log is documented.

7) Proposed Name and Tagline

Proposed name: Ladan

Tagline: Every crash reshapes the road, and every vehicle tells its story through physics.
