Raqim — Complete Project Plan

An infinite canvas that turns your handwriting into searchable notes, local and private to you.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

An original product plan: a note-taking and handwriting app

1) Target audience

• Students in schools and universities who take lecture notes and draw diagrams.

• Researchers and writers who prefer thinking with a pen on digital paper.

• Designers and concept engineers who sketch quick ideas before moving to heavy software.

• Users in low-connectivity areas who need to work locally without the internet.

• Arabic-speaking users and users of non-Latin languages looking for good optical recognition of their handwriting.

2) Differentiators (why choose our product)

• Local-first foundation: notes are saved to the device first, so they work without the internet, with blazing speed and full privacy.

• Smart multilingual handwriting recognition: text search within handwriting, with advanced support for Arabic and cursive script.

• A lightweight infinite canvas: a new drawing engine built from scratch with stable performance even with thousands of strokes.

• Open, long-lived format: notes saved in a documented open format that prevents lock-in and gives users ownership of their data.

• A plugin ecosystem: a simple API that lets the community add tools without modifying the core.

• Genuinely free to start: all core features with no account and no artificial limits.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. A vector drawing engine with pen, ruler, eraser, and pressure sensitivity tools.

2. An infinite canvas with smooth pan and zoom.

3. Layers, element grouping, and unlimited undo/redo.

4. Local saving in an open format + automatic file indexing.

5. Text search in handwriting (offline optical recognition).

6. Export to PDF, PNG, and SVG.

7. Desktop apps (Windows/macOS/Linux) with pen and tablet support.

8. An optional end-to-end encrypted sync system as a later, opt-in service.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Always free: all core features locally with no account, with an unlimited number of notes.

• Pro plan (monthly/yearly subscription): encrypted cloud sync, advanced optical recognition, bulk export, version history.

• Teams plan: shared spaces for class groups and teams with permissions.

• A payment alternative for users in developing regions: variable local pricing to expand the base.

• Monetization principle: we fund cloud features without blocking the value of individual use.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Open source: opening the core on code hosting platforms to attract contributors and trust.

• Product Hunt and Hacker News: a launch focused on the "local-first" and "privacy" angles.

• Note-taking and open-source communities: Reddit, Discord, and adjacent product forums.

• Visual content: Shorts, Reels, and TikTok to showcase writing speed and handwriting search.

• App store guide: publishing a free version on desktop and tablet stores.

• University partnerships: free offering for students and professors to drive organic growth.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: reviewing patents related to pen input and tablets; avoiding any copying of others' interfaces or assets; documenting library licenses.

• Legal/privacy: compliance with data protection rules (GDPR), especially for young users, and transparency of the sync policy.

• Technical: achieving very low latency for pen writing, and reasonable optical recognition accuracy on Arabic scripts.

• Technical: resolving sync conflicts without data corruption, and maintaining canvas performance at large scale.

• Technical: ensuring the sustainability of the open file format and providing migration tools between versions.

Proposed name: Raqim

Tagline: An infinite canvas that turns your handwriting into searchable notes, local and private to you.
