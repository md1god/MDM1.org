Kashshaf — Full Project Plan

Search the way your customer talks, and discover what they love before they ask for it.

A complete plan for a new digital project covering idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market, and risks. The project is under construction and not yet launched.

Product plan: a search and discovery engine for e-commerce stores

1) Target audience

• Owners of small and medium e-commerce stores on ready-made platforms (Shopify, WooCommerce, OpenCart) with catalogs between 500 and 50,000 products.

• E-commerce startups building a custom store needing a quickly deployable solution without a search team.

• Multilingual stores (especially Arabic and English) wanting results that understand colloquial dialect and typos.

• Technical decision-makers (developer/founder) are the buyer: they evaluate integration, ease of installation, and cost.

2) Differentiation (why choose us)

• Understands "the customer's way of speaking": semantic search tolerating typos, synonyms, and colloquialisms, and rewarding vague specs ("a light laptop for studying") over literal matching.

• Hybrid text + image search: the customer uploads a product image, and the system finds the identical or visually similar match via unified embeddings.

• Continuous learning from behavior: real-time re-ranking based on clicks and add-to-cart, so discovery improves without manual setup.

• Nearly no-code: a ready-made plugin/script that installs on the store and auto-imports the catalog.

• Data privacy: a self-hosted Docker mode for those who refuse to send data to the cloud.

• Multilingual from day one: open embedding models trained on Arabic and others.

• Clear analytics: a dashboard showing "why this product appeared" and attributing revenue to search, turning search from a cost into a decision driver.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Import the product catalog from CSV/JSON files and Shopify/WooCommerce connections, with periodic synchronization.

2. Build a multilingual embedding index for texts and images (open pre-trained models, separately).

3. A semantic search API with filters (price, category, stock) handling spelling mistakes and synonyms.

4. An autocomplete tool with suggestions while typing, embeddable in the store.

5. Re-rank results based on simple customer behavior (clicks/cart) for the first 2-4 weeks.

6. A dashboard: index status, query log, and conversion rate attributed to search.

7. A ready-made front-end store widget/script with simple visual customization.

8. Two operating modes: managed cloud, or self-install from one Docker image.

4) Pricing and revenue model

• Free-to-start: up to 1,000 products and 5,000 queries/month — includes core functions to test value.

• Growth subscription: fixed monthly fee based on index size and query count (switch packages as you scale).

• Enterprise license: self-hosting + result optimization support and model customization, at an annual price.

• Optional add-ons: image search, advanced reports, and additional team accounts.

• Revenue model: recurring subscriptions as the main source, with a "free = acquisition" strategy converted upon growth.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Launch on community platforms: Product Hunt, Hacker News, Indie Hackers.

• Publish a free component in app stores (Shopify App Store, WooCommerce/WordPress) and rank high on "free".

• Arabic/English SEO content: articles on search speed and conversion impact on sales, plus comparison guides and benchmarks.

• A dual "Design in Public" practice on Twitter/LinkedIn with internal numbers on conversion improvements.

• Short video lessons on YouTube and TikTok to attract store owners.

• Referral partnerships with e-commerce store-building agencies and marketers (percentage commission).

6) Risks (legal and technical)

• Legal: trade name conflicts (check trademarks before launch), privacy compliance (GDPR for those sending user data), rights to use embedding models (open models with explicit licenses preferred), and liability for customers' catalog image content.

• Technical: poor Arabic and specialized-category result quality (requires continuous model tuning), response delays with large indexes, the "cold start" problem for personalization before behavior data exists, image-matching accuracy limits, and quality drift on updates — so we rely on periodic re-evaluation on solid sets.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Kashshaf

Marketing tagline: Search the way your customer talks, and discover what they love before they ask for it.
