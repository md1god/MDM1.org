Rasad — Complete Project Plan

One platform that understands your users' behavior and reveals what should be fixed before anyone complains.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Original product plan for a product analytics and behavior tracking platform

1) Target Audience

• Emerging product development teams (2–50 engineers) that want to understand user behavior without building complex data infrastructure.

• Growth and product marketing teams looking for a clear conversion funnel and data-driven decisions.

• Individual developers and SaaS freelancers who need a lightweight tool that starts free and scales later.

• Support and QA teams that track errors and their impact on the user journey.

2) Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• A free start with no credit card and a generous limit that genuinely suffices for small projects.

• Setup in under five minutes with a single snippet, with no need for a data team or data warehouse.

• Everything in one place: events, user journeys, conversion funnels, and errors automatically linked together.

• Privacy first: sensitive data masked by default and a self-hosting option for sensitive teams.

• A native Arabic/English interface with full right-to-left (RTL) support — a market neglected by competitors.

• Open source for the core to build developer trust, with a paid cloud layer for convenience.

• A smart assistant built on your data's context that suggests the causes of a problem, not just numbers.

3) Scope of the First Version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Event collection via a lightweight SDK (Web + a CLI tool for quick testing).

2. A User Journeys dashboard and a conversion Funnel.

3. Core metrics: active users, churn rate, retention.

4. Error tracking and linking each error to the preceding user journey.

5. Sensitive data masking and deleting a user's data on request.

6. A self-hosted version via a single Docker command.

7. A bilingual interface (Arabic/English) with full RTL support.

8. Team invitation system and basic permissions (owner/member).

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free: 1 million events per month, two users, 3-month retention — to start with no commitment.

• Growth — paid monthly: more events, one-year retention, alerts, unlimited members, and priority support.

• Enterprise: managed self-hosting, SSO, compliance contracts, and dedicated support.

• An additional revenue source: deployment consulting for large organizations, and paid add-ons (advanced integrations).

5) Global Go-to-Market Plan with Free Channels

• Publishing the core on open-source repositories and tech pages (Hacker News, Product Hunt).

• Educational content in Arabic and English: "How to measure X" articles and short video lessons.

• Community support via Discord and Slack, and a developer ambassador program.

• Integrations with well-known community tools (Trello, Notion, Slack) to attract their user base.

• A "Build in Public" series on X/LinkedIn with transparent numbers.

• Open-source contributions and fast issue resolution to attract contributors.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal: data protection laws (GDPR/CCPA) and responsibility for storing user data — the solution: explicit consent, default masking, and a clear retention policy.

• Legal: the possibility of similarity in name or features with existing products — the solution: an early trademark check and clear differentiation.

• Technical: massive event volume and cost — the solution: an efficient streaming architecture (Queue + columnar storage) and pre-aggregation.

• Technical: open-code privacy versus the revenue model — the solution: a clear separation between the free core and cloud features.

• Technical: measurement accuracy and ad-blockers — the solution: first-party endpoints and compensating metrics.

7) Proposed Name and Tagline

Proposed name: Rasad

Tagline: One platform that understands your users' behavior and reveals what should be fixed before anyone complains.
