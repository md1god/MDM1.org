Mirsaad AI — Complete Project Plan

Your global observatory for measuring your brand’s presence in AI answers and turning gaps into an actionable content plan.

Complete plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue, launch, and risks. The project is under construction and not yet launched.

1) Target Audience

• Marketing/content teams at SMBs relying on search and blogs.

• SEO/content agencies managing multiple brands and needing resellable reports.

• Startup and SaaS founders wanting to know how their brand appears in AI answers.

• Brand and PR managers tracking reputation and citations.

• Global English/Arabic markets, prioritizing multilingual companies.

2) Differentiators (Why our product)

• Repeatable measurement: same Prompts run periodically, every answer saved, not a random snapshot.

• Evidence, not vague numbers: each result links to the citation, URL, and snippet mentioning the brand.

• Competitive gap analysis: identifies sources cited for competitors but not the client.

• Actionable recommendations tied to specific content assets: pages, guides, data, or third-party sources.

• Multilingual from day one, including Arabic, with understanding of engine differences.

• Methodological transparency: engine version, sample size, confidence interval, and analysis limits shown.

• Lightweight experience: initial scan without registration, then a permanent free quota for repeat use.

3) First-Version Scope (max 8 tasks)

1. Create a brand project and add up to 3 competitors.

2. Generate or import 15 targeted Prompts across purchase intent, comparison, and problem.

3. Query ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Perplexity via official APIs, storing full answers and version data.

4. Automatically extract brand/competitor mentions, citations, and source links.

5. Analyze sentiment as positive/neutral/negative with a confidence score and optional manual review.

6. Calculate visibility metrics: mention rate, citation share, average ranking, and competitor gap.

7. Generate recommendations tied to assets: missing content, a source to target, or a claim needing support.

8. Public shareable report, CSV/PDF export, and enforcement of free quota and limits.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Permanent free: 1 project, 15 Prompts, 4 engines, weekly runs, last 4 reports.

• Starter — $19/month: 3 projects, 50 Prompts, daily runs, 3-month history.

• Growth — $79/month: 10 projects, 300 Prompts, 5 competitors, full export, limited API.

• Agency — $199/month: 30 projects, team seats, scheduled reports, white label.

• Extra credits for heavy usage, 20% annual discount, 25% referral commission for 3 months.

• Revenue mainly subscriptions, later aggregated sector reports and API.

5) Global Launch Plan (Free Channels)

• Free launch on Product Hunt and Show HN with an open sector comparison report.

• Responsible programmatic SEO: “How your brand is mentioned in AI answers” and data-driven comparison pages, not thin pages.

• Free weekly index of most-cited sources in selected sectors, with aggregated, anonymized data.

• LinkedIn/X series: before/after case, discovered gap, implemented recommendation.

• Valuable participation in r/SEO, r/marketing, and agency groups; direct links only when requested.

• YouTube and short lessons: “Check your brand’s presence in 10 minutes,” with a free Prompt template.

• Partnerships with small agencies and universities, plus WordPress, Webflow, and Shopify integration guides.

• Referral program giving scan credits per active customer, plus English/Arabic landing pages.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Model provider terms: official API only, respect rate limits, no misleading resale, periodic policy review.

• Privacy: no personal data to models, data minimization, GDPR/CCPA compliance, clear retention policy.

• Output instability: results vary between runs; use multiple samples, confidence intervals, and trends, not absolute numbers.

• Model and price changes: record engine versions, run regression tests, set monthly budgets, use cheaper analysis models.

• Sentiment/recommendation errors: automation may err or mislead; show confidence, use testable hypotheses, offer human review.

• Prompt Injection and misleading sources: sanitize inputs, do not execute external page instructions, verify links.

• IP and reputation: do not copy engine text; use limited excerpts with attribution; avoid defamatory claims about brands.

7) Proposed Name and Tagline

Proposed name: Mirsaad AI

Tagline: Your global observatory for measuring your brand’s presence in AI answers and turning gaps into an actionable content plan.
