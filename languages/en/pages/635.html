Ruknly — Complete Project Plan

Mini apps that install in minutes and work together from one dashboard to grow with your store.

Complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, go-to-market, and risks. It is under construction, not yet launched.

1) Target Audience

• Independent Shopify merchants and small and medium brands (10–500 orders/month) seeking better conversion without 5–10 heavy apps.

• Stores in emerging markets (Gulf, Egypt, Morocco, South Asia) with high price sensitivity and burdensome dollar subscriptions.

• Shopify agencies and consultants managing several stores needing fast deployment of unified tools.

• E-commerce teams needing native Arabic and RTL support.

2) Differentiators (Why choose our product)

The problem: Merchants need multiple small functions, but the market forces separate apps, multiple subscriptions, and heavy performance.

• Interconnected suite: Each app works standalone; multiple installs share session, billing, and analytics.

• One invoice and a permanent free plan instead of 5–10 subscriptions.

• Performance first: lazy-loaded UI, no heavy libraries, and Core Web Vitals checks before every release.

• Native Arabic/RTL, not afterthought translation.

• Original identity and design: custom design system and components built from scratch, not templates.

• One-click install/uninstall with data export or immediate deletion.

• Cross-app analytics reveal each store's best tool mix.

• Agency workspace for multiple stores under one identity, with transparent pricing and no hidden fees.

3) Scope of the first version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Unified registration and one-click install (Shopify OAuth + App Bridge), with data isolation per store.

2. One dashboard + unified billing + subscription management + 7-day trial.

3. Bundles app: create simple bundles and sync inventory and prices.

4. Upsell app: offers on product page and cart.

5. Quantity Breaks app: clear, editable discount rules.

6. Trust badges and countdown app to improve conversion.

7. Media player app (music/short video) with a lightweight UI.

8. Unified analytics + cross-sell between apps + RTL/Arabic support.

Note: Virtual try-on is postponed to version two due to complexity and cost.

4) Pricing and profit model

• Permanent free tier: 100 orders/month per app, no card required.

• Starter: $9/month per app up to 1,000 orders.

• Growth: $19/month per app up to 5,000 orders.

• Bundle: $39/month for all apps up to 5,000 orders.

• Agency: $99/month up to 10 stores + agency dashboard.

• Overage: $5 per 1,000 orders.

• Payment via Shopify Billing API, 7-day paid trial, immediate cancellation.

• Revenue: subscriptions + usage + agency packages + paid customization/support. We do not sell merchant data.

5) Global go-to-market plan with free channels

• Shopify App Store SEO: standalone page per app, specific keywords, screenshots, short video, and cross-linked apps.

• Free site tools: bundles savings calculator, quantity discount calculator, and trust badge generator to attract SEO traffic.

• Educational content: Arabic/English articles and YouTube tutorials, plus "alternatives" comparison pages.

• Communities: Shopify forum, Reddit/Facebook merchant groups, and Discord/Slack, sharing value not ads.

• Agency program: free dashboard + 20% commission on referrals.

• Launch: Product Hunt, Indie Hackers, BetaList, Show HN.

• Merchant referrals: one free month per party.

• Local landing pages for the Gulf, Egypt, India.

6) Risks (legal and technical)

Legal:

• Shopify Partner/App Store terms compliance; review rejection or app suspension.

• GDPR/CCPA/PDPL (Saudi Arabia, UAE): consent, data retention, right to deletion/export.

• Billing, refunds, taxes, and consumer protection.

• Intellectual property: original code, UI, identity; open-source license checks.

• Accessibility (WCAG/EAA) and RTL support.

Technical:

• API limits, webhook reliability, service outages.

• Theme compatibility (Online Store 2.0/Hydrogen) and checkout extensibility limits.

• Tenant isolation, token encryption, secrets management.

• Bundle edge cases: inventory, taxes, shipping, currency rounding.

• Performance degradation or app conflicts.

• Shopify dependency: independent integration layer and data export to reduce lock-in.

Mitigation: modular monolith, feature flags, staging, automated tests, monitoring, security review.

7) Proposed name and tagline

Proposed name: Ruknly

Tagline: Mini apps that install in minutes and work together from one dashboard to grow with your store.
