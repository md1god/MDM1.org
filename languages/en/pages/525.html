Dermicue — Complete Project Plan

Try your dream tattoo before it's inked forever.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, outreach plan, and risks. The project is currently under development and has not yet been launched.

1) Target Audience

• Tattoo enthusiasts: Those who want to try a tattoo design before getting it permanently inked.

• Beginner tattoo artists: Those who need a tool to explain client ideas quickly and professionally.

• Couples and groups: Those looking for a shared experience to choose one design together.

• Temporary fashion enthusiasts: Those who want to try different looks without a permanent commitment.

2) Key Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

• Realistic simulation: Using simple light-based modeling to show how a design fits the curves of the body, rather than just a flat overlay.

• Professional artist sheet: Automatically generates measurements, angle, color, placement area, and ready-to-send notes for the artist.

• Guaranteed privacy: Body images are not permanently stored in the cloud; they are processed locally as much as possible.

• Freedom of customization: Create designs from text or a reference image with precise control over position, size, and rotation.

• Free to start: All core features are free with no need for complicated registration.

3) Scope of the First Version (Maximum of 8 Tasks)

1. Upload body image: A simple interface to capture or upload an image with guidance for good lighting.

2. Import design: Upload a design image or generate one from a text description.

3. Editing tools: Drag, resize, rotate, and flip the design.

4. Realistic layer: Apply a subtle effect to blend the design with the skin.

5. Quick comparison: Side-by-side view of two different designs on the same image.

6. Generate technical sheet: Create a ready summary including placement, size, colors, and notes.

7. Export: Save a preview image and a text file of the artist sheet.

8. Local mode: Run all core functions without requiring account creation.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

• Free to start: All first-version tasks are fully free to use.

• Ready-made design library: Offer a selection of licensed designs for sale as digital files for a low commission.

• Advanced technical sheet templates: Add professional, customizable templates available as a one-time purchase.

• Partnerships with tattoo studios: Earn commission by referring real clients through links, without requiring a subscription for regular users.

5) Global Outreach Plan Through Free Channels

• Creative forums: Participate in online design and fashion communities to showcase a tested version of the tool.

• Short video platforms: Create before/after clips to demonstrate the realistic effect, with a direct link to the tool.

• Artist directories: List the tool in free directories aimed at tattoo artists as an aid for their clients.

• Open translations: Provide the interface in many common languages with help from volunteer contributors.

• Organic search: Create simple content that answers common questions about trying a tattoo before getting inked.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal: Avoid collecting sensitive data, comply with image privacy policies, and refrain from any medical claims or permanent guarantees.

• Technical: Achieving realistic blending across all skin tones and lighting conditions is difficult, so the focus should be on an accurate, adjustable visual effect.

• Security: Process images locally when possible, and avoid sending full images to servers unless absolutely necessary and with explicit consent.

• Performance: Optimize images for lower-end devices to prevent slow performance on the web.

7) Proposed Name and Marketing Slogan

Proposed name: Dermicue

Marketing slogan: Try your dream tattoo before it's inked forever.
