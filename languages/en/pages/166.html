OrionLink — Complete Project Plan

Your private network, secure, and accessible from anywhere.

A complete implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, business model, rollout plan, and risks. The project is under development and has not yet been launched.

1) Target Audience

  • Developers: Need secure access to their servers or development environments from anywhere.
  • Home server owners: Want to access their personal devices without opening direct ports to the internet.
  • Small remote teams: Looking for a fast and secure way to connect team devices together.
  • Privacy-conscious individuals: Want a private, self-controlled network to protect themselves while using the internet.

2) Key Differentiators (Why Choose Our Product)

  • Ease of use: A simple interface that allows connecting devices with just a single invitation, without complex configuration.
  • Built-in security: Uses the WireGuard protocol with mandatory two-factor authentication (2FA).
  • Free to start: Completely free to begin, with the option for self-hosting at no cost.
  • Lightweight design: Focused on performance and minimizing resource consumption on low-powered devices.
  • Optional decentralization: Supports self-hosting, giving users full control over their data.
  • Privacy-first: Does not collect tracking data or virtual activity logs by default.

3) Scope of First Version (Max 8 Tasks)

1. Authentication management: Support email login + 2FA or Passkeys.

2. Key management: Automatically generate and exchange WireGuard keys between devices.

3. Network routing: Implement Mesh routing to enable direct communication between devices.

4. Device approval: Simple invitation system to approve new devices.

5. Access Control Lists (ACLs): Simple settings to control which devices can communicate with each other.

6. User interface: Simple design for desktop and mobile.

7. CLI tool: For advanced users to automate setup.

8. Installation scripts: Ready-made scripts for common systems to speed up setup.

4) Pricing and Revenue Model

  • Permanently free: Full-featured self-hosting is free.
  • Free hosted tier: A reasonable limit on devices and groups for new users at no cost.
  • Optional add-ons: Team-focused features (such as audit logs, advanced team management, and priority support) via a low-cost monthly subscription.
  • Slow-growth model: Focus on community growth first, then generate revenue only from add-ons, without charging for basic usage.

5) Global Distribution Plan via Free Channels

  • Technical communities: Engage with Self-Hosting and Homelab communities like Reddit and Lemmy.
  • Educational content: Create short videos and written tutorials on platforms like YouTube and Dev.to in common languages.
  • Developer forums: Interact in forums like Hacker News and Indie Hackers to showcase the idea.
  • Open documentation: Provide clear documentation in Arabic and English to facilitate adoption.
  • Community localization: Encourage volunteers to translate the interface and documentation into multiple languages.
  • Word of mouth: Focus on a simple experience that encourages users to share it with others.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

Legal Risks

  • Regulatory restrictions: Some countries impose restrictions on certain encryption tools, so the product's purpose (a secure private network) must be clearly stated.
  • Data privacy: Comply with best privacy practices, with a focus on not collecting sensitive data.
  • Licensing: Use clear open-source licenses to maintain transparency and protect the project.

Technical Risks

  • NAT traversal: Connection may face difficulties on some networks, so alternative solutions such as Relays must be supported with a simple design.
  • Key management: A secure mechanism is needed for key rotation and access recovery in case a device is lost.
  • Security: Ensure proper implementation of WireGuard with mandatory 2FA, and conduct regular security audits.
  • Scalability: Maintain design simplicity to avoid complexity as the user base grows.

7) Proposed Name and Tagline

Proposed name: OrionLink

Tagline: Your private network, secure, and accessible from anywhere.
