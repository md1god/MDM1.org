TraceZero — Complete Project Plan

Find out who sells your data, then erase your digital footprint step by step, for free.

A complete execution plan for a new digital project: the idea, audience, product, revenue model, growth plan, and risks. The project is under construction and has not launched yet.

Product plan: a free tool to detect exposure of your data at data brokers and help you remove it.

1) Target Audience

• Ordinary individuals interested in privacy (25–55 years old) who want to know who owns their data without technical expertise.

• Families: parents who want to protect their children's data and minors under their guardianship.

• Victims of data breaches (victims of phishing and impersonation) seeking rapid removal.

• Freelancers and small business owners exposed to professional impersonation.

• Legally sensitive groups: healthcare workers, lawyers, spokespeople, crime victims — those who need to reduce public visibility.

• Primary market: the United States (especially California with its active legislation), then the European Union, then the Gulf.

2) Points of Distinction (Why choose our product)

• Fully free at its core: exposure detection and basic removal guidance with no payment or registration — unlike tools that hide results behind a paywall.

• Step-by-step guidance, not just a report: we turn the result into a ready-made action list (link + request template + follow-up deadline), instead of throwing the user into a maze of forms.

• Based on the official legal record: we rely on the official broker registry and updated privacy deletion tools, not stale static lists.

• Complete transparency: we show the source of every piece of information and how it was found, without concealment.

• Free family coverage: add family members under the same account with no multiplied fees.

• Neutrality: we never sell user data and never share it with the brokers themselves.

3) Scope of the First Version (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Detection engine: check name/email/phone/city across a prioritized set of brokers without permanent data storage.

2. Exposure dashboard: show the brokers holding the information and the sensitivity level of each match.

3. Guided removal guide: an interactive form that generates a customized deletion/objection request for each broker.

4. Legal correspondence templates: official-entity templates for deletion and objection with execution links.

5. Request tracker: the status of each request (sent/in progress/done/rejected) with follow-up alerts.

6. Breached email checker: optional email linking to search for exposure in leaks.

7. Self-protection guide: brief tips to reduce the footprint (accounts, settings, passwords).

8. Impact dashboard: a "data removed" and "open requests" counter to encourage continuation.

4) Pricing and Profit Model

• Free (Free-to-Start): full detection + removal guidance for the top 5 brokers + a basic request tracker.

• Monthly Pro plan: continuous monitoring, periodic automatic removal, coverage of all brokers, a larger number of family members — at a symbolic price.

• Annual plan with a prepayment discount.

• Additional revenue sources: family/team subscriptions, compliance reports for small companies, and licensing the tool to nonprofit organizations — without ever selling user data.

5) Global Growth Plan with Free Channels

• Educational SEO content: Arabic and English guides on "deleting your data from data brokers" and "deletion rights" — attracting high-intent demand.

• A shareable free tool: a public tool page with no registration that spreads on its own.

• Reddit and privacy forums + Hacker News via a "we built it transparently" story.

• Product Hunt at launch.

• Partnerships with journalists, privacy activists, and tech influencers.

• Local channels: Arab and tech communities in the Gulf, and consumer protection organizations.

• Free referral program: a free Pro month for a successful referral.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Legal: privacy and the collection of sensitive data — we build a "no permanent storage" architecture, encryption, and explicit consent, with a review of laws such as GDPR/CCPA.

• Scraping limits: broker sites may block checking — we balance official tools, public interfaces, and respect for terms of use.

• Accuracy of results: a person's internet presence may appear for someone else (error rates) — we highlight confidence levels clearly.

• Impersonation and malice: the tool could be used to harm — identity verification and limiting campaigns.

• Technical sustainability: broker interfaces change constantly — we rely on flexible centralized updates.
