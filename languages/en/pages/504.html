Mihwar — Complete Project Plan

Turn your consulting expertise into sales-ready proposals and deal campaigns—without a marketing team.

Implementation plan for a new digital project: idea, audience, product, revenue model, launch plan, and risks. Under construction; not yet launched.

1) Target Audience

• Independent consultants and experts selling knowledge via daily or monthly models.

• Small consulting firms (2–20 people) lacking marketing or dedicated sales.

• Agencies and Fractional Executives needing fast, customizable proposals and campaigns.

• Coaches and professional providers in the Arab market, Europe, and North America; Arabic and English first.

• Core problem: experts solve problems but waste time turning knowledge into clear proposals, organized campaigns, and sales follow-ups.

2) Differentiators

• Built on users' own knowledge, not cloning another expert; no imitation of identity, voice, or image.

• Turns scattered materials (files, lectures, call notes, articles) into sales assets, citing each source.

• Focuses on sales execution, not just text: proposal, pricing, campaign, call script, technical proposal, follow-up.

• Arabic-first interface with RTL, bilingual outputs, and customization by country and sector.

• Stronger privacy: isolated workspace, no customer-data training by default, clear permissions.

• Results loop: win/loss reasons improve later proposals and campaigns.

• Free start without a credit card; full ownership export of outputs.

3) MVP Scope (maximum 8 tasks)

1. Create a workspace and import knowledge from files, a website, and call notes, with indexing and citations.

2. Proposal builder: turn problem into promise, value, service package, pricing, guarantee, success metrics.

3. Define the ideal segment, value messages, and common objections.

4. Generate a 4-week marketing/sales campaign: email, LinkedIn, short content, and calls to action.

5. Call and qualification guide: discovery questions, objection scenarios, and follow-ups.

6. Technical proposal and SOW generator in editable format.

7. Record deal outcomes (win/loss/postpone); auto-update templates.

8. Export/share: PDF/DOCX, collaboration link, and initial email or CRM integration.

4) Pricing and Profit Model

• Free: one workspace, 3 proposals monthly, 20 generation operations, basic Arabic/English templates.

• Pro — $29/month: practically unlimited proposals, campaigns, full export, larger knowledge memory.

• Team — $79/month: 3 seats, collaboration, permissions, results reports.

• Agency — $199/month: 10 seats, multiple client workspaces, custom templates.

• Additional revenue: usage add-ons, 20% annual discount, optional template marketplace at 30% commission, white-label later.

• No mandatory early subscription or setup fees; paid conversion begins after free-tier limits.

5) Global Go-to-Market Plan with Free Channels

• Programmatic SEO pages in Arabic, English, French, and Spanish: “consulting proposal template for [sector]” and “campaign plan for [specialty] services.”

• Practical content on LinkedIn and X: anonymized proposal breakdowns and free copyable templates.

• Freelancer and small-business groups on Reddit, Discord, and Slack, sharing value first, not ads.

• Launch on Product Hunt and emerging product platforms, with a limited free founders' offer.

• Free partnerships with coworking spaces, incubators, and freelancing platforms.

• Weekly newsletter, short YouTube series, and referral program granting free credit.

• Localize pages by country and language; free tools like a “proposal clarity checker” for traffic.

6) Risks (Legal and Technical)

• Privacy/compliance: GDPR and PDPL, data processing agreements, explicit consent, full deletion on request.

• Intellectual property: users own outputs; block materials they lack rights to; never imitate a real expert's persona, voice, or image.

• Professional liability: outputs are assistance, not guaranteed legal, financial, or tax advice.

• Hallucination/errors: mandatory citations, human review before export, periodic quality checks.

• Data security: encryption in transit and at rest, tenant isolation, granular permissions, audit logging.

• Cost/provider dependence: usage limits, caching, an alternative model to cut cost and downtime risk.

• Prompt injection/leakage: input filtering, user-context separation, security tests before release.

• Multilingual quality: human review for Arabic and dialects; no claims for untested languages.

7) Proposed Name and Tagline

Proposed name: Mihwar

Tagline: Turn your consulting expertise into sales-ready proposals and deal campaigns—without a marketing team.
