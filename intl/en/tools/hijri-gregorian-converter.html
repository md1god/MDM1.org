FAQ

How do I calculate the corresponding date?

Choose the conversion direction, enter the day, month, and year, then click Convert now. The other date, with the month name, will appear in the result field.

What is the supported range?

The converter supports Gregorian dates from 1900 to 2077 and the corresponding Umm al-Qura years. Outside this range, calendar data may not be available in the browser.

Is my data stored?

No. All calculations happen on the page, with no data upload or storage outside the browser.

Why does the day sometimes differ from a printed calendar?

Calendars may differ due to different observation methods or calculation tables across references. This converter uses the calendar data available in the browser and is suitable for general use and quick comparison.

Can I copy the result?

Yes, the result appears in a text field you can select and copy directly into your documents or spreadsheets.
