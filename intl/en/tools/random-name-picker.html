Random raffle and winner picker

On-page tool to paste a list of names or options, draw one or more winners without repeats, or split the list into equal teams, with your last list saved…

Quick answer: You can easily pick a winner or split a group into teams with one on-page tool. Paste any list of names or options, set the number of winners or equal teams, and the tool runs the random draw instantly while saving your last list in the browser so you can use it again without re-entering it. It handles large lists and ensures winners aren't repeated and members are distributed fairly across teams. Just enter the details and press «Start». Results appear at the bottom of the page, and you can copy them or rerun the tool anytime. The last list is also stored locally, so it's easy to restore when you visit again, saving you time and effort. Enjoy a fast, simple way to pick winners or form teams.













FAQ

How is the last list saved?

The list you paste is stored in your browser using localStorage, and when you visit the page again it's loaded automatically into the input field.

Can I pick more than one winner?

Yes, you can set the number of winners in the «Number of winners» field. Winners are drawn randomly without repeats.

How is the list split into teams?

After enabling «Split into equal teams», enter the number of teams you need. The tool redistributes the names randomly across the teams, keeping them as equal as possible.

Does the tool support large lists?

Yes, the interface can handle lists with hundreds of items, but keep in mind that performance may be affected in older browsers.

Can I use the tool offline?

Absolutely, all operations run client-side and require no network requests, so you can use it even when you're offline.
