Convert cooking measurements accurately: cups, spoons, grams, milliliters and oven temperature

Easily convert cooking measurements between cups, tablespoons, teaspoons, milliliters, and grams for flour, sugar, rice, butter, and water, with oven…

Quick answer: One cup equals 240 ml, a tablespoon is 15 ml, and a teaspoon is 5 ml, while converting amounts to grams depends on the density of the ingredient itself. That's why there is no single rule that works for flour, sugar, rice, butter, and water, as each ingredient has a different weight for the same volume. This tool helps you convert any amount in cups, spoons, or milliliters to grams, and shows you the result in all units at a glance. It also lets you convert oven temperatures between Celsius and Fahrenheit accurately, so you can set your recipe correctly before you start baking or cooking.

Approximate weights used: a cup of flour weighs 120 g, a cup of sugar 200 g, a cup of rice 185 g, a cup of butter 227 g, and a cup of water 240 g.

Frequently asked questions

How many milliliters are in one cup?

The standard cup used in recipes equals 240 ml, which is 16 tablespoons or 48 teaspoons.

Why does the weight of a cup vary by ingredient?

Because each ingredient has a different density; a cup of flour weighs about 120 g, while a cup of water weighs 240 g, so converting to grams depends on the type of ingredient.

How do I convert oven temperature from Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply the Celsius temperature by 9 then divide by 5 and add 32. For example, 180 degrees Celsius equals 356 degrees Fahrenheit.

Are the results completely accurate?

The results are approximate and depend on the measuring method and density used; use a digital scale for recipes that need high precision.

Do I need an internet connection to use the tool?

No, the tool runs entirely in your browser with no internet connection or sign-up.
