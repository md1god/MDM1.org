FAQ

How are overtime hours calculated?

Each day's net work hours are compared with the standard hours you set in the settings. Any time above that limit is recorded as overtime, and anything below counts as regular hours.

What should I do if the clock-out time is after midnight?

Enter the clock-out time exactly as shown on the clock, without changing it. The calculator will recognize that the elapsed time is shorter than the clock-in time and automatically treat it as the next day.

Is break time included in pay?

No. The break is subtracted from the total time between clock-in and clock-out before hours are calculated, and only net time is counted. You can enter zero if there's no break.

How is pay for overtime calculated?

The hourly rate is multiplied by the overtime multiplier you enter, then the result is multiplied by the number of overtime hours. The final pay is the sum of standard pay and overtime pay.

Can I use the calculator without signing up?

Yes. All calculations happen directly in your browser, with no need for an account, profile, or connection to any external service. Your results aren't saved or sent anywhere.
