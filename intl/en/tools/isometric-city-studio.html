FAQ

Do I need to sign up or connect to the internet?

No. The drawing runs entirely in your browser, and you can change the settings and seed to get a new result without uploading any data.

How do I get a different layout every time?

Enter another value in the seed field or press the Random seed button. The same value always gives the same drawing, which makes it easy to repeat the experiment later.

What does building density mean?

It sets the share of squares that become buildings instead of roads and parks. Choose Light for an open city, or Dense for a busy neighborhood.

Can I save the result?

Yes. The Download PNG button saves the displayed image, and the Copy description button copies a summary of the grid, the number of buildings and roads, and the seed.

Are the names I enter saved?

They are used only in the current drawing to label a few places, and are not sent or stored after you close the page.
