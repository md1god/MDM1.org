Find the right Vim command in seconds: type what you want to do or paste an existing command, and get the explanation, mode, and correct syntax with…

Quick answer: type what you want to do or paste a command you're stuck on, and you'll see a list of suitable commands with a short explanation, the mode it works in, and copyable syntax. Many people learn a few shortcuts, then stop at the first unfamiliar task, wasting time browsing long references or forgetting the difference between modes. Here you can describe the task in simple words, or choose a category like movement, editing, copying, search, and files, then narrow results by mode. Each command appears with a practical example and a tip about the context where it works, so you understand why it works, not just what it does. The page runs entirely in the browser, with no account, connection, or storage, and your text stays on your device. Try typing «save», «undo», or «copy line», and you'll find what you need right away.

FAQ

Do I need to install or sign up?

No. Open the page and start right away; no download, account, or setup.

How do I search for a command when I don't know its name?

Type what you want to do in simple words like save, undo, or copy line. The search compares meaning and synonyms, then you can narrow results by category and mode.

What do the modes shown with each result mean?

Normal mode is for navigation and commands, Insert for writing, Visual for applying a change to selected text, and Command for input that starts with a colon at the bottom of the screen.

Is the text I type saved?

No. Everything runs in the browser; there's no internet connection and no storage for any text you enter.
