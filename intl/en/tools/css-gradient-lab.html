FAQ

What does this tool do?

It turns your color and position choices into a ready-to-use CSS gradient, with a live preview and text you can copy directly.

Do I need to upload images or create an account?

No. Everything runs in the browser, and colors or names aren't sent to any other party.

When should I choose a conic gradient?

Use it for wheels, percentage indicators, and backgrounds that rotate around a central point instead of moving along a single line.

What does the displayed contrast percentage mean?

It's an estimate of the brightness difference between the two ends of the gradient; the higher it is, the clearer the difference, which is useful when placing text over the gradient.
