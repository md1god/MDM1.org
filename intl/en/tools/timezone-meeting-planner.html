Quick answer: Choose the reference city, enter the meeting date and time, then select the cities you'd like to compare and click Calculate to instantly show local times for each city with clear highlighting of suitable working hours. This planner helps you coordinate meetings between Cairo, Riyadh, Dubai, London, and New York without manual calculations or searching other sites. The calculation uses the time zone rules in your browser, so daylight saving and seasonal changes are handled automatically, and it shows the exact time difference between any two cities. It also highlights times within normal working hours, so you can pick a slot that suits everyone easily and without awkwardness. Whether you're coordinating a business meeting, a work call, or a cross-border meetup, this tool saves you time and gives you a complete view of the right time for each party before you send the invite.

FAQ

How do I choose the reference city?

The reference city is the city whose local time you use for the meeting. Select it from the list first, then enter the date and time, and times in the other cities will be calculated from it automatically.

Does the planner account for daylight saving time?

Yes, the browser uses each city's official time zone rules, so the time difference changes automatically when daylight saving time starts or when standard time returns.

What do the highlighted working hours mean?

It's the period from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. local time in each city, Monday through Friday. It appears shaded green when inside working hours and neutral when outside.

Do I need an internet connection to use the tool?

No, the planner runs entirely in your browser without sending any data to an external server, and you can use it anytime even if you're offline.
