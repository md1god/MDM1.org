Rent vs. Buy Calculator: Which option costs less?

Compare renting and buying costs over the years you choose: calculate payments, interest, ownership costs, and rent increases with clear assumptions to…

Quick answer: Start by comparing the total cash you'll actually pay over the period that matters to you, not just the monthly payment. This calculator compares renting and buying costs side by side over the number of years you choose, using the property price, down payment, financing share and term, annual financing rate, and monthly rent. You can also add expected annual rent increases, annual ownership costs, and the return your money could have earned if you invested it instead of paying it upfront. It shows a clear number telling you which option costs less in your case, then breaks down the monthly payment, remaining loan balance, and cash needed at purchase. All assumptions are shown in the results so you can adjust them or ignore what doesn't fit your situation before deciding.

Enter the numbers as they apply to you; you can leave optional fields at their defaults.

Assumptions used in the calculation:

1) The monthly payment is based on equal installments with monthly compounding over the financing term.

2) The property value is assumed to stay the same at the end of the comparison period, with no selling or brokerage fees included.

3) The unfinanced amount is paid in cash at purchase, plus any remaining loan balance if the comparison ends before the loan is paid off.

4) Annual ownership costs are calculated as a fixed share of the property price and spread evenly over the comparison years.

5) Rent increases annually by the rate you enter, while the financing payment stays fixed.

6) The alternative return is calculated only on the cash paid upfront, and the effects of inflation or taxes are not included.

This calculation is an estimate based on the numbers and assumptions you entered; review the details with your lender before taking on any commitment.

FAQ

What exactly does the calculator compare?

It compares the total you actually pay over the number of years you choose for both renting and buying, showing the monthly payment, remaining balance, ownership costs, and rent increases.

How are financing payments calculated?

The financed amount is split into equal monthly payments calculated from the annual financing rate and term, with interest compounded monthly on the balance.

What happens if the comparison period is shorter than the financing term?

The remaining loan balance is added to the cost of buying at the end of the comparison period, so the result is fair as if the property had been fully paid off during that period.

Does the calculator include property appreciation?

No; the calculator assumes a constant property value and no selling fees, but it lets you enter annual rent increases and annual ownership costs to bring the result closer to your situation.

How do I read the alternative return result?

The alternative return is what the cash could have earned if invested instead of being paid upfront; an estimated amount is added only to the cost of buying to show the opportunity cost of capital.
