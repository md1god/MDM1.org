Frequently Asked Questions

How does the calculator compute the cumulative GPA?

It multiplies each course's grade points by its credit hours, adds them together, then divides by the total credit hours to get the semester GPA. It then combines previous semesters' points with this semester's points to accurately calculate the new cumulative GPA.

What is the difference between the 4-point and 5-point scales?

The 4-point scale is the most common in universities, while the 5-point scale is used by some institutions. Choose the scale adopted by your university, and the calculator will automatically convert your percentage grade to the appropriate points.

Are my data saved or sent to the server?

No, the tool runs entirely in your browser. No data is sent or saved to any server, and you can use it offline after the page loads.

What should I do if the required GPA is above the scale?

This means your goal is hard to achieve with the specified remaining hours. You can consider increasing the remaining hours, raising your current GPA first, or adjusting your goal to a more realistic value.
