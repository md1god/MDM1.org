FAQ

How is the number of tiles calculated accurately?

The number of tiles is calculated by dividing the floor area by the area of one tile, then adding the specified waste percentage, and finally rounding the result up to the nearest whole number since tiles cannot be divided.

Why is waste added to the calculation?

The waste percentage accounts for loss during cutting, breakage, or installation errors, so it is added to ensure you don't run out of materials during work.

How is the amount of paint needed for walls calculated?

We calculate wall area by multiplying wall height by the room perimeter, then subtract the areas of doors and windows. After that, we divide the net area by the paint coverage rate (L/m²) to get the number of liters.

Can I adjust the size of doors and windows in the calculator?

Yes, you can enter the number of doors and windows as well as their dimensions (width and height) to customize the calculation to your actual design.

What is the best way to round the required amount of paint?

It's always best to round the quantity up to the nearest 0.1 liter to ensure you have enough, especially if the coverage rate varies or the surface is uneven.
