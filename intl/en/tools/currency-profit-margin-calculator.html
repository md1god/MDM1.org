FAQ

How do I choose between margin and markup on cost?

Use margin when you want the profit percentage of the selling price to be known, and use markup when you set a profit above full cost after fees and shipping.

Is tax counted in profit?

No; tax is added to the customer invoice and is not seller revenue. Profit is based on the selling price before tax after applying the discount and platform fees.

What does break-even point mean here?

It is the lowest listed price before tax that covers unit cost, shipping, fixed fees, and variable fees with no profit or loss.

Can I use the calculator for multiple units?

Yes; enter the number of units to see total revenue, cost, and profit alongside the figures for one unit, and you can change the quantity at any time.

What should I do if a warning says the target cannot be reached?

Lower the required margin or markup, or reduce the variable fee rate; some targets cannot be reached when fees are close to the selling price.
