FAQ

Which formats does the tool support?

The tool supports the three common formats: HEX, RGB, and HSL. Enter a value in any field and the other two formats and the preview will update automatically with no extra steps.

How is the contrast ratio calculated?

Relative luminance is calculated for each color according to WCAG, then the larger value is divided by the smaller after adding 0.05. The higher the result, the more legible the text is against the background.

What is the difference between AA and AAA?

Level AA requires a ratio of 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text, while level AAA requires 7:1 for normal text and 4.5:1 for large text. The tool shows a pass or fail result for each level separately.

Are colors sent to any server?

No. All calculations are performed in your browser using local JavaScript, and there is no connection to any network or external service.
