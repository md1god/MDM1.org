Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate the required monthly amount?

Subtract your current savings from the goal, then divide the result by the number of months. The tool rounds the contribution up to two decimal places so you actually reach the amount needed.

What if the remainder doesn't divide evenly across the months?

The contribution shows decimals, and the tool uses the next whole number up to ensure you reach the goal instead of falling slightly short in the last month.

Do the calculations include interest or investment returns?

No, the plan assumes fixed deposits with no return. This keeps the estimate conservative and easy to understand, and you can adjust the monthly amount later if you earn extra returns.

Why do I get an error message when I enter a very long period?

Because the tool caps the period at 1200 months to keep the result practical and the table easy to read. Shorten the period or increase the monthly amount to see the plan.

Are my numbers saved after I use the tool?

No, everything happens in the browser and no data is sent. You can close the page or reset and start with new numbers at any time.
