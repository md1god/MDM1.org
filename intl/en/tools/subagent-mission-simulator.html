Sub-agent simulator — five tasks and a limited budget

Play a sub-agent inside an AI system: read the task, choose the right tool for your budget, and write an accurate answer. Five rounds, instant scoring,…

Quick answer: pick the cheapest tool that actually answers the question, write a short, accurate result, and submit it before the context budget runs out. In this game you'll play a sub-agent working inside a larger system: the task manager sends you a specific request and puts five tools in front of you that differ in cost and usefulness, and some are misleading or harmful. You need to read the request, identify the tool that unlocks the required information, then phrase the final answer in the requested format. Each choice consumes budget units, and every correct answer adds points, while the wrong tool lowers your score. You'll move through five consecutive tasks covering search, policies, calculation, general knowledge, and reading records. At the end you'll see your full score and feedback explaining where you went wrong and how to choose better. You don't need an internet connection or sign-up; everything runs on this page.

FAQ

How is my score calculated?

You get 60 points for a correct answer, up to 30 points for choosing the right tool, and an efficiency bonus of up to 20 points based on the remaining budget. Harmful tools deduct 20 points.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The game runs entirely in your browser, sends no data anywhere, and you can close and reopen the page to start a new round.

What happens if I choose the wrong tool?

You'll get a lower score, and feedback will show which tool you should have used, so you can learn to read the task before acting.

Can I try again?

Yes, the Restart button resets the score and returns you to the first task, and you can play several times to improve.

Why is there a context budget?

Because every tool consumes units, and a good agent balances accuracy against cost; the budget simulates the model's available memory limits.
