Frequently Asked Questions

Does the app require an account or a server?

No, it runs entirely locally in your browser. Your data is saved in local storage and is never sent to any server or account.

How is sleep duration calculated when falling asleep after midnight?

It is calculated automatically by adding 24 hours to the wake-up time if it is earlier than the bedtime, showing the correct duration for records that extend into the next day.

Can I review dreams and caffeine later?

Yes, dreams, notes, caffeine amount, and number of awakenings are saved in an organized log that can be searched, filtered, sorted, and reviewed at any time.

How does the chart display daily and weekly trends?

The chart displays recent selected records as bars for sleep duration with a line for sleep quality. It also shows average sleep duration and the last 7-day average to compare your progress.

Can I export or import data?

Yes, the export button displays a JSON copy that can be copied and saved to a file, and the import button accepts pasted text or a JSON file selected from your device to restore your data.
