City simulation: build your district and collect advertiser coins

A lightweight browser-based city game: buy plots, build billboards and rental spaces, collect coins from advertisers, and track your city's growth with…

Yes, you can play this full city simulation in your browser without installing anything. You start with a coin balance and one plot, then gradually expand your district by buying new land and building billboards and spaces to rent to virtual advertisers. Each location you own generates daily income when rented, and as your district's population or appeal grows, land and rent prices increase. Advance to a new day to collect earnings, and open the sponsored statistics page to track your city's growth, profits, and occupancy. Your progress is saved automatically in your browser's local storage, so you can return later without losing your city, coins, or billboards.

Frequently asked questions

Does the game work without installation or an internet connection?

Yes, it runs entirely in your browser with no installation required. Your progress is saved in your browser's local storage.

How do I collect coins from virtual advertisers?

Build billboards, then rent out empty billboards, advance to a new day, and collect pending earnings.

How do I buy a new plot?

Choose a name and plot type, then click Buy plot. The price increases as your city expands and varies by plot type.

Where can I find my city's growth statistics?

Choose a sponsor and click Open statistics page to view plots, billboards, occupancy, earnings, and visitors, along with a daily bonus.

Will I lose my progress if I close the page?

No, progress is saved automatically after every action in local storage. You can clear storage or reset manually to start a new city.
