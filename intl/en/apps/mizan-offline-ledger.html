Track expenses and income locally

An offline app to track expenses and income with multiple accounts and currencies, categories, tags and notes, monthly charts, plus CSV export and JSON…

Quick answer: This app saves expenses and income in the browser only and works offline. It supports multiple accounts and currencies, categories, tags and detailed notes, monthly charts to summarize spending, and CSV export plus restorable and importable JSON backups. No account or connection is needed, and data stays on your device. Transactions can be filtered by month, account, category, tag and text search, and any transaction can be edited or deleted at any time. You can also manage accounts and categories, calculate balances, and back up your data.

FAQ

Does the app work offline?

Yes, all data is saved in the browser and no connection or account is required.

How do I back up data?

Use Export JSON to save a copy, then import it later to restore.

Can I use multiple currencies?

Yes, each account has its own currency, and balances are shown by account.

How do I filter transactions?

Use the filters by month, account, category, tag and search.

Can I edit or delete a transaction?

Yes, each transaction has edit and delete buttons in the transactions list.
