FAQ

Is my image uploaded to a server?

No. Processing happens in your browser on your device, and images aren't sent to any server.

What formats are supported?

The tool supports JPEG and PNG and WebP images that your browser can open.

Can I export the result?

Yes. You can export the resulting image as PNG or JPEG with one click.

Does it work on an old computer?

Yes. Processing happens locally, and preview resolution is lowered automatically to keep things running smoothly.
