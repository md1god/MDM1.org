FAQ

Do I need an account or email?

No. Open the page and enter your numbers directly, with no signup, email, or subscription.

Is my data saved after I close the page?

No. Calculations happen in the browser only while the page is open, and the page writes no information to device storage or to a server.

Does it work offline?

Once the page is open, it makes no network requests, so the calculator stays usable in the open page even if the connection drops.

How are estimated calories calculated?

The calculator uses activity type, your weight, and workout duration, with a small adjustment for elevation gain; the result is an estimate, not a lab measurement.

Can I use it for cycling?

Yes. Choose cycling, and the default split becomes five kilometers, which you can change manually.
