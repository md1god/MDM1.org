FAQ

Which files can I import?

Local video files and SRT or VTT subtitles or timed text, plus JSON.

Does the tool work offline?

Yes, all processing, previewing, and exporting happen inside the browser.

How do I add a second translation?

Fill in the translation column next to each source line, then enable bilingual mode.

Can I preview the dubbing?

Yes, using the built-in speech engine with a choice of language, voice, and speed.

What gets exported?

A video with burned-in subtitles and original audio, a dubbing script, and an SRT file.
