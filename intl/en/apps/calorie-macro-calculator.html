Calorie and Macro Calculator

Calculate calories, protein, carbs, and fat for any meal instantly, add custom foods, track your daily total, with no account or data storage.

Short answer: enter your food and its amount in grams and get calories, protein, carbs, and fat instantly, with the option to add custom foods and calculate your daily total. This calorie and macro calculator runs entirely in your browser with no account or data storage, making it ideal for anyone tracking nutrition or following diets like keto and intermittent fasting. Values are based on per 100 g standards and can be adjusted manually to match your actual serving size, giving you full control over the results. Use it on any device and share the result easily with your trainer or nutritionist. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice.

FAQ

Values are estimates based on per 100 g standards; adjust them manually to match your serving size.
