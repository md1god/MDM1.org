Analyze ad reports and cut spend

A browser-based tool that imports CSV files from ad platform reports, analyzes spend, identifies ineffective keywords and campaigns, classifies waste…

This tool gives a quick answer to where your ad budget goes without generating any conversions. Import a CSV from your ad platform reports, and it analyzes spend, identifies keywords and campaigns consuming budget with no revenue, then classifies the waste level and estimates the amount you can save. It then shows specific optimization actions, such as lowering bids, pausing ineffective keywords, and adding negative keywords, and lets you review and approve each action before exporting a change file ready to apply manually on each platform.

FAQ

How do I import a CSV file?

Choose the file with the “CSV report file” button, then click “Analyze data”. No data is sent to any server.

What criteria are used to classify waste?

Waste is classified by spend with no conversions, with low, medium, and high levels based on the amount spent.

Can I edit or remove a recommendation before exporting?

Yes, use the checkboxes next to each row to choose what to include in the change file.

What format are the results exported in?

The file is exported as JSON that can be imported manually into any ad platform to apply the changes.

Does the tool work in all modern browsers?

It works in browsers that support HTML5 and FileReader, with no extensions or internet connection needed.
