FAQ

How do I make a valid match?

Select a piece and then an adjacent piece, or drag from one cell to a neighboring cell. A move is accepted only if it forms three connected matching pieces horizontally or vertically, or triggers a special effect.

Do cascades cost extra moves?

No. After a valid swap, groups are removed, pieces fall, and cells refill automatically; any new matches resolve without costing you another move.

What happens if there are no legal moves?

Pieces are reshuffled automatically, keeping obstacles and progress intact. If 配置为 a valid arrangement is impossible, the level ends according to the failure condition.

Can I pause the level?

Yes. The pause button freezes the timer and prevents board changes until you press Resume. Restart resets the board, goal, score, and counter to their original values.

FAQ

How do I start the first level?

Choose the game mode, board size, and goal type, then press «Start level». Then select a piece and an adjacent piece, or drag toward a neighboring cell.

What's the difference between moves and time?

In moves mode, one move is deducted for each valid swap. In time mode, the seconds keep counting during play and aren't interrupted by falling pieces or cascades.

How do I get special pieces?

A special piece is created when configuring four consecutive pieces or five or more, as well as at the intersection of two groups. Its effect stays saved until you use it in a later swap.

What do obstacles and exits do?

Obstacles block matches and movement and are cleared after a set number of nearby hits or special effects. Exits count toward the goal of moving pieces to the target cells.

Does the game need an internet connection?

No. The game runs entirely in the browser using local JavaScript and sends no data or requests any external connection.
