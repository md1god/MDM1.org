Printable Tattoo Stencil from a Reference Image

Get a high resolution tattoo stencil from any image, with full control over line thickness, shading level, skin tone, and real size in centimeters before…

The short answer is that the app turns any image into a tattoo stencil ready to print directly in the browser. It lets you adjust line thickness, shading level, skin tone, and set real dimensions in centimeters to fit the target body area exactly. After a realistic preview on skin, you can export the stencil as a PDF or a high resolution image, with no server or account registration required.

FAQ

How do I import an image to design the stencil?

Click the “Reference Image” button and choose an image file from your device; it will load automatically onto the canvas.

Can I resize the stencil to fit my desired area?

Yes, enter the desired values for width and height in centimeters, and the canvas dimensions will be recalculated automatically.

What file types can I export?

The app supports exporting the stencil in PNG format at high resolution, and you can convert it to PDF later using any external conversion tool.
