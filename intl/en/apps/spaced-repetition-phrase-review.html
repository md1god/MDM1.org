Spaced repetition phrase review

Free web tool for reviewing phrases with spaced repetition: add a phrase, start the review, and log remembered or forgot to set the next review date and…

Quick answer: You can add a new phrase with its meaning and topic, then start reviewing when cards are due, and log remembered or forgot to change the next review date according to spaced repetition. This tool helps you cut daily review down to a few minutes, because cards you know well get deferred, while difficult cards come back soon. Add your own phrases or use the samples, track progress in the chart, and repeat daily to build lasting memory without rote memorization or complicated lists. This is a lightweight version that runs in the browser with no account or connection, focused on the most important skill: reviewing a phrase at the right time.

FAQ

How do I add a new phrase?

Enter the phrase and meaning, choose a topic, then click Add phrase.

When is a card due?

It's due when its spaced repetition review time arrives.

What does the Remembered button do?

It defers the card to a longer interval and improves its ranking in your progress.

What does the Forgot button do?

It returns the card to a shorter interval so you review it soon.

Is my data saved?

No, it's kept in memory for the session only and isn't sent to any server.
