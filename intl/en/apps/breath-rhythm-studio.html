Guided breathing to calm the mind and reduce stress

Practice guided breathing with a visual and audio timer and a cycle counter. Choose a preset program or design your own, and save your settings and stats…

Yes, you can easily practice guided breathing anywhere without an account or internet connection through this app designed to be simple and clear and suitable for both beginners and advanced users. The app explains each inhale, hold and exhale phase in simple words and displays a large visual timer that fills and empties with each cycle, along with a clear counter for remaining cycles and a calm audio tone that alerts you when each phase begins. You can choose a preset program for relaxation, focus or quick calming, or create your own program by adjusting inhale, hold and exhale durations and the number of cycles to suit your body and comfort. The app also lets you choose the instruction language from two languages, adjust the voice tone and colors, and enable silent mode when needed. Your settings are saved automatically on your device, and simple stats about your completed sessions are shown to help you track your progress and maintain healthy daily breathing habits.

FAQ

Does the app work without an account or internet?

Yes, it works locally in the browser without an account or internet connection, and settings and stats are saved only on your device.

How do I use silent mode and change the voice?

Enable the silent mode option to turn off voice instructions and the tone completely while keeping the visual timer, change the instruction language between two languages, and choose a female or male voice, then save the settings.

What happens when I complete or end a session?

When all cycles are completed, the session is saved to statistics with the number of minutes and its date; ending early is not counted as a completed session.

Do I need previous breathing experience?

No, choose the beginner relaxation program and follow the rising and falling circle and audio instructions. You can skip any difficult stage or pause and resume.

How do I set a program that suits me?

Enter the inhale, hold, and exhale durations and the number of cycles in the custom program section, then press Apply Custom Program, and then press Start Session Now.
