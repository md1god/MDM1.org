Store readiness check for smart search and shopping

A local tool to check the readiness of your store's catalog, policies, structured data, product options, inventory, and feeds, with a printable report…

You can check your store's readiness for smart search and shopping in seconds: paste a product page source or upload CSV, XML, or JSON files with policy text, and get a comprehensive scorecard for catalog, policies, structured data, product options, inventory, and feeds, with missing or conflicting fields highlighted, a visual chart, and a printable report. The tool generates copy-ready fixes for JSON-LD snippets, meta tags, feed column mapping, and shipping and return templates. It is designed especially for non-Shopify stores, and all processing happens locally offline.
