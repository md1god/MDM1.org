A wake-up alarm that only stops after completing a verified task

A smart alarm that only stops after solving math problems, holding a button, or taking a live photo, with recurring schedules and a local achievement…

In short, this alarm runs entirely in your browser and won't stop until you complete a wake-up task that the browser verifies itself—no server, account, or internet connection needed. You can set a schedule that repeats daily, on weekdays, weekends, a chosen day each week, or once, and name the required task, like drinking water, stretching, or making breakfast. When the time comes, a browser-generated alarm sound plays and a challenge appears, chosen automatically from three types: solving random math problems in three consecutive questions at an easy, medium, or advanced level; holding a button for a set number of seconds until the progress bar fills; or taking a live photo with the camera and saving it locally as proof of waking up. The stop button only becomes active after you successfully complete the challenge. The sound then stops immediately, and the achievement is logged with its date and task type in the browser's local storage, along with the photo if taken. You can also test the alarm instantly before the scheduled time, edit settings, and delete the achievement log at any time—all without an internet connection after loading the page once.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Does the alarm work without an internet connection or account?

    Yes, all settings, sounds, and the achievement log are saved in the browser's local storage and work without a server or account after the page loads.

    Can the alarm be silenced without completing a wake-up task?

    No, the stop alarm button remains disabled until you solve the math problems, complete the long press, or successfully take a live photo.

    Where are the live photo and achievement log saved?

    The captured photo and achievement log are saved locally in this browser only and can be cleared at any time with the Clear local achievement log button.

    How can I test the alarm before the scheduled time?

    Press the Test alarm now button after selecting the time and task type, and the sound and challenge will start immediately for testing.

    Does the alarm support recurring schedules?

    Yes, it supports daily, weekdays, weekends, weekly on a specific day, or once, with the next time shown automatically.
