Flight Deals Explorer & Comparison

Offline web app to explore and compare flight deals... Search by airport, dates, time, and price, export results, and save favorites

Quick answer: This app searches a local flight schedule (JSON or CSV) or built-in sample data, and filters results by airport or city, dates, local time, flight duration, stops, airline, price, and currency. It then displays them in a sortable table with baggage and stop badges, with the ability to save searches and favorites in the browser, edit offers, compare up to three routes side by side, and export results. No internet connection required after loading.

FAQ

Does the app work offline?

Yes, after the page loads it works fully offline, and data is stored in the browser.

What import formats are supported?

JSON (an array of flights or {flights:[]}) and CSV with a header row.

Can I edit or delete offers?

Yes, each row has edit and delete buttons, and changes are saved locally.

How many routes can be compared?

Up to three routes side by side.

Where are favorites and saved searches stored?

In the browser's localStorage, and results can be exported as CSV.
