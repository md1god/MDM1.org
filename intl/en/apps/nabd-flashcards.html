Pulse — From Note to Card

Turn Markdown notes into spaced-repetition flashcards in seconds, and review one card at a time with smart scheduling, no account or personal data.

Quick answer: Paste your Markdown notes and turn them into spaced-repetition flashcards in seconds, then review one card at a time and rate your recall (1–4) so the app automatically adjusts the intervals. A lightweight browser-based tool with no account, server, or data sharing, with JSON export and import. Ideal for students and researchers who write notes in Markdown and want spaced repetition without manual copy-pasting, with smart scheduling that adapts to your performance and recall (simplified FSRS), a daily view of due cards and keyboard shortcuts, full local storage and JSON export/import, card generation from lists, tables, and headings, a lightweight web experience that works offline, and free to start with no limit on basic cards.

FAQ

Does it work offline?

Yes, after the page loads it runs in your browser with no server or connection.

Is my data stored?

No, data stays in memory and is exported on request; it is never sent to any server.

What Markdown formats are supported?

Lists, two-column tables, headings followed by a paragraph, and lines with ::.

How is review scheduled?

Simplified scheduling (SM-2) adjusts intervals based on your 1–4 rating.

Can I import/export?

Yes, via JSON in the text box.
