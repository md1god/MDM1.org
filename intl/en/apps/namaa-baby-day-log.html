Daily baby log: sleep, feeding, diaper, and tummy time

Log baby sleep, feeding, diapers, and tummy time with one tap, then see today's summary and a weekly chart with no account, contact info, or permanent…

Yes, you can track your baby's naps, feedings, diapers, and tummy time from one page, with no account, setup, or contact info. Press Start nap when they fall asleep, then Wake up on arrival, and the app calculates the duration automatically and shows it in the day bar. Log a feeding by choosing type and amount, a diaper by choosing status, and add a short note if needed. The summary shows the number of feeds, diaper changes, sleep hours, and tummy-time minutes, plus a chart for a full week. You can change the displayed day to return to any day logged in the current session. Data stays on this page only while it's open and disappears on refresh; there's no sign-in, no sending, and no permanent storage. This is a simple daily organizer, not medical advice, and if any symptom worries you, contact a trusted pediatrician.

    FAQ

    Do I need to create an account?

    No. The tool runs right in the page and doesn't ask for a name, email, or any identifying data.

    Is data saved after I close the page?

    No. Entries stay in memory for the session only and disappear on refresh or close.

    How is sleep calculated?

    Press Start nap when they fall asleep, then Wake up on arrival; the difference between the two times is calculated in minutes and shown in the summary.

    Is this app a substitute for a doctor?

    No. It's just an organizational log and offers no diagnosis or medical advice; see a pediatrician with any concern.

    Can I use it at daycare?

    Yes, but without names or photos; use numbers or general descriptions because data isn't saved or sent.
