FAQ

Do you need an account or an internet connection to use Deal Hub?

No. The app runs entirely in your browser, your data stays in memory only during the session, and you can export and import it as a local file whenever you want.

How do I move a deal between Kanban stages?

Drag the deal card and drop it into the desired column, and the move is logged automatically in the unified log with the new stage name.

What's the difference between the unified log and custom views?

The unified log collects activities, comments, notes, and tasks for all deals, while custom views let you filter and sort deals and choose which columns appear.

Is my data saved after I close the page?

It isn't saved automatically; click «Export JSON file» before closing, then use «Import file» to restore it in a later session.
