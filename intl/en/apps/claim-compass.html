Compensation and Unclaimed Funds Matcher

Enter your anonymous details to match class actions, refund programs, and unclaimed funds, with deadlines and a printable checklist.

Quick answer: You can find opportunities to recover money or claim compensation in minutes by entering only anonymous details such as country, years as a customer, product or service type, and bank or store name, then you'll instantly get matching opportunities from a built-in database covering class actions, fee-refund programs, and unclaimed funds. For each opportunity, the tool shows the deadline, required proof documents, and expected amount range, with the option to import a local list for your country from a data file and export a printable checklist without creating an account, saving your data in your browser, or sending anything out.

Frequently asked questions

Is my data saved or sent?

No. The tool runs only in your browser, uses no local storage, and never sends your data; you can export the results as a file when needed.

What types of opportunities can be matched?

You can match class actions, fee refund programs, and unclaimed funds based on the anonymous data you enter and available lists.

How do I add opportunities for my country?

Use the template export button to see the format, then fill a JSON file with local opportunities and import it via the import option to merge it with the built-in database.

Are the amounts and deadlines confirmed?

No, they're estimates based on the list data. Always check the official agency's terms and required documents before applying.

Can I print without an account?

Yes. Add the relevant opportunities to your checklist, then use the print button or export the list as a file and keep it locally.
