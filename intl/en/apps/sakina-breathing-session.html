This page offers a guided breathing session that runs in your browser, with no registration, account, or internet connection; all you need is one minute and a display tracking the inhale, hold, and exhale rhythm. FAQ

How do I start a session?

Choose your energy, stress, sleep, and time, or press «Suggest a rhythm», then press «Start session» and follow the moving circle until the timer ends.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No; the exercise runs right on the page, no registration is required, and your notes aren't saved after you close the page.

What's the difference between patterns?

Each pattern varies in inhale, hold, and exhale timing; short patterns suit a work break, patterns with a longer exhale help when you're stressed, and you can try more than one pattern.

Can I pause the session?

Yes; the Pause button stops the timer and keeps your place, then you can resume from the Start button or reset with the Restart button.

Is this a therapeutic exercise?

No; the tool is only for relaxation and self-focus, and is not a medical device or a substitute for professional advice.
