      FAQ

      Where is my data saved after I enter it?

      Data stays only in the current browser session, is not saved permanently, and is not sent to any server. You can export it to a JSON file and import it whenever you want.

      How do I calculate the total when using a currency other than the base currency?

      Select the currency and enter its exchange rate against the base currency at the top of the dashboard. Every total is automatically converted to the base currency in summary reports.

      Can I change an order's status after creating it?

      Yes, select the new status from the menu next to the order in the orders table, and the number of active orders in the reports will change immediately.

      What happens to inventory when I record a sale or purchase?

      A purchase increases the quantity and updates the unit cost, and a sale decreases the quantity. You can also add or remove items manually from the inventory section.

      Does the dashboard work offline?

      Yes, all calculations, tables, reports, and printing run locally within the page, and do not require an account, connection, or external services.
