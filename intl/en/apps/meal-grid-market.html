      FAQ

      How do I add a new dish?

      Enter the dish name, then add each ingredient with its quantity, unit, and store section. Once the ingredients are complete, press Save dish.

      Can I edit the weekly schedule?

      Yes; drag the dish to the desired day, or choose the dish, day, and meal from the schedule tools. You can remove any meal from inside its cell.

      How are quantities calculated in the shopping list?

      Each ingredient quantity is multiplied by the number of times the dish repeats, then matching items are combined, kilograms are converted to grams, and liters to milliliters.

      What happens to my data when I close the page?

      Your data isn't saved in the browser. Export a JSON file before leaving if you want to keep your dishes and meal plan, then import it later.
