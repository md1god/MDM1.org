FAQ

What are moratorium zones?

These are areas where issuing licenses or permits for data centers is temporarily paused while restrictions are reviewed.

How do I filter events?

Use the type, status, and date filters at the top, then press Apply filters.

Can watchlists be saved?

Watchlists are saved only for the session and are lost when the page closes; export the data to keep it.

How do I import new data?

Choose a JSON file with the required structure, then press Import.

What do evidence cards include?

Each card includes the area, type, title, date, status, deadline, limit, and a clickable source.
