Hybrid race tool with percentile calculation and a personal training plan

Explore your races by country, date, and entry type, add a race manually, get a result percentile and a personalized plan with eight stations and…

This app lets you explore hybrid races and generate a personalized training plan based on your result compared with reference curves. You can filter races by country, date, or entry type (individual, doubles, relay), or add a local race manually, then calculate your result percentile by category, gender, and age. It then generates a strength-and-endurance training plan with eight stations and interval runs, along with a printable weekly schedule and team time comparisons to spot strengths and weaknesses. All of this works offline in the current page.

FAQ
Can the app be used offline?
Yes, all data is stored in the cache and requires no internet.
How is the result percentile calculated?
The entered time is compared with reference curves by category, gender, and age to determine the percentile.
Can data be exported and imported?
Yes, there are buttons to export data as JSON and import it again.
How can I print the training plan?
After generating the plan, click the "Print" button to open your browser's print dialog.
Does the app support team comparisons?
Yes, you can delete or add team races so the differences appear in the table and times can be compared.
