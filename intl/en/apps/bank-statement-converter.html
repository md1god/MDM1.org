Turn bank statements into clean spreadsheets

Turn your bank statements into a clean spreadsheet in your browser without uploading files to any server. Secure local processing, balance verification,…

Quick answer: Yes, you can convert your bank statements into a clean spreadsheet directly in your browser without uploading your files to any server. This tool takes your statement data and turns it into organized rows with date, description, deposit, withdrawal, and balance, then verifies the totals and detects duplicate transactions before exporting to CSV or Excel. All processing happens locally in your browser—your data never leaves your device, protecting your financial privacy and reducing compliance risks.

FAQ

Does my data leave my device?

No, all processing happens locally in your browser and no files are uploaded to any server.

What export formats are supported?

Export to a CSV file that can be imported into Excel and Google Sheets.

Do I need to create an account or pay?

No, the core feature is available to use right away with no sign-up and no payment.

How does the tool verify the balance?

It checks that the opening balance plus deposits minus withdrawals equals the closing balance, and alerts you if there is even a one-cent difference.
