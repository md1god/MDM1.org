    FAQ

    How do I copy a single platform draft?

    Choose the platform, review the text in the ready platform copy box, then click Copy platform draft.

    Can timing be adjusted by time zone?

    Yes, choose a time zone from the list and the time display and export will update.

    How do I reorder posts?

    Drag an item in the timeline or use the up and down buttons.

    How do I save the session without browser storage?

    Export the JSON package, then import it later from a file.

    What does the ICS file contain?

    It contains publishing events with times, titles, and descriptions.
