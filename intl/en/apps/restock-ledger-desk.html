FAQ

Can data be saved between sessions?

No, the app runs entirely in your browser and stores nothing on the server. You can export the data manually and save it on your device.

Does the app support Excel files?

It only supports CSV files for import, and you can convert Excel files to CSV before use.

How is shipping cost calculated?

The "Allocate costs" button adds shipping and customs costs to the unit cost based on the entered values (simple simulation).

Can variances be edited manually?

The "Manual stock count" button opens a space to record variances, and you can add these values manually in future versions.

Does the app work in all browsers?

Yes, it uses standard JavaScript and does not rely on features unsupported in modern browsers.
