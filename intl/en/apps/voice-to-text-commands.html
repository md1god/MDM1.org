FAQ

Do I need an account?

No. Open the page, allow the microphone, and start; nothing is saved after you close the tab.

Where does the data go?

It isn't sent to a server. The text stays in the page's temporary memory until you copy it or close the page.

What should I do if the microphone doesn't work?

Try a modern browser, make sure the page is allowed to use the microphone, then press the start button again. If recognition isn't available, you can type directly and apply the rules.

How do I use custom commands?

Type the phrase in the command field and its wording in the template field, then say the phrase while dictating; the template will be inserted in its place.

Can I copy the result?

Yes, press the Select text to copy button; the text will be selected automatically and you can paste it into any app you use.
